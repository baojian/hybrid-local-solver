## Closest 2026 results

Fountoulakis--Martinez-Rubio remains arXiv 2602.21138v2 (8 April 2026); Wei--Yang remains 2608.16339v1 (17 August 2026). Their confinement-dependent and support-cardinality-dependent guarantees remain correctly distinguished. Wei--Yang already permits deterministic almost-linear SDD substitution; the claim here is the precise bound with polylogarithmic overhead, not the first deterministic method.
