\subsection{Semantic PPR accuracy and the RPPR bridge}

An output $\widehat\vx$ represents
$\widehat\vpi=\mD^{1/2}\widehat\vx$.  Our end-to-end semantic target is
\begin{equation}
 \norm{\mD^{-1}(\widehat\vpi-\vpi)}_\infty
 =\norm{\mD^{-1/2}(\widehat\vx-\vx_0^*)}_\infty
 \leq\epsppr.
 \label{eq:semantic-ppr-target}
\end{equation}
This is an output guarantee, not an activation threshold.  Objective error in
the regularized problem is denoted by $\epsobj$ and is never silently
identified with $\epsppr$.

The identities
\begin{equation}
 \mQ\mD^{1/2}\one=\alpha\mD^{1/2}\one,
 \qquad \mQ^{-1}\geq\vzero
 \label{eq:constant-vector-inverse-positive}
\end{equation}
follow respectively from the degree definition and the fact that $\mQ$ is a
nonsingular Stieltjes matrix.  They imply the standard RPPR--PPR bridge.

\begin{proposition}[RPPR bias and objective conversion]
\label{prop:rppr-ppr-bridge}
For every $\rho>0$,
\begin{equation}
 \vzero\leq\vx_\rho^*\leq\vx_0^*,
 \qquad
 \norm{\mD^{-1/2}(\vx_0^*-\vx_\rho^*)}_\infty\leq\rho.
 \label{eq:rppr-bias}
\end{equation}
Moreover, every feasible $\widehat\vx\geq\vzero$ satisfying
$F_\rho(\widehat\vx)-F_\rho(\vx_\rho^*)\leq\epsobj$ obeys
\begin{equation}
 \norm{\mD^{-1/2}(\widehat\vx-\vx_0^*)}_\infty
 \leq \rho+\sqrt{2\epsobj/\alpha}.
 \label{eq:objective-to-ppr}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
On the nonnegative orthant the RPPR load is obtained from the PPR load by
subtracting $\alpha\rho\mD^{1/2}\one$.  Inverse positivity and
\cref{eq:constant-vector-inverse-positive}, together with the obstacle KKT
conditions proved in \cref{thm:obstacle-reduction}, give
$\vzero\leq\vx_\rho^*\leq\vx_0^*$ and
$\vx_0^*-\vx_\rho^*\leq\rho\mD^{1/2}\one$.  Strong convexity gives
\[
 F_\rho(\widehat\vx)-F_\rho(\vx_\rho^*)
 \geq\frac\alpha2\norm{\widehat\vx-\vx_\rho^*}_2^2.
\]
Since every $d_i\geq1$, the normalized coordinate error from
$\widehat\vx$ to $\vx_\rho^*$ is at most
$\sqrt{2\epsobj/\alpha}$.  The triangle inequality proves
\cref{eq:objective-to-ppr}.
\end{proof}

The RPPR optimum is zero exactly when $\rho\geq1/d_v$.  Indeed, the only
possibly positive shifted load is at the seed and equals
$\alpha(1-\rho d_v)/\sqrt{d_v}$.  We therefore call
\begin{equation}
 0<\rho<1/d_v
 \label{eq:nonzero-rppr-regime}
\end{equation}
the nontrivial regime; it is recognized with one degree query.

\subsection{Fully charged local work}

A scan of $U\subseteq\gV$ costs $\vol(U)$.  Our work bounds additionally
charge construction of every principal matrix, every repeated row and
boundary scan, candidate insertion, numerical solver state, residual
certification, projection, materialization, state access, and output.  There
is no free support oracle and no ambient preprocessing.  The theorem is in the
exact-real algebraic word model from the shared block.  The parameter
$\zeta\in(0,1)$ denotes the probability of an explicit reported failure.

\subsection{Main theorems}

The regularized result will be proved as \cref{thm:local-rppr}.

\begin{theorem}[Local RPPR, informal]
\label{thm:local-rppr-informal}
For a canonical point-source instance, there is an exact-real randomized
local algorithm which, with failure probability at most $\zeta$, returns
$\widehat\vx\geq\vzero$ with RPPR objective gap at most $\epsobj$.  Its
expected fully charged work is
\begin{equation}
 \softO\!\left(
   \frac{1}{\rho\sqrt\alpha}
   \log\frac1{\epsobj}\log\frac2\zeta
 \right).
 \label{eq:informal-rppr-work}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

The two-stage consequence will be proved as
\cref{thm:two-stage-completion}.

\begin{theorem}[Local point-source PPR, informal]
\label{thm:local-ppr-informal}
For $0<\epsppr<1$, a strict two-stage algorithm returns an output satisfying
\cref{eq:semantic-ppr-target}, with failure probability at most $\zeta$ and
expected fully charged work
\begin{equation}
 \softO\!\left(\frac{1}{\epsppr\sqrt\alpha}\right).
 \label{eq:informal-ppr-work}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

The soft notation hides polylogarithms in the displayed accuracy and failure
parameters and in the local supplied-matrix SDD routine; it hides no
polynomial dependence on $n$, $m$, $|\mathcal S_\rho^*|$, or an unknown sign
margin.
