Classical APPR has worst-case degree-weighted work
\(\Theta(1/(\alpha\epsappr))\), even on an unweighted center-seeded star and
independently of the legal active-vertex ordering. The construction isolates
the mass-processing and high-degree rescanning mechanisms that accelerated and
hybrid local solvers must overcome. The remaining goal is to identify when a
hybrid method improves one or both factors while retaining a compatible local
stopping certificate and work model.
