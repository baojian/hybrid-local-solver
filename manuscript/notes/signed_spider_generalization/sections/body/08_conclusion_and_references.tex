\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The SOR and FISTA results are comparable at the level of mechanism, not yet
as an end-to-end race.  Their legitimate common ground is the PageRank
operator, the degree-work model, and the question of whether acceleration
preserves locality.  Their objectives, seeds, and stopping targets differ.
The bridge in \Cref{thm:rppr-ppr-bias,cor:objective-gap-conversion} shows how
to design a matched comparison rather than pretending those differences do
not exist.

For generalization, the situation is more positive than the star note alone
suggests.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item The signed SOR spectral mechanism extends exactly to every fixed
      bipartite face, with one global relaxation parameter available for all
      such faces.  Face-specific tuning remains valid when its certified
      spectral-estimation cost is charged explicitly.
\item Spider prefixes have the accelerated scale
      $1/\sqrt{\alpha+L^{-2}}$.  On complete equal-arm hub-seeded spiders,
      \Cref{thm:radial-semantic-damping} proves a dimension-free, log-free
      semantic envelope for plain optimal SOR.
\item That maximum-semantic envelope is genuinely radial rather than
      graph-uniform.  The layered family in
      \Cref{thm:global-semantic-sor-stop} defeats every universal constant
      envelope, and \Cref{cor:global-semantic-sor-work-stop} rules out the
      target product work for literal complete-face full-sweep plain SOR.
      \Cref{thm:adaptive-polynomial-propagation-stop} extends this locality
      stop to adaptive scalar block-SOR and zero-start complete-matvec
      Chebyshev/Krylov recurrences, while excluding nonlocal vector response.
\item End-to-end semantic PPR at the target product scale is already proved on
      arbitrary rooted trees through exact responses, and on graphs with
      bounded biconnected blocks through dynamic block responses.
\item The first genuinely unresolved graph class is therefore not ``spiders''
      or ``trees,'' but large biconnected cyclic cores with changing signed
      state and many coupled boundary demands.
\end{itemize}

For changing bipartite faces,
\eqref{eq:nonsettled-face-pythagoras} proves that a zero-padded nonsettled
iterate pays exactly the old numerical error plus the optimum-shift energy.
Together with the fixed-face power bound this gives supplied,
geometrically-growing-face continuation at the soft product scale.  The open
part is now repeated face scans and output-sensitive boundary reporting, not
an unexplained numerical reset shock.

This also suggests the right algorithmic philosophy.  SOR should be retained
where its signed fixed-face recurrence is cheap and its state can be carried.
Scalar or bounded-dimensional response methods should be used on tree-like
regions where they are strictly stronger.  A future graph-uniform method is
likely a composition of these primitives plus a finite-band boundary
reporter, not one recurrence applied indiscriminately to every graph.  The
layered stop makes that last distinction a theorem for scalar-adaptive
color-block SOR and source-polynomial complete-matvec recurrences, not merely
a design preference.

\section*{Reproducibility note}

The companion script \texttt{verify\_spider.py} checks deterministic
bipartite mode matrices, finite powers, spider spectra, the reflected radial
recurrence and sharp envelope, the sweep-order witness, the PPR--RPPR bias
box, the exact layered-funnel transition, Green, hitting-time, output-scale,
and finite-propagation identities, and nonsettled face-shock Pythagoras on
paths, cycles, and stars.  The independent
\texttt{verify\_fixed\_face.py} audit checks exact rational trace,
determinant, and optimal-parameter identities, the universal-$\omega$ bound,
finite powers, and spider-prefix formulas.  These checks are counterexample
guards; the displayed derivations remain the proof authority.
The exact-arithmetic \texttt{verify\_adaptive\_spectral.py} audit checks safe
certified upper tuning, the failure of lower spectral estimates as upper
certificates, adaptive scalar light cones, the interleaved-order witness, and
both red two-step and full PageRank polynomial propagation.
