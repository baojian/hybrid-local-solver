\section{A staged proof and falsification plan}
\label{sec:research-plan}

The best plan is not ``prove the arbitrary-graph theorem'' in one jump.  Each
stage below adds exactly one difficulty, has a concrete deliverable, and has a
stop condition that prevents a conditional rate from being promoted as an
algorithm.

\subsection{Stage 0: freeze the common comparison contract---completed here}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.7cm,style=nextline]
\item[Deliverable]
The bias inequality \eqref{eq:rppr-ppr-bias}, objective conversion
\eqref{eq:objective-to-semantic}, fixed-face equations
\eqref{eq:rppr-active-face-system}, and the three legitimate comparison
protocols.
\item[Go condition]
Every future SOR--FISTA plot or theorem names $(\rho,\tau,\delta)$ separately
and maps them to $\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}$.
\item[Stop condition]
Reject any ranking that compares the center-SOR semantic theorem directly to
the leaf-FISTA objective-gap obstruction.
\end{description}

\subsection{Stage 1: log-free SOR on radial spiders---completed here}

The exact fixed-face spectral theorem leaves a maximum-norm gap.  Theorem
\ref{thm:radial-semantic-damping} closes it for the complete equal-arm,
hub-seeded spider.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.7cm,style=nextline]
\item[Deliverable]
The reflected-cycle recurrence
\eqref{eq:radial-chebyshev-recurrence}, exact shift formulas
\eqref{eq:radial-chebyshev-first-kind}--%
\eqref{eq:radial-chebyshev-second-kind}, and semantic envelope
\eqref{eq:radial-semantic-exact-envelope}.
\item[Result]
The constants are uniform in both the arm count and arm length, and
\Cref{cor:radial-output-scale-sor} gives the desired log-free output-scale
work.
\item[Scope guard]
The source must be on the first color, the arms must be equal, and complete
full-graph sweeps are charged.  With the source on the second color, the sharp
envelope already fails after one unswapped sweep on $P_2$; swapping the color
order restores the symmetric statement.  Unequal-arm numerical cells pass,
but no theorem is inferred from them.
\end{description}

\subsection{Stage 2: carry SOR across nested bipartite faces}

The global parameter in \Cref{lem:one-omega-all-bipartite-faces} removes
retuning.  Lemma~\ref{lem:settled-face-shock} and
\Cref{prop:windowed-sor-continuation} now close numerical state transfer
under a supplied geometric volume gate.  The remaining task is to remove
that gate or charge its reporter.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.7cm,style=nextline]
\item[Algorithm candidate]
Maintain a safe nested RPPR face, use $\omega_\alpha$ on its bipartition,
zero-pad newly admitted coordinates, retain the signed SOR iterate, and query
a finite-band KKT reporter only at declared checkpoints.
\item[Proved window]
With $H=\widetilde O(1/\sqrt\alpha)$, the actual nonsettled iterate obeys
\eqref{eq:windowed-energy-recursion}; no exact old-face reset is inserted.
\item[Work obligation]
Over the same window, charge row reads, state writes, admissions, and reporter
events by
\begin{equation}
 \widetilde O\!\left(
  \frac{\operatorname{vol}(U_{t+H}\setminus U_t)
        +\text{paid shock mass}}
       {\sqrt\alpha}
 \right)
 \label{eq:windowed-work-target}
\end{equation}
without rescanning the whole prefix after every singleton event.
\item[Go condition]
Replace the geometric-volume hypothesis by an output-sensitive admission and
reporter ledger while retaining \eqref{eq:windowed-energy-recursion} and
\eqref{eq:windowed-work-target}.
\item[Stop condition]
An explicit path, T tree, or bipartite core accumulates unpaid
boundary-refresh or repeated-face-scan work across more than one acceleration
window.  Numerical state-reset energy alone is no longer a valid blocker.
\end{description}

\subsection{Stage 3: move from bipartite to nearly bipartite cores}

Odd cycles remove the exact red--black mode decomposition.  They should first
be admitted as a controlled perturbation rather than handled by an
unstructured multicolor claim.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.7cm,style=nextline]
\item[Structural hypothesis]
For a locally constructed partition, write
$S=S_{\rm cross}+S_{\rm same}$ and assume either
\begin{equation}
 \|S_{\rm same}\|_2\leq c_0\sqrt\alpha
 \label{eq:near-bipartite-target}
\end{equation}
or a stronger locally checkable energy version.  Charge the construction and
verification of the partition.
\item[Target theorem]
Show that the within-color correction is a relative perturbation small enough
to preserve the windowed contraction, perhaps through one symmetric cleanup
or a short inner correction.
\item[Counterexamples]
Odd cycles with one chord, tailed triangles, stars of odd cycles, and
high-degree graphs with few but spectrally heavy same-color edges.
\item[Stop condition]
Do not replace the spectral or energy hypothesis by a raw count of odd-cycle
edges unless that count provably implies \eqref{eq:near-bipartite-target}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Stage 4: separators and bounded treewidth}

Bounded block size is already solved because every child region communicates
through one articulation.  The next response class has separators of size
$w>1$.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.7cm,style=nextline]
\item[Representation]
Maintain an online separator tree whose node stores a $w$-port Schur response
or certified approximation.  Admissions update only the nodes on one charged
route.
\item[Target scale]
For $w=\log^{O(1)}(1/(\alpha\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}))$, prove total maintenance,
query, SOR repair, and output work
$\widetilde O(1/(\sqrt\alpha\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}))$.
\item[Hard obligation]
The decomposition cannot be future-support advice.  Either construct it from
exposed adjacency or state and charge a promised-decomposition input model.
\item[Stop condition]
A response node whose update explicitly rewrites all descendants is a hidden
repeated-prefix algorithm and does not meet the target.
\end{description}

\subsection{Stage 5: arbitrary biconnected cores}

This is the graph-uniform frontier.  Exact demand arrays and fixed global
low-rank response banks both have known representation obstructions.  The
weaker finite-band problem should be attempted first.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.7cm,style=nextline]
\item[Reporter contract]
At a checkpoint, return every boundary coordinate whose normalized KKT demand
is above a positive margin, certify that no coordinate lies above a larger
margin, and refine only intervals intersecting the transition band.
\item[Energy route]
Use the squared norm of exterior-demand change to pack heavy crossings;
couple light unreported changes to the semantic error budget rather than
forcing exact zero-sign maintenance.
\item[Iterative route]
Between reports, use the global bipartite SOR block where possible and a
spectrally controlled correction on the cyclic remainder.  The report and
the correction must share one potential so neither cost is double counted.
\item[Promotion gate]
No graph-uniform theorem until the algorithm is specified under ordinary
adjacency access, the face-change recurrence is actual rather than idealized,
all repeated state operations are charged, and one terminal semantic
certificate is proved.
\end{description}

\subsection{Immediate continuation work package}

The following ordering maximizes useful progress in one uninterrupted pass.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item Verify the comparison bridge and the bipartite mode algebra symbolically
      and numerically.
\item Implement the radial spider recurrence without constructing all $B$
      arms; scan the parameter grid in Stage~1 and preserve the first worst
      cell, not only averages.
\item Reuse the proved Chebyshev/Green-profile theorem
      \eqref{eq:radial-semantic-exact-envelope} as the radial baseline.
\item Test the global-$\omega_\alpha$ zero-padded recurrence on endpoint paths,
      unequal spiders, and the asymmetric T-tree witness.  Measure semantic
      error, face-shock energy, repeated volume, and state writes separately.
\item Build the smallest output-sensitive reporter ledger compatible with
      \eqref{eq:windowed-energy-recursion}; do not start with arbitrary cyclic
      graphs until it survives the bipartite tests.
\end{enumerate}

The radial semantic lemma and the geometrically gated continuation theorem
are now proved.  The next useful outcome is either a paid finite-band reporter
that removes the geometric-volume hypothesis, or a small reproducible
counterexample showing that repeated singleton reporting defeats this SOR
route and should be replaced by response composition.
