\title{From Signed Stars to Spiders and Beyond:\\
What SOR and FISTA Really Compare, and a Graph-Family Generalization Plan}
\author{Baojian Zhou\\
\small Working research note for \texttt{hybrid-local-solver}}
\date{August 2026}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
The optimal signed-SOR star theorem and the standard FISTA leaf-star
obstruction are informative together, but they are not a head-to-head
algorithm comparison: SOR solves the unregularized PageRank linear system,
whereas FISTA solves an $\ell_1$-regularized PageRank objective; the two
examples also use different seeds and accuracy namespaces.  This note makes
the legitimate bridge explicit.  The RPPR minimizer is within $\rho$ of PPR
in degree-normalized semantic norm, an RPPR objective gap $\delta$ contributes
at most $\sqrt{2\delta/\alpha}$, and on a known positive face RPPR is exactly
the same PageRank principal linear system with a shifted right-hand side.

We then extend the algebraic star mechanism.  On every fixed exposed
bipartite face, red--black SOR decomposes into two-dimensional singular-mode
blocks.  Its optimal relaxation has exact spectral factor
$(1-t)/(1+t)$, where $t=\sqrt{1-\rho_J^2}$ and $\rho_J$ is the Jacobi spectral
radius.  This gives a finite fixed-face semantic solve in
$\widetilde O(\operatorname{vol}(U)/\sqrt\alpha)$ work using one graph-global
parameter.  If the face spectrum is supplied, a depth-$L$ spider prefix has
the sharper sweep scale
$\Theta(1/\sqrt{\alpha+L^{-2}})$.  On every complete equal-arm,
hub-seeded spider, an even reflection converts the semantic recurrence into
a Chebyshev wave on a cycle and proves the log-free envelope
$\lambda_\alpha^{2k}(1+2k(1-\lambda_\alpha^2))$.  This yields the target
plain-SOR work on the output-scale spider family.  This maximum-norm statement
does not extend graph-uniformly to all bipartite faces: a layered family with
geometrically decreasing channel multiplicities keeps a far semantic error
arbitrarily close to its initial maximum through any fixed multiple of
$1/\sqrt\alpha$ source-first full sweeps.  Consequently no graph-independent
constant can multiply the spider's exponential envelope on all finite
connected bipartite graphs.  This is a relative semantic-contraction stop,
and the same family puts the semantic output at inverse-volume scale, ruling
out graph-uniform $O(1/(\sqrt\alpha\,\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}))$ work for the
literal complete-face full-sweep plain-SOR primitive.  It is not a lower bound
for all algorithms.  The same finite-propagation stop does extend to every
finite scalar-adaptive source-first red--black SOR schedule, even with exact
eigenvalue advice, and to zero-start Chebyshev/Krylov recurrences whose only
vector operation is a complete sparse PageRank matvec.  It does not extend to
eigenvector transforms, inverse or Green responses, nonlocal warm starts, or
other vector-valued advice.  Plain SOR also does not by itself discover the
face.  Face-specific spectral tuning is therefore stated with its estimation
cost: a certified upper Jacobi-radius bound gives the advertised rate, while
a raw lower Rayleigh estimate does not.  A componentwise Collatz iteration
now supplies such an upper certificate a posteriori: every estimator pass is
charged as a complete face scan, the relaxation is frozen only after a
checkable relative-gap test succeeds, and a scan-budget expiry safely falls
back to the graph-global parameter.  Certification can be arbitrarily slow,
and on a complete face it gives exactly the global parameter and no sweep
benefit.
For discovery, stronger proved response results already cover hub-rooted spiders,
arbitrary rooted trees, and bounded-biconnected-block graphs, yielding the
target product scale after the RPPR-to-PPR bias conversion.  The unresolved
frontier is therefore narrowed to large biconnected cyclic cores, where a
local face change may alter many boundary demands and signed state records.
Nonsettled face-shock Pythagoras and a supplied geometrically growing-face
SOR continuation theorem close the numerical state-transfer layer, but not
arbitrary singleton reporting or repeated-prefix work.
On any genuinely supplied fixed face, a separate CG/Chebyshev dispatch gives
the stronger two-sided residual certificate in
$O(\operatorname{vol}(U)\min\{|U|,\alpha^{-1/2}\log(2/\tau)\})$ work:
exact CG supplies the dimension cap and interval Chebyshev supplies the
spectral cap.  This terminal rule does not amortize changing faces.
The final sections give a staged plan with explicit proof obligations,
counterexample tests, and promotion gates.
\end{abstract}
