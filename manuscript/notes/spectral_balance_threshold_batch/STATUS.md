# Status: deterministic spectral balance for threshold-batch RPPR

Updated 2026-08-31.  `README.md` contains the arguments and witnesses.

## Target

Determine whether deterministic Chebyshev/CG, possibly with a tunable cutoff,
can replace the randomized supplied-face SDD calls while preserving fully
charged local work

```text
O_tilde(M/sqrt(alpha)),       M=vol(S_rho^*),
```

and in particular whether a valid end-to-end bound of the form

```text
O_tilde(M/tau + M tau/alpha)
```

can be proved.

The model is the canonical finite simple connected unit-weight graph with one
point source, `Q=alpha I+(1-alpha)L_norm/2`,
`S_rho^*=supp(x_rho*)`, and `M=vol(S_rho^*)`.  Accuracy in this handoff is
the Stage-I RPPR objective namespace `eps_obj`, not `eps_ppr`; conversion to a
terminal PPR statement is outside this note.  Work is exact-real
adjacency-list work and includes every row exposure, repeated scan, sparse
product, response application, factor/hierarchy construction, certificate,
materialization, and output.  Bit complexity is **Open**.

This directory is an unregistered working companion, so it has no formal
`registry.toml` dependency edges and does not supersede any registered
`main.tex`.  Its mathematical imports come from `active_edge_lcp`,
`incremental_active_set_sdd`, `response_preconditioned_hybrid`, and the shared
problem definition.  Context/provenance also includes `evolving_support_cg`
and the source papers audited in `README.md`.  Registration and any promotion
to the controller-owned shared ledger require a separate controller review.

Canonical claim classes are **Source**, **Proved here**, **Conditional**,
**Measured**, **Open**, and **Refuted**.  The first column below retains more
specific audit tags, but each tag has the following class: `Proved`, `Closed`,
or `Exact` means **Proved here**; `Empirical` means **Measured**;
`Conjectural` means **Conditional**; `stop` or `obstruction` means
**Refuted** only for the named inference; and `audit` means **Source** unless
the row itself says otherwise.

| **Proved cycle-rank first-`J` theorem** | A connected face of cyclomatic number `k_U` is a grounded tree matrix plus the positive rank-`k_U` feedback-edge Laplacian.  Tree pair-Green queries form its Woodbury core without materializing `k_U` dense columns. Rebuilding over the first capped `J=O_tilde(1/sqrt(alpha))` maximal threshold faces gives `O_tilde((M+k^omega_mat)/sqrt(alpha))`.  Degree coordinates remove radicals.  This is not a bit/stability theorem, and the output is the manuscript's certified approximate/capped point, not necessarily the exact full-support solution.  The chronological singleton-persistent bound remains `O_tilde(Mk^2+k^3)`, and cycle rank alone does not provide a no-scan reporter for multi-contact exterior rows. |

## Current decision boundary

Chebyshev/CG now solves the numerical and support-certification part, and the
one-positive-probe theorem removes Gaussian source/trace sampling from fixed
and causally accumulated faces with an explicit candidate-volume ledger.  The
remaining `o(1)` issue is response-summary *production* (`CenterLift`):
whether the positive old-face inverse response can be reused across nested
faces deterministically and locally, without recomputing or scanning the
accumulated face after every small expansion.  Candidate reporting after
those summaries are available is closed.  Any positive route must prove one
of the two open producer items above.
Any negative conclusion must be scoped to a specified access/recurrence
model; the current examples are not a lower bound against all possible
deterministic graph algorithms.
