## Central blocker

Close the epoch-internal query without a full old-face pass after every light correction. Slack normalization already reduces all far crossings over a checkpoint segment to one packed heavy-row problem, Gaussian compression makes the source width logarithmic, and a fixed bank accumulates exact prefix decoder norms without source replay while its weights stay fixed. Round 020 proves that a genuine slack-weight change cannot in general reuse only those norms: the minimal three-label collision requires richer prechange state, charged old-source/response replay, a cut rebuild, a different bank/decoder, or rejection. The first-failure theorem removes per-event fresh target builds; bidirectional application and online rent-or-buy close the anchor-right-hand-side count; and the frozen-face aggregate Chebyshev theorem removes both row-by-row shifted cleanup and the fixed-face `1/alpha` factor only when all debt can wait for that flush. The canonical singleton-path proposition eliminates literal geometric sleep, while the frozen notched-sun proposition eliminates eager separately addressed exact slack vectors even when the delta output is perfectly separated. `NotchedSunScaleDelta` shows that a verified template and a computable scalar future-tail certificate can avoid both persistent slack materialization and on-query response work on that exact trace; `NotchedSunPermutationDelta` removes the prescribed common order; `NotchedSunMultiplicityDelta` handles declared common multisets; `NotchedSunAmplitudeDelta` handles finite declared positive occurrence masses, including seen-but-unreported labels; `NotchedSunColumnAmplitudeDelta` separates independently declared column schedules and arbitrary asynchronous column interleavings; and `NotchedSunBatchAmplitudeDelta` admits finite simultaneous batches with exact endpoint crossings and atomic staged validation. These theorems isolate the reusable condition: global latency and batch grouping may be separated from certificate mass only when unrelated events provably leave that state block unchanged and same-block increments are positive and linear. The missing proof is therefore genuinely dynamic: an implicit/packed output-sensitive implementation of the slack-weighted target rows—or a packed partial Chebyshev flush—covering undeclared or unbounded mass, arbitrary fragment support, cross-column coupled state, changing capacity/weights, and the explicit transition band without repeated source-side anchor-cut scans or dense target-side stored-response reads, unless a different support-safe trace is proved. The next falsifiable target is a sparse richer-state invariant or a geometric paid replay/rebuild theorem that survives more than one weight refresh on a high-rank core and charges actual old-face incidences, response reads/writes, validation, staging, rejection, workspace, and output. The batched columnwise theorem is only a verified-template, exactly separable Neumann-tail statement and is not evidence for a generic scalar correction potential, free phase transition, or hidden within-batch chronology.
