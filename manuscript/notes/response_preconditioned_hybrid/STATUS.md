## Central blocker

Close the epoch-internal query without a full old-face pass after every light correction. Slack normalization already reduces all far crossings over a checkpoint segment to one packed heavy-row problem, Gaussian compression makes the source width logarithmic, and a fixed bank accumulates exact prefix decoder norms without source replay. The first-failure theorem removes per-event fresh target builds; bidirectional application and online rent-or-buy close the anchor-right-hand-side count; and the frozen-face aggregate Chebyshev theorem removes both row-by-row shifted cleanup and the fixed-face `1/alpha` factor only when all debt can wait for that flush. The canonical singleton-path proposition eliminates literal geometric sleep, while the frozen notched-sun proposition eliminates eager separately addressed exact slack vectors even when the delta output is perfectly separated. `NotchedSunScaleDelta` now shows that a verified template and a computable scalar future-tail certificate can avoid both persistent slack materialization and on-query response work on that exact trace. The missing proof is therefore genuinely dynamic: an implicit/packed output-sensitive implementation of the slack-weighted target rows—or a packed partial Chebyshev flush—covering arbitrary legal fragment orders, capacity/weight changes, and the explicit transition band without repeated source-side anchor-cut scans or dense target-side stored-response reads, unless a different support-safe trace is proved. The falsifiable target is an aggregate charge on a genuinely multiscale high-rank core including actual old-face incidences read, response-state writes, fresh source probes, validation, membership, workspace, and output.
