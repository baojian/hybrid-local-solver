\subsection{Finite-band cyclic response}

The strongest immediate target is a data structure that reports every
boundary demand whose normalized lower interval exceeds the gate, certifies
all demands below the safe rejection threshold, and refines only the finite
transition band.  Its total charge must include hierarchy construction,
old-face reads, response applications, rekeys, and output.  Coordinate-specific
leverage estimation is no longer part of the target by
\cref{cor:boundary-leverage-one}.

One plausible route is a hierarchical terminal sparsifier or approximate Schur
representation.  Spectral approximation supplies energy control, and
\cref{lem:boundary-interval} converts it to queried coordinate intervals.  The
search combinatorics reduce, by
\cref{lem:source-aware-hierarchical-locator}, to constant-factor group traces
of source-conditioned response rows.  The unresolved step is to maintain
those traces dynamically on arbitrary cyclic cores and then evaluate all
reached leaves.  By \cref{thm:source-leverage-diagonal-loss}, the same group
quantity is exactly a weighted aggregate decrease of terminal Schur
diagonals; \cref{cor:additive-group-locator} requires only one-sided additive
accuracy at the current pruning scale.  Thus source-response sketches are an
optional estimator, not a second necessary state.  The reporter's unavoidable
output is already packed:
\cref{lem:response-energy-packing} charges every normalized
$\theta$-movement by degree volume to objective shock, while
\cref{lem:boundary-variation} proves one-sided motion and a global weighted
variation ledger.  Before an exact correction is materialized,
\cref{lem:source-aware-leverage-packing} also packs the rows whose certified
intervals can be wide.  By \cref{cor:associative-dual-batching}, a hierarchy
may merge consecutive light sources without changing the exact trajectory at
its checkpoints.  The remaining primitive is therefore the target-side
implementation of a margin-adaptive aggregate Schur-diagonal-loss oracle,
equivalently a source-aware $\ell_2$ locator, over a dynamically maintained
terminal representation, with initialization and every update charged to
exposed local volume.
\Cref{thm:static-diagonal-loss-checkpoint} closes the initialization part at
the accelerated product scale, even when every boundary leaf is materialized.
The unresolved charge in this subsection is now only the sequence of light
post-checkpoint updates and reached-node target queries.  Source whitening is
already charged by \cref{thm:chebyshev-frontier-whitening}: a spectral
frontier matrix-vector product, rather than an exact inverse square root,
suffices.

The measurement count is now closed by
\cref{cor:shared-coded-harmonic-bank}: one query needs only
$\widetilde O(1+|F|E^2/m^2)$ target rows and candidates.  Moreover,
\cref{thm:certified-adaptive-coded-bank-reuse} reuses one target bank per
doubling capacity, so the epoch build dimension is controlled by the largest
packed query rather than their sum.  Finally,
\cref{thm:bidirectional-harmonic-application,cor:online-harmonic-rent-or-buy}
remove an additional artificial choice: per capacity, an online rule spends
at most twice the smaller of the target-row count and cumulative source width
in anchor right-hand sides.  For an entire checkpoint segment,
\cref{thm:slack-normalized-partial-gate-packing} removes the requirement that
all far labels have the same margin: scaling each response row by its own
checkpoint slack produces one packed query with target dimension and crossing
degree volume $\widetilde O(1+b\mathcal E/m^2)$.
\Cref{cor:realized-directional-slack-bank} shows that the literal realized
gate needs only one accumulated scalar measurement column, updated causally
without source history.  If a direction-independent potential bank is needed,
the Gaussian corollary compresses the concatenated source to logarithmic
width, and
\cref{lem:append-only-bucket-norm-accumulation} proves that a fixed bank can
absorb causal source blocks without retaining or replaying the old columns.
Thus heterogeneous slack, realized source dimension, and fixed-bank source
history are no longer missing.  However,
\cref{prop:three-label-norm-only-reweight-obstruction} shows that a genuine
slack reweight cannot universally refresh a collided bucket from those norms
alone: the implementation must retain richer state, replay old information,
rebuild, change the bank/decoder, or reject.  The exact remaining epoch theorem is
an aggregate bound of the form
\begin{equation}
 \sum_j\mathcal C_{{\rm harm},j}
 =\widetilde O\!\left(
   \frac{\operatorname{cvol}(S_{\rm epoch})}{\sqrt\alpha}
   +\sum_j|\mathcal C_j|
  \right),
 \label{eq:desired-epoch-harmonic-charge}
\end{equation}
including the one-time multiscale bank builds, every source-oriented anchor
cut scan, every response-state write and read, fresh source probes, and exact
validation.  Equivalently, the harmonic part must bound the rent/buy
implementation costs refining the build/apply split in
\cref{eq:certified-harmonic-build-apply-split}.  This is a falsifiable
data-structure statement rather than an unspecified oracle.  In particular,
it must pay for target-row installation/application, any capacity or
slack-weight changes, transition-band maintenance, and validation; none of
these operations is hidden by the append-only norm state.

The notched double sun is now a calibration family, not a reporter lower-bound
family for \cref{eq:desired-epoch-harmonic-charge}.  Its exact updates have
linear dimension, but
\cref{cor:notched-sun-finite-band-separation} proves that the separated
finite-band oracle follows one path and validates one leaf.  It nevertheless
audits a concrete implementation: with fresh source and target randomness and
a summable failure schedule,
\cref{prop:notched-sun-fresh-dense-coded-table-charge} proves that rebuilding
the explicit dense harmonic table at every event pays an unsuppressed factor
proportional to the anchor size.  The certified-reuse theorem bypasses that
specific policy, and the online orientation theorem bounds its anchor
right-hand-side count by the cheaper of buying target rows and renting source
blocks.  It still leaves repeated cut scans on the rental branch and stored
response reads on the purchase branch.  Any future negative family must keep many
response coordinates at the \emph{same} declared pruning scale through a long
certified trace; exact density below the margin is irrelevant.  A positive
proof must instead establish \cref{eq:desired-epoch-harmonic-charge} through
an implicit block response, a terminal sparsifier, compressed row access, or
the aggregated shifted-debt state.  The last route now has a complete
fixed-face primitive: \cref{thm:aggregate-chebyshev-debt-flush} sums every
outstanding positive fragment before propagation, permits signed Chebyshev
iterates internally, and certifies the mathematical one-sided exterior
interval family in one product-scale pass.  The separate
\textsc{AllBoundaryFlush} vector pays for the cut contraction,
materialization, classification, and emission needed to return those
intervals.  Although
\cref{cor:geometric-aggregate-debt-flush} gives the fully charged fixed-
decision checkpoint target at fixed column count for one flush per
geometrically growing face,
\cref{prop:singleton-path-geometric-sleep-obstruction} rules out that literal
schedule for the exact gate.  The separate fixed-face test
\cref{prop:notched-sun-eager-slack-vector-obstruction} shows that merely
replacing each all-boundary flush by an eagerly maintained exact boundary
slack vector also fails: at a separated one-label-per-event gate, every exact
response increment is dense and the named representation rewrites
$\Theta(pn^2)$ cells.  This is useful scope, not a reporter lower bound; the
matching label is exposed by the graph incidence and implicit or packed
response remains viable.  Indeed,
\cref{thm:notched-sun-scale-delta-reporter} closes this exact calibration
trace in the explicit regime
$\vartheta\leq1/(1296n^2)$: a verified-template Neumann tail is a computable
universal future-safe certificate, so no exterior response coordinate is
persisted or queried.  That reporter depends on the prescribed labels,
amplitudes, and scale.  The order dependence is removed by
\cref{thm:notched-sun-permutation-delta-reporter}: a seen-label dictionary
verifies every common permutation online and leaves the complete linear
ledger unchanged.  Bounded repetition on the same template is removed by
\cref{thm:notched-sun-multiplicity-delta-reporter}: exact declared
multiplicities give a last-unseen event horizon and one count cell per label,
again without a response application.  Declared positive occurrence
amplitudes are removed by
\cref{thm:notched-sun-amplitude-delta-reporter}: a per-label response-mass
capacity, gap, and whole-future mass horizon identify the first certified
crossing even after earlier subthreshold occurrences.  The common-column
schedule is removed by
\cref{thm:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-delta-reporter}: each column has
its own schedule, mass horizon, and margin, while the external event stream
may interleave columns arbitrarily.  Exact column separation keeps unrelated
events out of the affected response and permits one updated certificate per
event.  The one-event restriction is removed by
\cref{thm:notched-sun-batched-columnwise-amplitude-delta-reporter}: an atomic
batch may contain any tagged next consecutive occurrences from many cells and
columns, and its endpoint delta set and one certificate per affected column
are exact.  Batch interactions, fragments, atomic validation, temporary
staging, and output remain separate in the full ledger.  What remains is an
output-sensitive partial flush for undeclared or unbounded mass, arbitrary
fragment support, cross-column coupled response, or a separately proved
alternative support-safe trace.
