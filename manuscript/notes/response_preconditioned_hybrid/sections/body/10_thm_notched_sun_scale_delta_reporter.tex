\begin{theorem}[A scale-certified delta reporter on the frozen notched sun]
\label{thm:notched-sun-scale-delta-reporter}
Use exactly the fixed face, exposed cut, complete shifted ladder, first-rung
matrix $M$, unit-fragment order, logical output columns, gate $g$, and band
$m=g/2$ of
\cref{prop:notched-sun-eager-slack-vector-obstruction}.  Thus $n\geq5$,
$\eta\in(0,1/8)$, $\vartheta=(1-\alpha)/2$, and
$c_\eta=(1+\eta)^2\eta^2$.  Impose the explicit near-one regime
\begin{equation}
 0<\vartheta\leq\frac1{1296n^2}.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-scale-regime}
\end{equation}
Then, for every event $k$,
\begin{align}
 h_k^{(k)}
 &\geq\frac{\vartheta^2}{d_{u_k}(1+c_\eta)}
 >\vartheta^{5/2}=g,
 \label{eq:notched-sun-scale-matching}\\
 \sum_{\ell=1}^k h_i^{(\ell)}
 &\leq\frac{9k\vartheta^3}{1-3\vartheta}
 \leq\frac12\vartheta^{5/2}=g-m,
 \qquad i>k.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-scale-future-tail}
\end{align}

Name the following promised-template partial reporter
\textsc{NotchedSunScaleDelta}.  Its preprocessing scans the frozen face,
queries the report degrees in a second adjacency round, verifies the complete
cycle--chord--petal--report template and label pairing, constructs the fixed
ladder, and checks \cref{eq:notched-sun-scale-regime}.  Its persistent state
after event $k$ is only
\begin{equation}
 \mathsf{State}_k
 =(\text{verified template and label map},\text{fixed ladder},k,
   \text{logical-column/sign metadata});
 \label{eq:notched-sun-scale-state}
\end{equation}
it contains no entry of $h^{(\ell)}$, no cumulative response $y^{[k]}$, and
no exterior slack cell.  At event $k+1$, it reads and verifies every supplied
first-rung fragment coordinate and its declared amplitude.  It then emits,
for each logical output column $q$, the delta record $(q,w_{k+1})$ and one
certificate asserting the universal statement
\begin{equation*}
 y_{q,i}^{[k+1]}\leq g-m
 \qquad\text{for every still-unreported }i>k+1.
\end{equation*}
The certificate is computationally available from the exact scalar right side
of \cref{eq:notched-sun-scale-future-tail} and the verified label order; it is
one record per logical column, not one scan or record per future label.

For $r$ nonnegative logical columns, event $k$ supplies $e_{f_k}$ in each of
the $p=r$ streams.  For a signed logical column, it supplies $2e_{f_k}$ in
its positive stream and $e_{f_k}$ in its negative stream, so $p=2r$ and the
exact logical response is $2y^{[k]}-y^{[k]}=y^{[k]}$.  The reporter checks
both signed-stream coordinates and amplitudes before combining their exact
template certificates; neither stream read is omitted.  Hence in the two
cases respectively
\begin{equation*}
 \mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}=pn=rn
 \qquad\text{and}\qquad
 \mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}=pn=2rn.
\end{equation*}

Generate and retain the complete $J$-rung ladder once, process the $p$ stream
records sequentially, and stream the $r$ logical replies.  In the shared
resource order, the complete epoch vector is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 &(C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},C_{\rm rec},
 C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit})\\
 =\bigl(&\Theta(n),2,n,\Theta(n+J+p),
 \Theta(\mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}+rn+n),0,0,\\
 &\Theta(n+J+p),O(1),0,\Theta(rn)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:notched-sun-scale-delta-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Here preprocessing includes structural validation, label dictionaries,
ladder coefficients, gate/band generation, and stream-mode initialization.
Control includes every fragment read/check, event-order check, scalar tail
comparison, membership update, and construction of all delta and certificate
records.  There is no response application and no numerical response/slack
materialization.  Thus total additive exact-cell work is
$\Theta(n+J+p+\mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}+rn)$, which is output-sensitive
on this trace and strictly replaces the quadratic
\textsc{EagerExactSlack} state.

This GO is exact-real, fixed-face, fixed-cut, fixed-ladder, fixed-order, and
template-aware.  It is not an RPPR load or KKT chronology, a terminal
PPR/RPPR solver, a uniform fixed-$\alpha$ family as $n$ grows, a reporter for
arbitrary fragment amplitudes or orders, or an arbitrary high-cut-rank cyclic
partial flush.  It gives no coefficient-bit, word-RAM, rounding-stability, or
finite-precision guarantee.  In particular, it does not close the general
slack-normalized bank application problem: its future-safe certificate comes
from the proved template scale separation, not from a dynamic response query.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $N_T:=D_T^{-1/2}A_{TT}D_T^{-1/2}$ use the ambient graph degrees, and put
\begin{equation}
 B_0:=\operatorname{diag}(I_A,(1+c_\eta)I_F),
 \qquad
 R:=\operatorname{diag}(I_A,(1+2c_\eta)I_F)+N_T.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-scale-blocks}
\end{equation}
The identities $\alpha=1-2\vartheta$ and
$Q_{TT}=(1-\vartheta)I_T-\vartheta N_T$ give the exact decomposition
\begin{equation}
 M=B_0-\vartheta R
 =B_0^{1/2}(I-\vartheta\mathsf K)B_0^{1/2},
 \qquad
 \mathsf K:=B_0^{-1/2}RB_0^{-1/2}.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-scale-neumann-decomposition}
\end{equation}
Every entry of $\mathsf K$ is nonnegative; no positive-semidefinite order is
claimed.  The full normalized adjacency has operator norm at most one, hence
so does its principal block $N_T$.  Since $B_0^{-1/2}$ is a contraction and
$(1+2c_\eta)/(1+c_\eta)<2$,
\begin{equation*}
 \norm{\mathsf K}_2\leq3.
\end{equation*}
Therefore \cref{eq:notched-sun-scale-regime} permits the entrywise
nonnegative Neumann expansion
\begin{equation}
 M^{-1}
 =B_0^{-1/2}\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}
   \vartheta^t\mathsf K^tB_0^{-1/2}.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-scale-neumann-series}
\end{equation}

The length-one term from $f_k$ to its unique anchor neighbor $u_k$ is
$\vartheta/[\sqrt{d_{u_k}}(1+c_\eta)]$.  All later terms are nonnegative, so
\cref{eq:notched-sun-frozen-response-entry} proves the first weak inequality
in \cref{eq:notched-sun-scale-matching}.  Moreover
$c_\eta<1/50$, $d_{u_k}\leq5$, and
$\sqrt\vartheta\leq1/(36n)\leq1/180<10/51$.  Hence
\begin{equation*}
 \frac1{d_{u_k}(1+c_\eta)}>\sqrt\vartheta,
\end{equation*}
which proves the strict gate inequality.

If $i\neq\ell$, the length-zero and length-one terms between $f_\ell$ and
$u_i$ vanish.  Using only the operator-norm bound on each remaining
entry---not a spectral sign claim---gives
\begin{equation*}
 (M^{-1})_{u_if_\ell}
 \leq\sum_{t=2}^{\infty}(3\vartheta)^t
 =\frac{9\vartheta^2}{1-3\vartheta}.
\end{equation*}
Equation~\eqref{eq:notched-sun-frozen-response-entry} and
$1/\sqrt{d_{u_i}}\leq1$ therefore give
$h_i^{(\ell)}\leq9\vartheta^3/(1-3\vartheta)$.  Sum over
$\ell\leq k$.  Because \cref{eq:notched-sun-scale-regime} implies
$1-3\vartheta\geq1/2$ and
$\sqrt\vartheta\leq1/(36n)$,
\begin{equation*}
 \frac{9k\vartheta^3}{1-3\vartheta}
 \leq18n\sqrt\vartheta\,\vartheta^{5/2}
 \leq\frac12\vartheta^{5/2},
\end{equation*}
proving \cref{eq:notched-sun-scale-future-tail}.  Positivity preserves every
earlier crossing, so the unique delta at event $k$ is $w_k$.

The verified template makes the two scalar inequalities uniform over exactly
the stored future-label range.  Consequently an event counter plus the
checked fragment label/amplitude is sufficient to instantiate both the delta
and the universal future-safe certificate; no exterior response coordinate
is queried.  In the signed case the two checked exact template multiples
combine only after both stream inputs have been processed.  Counting the
declared scans, scalar checks, retained structural cells, and streamed replies
proves \cref{eq:notched-sun-scale-delta-eleven-vector} coordinate by
coordinate.  The final exclusions follow from the reporter's promised
template, event order, scale, and exact-real certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The scale reporter is invariant under verified permutations]
\label{thm:notched-sun-permutation-delta-reporter}
Keep every graph, cut, ladder, scale, gate, band, logical-column, and stream
hypothesis of \cref{thm:notched-sun-scale-delta-reporter}, but remove its
prescribed event order.  At interaction $t$, let all streams declare one
common label $\pi_t\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$ and supply the checked fragment on
$f_{\pi_t}$ with exactly the same nonnegative unit or signed $2$-minus-$1$
amplitudes as in that theorem.  Name the resulting online reporter
\textsc{NotchedSunPermutationDelta}.  It accepts the interaction exactly
when the declared petal belongs to the verified template, every stream uses
that petal and its required amplitude, and
\begin{equation}
 \pi_t\notin\mathsf{Seen}_{t-1},
 \qquad
 \mathsf{Seen}_t:=\mathsf{Seen}_{t-1}\cup\{\pi_t\},
 \qquad
 \mathsf{Seen}_0:=\varnothing.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-permutation-prefix}
\end{equation}
It rejects an invalid interaction before changing its state or emitting a
record.  Thus every accepted length-$n$ trace is a permutation, verified
online rather than promised in preprocessing.

Write
$y_q^{[t]}:=\sum_{s=1}^t h^{(\pi_s)}$ for each exact logical response, after
the verified signed combination when applicable.  For every accepted prefix
and every logical column $q$, the unique new delta at interaction $t$ is
$(q,w_{\pi_t})$, and the reporter emits one universal certificate with the
exact scalar payload
\begin{equation}
 y_{q,i}^{[t]}
 \leq \frac{9t\vartheta^3}{1-3\vartheta}
 \leq g-m
 \qquad
 \text{for every }i\notin\mathsf{Seen}_t.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-permutation-certificate}
\end{equation}
Its persistent state is
\begin{equation}
 \mathsf{PState}_t
 =(
   \text{verified template and label map},\text{fixed ladder},t,
   \mathsf{Seen}_t,\text{logical-column/sign metadata}),
 \label{eq:notched-sun-permutation-state}
\end{equation}
with no response entry, cumulative response coordinate, or exterior slack
cell.  A bit or dictionary entry for each template label implements
$\mathsf{Seen}_t$ and is already linear in the validated template size.

For $r$ nonnegative logical columns, $p=r$ and
$\mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}=rn$.  For the separately checked signed
$2$-minus-$1$ streams, $p=2r$ and
$\mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}=2rn$.  Processing the $p$ records of one
interaction sequentially and streaming the $r$ replies gives the complete
epoch ledger
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 &(C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},C_{\rm rec},
 C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit})\\
 =\bigl(&\Theta(n),2,n,\Theta(n+J+p),
 \Theta(\mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}+rn+n),0,0,\\
 &\Theta(n+J+p),O(1),0,\Theta(rn)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:notched-sun-permutation-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Control charges every fragment-coordinate read, cross-stream label and
amplitude comparison, label-dictionary lookup, unused-bit test and update,
scalar-tail comparison, membership change, and reply construction.  The
extra permutation validation is $\Theta(pn+n)$ and is absorbed by the
displayed fragment/control and persistent-state coordinates.  There is no
response application, future-label scan, or numerical materialization.

This GO relaxes exactly the prescribed common event order of
\cref{thm:notched-sun-scale-delta-reporter}.  The face, exposed cut, label
pairing, complete ladder, fixed $n$, explicit near-one scale, gate and band,
one distinct unit logical fragment per petal, common cross-stream order, and
exact-real model remain fixed.  It does not cover repeated petals, missing
petals, different per-column orders, simultaneous fragment batches,
arbitrary amplitudes or fragments, template mutation, RPPR chronology,
fixed-$\alpha$ uniformity, a terminal solver, finite precision, or a general
reporter lower or upper bound.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The pointwise estimates in
\cref{eq:notched-sun-scale-matching,eq:notched-sun-scale-future-tail} do not
use the numerical order of the petal labels.  At accepted interaction $t$,
entrywise positivity and the matching term give
\begin{equation*}
 y_{q,\pi_t}^{[t]}
 =\sum_{s<t}h_{\pi_t}^{(\pi_s)}+h_{\pi_t}^{(\pi_t)}
 \geq h_{\pi_t}^{(\pi_t)}>g.
\end{equation*}
If $i\notin\mathsf{Seen}_t$, then $i\neq\pi_s$ for every $s\leq t$.
Consequently every summand is off-matching, and the same operator-norm tail
used in \cref{thm:notched-sun-scale-delta-reporter} gives
\begin{equation*}
 y_{q,i}^{[t]}
 =\sum_{s=1}^t h_i^{(\pi_s)}
 \leq\frac{9t\vartheta^3}{1-3\vartheta}
 \leq\frac12\vartheta^{5/2}=g-m.
\end{equation*}
Thus every unreported label was outside the band before the interaction,
only $w_{\pi_t}$ crosses now, and
\cref{eq:notched-sun-permutation-certificate} is available from the prefix
length and verified complement.  At $t=n$ the complement is empty and the
same certificate record is vacuously valid.

In the signed mode, checking both declared fragments first makes their exact
logical combination $2e_{f_{\pi_t}}-e_{f_{\pi_t}}=e_{f_{\pi_t}}$; no stream
read is credited away.  An array or dictionary indexed by the already
validated label map implements \cref{eq:notched-sun-permutation-prefix} with
one lookup and update per interaction.  Adding those operations to the
coordinate-by-coordinate ledger of
\cref{eq:notched-sun-scale-delta-eleven-vector} proves
\cref{eq:notched-sun-permutation-eleven-vector}; none of its asymptotic
coordinates changes.  The rejection rule and final exclusions retain every
other promised-template axis.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Verified bounded repetitions need no response state]
\label{thm:notched-sun-multiplicity-delta-reporter}
Keep every hypothesis of
\cref{thm:notched-sun-permutation-delta-reporter} except the requirement that
each petal occur exactly once and the resulting $n$-event scale bound.  Replace
those two linked clauses by the following repetition contract and its exact
tail scale.  Before the epoch, declare and validate an exact multiplicity
vector
\begin{equation}
 \boldsymbol\mu=(\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_n)\in\mathbb N_{\geq1}^{n},
 \qquad
 L:=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mu_i,
 \qquad
 H:=L-\min_i\mu_i.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-multiplicity-contract}
\end{equation}
Thus $L$ is the authoritative total number of accepted interactions, while
the entries $\mu_i$ are exact final multiplicities and online per-label caps.
In particular, a standalone maximum multiplicity is not used as a surrogate
for the total length.  Impose the exact scalar-envelope scale condition
\begin{equation}
 0<\vartheta
 \leq
 \frac{1}{\bigl(\sqrt{81H^2+3}+9H\bigr)^2},
 \quad\text{equivalently}\quad
 18H\sqrt\vartheta+3\vartheta\leq1.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-multiplicity-scale}
\end{equation}

Name the reporter \textsc{NotchedSunMultiplicityDelta}.  At interaction $t$
all streams must still name one common verified petal $\pi_t$ and carry the
same checked nonnegative-unit or signed $2$-minus-$1$ amplitudes as before.
With
\begin{equation}
 c_i(t):=\#\{s\leq t:\pi_s=i\},
 \qquad
 \mathsf{Seen}_t:=\{i:c_i(t)>0\},
 \label{eq:notched-sun-multiplicity-prefix}
\end{equation}
the reporter accepts only if $t\leq L$ and
$c_{\pi_t}(t-1)<\mu_{\pi_t}$.  It checks the interaction completely before
incrementing the counter or emitting anything.  Hence invalid labels,
cross-stream label or amplitude disagreements, exhausted multiplicities, and
events after $L$ are rejected atomically.  Every accepted length-$L$ trace
automatically satisfies $c_i(L)=\mu_i$ for all $i$, so it is a permutation of
the declared multiset and no petal is missing.

At the first occurrence of $j$, the reporter emits the unique new delta
$(q,w_j)$ for every logical column $q$.  At a repeated occurrence it emits no
delta: $w_j$ was already reported, and no unseen label crosses.  After every
accepted interaction it emits one universal future-safe certificate per
logical column.  If the unseen complement is nonempty, its exact scalar
payload is
\begin{equation}
 y_{q,i}^{[t]}
 \leq\frac{9t\vartheta^3}{1-3\vartheta}
 \leq\frac{9H\vartheta^3}{1-3\vartheta}
 \leq g-m
 \qquad(i\notin\mathsf{Seen}_t).
 \label{eq:notched-sun-multiplicity-certificate}
\end{equation}
If the complement is empty, the certificate instead records that verified
fact and the scalar comparison is unnecessary.  Thus already-reported
matching labels are never incorrectly subjected to the unseen-tail bound;
their later matching repeats may only increase their exact responses.

The persistent state is
\begin{equation}
 \mathsf{MState}_t
 =\left(
  \begin{gathered}
   \text{verified template and label map},\text{fixed ladder},t,\\
   \boldsymbol\mu,(c_i(t))_{i=1}^{n},
   \text{logical-column/sign metadata}
  \end{gathered}
 \right),
 \label{eq:notched-sun-multiplicity-state}
\end{equation}
where $\mathsf{Seen}_t$ is the predicate $c_i(t)>0$.  Replacing one seen bit
by one exact-cell counter per template label does not change the linear state
order.  No response entry, cumulative response coordinate, or slack cell is
stored.

For $r$ nonnegative logical columns, $p=r$ and every one of the $L$
interactions supplies one coordinate in each stream.  For signed logical
columns the positive and negative streams are separately read and checked,
so $p=2r$.  Respectively,
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}=pL=rL
 \qquad\text{or}\qquad
 \mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}=pL=2rL.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-multiplicity-fragments}
\end{equation}
Streaming the $p$ records and $r$ replies gives the complete epoch ledger
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 &(C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},C_{\rm rec},
 C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit})\\
 =\bigl(&\Theta(n),2,L,\Theta(n+J+p),
 \Theta(\mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}+rL+n),0,0,\\
 &\Theta(n+J+p),O(1),0,\Theta(rL)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:notched-sun-multiplicity-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Preprocessing charges validation and summation of all $n$ multiplicities.
Control charges all fragment reads, cross-stream comparisons, total-event and
per-label-cap tests, counter and seen-status updates, scalar comparisons,
first-occurrence tests, and reply construction.  Emission consists of $rL$
certificate records and exactly $rn$ delta records; since $L\geq n$, this is
$\Theta(rL)$.  There is no response application, future-label scan, response
state, slack materialization, or numerical materialization.

This GO relaxes exactly the no-repetition axis.  The frozen face, cut, label
pairing, complete ladder, fixed $n$, unit logical amplitudes, one common
cross-stream schedule, exact declared multiset, explicit multiplicity-
dependent near-one scale, and exact-real model remain promised.  It does not
cover unbounded repetitions, omitted petals in a completed epoch, arbitrary
amplitudes or fragments, batches, different per-column orders, template
mutation, RPPR chronology, fixed-$\alpha$ uniformity as $L$ grows, a terminal
solver, or coefficient-bit, word-RAM, rounding, or finite-precision costs.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The Neumann matching and off-matching estimates in
\cref{thm:notched-sun-scale-delta-reporter} are pointwise and tolerate
repetition.  If $i\notin\mathsf{Seen}_t$, then every one of the first $t$
events is off-matching for $i$.  Moreover the online caps imply
\begin{equation*}
 t=\sum_{j\ne i}c_j(t)
 \leq\sum_{j\ne i}\mu_j
 =L-\mu_i\leq H.
\end{equation*}
Summing the pointwise bound
$h_i^{(j)}\leq9\vartheta^3/(1-3\vartheta)$ therefore gives the first two
inequalities in
\cref{eq:notched-sun-multiplicity-certificate}.  Writing
$x=\sqrt\vartheta$, the last one is exactly
\begin{equation*}
 \frac{9H\vartheta^3}{1-3\vartheta}
 \leq\frac12\vartheta^{5/2}
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 3x^2+18Hx\leq1.
\end{equation*}
The positive root is
$x=(\sqrt{81H^2+3}+9H)^{-1}$, which proves that
\cref{eq:notched-sun-multiplicity-scale} is the unrelaxed, and hence sharp,
condition for this uniform scalar Neumann envelope.  This sharpness statement
is about the displayed envelope, not a lower bound on every possible
reporter.

Because $n\geq5$ and every $\mu_i\geq1$, $H\geq4$.  The scale condition gives
$\sqrt\vartheta<1/(18H)\leq1/72<10/51$.  Together with $d_{u_i}\leq5$ and
$c_\eta<1/50$, the same length-one Neumann term as in
\cref{eq:notched-sun-scale-matching} is strictly greater than
$g=\vartheta^{5/2}$.  Immediately before the first occurrence of $j$, its
response contains only off-matching terms and is at most $g-m$; adding the
positive matching term puts it strictly above $g$.  Every other unseen label
continues to satisfy
\cref{eq:notched-sun-multiplicity-certificate}, so precisely $w_j$ is new.
On a repeat, the matching label is already in $\mathsf{Seen}$ and all labels
outside $\mathsf{Seen}$ still satisfy the same certificate, so no delta is
new.  Once the complement is empty, the certificate is vacuous regardless of
the remaining repeats.

The signed streams again combine only after both exact fragments pass their
common-label, amplitude, and multiplicity checks.  The count vector both
implements seen membership and makes exhaustion atomic.  Finally, summing
the declared template scan, ladder and multiplicity preprocessing, $pL$
fragment reads, $rL$ certificates, $rn$ first-occurrence deltas, and
linear-size persistent cells proves
\cref{eq:notched-sun-multiplicity-eleven-vector} coordinate by coordinate.
The state and scope claims follow directly from the named interface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Declared positive amplitudes have a mass-capacity reporter]
\label{thm:notched-sun-amplitude-delta-reporter}
Keep the frozen face, exposed cut, label pairing, complete ladder, fixed
$n\geq5$, gate $g=\vartheta^{5/2}$, exact-real model, and common
cross-stream label order of
\cref{thm:notched-sun-multiplicity-delta-reporter}.  Replace only its unit
logical amplitudes, and consequently its unit-specific half-gate band and
event-count scale, by the following declared common amplitude contract.
For every label $i$, declare one occurrence-indexed schedule
\begin{equation}
 \mu_i\in\mathbb N_{\geq1},
 \qquad
 a_{i,1},\ldots,a_{i,\mu_i}\in\mathbb R_{>0},
 \qquad
 A_{i,k}:=\sum_{s=1}^{k}a_{i,s},
 \qquad A_{i,0}:=0.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-schedule}
\end{equation}
The same schedule is used by every logical column.  Put
\begin{equation}
 L:=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mu_i,
 \qquad A_i:=A_{i,\mu_i},
 \qquad \mathcal A:=\sum_{i=1}^{n}A_i.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-total-mass}
\end{equation}
Thus $L$ is the total event count, $A_i$ is the final logical mass on label
$i$, and $\mathcal A$ is the total positive logical amplitude mass.  These
are distinct from the exact-cell fragment count below.

Assume $0<\vartheta<1/3$ and define the exact scalar matching coefficient,
Neumann remainder envelope, and response-mass capacity
\begin{equation}
 \lambda_i:=\frac{\vartheta^2}{d_{u_i}(1+c_\eta)},
 \qquad
 \epsilon_\vartheta:=\frac{9\vartheta^3}{1-3\vartheta},
 \qquad
 \kappa_i:=\frac{g}{\lambda_i}
 =d_{u_i}(1+c_\eta)\sqrt\vartheta.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-capacity}
\end{equation}
The first \emph{certified} occurrence index is
\begin{equation}
 k_i^*:=\min\{k\in\{1,\ldots,\mu_i\}:A_{i,k}>\kappa_i\}.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-crossing-index}
\end{equation}
Require this set to be nonempty for every $i$.  Do not identify $k_i^*$ with
one: small first amplitudes are legal.  Define its positive pre-crossing mass
gap, and the maximum event count and logical mass that can have arrived while
label $i$ is still unreported, by
\begin{align}
 \gamma_i
 &:=\kappa_i-A_{i,k_i^*-1}>0,
 \label{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-capacity-gap}\\
 H_i
 &:=L-\mu_i+k_i^*-1,
 &
 F_i
 &:=\mathcal A-A_i+A_{i,k_i^*-1}.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-future-capacities}
\end{align}
Finally impose the exact schedule-dependent scale/certificate test
\begin{equation}
 m_{\mathcal A}
 :=\min_{1\leq i\leq n}
   \{\lambda_i\gamma_i-\epsilon_\vartheta F_i\}>0.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-margin}
\end{equation}
It automatically gives $0<m_{\mathcal A}<g$, so use
$[g-m_{\mathcal A},g]$ as the declared transition band.  For every fixed
finite positive schedule, all of
\cref{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-crossing-index,eq:notched-sun-amplitude-margin}
hold for all sufficiently small positive $\vartheta$: then $k_i^*=1$,
$\lambda_i\gamma_i=g$, and
$\epsilon_\vartheta F_i/g\to0$.  The theorem also covers validated scales at
which some $k_i^*>1$.

Name the resulting reporter \textsc{NotchedSunAmplitudeDelta}.  At
interaction $t$, all streams first name one common verified label $\pi_t$.
If $c_i(t-1)$ is the number of earlier accepted occurrences of $i$, the
required logical amplitude is
\begin{equation}
 a_t:=a_{\pi_t,c_{\pi_t}(t-1)+1}.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-event}
\end{equation}
The reporter atomically checks the common label, the exact required stream
amplitudes, the per-label cap $c_{\pi_t}(t-1)<\mu_{\pi_t}$, and the total
cap $t\leq L$ before changing state.  Let
\begin{equation}
 S_i(t):=A_{i,c_i(t)},
 \qquad
 \mathcal A(t):=\sum_{i=1}^{n}S_i(t)=\sum_{s=1}^{t}a_s,
 \qquad
 \mathsf{Reported}_t:=\{i:c_i(t)\geq k_i^*\}.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-prefix-state}
\end{equation}
Every accepted length-$L$ trace has $c_i(L)=\mu_i$ for every $i$, because
the counts are capped coordinatewise and their sum is $L=\sum_i\mu_i$.
Unseen labels have $c_i(t)=0$; seen but unreported labels have
$0<c_i(t)<k_i^*$; later occurrences of a reported label have
$c_i(t)>k_i^*$.  These three cases are not conflated.

For each logical column $q$, let the exact combined logical response be
\begin{equation*}
 y_q^{[t]}:=\sum_{s=1}^{t}a_sh^{(\pi_s)}.
\end{equation*}
If interaction $t$ changes $c_j$ from $k_j^*-1$ to $k_j^*$, the reporter
emits the single new delta $(q,w_j)$.  At every other occurrence it emits no
delta.  After every accepted interaction it also emits one universal
certificate per logical column asserting
\begin{equation}
 y_{q,i}^{[t]}\leq g-m_{\mathcal A}
 \qquad(i\notin\mathsf{Reported}_t).
 \label{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-certificate}
\end{equation}
The certificate remains valid simultaneously for unseen and seen-but-
unreported labels and already accounts for the largest possible future mass
from every other label.  If every label has been reported, it records the
verified empty complement.  Consequently the event at which count $k_i^*$
arrives is both the first certified crossing and the first exact strict
crossing of $g$ by label $i$, for every accepted interleaving of the declared
occurrence schedules.

The reporter retains one common copy of the declared schedules or their
prefix arrays.  Before the epoch it reads every one of the $L$ exact
amplitude cells, verifies positivity and the multiplicities, constructs all
prefix sums, and rejects unless the capacities, crossing indices, horizons,
and margin above pass.  Its persistent state is
\begin{equation}
 \mathsf{AState}_t=\left(
 \begin{gathered}
  \text{verified template and label map},\text{fixed ladder},t,\mathcal A(t),\\
  (\mu_i),(A_{i,k}),(k_i^*),(c_i(t)),
  \text{logical-column/sign metadata}
 \end{gathered}
 \right).
 \label{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-reporter-state}
\end{equation}
Reported membership is the count comparison in
\cref{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-prefix-state}.  No response coordinate,
cumulative response coordinate, exterior slack, or future-label scan is
stored or performed.

For $r$ nonnegative logical columns, event $t$ supplies
$a_te_{f_{\pi_t}}$ in each of the $p=r$ streams.  For the existing signed
$2$-minus-$1$ interface, each logical column supplies
$2a_te_{f_{\pi_t}}$ and $a_te_{f_{\pi_t}}$ in separately checked positive
and negative streams, so $p=2r$ and their exact logical difference is again
$a_te_{f_{\pi_t}}$.  Hence the exact-cell counts and total absolute supplied
coefficient masses are respectively
\begin{align}
 \mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}
 &=pL=rL,
 &
 \mathcal A_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}&=r\mathcal A,
 &&\text{nonnegative},
 \notag\\
 \mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}
 &=pL=2rL,
 &
 \mathcal A_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}&=3r\mathcal A,
 &&\text{signed $2$-minus-$1$}.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-fragments}
\end{align}
No cancellation is credited before both signed fragments are read and
checked.

Preprocess the $L$ amplitude cells and their prefixes once, process the $p$
stream records sequentially, and stream the $r$ replies.  The complete epoch
vector is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 &(C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},C_{\rm rec},
 C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit})\\
 =\bigl(&\Theta(n),2,L,\Theta(n+J+p+L),
 \Theta(\mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}+rL+L+n),0,0,\\
 &\Theta(n+J+p+L),O(1),0,\Theta(rL)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The $\Theta(rL)$ emission contains one certificate per column and event plus
exactly $rn$ delta records.  Control charges every fragment read, exact
amplitude and cross-stream comparison, count/capacity update, first-crossing
test, membership change, and reply construction.  There is no factor
application, numerical recurrence, response-state write, or numerical
materialization.

This GO relaxes the unit-logical-amplitude clause to a finite declared
positive common occurrence schedule and recalibrates only the scale-derived
band needed by that relaxation.  It keeps the frozen face, cut, ladder,
pairing, fixed $n$, common cross-stream label interleaving, exact
multiplicities, complete label coverage, and exact-real arithmetic.  It does
not cover undeclared or unbounded amplitude mass, arbitrary signed logical
amplitudes beyond the checked $2$-minus-$1$ encoding, different per-column
schedules, batches, arbitrary fragment support, template mutation, RPPR
chronology, fixed-$\alpha$ uniformity, a terminal solver, or coefficient-bit,
word-RAM, rounding, or finite-precision costs.  Failure of
\cref{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-margin} only rejects this scalar-envelope
certificate; it is not a lower bound requiring a response query.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The entrywise nonnegative Neumann series in
\cref{eq:notched-sun-scale-neumann-series} is valid when
$0<\vartheta<1/3$.  For a matching pair $(u_i,f_i)$, its length-one term and
the norm bound on all later terms give
\begin{equation}
 \lambda_i\leq h_i^{(i)}\leq\lambda_i+\epsilon_\vartheta.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-pointwise-matching}
\end{equation}
For $j\ne i$, the length-zero and length-one terms vanish, so
\begin{equation}
 0<h_i^{(j)}\leq\epsilon_\vartheta.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-pointwise-off}
\end{equation}
Linearity and positivity therefore imply, at every accepted prefix,
\begin{equation}
 \lambda_iS_i(t)
 \leq y_{q,i}^{[t]}
 \leq \lambda_iS_i(t)+\epsilon_\vartheta\mathcal A(t).
 \label{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-prefix-envelope}
\end{equation}

If $i\notin\mathsf{Reported}_t$, then $c_i(t)\leq k_i^*-1$.  Positivity of
the schedule and the other per-label caps give, without any assumption on
the interleaving,
\begin{equation*}
 S_i(t)\leq A_{i,k_i^*-1}=\kappa_i-\gamma_i,
 \qquad
 t\leq H_i,
 \qquad
 \mathcal A(t)\leq F_i.
\end{equation*}
Substitute these bounds into
\cref{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-prefix-envelope}:
\begin{equation*}
 y_{q,i}^{[t]}
 \leq\lambda_i(\kappa_i-\gamma_i)
      +\epsilon_\vartheta F_i
 =g-(\lambda_i\gamma_i-\epsilon_\vartheta F_i)
 \leq g-m_{\mathcal A}.
\end{equation*}
This proves the universal certificate for unseen and seen-but-unreported
labels, including all mass that may already have arrived from future events
in other label schedules.

When the $k_i^*$th occurrence of $i$ is accepted,
$S_i(t)=A_{i,k_i^*}>\kappa_i$.  The lower half of
\cref{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-prefix-envelope} gives
\begin{equation*}
 y_{q,i}^{[t]}>\lambda_i\kappa_i=g.
\end{equation*}
Immediately before that event, the same bound puts the label at most
$g-m_{\mathcal A}$; for a first interaction this is simply the initialized
zero response.  Thus no small earlier occurrence or
off-matching future mass caused an unreported crossing, and precisely this
event is its first exact and certified crossing.  Other unreported labels
continue to satisfy the certificate.  Once a label is reported, positivity
may increase it further but creates no second delta.

All quantities on the right side of the certificate are computed by one
preprocessing pass over the declared schedules.  Online operation needs only
the current label's count, next amplitude, and the global prefix mass; the
proof, not an online scan, quantifies over the complement.  The signed
streams combine to the same positive logical increment only after their
separate exact checks.  Summing the structural scan, ladder, schedule cells,
fragment records, constant-cell count updates, certificates, and deltas
proves \cref{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-eleven-vector} coordinate by
coordinate.  The state and exclusions follow from the named interface.
\end{proof}
