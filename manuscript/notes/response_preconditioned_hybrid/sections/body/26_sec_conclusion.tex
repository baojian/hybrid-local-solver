\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The common object is the restricted Green operator, and preconditioning is the
mathematical bridge between the project's two solver families.  The mixed
architecture is presently the most robust research plan because it can use
exact response where structure is exposed and iterative repair where exact
transport is too expensive.  It is not proved universally best.  The decisive
next theorem is now specifically an online scheduling theorem for the proved
aggregate shifted-debt flush, or a dynamic sparse-recovery partial-flush
interface with the same charge.  Literal exact coordinate cleanup is excluded
by the one-edge obstruction, but
\cref{thm:aggregate-chebyshev-debt-flush} removes its $1/\alpha$ factor:
all fixed-face fragments are summed first, signed acceleration is confined to
the interior, and a product-scale energy certificate protects the exterior
gate.  This is not an online partial-flush or output-sensitive reporting
theorem; the all-boundary realization pays every listed response and output
coordinate.
The two-sided sketch reduces every packed hierarchy-node test to this
interface, avoiding both additive all-leaf diagonal maintenance and a dense
exterior response.  The coded bucket theorem goes further: a single bank
shared by the whole query has output-sensitive dimension and candidate count,
even at full cut rank.  Exact validation permits one such target bank per
doubling capacity to be reused safely across the adaptive epoch, so fresh
target builds are no longer the obstruction.  It is not yet a fully charged
heavy-change reporter on general cyclic cores, because constructing the
multiscale banks once and applying them after every light event may
still dominate.  Bidirectional harmonic application sharpens this further:
an online rent-or-buy rule closes the number of anchor right-hand sides up to
a factor two, but repeated anchor-cut scans or stored-response reads can still
dominate.  Slack normalization now extends the same packing to a complete
checkpoint segment with heterogeneous far-label margins: if total admitted
batch size is $b$, orthogonal correction energy is $\mathcal E$, and the
explicit transition band has width $m$, then both target dimension and
far-crossing degree volume are
$\widetilde O(1+b\mathcal E/m^2)$.  The realized gate needs only one causal
directional measurement column; logarithmic Gaussian width is needed only
for direction-independent row energies.  Both fixed-bank states accumulate
exactly without retaining source history.  The remaining implementation problem is
therefore narrower.  Fixed-weight append is free of source replay, but the
minimal three-label collision in
\cref{prop:three-label-norm-only-reweight-obstruction} refutes a free
norm-only slack refresh.  The next route must apply or refresh the bank within
the product ledger by paying for richer state, old response/source replay, or
a geometric rebuild, together with capacity changes, transition-band state,
validation, and rejection.  Full exterior adjacency is not needed: the response object is fixed by
the exposed face, its cut incidences, and boundary degrees, so geometric
static rebuilding is already chargeable to final exposed volume.  More
precisely, the Chebyshev checkpoint theorem constructs every boundary
diagonal loss in $\widetilde O(V/\sqrt\alpha)$ work, and the geometric ledger
absorbs all such snapshots.  The open cost is now epoch-internal incremental
update and locator query work.  The diagonal losses
telescope per label, combine with correction energy through a square-root
wake-up budget, and need only scale-matched one-sided accuracy; neither search
combinatorics, source conditioning, coordinate leverage, nor oscillating keys
remain an obstacle.  In particular, Chebyshev whitening accepts a spectral
frontier approximation and replaces every exact $K_F^{-1/2}$ source solve by
an additive $\widetilde O(T_{\rm mv}/\sqrt\alpha)$ charge.  Independently, the
positive resolvent theorem replaces the inverse square root by logarithmically
many shifted Stieltjes solves with total work mass $O(1/\sqrt\alpha)$.  It
proves an accelerated monotone Schur-coordinate settlement bound and realizes
each rung as a sparse face system whose unfinished part is exact nonnegative
anchor debt.  The aggregate-flush theorem then realizes one scalar stream of
outstanding debt on a fixed face in
$\widetilde O(\operatorname{cvol}(T)/\sqrt\alpha+\mathcal C_{\rm frag})$
exact-cell work; multiple columns add and signed columns use two nonnegative
streams.  One all-boundary flush per geometrically growing fixed-decision face
has the corresponding fully charged final-volume ledger.  At a fixed anchor,
transposed harmonic measurements also remove dense old-face materialization
and boundary scans.  At arbitrary cut rank, the coded bank proves that the
number of target measurements is only the packed ambiguity budget up to
logarithms.  If the normalized anchor--boundary cut has rank $s$, one
bank of $s$ harmonic basis vectors plus additive low-dimensional group
summaries gives a complete source-only packed reporter.  A linear-volume comb
has full cut rank and rank-deficient energy error bounded below independently
of the comb length for fixed $\alpha<1$, so no universal low-rank bank can
close the general case.  On the frozen notched-double-sun face, even a
perfectly separated one-new-label debt trace has dense exact response columns;
the named \textsc{EagerExactSlack} vector consequently pays quadratic state
writes.  This stops only eager separately addressed exact slack maintenance,
not an implicit or packed representation.
\Cref{thm:notched-sun-scale-delta-reporter} gives the precise fixed-template
counterpoint: after one structural verification, its scalar Neumann-tail
certificate returns every delta and all-future-safe reply in linear total
work, without a factor application or slack materialization.  It does not
handle a changing template or fragment amplitude.
\Cref{thm:notched-sun-permutation-delta-reporter} removes only the prescribed
common event order: an online seen-label check handles every permutation
without changing the resource vector.
\Cref{thm:notched-sun-multiplicity-delta-reporter} further handles every
declared bounded common multiset permutation with one exact counter per label
and the sharp scale for its uniform template-tail envelope.  It still excludes
unbounded or undeclared repetitions.
\Cref{thm:notched-sun-amplitude-delta-reporter} then replaces unit events by
one declared positive occurrence schedule common to all columns.  Its
mass-capacity rule handles labels seen several times before their first exact
crossing, with a schedule-derived transition band, full future-mass charge,
and no response or slack state.
\Cref{thm:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-delta-reporter} further assigns an
independent declared schedule and margin to each logical column and permits
arbitrary one-event-at-a-time interleaving.  Its local mass horizon excludes
unrelated column mass while its global event horizon charges the resulting
latency; the signed physical pair stays atomic.
\Cref{thm:notched-sun-batched-columnwise-amplitude-delta-reporter} then groups
arbitrary tagged next consecutive occurrences into finite simultaneous
batches.  It reports every exact batch-boundary crossing, emits one remaining-
label certificate per affected column, charges one interaction per batch and
all physical fragments, and stages the current batch before an atomic commit.
It does not impose or recover an internal subevent order.  The fixed-template
line still excludes undeclared or unbounded mass, arbitrary signed logical
amplitudes, arbitrary fragment support, and cross-column coupled response.  The
remaining quantitative
obligation is therefore online completeness: show that the proved
slack-normalized packed queries can be installed and applied with
output-sensitive aggregate harmonic work on high-rank nonequitable cores,
including bank-capacity and slack-weight changes, transition-band maintenance,
and exact validation.  The fixed-weight norm-only state cannot supply a free
refresh by \cref{prop:three-label-norm-only-reweight-obstruction}; a positive
route must pay richer prechange state, replay, or rebuilding.  Alternatively,
prove a different safe response trace
that avoids the literal path schedule.
Numerical
frontier-error accumulation is also closed by
the orthogonal lift theorem.  Response-free continuation over safely expanding
subspaces without repeated restarts remains a separate iterative-track
question.
