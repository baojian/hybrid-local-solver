\begin{theorem}[Independent positive column schedules have an asynchronous
reporter]
\label{thm:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-delta-reporter}
Keep the frozen face, exposed cut, label pairing, complete ladder, fixed
$n\geq5$, gate $g=\vartheta^{5/2}$, exact-real model, positive logical
amplitudes, and the nonnegative or signed $2$-minus-$1$ stream interface of
\cref{thm:notched-sun-amplitude-delta-reporter}.  Relax exactly its common
logical-column schedule.  There are $r\geq1$ logical columns.  For every
column $q\in\{1,\ldots,r\}$ and label $i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$, independently
declare
\begin{equation}
 \begin{gathered}
  \mu_{qi}\in\mathbb N_{\geq1},
  \qquad
  a_{qi1},\ldots,a_{qi\mu_{qi}}\in\mathbb R_{>0},\\
  A_{qi,k}:=\sum_{s=1}^{k}a_{qis},
  \qquad A_{qi,0}:=0,
  \qquad
  A_{qi}:=A_{qi,\mu_{qi}}.
 \end{gathered}
 \label{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-schedule}
\end{equation}
Put
\begin{equation}
 L_q:=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mu_{qi},
 \qquad
 \mathcal A_q:=\sum_{i=1}^{n}A_{qi},
 \qquad
 L_\Sigma:=\sum_{q=1}^{r}L_q,
 \qquad
 \mathcal A_\Sigma:=\sum_{q=1}^{r}\mathcal A_q.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-totals}
\end{equation}
Thus $L_\Sigma$ counts externally accepted logical events and
$\mathcal A_\Sigma$ is their total positive logical coefficient mass.  They
are not the fragment-cell and absolute physical masses charged below.

For $0<\vartheta<1/3$, retain the structural matching coefficient and
Neumann remainder envelope
\begin{equation}
 \lambda_i:=\frac{\vartheta^2}{d_{u_i}(1+c_\eta)},
 \qquad
 \epsilon_\vartheta:=\frac{9\vartheta^3}{1-3\vartheta},
 \qquad
 \kappa_{qi}:=\frac{g}{\lambda_i}
 =d_{u_i}(1+c_\eta)\sqrt\vartheta.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-capacity}
\end{equation}
The notation $\kappa_{qi}$ records which declared column--label cell is
tested; its numerical value is column-independent because the frozen response
operator is shared.  Define
\begin{equation}
 k_{qi}^*:=
 \min\{k\in\{1,\ldots,\mu_{qi}\}:A_{qi,k}>\kappa_{qi}\},
 \label{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-crossing-index}
\end{equation}
and require every displayed set to be nonempty.  The exact pre-crossing gap,
local event horizon, global asynchronous event horizon, and response-relevant
mass horizon are
\begin{align}
 \gamma_{qi}
 &:=\kappa_{qi}-A_{qi,k_{qi}^*-1}>0,
 \label{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-gap}\\
 H_{qi}^{\rm loc}
 &:=L_q-\mu_{qi}+k_{qi}^*-1,
 &
 H_{qi}^{\rm async}
 &:=L_\Sigma-\mu_{qi}+k_{qi}^*-1
 =H_{qi}^{\rm loc}+L_\Sigma-L_q,
 \label{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-event-horizons}\\
 F_{qi}
 &:=\mathcal A_q-A_{qi}+A_{qi,k_{qi}^*-1}.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-mass-horizon}
\end{align}
The distinction is essential: events in other columns can consume the extra
$L_\Sigma-L_q$ global interactions, but they contribute no mass to column
$q$'s response.  Give every column its own margin
\begin{equation}
 m_q:=\min_{1\leq i\leq n}
 \{\lambda_i\gamma_{qi}-\epsilon_\vartheta F_{qi}\},
 \qquad
 m_\Sigma:=\min_{1\leq q\leq r}m_q>0.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-margin}
\end{equation}
Then $0<m_q<g$ and column $q$ uses its own transition band $[g-m_q,g]$.
For every fixed finite family of positive schedules, all crossing sets and
margins pass simultaneously at every sufficiently small positive
$\vartheta$: then every $k_{qi}^*=1$,
$\lambda_i\gamma_{qi}=g$, and
$\epsilon_\vartheta F_{qi}/g\to0$.

Name the reporter \textsc{NotchedSunColumnAmplitudeDelta}.  The external
stream is any asynchronous interleaving of the declared column events.  At
global interaction $t\in\{1,\ldots,L_\Sigma\}$, one logical-event header
first names a column $q_t$ and a label $\pi_t$.  With $c_{qi}(t-1)$ the
earlier accepted occurrence count in that cell, its required logical amplitude is
\begin{equation}
 a_t:=a_{q_t\pi_t,\,c_{q_t\pi_t}(t-1)+1}.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-event}
\end{equation}
In the nonnegative interface, the event contains exactly the one fragment
$a_te_{f_{\pi_t}}$ on physical stream $q_t$.  In the signed interface, it
contains exactly the paired positive fragments
$2a_te_{f_{\pi_t}}$ and $a_te_{f_{\pi_t}}$ on the positive and negative
physical streams belonging to that same column.  The reporter atomically
checks the column, physical stream identity, paired labels and amplitudes,
cell cap $c_{q_t\pi_t}(t-1)<\mu_{q_t\pi_t}$, column cap, and global cap before
changing state.  No event requires a label or occurrence to agree with any
other column, and one event is not a batch.

For every prefix define
\begin{equation}
 \begin{gathered}
  \ell_q(t):=\sum_{i=1}^{n}c_{qi}(t),
  \qquad
  S_{qi}(t):=A_{qi,c_{qi}(t)},
  \qquad
  \mathcal A_q(t):=\sum_{i=1}^{n}S_{qi}(t),\\
  \mathsf{Reported}_{q,t}:=
  \{i:c_{qi}(t)\geq k_{qi}^*\},
  \qquad
  \sum_q\ell_q(t)=t.
 \end{gathered}
 \label{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-prefix-state}
\end{equation}
Every accepted length-$L_\Sigma$ trace exhausts every declared cell: all
counts are coordinatewise capped and their sum equals the sum of those caps.
The exact logical response in column $q$ is
\begin{equation}
 y_q^{[t]}:=
 \sum_{\substack{1\leq s\leq t\\q_s=q}}a_sh^{(\pi_s)}.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-response}
\end{equation}
When the event changes $c_{qi}$ from $k_{qi}^*-1$ to $k_{qi}^*$, the reporter
emits exactly the new delta $(q,w_i)$.  Every other occurrence emits no delta.
After an event in column $q_t$, it emits one updated universal certificate
\begin{equation}
 y_{q_t,i}^{[t]}\leq g-m_{q_t}
 \qquad(i\notin\mathsf{Reported}_{q_t,t}).
 \label{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-certificate}
\end{equation}
Certificates for $q\ne q_t$ remain valid without re-emission because neither
$y_q$ nor its counts changed.  Hence occurrence $k_{qi}^*$ is the first exact
strict crossing of $g$ in cell $(q,i)$ for every global interleaving.  The
certificate includes unseen and seen-but-unreported labels; an empty
complement is recorded as such.

Before the epoch, the reporter reads all $L_\Sigma$ amplitude cells, validates
positivity and complete label coverage, constructs every prefix, and rejects
unless all capacities, crossing indices, horizons, and margins pass.  Its
persistent state is
\begin{equation}
 \mathsf{CAState}_t=\left(
 \begin{gathered}
  \text{verified template and label map},\text{fixed ladder},t,\\
  (L_q),(\mathcal A_q(t)),(m_q),(\mu_{qi}),(A_{qi,k}),
  (k_{qi}^*),(c_{qi}(t)),\\
  \text{logical-column and physical-stream metadata}
 \end{gathered}
 \right).
 \label{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-reporter-state}
\end{equation}
All $rn$ counter, crossing, and margin-heading cells and all schedule prefixes
are charged.  Since every $\mu_{qi}\geq1$, $L_\Sigma\geq rn$.  There is no
response coordinate, cumulative response coordinate, exterior slack cell, or
future-label scan.

There are $p=r$ persistent physical stream identities in the nonnegative
interface and $p=2r$ in the signed interface, but an asynchronous event reads
only its affected column.  Consequently
\begin{align}
 \mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}
 &=L_\Sigma,
 &
 \mathcal A_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}
 &=\mathcal A_\Sigma,
 &&\text{nonnegative},
 \notag\\
 \mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}
 &=2L_\Sigma,
 &
 \mathcal A_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}
 &=3\mathcal A_\Sigma,
 &&\text{signed $2$-minus-$1$}.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-fragments}
\end{align}
No cancellation is credited until both signed physical records pass their
atomic pairing check.  The complete epoch vector is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 &(C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},C_{\rm rec},
 C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit})\\
 =\bigl(&\Theta(n),2,L_\Sigma,\Theta(n+J+p+L_\Sigma),\\
 &\Theta(\mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}+L_\Sigma+rn+n),0,0,\\
 &\Theta(n+J+p+L_\Sigma),O(1),0,
 \Theta(L_\Sigma+rn)\bigr)\\
 =\bigl(&\Theta(n),2,L_\Sigma,\Theta(n+J+p+L_\Sigma),
 \Theta(\mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}+L_\Sigma),0,0,\\
 &\Theta(n+J+p+L_\Sigma),O(1),0,\Theta(L_\Sigma)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The first line deliberately exposes the $rn$ declared cell headings, all
$L_\Sigma$ event validations, one certificate per event, and exactly $rn$
delta records before using $L_\Sigma\geq rn$.  Control also charges column and
stream identifiers, every fragment read, signed-pair comparison, amplitude
comparison, cap/count update, membership test, and reply construction.
Preprocessing and persistent memory charge the template, ladder, $p$ stream
identities, $rn$ schedule headers, all $L_\Sigma$ amplitude/prefix cells, and
per-column margins.  There is no factor application, numerical recurrence,
response-state write, or numerical materialization.

This GO relaxes only the common logical-column occurrence schedule and its
lockstep all-column event interface.  It preserves the frozen graph, face,
cut, ladder, label pairing, fixed $n$, complete label coverage in every
column, declared finite positive mass, exact-real arithmetic, and the atomic
signed pairing within each column.  It does not cover undeclared or unbounded
mass, arbitrary signed logical amplitudes, a signed pair split across global
interactions, simultaneous batches, arbitrary fragment support, incomplete
labels, template or ladder mutation, RPPR chronology, fixed-$\alpha$
uniformity, a terminal solver, coefficient-bit complexity, word-RAM costs,
rounding, or finite precision.  Failure of any margin in
\cref{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-margin} rejects only this declared
scalar-envelope reporter; it is not a lower bound on response queries or
other reporters.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fix one column $q$.  By
\cref{eq:notched-sun-amplitude-pointwise-matching,eq:notched-sun-amplitude-pointwise-off},
linearity and positivity give, for
every global prefix,
\begin{equation}
 \lambda_iS_{qi}(t)
 \leq y_{q,i}^{[t]}
 \leq\lambda_iS_{qi}(t)+\epsilon_\vartheta\mathcal A_q(t).
 \label{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-prefix-envelope}
\end{equation}
Only events with $q_s=q$ occur in this sum.  Thus asynchronous events in the
other $r-1$ columns affect global time but neither side of this envelope.

If $i\notin\mathsf{Reported}_{q,t}$, then
$c_{qi}(t)\leq k_{qi}^*-1$.  Positivity and the cell caps imply
\begin{equation*}
 S_{qi}(t)\leq A_{qi,k_{qi}^*-1}=\kappa_{qi}-\gamma_{qi},
 \qquad
 \ell_q(t)\leq H_{qi}^{\rm loc},
 \qquad
 t\leq H_{qi}^{\rm async},
 \qquad
 \mathcal A_q(t)\leq F_{qi}.
\end{equation*}
The event bounds are tight when all other permitted events are exhausted;
the response bound uses the local mass $F_{qi}$ rather than the generally
larger and irrelevant $\mathcal A_\Sigma$.  Substitution in
\cref{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-prefix-envelope} gives
\begin{equation*}
 y_{q,i}^{[t]}
 \leq\lambda_i(\kappa_{qi}-\gamma_{qi})
      +\epsilon_\vartheta F_{qi}
 \leq g-m_q.
\end{equation*}
This proves the per-column certificate at every asynchronous prefix.

When occurrence $k_{qi}^*$ arrives,
$S_{qi}(t)=A_{qi,k_{qi}^*}>\kappa_{qi}$.  The lower envelope yields
$y_{q,i}^{[t]}>\lambda_i\kappa_{qi}=g$, while the preceding prefix was at
most $g-m_q$.  Every other unreported label in column $q$ retains its
certificate.  Every other column is exactly unchanged, so its last emitted
certificate remains valid.  Positivity prevents a second delta after a cell
is reported.  This proves exact first crossing and validates emitting only
the affected column's certificate.

The count caps and authoritative total $L_\Sigma=\sum_{q,i}\mu_{qi}$ prove
completion.  The paired signed fragments have exact logical difference
$a_te_{f_{\pi_t}}$ only after both positive records are validated.  Summing
the structural scan, schedule and prefix cells, physical records, atomic
checks, counters, certificates, and deltas gives
\cref{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-eleven-vector}; serial processing
uses constant temporary cells.  The stated state and scope follow from the
named interface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Declared simultaneous batches preserve the column-local
reporter]
\label{thm:notched-sun-batched-columnwise-amplitude-delta-reporter}
Keep every hypothesis, schedule, capacity, and positive margin of
\cref{thm:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-delta-reporter}, but replace its
one-event interactions by atomic simultaneous batches.  A batch
$\mathcal B_h$, $h\in\{1,\ldots,B\}$, is a nonempty unordered collection of
tagged logical records $(q,i,k,a_{qik})$.  If $c_{qi}^{h-1}$ is the accepted
count before the batch, then, for some integer $b_{hqi}\geq0$, the records of
cell $(q,i)$ must have exactly the tagged amplitude records
\begin{equation}
 \left\{(k,a_{qik}):
 k=c_{qi}^{h-1}+1,\ldots,c_{qi}^{h-1}+b_{hqi}\right\}.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-batch-contract}
\end{equation}
The set in \cref{eq:notched-sun-batch-contract} is empty when $b_{hqi}=0$.
Thus a batch may contain several consecutive occurrences of one cell and may
mix arbitrary labels and columns, but it may not duplicate, replay, skip, or
invent an occurrence.  Put
\begin{equation}
 \begin{gathered}
  b_h:=\sum_{q,i}b_{hqi},
  \qquad
  s_h:=\left|\left\{q:\sum_i b_{hqi}>0\right\}\right|,
  \qquad
  b_{\max}:=\max_h b_h,\\
  S_\Sigma:=\sum_{h=1}^{B}s_h,
  \qquad
  \sum_{h=1}^{B}b_h=L_\Sigma,
  \qquad
  c_{qi}^{h}:=c_{qi}^{h-1}+b_{hqi}.
 \end{gathered}
 \label{eq:notched-sun-batch-counts}
\end{equation}
Every batch is nonempty, so $B\leq S_\Sigma\leq L_\Sigma$ and
$b_{\max}\leq L_\Sigma$.

Name the reporter \textsc{NotchedSunBatchAmplitudeDelta}.  Before changing
any persistent state, it stages the whole current batch and atomically checks
its nonempty header, every column and label, every occurrence tag and exact
declared amplitude, the gap-free blocks in
\cref{eq:notched-sun-batch-contract}, every cell/column/global cap, and every
physical stream identity.  In the nonnegative interface, logical record
$(q,i,k,a_{qik})$ contains exactly $a_{qik}e_{f_i}$ on stream $q$.  In the
signed interface it contains exactly the same-column, same-label, same-tag
pair $2a_{qik}e_{f_i}$ and $a_{qik}e_{f_i}$ on the positive and negative
streams.  A missing, split, mismatched, or cross-column pair rejects the whole
batch without changing a count or emitting a certificate.  No cancellation
is credited during validation.

At batch boundary $h$, define
\begin{equation}
 \begin{gathered}
  S_{qi}^{h}:=A_{qi,c_{qi}^{h}},
  \qquad
  \mathcal A_q^{h}:=\sum_i S_{qi}^{h},
  \qquad
  \mathsf{Reported}_{q,h}:=\{i:c_{qi}^{h}\geq k_{qi}^*\},\\
  y_q^{[h]}:=\sum_{i=1}^{n}S_{qi}^{h}h^{(i)},
  \qquad
  \mathsf{New}_{q,h}:=
  \{i:c_{qi}^{h-1}<k_{qi}^*\leq c_{qi}^{h}\}.
 \end{gathered}
 \label{eq:notched-sun-batch-boundary-state}
\end{equation}
After a successful commit, it emits exactly the delta records
$(q,w_i)$ for $i\in\mathsf{New}_{q,h}$.  For each of the $s_h$ affected
columns, and only those columns, it emits one universal certificate
\begin{equation}
 y_{q,i}^{[h]}\leq g-m_q
 \qquad(i\notin\mathsf{Reported}_{q,h}).
 \label{eq:notched-sun-batch-certificate}
\end{equation}
An unaffected column retains its preceding response, counts, and certificate
verbatim.  For every cell $(q,i)$, the unique batch whose endpoints satisfy
$c_{qi}^{h-1}<k_{qi}^*\leq c_{qi}^{h}$ is its first exact strict
\emph{batch-boundary} crossing of $g$.  No within-batch exact-crossing order
is defined or emitted, and no separate certificate semantics are assigned to
the simultaneous records inside a batch.

The reporter keeps the schedule state from
\cref{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-reporter-state}, replaces the event
counter by a batch counter, and stages at most one batch:
\begin{equation}
 \mathsf{BAState}_h=\left(
 \begin{gathered}
  \text{verified template, label map, ladder, schedules, and stream metadata},\\
  h,(\mathcal A_q^h),(m_q),(k_{qi}^*),(c_{qi}^{h}),
  \text{one atomic batch buffer}
 \end{gathered}
 \right).
 \label{eq:notched-sun-batch-reporter-state}
\end{equation}
The persistent part has the same asymptotic size as before; the batch buffer
uses $O(b_{\max})$ exact cells, including the physical-pair staging by a
constant factor.  Batch contents and history are not retained after commit.

The logical event and physical fragment totals do not change under batching:
\begin{align}
 \mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}
 &=L_\Sigma,
 &
 \mathcal A_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}
 &=\mathcal A_\Sigma,
 &&\text{nonnegative},
 \notag\\
 \mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}
 &=2L_\Sigma,
 &
 \mathcal A_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}
 &=3\mathcal A_\Sigma,
 &&\text{signed $2$-minus-$1$}.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-batch-fragments}
\end{align}
Here $p=r$ or $p=2r$, respectively.  There are exactly $S_\Sigma$
universal certificate records and exactly $rn$ delta records.  The complete
accepted-epoch vector is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 &(C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},C_{\rm rec},
 C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit})\\
 =\bigl(&\Theta(n),2,B,\Theta(n+J+p+L_\Sigma),\\
 &\Theta(\mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}+L_\Sigma+B+S_\Sigma+rn+n),0,0,\\
 &\Theta(n+J+p+L_\Sigma),O(b_{\max}),0,
 \Theta(S_\Sigma+rn)\bigr)\\
 =\bigl(&\Theta(n),2,B,\Theta(n+J+p+L_\Sigma),
 \Theta(\mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}+L_\Sigma),0,0,\\
 &\Theta(n+J+p+L_\Sigma),O(b_{\max}),0,
 \Theta(S_\Sigma+rn)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:notched-sun-batch-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The unsimplified line exposes every batch header, logical occurrence and
physical fragment read, atomic validation, affected-column reply, and delta
construction.  The simplification uses
$B,S_\Sigma,rn,n\leq L_\Sigma$ and
$\mathcal C_{\rm frag}^{\Sigma}\geq L_\Sigma$.  An invalid attempted batch of
$\widehat b$ supplied logical records is charged one interaction, its staged
header/records and validation work, $O(1+\widehat b)$ temporary cells, and one
rejection record; it makes no persistent write.  Such attempts add to, rather
than disappear inside, the accepted-epoch vector.

This GO removes only the one-logical-event interaction restriction.  It keeps
the frozen graph, face, cut, ladder, pairing, fixed $n$, complete declared
positive schedules, per-column scalar margins, exact column separation,
exact-real arithmetic, and atomic within-record signed pairing.  It does not
cover a hidden chronology inside a simultaneous batch, undeclared or
unbounded mass, arbitrary signed logical amplitudes, a signed pair split
between records or batches, cross-column coupled response state, arbitrary
fragment support, incomplete labels, template or ladder mutation, RPPR
chronology, fixed-$\alpha$ uniformity, a terminal solver, coefficient-bit or
word-RAM complexity, rounding, or finite precision.  A failed margin still
rejects only this scalar-envelope reporter.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fix a batch boundary $h$ and column $q$.  The batch response in
\cref{eq:notched-sun-batch-boundary-state} is the sum of the same positive
logical fragments as any serialization of the accepted records.  Addition is
commutative, and the response column is linear, so
\cref{eq:notched-sun-columnwise-amplitude-prefix-envelope} applies at the
boundary without choosing an internal order:
\begin{equation}
 \lambda_iS_{qi}^{h}
 \leq y_{q,i}^{[h]}
 \leq\lambda_iS_{qi}^{h}+\epsilon_\vartheta\mathcal A_q^{h}.
 \label{eq:notched-sun-batch-prefix-envelope}
\end{equation}
No record from another column occurs in this sum.

If $i\notin\mathsf{Reported}_{q,h}$, then
$c_{qi}^{h}\leq k_{qi}^*-1$.  The same coordinatewise caps and positivity as
in the one-event proof give
\begin{equation*}
 S_{qi}^{h}\leq A_{qi,k_{qi}^*-1}
 =\kappa_{qi}-\gamma_{qi},
 \qquad
 \mathcal A_q^{h}\leq F_{qi}.
\end{equation*}
Substitution in \cref{eq:notched-sun-batch-prefix-envelope} proves
\cref{eq:notched-sun-batch-certificate}.  Thus simultaneous mass on other
labels in the same column is already paid by $F_{qi}$, and mass in other
columns is absent because it changes a different response column.

If $i\in\mathsf{New}_{q,h}$, then before the batch
$c_{qi}^{h-1}\leq k_{qi}^*-1$, so the preceding certificate puts its exact
response at most $g-m_q$.  After the batch,
$S_{qi}^{h}\geq A_{qi,k_{qi}^*}>\kappa_{qi}$, and the lower envelope gives
$y_{q,i}^{[h]}>\lambda_i\kappa_{qi}=g$.  Conversely, every cell not in
$\mathsf{New}_{q,h}$ is either still certified below the band or was already
reported.  Hence the displayed delta set is exact, even when one batch jumps
over several occurrence indices or crosses several cells at once.  Positivity
prevents a second crossing.

The gap-free occurrence-tag check makes the post-batch counts independent of
record order and proves completion after $L_\Sigma$ logical records.  The
two signed physical fragments give the same positive logical increment only
after their same-record pairing check.  Staging before commit proves atomic
rejection and the $O(b_{\max})$ temporary-space charge.  Summing template and
schedule preprocessing, $B$ batch headers, all logical and physical records,
affected-column certificates, and the unique cell deltas proves
\cref{eq:notched-sun-batch-eleven-vector} coordinate by coordinate.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Cross-direction handoff.}
The reusable distinction is between global event latency and the state on
which a certificate depends.  Exact column separation lets unrelated events
increase $H_{qi}^{\rm async}$ without entering $F_{qi}$ or forcing a
recertification.  Atomic batching further shows that a positive linear state
needs only endpoint counts and total local mass; this says nothing about a
hidden within-batch chronology or about coupled state blocks.  An incremental
lower-state or reset-bank argument in another
direction may use the same accounting only after proving the analogous block
separation; a shared phase transition, response row, or reset reserve must
still pay for every component it actually changes.  In particular, an
event-local certificate or reset payment does not imply neighboring-stage
monotonicity or a generic scalar potential.  This observation adds no formal
dependency edge.

\begin{proposition}[Canonical singleton paths obstruct literal geometric
sleep]
\label{prop:singleton-path-geometric-sleep-obstruction}
Fix $\alpha\in(0,1)$ and $\gamma>0$.  For every sufficiently long unweighted
path $P_n=(v_0,\ldots,v_{n-1})$ with single seed $s=e_{v_0}$, form $D,Q,b$
using the \emph{ambient} degrees in $P_n$:
$d_{v_0}=d_{v_{n-1}}=1$ and $d_{v_i}=2$ for $0<i<n-1$.
There is a family-dependent $\rho_n>0$ for which the canonical
all-violations RPPR gate, initialized at $S_0=\{v_0\}$, has the forced trace
\begin{equation}
 S_k=\{v_0,\ldots,v_k\},
 \qquad
 B_k=\{v_{k+1}\},
 \qquad 0\leq k<n-1.
 \label{eq:singleton-path-gate-trace}
\end{equation}
Here, at the exact positive restricted point $x[S]$, the canonical batch is
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal B(S)
 :=\{v\in\partial S:
 c_v-Q_{vS}x[S]_S>0\},
 \qquad c:=b-\alpha\rho_nD^{1/2}\one,
 \label{eq:singleton-path-canonical-gate}
\end{equation}
so equality is inactive and every strict violation is admitted in the same
batch.  The proof chooses $\rho_n$ only after $n$ is fixed; no positive lower
bound uniform in $n$ is asserted.

For $1\leq k\leq n-3$,
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\operatorname{cvol}(S_{k+1})}
      {\operatorname{cvol}(S_k)}
 =1+\frac{3}{2+3k}.
 \label{eq:singleton-path-volume-ratio}
\end{equation}
Hence all but $O(1/\gamma)$ of the required gate certifications occur without
$\gamma$-multiplicative face growth.  A controller whose only certified
\textsc{BoundaryBounds} calls occur on a geometrically growing face sequence
cannot reproduce the literal gate on this family.

This does not give a work lower bound.  To make that exclusion fully charged,
use the non-prefetching append-only tridiagonal $LDL^T$ response written out
in the proof below.  It is the same response independently audited by the
stopping-time path materialization theorem in the companion
local-solver-oracle-hierarchy note (stable label
\texttt{thm:online-path-}\allowbreak\texttt{resource-separation}), now applied
to the append-only load $c_{S_k}$: old load entries retain their ambient
degrees and only the new coordinate is appended.  Request one
\textsc{BoundaryBounds} reply for each of the $n-1$ singleton batches and
co-return the empty-boundary terminal certificate and final vector at the
$n$th external stage.  The named exact-real execution has
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 &(C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},C_{\rm rec},
 C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit})\\
 &\qquad=(\Theta(n),n,n,0,\Theta(n),0,\Theta(n),
           \Theta(n),O(1),0,\Theta(n)).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:singleton-path-sleep-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
This is an upper-bound realization, not a lower bound on its adjacency or
interaction rounds.  Terminal coordinates are final emissions, so they do
not enter intermediate $C_{\rm mat}$.  The proposition proves only that
\cref{cor:geometric-aggregate-debt-flush} cannot close online completeness by
itself.  A general theorem still needs an output-sensitive partial reporter
between checkpoints or a separately proved support-safe trace that bypasses
the literal canonical batches.  No finite-precision, word-RAM, bit-complexity,
or PPR-conversion claim is made.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
First set $\rho=0$.  For every prefix $S_k$, with all entries of $Q$ still
formed from the ambient $P_n$ degrees, the principal matrix
$Q_{S_kS_k}$ is an irreducible SPD Stieltjes matrix.  Its inverse is strictly
positive, while the restricted load $b_{S_k}$ is positive at $v_0$ and zero
elsewhere.  Therefore
\begin{equation*}
 x^{(k)}(0):=Q_{S_kS_k}^{-1}b_{S_k}>0.
\end{equation*}
The only boundary vertex is $v_{k+1}$, and its exact demand is
\begin{equation*}
 h_k(0)
 :=b_{v_{k+1}}-Q_{v_{k+1},S_k}x^{(k)}(0)
 =-Q_{v_{k+1},v_k}x^{(k)}_{v_k}(0)>0.
\end{equation*}
The active coordinates and the finitely many strict demands
\begin{equation*}
 h_k(\rho)
 :=c_{v_{k+1}}-Q_{v_{k+1},S_k}
 Q_{S_kS_k}^{-1}c_{S_k}
\end{equation*}
depend continuously (indeed affinely) on $\rho$.  Choose $\rho_n>0$ after
fixing $n$ so that every restricted prefix remains positive and every
$h_k(\rho_n)>0$.  This finite continuity choice supplies no $n$-uniform
lower bound.  Since the path prefix has exactly one boundary vertex,
canonical all-violations batching gives
\cref{eq:singleton-path-gate-trace}; the full restricted point is positive as
well.

The endpoint $v_0$ contributes $2$ to charged volume, and each
$v_i$, $1\leq i\leq k<n-1$, has full-graph degree two and contributes $3$.
Thus $\operatorname{cvol}(S_k)=2+3k$, proving
\cref{eq:singleton-path-volume-ratio}.  Once
$3/(2+3k)<\gamma$, the next required certification precedes
$\gamma$-multiplicative growth.  There are $n-O(1/\gamma)$ such indices.

Finally, write the tridiagonal factor of $Q_{S_kS_k}$ as
$L_k\Delta_kL_k^T$.  Starting with
$\Delta_0=Q_{v_0v_0}$ and $u_0=c_{v_0}$, admission of $v_{i+1}$ appends only
\begin{equation*}
 \ell_i:=\frac{Q_{v_{i+1},v_i}}{\Delta_i},
 \qquad
 \Delta_{i+1}:=Q_{v_{i+1},v_{i+1}}
   -\frac{Q_{v_{i+1},v_i}^2}{\Delta_i},
 \qquad
 u_{i+1}:=c_{v_{i+1}}-\ell_i u_i.
\end{equation*}
Put $w_i:=u_i/\Delta_i$.  At prefix $S_k$, back substitution starts with
$x[S_k]_{v_k}=w_k$, so the unique next demand is computed in constant work as
$c_{v_{k+1}}-Q_{v_{k+1},v_k}w_k$.  At termination, the one reverse response
application
\begin{equation*}
 x_{v_{n-1}}=w_{n-1},
 \qquad
 x_{v_i}=w_i-\ell_i x_{v_{i+1}}
 \quad(i=n-2,\ldots,0)
\end{equation*}
streams the terminal vector.  Thus appending the new ambient-degree row and
load entry costs constant response and control work.  Counting the $n$ row
exposures, $n$ external stages, retained records, scalar replies, and final
coordinates gives \cref{eq:singleton-path-sleep-eleven-vector}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Packed node tests with charged source whitening]
\label{cor:packed-harmonic-node-tests}
Suppose $\widetilde K_F$ satisfies
\cref{eq:frontier-spectral-approximation}, one multiplication by it costs
$T_{\rm mv}$ work, and one scalar measurement in
\cref{eq:chebyshev-whitened-group-estimator}, including construction or
maintenance of its target harmonic row, costs $T_{\rm harm}$ work.  Under
the hypotheses of \cref{lem:source-aware-hierarchical-locator}, all
potentially ambiguous leaves at one event are found with high probability in
\begin{equation}
 \widetilde O\!\left(
 \frac{T_{\rm mv}}{\sqrt\alpha}
 +
 T_{\rm harm}\,bL
 \left(1+|F|\frac{E^2}{m^2}\right)
 \right)
 \label{eq:packed-harmonic-query-work}
\end{equation}
work, plus exact reached-leaf validation and output.  No dense exterior
response or all-leaf squared range-add is used.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Fix absolute constants $\xi,\zeta,\upsilon<1$.  The estimator
\cref{eq:chebyshev-whitened-one-sided} gives
\cref{eq:source-aware-group-oracle} with the absolute constant
$\gamma=\Gamma_{\xi,\zeta,\upsilon}$.  Apply
\cref{lem:source-aware-hierarchical-locator}; the
$O(\log^2(M/\delta))$ scalar measurements per reached group are absorbed by
$\widetilde O$.  The source vectors are prepared once and reused by every
reached group, so \cref{eq:frontier-whitening-work} is additive rather than
multiplied by the query count.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Low-cut-rank harmonic bank]
\label{thm:low-cut-rank-harmonic-bank}
Fix an anchor $A$, put $U:=V\setminus A$, and define its normalized cut
matrix
\begin{equation}
 \mathsf C_A:=Q_{AU}D_U^{-1/2}.
 \label{eq:normalized-anchor-cut}
\end{equation}
Suppose an exact rank-$s$ factorization
\begin{equation}
 \mathsf C_A=BC,
 \qquad
 B\in\mathbb R^{A\times s},
 \quad
 C\in\mathbb R^{s\times U}
 \label{eq:cut-rank-factorization}
\end{equation}
is available, and precompute the harmonic basis
\begin{equation}
 Y:=Q_{AA}^{-1}B.
 \label{eq:cut-rank-harmonic-basis}
\end{equation}
Then every target vector $a\in\mathbb R^U$ has harmonic extension
\begin{equation}
 Q_{AA}^{-1}Q_{AU}D_U^{-1/2}a=Y(Ca).
 \label{eq:cut-rank-target-extension}
\end{equation}
For every frontier $F\subseteq U$, $y\in\mathbb R^F$, and
$w:=Q_{AF}y$, its scalar transposed harmonic response is therefore
\begin{equation}
 a^TD_U^{-1/2}Q_{UA}Q_{AA}^{-1}w
 =(Ca)^TY^Tw.
 \label{eq:cut-rank-scalar-response}
\end{equation}

The low-rank factorization in fact removes target-side random measurements
entirely.  For $y\in\mathbb R^F$, define on $U$
\begin{equation*}
 b(y):=D_U^{-1/2}Q_{UF}y,
 \qquad
 t(y):=Y^TQ_{AF}y.
\end{equation*}
The normalized dual signature of the frontier lift is
\begin{equation}
 D_U^{-1/2}q_U=b(y)-C^Tt(y).
 \label{eq:cut-rank-dual-decomposition}
\end{equation}
For every hierarchy node $\mathcal C\subseteq U\setminus F$, store the
anchor-only Gram summary
\begin{equation}
 G_{\mathcal C}:=C_{:,\mathcal C}C_{:,\mathcal C}^T
 \label{eq:cut-rank-group-gram}
\end{equation}
and, for the current $y$, form the direct summaries
\begin{equation}
 r_{\mathcal C}(y):=C_{:,\mathcal C}b_{\mathcal C}(y),
 \qquad
 \nu_{\mathcal C}(y):=\|b_{\mathcal C}(y)\|_2^2.
 \label{eq:cut-rank-direct-summaries}
\end{equation}
Then the complete node response norm is the exact scalar
\begin{equation}
 \left\|D_{\mathcal C}^{-1/2}q_{\mathcal C}\right\|_2^2
 =\nu_{\mathcal C}(y)
  -2t(y)^Tr_{\mathcal C}(y)
  +t(y)^TG_{\mathcal C}t(y).
 \label{eq:cut-rank-exact-group-norm}
\end{equation}
All three group summaries are additive over hierarchy children.
$G_{\mathcal C}$ is built once from the cut columns.  The vector $b(y)$ is
formed by scanning frontier incidences; propagating each touched leaf to its
ancestors constructs every nonzero $r_{\mathcal C}(y)$ and
$\nu_{\mathcal C}(y)$.  If the hierarchy depth is $L$, this costs
$O(sL)$ per touched exterior label after the frontier scan.  Forming $t(y)$
reads $s$ entries of $Y$ per touched anchor row, and evaluating
\cref{eq:cut-rank-exact-group-norm} at a requested node costs $O(s^2)$.

Consequently, after the $s$ anchor solves and
$O(s|A|+s^2M)$ stored scalars for an $M$-node hierarchy, all fixed-frontier
group norms for one vector $y$ are obtained without a target harmonic solve,
a target Gaussian probe, or a scan of an untouched hierarchy subtree.
Polylogarithmic cut rank therefore closes the target side of
\cref{cor:packed-harmonic-node-tests}, conditional only on the declared
factorization, anchor-solve, and touched-incidence charges.

This rank dependence is exact.  The full harmonic bank
\begin{equation*}
 \mathsf H_A:=Q_{AA}^{-1}\mathsf C_A
\end{equation*}
has $\operatorname{rank}(\mathsf H_A)=
\operatorname{rank}(\mathsf C_A)$.  Moreover, for every integer $k\geq0$,
\begin{equation}
 \min_{\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde{\mathsf H})\leq k}
 \left\|Q_{AA}^{1/2}
   (\mathsf H_A-\widetilde{\mathsf H})\right\|_2
 =\sigma_{k+1}\!\left(Q_{AA}^{-1/2}\mathsf C_A\right).
 \label{eq:harmonic-bank-numerical-rank}
\end{equation}
An energy-operator error at most $\eta$ consequently guarantees, for all
$a$ and $w$,
\begin{equation}
 \left|a^T(\mathsf H_A-\widetilde{\mathsf H})^Tw\right|
 \leq\eta\|a\|_2\|w\|_{Q_{AA}^{-1}}.
 \label{eq:harmonic-bank-bilinear-error}
\end{equation}
The squared singular values have the general packing bound
\begin{equation}
 \sum_i\sigma_i^2\!\left(Q_{AA}^{-1/2}\mathsf C_A\right)
 =\sum_{v\in U}
   \frac{Q_{vA}Q_{AA}^{-1}Q_{Av}}{d_v}
 \leq |A|.
 \label{eq:harmonic-bank-singular-packing}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\Cref{eq:cut-rank-target-extension,eq:cut-rank-scalar-response} follow by
substituting \cref{eq:cut-rank-factorization} and using symmetry.  The same
substitution in the exterior block
$q_U=Q_{UF}y-Q_{UA}Q_{AA}^{-1}Q_{AF}y$ proves
\cref{eq:cut-rank-dual-decomposition}.  Expanding the squared norm of
$b_{\mathcal C}(y)-C_{:,\mathcal C}^Tt(y)$ gives
\cref{eq:cut-rank-exact-group-norm}.  Each displayed summary is a sum of
leaf contributions, proving additivity.  Scanning the nonzeros of $b(y)$ and
the touched rows of $Q_{AF}y$, then propagating leaf contributions along
their ancestor paths, gives the work and storage statements.

Invertibility of $Q_{AA}$ preserves rank.  The energy-scaled approximation
problem is ordinary best rank-$k$ approximation of
$Q_{AA}^{1/2}\mathsf H_A=Q_{AA}^{-1/2}\mathsf C_A$; the
Eckart--Young theorem proves
\cref{eq:harmonic-bank-numerical-rank}.  Cauchy--Schwarz between
$Q_{AA}^{1/2}(\mathsf H_A-\widetilde{\mathsf H})a$ and
$Q_{AA}^{-1/2}w$ proves \cref{eq:harmonic-bank-bilinear-error}.  Finally,
expanding the Frobenius norm gives the equality in
\cref{eq:harmonic-bank-singular-packing}.  Apply
\cref{eq:source-aware-leverage-sum} with source set equal to the whole face
$A$, then use $d_v\geq1$, to obtain the inequality.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Low-cut-rank packed group reporter]
\label{cor:low-cut-rank-packed-reporter}
Assume the factorization and harmonic bank of
\cref{thm:low-cut-rank-harmonic-bank}, and let the boundary hierarchy have
depth $L$ and at most $M$ nodes.  Use the Chebyshev-whitened source vectors
$y_t=p_q(\widetilde K_F)g_t$ from
\cref{thm:chebyshev-frontier-whitening}, but replace the target-side Gaussian
estimator by
\begin{equation}
 \overline Z_{F,q}^{\rm lr}(\mathcal C)
 :=\frac1r\sum_{t=1}^r
 \left\|D_{\mathcal C}^{-1/2}q_{\mathcal C}(y_t)\right\|_2^2,
 \label{eq:low-rank-source-only-estimator}
\end{equation}
where every summand is evaluated exactly by
\cref{eq:cut-rank-exact-group-norm}.  For
$r=O(\xi^{-2}\log(2M/\delta))$, with probability at least $1-\delta$,
simultaneously for every node,
\begin{equation}
 (1-\xi)a_-Z_F(\mathcal C)
 \leq\overline Z_{F,q}^{\rm lr}(\mathcal C)
 \leq(1+\xi)a_+Z_F(\mathcal C),
 \label{eq:low-rank-source-only-guarantee}
\end{equation}
with $a_-,a_+$ from
\cref{eq:frontier-whitening-sandwich}.  Rescaling the estimator therefore
gives the one-sided group oracle required by
\cref{lem:source-aware-hierarchical-locator}.

Moreover, the factorization makes one exact frontier multiplication local.
Indeed,
\begin{equation}
 Q_{AA}^{-1}Q_{AF}y
 =Y\,C_{:,F}D_F^{1/2}y,
 \label{eq:low-rank-anchor-application}
\end{equation}
so \cref{eq:exact-frontier-matvec} costs
$O(s\operatorname{cvol}(F))$ row reads and arithmetic.  After the separately
charged factorization, $s$ anchor solves, and hierarchy-Gram construction,
one packed ambiguous-row search consequently costs
\begin{equation}
 \widetilde O\!\left(
  \frac{s\operatorname{cvol}(F)}{\sqrt\alpha}
  +s^2bL\left(1+|F|\frac{E^2}{m^2}\right)
 \right),
 \label{eq:low-rank-packed-reporter-work}
\end{equation}
plus exact reached-leaf validation and output.  Thus a polylogarithmic-rank
cut gives a complete charged group reporter at the accelerated frontier
scale; no target harmonic query remains.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
For fixed $\mathcal C$, the vector
$D_{\mathcal C}^{-1/2}q_{\mathcal C}(y_t)$ is the matrix
$R_{\mathcal C}p_q(\widetilde K_F)$ applied to a standard Gaussian source.
The first Gaussian quadratic-form argument in
\cref{lem:two-sided-harmonic-group-sketch}, followed by a union bound over
the $M$ nodes, compares
\cref{eq:low-rank-source-only-estimator} with
$Z_{F,q}(\mathcal C)$.  Combine this with
\cref{eq:frontier-whitening-sandwich} to prove
\cref{eq:low-rank-source-only-guarantee}.

The cut factorization gives
$Q_{AF}=BC_{:,F}D_F^{1/2}$, proving
\cref{eq:low-rank-anchor-application}.  The products incident on $F$ scan
its rows and read only the corresponding rows of $Y$.  Hence
$T_{\rm mv}=O(s\operatorname{cvol}(F))$ in
\cref{eq:frontier-whitening-work}.  For the logarithmic number of final
source probes, construct $b(y_t)$ from the same frontier incidences, propagate
\cref{eq:cut-rank-direct-summaries} along at most $L$ ancestors, and form
$t(y_t)$ from touched rows of $Y$; these costs are absorbed by the first term
up to logarithms.  Each queried group then costs $O(s^2)$ by
\cref{eq:cut-rank-exact-group-norm}.  Substitute the query count from
\cref{eq:source-aware-group-query-count} to obtain
\cref{eq:low-rank-packed-reporter-work}.
\end{proof}
