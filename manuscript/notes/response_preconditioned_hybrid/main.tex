\begin{abstract}
The project currently has two credible routes to faster local PageRank.  Local
first-order and Krylov methods seek work
$\widetilde O(V/\sqrt\alpha)$ on a certified active envelope of charged volume
$V$.  Growing-active-set SDD and directed-response methods can remove every
polynomial dependence on $1/\alpha$ on paths and several structured graph
classes, and could reach $\widetilde O(V)$ if their evolving boundary response
were maintainable at output-sensitive cost.  Neither endpoint is known on
arbitrary graphs.

This note formulates their common operator and a response-preconditioned
composition.  A settled anchor stores an implicit approximation to its
restricted inverse, newly admitted vertices form a frontier buffer, and
iterative repair acts on the preconditioned current face.  The proved
backend-independent facts include the exact bordered Schur correction, an
energy-to-coordinate boundary interval with graph-universal leverage at most
$\sqrt{(1-\alpha)/2}$, a source-aware leverage-packing theorem, a
Schur-diagonal-loss identity with a per-label temporal telescope, a
margin-adaptive hierarchical-locator reduction, a two-ledger square-root
wake-up bound, a grounded-Laplacian reduction to dynamic electrical-flow
location, exposed-incidence sufficiency, a transposed fixed-anchor harmonic
sketch identity, a fixed-event coded bucket bank with output-sensitive target
dimension, a certified first-failure coupling that reuses one target bank per
doubling capacity throughout an adaptive epoch, a slack-normalized segment
packing theorem with target dimension and far-crossing degree volume
$\widetilde O(1+b\mathcal E/m^2)$, a one-column causal bank for the realized
gate, logarithmic Gaussian source compression for direction-independent row
energies, and append-only exact decoder-norm accumulation for a fixed bank,
a minimal three-label obstruction showing that a genuine checkpoint-slack
reweight cannot be refreshed from those norms alone without replay, richer
prechange state, or rejection,
a matching three-label pivot-Gram state that uses one additional scalar and
supports arbitrary repeated positive reweights, together with a sharp
four-label obstruction to that one-pivot state and a general co-side Gram
state whose exact linear-statistic dimension is quadratic in bucket occupancy,
a Chebyshev all-boundary diagonal-loss checkpoint costing
$\widetilde O(V/\sqrt\alpha)$, approximation-stable Chebyshev whitening of
the frontier Schur complement, a positive shifted-resolvent ladder with
$O(1/\sqrt\alpha)$ monotone settlement mass, an exact delayed-anchor debt
realization, a matched one-edge obstruction showing that literal sparse
coordinate realization can restore an $\Omega(1/\alpha)$ repeated-work
factor, and an exact-real fixed-face aggregate Chebyshev flush with scalar,
multiple-column, signed-split, fragment, and exhaustive resource ledgers.
The flush removes the one-edge factor in
$\widetilde O(\operatorname{cvol}(T)/\sqrt\alpha)$ scalar recurrence work,
while a separate charged all-boundary pass materializes the certified
one-sided exterior intervals.  On one frozen notched-double-sun face, a
separated exact debt trace has one delta label per light event but a dense
response increment; this forces quadratic response-state writes only for the
named \textsc{EagerExactSlack} representation and does not lower-bound an
implicit or packed partial reporter.  In fact, on the same fixed trace and an
explicit sufficiently near-one parameter range, the named
\textsc{NotchedSunScaleDelta} reporter validates the graph and every incoming
stream but stores no response or slack cell: an entrywise Neumann-tail
certificate emits the unique crossing and one all-future-safe record per
logical column in linear total work.  A seen-label dictionary further makes
the certificate invariant under every online-verified common permutation of
those unit events, with the same eleven-coordinate ledger.  Declared positive
multiplicities may also replace that permutation by any online-verified common
multiset permutation: a per-label counter distinguishes first occurrences
from repeats, and the exact total-event horizon gives a sharp scalar
future-tail scale without storing or applying a response.  More generally, a
declared positive occurrence-amplitude schedule assigns each label a scalar
response capacity.  A schedule-dependent mass horizon and positive capacity
gap certify unseen and already-seen labels until their first accumulated-mass
crossing, which need not be their first occurrence; this reporter remains
linear in declared fragments and output and stores no response or slack
coordinate.  Independent schedules may in turn be assigned to the logical
columns and interleaved asynchronously.  Per-column mass horizons remain
local even though global interaction horizons grow, so an affected-column
certificate still gives a linear complete ledger with no response or slack
state.  Finally, any finite collection of next consecutive positive
occurrences may be committed as one simultaneous atomic batch.  At batch
boundaries the same column-local envelope identifies every newly crossing
cell exactly; the ledger separates batch interactions, physical fragments,
affected-column certificates, delta output, and peak batch workspace.  No
within-batch chronology is inferred.  The note also
gives a low-cut-rank target harmonic bank
with a matched full-rank comb
obstruction, and a nonequitable cyclic structural stress test for which, for
every fixed size $n$ and $\alpha$ in an $n$-dependent interval
$(\alpha_n,1)$, a prescribed geometric epoch contains $\Theta(n)$ linearly
independent exact squared-loss updates, together with
geometric rebuild
amortization.  Two
complementary response
ledgers sharpen the reporting target.  The first is a monotone-variation ledger:
inactive normalized boundary demands move in only one direction and have
total degree-weighted variation at most $(1-\alpha)/2$.  The second is an
energy-packing ledger: the squared exterior demand change is
at most the exact primal shock.  Under an exhaustively charged response and
boundary interface, these yield a conditional work
theorem of the form
\[
 \widetilde O\bigl(
 U_{\rm resp}(V)+V\sqrt{\kappa_{\rm eff}}+V
 \bigr).
\]
The identity preconditioner recovers the iterative product target; a
near-linear response interface with bounded effective condition approaches the
output scale.  The theorem is not a graph-uniform construction.  The missing
ingredient is now online completeness.  A single-seed path refutes literal
sleep until multiplicative face growth, so the exact gate needs
output-sensitive partial flushes or a separately justified alternative safe
trace while locating finite-band crossings on high-rank nonequitable cores.
Certified response-preconditioned frontier errors
already accumulate without numerical restart; response-free or
approximate-response expanding-subspace continuation is a separate open
problem.
\end{abstract}
