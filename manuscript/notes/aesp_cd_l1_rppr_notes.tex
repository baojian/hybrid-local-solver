\section{Scope and motivation}

This note records the design of an Accelerated Evolving Set Process with local proximal coordinate descent (AESP-CD) for the $\ell_1$-regularized PageRank problem. The note is independent from the main manuscript and separates proved statements, implementation choices, and open questions.

The motivation is the difficulty of extending locality-preserving acceleration from ordinary PageRank to the composite problem. Classical momentum methods may create transient high-degree activations. Therefore acceleration should be applied through an evolving-set/Catalyst framework, while the inner solver must maintain a local KKT certificate.

\section{Problem formulation}

Let $G=(V,E)$ be an undirected simple graph without isolated vertices. Let $A$ be the adjacency matrix and $D$ the degree matrix. We solve
\begin{equation}
\min_x \Psi_\rho(x)=\frac12x^\top Qx-\alpha x^\top D^{-1/2}s+\alpha\rho\|D^{1/2}x\|_1,
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}
Q=\frac{1+\alpha}{2}I-\frac{1-\alpha}{2}D^{-1/2}AD^{-1/2},
\qquad s\geq0,\quad \|s\|_1=1.
\end{equation}

The regularization parameter is $\rho$ and the returned solution must satisfy the certified condition
\begin{equation}
\frac{R_\rho(x)}{\alpha}\leq \texttt{eps\_tol}.
\end{equation}

Here $R_\rho(x)$ is the degree-normalized minimum KKT violation defined below.

\section{KKT residual}

Let $f(x)=\frac12x^\top Qx-\alpha x^\top D^{-1/2}s$. Define
\begin{equation}
\kappa_{\rho,i}(x)=
\begin{cases}
\nabla_i f(x)+\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_i},&x_i>0,\\
\nabla_i f(x)-\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_i},&x_i<0,\\
\nabla_i f(x)-\operatorname{clip}(\nabla_i f(x),-\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_i},\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_i}),&x_i=0.
\end{cases}
\end{equation}

The raw KKT residual is
\begin{equation}
R_\rho(x)=\|D^{-1/2}\kappa_\rho(x)\|_\infty.
\end{equation}
The quantity $R_\rho(x)$ is a diagnostic; the certified solution error is bounded by $R_\rho(x)/\alpha$.

\section{Catalyst/AESP outer framework}

At outer iteration $t$, solve the proximal subproblem
\begin{equation}
\Psi_{\rho,t}(z)=\Psi_\rho(z)+\frac{\eta}{2}\|z-y^{(t-1)}\|_2^2.
\end{equation}
The standard AESP choice is
\begin{equation}
\eta=1-2\alpha,
\qquad
\beta=\frac{\sqrt{1-\alpha}-\sqrt\alpha}{\sqrt{1-\alpha}+\sqrt\alpha},
\end{equation}
for $\alpha<1/2$.

The inner initialization uses the momentum center
\begin{equation}
z^{(0)}_t=y^{(t-1)}.
\end{equation}

\section{Inner local proximal coordinate descent}

The smooth inner gradient is
\begin{equation}
g_t(z)=(Q+\eta I)z-\alpha D^{-1/2}s-\eta y^{(t-1)}.
\end{equation}

Define the inner KKT residual $\kappa_t(z)$ by replacing $\nabla f$ with $g_t$ in the previous definition and define
\begin{equation}
C_t(z)=\|D^{1/2}\kappa_t(z)\|_1.
\end{equation}

For coordinate $i$, exact proximal coordinate descent updates
\begin{equation}
z_i^+=\mathcal T_{\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_i}/\ell}(z_i-g_{t,i}(z)/\ell),
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}
\ell=(Q+\eta I)_{ii}=\frac{1+\alpha}{2}+\eta.
\end{equation}

Only the selected vertex and its neighbors need gradient updates.

\section{Local monotonicity target}

The central lemma required for the AESP-CD locality proof is
\begin{proposition}[KKT mass decrease]
For a sequential proximal coordinate update at coordinate $i$,
\begin{equation}
C_t(z^+)\leq C_t(z)-\tau_{\rm cd}\sqrt{d_i}|\kappa_{t,i}(z)|,
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}
\tau_{\rm cd}=\frac{2(\alpha+\eta)}{1+\alpha+2\eta}.
\end{equation}
Under $\eta=1-2\alpha$, $\tau_{\rm cd}=2/3$.
\end{proposition}

This statement is the composite analogue of the residual-mass contraction used by AESP for ordinary PageRank. A complete formal proof should remain isolated from empirical implementation notes until all subgradient cases are verified.

\section{Inner stopping rule}

The inner active set is defined by
\begin{equation}
S_t^{(k)}=\left\{i:\frac{|\kappa_{t,i}(z^{(k)})|}{\sqrt{d_i}}\geq \epsilon_t^{\rm in}\right\}.
\end{equation}

The initial tolerance is chosen from the inner objective certificate. If the oracle does not expose $m=|E|$, use the conservative simple-graph bound
\begin{equation}
m\leq n(n-1)/2.
\end{equation}
This preserves correctness but may oversolve sparse instances.

\section{Implementation notes}

The implementation should record:
\begin{itemize}
\item raw KKT residual $R_\rho(x)$ and certified error $R_\rho(x)/\alpha$;
\item graph parameters, $\alpha$, $\rho$, stopping tolerance, random seed, and code version;
\item inner KKT mass decrease diagnostics;
\item local work measured by scanned degree volume.
\end{itemize}

\section{Open questions}

The remaining theoretical tasks are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item complete the rigorous proof of the sequential coordinate KKT mass contraction;
\item derive the total complexity of AESP-CD over Catalyst outer iterations;
\item design a hybrid method switching from accelerated AESP-CD to direct local coordinate descent after sufficient KKT compression.
\end{enumerate}
