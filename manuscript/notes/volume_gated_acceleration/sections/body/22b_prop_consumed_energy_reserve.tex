\subsection{Consumed transported energy is a causal reserve with a cubic
inverse-parameter star cost}
\label{sec:consumed-energy-reserve}

The all-history STOP in
\cref{prop:three-admission-all-history-stop} leaves one particularly narrow
repair: retain the transported energy already consumed by fixed-face steps.
This reserve is not an arbitrary initial endowment.  It starts from zero,
increases only after an executed contraction, and is unchanged by an exact
transported admission.

Work in the normalized coordinates of
\cref{prop:three-admission-all-history-stop}.  Thus
$H_U=D_U^{-1/2}Q_{UU}D_U^{1/2}$, $B_U:=D_UH_U$ is symmetric positive
definite, and
\begin{equation*}
 \Phi_U(z):=\frac12z^\top B_Uz-h_U^\top D_Uz.
\end{equation*}
When the restricted optimum $z_U^\star$ is interior, define the normalized
form of \cref{eq:transported-energy} by
\begin{equation}
 E_U(z,v):=\Phi_U(z)-\Phi_U(z_U^\star)
       +\frac{q^2}{2}\norm{v-z_U^\star}_{D_U}^2.
 \label{eq:consumed-reserve-energy}
\end{equation}
At a checkpoint $k$, let $E_k$ be this energy, let
\begin{equation*}
 D_k:=\sum_{j\leq k:\,j\ {m admitted}}\Delta_j
\end{equation*}
contain only already realized Schur drops, and put
\begin{equation}
 R_k:=E_0+D_k-E_k,
 \qquad
 \mathcal B_k(\lambda):=D_k-\Xi_k+\lambda R_k,
 \qquad \Xi_k:=\delta_k^2.
 \label{eq:consumed-reserve-ledger}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Exact consumed-energy identities and structural solvency]
\label{prop:consumed-energy-structural-solvency}
Consider the exact transported-center recurrence on nested faces whose
restricted optima are interior, started at the actual zero initialization so
that $\Xi_0=0$.  Then $R_0=0$, and:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item across a fixed-face step from energy $E$ to $E^+$,
 \begin{equation}
  R^+-R=E-E^+\geq qE\geq0;
  \label{eq:consumed-reserve-fixed-step}
 \end{equation}
 \item across an exact frozen-candidate admission with Schur drop $\Delta$,
 \begin{equation}
  E^+=E+\Delta,\qquad D^+=D+\Delta,\qquad R^+=R;
  \label{eq:consumed-reserve-admission}
 \end{equation}
 \item if that admission contains exactly the rows selected by the named
 complete violating-boundary gate, then
 \begin{equation}
  \Xi_{k^+}=\Xi_{k^-},
  \qquad
  \mathcal B_{k^+}(\lambda)-\mathcal B_{k^-}(\lambda)=\Delta_k.
  \label{eq:consumed-reserve-admission-score}
 \end{equation}
 \item on any finite trace, provided no checkpoint has $R_k=0$ and
 $\Xi_k>D_k$, the least nonnegative coefficient making every reviewed
 checkpoint solvent is exactly
 \begin{equation}
  \lambda_\star
  =\max_{k:R_k>0}\frac{[\Xi_k-D_k]_+}{R_k}.
  \label{eq:consumed-reserve-minimum-coefficient}
 \end{equation}
\end{enumerate}

The following are sufficient at every initial, post-step, and
post-admission checkpoint:
\begin{align}
 \lambda_{\rm en}(q)
 &:=\frac{(1+q^2)(1-q)}{q^5},
 \label{eq:consumed-reserve-universal-coefficient}\\
 \lambda_{\rm low}(q)
 &:=\frac{2c_{\rm low}(1-q)}{q^3},
 \label{eq:consumed-reserve-low-frequency-coefficient}
\end{align}
where the second coefficient replaces the first if every post-step residual
$r_k=H_{U_k}z_k-h_{U_k}$ obeys the structural low-frequency condition
\begin{equation}
 \norm{r_k}_{D_{U_k}}^2
 \leq 2c_{\rm low}q^2
 \bigl(\Phi_{U_k}(z_k)-\Phi_{U_k}(z_{U_k}^\star)\bigr).
 \label{eq:consumed-reserve-low-frequency-condition}
\end{equation}
That is, either declared coefficient makes
$\mathcal B_k(\lambda)\geq0$ causally, without future score recovery.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The fixed-face estimate-center contraction gives
$E^+\leq(1-q)E$; substituting in
\cref{eq:consumed-reserve-ledger} proves
\cref{eq:consumed-reserve-fixed-step}.  The transported-center identity
\cref{eq:transported-center-identity} and the realized-credit update give
\cref{eq:consumed-reserve-admission}.  Hence $R_k$ is exactly the sum of
energy drops on already executed fixed-face steps.  Its nonnegativity and
\cref{eq:consumed-reserve-minimum-coefficient} follow immediately.

It remains to check the score across admission.  Let $\ell\leq z$ be the safe
envelope at the frozen candidate.  For every admitted boundary row $j$, the
complete gate and the nonpositive off-diagonal entries of $H$ give
\begin{equation*}
 r_j(z)\leq r_j(\ell)<-q^2\tau<0.
\end{equation*}
After zero-padding $z$, every old active residual row is unchanged because the
old principal block is unchanged and every new coordinate is zero.  Every new
active row equals the just displayed frozen-candidate boundary residual and is
strictly negative.  The positive residual maximum, hence $\delta$ and $\Xi$,
is therefore unchanged.  Together with
\cref{eq:consumed-reserve-admission}, this proves
\cref{eq:consumed-reserve-admission-score}.  If an implementation adds
nonviolating closure vertices to the same batch, their frozen-candidate
residuals must be checked separately; the named complete gate adds none.

For the sufficient coefficients, let $e=z-z_U^\star$ and
$P:=\Phi_U(z)-\Phi_U(z_U^\star)=e^\top B_Ue/2$.  Interiority gives
$r=H_Ue$.  Cauchy--Schwarz in the $B_U$ inner product and
$(B_U)_{ii}=d_i(1+q^2)/2$ imply
\begin{equation*}
 r_i^2=\frac{(e_i^\top B_Ue)^2}{d_i^2}
 \leq\frac{(1+q^2)P}{d_i}
 \leq(1+q^2)P.
\end{equation*}
Consequently $\Xi\leq(1+q^2)E/q^4$.  At a post-step checkpoint,
\cref{eq:consumed-reserve-fixed-step} also gives
$E^+\leq(1-q)R^+/q$.  Therefore
$\Xi^+\leq\lambda_{\rm en}(q)R^+$, which is stronger than
$D^+-\Xi^++\lambda_{\rm en}R^+\geq0$.

Under \cref{eq:consumed-reserve-low-frequency-condition}, instead use
$\max_i r_i^2\leq\norm r_D^2$ to obtain
$\Xi\leq2c_{\rm low}P/q^2\leq2c_{\rm low}E/q^2$ and then
\cref{eq:consumed-reserve-low-frequency-coefficient}.  Finally,
\cref{eq:consumed-reserve-admission-score} preserves the bound across every
named admission.
\end{proof}

The coefficient in
\cref{eq:consumed-reserve-universal-coefficient} is deliberately coarse.  A
finite atlas suggests a small constant at one fixed $q$, but the following
exact family shows that no constant or polylogarithmic coefficient is uniform
as $q\downarrow0$.

\begin{proposition}[Finite atlas GO and a small-$q$ star STOP]
\label{prop:consumed-energy-star-stop}
For the exact six-vertex execution in
\cref{prop:three-admission-all-history-stop},
\begin{align}
 E_0&=\frac{3703}{5281250},\notag\\
 R_7&=
 \frac{5238790326812421787092421075496}
 {5629419085753779695034027099609375},
 \label{eq:three-admission-consumed-reserve}\\
 \lambda_\star
 &=\frac{1772625800261634289972166085525}
 {136208548497122966464402947962896}
 =0.0130140568989\ldots,
 \label{eq:three-admission-reserve-minimum}
\end{align}
and the maximum in
\cref{eq:consumed-reserve-minimum-coefficient} occurs at held stage $7$.
Thus a unit reserve coefficient repairs that witness.

At $q=1/5$, exact enumeration of every seed of every connected graph in the
NetworkX graph atlas on two through seven vertices gives $6780$ rooted runs.
The raw $D_k-\Xi_k$ account is negative in $2847$ runs.  The largest exact
required coefficient is
\begin{equation}
 \lambda_{\rm atlas}
 =\frac{2786829024075210327}{1036704485947225385}
 =2.688161440267\ldots<3.
 \label{eq:consumed-reserve-atlas-maximum}
\end{equation}
It is already attained at stage $4$ on the leaf-seeded star $K_{1,5}$, and
\begin{equation*}
 3-\lambda_{\rm atlas}
 =\frac{323284433766465828}{1036704485947225385}>0.
\end{equation*}
Hence coefficient $3$ repairs this finite atlas at $q=1/5$.

In contrast, let $G=K_{1,4}$ have edges
$\{(0,1),(1,2),(1,3),(1,4)\}$ and seed the leaf $0$.  Take
$\alpha=q^2$ and $\rho=\tau=q/5$.  For every sufficiently small positive
$q$, the actual zero-start recurrence admits the hub $1$ at stage $1$, admits
the other three leaves simultaneously at stage $2$, and holds on the full
face at stage $3$.  Put
\begin{align*}
 p(q)&:=9q^5-59q^4+72q^3-56q^2+23q-5,\\
 p_D(q)&:=81q^{14}-1386q^{13}+9511q^{12}-35308q^{11}
 +83213q^{10}-136886q^9\\
 &\quad+165371q^8-156264q^7+110787q^6-63382q^5
 +27077q^4-7852q^3\\
 &\quad+1759q^2-330q+25,
\end{align*}
and
\begin{align*}
 A(q)&:=-351q^{21}+5886q^{20}-41832q^{19}+180450q^{18}
 -575637q^{17}+1452888q^{16}\\
 &\quad-2980028q^{15}+5080528q^{14}-7264518q^{13}
 +8711380q^{12}-8729164q^{11}\\
 &\quad+7379436q^{10}-5015994q^9+3155456q^8-1158068q^7
 +834896q^6+11221q^5\\
 &\quad+156238q^4+47652q^3+17298q^2+8415q+1800.
\end{align*}
At the held stage-$3$ checkpoint, exact substitution gives
\begin{align}
 \Xi_3
 &=\frac{(1-q)^4p(q)^2}{6400(1+q^2)^2}
 =\frac1{256}-\frac{33}{640}q+\frac{2109}{6400}q^2
   -\frac{1059}{800}q^3+\bigO(q^4),
 \label{eq:star-reserve-score}\\
 D_3
 &=\frac{q^2(73q^4+80q^3+14q^2-80q+25)}{400(1+q^2)}
 =\frac{q^2}{16}-\frac{q^3}{5}-\frac{11q^4}{400}
   +\bigO(q^5),
 \label{eq:star-reserve-credit}\\
 E_0
 &=\frac{q^4(q-5)^2(3q^2+1)}{25(1+q^2)^2},
 \label{eq:star-reserve-initial-energy}\\
 R_3
 &=\frac{q^3A(q)}
 {1600(1+q^2)^2(q^2+3)^2(3q^2+1)^2}
 =\frac{q^3}{8}+\frac{187q^4}{320}
   +\frac{283q^5}{2400}+\bigO(q^6),
 \label{eq:star-reserve-consumed-energy}\\
 \Xi_3-D_3
 &=\frac{p_D(q)}{6400(1+q^2)^2}
 =\frac1{256}-\frac{33}{640}q+\frac{1709}{6400}q^2
   -\frac{899}{800}q^3+\bigO(q^4).
 \label{eq:star-reserve-debt}
\end{align}
Therefore every solvent coefficient for this trace satisfies
\begin{align}
 \lambda(q)&\geq\lambda_3(q)
 :=\frac{(q^2+3)^2(3q^2+1)^2p_D(q)}{4q^3A(q)},
 \label{eq:star-reserve-required-coefficient}\\
 \lambda_3(q)
 &=\frac1{32q^3}-\frac{143}{256q^2}
   +\frac{144943}{30720q}+\bigO(1),
 \qquad
 \lim_{q\downarrow0}q^3\lambda_3(q)=\frac1{32}.
 \label{eq:star-reserve-q-cubed-stop}
\end{align}
No constant or $\bigO(\log^{O(1)}(1/q))$ multiple of the consumed-energy
reserve can consequently make the named ledger uniformly solvent.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The six-vertex fractions follow by evaluating
\cref{eq:consumed-reserve-ledger} along the exact trace; dividing the stage-$7$
debt in \cref{eq:three-admission-stage-seven-stop} by $R_7$ gives
\cref{eq:three-admission-reserve-minimum}.  Exact comparison with every other
checkpoint proves its maximality.  The atlas statement repeats the same
calculation with rational arithmetic for every listed root.  It is finite
evidence, not an extrapolation beyond order seven or away from $q=1/5$.

For the star chronology, the stage-$1$ seed candidate is
$z_{1,0}=q^2(1-q/5)$.  The active residual divided by $q^2$ tends to
$-1/2$, while the hub boundary residual divided by $q^2$ tends to $-1/8$;
the divided gate threshold is $-q/5$.  Hence the hub is admitted.  At stage
$2$, both active residuals divided by $q^2$ tend to $-1/8$, so the correction
is zero, while each exterior leaf residual divided by $q^2$ tends to
$-1/8$.  Hence all three leaves are admitted.  At stage $3$, every candidate
coordinate divided by $q$ tends to $1/8$, and the active residual divided by
$q^2$, in vertex order $(0,1,2,3,4)$, tends to
\begin{equation*}
 \left(\frac1{16},-\frac18,-\frac3{16},-\frac3{16},-\frac3{16}\right).
\end{equation*}
The seed is therefore the strict correction maximizer and
$\delta_3\to1/16$.  The safe envelope clips to zero for sufficiently small
$q$; at zero the seed row is strictly below the threshold, so the full-face
gate holds rather than certifies.  All comparisons are strict in the limit,
and continuity proves the claimed chronology on a punctured interval.

Solving the seed, two-vertex, and full-star restricted systems and substituting
the three fixed-face steps and two center transports gives
\cref{eq:star-reserve-score,eq:star-reserve-credit,eq:star-reserve-initial-energy,eq:star-reserve-consumed-energy}.
Subtracting gives \cref{eq:star-reserve-debt}.  Since
$A(0)=1800$ and $p_D(0)=25$, division proves
\cref{eq:star-reserve-required-coefficient,eq:star-reserve-q-cubed-stop}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact scope and next theorem target.}
The atlas GO is only a finite exact-real audit at $q=1/5$.  The star is an
asymptotic STOP for the named zero-start transported-center recurrence,
complete gate, score $\Xi=\delta^2$, and the particular causal reserve
$R=E_0+D-E$.  It refutes neither another locally justified reserve nor solver
convergence, objective accuracy, or a work bound.  Maintaining $E_k$ requires
the current restricted optimum and a $D_U$-energy evaluation; if these are not
already cached by the transported recurrence, their response solve, active
scan, arithmetic, state, and validation costs must be charged.  The sharp
next target is to prove
\cref{eq:consumed-reserve-low-frequency-condition} with graph-independent
$c_{\rm low}$ on a useful reachable promised class, which would give the
star-order $\Theta(q^{-3})$ coefficient, or else find an exact reachable family
requiring $\omega(q^{-3})$.
