\section{The correct open lemma: one-sided expanding-subspace acceleration}
\label{sec:open-lemma}

The path in \cref{sec:path} shows that support may expand on nearly every
accelerated-scale propagation step.  The desired algorithm must therefore
continue through expansions, or charge each expansion only to newly exposed
edges.

A concrete candidate zero-pads its history when $U_t$ expands and otherwise
uses the standard strongly convex momentum
\begin{align}
 y^{(t)}
 &=x^{(t)}+\beta_{\mathrm F}\bigl(x^{(t)}-x^{(t-1)}\bigr),
 \qquad
 \beta_{\mathrm F}:=\frac{1-\sqrt\alpha}{1+\sqrt\alpha},
 \label{eq:dynamic-y}\\
 z^{(t+1)}
 &=\Pi_{\R_+^{U_t}}
   \bigl(y^{(t)}-(Qy^{(t)}-c_\rho)\bigr),
 \label{eq:dynamic-step}
\end{align}
where vectors are zero outside $U_t$.  Construct $\ell^{(t+1)}$ from
$z^{(t+1)}$ by \cref{eq:envelope-delta,eq:safe-envelope}, set
$x^{(t+1)}:=z^{(t+1)}$, and then set
\begin{equation}
 U_{t+1}:=U_t\cup
 \left\{i:
 \kappa_{\rho,i}(\ell^{(t+1)})
 <-\alpha\tau\sqrt{d_i}
 \right\}.
 \label{eq:dynamic-gate}
\end{equation}
The proved gate invariants hold regardless of whether the following
conjecture is true:
\begin{equation}
 \ell^{(t)}\leq x_\rho^\star,
 \qquad
 U_t\subseteq S_\rho,
 \qquad
 \vol(U_t)\leq\frac1\rho.
 \label{eq:dynamic-invariants}
\end{equation}

The next exact calculation rules out the first, shock-free way of importing a
fixed-subspace estimate sequence.  Let an energy on a face be
\emph{fixed-face exact} if it is nonnegative, dominates the restricted
objective gap, and vanishes at an exact restricted minimizer with zero
momentum.  Standard strongly convex estimate-sequence energies have these
three properties.

\begin{proposition}[One edge forbids zero-shock continuation]
\label{prop:one-edge-zero-shock-fails}
Fix $0<\alpha<1$, let
\begin{equation}
 a:=\frac{1+\alpha}{2},
 \qquad
 \eta:=\frac{1-\alpha}{2},
 \qquad
 0<r<\frac{1-\alpha}{3+\alpha},
 \qquad
 \rho=\tau=r.
 \label{eq:one-edge-parameters}
\end{equation}
On the endpoint-source edge $P_1=(v_0,v_1)$ with $s=e_{v_0}$, initialize one
stage of \cref{eq:dynamic-y,eq:dynamic-step,eq:dynamic-gate} at
\begin{equation}
 U_t=\{v_0\},
 \qquad
 x^{(t-1)}=x^{(t)}=(u,0),
 \qquad
 u:=\frac{\alpha(1-r)}{a}.
 \label{eq:one-edge-old-state}
\end{equation}
This is the exact restricted minimizer with zero momentum.  The safe gate
admits precisely $B=\{v_1\}$, and the first subsequent enlarged-face step has
strictly positive objective error
\begin{equation}
 G_\rho(x^{(t+2)})-G_\rho(x_{\{v_0,v_1\}}^\star)
 =\alpha\eta^2m^2
 =\frac{2\eta^2}{a}\,\Delta_B>0,
 \qquad
 m:=\frac{\eta(1-r)}a-r,
 \label{eq:one-edge-positive-remainder}
\end{equation}
where
$\Delta_B=\alpha a m^2/2$ is the exact expansion gain.  Consequently no
shock-free one-expansion inequality
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal E_{t+2}^{U_t\cup B}
 \leq\theta\mathcal E_{t+1}^{U_t}
 \label{eq:zero-shock-inequality}
\end{equation}
can hold universally: for every fixed-face exact energy and every finite
$\theta$, this run violates \cref{eq:zero-shock-inequality}.  Any valid
continuation inequality must include an explicit optimum-shift/shock term (or
an equivalent transported-state term).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Both path degrees equal one and
\begin{equation}
 Q=\begin{pmatrix}a&-\eta\\-\eta&a\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 c_\rho=\alpha\begin{pmatrix}1-r\\-r\end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
The point in \cref{eq:one-edge-old-state} solves the one-coordinate
restricted problem.  Its envelope correction is zero, while its outside KKT
value is
\begin{equation}
 h:=\kappa_{\rho,1}(u,0)
 =-\eta u+\alpha r=-\alpha m.
\end{equation}
The parameter range in \cref{eq:one-edge-parameters} is exactly the strict
inequality $m>r$, so $h<-\alpha\tau$ and the gate admits $v_1$.  There is no
other outside vertex.  In particular $m>0$, and the full restricted optimum
is interior.  Its Schur complement and correction from $(u,0)$ are
\begin{equation}
 S=a-\frac{\eta^2}{a}=\frac\alpha a,
 \qquad
 x_{\{v_0,v_1\}}^\star-(u,0)=(\eta m,am).
 \label{eq:one-edge-optimum-shift}
\end{equation}
Hence \cref{prop:shock} gives
$\Delta_B=h^2/(2S)=\alpha a m^2/2$.

The restricted step preceding admission leaves the exact old-face point
unchanged, so $x^{(t+1)}=(u,0)$.  Zero-padding also leaves the next momentum
point equal to $(u,0)$.  On the enlarged face,
\begin{equation}
 Q(u,0)-c_\rho=(0,-\alpha m),
 \qquad
 x^{(t+2)}=(u,\alpha m),
\end{equation}
and the projection is inactive.  Since $a-\alpha=\eta$, the oriented error is
$x_{\{v_0,v_1\}}^\star-x^{(t+2)}=\eta m(1,1)$.  Finally
$Q(1,1)^\top=\alpha(1,1)^\top$, so its quadratic objective gap is
$\alpha\eta^2m^2$, proving \cref{eq:one-edge-positive-remainder}.  Every
fixed-face exact old energy is zero at \cref{eq:one-edge-old-state}, whereas
the enlarged energy dominates this positive gap.  Thus
\cref{eq:zero-shock-inequality} is impossible.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact scope and charged work.}
The proposition refutes only a pointwise carryover with no shock term.  It
does not refute an inequality containing $\Delta_B$, the telescoping identity
\cref{eq:shock-telescope}, a multistep transported potential, or the
cumulative newly admitted-volume target \cref{eq:new-volume-charge}.  On this
single edge, a direct materialized implementation charges constant work for
the old-face residual/envelope check, the cut-edge gate evaluation and
admission, two zero-padding state writes, the two-coordinate enlarged
recurrence, and its two-coordinate persistent state; both old and new degree
volumes are one.  This prefix requires no terminal output, and materializing
one would cost two coordinate writes.  Thus the example gives no asymptotic
work lower bound.  On a longer prefix,
the displayed full restricted recurrence, envelope maximum, and boundary
test must still charge every old-face read they actually perform; an
incremental residual or response representation may avoid such reads, but
must name and charge its update, state, query, validation, and output costs.
