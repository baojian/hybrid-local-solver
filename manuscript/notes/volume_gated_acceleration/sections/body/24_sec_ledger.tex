The exact constant-ratio gate is determined by the raw frontier coordinate &
Refuted by the nonlocal stage-$6$ suppression and stage-$9$ maximizer switch,
\cref{prop:path-frontier-only-gate-fails} \\
Arbitrary correction-maximizer switches preclude any uniform chronology
upper bound near the sampled powers & Refuted up to one logarithm:
$T=O(q^{-2}\log(1/q))$ and
$\mathfrak V_T=O(q^{-3}\log(1/q))$ regardless of maximizer switches,
\cref{thm:path-moving-max-soft-upper} \\
The full moving correction decreases on every held face, or is always at most
$q^{-1}$ times the normalized error to the restricted optimum & Refuted for

stage-$6$--$7$ trace,
\cref{prop:path-monotone-correction-potential-fails}.  Nonmonotone,
phase-aware, and amortized arguments remain open \\
The correction--error bank
$\Phi_t^{\rm bank}=\delta_t+q^{-1}e_t$ decreases on every consecutive held
pair & Refuted by the earliest later held pair of the same actual $q=1/5$
trace: $\Phi_{10}^{\rm bank}>\Phi_9^{\rm bank}$ on $U_4$, even though
$\Phi_7^{\rm bank}<\Phi_6^{\rm bank}$ on $U_3$,
\cref{prop:path-correction-error-bank-potential-fails}.  The exact constraints
from those two pairs have no common nonnegative constant coefficient,
\cref{prop:path-no-constant-coefficient-bank}.  The tight endpoint
face-dependent bank is flat on those pairs, and its squared stage-$8$ reset is
strictly paid by the exact Schur/optimum drop, while its raw linear reset is
not, \cref{prop:path-tight-pair-schur-bank}.  The same squared bank plus
reserve nevertheless rises first on $7\to8^-$ and again on $8^+\to9$, with
positive net stage-$7$--$9$ change,
\cref{prop:path-tight-pair-local-chain-stop}.  This finite complete-chain
STOP is not an all-held-pairs or global stagewise result.  The explicit
post-reset recovery telescope first pays that deficit at stage $13$, while
stage $12$ still stops; its $7\to13$ endpoint GO is uniform over the feasible
rectangle,
\cref{prop:path-tight-pair-recovery-block}.  This finite one-admission block
does not give a face-general horizon, multi-admission telescope, or logarithm
removal \\
The observable score $\Xi=\delta^2$ has a causal realized-credit condition
that survives a second canonical admission & Proved as a face-general
accounting identity and finite exact-real path instantiation.  Starting at
stage $3$, the balance stays nonnegative through the consecutive stage-$4$
and stage-$8$ admissions; carried stage-$4$ credit makes the narrow stage-$9$
margin positive.  Without that carry, the stage-$8$ block stops through stage
$11$ and first closes at stage $12$,
\cref{prop:path-causal-two-admission-recovery}.  This does not prove a uniform
closing horizon, an all-admission telescope, or a global work bound \\
A restarted $\Xi=\delta^2$ block always recovers before its next safe
admission & Refuted by the exact asymmetric T-tree execution: the balance is
negative at stage $5$, remains negative immediately before and after the next
admission at stage $9$, and first becomes positive at stage $14$,
\cref{prop:t-tree-causal-next-admission-stop}.  This is a finite
algorithm-specific STOP, not an all-history-ledger failure or a nonpath
response-work lower bound \\
The zero-balance all-history $\Xi=\delta^2$ account is solvent for the named
zero-start recurrence and complete gate & Refuted by an exact six-vertex
execution with three consecutive singleton admissions.  Starting at the
actual zero initialization and retaining all three Schur credits, its balance
is negative at held stages $7$ and $8$ and recovers at stage $9$,
\cref{prop:three-admission-all-history-stop}.  This finite algorithm-specific
STOP is not a convergence failure, asymptotic or work lower bound, or
refutation of structural or different-score ledgers \\
The consumed transported-energy reserve repairs the all-history account with
a constant or polylogarithmic coefficient uniformly in $q$ & Refuted.  The
reserve $R_k=E_0+D_k-E_k$ is causal and nonnegative, and coefficient $3$
repairs the finite rooted graph atlas through order seven at $q=1/5$.
However, the actual leaf-seeded $K_{1,4}$ trace forces
$\lambda(q)\sim1/(32q^3)$.  For the named complete gate, admission preserves
the score exactly.  The original score is solvent with baseline $O(q^{-5})$
under arbitrary projection and $(1-q^2)/q^4$ when projection is inactive.
More generally, the original score has
$(C_{\rm n}+1-q^2)/q^4$ under the causal normal--anchor condition.  A safe
positive-support modification is solvent with $(1-q^2)/q^4$ under arbitrary
projection.  An actual zero-start $K_{2,3}$-plus-leaf trace remains inactive
through stage $4$ and forces
$\lambda(q)\sim49/(3884q^4)$ for both scores.  Thus their graph-uniform order
is exactly $\Theta(q^{-4})$ on the inactive subclass.  For the point-seed
gate with interior initial singleton and $\rho=\tau=q/5$, the first admission
forces enough already consumed energy to pay every clipped-zero residual;
hence \cref{thm:original-score-projected-quartic} proves the same
$(1-q^2)/q^4$ coefficient for the original score under arbitrary projection
and closes its graph-uniform order at $\Theta(q^{-4})$ too.  A separate exact 30-vertex
zero-start trace proves that a clipped positive-residual row and positive
debt are reachable, but at one fixed $q$ with required coefficient below
$0.087$,
\cref{prop:consumed-energy-structural-solvency,prop:consumed-reserve-quartic-projection,thm:original-score-projected-quartic,prop:original-score-normal-anchor,prop:reachable-positive-residual-projection,prop:consumed-energy-star-stop,prop:consumed-energy-quartic-lower}.
This is a scalar-ledger result, not a convergence or work theorem \\
The exact point-seed transported recurrence with $\rho=\tau=q/5$ terminates
with a graph-uniform chronology bound & Proved.  The exact shock telescope
gives $E_0+D_k\leq q^2$; hence every face admits or certifies within
$O(q^{-1}\log(1/q))$ steps.  Globally
$T=O(q^{-2}\log(1/q))$, swept active volume is
$O(q^{-3}\log(1/q))$, and terminal PPR error is at most $2q/5$.  Dense
incremental Cholesky charges every optimum shift in the same exact-real
arithmetic order with $O(q^{-2})$ storage.  Product-scale work, finite
precision, bit complexity, and stability remain open,
\cref{thm:complete-gate-soft-horizon,cor:complete-gate-dense-fallback} \\
Gating directly on each fully materialized exact restricted optimum removes
the hold logarithm from the dense fallback & Proved for the exact active-set
comparator, not the named fixed-step recurrence.  The safe correction is zero,
so one complete gate call per face gives $O(q^{-2})$ scans and
$O(q^{-3})$ dense factor/solve arithmetic,
\cref{cor:complete-gate-exact-optimum-comparator} \\
Sparse ordering makes the explicit scalar Cholesky response product-scale on
all graphs & Refuted for that representation.  Full-support fixed-gap
$3$-regular expanders at $q=1/(2n)$ force $\Omega(q^{-2})$ stored scalars and
$\Omega(q^{-3})$ ordinary pivot arithmetic even with the best offline
ordering.  Non-Cholesky responses remain open,
\cref{prop:complete-gate-explicit-cholesky-expander-stop} \\
A uniform $O(q^{-1})$ fixed-face chronology bound holds for the exact
constant-ratio path execution & Open.  The conditional roots in
\cref{lem:path-full-face-linearized-roots} and finite exact traces do not
prove or refute it; the audited packet/range lower bound failed \\
Continuous restricted re-solving has accelerated amortized cost & Proved
under fixed-subspace contraction, \cref{thm:amortized} \\
The dynamic gate achieves
$\softO(1/(\rho\sqrt\alpha))$ graph-uniform work & Open,
\cref{conj:one-sided-continuation} \\
