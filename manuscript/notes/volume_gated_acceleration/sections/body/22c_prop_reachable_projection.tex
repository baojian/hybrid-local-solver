\subsection{A reachable clipped positive-residual row}
\label{sec:reachable-positive-residual-projection}

The algebraic projection STOP above leaves open whether the same mechanism is
reachable from the declared zero start.  The next finite witness closes that
reachability qualification without closing the asymptotic coefficient gap.

\begin{proposition}[A reachable clipped positive-residual row with positive
causal debt]
\label{prop:reachable-positive-residual-projection}
Run the literal zero-start transported-center recurrence and named complete
gate on $G_{\rm reach}$ from the point seed $s=7$, with
\begin{equation*}
 q=\frac{12}{625},\qquad \alpha=q^2,
 \qquad \rho=\tau=\frac q5.
\end{equation*}
Every visited restricted optimum is interior, every gate decision is strict,
and the run certifies at stage $32$.  Its action word, with admission batch
sizes as superscripts, is
\begin{equation}
 A^3A^3H^7A^9H^2A^1H^{11}A^9HA^2A^1A^1H^2C.
 \label{eq:reachable-projection-action-word}
\end{equation}
Projection is active only at vertex $23$ on stages $11,12,13$.  At the held
stage-$12$ checkpoint the active face has $16$ vertices and
\begin{equation}
 y_{12,23}=p_{12,23}=0,\qquad
 n_{12,23}>0,\qquad
 r_{23}(p_{12})=\max_i r_i(p_{12})>0.
 \label{eq:reachable-projection-positive-row}
\end{equation}
For $b_{12}=(1-q)z_{11}+qz_{U_{12}}^\star$, exact rational arithmetic gives
\begin{align}
 \frac{553}{100}
 &<\frac{n_{12,23}}{b_{12,23}}<\frac{55301}{10000},
 \label{eq:reachable-projection-anchor-ratio}\\
 \Xi_{12}-D_{12}&>0,
 \label{eq:reachable-projection-positive-debt}\\
 \frac{433}{5000}
 &<\lambda_{12}^{\rm reach}
 :=\frac{\Xi_{12}-D_{12}}{R_{12}}
 <\frac{867}{10000},
 \label{eq:reachable-projection-required-coefficient}\\
 \frac{1178}{10^{11}}
 &<q^4\lambda_{12}^{\rm reach}<\frac{1179}{10^{11}}.
 \label{eq:reachable-projection-scaled-coefficient}
\end{align}
Thus clipped positive-residual rows and positive original-score debt are both
reachable from the declared zero start.  The witness does not raise the known
$\Omega(q^{-4})$ lower order: it is one fixed $q$, and its actual required
coefficient is below $0.087$.  It cannot establish a $q^{-5}$ asymptotic
order.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The registered exact audit
\texttt{volume\_gated\_acceleration.reachable\_projection\_witness}
uses \texttt{Fraction} arithmetic for every restricted solve, transported
center, projection, residual, Schur drop, energy, reserve, and complete-gate
comparison.  It verifies the admitted batches
\begin{align*}
1&:\{8,13,15\},&2&:\{10,12,18\},\\
10&:\{0,1,5,6,11,14,23,24,29\},&13&:\{3\},\\
25&:\{2,4,9,16,17,21,22,26,28\},&27&:\{25,27\},\\
28&:\{20\},&29&:\{19\},
\end{align*}
the holds and terminal certificate in
\cref{eq:reachable-projection-action-word}, and strict positivity of every
restricted optimum.  The least exact decision margin over all $32$ stages is
larger than $1/(2\cdot10^7)$.

The only negative raw coordinates are row $23$ at stages $11,12,13$.  Its
post-step residual is negative at stages $11$ and $13$.  At stage $12$ the
audit checks \cref{eq:reachable-projection-positive-row}; in particular the
positive residual is the literal all-coordinate maximum that defines
$\Xi_{12}$.  Direct exact evaluation of the normal, anchor, cumulative
realized admission credit $D_{12}$, and consumed reserve $R_{12}$ proves
\cref{eq:reachable-projection-anchor-ratio,eq:reachable-projection-positive-debt,eq:reachable-projection-required-coefficient,eq:reachable-projection-scaled-coefficient}.
\end{proof}

For separation of scales, the same audit has an optional exact finite screen
on
\begin{equation*}
 q\in\{187,188,\ldots,195\}/10^4.
\end{equation*}
A clipped positive-residual row exists only from $0.0188$ through $0.0194$.
Across those seven points, $q(n_{23}/b_{23})$ lies strictly between $0.098$
and $0.111$; causal debt is zero through $q=0.0191$, and $q^4\lambda$ is only
$1.178\ldots\,10^{-8}$, $3.170\ldots\,10^{-8}$, and
$5.558\ldots\,10^{-8}$ at the last three points.  These are exact finite
evaluations.  The apparent $1/q$ normal--anchor scaling on this narrow window
is not an asymptotic statement, and the same fixed graph loses the relevant
row at both endpoints of the screen.
