\subsection{A zero-start path can force an accuracy-driven swept-prefix factor}
\label{sec:path-swept-separation}

The path ledger also has an exact lower-bound regime.  The construction below
does not prove the numerically observed separation when $\rho=\tau=q/5$.
Instead, it chooses a much smaller rational accuracy so that the mandatory
support-discovery distance itself is $q^{-2}$.  This gives a clean first
separation for the literal full-prefix implementation, while also showing why
it is not yet the desired soft-order obstruction.

\begin{proposition}[Zero-start accuracy-driven swept-prefix separation]
\label{prop:path-zero-start-swept-separation}
Fix an integer $n\geq2$ and put
\begin{equation}
 q_n:=\frac1n,
 \qquad \alpha_n:=q_n^2,
 \qquad \chi_n:=\frac{1-q_n}{1+q_n},
 \qquad L_n:=n^2,
 \qquad N_n:=L_n+1,
 \label{eq:path-swept-parameters-a}
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}
 \rho_n=\tau_n:=\frac{q_n}{3}\chi_n^{L_n},
 \qquad \epspprinst{n}:=2\rho_n.
 \label{eq:path-swept-parameters-b}
\end{equation}
On the endpoint-seeded path
$P_{N_n}=(v_0,\ldots,v_{N_n})$, use ambient path degrees and initialize the
internally gated transported-center execution at
\begin{equation}
 U_0=\{v_0\},
 \qquad x^{(0)}=v^{(0)}=0.
 \label{eq:path-swept-zero-start}
\end{equation}
At every stage perform
\cref{eq:center-prox-step,eq:center-update}, construct the safe envelope, admit
the complete all-violations batch from \cref{eq:dynamic-gate}, and transport
the center exactly as in \cref{prop:path-implicit-transport-ledger}.  Stop at
the first envelope satisfying \cref{eq:global-certificate}.  This exact-real
execution terminates, and if $T_n$ is its number of fixed-face steps and $J_n$
its number of admissions, then
\begin{equation}
 L_n\leq J_n\leq L_n+1,
 \qquad T_n\geq J_n,
 \qquad
 1+2L_n\leq\nu_{\rm fin}\leq2(L_n+1).
 \label{eq:path-swept-stage-bounds}
\end{equation}
Moreover,
\begin{equation}
 \mathfrak V_{T_n}
 \geq\sum_{j=1}^{L_n}\bigl(1+2(j-1)\bigr)
 =L_n^2=q_n^{-4}
 \geq\frac{\nu_{\rm fin}}{4q_n^2}
 =\frac1{4q_n}\frac{\nu_{\rm fin}}{q_n}.
 \label{eq:path-swept-separation}
\end{equation}
For the literal full-prefix implementation in
\cref{prop:path-implicit-transport-ledger}, the exhaustive resource vector
specializes to
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 (&C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},C_{\rm rec},
 C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit})\\
 =\bigl(&\Theta(\nu_{\rm fin}),\Theta(J_n+1),1,0,
 \Theta(\mathfrak V_{T_n}),\Theta(\mathfrak V_{T_n}),
 \Theta(\mathfrak V_{T_n}),\\
 &\Theta(\nu_{\rm fin}),O(\nu_{\rm fin}),
 \Theta(\mathfrak V_{T_n}),\Theta(\nu_{\rm fin})\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:path-swept-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Thus its charged exact-cell work is $\Omega(q_n^{-4})$, whereas the realized
final-volume product is
$\nu_{\rm fin}/q_n=\Theta(q_n^{-3})$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
First, \cref{eq:path-rppr-lower} at $i=0$ gives
$x_{\rho_n,0}^\star/\sqrt{d_0}\geq q_n-\rho_n>0$, so the seed face lies in
$S_{\rho_n}$ and has an interior restricted optimum.  Every subsequent
strong admission preserves interiority by \cref{eq:positive-expansion}.
The execution is finite.  On any fixed prefix without an admission,
\cref{lem:fixed-face-estimate-center} drives both the primal point and the
estimate center to the interior restricted optimum.  The residual and the
envelope correction therefore converge to zero, so the safe envelope
converges to that optimum.  If the immediate successor is a strict strong
violation at the restricted optimum, it is admitted after finitely many
steps.  Otherwise the successor satisfies the one-sided threshold; equality
is harmless because the envelope lies below the restricted optimum and the
cut row is nonpositive.  The active-coordinate KKT values converge to zero,
and every more distant coordinate has positive KKT slack.  Hence the global
certificate holds after finitely many further steps.  There are only
$N_n+1$ faces, so induction proves termination.

For the terminal safe envelope $\ell_T$, the endpoint-path estimates
\cref{eq:path-solution,eq:path-rppr-lower} give, for every $0\leq i\leq L_n$,
\begin{equation}
 \frac{x_{\rho_n,i}^\star}{\sqrt{d_i}}
 \geq q_n\chi_n^i-\rho_n
 \geq q_n\chi_n^{L_n}-\rho_n
 =2\rho_n.
 \label{eq:path-swept-mandatory-prefix}
\end{equation}
The terminal certificate and \cref{lem:one-sided-certificate} give
$\norm{D^{-1/2}(x_{\rho_n}^\star-\ell_T)}_\infty\leq\tau_n=\rho_n$.
Since $\ell_T$ vanishes outside $U_T$, \cref{eq:path-swept-mandatory-prefix}
forces $v_0,\ldots,v_{L_n}\in U_T$.

Every safe envelope is supported on a path prefix.  Beyond that prefix only
its immediate successor has an active neighbor; every farther nonseed row has
KKT value $\alpha_n\rho_n\sqrt{d_i}>0$.  Thus each all-violations batch has at
most one newly admitted outside vertex (active violations, if any, do not
change the face).  Starting from $v_0$, reaching $v_{L_n}$ takes at least
$L_n$ admissions in distinct fixed-face steps, while the ambient path has
only one additional vertex.  This proves \cref{eq:path-swept-stage-bounds}.

Immediately before the $j$th mandatory admission, the active prefix has
ambient degree volume $1+2(j-1)$.  These $L_n$ distinct steps alone contribute
the sum in \cref{eq:path-swept-separation}.  Since $L_n=q_n^{-2}$ and
$\nu_{\rm fin}\leq2(L_n+1)\leq4L_n$, the remaining inequalities follow.

Finally, this implementation exposes every retained path incidence once and
has one terminal external return.  Every fixed-face step performs a full
proximal recurrence, a full response application, an envelope maximum, and
full active-state writes; bounded path degrees make each of those charges
equivalent to the current face volume.  The factor records and active vectors
occupy $\Theta(\nu_{\rm fin})$ persistent cells, a streamed reverse pass uses
$O(\nu_{\rm fin})$ scratch cells, and the terminal sparse vector and
certificate emit $\Theta(\nu_{\rm fin})$ cells.  Together with one exposure
round per admitted vertex, this proves
\cref{eq:path-swept-eleven-vector}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Limitation of the separation.}
The certified lower-bound factor in \cref{eq:path-swept-separation} is
polynomial in $1/q_n$, but this family deliberately encodes it in the
accuracy:
\begin{equation}
 \log\frac{q_n}{3\rho_n}
 =L_n(-\log\chi_n)
 =\Theta(q_nL_n)
 =\Theta(q_n^{-1}).
 \label{eq:path-swept-log-accuracy}
\end{equation}
Here $-\log\chi_n=2\operatorname{artanh}(q_n)=\Theta(q_n)$ uniformly for
$n\geq2$.
Consequently the result refutes a log-free
$O(\nu_{\rm fin}/q_n)$ charge for this literal swept-prefix implementation,
but not a $\softO(\nu_{\rm fin}/q_n)$ theorem that may hide logarithms in the
accuracy.  It is also far below the RPPR volume-cap benchmark
$1/(\rho_nq_n)$, makes no claim about implicit implementations that avoid the
full-prefix operations defining $\mathfrak V_T$, and does not prove the
observed $\Theta(q^{-3})$ swept work when $\rho=\tau=q/5$.  The latter
constant-ratio support scale is fixed by
\cref{prop:path-constant-ratio-floor};
\cref{thm:path-moving-max-soft-upper} later bounds its stage and swept-volume
powers up to one logarithm, but the exact exponents remain open because the
global envelope correction couples frontier admission to old-coordinate
momentum overshoot.
