\label{prop:three-admission-all-history-stop}

 \label{eq:three-admission-witness}
\end{equation}
and use the exact zero-start transported-center recurrence and complete

\[

 \qquad

\]

\begin{equation}
 \frac{122521790117}{27766447109375}>0.
 \label{eq:three-admission-positive-margin}
\end{equation}
The exact chronology is

\begin{proof}
Use normalized coordinates $z=D^{-1/2}x$.  On an active face $U$, put
\begin{align*}
 (H_Uz)_i
 &:=\frac{1+\alpha}{2}z_i
   -\frac{1-\alpha}{2d_i}
    \sum_{j\in U:\,j\sim i}z_j,\\
 h_i&:=\alpha\left(\frac{\mathbf 1_{\{i=0\}}}{d_0}-\rho\right),
 \qquad r_U(z):=H_Uz-h_U.
\end{align*}
For a primal state $z$ and estimate center $v$, the declared fixed-face step
is
\begin{equation*}
 y=\frac{z+qv}{1+q},
 \qquad
 z^+=\left[y-r_U(y)\right]_+,
 \qquad
 v^+=z^++\frac{1-q}{q}(z^+-z).
\end{equation*}
The complete gate forms
\begin{equation*}
 \delta=\frac1\alpha\left[\max_{i\in U}r_i(z^+)\right]_+,
 \qquad \ell=[z^+-\delta\mathbf 1]_+,
\end{equation*}
and admits every boundary row with normalized KKT value below
$-\alpha\tau$.  On an admission, it freezes and zero-pads $z^+$ and
transports $v^+$ by the exact restricted-optimum displacement.  These are
exactly the recurrence and gate used in the preceding causal propositions.

At the zero start, the only active residual is $-23/1250$, so
$\delta_0=0$; this is the actual initialization rather than an abstract
filter state.  Repeated exact substitution in the displayed recurrence gives
\cref{eq:three-admission-positive-margin}.  Substitution of the safe envelope
in every boundary row gives
\cref{eq:three-admission-gate-one,eq:three-admission-gate-two,%
eq:three-admission-gate-three}.  At stages $4$--$11$, every boundary row is
at least $-1/625$ while some active envelope row is below $-1/625$; at stage
$12$ every row is at least the threshold.  This proves the full chronology.

Exact Gaussian elimination gives each restricted optimum.  Eliminating the
new cell gives the response loads and Schur pivots in
\cref{eq:three-admission-schur-drops}; equivalently, direct subtraction of
the two restricted objective values gives the same three drops.  All three
pre- and post-admission corrections vanish, so after stage $3$ the exact
causal invariant is
\begin{equation*}
 \mathcal C_k=\Delta_1+\Delta_2+\Delta_3-\delta_k^2.
\end{equation*}
Exact recurrence evaluation and squaring at stages $7,8,9$ give
\cref{eq:three-admission-stage-seven-stop,%
eq:three-admission-stage-eight-stop,eq:three-admission-stage-nine-go}.
Finally, summing the active volumes along the displayed chronology gives
$2+4+7+9\cdot9=94$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact scope of the all-history STOP.}
This is a finite exact-real counterexample for the named graph, seed,
zero-start transported-center recurrence, complete simultaneous boundary
gate, phase origin, and score $\Xi=\delta^2$ with zero initial balance.  It
refutes unconditional solvency of that specific all-history scalar account;
unlike a restarted account, it discards no earlier admission credit.  The
debt is transient and closes at stage $9$, while the solver certifies at stage
$12$.  The proposition therefore proves no convergence failure, objective or
work lower bound, asymptotic family, finite-precision result, or eleven-
resource nonpath response ledger.  It does not refute a different observable,
an explicitly justified initial reserve, a stronger energy account, another
recurrence or gate, or a structural promised graph class.
