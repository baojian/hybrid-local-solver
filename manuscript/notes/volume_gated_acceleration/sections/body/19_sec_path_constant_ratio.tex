\subsection{Constant ratio: support scale, nonlocal correction, and a soft chronology bound}
\label{sec:path-constant-ratio}

The constant-ratio regime does have an exact baseline.  It fixes the support
and product-scale floors.  A later moving-maximum energy argument supplies
stage and swept upper bounds up to one logarithm, but does not prove the
sampled exact powers.  The first lemma records why a frontier-only recurrence
cannot settle the remaining question.

For a path-prefix candidate $x$ define normalized coordinates and the global
envelope correction
\begin{equation}
 p_i(x):=\frac{x_i}{\sqrt{d_i}},
 \qquad
 \delta(x):=
 \max_{i\in U_j}
 \left(\frac{\kappa_{\rho,i}(x)}{\alpha\sqrt{d_i}}\right)_+.
 \label{eq:path-constant-ratio-correction}
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[The path admission gate contains the global correction]
\label{lem:path-global-envelope-gate}
Let $U_j=\{v_0,\ldots,v_j\}$ be a proper endpoint prefix whose immediate
successor $v_{j+1}$ is an ambient degree-two vertex.  For any nonnegative
candidate $x$ supported on $U_j$, its safe envelope satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\ell_i}{\sqrt{d_i}}
 =\bigl[p_i(x)-\delta(x)\bigr]_+.
 \label{eq:path-normalized-envelope}
\end{equation}
The complete all-violations gate strongly admits $v_{j+1}$ if and only if
\begin{equation}
 p_j(x)>\delta(x)+\frac{2\alpha(\rho+\tau)}{\eta}.
 \label{eq:path-global-correction-gate}
\end{equation}
In particular, for $\alpha=q^2$ and $\rho=\tau=q/5$, the additive threshold
in \cref{eq:path-global-correction-gate} is
\begin{equation}
 \frac{2\alpha(\rho+\tau)}{\eta}
 =\frac{8q^3}{5(1-q^2)}.
 \label{eq:path-constant-ratio-threshold}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Dividing \cref{eq:safe-envelope} by $\sqrt{d_i}$ gives
\cref{eq:path-normalized-envelope}.  The normalized outside KKT value is
\begin{equation*}
 \frac{\kappa_{\rho,j+1}(\ell)}{\sqrt{d_{j+1}}}
 =\alpha\rho-\frac{\eta}{2}
   \frac{\ell_j}{\sqrt{d_j}}.
\end{equation*}
It is smaller than $-\alpha\tau$ exactly when
$\ell_j/\sqrt{d_j}>2\alpha(\rho+\tau)/\eta$.  The right-hand side is positive, so
\cref{eq:path-normalized-envelope} makes this equivalent to
\cref{eq:path-global-correction-gate}.  Substitution of
$\eta=(1-q^2)/2$ proves \cref{eq:path-constant-ratio-threshold}.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Exact constant-ratio support and product-scale floors]
\label{prop:path-constant-ratio-floor}
Fix $0<q\leq1/4$, set
\begin{equation}
 \alpha=q^2,
 \qquad \rho=\tau=\frac q5,
 \qquad \chi=\frac{1-q}{1+q},
 \qquad
 L_q:=\left\lfloor
 \frac{\log(5/3)}{-\log\chi}
 \right\rfloor,
 \label{eq:path-constant-ratio-parameters}
\end{equation}
and take an endpoint-seeded ambient-degree path with at least $L_q+1$ edges.
Run the exact-real zero-start, internally gated transported-center execution
of \cref{prop:path-zero-start-swept-separation}, now with the parameters in
\cref{eq:path-constant-ratio-parameters}, until its first terminal one-sided
certificate.  If $J_q,T_q,\nu_{\rm fin}$, and $\mathfrak V_{T_q}$ denote its
admissions, fixed-face steps, final degree volume, and swept old-face volume,
then
\begin{align}
 L_q&\leq J_q\leq\frac5q,
 &T_q&\geq J_q,
 \label{eq:path-constant-ratio-JT}\\
 1+2L_q&\leq\nu_{\rm fin}\leq\frac5q,
 &\mathfrak V_{T_q}&\geq L_q^2.
 \label{eq:path-constant-ratio-volume}
\end{align}
Since
\begin{equation}
 L_q\sim\frac{\log(5/3)}{2q}
 \qquad(q\downarrow0),
 \label{eq:path-constant-ratio-radius}
\end{equation}
this proves
\begin{equation}
 J_q=\Theta(q^{-1}),
 \qquad \nu_{\rm fin}=\Theta(q^{-1}),
 \qquad T_q=\Omega(q^{-1}),
 \qquad
 \mathfrak V_{T_q}=\Omega(q^{-2})
 =\Omega(\nu_{\rm fin}/q).
 \label{eq:path-constant-ratio-floors}
\end{equation}

For the named literal full-prefix implementation, with one terminal external
certificate/output return, the exhaustive resource vector is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 (&C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},C_{\rm rec},
 C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit})\\
 =\bigl(&\Theta(\nu_{\rm fin}),\Theta(J_q+1),1,0,
 \Theta(\mathfrak V_{T_q}),\Theta(\mathfrak V_{T_q}),
 \Theta(\mathfrak V_{T_q}),\\
 &\Theta(\nu_{\rm fin}),O(\nu_{\rm fin}),
 \Theta(\mathfrak V_{T_q}),\Theta(\nu_{\rm fin})\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:path-constant-ratio-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The terminal output has note-scoped PPR error at most
$\rho+\tau=2q/5$.  Equations
\cref{eq:path-constant-ratio-JT,eq:path-constant-ratio-volume} do not prove
$T_q=\Theta(q^{-2})$ or $\mathfrak V_{T_q}=\Theta(q^{-3})$ and give no
separation above the realized product scale.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Finite termination follows from the fixed-face contraction and finite-face
argument in the proof of
\cref{prop:path-zero-start-swept-separation}.  At the terminal envelope,
\cref{eq:path-solution,eq:path-rppr-lower} and the definition of $L_q$ give,
for $0\leq i\leq L_q$,
\begin{equation*}
 \frac{x_{\rho,i}^\star}{\sqrt{d_i}}
 \geq q\chi^i-\rho
 \geq\frac{3q}{5}-\frac q5
 =2\rho.
\end{equation*}
The terminal certificate bounds the normalized difference between
$x_\rho^\star$ and the safe envelope by $\tau=\rho$.  Thus the terminal
envelope is positive on $v_0,\ldots,v_{L_q}$, and all those vertices belong
to $U_T$.  Since only the immediate successor of a path prefix can have a
negative outside KKT value, at least $L_q$ singleton admissions in distinct
fixed-face steps are necessary.  This proves the lower bounds for $J_q,T_q$,
and $\nu_{\rm fin}$.

Safe admission gives $\vol(U_T)\leq1/\rho=5/q$, which proves both upper
bounds.  Immediately before the $j$th mandatory admission the prefix volume
is at least $1+2(j-1)$.  Summing these distinct charged steps gives
\begin{equation*}
 \mathfrak V_{T_q}
 \geq\sum_{j=1}^{L_q}\bigl(1+2(j-1)\bigr)=L_q^2.
\end{equation*}
Finally, $-\log\chi=2\operatorname{artanh}(q)\sim2q$, proving
\cref{eq:path-constant-ratio-radius} and the asymptotic consequences.  The
resource vector follows from the same charged literal implementation as
\cref{eq:path-swept-eleven-vector}; bounded path degrees make terminal output
size equivalent to final degree volume.  The accuracy statement is
\cref{cor:accuracy-volume}.
\end{proof}

The correction in \cref{lem:path-global-envelope-gate} is genuinely
nonlocal even on an actual zero-start constant-ratio run.

\begin{proposition}[A zero-start path refutes frontier-only gate logic]
\label{prop:path-frontier-only-gate-fails}
Set
\begin{equation*}
 q=\frac15,
 \qquad \alpha=\rho=\tau=\frac1{25},
 \qquad \eta=\frac{12}{25},
\end{equation*}
and use an endpoint-seeded ambient-degree path with at least six edges.  In
the exact execution of \cref{prop:path-constant-ratio-floor}, stages $1,2,4$
admit $v_1,v_2,v_3$, respectively, while stages $3,5$ do not admit.  At stage
$6$, on $U_3$, the raw normalized frontier candidate and global correction
are
\begin{equation}
 p_3=\frac{145182985951}{10558361328125},
 \qquad
 \delta_6=\frac{14898928}{2462890625},
 \qquad
 p_3-\delta_6
 =\frac{7887224114511}{1024161048828125}.
 \label{eq:path-stage-six-correction}
\end{equation}
Here the maximum defining $\delta_6$ is attained at the old seed $v_0$;
the other three normalized KKT values are negative.  The exact comparisons
are
\begin{equation}
 p_3>\frac1{75}
 \qquad\text{but}\qquad
 p_3-\delta_6<\frac1{75}.
 \label{eq:path-stage-six-suppression}
\end{equation}
Thus the uncorrected frontier would pass the strong admission test for
$v_4$, but the actual safe-envelope all-violations gate does not admit it.

For clarity, the exact first nine stages have the following face/action
chronology.  ``None'' means that every active normalized KKT value is
nonpositive, hence $\delta=0$.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ccll@{}}
\toprule
stage & face before gate & correction maximizer & gate action \\
\midrule
$1$ & $U_0$ & none & admit $v_1$ \\
$2$ & $U_1$ & none & admit $v_2$ \\
$3$ & $U_2$ & none & hold \\
$4$ & $U_2$ & none & admit $v_3$ \\
$5$ & $U_3$ & none & hold \\
$6$ & $U_3$ & $v_0$ & hold \\
$7$ & $U_3$ & $v_0$ & hold \\
$8$ & $U_3$ & $v_0$ & admit $v_4$ \\
$9$ & $U_4$ & $v_2$ & hold \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The run remains on $U_3$ at stage $7$ and admits $v_4$ at stage $8$, where
\begin{equation*}
 \delta_8=\frac{28990137728}{3755908203125},
 \qquad
 p_3-\delta_8
 =\frac{346381814596089}{25604026220703125}>\frac1{75},
\end{equation*}
and the correction maximum is still at $v_0$.  After transporting to $U_4$,
the stage-$9$ positive correction candidates
$\kappa_{\rho,i}/(\alpha\sqrt{d_i})$ at $v_0,v_1,v_2$ are
\begin{equation}
 \frac{154106389088}{18779541015625},
 \quad
 \frac{185836991328}{18779541015625},
 \quad
 \frac{228031410368}{18779541015625},
 \label{eq:path-stage-nine-switch}
\end{equation}
while the remaining two values are negative.  Hence the correction maximizer
has switched from the seed to the old interior vertex $v_2$.  In particular,
the stage-$9$ frontier coordinate also satisfies
\begin{equation*}
 p_4=\frac{2901661181152715177}{591581025829345703125}
 <\delta_9=\frac{228031410368}{18779541015625},
\end{equation*}
so the safe-envelope frontier is zero and the stage-$9$ gate holds.  Thus
neither the raw frontier coordinate nor a correction pinned to one fixed old
coordinate determines the exact admission chronology.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
In normalized path coordinates all coefficients, restricted optima, center
transports, proximal steps, and envelope corrections are rational.  Direct
substitution into
\cref{eq:center-prox-step,eq:center-update,eq:path-global-correction-gate}
gives the stated admission schedule.  At stage $6$ the normalized KKT values
$\kappa_{\rho,i}/\sqrt{d_i}$ are
\begin{equation*}
 \frac{14898928}{61572265625},
 \quad
 -\frac{32479266}{150236328125},
 \quad
 -\frac{4135906804}{3755908203125},
 \quad
 -\frac{5413122}{4736328125}.
\end{equation*}
Dividing their positive maximum by $\alpha=1/25$ proves the middle equality
in \cref{eq:path-stage-six-correction}.  Exact cross multiplication gives
\begin{align*}
 p_3-\frac1{75}
 &=\frac{13214504728}{31675083984375}>0,\\
 p_3-\delta_6-\frac1{75}
 &=-\frac{17304769609592}{3072483146484375}<0,
\end{align*}
which proves \cref{eq:path-stage-six-suppression}.  The stage-$8$ comparison
and the ordering in \cref{eq:path-stage-nine-switch} are again immediate
positive-denominator cross multiplications.  These computations use the
global correction at every stage, not a frontier surrogate.
\end{proof}

The same exact trace also stops the first two pointwise attempts to improve
the moving-maximum estimate itself.  Importantly, the calculation below is
inside the actual projected, globally corrected execution; it does not invoke
the conditional inactive-projection recurrence from
\cref{lem:path-full-face-linearized-roots}.

\begin{proposition}[Monotone moving-correction debt and a $q^{-1}$ error bound fail]
\label{prop:path-monotone-correction-potential-fails}
In the exact $q=1/5$ execution of
\cref{prop:path-frontier-only-gate-fails}, stages $6$ and $7$ are consecutive
held-face steps on the same face $U_3$.  Their full moving corrections are
\begin{equation}
 \delta_6=\frac{14898928}{2462890625},
 \qquad
 \delta_7=\frac{6291729688}{751181640625},
 \qquad
 \delta_7-\delta_6
 =\frac{1747556648}{751181640625}>0.
 \label{eq:path-stage-seven-correction-growth}
\end{equation}
Let
\begin{equation*}
 e_7:=\norm{D_{U_3}^{-1/2}
                   (x^{(7)}-x_{U_3}^{\star})}_\infty .
\end{equation*}
Then the same actual iterate satisfies
\begin{equation}
 e_7=\frac{1373692513944}{1024161048828125},
 \qquad
 \frac{\delta_7}{e_7}
 =\frac{5361340160387}{858557821215}
 =5+\frac{1068551054312}{858557821215}
 >\frac1q.
 \label{eq:path-stage-seven-correction-error-stop}
\end{equation}
Both the proximal candidate and the envelope subtraction are strictly
unclipped at stage $7$.  Consequently the two pointwise statements
\begin{equation*}
 \delta_{t+1}\leq\delta_t
 \quad\hbox{on every held face},
 \qquad
 \delta_t\leq q^{-1}
 \norm{D_{U}^{-1/2}(x^{(t)}-x_U^\star)}_\infty
\end{equation*}
are false for the named exact execution.

The complete $q=1/5$ run has
$J=4$, $T=16$, $\nu_{\rm fin}=9$,
$n_{\rm fin}=5$, and $\mathfrak V_T=114$.  It performs no intermediate
external emission and makes one terminal certificate/output return.  Thus,
using the same named implementation and cost model, substitution into the
already charged literal-path ledger
\cref{eq:path-constant-ratio-eleven-vector} gives
\begin{equation}
 \bigl(\Theta(9),\Theta(5),1,0,
       \Theta(114),\Theta(114),\Theta(114),
       \Theta(9),O(9),\Theta(114),\Theta(9)\bigr)
 \label{eq:path-monotone-correction-stop-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
in the shared eleven-coordinate order.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The stage-$6$ value is
\cref{eq:path-stage-six-correction}.  Direct exact replay of the next
fixed-face step gives the stage-$7$ normalized active KKT vector
\begin{equation*}
 \left(
 \frac{6291729688}{18779541015625},
 \frac{229902132}{3755908203125},
 -\frac{19945416}{61572265625},
 -\frac{5682971052}{18779541015625}
 \right).
\end{equation*}
Its positive maximum divided by $\alpha=1/25$ is the displayed $\delta_7$.
Putting the two corrections over their common denominator proves
\cref{eq:path-stage-seven-correction-growth}.

Solving the four-row restricted system exactly and subtracting its optimum
from the actual stage-$7$ candidate gives normalized error
\begin{equation*}
 \left(
 \frac{1619429295784}{5120805244140625},
 -\frac{18761289036}{52791806640625},
 -\frac{1373692513944}{1024161048828125},
 -\frac{473086325916}{393908095703125}
 \right).
\end{equation*}
Positive-denominator cross multiplication shows that the third absolute
value is maximal, which gives $e_7$.  Reducing $\delta_7/e_7$ gives
\cref{eq:path-stage-seven-correction-error-stop}.  The smallest normalized
candidate coordinate is the frontier coordinate, and it obeys
\begin{equation*}
 p_3(x^{(7)})-\delta_7
 =\frac{51347070933121}{5120805244140625}>0.
\end{equation*}
The raw proximal candidate has this same strictly positive coordinate, so
both the nonnegativity projection and the safe-envelope truncation are
inactive at this stage as claimed.

The exact gate replay admits at stages $1,2,4,8$, holds at all other
nonterminal stages, and certifies at stage $16$.  Hence $J=4$, $T=16$, and
$n_{\rm fin}=5$.  Ambient degrees give $\nu_{\rm fin}=1+2\cdot4=9$, while
the face chronology gives
\begin{equation*}
 \mathfrak V_T=1+3+2\cdot5+4\cdot7+8\cdot9=114.
\end{equation*}
The resource statement is therefore exactly the specialization of
\cref{eq:path-constant-ratio-eleven-vector}; no operation is removed from
that ledger.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact scope of the STOP.}
The proposition refutes only a potential that treats $\delta_t$ itself as a
monotone held-face debt and the displayed coefficient-one $q^{-1}$ pointwise
comparison.  A larger or $q$-dependent coefficient, a nonmonotone
space--time potential, a phase-aware residual argument, or an amortized
moving-correction charge is not excluded.  In particular, the reviewed
$\delta(x)\leq q^{-2}e$ estimate in
\cref{eq:path-moving-max-delta-energy-a}, the one-log upper theorem, a
uniform $K_{\rm face}=O(q^{-1})$ proof by another route, and every matching
lower-bound question remain intact.  This finite exact-real STOP proves
nothing about finite precision, branching or cyclic graphs, intermediate
external returns, or the literal zero-padded recurrence.

The first correction-aware bank suggested by that STOP is also testable on
the same literal trace.  It does absorb the already reviewed correction
increase, so its failure below is logically separate from
\cref{eq:path-stage-seven-correction-growth,eq:path-stage-seven-correction-error-stop}.

\begin{proposition}[A coefficient-one correction--error bank fails on held steps]
\label{prop:path-correction-error-bank-potential-fails}
On a fixed visited face $U$, let
\begin{equation}
 e_t(U):=\norm{D_U^{-1/2}(x^{(t)}-x_U^\star)}_\infty,
 \qquad
 \Phi_t^{\rm bank}(U):=\delta(x^{(t)})+q^{-1}e_t(U).
 \label{eq:path-correction-error-bank}
\end{equation}
The stage indices are those of the literal transported-center gate chronology;
the bank is reinitialized with the new restricted optimum after a transport.
No alternate phase origin or event permutation is assumed.
The proposed bank inequality is
\begin{equation*}
 \Phi_{t+1}^{\rm bank}(U)\leq \Phi_t^{\rm bank}(U)
\end{equation*}
whenever stages $t$ and $t+1$ are consecutive gate-held candidates computed
on the same face $U$.  It allows a rise in the moving correction to be paid
by a fall in normalized face error.  Indeed, on the $q=1/5$ execution of
\cref{prop:path-frontier-only-gate-fails}, it survives the reviewed held pair
on $U_3$:
\begin{equation}
 \Phi_7^{\rm bank}(U_3)-\Phi_6^{\rm bank}(U_3)
 =-\frac{156536838996284}{5120805244140625}<0.
 \label{eq:path-correction-error-bank-stage-seven-pass}
\end{equation}
Thus neither $\delta_7>\delta_6$ nor $\delta_7/e_7>1/q$ alone refutes this
bank.

The next face gives the exact STOP.  Stages $9$ and $10$ are consecutive
gate-held candidates on $U_4$, and
\begin{align}
 \delta_9
 &=\frac{228031410368}{18779541015625},
 &e_9(U_4)
 &=\frac{2309349016903925448}{591581025829345703125},
 \notag\\
 \delta_{10}
 &=\frac{1115890388544}{93897705078125},
 &e_{10}(U_4)
 &=\frac{14055067155984414192}{2957905129146728515625}.
 \label{eq:path-correction-error-bank-components}
\end{align}
Consequently
\begin{equation}
 \begin{split}
 \Phi_9^{\rm bank}(U_4)
 &=\frac{18730043949735496616}{591581025829345703125},\\
 \Phi_{10}^{\rm bank}(U_4)
 &=\frac{105427397282315325168}{2957905129146728515625},\\
 \Phi_{10}^{\rm bank}(U_4)-\Phi_9^{\rm bank}(U_4)
 &=\frac{11777177533637842088}
         {2957905129146728515625}>0.
 \end{split}
 \label{eq:path-correction-error-bank-stage-ten-stop}
\end{equation}
Here the correction itself decreases by
$24266663296/93897705078125$, but the increase in $q^{-1}e_t$ is larger.
The failure is therefore not a restatement of correction growth.

The complete literal run and all charged operations are unchanged:
$J=4$, $T=16$, $\nu_{\rm fin}=9$, $n_{\rm fin}=5$, and
$\mathfrak V_T=114$, with no intermediate external emission and one terminal
certificate/output return.  Scanning the current prefix for the moving
global correction, forming the clipped safe envelope, applying the complete
gate, performing every old-face recurrence and transported response pass,
writing/materializing the live state, and returning the terminal output give
the complete specialized ledger
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 &(C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},C_{\rm rec},
 C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit})\\
 &\quad=\bigl(\Theta(9),\Theta(5),1,0,
       \Theta(114),\Theta(114),\Theta(114),
       \Theta(9),O(9),\Theta(114),\Theta(9)\bigr)
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:path-correction-error-bank-stop-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
in the shared eleven-coordinate order.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The exact rational replay used in
\cref{prop:path-monotone-correction-potential-fails} also computes the
restricted optimum after each transport.  Substituting its stage-$6$ and
stage-$7$ values into \cref{eq:path-correction-error-bank} and putting them
over a common denominator gives
\cref{eq:path-correction-error-bank-stage-seven-pass}.

Stage $8$ admits $v_4$.  At each of stages $9$ and $10$, the positive
proximal candidate is computed on that same transported face $U_4$ and the
literal clipped envelope $[p-\delta\one]_+$ is retained.  At stage $9$ the
envelope KKT value at $v_3$ is
\begin{equation*}
 -\frac{5807889226569967}{2169506475830078125}
 <-\frac1{625}=-\alpha\tau,
\end{equation*}
and at stage $10$ the envelope KKT value at $v_4$ is
\begin{equation*}
 -\frac{135334265006654209513}{73947628228668212890625}
 <-\frac1{625}.
\end{equation*}
The frontier envelope is zero at both stages, so the normalized outside KKT
value at $v_5$ is $\alpha\rho=1/625>0$.  Thus neither stage admits nor
certifies: both are literal gate-held steps on $U_4$.

Exact Thomas solves for $x_{U_4}^\star$ give the four quantities in
\cref{eq:path-correction-error-bank-components}.  Substitution into
\cref{eq:path-correction-error-bank} gives
\cref{eq:path-correction-error-bank-stage-ten-stop}; direct subtraction also
gives the stated decrease of $\delta_t$.  The chronology and ledger are the
same complete-run specialization proved in
\cref{prop:path-monotone-correction-potential-fails}; no clipping, scan,
response pass, state write, materialization, round, or output is omitted.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact scope of the correction--error-bank STOP.}
This is a finite exact-real counterexample only to the coefficient-one bank
\cref{eq:path-correction-error-bank} being nonincreasing on every consecutive
held pair of the named $q=1/5$ transported-center endpoint-path execution.
Its face origin and event order are the literal algorithmic ones, not a claim
of permutation-robustness or an optimized rephasing.
It proves no asymptotic lower bound and does not refute a different
$q$-dependent coefficient, a signed residual phase, a longer space--time
block, a nonlocal sum across faces, or an amortized potential that is allowed
to rise locally.  It says nothing about literal zero-padded momentum,
nonpaths, floating point, bit complexity, or intermediate external returns.

The two reviewed held pairs in fact stop the whole nonnegative
constant-coefficient family.  The next proposition keeps the restricted
optimum face-local: its error term is reset after the stage-$8$ transport and
is never compared across that transport.

\begin{proposition}[No constant correction--error coefficient is held-pair monotone]
\label{prop:path-no-constant-coefficient-bank}
For a fixed constant $c\geq0$, define on each visited face $U$
\begin{equation}
 \Phi_t(c;U):=\delta(x^{(t)})+c q^{-1}e_t(U),
 \qquad
 e_t(U):=\norm{D_U^{-1/2}(x^{(t)}-x_U^\star)}_\infty .
 \label{eq:path-constant-coefficient-bank}
\end{equation}
Use the literal transported-center chronology and reset $x_U^\star$ after
each admission.  On the actual $q=1/5$ execution, the consecutive held
$U_3$ pair at stages $6$ and $7$ gives
\begin{align}
 \delta_6&=\frac{14898928}{2462890625},
 &e_6(U_3)&=\frac{623976140044}{78781619140625},
 \notag\\
 \delta_7&=\frac{6291729688}{751181640625},
 &e_7(U_3)&=\frac{1373692513944}{1024161048828125},
 \notag\\
 \Phi_7(c;U_3)-\Phi_6(c;U_3)
 &=\frac{1747556648}{751181640625}
   -c\frac{6737997306628}{204832209765625}.
 \label{eq:path-constant-coefficient-stage-seven-constraint}
\end{align}

\begin{equation}
