\subsection{Structured exact responses close the comparator ledger}
\label{sec:structured-response-composition}

The exact-optimum comparator in
\cref{cor:complete-gate-exact-optimum-comparator} removes fixed-face holds,
but its dense implementation still pays a cubic response bill.  The next
result separates that representation cost from the already-closed gate
chronology.  Throughout this subsection the arithmetic is exact-real and
\begin{equation*}
  \alpha=q^2,
  \qquad \rho=\tau=\frac q5,
  \qquad 0<q<1.
\end{equation*}

\begin{proposition}[Charged structured-response composition]
\label{prop:complete-gate-structured-response-composition}
Retain the finite-graph point-seed hypotheses and the changed control policy
of \cref{cor:complete-gate-exact-optimum-comparator}.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item Suppose a response backend realizes the actual nested trace of this
  comparator and, on every visited face $U$, exactly materializes
  $z_U^\star$, returns the complete set of genuinely violating boundary
  rows, and charges all response arithmetic, old-face and boundary reads,
  degree queries, materialized-state writes, and gate control.  If its
  cumulative response, materialization, and gate work is
  \begin{equation}
    O\!\left(\sum_{U\ \text{ visited}}\vol(U)\right),
    \label{eq:structured-response-conditional-charge}
  \end{equation}
  its persistent and transient state is $O(\max_U\vol(U))$, and final
  validation and output are charged once, then the comparator terminates
  with PPR error at most $2q/5$ in total work $O(q^{-2})$, using
  $O(q^{-1})$ persistent and transient cells and emitting $O(q^{-1})$
  cells.  This clause is a conditional ledger, not a construction of such a
  backend on arbitrary graphs.

\item On an endpoint-seeded path, the append-only tridiagonal response
  \cref{eq:path-append-ldl,eq:path-response-apply} satisfies (i)
  unconditionally.  Even if the implementation performs a complete reverse
  solve, writes every coordinate of the exact restricted optimum (including
  the coordinatewise map to $z_U^\star$), and scans the whole active
  prefix and boundary on every face, its total exact-real work is
  $O(q^{-2})$ and its storage and terminal output are $O(q^{-1})$.
  No response, scan, or intermediate state write is hidden in this bound.

\item On every finite tree, a fresh leaf-elimination solve on each comparator
  face, followed by full materialization and the complete gate scan, satisfies
  (i) unconditionally.  Its total exact-real work is $O(q^{-2})$ and its peak
  storage and terminal output are $O(q^{-1})$.

\item More generally, suppose every biconnected block of the ambient graph
  has at most $b$ vertices.  Recomputing the block--cut forest on every face
  and eliminating leaf blocks by dense exact arithmetic gives comparator work
  $O(b^3q^{-2})$ and peak response storage $O(b^2q^{-1})$, with
  $O(q^{-1})$ terminal output.  Thus the literal comparator is
  product-scale up to polylogarithms whenever
  $b=\operatorname{polylog}(1/q)$; no supplied block decomposition is
  assumed.

\item The activation-once endpoint-path solver of the companion
  \texttt{incremental\_active\_set\_sdd} note realizes the same exact gate
  trace more strongly: it appends one constant-size forward record per
  admission, queries the only boundary row from the endpoint record, and
  performs one terminal reverse substitution.  Under the exact variable and
  parameter map below, its total charged work is $O(q^{-1})$, including
  adjacency exposure, terminal validation, materialization, and output.
\end{enumerate}
In particular, trees and polylogarithmic-size biconnected blocks admit an
unconditional product-scale implementation of the exact-optimum comparator;
on endpoint paths clause (v) is output-linear.  None of the five clauses
changes or closes the logarithmic
hold question for the named fixed-step candidate-envelope recurrence.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The comparator visits exactly one initial face and one new face after every
nonempty admission batch.  Its batches are disjoint subsets of
$S_\rho\setminus U_0$, and every visited face is contained in $S_\rho$.
Hence
\begin{equation}
 N_{\rm face}\leq\frac5q+1,
 \qquad
 \max_U\vol(U)\leq\frac5q,
 \qquad
 \sum_{U\ \text{ visited}}\vol(U)
 \leq\left(\frac5q+1\right)\frac5q
 \leq\frac{30}{q^2}.
 \label{eq:structured-response-face-sum}
\end{equation}
The last quantity is at most six times the product target
$1/(\rho\sqrt\alpha)=5/q^2$.  Reading each finally admitted adjacency row
once, the terminal certificate, and the terminal sparse-vector output cost
$O(q^{-1})$.  The assumptions in (i), together with
\cref{eq:structured-response-face-sum}, therefore charge every remaining
operation and prove the stated work and memory bounds.  Correctness and the
$2q/5$ semantic error are exactly
\cref{cor:complete-gate-exact-optimum-comparator}.

On an endpoint path every active set is a prefix.  Indeed, outside a prefix
only its immediate successor has an incident active neighbor; every more
distant nonseed row has positive KKT value $\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_i}$.  Thus a
complete all-violations gate admits either that one successor or nobody, so
there are no nonprefix or multirow batches.  Appending
\cref{eq:path-append-ldl} costs constant work.  On each face,
\cref{eq:path-response-apply} is one complete reverse pass and hence costs
$O(|U|)\leq O(\vol(U))$, including the writes that materialize
the exact optimum and map it coordinatewise to $z_U^\star$.  A deliberately
literal active-prefix and boundary scan has the
same bound.  Summing with \cref{eq:structured-response-face-sum}, while
charging factor records, scratch state, final validation, and output as in
\cref{prop:path-implicit-transport-ledger}, proves (ii).  Notice that this
changed policy performs no fixed-face recurrence step and no estimate-center
transport; its repeated writes are exactly the per-face optimum
materializations just charged.

For (iii), the point-seed trace starts from a connected singleton, and adding
boundary vertices preserves a connected induced subtree.  On a tree,
eliminate the leaves of $Q_{UU}$ toward the seed in the symmetric $x$
coordinates.  Every leaf pivot updates
only its parent diagonal and right-hand side, so the forward elimination and
reverse substitution each cost $O(|U|)$ exact operations and cells.  The
reverse pass writes $x_U^\star$ and the coordinatewise conversion
$z_U^\star=D_U^{-1/2}x_U^\star$ costs one more charged pass.  Scanning the active incidences
finds every boundary row in $O(\vol(U))$ work.  Summing over faces by
\cref{eq:structured-response-face-sum} proves (iii), including materialized
state and gate writes.

For (iv), first find the block--cut forest of the current induced graph by a
charged linear adjacency scan.  Every current biconnected block lies inside
an ambient biconnected block and hence has at most $b$ vertices.  Eliminate a
leaf block's nonarticulation vertices by dense exact Gaussian elimination,
leaving its scalar Schur contribution and transformed load at the parent
articulation; then continue toward the root and reverse the process to
materialize the solution.  There are at most $|U|$ blocks, each costs
$O(b^3)$ arithmetic and $O(b^2)$ factor cells, and the block forest and gate
scans cost $O(\vol(U))$.  Rebuilding the response on the next face releases
the previous one, so the peak response storage is
$O(b^2|U|)=O(b^2q^{-1})$.  Summing the arithmetic over faces gives
$O(b^3q^{-2})$ and proves (iv).

For (v), put
\begin{equation}
 a:=\frac{1+\alpha}{2},
 \qquad
 \bar\alpha:=\frac{\alpha}{a}=\frac{2\alpha}{1+\alpha},
 \qquad
 M:=D-(1-\bar\alpha)A,
 \qquad x=D^{1/2}z.
 \label{eq:structured-response-source-map}
\end{equation}
If $r(z):=s-\bar\alpha^{-1}Mz$ is the source-note residue, then the shared
RPPR KKT vector obeys the exact coordinate identity
\begin{equation}
 D^{1/2}\kappa_\rho(D^{1/2}z)
 =\alpha\bigl(\rho d-r(z)\bigr).
 \label{eq:structured-response-kkt-map}
\end{equation}
The shared exact gate condition
$\kappa_{\rho,i}< -\alpha\tau\sqrt{d_i}$ is consequently equivalent to
\begin{equation}
 r_i(z)>(\rho+\tau)d_i,
 \label{eq:structured-response-gate-map}
\end{equation}
which is the source exact gate with its parameters
$\lambda=\rho$ and $\kappa=\tau$.  Restricted stationarity maps in the same
way, so both algorithms visit the same prefixes and make the same terminal
decision.  The activation-once theorem in
\texttt{incremental\_active\_set\_sdd} charges
$O(\operatorname{cvol}(U_T)+d_{\rm next})$, including one final reverse
materialization and certificate.  Here
$\operatorname{cvol}(U_T)\leq2\vol(U_T)\leq10/q$ and
$d_{\rm next}\leq2$, which proves (v).
\end{proof}

There are also structured product-scale solvers that solve the same terminal
RPPR task without reproducing the exact-optimum comparator's intermediate
faces.  The condition-free lazy-response theorem in the companion
\texttt{delayed\_reflection\_ladder} note finds the exact one-seed RPPR
solution on every finite tree in
\begin{equation*}
 \widetilde O\!\left(\frac1{\rho\sqrt\alpha}\right)
 =\widetilde O(q^{-2})
\end{equation*}
degree work and $O(\vol(S_\rho))=O(q^{-1})$ storage.  It scans every active
adjacency list once, charges every lazy event through its ancestors, and
charges one final forest solve and boundary certificate.  The causal
bounded-block theorem in the same note gives
$\widetilde O(b^3/(\rho\sqrt\alpha))$ work and
$O(b\vol(S_\rho))$ storage when every biconnected block has at most $b$
vertices, without requiring the block decomposition as input.  It is
product-scale when $b$ is polylogarithmic in $1/q$.

These two response-generated algorithms are unconditional terminal-task
results on their stated graph classes, but they do not prove a work bound for
the literal comparator chronology: their support traces are generated by
their own response events and need not equal its admission batches.  On
arbitrary cyclic graphs the companion
\texttt{incremental\_active\_set\_sdd} result is only conditional on an
implicit interface that reports all violations, updates the exact nested
state, and materializes the terminal vector within its declared total
charge.  A dynamic solve or a Schur-energy telescope alone does not maintain
complete changing-face boundary violations.  Consequently no existing
result cited here composes into an arbitrary-graph
$\widetilde O(q^{-2})$ implementation; compressed, matrix-free, iterative,
approximate, and other non-Cholesky responses remain open there.
