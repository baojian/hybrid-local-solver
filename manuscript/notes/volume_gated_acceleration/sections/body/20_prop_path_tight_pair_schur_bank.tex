\begin{proposition}[A causal realized-credit condition survives two admissions]
\label{prop:path-causal-two-admission-recovery}
At any candidate checkpoint on any safe face, define the observable score
\begin{equation}
 \Xi_t:=\delta(x^{(t)})^2.
 \label{eq:causal-face-score}
\end{equation}
For a transition from checkpoint $t$ to checkpoint $t+1$, let
$\Gamma_t=0$ on a fixed-face transition.  If the transition is a frozen-
candidate safe admission $U\subset U^+$, let
\begin{equation*}
 \Gamma_t:=\Delta_{U\to U^+}
 =G_\rho(x_U^\star)-G_\rho(x_{U^+}^\star)\geq0
\end{equation*}
be the exact restricted-optimum drop, credited only after the admission and
its charged Schur/response query have occurred.  Starting from any scalar
balance $\mathcal C_0$, update
\begin{align}
 R_t^\downarrow&:=[\Xi_t-\Xi_{t+1}]_+,
 &D_t^\uparrow&:=[\Xi_{t+1}-\Xi_t]_+,
 \notag\\
 \mathcal C_{t+1}
 &:=\mathcal C_t+\Gamma_t+R_t^\downarrow-D_t^\uparrow.
 \label{eq:causal-recovery-update}
\end{align}
Every credit in this update is realized: $R_t^\downarrow$ is added only after
the new score is known, and $\Gamma_t$ only after the new restricted face is
available.  Exact telescoping gives
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_k
 =\mathcal C_0+\Xi_0-\Xi_k+\sum_{t<k}\Gamma_t.
 \label{eq:causal-recovery-invariant}
\end{equation}
Equivalently,
\[
 \mathcal C_k\geq0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \sum_{t<k}D_t^\uparrow
 \leq
 \mathcal C_0+\sum_{t<k}R_t^\downarrow+\sum_{t<k}\Gamma_t.
\]
Thus $\mathcal C_k\geq0$ is only a causal, face-general
\emph{scalar-ledger-closing} certificate: all realized $\Xi$-increase debt
through checkpoint $k$ is paid by the declared initial balance, already
realized score decreases, and already realized restricted-optimum drops.  It
does not certify candidate convergence, monotonicity of the candidate
objective, or a work bound.  A block with negative balance makes no claim
before the first later checkpoint at which the balance becomes nonnegative.

\emph{ledger-solvent}.
