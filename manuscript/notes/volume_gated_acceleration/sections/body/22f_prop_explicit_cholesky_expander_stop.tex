\subsection{Full-support expanders stop sparse explicit Cholesky transport}
\label{sec:explicit-cholesky-expander-stop}

The dense fallback in \cref{cor:complete-gate-dense-fallback} leaves two
logically separate gaps: repeated fixed-face work and the representation of
exact restricted responses.  If an exact restricted optimum is already
materialized, the named candidate-envelope chronology is not the best use of
that information.

\begin{corollary}[Exact-optimum gate comparator removes the hold logarithm]
\label{cor:complete-gate-exact-optimum-comparator}
Retain the finite-graph, exact-real point-seed parameters, initial singleton,
and complete all-genuinely-violating-boundary gate of
\cref{thm:complete-gate-soft-horizon}, but change the control policy as
follows.  On each visited face, materialize its exact restricted optimum
$z_U^\star$, use $z_U^\star$ itself as the gate candidate, and invoke the
complete gate immediately, without an intervening fixed-face step.  Then the
policy admits or certifies after one gate call per face, has at most $5/q$
nonempty admission events, and returns the same terminal error
$2q/5$.  Its cumulative active/gate scans cost $O(q^{-2})$.

With the dense incremental scalar Cholesky implementation and the charged
exact-real conventions of \cref{cor:complete-gate-dense-fallback}, all factor
updates and one exact solve per face cost $O(q^{-3})$ arithmetic, persistent
factor storage is $O(q^{-2})$, and admitted-row exposure and terminal output
are $O(q^{-1})$.  Thus this fully materialized exact-optimum comparator removes
the hold logarithm from the dense fallback, but remains one polynomial factor
above $O(q^{-2})$ product-scale work.

This is an exact active-set comparator, not a sharper chronology theorem for
the named fixed-step candidate-envelope recurrence.  It assumes that every
restricted optimum and boundary response is fully materialized and charged;
it gives no finite-precision or implicit-response result.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Interiority gives zero active residual at $z_U^\star$.  Hence its original
safe-envelope correction is zero and its safe envelope is exactly
$z_U^\star\leq z_\rho^\star$.  The complete gate therefore either admits a
nonempty genuinely violating boundary batch or returns the one-sided global
certificate.  The safe-support volume bound and disjoint-batch chronology
give at most $5/q$ nonempty events and the terminal semantic conversion in
\cref{thm:complete-gate-soft-horizon} gives error $2q/5$.

There are at most $5/q+1$ faces and each has volume at most $5/q$, so scanning
each face and its boundary once costs $O(q^{-2})$.  If $n_{\rm fin}$ is the
final active dimension, dense block factor updates cost $O(n_{\rm fin}^3)$.
At most $n_{\rm fin}+1$ triangular-solve pairs, each costing
$O(n_{\rm fin}^2)$, have the same cubic upper bound.  Now use
$n_{\rm fin}\leq\vol(U_T)\leq5/q$ and charge one-time incidence exposure,
state writes, the final certificate scan, and output exactly as in
\cref{cor:complete-gate-dense-fallback}.
\end{proof}

The next proposition gives a narrow STOP for the remaining dense-response
factor.  It does not make the named recurrence's per-face logarithm necessary.

Call a response implementation an \emph{explicit original-basis scalar
Cholesky response} if, under any online or supplied offline scalar elimination
ordering, it factors each final restricted Hessian by ordinary scalar Schur
updates and explicitly stores every numerical nonzero of the resulting
$LDL^\top$ factor, Cholesky factor, or frontal matrices.  One stored scalar is
one persistent cell, and each scalar multiply--add in a pivot update is
charged.  This class excludes compressed, matrix-free, iterative,
approximate, and fast-matrix-multiplication representations.

\begin{proposition}[Expander STOP for explicit scalar Cholesky response]
\label{prop:complete-gate-explicit-cholesky-expander-stop}
Fix $\gamma\in(0,1]$ and let $(G_n,s_n)$ be any infinite family of connected
$3$-regular $n$-vertex graphs with point seed $s_n$ and
\begin{equation}
 P_n:=\frac{A_n}{3},
 \qquad \lambda_2(P_n)\leq1-\gamma.
 \label{eq:complete-gate-expander-gap}
\end{equation}
Put
\begin{equation}
 q_n:=\frac1{2n},
 \qquad \alpha_n=q_n^2,
 \qquad \rho_n=\tau_n=\frac{q_n}{5},
 \label{eq:complete-gate-expander-scale}
\end{equation}
and run the exact recurrence and complete gate of
\cref{thm:complete-gate-soft-horizon}.  For all sufficiently large $n$,
the RPPR optimum has full support and in fact
\begin{equation}
 \frac{x_{\rho_n,i}^\star}{\sqrt3}>\tau_n
 \qquad(i\in V_n).
 \label{eq:complete-gate-expander-rppr-margin}
\end{equation}
The named execution terminates only after its active face has become all of
$V_n$.

On this family, every explicit original-basis scalar Cholesky response,
including one given the best offline ordering and the final support, requires
\begin{equation}
 \Omega(n^2)=\Omega(q_n^{-2})
 \quad\hbox{persistent scalar cells},
 \qquad
 \Omega(n^3)=\Omega(q_n^{-3})
 \quad\hbox{scalar pivot arithmetic}.
 \label{eq:complete-gate-expander-cholesky-lower}
\end{equation}
Since
\begin{equation}
 \frac1{\rho_n\sqrt{\alpha_n}}=5q_n^{-2}=20n^2,
 \label{eq:complete-gate-expander-product-scale}
\end{equation}
no ordering or ordinary sparse-fill choice makes this explicit exact-response
class product-scale.  The conclusion is exact-real and representation
specific.  It is not a lower bound for the recurrence itself, for all exact
linear algebra, or for compressed, matrix-free, iterative, or approximate
responses, and it proves neither that the logarithm in
\cref{eq:complete-gate-hold-bound} is necessary nor that a non-Cholesky
product-scale implementation is impossible.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Fix $n$ and abbreviate $q=q_n$.  On a $3$-regular graph,
\begin{equation*}
 Q=a(I-\beta P),
 \qquad
 a:=\frac{1+q^2}{2},
 \qquad
 \beta:=\frac{1-q^2}{1+q^2},
 \qquad
 1-\beta=\frac{2q^2}{1+q^2}.
\end{equation*}
The lazy personalized PageRank vector from
\cref{eq:shared-ppr-solution} is therefore
\begin{equation}
 \pi=q^2Q^{-1}e_{s_n}
     =(1-\beta)(I-\beta P)^{-1}e_{s_n}.
 \label{eq:complete-gate-expander-ppr-resolvent}
\end{equation}
Let $u:=n^{-1}\one$.  The constant mode in
\cref{eq:complete-gate-expander-ppr-resolvent} is exactly $u$.  On its
orthogonal complement, symmetry of $P$ and
\cref{eq:complete-gate-expander-gap} give
\begin{align}
 \norm{\pi-u}_2
 &\leq
 \frac{1-\beta}{1-\beta(1-\gamma)}
 \norm{e_{s_n}-u}_2
 \leq\frac{2q^2}{\gamma}.
 \label{eq:complete-gate-expander-ppr-deviation}
\end{align}
Here negative eigenvalues only enlarge the positive denominator, and
$1-\beta(1-\gamma)\geq\gamma$.  Since $1/n=2q$,
\begin{equation}
 \pi_i\geq2q-\frac{2q^2}{\gamma}
 \qquad(i\in V_n).
 \label{eq:complete-gate-expander-ppr-floor}
\end{equation}

The row-sum identity $Q\sqrt3\one=q^2\sqrt3\one$ shows that
\begin{equation}
 x_{\rho} := x^0-\rho\sqrt3\one
 \label{eq:complete-gate-expander-shifted-point}
\end{equation}
solves the all-active RPPR stationarity equation
$Qx_\rho=b-q^2\rho\sqrt3\one$.  Moreover
\begin{align}
 \frac{x_{\rho,i}}{\sqrt3}
 &=\frac{\pi_i}{3}-\rho
 \geq\frac{2q}{3}-\frac{2q^2}{3\gamma}-\frac q5,
 \\
 \frac{x_{\rho,i}}{\sqrt3}-\tau
 &\geq\frac{4q}{15}-\frac{2q^2}{3\gamma}>0
 \label{eq:complete-gate-expander-shift-margin}
\end{align}
whenever $q<2\gamma/5$.  Thus the stationary point is strictly positive and
is the obstacle optimum $x_\rho^\star$, proving
\cref{eq:complete-gate-expander-rppr-margin}.  The initial singleton optimum
is interior because its normalized load has seed entry
$q^2(1/3-q/5)>0$, so all hypotheses of
\cref{thm:complete-gate-soft-horizon} hold.  That theorem gives finite
termination and a terminal safe envelope $\ell_T$, supported on $U_T$, with
\begin{equation*}
 \norm{D^{-1/2}(x_\rho^\star-\ell_T)}_\infty\leq\tau.
\end{equation*}
If $i\notin U_T$, then $\ell_{T,i}=0$, contradicting
\cref{eq:complete-gate-expander-rppr-margin}.  Hence $U_T=V_n$.

It remains to charge the response representation.  We include the standard
separator-to-fill argument for completeness.  Let $X$ be a $2/3$-balanced
vertex separator.  If $\abs X<n/3$, grouping components of $G_n-X$ produces
a union $A$ with $n/3\leq\abs A\leq2n/3$.  For
$f=\mathbf1_A-(\abs A/n)\one$, the spectral gap gives
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\abs{E(A,V_n\setminus A)}}{3}
 =f^\top(I-P)f
 \geq\gamma\norm f_2^2
 \geq\frac{\gamma n}{6}.
 \label{eq:complete-gate-expander-balanced-cut}
\end{equation}
Every cut edge is incident on $X$, so $\abs X\geq\gamma n/6$.  Thus every
balanced separator has order $\Omega(n)$; the standard balanced-separator
characterization of treewidth implies $\operatorname{tw}(G_n)=\Omega(n)$.
Every scalar elimination ordering consequently creates a filled clique
$C$ of order $k=\Omega(n)$.

This fill is numerical, not merely symbolic.  Eliminating a Stieltjes pivot
$r$ performs
\begin{equation}
 q'_{ij}=q_{ij}-q_{ir}q_{rr}^{-1}q_{rj}.
 \label{eq:complete-gate-expander-strict-fill}
\end{equation}
Whenever $i$ and $j$ are both current neighbors of $r$, the two incident
off-diagonals are strictly negative, so the update is strictly negative and
cannot cancel.  Multiplication by the positive regular degree in
$B=DQ=3Q$ changes neither this sign argument nor the fill graph.  The factor
or its fronts therefore store at least $\binom{k}{2}=\Omega(n^2)$ numerical
off-diagonal scalars.  Ordinary scalar elimination of the clique performs at
least
\begin{equation*}
 \sum_{j=1}^{k-1}\binom j2=\binom k3=\Omega(n^3)
\end{equation*}
pair updates, regardless of where other remaining vertices are interleaved.
Substituting $q=1/(2n)$ proves
\cref{eq:complete-gate-expander-cholesky-lower} and the claimed separation
from \cref{eq:complete-gate-expander-product-scale}.
\end{proof}
