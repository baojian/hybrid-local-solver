\begin{abstract}
Accelerated local PageRank methods can reduce the number of iteration-scale
steps while enlarging the degree volume scanned by each step.  This note asks
whether one can flatten the observed bell-shaped active-volume curve: permit
$\bigO(\alpha^{-1/2}\log(1/\epsppr))$ iterations, but keep every active set
at volume $\bigO(1/\epsppr)$.  We first separate conditioning from locality.
Every principal PageRank system has condition number at most $1/\alpha$, and
the exact condition number of a depth-$L$ spider prefix is
$\Theta(1/(\alpha+L^{-2}))$.  Thus the spider obstruction comes from support
growth and edge revisits, not a fictitious local condition number
$\Theta(\alpha^{-2})$.

We then prove the support-certification part of a volume-gated method.  Exact
PPR has a degree-volume-$1/\tau$ superlevel core whose Dirichlet restriction is
$\tau$-accurate in degree-normalized infinity norm.  More importantly, the
$\ell_1$-regularized PageRank solution has support volume at most $1/\rho$ and
is $\rho$-accurate for unregularized PPR.  An arbitrary signed accelerated
candidate on a certified subspace can be converted into a componentwise lower
envelope.  Negative KKT violations at this envelope safely reveal only true
support vertices, while a one-sided global KKT test certifies solution error.
Taking $\rho=\tau=\epsppr/2$ therefore proves a uniform peak-volume cap
$2/\epsppr$ and final PPR error at most $\epsppr$.

The remaining acceleration claim is not closed.  We give two corrections to
the initial proof route.  First, an endpoint-source path forces
$\Omega(\alpha^{-1/2}\log(\sqrt\alpha/\rho))$ one-vertex support expansions,
so restarting after every expansion cannot yield only
$\bigO(\log(1/\epsppr))$ restarts uniformly in $\alpha$.  Second, the
Schur-complement ``shock energy'' lower bound has the wrong direction for a
stability proof.  Even a single endpoint edge shows that zero-padding cannot
carry a zero fixed-face estimate-sequence energy across an admission with no
explicit shock term: after the first enlarged-face step, a strictly positive
fraction of the exact Schur gain remains.  This pointwise obstruction does not
refute cumulative shock packing or newly admitted-volume amortization.  A
modified estimate-center recurrence does admit an exact positive identity:
transporting its center by the restricted-optimum displacement adds exactly
the Schur gain, and the gains telescope.  On endpoint paths an append-only
tridiagonal response represents that transport without materializing its
dense old-prefix displacement; all response applications and active-state
writes are nevertheless charged to the ordinary swept volume.  An exact
summation-by-parts identity further shows that the weighted Schur tail charges
discounted occupancy of the remaining face-optimum gain.  A terminal
one-edge family rules out replacing this occupancy by a universal
$\sqrt\alpha$ multiple of the unweighted Schur telescope.  This does not pack
the signed defect of the original zero-padded momentum or bound the stage
count and swept prefixes.  Separately, an exact rational zero-start path
family with an accuracy-driven $q^{-2}$ discovery radius forces
$\mathfrak V_T=\Omega(q^{-4})$, certifying a factor $q^{-1}$ above its
realized final-volume product.  This certified lower-bound factor equals a
logarithm of the deliberately tiny accuracy, so it does not refute a
soft-order product theorem.  At the constant ratio $\rho=\tau=q/5$, an exact
support argument proves only
$J,\nu_{\rm fin}=\Theta(q^{-1})$, $T\geq J$, and
$\mathfrak V_T=\Omega(q^{-2})=\Omega(\nu_{\rm fin}/q)$.  An exact $q=1/5$
trace additionally shows that the global safe-envelope correction can
suppress a raw frontier violation and that its maximizer moves between old
vertices.  A moving-maximum energy argument nevertheless controls its value
without locating the maximizer: every fixed face triggers an admission or the
terminal certificate within
$O(q^{-1}\log(1/q))$ steps.  Hence
$T=O(q^{-2}\log(1/q))$ and
$\mathfrak V_T=O(q^{-3}\log(1/q))$ for the named literal implementation,
regardless of maximizer switches.  Matching lower bounds and removal of this
logarithm remain open.  For the candidate family $q=1/(16m)$ on $P_m$, the
fixed-full-face residual recurrence has an exact damped cosine-mode
factorization, and exact rational runs verify finite prefixes at
$m\in\{2,4,8\}$.  An independent audit found that the proposed asymptotic
lower proof did not derive the mode-entry coefficients, anti-cancellation,
rapid-front boundary induction, or a uniform unclipped-envelope margin.
Accordingly those runs and roots are retained only as finite and conditional
scaffolding: they prove neither a logarithmic terminal block nor either
sampled power law.  A separate exact replay of the actual $q=1/5$ path shows
that the moving correction can increase across two consecutive held-face
steps and can exceed $q^{-1}$ times the normalized error to the restricted
optimum.  This retires only those two pointwise shortcuts; it proves neither
logarithm removal nor logarithm necessity.  A correction--error bank
$\delta_t+q^{-1}e_t$ absorbs that particular stage-$6$--$7$ spike, but the
same exact run refutes its held-face monotonicity already from stage $9$ to
stage $10$ on $U_4$: the correction decreases while the error-bank increase
is larger.  More strongly, for the full constant family
$\delta_t+cq^{-1}e_t$, the first pair requires
$c\geq2978273417354/42112483166425$ while the second requires
$c\leq95554102960761584/1567701294665491845$.  The intervals are disjoint.
This is another finite pairwise STOP, not an asymptotic obstruction to a
time- or face-dependent coefficient, a longer phase block, or an aggregate
potential.  The two half-line endpoints nevertheless define a canonical
two-phase test.  Squaring that scalar bank and reserving the exact
stage-$8$ restricted-optimum drop pays the literal coefficient switch/reset
strictly; the unsquared bank is not paid by a unit objective-shock charge.
Extending the same squared bank plus reserve through the complete local chain
gives the first exact rational STOP on the fixed-$U_3$ production step
$7\to8^-$; the paid reset decreases the account, but the first fixed-$U_4$
follow-up $8^+\to9$ rises again, and the net stage-$7$--$9$ change is
positive.  Both adjacent-step failures hold throughout the coefficient
rectangle already feasible for the two held pairs.  This remains a finite
local-chain obstruction, not a global stagewise potential result or
logarithm claim.  The smallest tested non-tautological longer repair is a
post-reset recovery reserve sourced only from later fixed-$U_4$ squared-bank
decreases.  It is still insufficient at stage $12$, but at stage $13$ it
first pays the exact stage-$7$--$9$ deficit.  Thus the $7\to13$ endpoint
comparison decreases uniformly over the held-pair-feasible coefficient
rectangle, even though production and follow-up still rise separately.  This
is one finite stage-$7$--$13$ space--time GO, not a face-general closing rule,
multi-admission telescope, or logarithm removal.  A second audit gives a
face-general causal condition rather than a forecast: use the observable score
$\Xi=\delta^2$, credit an exact restricted-optimum drop only after its charged
admission query, credit a score decrease only after the new state exists, and
debit every score increase.  Its one-scalar balance stays nonnegative from the
held stage-$3$ checkpoint through the consecutive canonical admissions at
stages $4$ and $8$ and through stage $12$.  The smallest positive carried
margin occurs after the stage-$8$ follow-up.  If the unused stage-$4$ credit is
discarded, the restarted stage-$8$ block is still negative at stage $11$ and
first closes at stage $12$.  This finite two-admission exact-real
scalar-ledger GO is an audit certificate for the declared debt only, not a
convergence, objective-decrease, or work certificate, uniform recovery
horizon, global potential, or asymptotic result.  A valid telescoping argument nevertheless
bounds all
continuous re-solving work by
$\bigO(\alpha^{-1/2}\log(1/\epsppr))$ accelerated sweeps plus one discrete
term per expansion.  Closing the desired
$\softO(1/(\sqrt\alpha\epsppr))$ work theorem now reduces to a precise
one-sided, projected, expanding-subspace acceleration lemma.  The universal
work bound is explicitly left open.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Note specialization.}
This note additionally assumes that \(G\) is connected and
\(0<\alpha<1\). The shared matrix \(Q\) is then a symmetric nonsingular
Stieltjes matrix:
\begin{equation}
  \alpha I\preceq Q\preceq I,
  \qquad Q_{ij}\leq0\ (i\neq j),
  \qquad Q^{-1}\geq0,
  \qquad QD^{1/2}\one=\alpha D^{1/2}\one.
  \label{eq:Q-properties}
\end{equation}
The shared unregularized point \(x^0\) is nonnegative. The last identity in
\cref{eq:Q-properties} gives
\begin{equation}
  \one^\top\pi=1.
  \label{eq:ppr-mass}
\end{equation}
It is useful to expose the one-sided obstacle structure of the shared RPPR
objective in \cref{eq:shared-rppr-objective}.

\begin{lemma}[Equivalent nonnegative quadratic program]
\label{lem:nonnegative-equivalence}
The unique minimizer \(x^\star(\rho)\), abbreviated here by
\(x_\rho^\star\), is nonnegative and is
equivalently the unique minimizer of
\begin{equation}
  \min_{x\geq0}G_\rho(x),
  \qquad
  G_\rho(x):=\frac12x^\top Qx-c_\rho^\top x,
  \qquad
  c_\rho:=b-\alpha\rho D^{1/2}\one.
  \label{eq:obstacle-QP}
\end{equation}
It satisfies the complementarity system
\begin{equation}
 x_\rho^\star\geq0,
 \qquad
 \kappa_\rho(x_\rho^\star):=Qx_\rho^\star-c_\rho\geq0,
 \qquad
 x_{\rho,i}^\star \kappa_{\rho,i}(x_\rho^\star)=0.
 \label{eq:KKT}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Because the off-diagonal entries of $Q$ are nonpositive,
$\abs{x}^\top Q\abs{x}\leq x^\top Qx$.  Moreover
$b^\top\abs{x}\geq b^\top x$ and the weighted $\ell_1$ term is unchanged.
Thus $F_\rho(\abs{x})\leq F_\rho(x)$.  Strong convexity makes the minimizer
unique, hence nonnegative.  On the nonnegative orthant the weighted
$\ell_1$ term is linear, giving \cref{eq:obstacle-QP};
\cref{eq:KKT} is its KKT system.
\end{proof}

For a vector $v$, write $(v_-)_i:=\max\{-v_i,0\}$.  All componentwise
inequalities below use the ordinary coordinate order.

\section{The spider obstruction is geometric, not spectral}
\label{sec:spider}

The first question in the discussion was whether the local system on a long
spider might have condition number $\Theta(\alpha^{-2})$, thereby erasing the
usual square-root acceleration.  Principal-submatrix interlacing rules this
out immediately.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform restricted conditioning]
\label{lem:principal-conditioning}
For every $U\subseteq V$,
\begin{equation}
  \alpha I_U\preceq Q_{UU}\preceq I_U,
  \qquad
  \kappa_2(Q_{UU})\leq\frac1\alpha.
  \label{eq:principal-kappa}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
$Q_{UU}$ is a principal submatrix of $Q$.  The claim follows from
\cref{eq:Q-properties}, equivalently from Cauchy interlacing.
\end{proof}

The exact spider calculation identifies the scale more sharply.  Let
$G_{B,N}$ have a center $o$ and $B$ disjoint arms of length $N$.  Let $U_L$
contain $o$ and the first $L$ vertices of each arm, where $1\leq L<N$; degrees
are always those of the full spider, so every truncated depth-$L$ vertex has
degree two.

\begin{proposition}[Exact spider-prefix spectrum]
\label{prop:spider-spectrum}
Let $S=D^{-1/2}AD^{-1/2}$.  Then
\begin{equation}
 \lambda_{\max}(S_{U_LU_L})
 =\cos\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2(L+1)}\right).
 \label{eq:spider-lambda}
\end{equation}
Consequently, with the spider-scoped ratio
$\beta_{\mathrm{sp}}:=(1-\alpha)/(1+\alpha)$,
\begin{align}
 \kappa_2(Q_{U_LU_L})
 &=\frac{1+\beta_{\mathrm{sp}}\cos(\pi/(2(L+1)))}
         {1-\beta_{\mathrm{sp}}\cos(\pi/(2(L+1)))}
 \label{eq:spider-kappa-exact}\\
 &=\Theta\!\left(\frac1{\alpha+L^{-2}}\right)
 =\Theta\!\left(\min\left\{L^2,\frac1\alpha\right\}\right).
 \label{eq:spider-kappa-asymptotic}
\end{align}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The Perron eigenvector is invariant under permutations of the arms.  In the
orthonormal radial basis consisting of the center and the uniform vector on
each depth, $S_{U_LU_L}$ becomes a tridiagonal matrix whose first off-diagonal
entry is $1/\sqrt2$ and whose remaining off-diagonal entries are $1/2$.
Its radial eigenvalues are
\[
  \cos\!\left(\frac{(2k-1)\pi}{2(L+1)}\right),
  \qquad 1\leq k\leq L+1.
\]
The nonradial arm-difference modes have strictly smaller spectral radius, so
\cref{eq:spider-lambda} follows.  The spider is bipartite, hence the extreme
restricted normalized-adjacency eigenvalues are the positive and negative of
\cref{eq:spider-lambda}. Substitution into
\cref{eq:shared-pagerank-matrices} gives
\cref{eq:spider-kappa-exact}.  Finally,
$1-\beta_{\mathrm{sp}}\cos(\pi/(2(L+1)))
=\Theta(\alpha+L^{-2})$, whereas the numerator is
bounded above and below by constants.
\end{proof}

Thus $L=\Theta(\alpha^{-1/2})$ already saturates the global condition number
$\Theta(1/\alpha)$.  Optimal polynomial or SOR-type acceleration still needs
only $\Theta(1/\sqrt\alpha)$ sweep-scale iterations.  The difficulty is the
cost of those sweeps.

\subsection{A space--time accounting model}

Write
\begin{equation}
  B:=\Theta(1/\epsppr),
  \qquad L:=\Theta(1/\sqrt\alpha).
  \label{eq:B-L}
\end{equation}
Suppose a FIFO wavefront scans all $B$ arm prefixes through depth $j$ at its
$j$th sweep.  This is a structural accounting model, not a graph-uniform
lower bound for every SOR implementation.  It gives
\begin{equation}
 \vol(S_j)=\Theta(Bj),
 \qquad
 \sum_{j=1}^{L}\vol(S_j)
 =\Theta(BL^2)
 =\Theta\!\left(\frac1{\alpha\epsppr}\right).
 \label{eq:triangular-work}
\end{equation}
Equivalently, a late sweep costs $BL$ and there are $L$ accelerated-scale
sweeps.  Merely splitting a size-$Bj$ sweep into $j$ batches of size $B$ does
not change the sum.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
Dynamics & Peak active volume & Iteration/batch count & Total work \\
\midrule
Growing FIFO prefix & $BL$ & $L$ sweeps & $BL^2$ \\
Same work split into batches & $B$ & $L^2$ batches & $BL^2$ \\
Desired volume-gated method & $B$ & $L\log(1/\epsppr)$ & $BL\log(1/\epsppr)$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The desired method must therefore freeze old interior coordinates or charge
their revisits amortizedly.  A change in iteration labels is insufficient.

\section{The conditional geometric-epoch tradeoff}
\label{sec:epoch-blueprint}

The cleanest algebraic expression of volume flattening uses geometrically
decreasing tolerances
\begin{equation}
  \tau_j=2^{-j}\tau_0,
  \qquad J=\Theta(\log(\tau_0/\tau_J)).
  \label{eq:geometric-tolerances}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Conditional geometric-epoch work]
\label{prop:conditional-epochs}
Suppose that at epoch $j$ an algorithm has a certified region $U_j$ with
\begin{equation}
  \vol(U_j)\leq\frac{C}{\tau_j},
  \label{eq:epoch-volume}
\end{equation}
and that $H=C_0/\sqrt\alpha$ restricted iterations reduce the relevant error
by a constant factor.  If discovery and transition between epochs introduce
no additional full scans, then
\begin{align}
 T&=\bigO\!\left(\frac1{\sqrt\alpha}
                  \log\frac{\tau_0}{\tau_J}\right),
 \label{eq:epoch-iterations}\\
 \mathcal W
 &\leq H\sum_{j=0}^{J}\vol(U_j)
 =\bigO\!\left(\frac1{\sqrt\alpha\,\tau_J}\right).
 \label{eq:epoch-work}
\end{align}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The iteration bound is $H(J+1)$.  Because $1/\tau_j$ grows geometrically,
$\sum_j1/\tau_j=\bigO(1/\tau_J)$, which gives \cref{eq:epoch-work}.
\end{proof}

The proposition is only an accounting implication.  It assumes precisely the
two properties that need proof: a locally discoverable certified region and
stable acceleration as that region grows.

\subsection{Why constant-frequency restarting loses acceleration}

For a degree-$H$ polynomial method on an SPD spectrum $[\alpha,1]$, the best
worst-case block contraction is
\begin{equation}
  \rho_H^\star
  =\frac1{\abs{T_H((1+\alpha)/(1-\alpha))}},
  \label{eq:cheb-block}
\end{equation}
where $T_H$ is the Chebyshev polynomial of the first kind.  If
$H\sqrt\alpha\ll1$, then
\begin{equation}
  1-\rho_H^\star=\Theta(H^2\alpha).
  \label{eq:short-block}
\end{equation}
Repeating such blocks therefore needs
\begin{equation}
  \Omega\!\left(\frac1{H\alpha}\log\frac1\epsppr\right)
  \label{eq:restart-lower}
\end{equation}
total polynomial degree.  In particular, $H=\bigO(1)$ returns to the
nonaccelerated $\alpha^{-1}$ scale.  An accelerated accuracy epoch must retain
momentum for $H=\Omega(1/\sqrt\alpha)$ steps.  This classical polynomial fact
does not by itself resolve support changes, but it rules out constant-step
ordinary restarts as the flattening mechanism.

\section{A canonical exact PPR core}
\label{sec:exact-core}

Before constructing an implementable gate, it is useful to see that a small
accurate region always exists.  Define normalized PPR values
\begin{equation}
  y^0:=D^{-1/2}x^0=D^{-1}\pi
\end{equation}
and the exact superlevel set
\begin{equation}
  U_\tau^0:=\{i:y_i^0>\tau\}.
  \label{eq:exact-core}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Exact superlevel core]
\label{thm:exact-core}
The core in \cref{eq:exact-core} satisfies
\begin{equation}
  \vol(U_\tau^0)\leq\frac1\tau.
  \label{eq:exact-core-volume}
\end{equation}
Let $x^{U}$ solve the Dirichlet restriction
\begin{equation}
  Q_{UU}x_U^U=b_U,
  \qquad x_{U^c}^U=0,
  \qquad U=U_\tau^0.
  \label{eq:dirichlet}
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}
  0\leq x^U\leq x^0,
  \qquad
  \norm{D^{-1/2}(x^0-x^U)}_\infty\leq\tau.
  \label{eq:dirichlet-error}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By \cref{eq:ppr-mass},
$\sum_i d_i y_i^0=1$.  Markov's inequality gives
\cref{eq:exact-core-volume}.  Positivity of $Q_{UU}^{-1}$ and the fact that
$Q_{UU}x_U^0=b_U-Q_{UU^c}x_{U^c}^0\geq b_U$ give
$0\leq x^U\leq x^0$.

Set $e=x^0-x^U$ and $h=D^{-1/2}e$.  For $i\in U$,
$(Qe)_i=0$, hence
\begin{equation}
  h_i=\frac{1-\alpha}{1+\alpha}
      \sum_j\frac{A_{ij}}{d_i}h_j.
  \label{eq:discounted-harmonic}
\end{equation}
On $U^c$, $0\leq h_i=y_i^0\leq\tau$.  If a positive maximum larger than
$\tau$ occurred in $U$, \cref{eq:discounted-harmonic} would make it strictly
smaller than a convex combination of values no larger than that maximum, a
contradiction.  This proves \cref{eq:dirichlet-error}.
\end{proof}

The theorem is existential because $U_\tau^0$ depends on the unknown exact
solution.  It nevertheless shows that a peak volume larger than
$\Theta(1/\epsppr)$ is not information-theoretically required by the output
criterion \cref{eq:note-error}.

\subsection{Why a residual wave can be unnecessary on a spider}

For the center-source spider $G_{B,N}$, direct solution of the radial
recurrence gives
\begin{equation}
  y_o^0
  =\frac{\sqrt\alpha}{B}
    \frac{1+\chi^{2N}}{1-\chi^{2N}},
  \qquad
  \chi:=\frac{1-\sqrt\alpha}{1+\sqrt\alpha}.
  \label{eq:spider-center-value}
\end{equation}
For long arms, $y_o^0\to\sqrt\alpha/B$.  If
$B=1/(4\epsppr)$ and $\alpha<1/16$, then the limiting center value is below
$\epsppr$, and all arm values are smaller.  The true $\epsppr$-important
core is empty even though a residual-based process may launch a wave from the
center.  This example motivates a gate based on solution/KKT importance rather
than momentum residual magnitude.

\section{RPPR supplies an implementable safe core}
\label{sec:rppr-core}

Let $S_\rho:=\supp(x_\rho^\star)$.  The following properties turn fixed
regularization into a localization certificate.

\begin{theorem}[RPPR support and approximation]
\label{thm:rppr-support}
For every $\rho>0$,
\begin{align}
  \vol(S_\rho)&\leq\frac1\rho,
  \label{eq:rppr-volume}\\
  0\leq D^{-1/2}(x^0-x_\rho^\star)&\leq\rho\one.
  \label{eq:rppr-ppr-error}
\end{align}
Moreover, if $\rho'\geq\rho$, then
$x_{\rho'}^\star\leq x_\rho^\star$ and
$S_{\rho'}\subseteq S_\rho$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Regularization-path monotonicity is the source result stated, for example, in
the appendix of \citet{fountoulakis2026}.  We prove the two quantitative
claims.  Let $\zeta_\rho:=b-Qx_\rho^\star$.  On $S_\rho$, KKT gives
$\zeta_{\rho,i}=\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_i}$.  Outside $S_\rho$,
$x_{\rho,i}^\star=0$ and the off-diagonal entries of $Q$ are nonpositive, so
$(Qx_\rho^\star)_i\leq0$ and hence $\zeta_{\rho,i}\geq0$.  The full
$\ell_1$ KKT condition also gives
$\zeta_\rho\leq\alpha\rho D^{1/2}\one$.  Therefore
\begin{align*}
 \alpha\rho\vol(S_\rho)
 &\leq\one^\top D^{1/2}\zeta_\rho\\
 &=\one^\top D^{1/2}b
   -\one^\top D^{1/2}Qx_\rho^\star\\
 &=\alpha-\alpha\one^\top D^{1/2}x_\rho^\star
 \leq\alpha,
\end{align*}
which proves \cref{eq:rppr-volume}.  Finally,
\[
  x^0-x_\rho^\star=Q^{-1}\zeta_\rho,
\]
and \cref{eq:Q-properties} gives
\[
  0\leq D^{-1/2}Q^{-1}\zeta_\rho
  \leq\alpha\rho D^{-1/2}Q^{-1}D^{1/2}\one
  =\rho\one.
\]
\end{proof}

Thus RPPR is not merely a different target: when $\rho$ is chosen on the
desired error scale, it is an internal localization device for unregularized
PPR.

\section{Safe envelopes for signed accelerated candidates}
\label{sec:safe-envelope}

Acceleration may overshoot, so its candidate cannot itself control support.
The next lemma converts any signed restricted candidate into a certified lower
point.

For $U\subseteq S_\rho$, let $x_U^\star$ solve the restricted nonnegative
quadratic program
\begin{equation}
  x_U^\star\in\arg\min_{z_U\geq0}
  \left\{\frac12z_U^\top Q_{UU}z_U-c_{\rho,U}^\top z_U\right\},
  \qquad x_{U^c}^\star=0.
  \label{eq:restricted-QP}
\end{equation}
The Stieltjes obstacle comparison principle gives
\begin{equation}
  0\leq x_U^\star\leq x_\rho^\star.
  \label{eq:restricted-comparison}
\end{equation}
Indeed, $x_{\rho,U}^\star$ is a supersolution of the restricted KKT system
because $U\subseteq S_\rho$ and
$Q_{UU}x_{\rho,U}^\star-c_{\rho,U}
=-Q_{UU^c}x_{\rho,U^c}^\star\geq0$.

\begin{theorem}[Signed candidate to safe lower envelope]
\label{thm:safe-envelope}
Let $z_U\in\R^U$ be arbitrary and define
\begin{equation}
 q_U:=c_{\rho,U}-Q_{UU}z_U,
 \qquad
 \delta_U:=\frac1\alpha
  \norm{D_U^{-1/2}(q_U)_-}_\infty.
 \label{eq:envelope-delta}
\end{equation}
Set
\begin{equation}
 \ell_U:=\left[z_U-\delta_UD_U^{1/2}\one\right]_+,
 \qquad \ell_{U^c}:=0.
 \label{eq:safe-envelope}
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}
  0\leq\ell\leq x_U^\star\leq x_\rho^\star.
  \label{eq:envelope-order}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $q_U^\star:=c_{\rho,U}-Q_{UU}x_U^\star\leq0$ be the restricted KKT
residual.  Since $Q_{UU}^{-1}\geq0$,
\begin{align*}
 x_U^\star-z_U
 &=Q_{UU}^{-1}(q_U-q_U^\star)
 \geq-Q_{UU}^{-1}(q_U)_-.
\end{align*}
The full eigenvector identity in \cref{eq:Q-properties} and the signs of the
cut entries imply
\begin{equation}
 Q_{UU}D_U^{1/2}\one
 =\alpha D_U^{1/2}\one-Q_{UU^c}D_{U^c}^{1/2}\one
 \geq\alpha D_U^{1/2}\one.
\end{equation}
Thus
$Q_{UU}^{-1}D_U^{1/2}\one\leq\alpha^{-1}D_U^{1/2}\one$.
By \cref{eq:envelope-delta},
$Q_{UU}^{-1}(q_U)_-\leq\delta_UD_U^{1/2}\one$, and hence
$z_U-\delta_UD_U^{1/2}\one\leq x_U^\star$.  Taking the positive part preserves
the inequality because $x_U^\star\geq0$.  The last comparison is
\cref{eq:restricted-comparison}.
\end{proof}

The accelerator is therefore free to use signed momentum in $z$; only
$\ell$ is allowed to govern expansion.

\subsection{Safe admission and a one-sided stopping certificate}

\begin{lemma}[Safe admission]
\label{lem:safe-admission}
Let $\ell\leq x_\rho^\star$ be nonnegative.  If $\ell_i=0$ and
\begin{equation}
  \kappa_{\rho,i}(\ell)<0,
  \label{eq:negative-violation}
\end{equation}
then $i\in S_\rho$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
If $i\notin S_\rho$, then $\ell_i=x_{\rho,i}^\star=0$.  Since all
off-diagonal entries in row $i$ of $Q$ are nonpositive and
$\ell\leq x_\rho^\star$,
\[
 \kappa_{\rho,i}(\ell)-\kappa_{\rho,i}(x_\rho^\star)
 =\sum_{j\neq i}Q_{ij}(\ell_j-x_{\rho,j}^\star)\geq0.
\]
KKT gives $\kappa_{\rho,i}(x_\rho^\star)\geq0$, contradicting
\cref{eq:negative-violation}.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[One-sided maximum-principle certificate]
\label{lem:one-sided-certificate}
Let $0\leq\ell\leq x_\rho^\star$.  If
\begin{equation}
  \kappa_{\rho,i}(\ell)\geq-\alpha\tau\sqrt{d_i}
  \qquad\text{for every }i,
  \label{eq:global-certificate}
\end{equation}
then
\begin{equation}
  \norm{D^{-1/2}(x_\rho^\star-\ell)}_\infty\leq\tau.
  \label{eq:certificate-error}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Let $e=x_\rho^\star-\ell\geq0$, and choose $i$ maximizing
$M:=e_i/\sqrt{d_i}$.  If $M=0$ there is nothing to prove.  Otherwise
$x_{\rho,i}^\star>0$, so $\kappa_{\rho,i}(x_\rho^\star)=0$.  The normalized
maximum principle gives
\begin{align*}
 \frac{(Qe)_i}{\sqrt{d_i}}
 &=\frac{1+\alpha}{2}M
   -\frac{1-\alpha}{2}
     \sum_j\frac{A_{ij}}{d_i}\frac{e_j}{\sqrt{d_j}}
 \geq\alpha M.
\end{align*}
On the other hand,
$(Qe)_i=\kappa_{\rho,i}(x_\rho^\star)-\kappa_{\rho,i}(\ell)
=-\kappa_{\rho,i}(\ell)$.  Condition \cref{eq:global-certificate} yields
$\alpha M\leq\alpha\tau$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[PPR accuracy and peak volume]
\label{cor:accuracy-volume}
Suppose a process maintains $U_t\subseteq S_\rho$, constructs $\ell_t$ by
\cref{eq:safe-envelope}, admits only vertices satisfying
\begin{equation}
  \kappa_{\rho,i}(\ell_t)<-\alpha\tau\sqrt{d_i},
  \label{eq:strong-admission}
\end{equation}
and stops when \cref{eq:global-certificate} holds.  Then
\begin{equation}
  \max_t\vol(U_t)\leq\frac1\rho,
  \qquad
  \norm{D^{-1/2}(x^0-\ell_t)}_\infty\leq\rho+\tau.
  \label{eq:gate-main-bound}
\end{equation}
In particular, $\rho=\tau=\epsppr/2$ gives peak volume at most
$2/\epsppr$ and PPR error at most $\epsppr$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Safe admission follows from \cref{lem:safe-admission}, and the volume bound
then follows from \cref{eq:rppr-volume}.  The error bound is the triangle
inequality applied to \cref{eq:rppr-ppr-error} and
\cref{eq:certificate-error}.
\end{proof}

Only $U_t$, its graph boundary, and source coordinates need inspection.  If a
vertex is outside $U_t\cup N(U_t)$, then $(Q\ell_t)_i=0$; if its initial
gradient was strongly negative it would already have been admitted, and
otherwise it automatically satisfies the tolerance in
\cref{eq:global-certificate}.  A boundary check is therefore chargeable to
the incident edges of the current certified set, subject to the usual sparse
source assumption.

\subsection{An exact stagewise gate is correct but not yet fast}

Consider the idealized procedure that begins with $U=\varnothing$, exactly
solves \cref{eq:restricted-QP}, adds every outside vertex satisfying
\cref{eq:strong-admission}, and repeats.  At an exact interior restricted
solution $x_U^\star$, the envelope correction is zero.  If $B$ is the next
batch, $h_B:=\kappa_{\rho,B}(x_U^\star)<0$.  The Schur-complement formula used in
\cref{sec:shock} below shows that the enlarged unconstrained minimizer has
positive correction
\begin{equation}
 w_B=-S_B^{-1}h_B>0,
 \qquad
 w_U=-Q_{UU}^{-1}Q_{UB}w_B\geq0.
 \label{eq:positive-expansion}
\end{equation}
Thus every admitted coordinate is positive at the enlarged restricted
minimizer, and induction justifies the interior claim.  Every batch lies in
$S_\rho$ by safe admission.  If no batch exists, the exact restricted
gradient vanishes on $U$ and the global certificate holds outside $U$.
Consequently the stagewise exact process terminates after at most
$|S_\rho|$ nonempty admissions and satisfies \cref{eq:gate-main-bound}.

This proves correctness and peak-volume flattening.  Exactly re-solving after
every batch can still repeat too much work; acceleration across expansions is
the remaining issue.

\section{A path refutes the logarithmic-restart claim}
\label{sec:path}

The initial proposal hoped to group all support changes into only
$\bigO(\log(1/\epsppr))$ ordinary restarts.  A path rules this out.

Let $P_N=(v_0,v_1,\ldots,v_N)$ be a path with source $s=e_{v_0}$.  Write
$y_i^0=x_i^0/\sqrt{d_i}$.  Solving the recurrence gives
\begin{equation}
 y_i^0
 =\sqrt\alpha\,
   \frac{\chi^i+\chi^{2N-i}}{1-\chi^{2N}}
 \geq\sqrt\alpha\,\chi^i,
 \qquad
 \chi=\frac{1-\sqrt\alpha}{1+\sqrt\alpha}.
 \label{eq:path-solution}
\end{equation}
By \cref{eq:rppr-ppr-error},
\begin{equation}
  \frac{x_{\rho,i}^\star}{\sqrt{d_i}}
  \geq\sqrt\alpha\,\chi^i-\rho.
  \label{eq:path-rppr-lower}
\end{equation}
Define
\begin{equation}
 J_{\mathrm{path}}(\rho):=\left\lfloor
   \frac{\log(\sqrt\alpha/(3\rho))}{-\log\chi}
 \right\rfloor,
 \label{eq:path-radius}
\end{equation}
assuming $3\rho<\sqrt\alpha$ and $N>J_{\mathrm{path}}(\rho)$.

\begin{theorem}[One-coordinate expansion on a path]
\label{thm:path-expansion}
Run the exact safe gate with $\tau=\rho$.  Once
$U_j=\{v_0,\ldots,v_j\}$, every stage with
$j<J_{\mathrm{path}}(\rho)$ admits precisely the
next vertex $v_{j+1}$ and no more distant vertex.  Consequently any method
that restarts after every safe expansion makes at least
\begin{equation}
 \Omega\!\left(
   \frac1{\sqrt\alpha}
   \log\frac{\sqrt\alpha}{\rho}
 \right)
 \label{eq:path-restarts}
\end{equation}
restarts for $0<\alpha\leq1/4$ and sufficiently small $\rho$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $x^{U_j}$ be the exact restricted minimizer.  For $i\geq j+2$, vertex
$v_i$ has no neighbor in $U_j$ and is not the source, so
\begin{equation}
  \kappa_{\rho,i}(x^{U_j})=\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_i}>0.
  \label{eq:path-far-gradient}
\end{equation}
On the other hand, \cref{eq:path-rppr-lower} gives normalized RPPR value at
least $2\rho$ through radius $J_{\mathrm{path}}(\rho)$. Since
$x^{U_j}_{j+1}=0$, the
restricted point is more than $\rho$ away from $x_\rho^\star$ in normalized
infinity norm.  The contrapositive of \cref{lem:one-sided-certificate} implies
that some coordinate has gradient below $-\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_i}$.  The exact
restricted gradient vanishes on $U_j$, while
\cref{eq:path-far-gradient} rules out every $i\geq j+2$.  Hence only
$v_{j+1}$ can violate, proving the first claim.  Finally,
\begin{equation}
  -\log\chi
  =2\operatorname{artanh}(\sqrt\alpha)
  =\Theta(\sqrt\alpha)
\end{equation}
for $\alpha\leq1/4$, which yields \cref{eq:path-restarts}.
\end{proof}

This lower bound is exactly on the accelerated propagation scale.  It does
not refute restart-free continuation; it refutes only the assertion that the
number of support-triggered ordinary restarts is independent of the
$1/\sqrt\alpha$ propagation distance.

\section{Expansion shock: exact identity and corrected direction}
\label{sec:shock}

Let $x_U^\star$ be an interior exact restricted minimizer and let $B$ be a
batch of negative-gradient coordinates.  Define
\begin{equation}
 h_B:=\kappa_{\rho,B}(x_U^\star),
 \qquad
 S_B:=Q_{BB}-Q_{BU}Q_{UU}^{-1}Q_{UB}.
 \label{eq:schur}
\end{equation}
Assume the enlarged optimum is interior, as it is for the negative-gradient
admissions in the exact stagewise process.

\begin{proposition}[Exact expansion gain]
\label{prop:shock}
The improvement in the restricted optimal value is
\begin{equation}
 \Delta_B
 :=G_\rho(x_U^\star)-G_\rho(x_{U\cup B}^\star)
 =\frac12h_B^\top S_B^{-1}h_B.
 \label{eq:shock-exact}
\end{equation}
Moreover,
\begin{equation}
  \alpha I\preceq S_B\preceq I,
  \qquad
  \frac12\norm{h_B}_2^2
  \leq\Delta_B
  \leq\frac1{2\alpha}\norm{h_B}_2^2.
  \label{eq:shock-bounds}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Eliminating the old variables from the enlarged quadratic gives the reduced
problem
$\min_w\{\frac12w^\top S_Bw+h_B^\top w\}$, whose minimizer is
$w=-S_B^{-1}h_B$ and whose gain is \cref{eq:shock-exact}.  The Schur
complement is the minimum of the full quadratic over the eliminated block;
using $\alpha I\preceq Q\preceq I$ gives
$\alpha I\preceq S_B\preceq I$.  Inverting these inequalities yields
\cref{eq:shock-bounds}.
\end{proof}

The lower bound in \cref{eq:shock-bounds} is useful for proving that an
expansion makes genuine progress.  It cannot upper-bound the perturbation of
an accelerated Lyapunov function.  The upper bound may be larger by
$1/\alpha$.  This corrects the earlier proposal to restart only when a sum of
lower shock bounds reaches a fixed energy fraction.

\section{What telescopes: an amortized re-solving lemma}
\label{sec:amortized}

Although the number of expansions can be large, the continuous optimization
work needed to adapt to them admits a clean amortization.

For a safe point $0\leq x\leq x_\rho^\star$, define the one-sided violation
\begin{equation}
  \nu(x):=\norm{D^{-1/2}(\kappa_\rho(x))_-}_\infty.
  \label{eq:nu}
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[Violation to objective gap]
\label{lem:violation-gap}
For every safe point,
\begin{align}
 \norm{D^{-1/2}(x_\rho^\star-x)}_\infty
 &\leq\frac{\nu(x)}\alpha,
 \label{eq:nu-error}\\
 \nu(x)\leq\alpha\sigma
 \quad&\Longrightarrow\quad
 G_\rho(x)-G_\rho(x_\rho^\star)
 \leq\frac{\sigma^2}{2\rho}.
 \label{eq:nu-gap}
\end{align}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The proof of \cref{lem:one-sided-certificate} with
$\tau=\nu(x)/\alpha$ gives \cref{eq:nu-error}.  Put
$e=x_\rho^\star-x$.  Because $e$ is supported on $S_\rho$ and complementarity
annihilates the linear term,
\begin{equation}
 G_\rho(x)-G_\rho(x_\rho^\star)=\frac12e^\top Qe
 \leq\frac12\norm{e}_2^2.
\end{equation}
If $\nu(x)\leq\alpha\sigma$, then $e_i\leq\sigma\sqrt{d_i}$ by
\cref{eq:nu-error}.  Hence
\[
 \norm{e}_2^2
 \leq\sigma^2\vol(S_\rho)
 \leq\frac{\sigma^2}\rho,
\]
using \cref{eq:rppr-volume}.
\end{proof}

\begin{assumption}[Restricted accelerated contraction]
\label{ass:contraction}
On every fixed restricted QP, $k$ full restricted iterations reduce objective
error by a factor at most $\exp(-k/H)$, where
$H=C/\sqrt\alpha$ and $C$ is universal.
\end{assumption}

Strongly convex accelerated proximal gradient has this fixed-subspace
property.  The assumption says nothing about continuing momentum across a
subspace change.

\begin{theorem}[Amortized re-solving]
\label{thm:amortized}
Let $\sigma_{j-1}=2\sigma_j$ be geometric accuracy epochs and put
\begin{equation}
  E_j:=\frac{\sigma_j^2}{2\rho}.
\end{equation}
Suppose epoch $j$ begins at a safe point with
$\nu(x)\leq\alpha\sigma_{j-1}$, and restore restricted objective error to
$E_j$ after every support expansion.  Under \cref{ass:contraction}, the total
number of restricted iterations over all epochs down to $\sigma_J=\tau$ is
\begin{equation}
 K
 \leq\bigO\!\left(
       \frac1{\sqrt\alpha}\log\frac{\sigma_0}{\tau}
      \right)+N_{\mathrm{exp}},
 \label{eq:amortized-iterations}
\end{equation}
where $N_{\mathrm{exp}}$ is the number of nonempty expansion events.  Since
$\vol(U)\leq1/\rho$, full-sweep work obeys
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal W
 \leq\bigO\!\left(
       \frac1{\rho\sqrt\alpha}\log\frac{\sigma_0}{\tau}
      \right)
      +\bigO\!\left(\frac{N_{\mathrm{exp}}}{\rho}\right).
 \label{eq:amortized-work}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By \cref{lem:violation-gap}, the global objective gap at the start of epoch
$j$ is at most $4E_j$.  Restricted optimal values decrease monotonically
toward the global value, so all expansion gains $\Delta_{j,\ell}$ in that
epoch telescope:
\begin{equation}
  \sum_\ell\Delta_{j,\ell}\leq4E_j.
  \label{eq:shock-telescope}
\end{equation}
If the pre-expansion restricted error is at most $E_j$, then immediately after
an optimum shift of size $\Delta_{j,\ell}$ it is at most
$E_j+\Delta_{j,\ell}$.  By \cref{ass:contraction}, restoring it to $E_j$
takes at most
\begin{equation}
 k_{j,\ell}
 =\left\lceil H\log\left(1+\frac{\Delta_{j,\ell}}{E_j}\right)\right\rceil.
\end{equation}
Therefore
\begin{align*}
 \sum_\ell k_{j,\ell}
 &\leq H\sum_\ell
       \log\left(1+\frac{\Delta_{j,\ell}}{E_j}\right)
       +N_{\mathrm{exp},j}\\
 &\leq\frac{H}{E_j}\sum_\ell\Delta_{j,\ell}
       +N_{\mathrm{exp},j}
 \leq4H+N_{\mathrm{exp},j}.
\end{align*}
Summing over $\bigO(\log(\sigma_0/\tau))$ epochs proves
\cref{eq:amortized-iterations}.  Multiplying full iterations by the uniform
volume cap proves \cref{eq:amortized-work}.
\end{proof}

This theorem improves over independently re-solving every enlarged subspace:
all continuous shock sizes telescope.  The unresolved term is discrete.  In
the worst case $N_{\mathrm{exp}}$ may be as large as the support size, and the
proof above charges a full current-support scan to every such event.

\section{The correct open lemma: one-sided expanding-subspace acceleration}
\label{sec:open-lemma}

The path in \cref{sec:path} shows that support may expand on nearly every
accelerated-scale propagation step.  The desired algorithm must therefore
continue through expansions, or charge each expansion only to newly exposed
edges.

A concrete candidate zero-pads its history when $U_t$ expands and otherwise
uses the standard strongly convex momentum
\begin{align}
 y^{(t)}
 &=x^{(t)}+\beta_{\mathrm F}\bigl(x^{(t)}-x^{(t-1)}\bigr),
 \qquad
 \beta_{\mathrm F}:=\frac{1-\sqrt\alpha}{1+\sqrt\alpha},
 \label{eq:dynamic-y}\\
 z^{(t+1)}
 &=\Pi_{\R_+^{U_t}}
   \bigl(y^{(t)}-(Qy^{(t)}-c_\rho)\bigr),
 \label{eq:dynamic-step}
\end{align}
where vectors are zero outside $U_t$.  Construct $\ell^{(t+1)}$ from
$z^{(t+1)}$ by \cref{eq:envelope-delta,eq:safe-envelope}, set
$x^{(t+1)}:=z^{(t+1)}$, and then set
\begin{equation}
 U_{t+1}:=U_t\cup
 \left\{i:
 \kappa_{\rho,i}(\ell^{(t+1)})
 <-\alpha\tau\sqrt{d_i}
 \right\}.
 \label{eq:dynamic-gate}
\end{equation}
The proved gate invariants hold regardless of whether the following
conjecture is true:
\begin{equation}
 \ell^{(t)}\leq x_\rho^\star,
 \qquad
 U_t\subseteq S_\rho,
 \qquad
 \vol(U_t)\leq\frac1\rho.
 \label{eq:dynamic-invariants}
\end{equation}

The next exact calculation rules out the first, shock-free way of importing a
fixed-subspace estimate sequence.  Let an energy on a face be
\emph{fixed-face exact} if it is nonnegative, dominates the restricted
objective gap, and vanishes at an exact restricted minimizer with zero
momentum.  Standard strongly convex estimate-sequence energies have these
three properties.

\begin{proposition}[One edge forbids zero-shock continuation]
\label{prop:one-edge-zero-shock-fails}
Fix $0<\alpha<1$, let
\begin{equation}
 a:=\frac{1+\alpha}{2},
 \qquad
 \eta:=\frac{1-\alpha}{2},
 \qquad
 0<r<\frac{1-\alpha}{3+\alpha},
 \qquad
 \rho=\tau=r.
 \label{eq:one-edge-parameters}
\end{equation}
On the endpoint-source edge $P_1=(v_0,v_1)$ with $s=e_{v_0}$, initialize one
stage of \cref{eq:dynamic-y,eq:dynamic-step,eq:dynamic-gate} at
\begin{equation}
 U_t=\{v_0\},
 \qquad
 x^{(t-1)}=x^{(t)}=(u,0),
 \qquad
 u:=\frac{\alpha(1-r)}{a}.
 \label{eq:one-edge-old-state}
\end{equation}
This is the exact restricted minimizer with zero momentum.  The safe gate
admits precisely $B=\{v_1\}$, and the first subsequent enlarged-face step has
strictly positive objective error
\begin{equation}
 G_\rho(x^{(t+2)})-G_\rho(x_{\{v_0,v_1\}}^\star)
 =\alpha\eta^2m^2
 =\frac{2\eta^2}{a}\,\Delta_B>0,
 \qquad
 m:=\frac{\eta(1-r)}a-r,
 \label{eq:one-edge-positive-remainder}
\end{equation}
where
$\Delta_B=\alpha a m^2/2$ is the exact expansion gain.  Consequently no
shock-free one-expansion inequality
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal E_{t+2}^{U_t\cup B}
 \leq\theta\mathcal E_{t+1}^{U_t}
 \label{eq:zero-shock-inequality}
\end{equation}
can hold universally: for every fixed-face exact energy and every finite
$\theta$, this run violates \cref{eq:zero-shock-inequality}.  Any valid
continuation inequality must include an explicit optimum-shift/shock term (or
an equivalent transported-state term).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Both path degrees equal one and
\begin{equation}
 Q=\begin{pmatrix}a&-\eta\\-\eta&a\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 c_\rho=\alpha\begin{pmatrix}1-r\\-r\end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
The point in \cref{eq:one-edge-old-state} solves the one-coordinate
restricted problem.  Its envelope correction is zero, while its outside KKT
value is
\begin{equation}
 h:=\kappa_{\rho,1}(u,0)
 =-\eta u+\alpha r=-\alpha m.
\end{equation}
The parameter range in \cref{eq:one-edge-parameters} is exactly the strict
inequality $m>r$, so $h<-\alpha\tau$ and the gate admits $v_1$.  There is no
other outside vertex.  In particular $m>0$, and the full restricted optimum
is interior.  Its Schur complement and correction from $(u,0)$ are
\begin{equation}
 S=a-\frac{\eta^2}{a}=\frac\alpha a,
 \qquad
 x_{\{v_0,v_1\}}^\star-(u,0)=(\eta m,am).
 \label{eq:one-edge-optimum-shift}
\end{equation}
Hence \cref{prop:shock} gives
$\Delta_B=h^2/(2S)=\alpha a m^2/2$.

The restricted step preceding admission leaves the exact old-face point
unchanged, so $x^{(t+1)}=(u,0)$.  Zero-padding also leaves the next momentum
point equal to $(u,0)$.  On the enlarged face,
\begin{equation}
 Q(u,0)-c_\rho=(0,-\alpha m),
 \qquad
 x^{(t+2)}=(u,\alpha m),
\end{equation}
and the projection is inactive.  Since $a-\alpha=\eta$, the oriented error is
$x_{\{v_0,v_1\}}^\star-x^{(t+2)}=\eta m(1,1)$.  Finally
$Q(1,1)^\top=\alpha(1,1)^\top$, so its quadratic objective gap is
$\alpha\eta^2m^2$, proving \cref{eq:one-edge-positive-remainder}.  Every
fixed-face exact old energy is zero at \cref{eq:one-edge-old-state}, whereas
the enlarged energy dominates this positive gap.  Thus
\cref{eq:zero-shock-inequality} is impossible.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact scope and charged work.}
The proposition refutes only a pointwise carryover with no shock term.  It
does not refute an inequality containing $\Delta_B$, the telescoping identity
\cref{eq:shock-telescope}, a multistep transported potential, or the
cumulative newly admitted-volume target \cref{eq:new-volume-charge}.  On this
single edge, a direct materialized implementation charges constant work for
the old-face residual/envelope check, the cut-edge gate evaluation and
admission, two zero-padding state writes, the two-coordinate enlarged
recurrence, and its two-coordinate persistent state; both old and new degree
volumes are one.  This prefix requires no terminal output, and materializing
one would cost two coordinate writes.  Thus the example gives no asymptotic
work lower bound.  On a longer prefix,
the displayed full restricted recurrence, envelope maximum, and boundary
test must still charge every old-face read they actually perform; an
incremental residual or response representation may avoid such reads, but
must name and charge its update, state, query, validation, and output costs.

\subsection{A transported estimate center gives an exact Schur ledger on paths}
\label{sec:transported-center}

There is a first positive continuation statement if the estimate-sequence
center, rather than only the primal iterates, is transported by the exact
restricted-optimum displacement.  This is a modified recurrence at an
expansion: it is not a proof for the literal zero-padded momentum in
\cref{eq:dynamic-y}.  The distinction is visible in an exact face-change
identity below.

Put $q:=\sqrt\alpha$.  On a fixed face $U$, let
$x_U^\star$ be its interior restricted optimum and define
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal E_U(x,v)
 :=G_\rho(x)-G_\rho(x_U^\star)
   +\frac{\alpha}{2}\norm{v-x_U^\star}_2^2,
 \qquad x\in\R_+^U,\quad v\in\R^U.
 \label{eq:transported-energy}
\end{equation}
The estimate center corresponding to two consecutive primal iterates is
\begin{equation}
 v=x+\frac{1-q}{q}(x-x^-),
 \qquad
 y=\frac{x+qv}{1+q}
   =x+\frac{1-q}{1+q}(x-x^-).
 \label{eq:estimate-center}
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[Fixed-face estimate-center contraction]
\label{lem:fixed-face-estimate-center}
Let
\begin{align}
 x^+&=\Pi_{\R_+^U}\bigl(y-(Q_{UU}y-c_{\rho,U})\bigr),
 \label{eq:center-prox-step}\\
 v^+&=x^++\frac{1-q}{q}(x^+-x).
 \label{eq:center-update}
\end{align}
Then
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal E_U(x^+,v^+)\leq(1-q)\mathcal E_U(x,v).
 \label{eq:fixed-face-center-contraction}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Write $p=x^+$ and $g=y-p$.  Smoothness, $\alpha$-strong convexity, and the
projection optimality condition give, for every $z\in\R_+^U$,
\begin{equation}
 G_\rho(p)-G_\rho(z)
 \leq \langle g,y-z\rangle-\frac12\norm{g}_2^2
      -\frac{\alpha}{2}\norm{y-z}_2^2.
 \label{eq:prox-three-point}
\end{equation}
Take the convex combination of \cref{eq:prox-three-point} with weight
$1-q$ at $z=x$ and weight $q$ at $z=x_U^\star$.  If
$w:=x-x_U^\star$, $u:=v-x_U^\star$, and
\begin{equation*}
 A:=(1-q)u+q(y-x_U^\star),
\end{equation*}
then \cref{eq:center-update} is
$v^+-x_U^\star=A-g/q$.  After adding its squared norm, all terms containing
$g$ cancel and the remaining norm identity is
\begin{align*}
 &\norm{A}_2^2
 -(1-q)\norm{y-x}_2^2-q\norm{y-x_U^\star}_2^2\\
 &\qquad=(1-q)\norm{u}_2^2
 -\frac{q(1-q)}{1+q}\norm{u-w}_2^2
 \leq(1-q)\norm{u}_2^2.
\end{align*}
Since $q^2=\alpha$, this proves
\cref{eq:fixed-face-center-contraction}.
\end{proof}

Now let $U^+:=U\cup B$ be one safe interior expansion, zero-pad old-face
vectors into $U^+$, and put
\begin{equation}
 d:=x_{U^+}^\star-x_U^\star.
 \label{eq:face-optimum-shift}
\end{equation}
The block optimality equations imply
$Q_{U^+U^+}d=-(0,h_B)$ and
$\Delta_B=\frac12d^\top Q_{U^+U^+}d$.

\begin{proposition}[Exact zero-padding defect and transported cancellation]
\label{prop:transported-schur-ledger}
For every $x\in\R_+^U$ and $v\in\R^U$,
\begin{align}
 \mathcal E_{U^+}(x,v)
 &=\mathcal E_U(x,v)+\Delta_B
   +\frac\alpha2\norm{d}_2^2
   -\alpha\langle v-x_U^\star,d\rangle,
 \label{eq:zero-padded-center-defect}\\
 \mathcal E_{U^+}(x,v+d)
 &=\mathcal E_U(x,v)+\Delta_B.
 \label{eq:transported-center-identity}
\end{align}
Consequently, if a fixed-face step is followed by a safe expansion and the
estimate center is changed from $v^+$ to $v^++d$, then
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal E_{U^+}(x^+,v^++d)
 \leq(1-q)\mathcal E_U(x,v)+\Delta_B.
 \label{eq:one-expansion-transported-energy}
\end{equation}
For a nested execution, with $\Delta_t=0$ on a nonexpanding step,
\begin{align}
 \mathcal E_{U_T}(x_T,v_T)
 &\leq(1-q)^T\mathcal E_{U_0}(x_0,v_0)
   +\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}(1-q)^{T-1-t}\Delta_t,
 \label{eq:weighted-schur-ledger}\\
 \sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\Delta_t
 &=G_\rho(x_{U_0}^\star)-G_\rho(x_{U_T}^\star).
 \label{eq:transported-shock-telescope}
\end{align}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Let $e:=x-x_U^\star$, zero-padded on $B$.  Since
$Q_{U^+U^+}d=-(0,h_B)$, one has $e^\top Q_{U^+U^+}d=0$.  Expanding the
quadratic gap about $x_{U^+}^\star=x_U^\star+d$ therefore gives
\begin{equation*}
 G_\rho(x)-G_\rho(x_{U^+}^\star)
 =G_\rho(x)-G_\rho(x_U^\star)+\Delta_B.
\end{equation*}
Expanding the center norm gives \cref{eq:zero-padded-center-defect}; replacing
$v$ by $v+d$ gives \cref{eq:transported-center-identity}.  Apply
\cref{lem:fixed-face-estimate-center} before the expansion to obtain
\cref{eq:one-expansion-transported-energy}, then iterate.  Finally, each
$\Delta_t$ is exactly the drop between two consecutive restricted optimal
values, proving \cref{eq:transported-shock-telescope}.
\end{proof}

The unweighted telescope in \cref{eq:transported-shock-telescope} does not
by itself give the factor $q$ needed to absorb every late shock into one
accelerated contraction.  The exact summation-by-parts formula identifies the
missing quantity.  Put $\vartheta:=1-q$ and, for a fixed terminal face
$U_T$, define the remaining face-optimum gain
\begin{equation}
 H_t:=G_\rho(x_{U_t}^\star)-G_\rho(x_{U_T}^\star)
     =\sum_{s=t}^{T-1}\Delta_s.
 \label{eq:remaining-face-gain}
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[Weighted-shock occupancy identity]
\label{lem:weighted-shock-occupation}
For every nested transported-center execution,
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\vartheta^{T-1-t}\Delta_t
 =\vartheta^{T-1}H_0
  +q\sum_{t=1}^{T-1}\vartheta^{T-1-t}H_t.
 \label{eq:weighted-shock-occupation}
\end{equation}
Consequently the weighted shock tail is not, in general, bounded by
$Cq\sum_t\Delta_t$ for a universal constant $C$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Since $\Delta_t=H_t-H_{t+1}$ and $H_T=0$, summation by parts gives
\begin{align*}
 \sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\vartheta^{T-1-t}(H_t-H_{t+1})
 &=\vartheta^{T-1}H_0
   +\sum_{t=1}^{T-1}
     \bigl(\vartheta^{T-1-t}-\vartheta^{T-t}\bigr)H_t,
\end{align*}
which is \cref{eq:weighted-shock-occupation} because
$1-\vartheta=q$.  The failure of the proposed universal collapse is made
gate-compatible and terminal in \cref{prop:terminal-edge-no-q-packing}.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[A terminal edge has no universal $q$-scaled shock packing]
\label{prop:terminal-edge-no-q-packing}
For $0<q<1$, set
\begin{equation}
 \alpha=q^2,
 \qquad
 \beta_{\rm edge}:=\frac{1-q^2}{1+q^2},
 \qquad
 \rho=\tau:=\frac{2\beta_{\rm edge}}{2\beta_{\rm edge}+5}.
 \label{eq:terminal-edge-parameters}
\end{equation}
On the endpoint edge of \cref{prop:one-edge-zero-shock-fails}, start at the
exact singleton optimum with its exact estimate center.  Apply the exact
all-violations gate after a stationary old-face step, transport the center
on admission, and apply one enlarged-face step.  This is a terminal
exact-real execution with
\begin{equation}
 T=2,\qquad J=1,\qquad \nu_{\rm fin}=2,\qquad
 \mathfrak V_T=1+2=3.
 \label{eq:terminal-edge-stage-ledger}
\end{equation}
Its only shock is $\Delta_0>0$, and hence
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}(1-q)^{T-1-t}\Delta_t}
      {q\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\Delta_t}
 =\frac{1-q}{q}.
 \label{eq:no-q-shock-packing}
\end{equation}
Thus no constant independent of $\alpha$ proves the proposed
$Cq$-scaled collapse, even after requiring a terminal one-sided certificate.
This does not refute a bound with the occupancy term in
\cref{eq:weighted-shock-occupation}, an explicit terminal-accuracy floor, or
a different potential.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Use $a=(1+q^2)/2$ and $\eta=(1-q^2)/2$, and let $m$ be the quantity in
\cref{eq:one-edge-positive-remainder}.  Since
$\beta_{\rm edge}=\eta/a$,
\cref{eq:terminal-edge-parameters} gives
\begin{equation}
 m=\beta_{\rm edge}(1-\rho)-\rho=\frac32\rho.
 \label{eq:terminal-edge-m}
\end{equation}
The singleton optimum is $u=\alpha(1-\rho)/a$, and the outside KKT value is
$h=-\alpha m< -\alpha\tau$.  The exact gate therefore admits the other
endpoint.  The Schur gain and optimum displacement are
\begin{equation*}
 \Delta_0=\frac{\alpha a m^2}{2}>0,
 \qquad
 d=(\eta m,am).
\end{equation*}
After transporting the center, the enlarged-face state is
$x=(u,0)$ and $v=x_{\{v_0,v_1\}}^\star=x+d$.  Thus
$y=x+qd/(1+q)$, while
\begin{equation*}
 Qy-c_\rho=\left(0,\frac{h}{1+q}\right)^\top.
\end{equation*}
The projection is inactive.  At the resulting $x^+$, both KKT coordinates
are exactly
\begin{equation}
 \kappa_{\rho,0}(x^+)=\kappa_{\rho,1}(x^+)
 =\frac{\eta h}{1+q}
 =-\frac{\eta\alpha m}{1+q}.
 \label{eq:terminal-edge-final-kkt}
\end{equation}
They are negative, so the envelope correction is zero and $\ell=x^+$.
Moreover,
$\eta m/(1+q)=3(1-q)\rho/4<\rho$.  Hence
\cref{eq:global-certificate} holds strictly after this
one enlarged-face step.  There is one singleton sweep before admission and
one two-vertex sweep afterward, proving
\cref{eq:terminal-edge-stage-ledger}.  With
$\Delta_1=0$, the weighted ledger is $(1-q)\Delta_0$, which proves
\cref{eq:no-q-shock-packing}.

For the implicit endpoint-path implementation, this finite execution has
the following fully charged eleven-coordinate vector in the shared order:
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 &(C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},C_{\rm rec},
 C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit})\\
 &\qquad=(O(2),O(2),1,0,O(5),O(5),O(6),
             O(2),O(2),O(5),\Theta(3)).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:terminal-edge-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Here the one terminal certificate and two-coordinate sparse vector share the
single external return stage.  In particular, this constant execution is an
analytical packing obstruction, not an asymptotic work lower bound.
\end{proof}

Equation \cref{eq:zero-padded-center-defect} isolates what remains open for
the literal recurrence \cref{eq:dynamic-y}: the signed cross term has no
proved cumulative packing.  Transporting the center cancels that term, but a
literal coordinate update by the displacement $d$ would touch every old
coordinate even for one endpoint-path admission, because the inverse of an
irreducible path principal matrix is strictly positive.  The next proposition shows
that this dense write is avoidable on a path, provided the response work is
reported rather than declared free.

As an exact rational check, take the edge parameters
$\alpha=1/4$ and $\rho=\tau=1/10$ from
\cref{prop:one-edge-zero-shock-fails}.  Then
\begin{equation*}
 q=\frac12,\quad
 u=\frac9{25},\quad
 m=\frac{11}{25},\quad
 d=\left(\frac{33}{200},\frac{11}{40}\right),\quad
 \Delta_B=\frac{121}{8000}.
\end{equation*}
The transported pre-step energy is exactly $\Delta_B$, and the next
fixed-face energy is $121/32000$, below
$(1-q)\Delta_B=121/16000$.  By contrast, zero-padding the center gives
$4477/160000=\Delta_B+2057/160000$, exactly the additional displacement term
in \cref{eq:zero-padded-center-defect}.

Let $U_j=\{v_0,\ldots,v_j\}$ be a prefix of an ambient endpoint path and put
$\gamma_i:=\eta/\sqrt{d_{i-1}d_i}$ for the edge
$(v_{i-1},v_i)$, where $\eta=(1-\alpha)/2$.  The append-only
$LDL^\top$ response stores
\begin{align}
 \sigma_0&=a,& z_0&=c_{\rho,0},
 \notag\\
 \lambda_i&=-\frac{\gamma_i}{\sigma_{i-1}},&
 \sigma_i&=a-\frac{\gamma_i^2}{\sigma_{i-1}},&
 z_i&=c_{\rho,i}-\lambda_i z_{i-1},
 \label{eq:path-append-ldl}
\end{align}
where $a=(1+\alpha)/2$.  Positivity of every principal matrix gives
$\sigma_i>0$.  The current boundary value, next Schur pivot, and shock are
\begin{equation}
 x_{U_j,j}^\star=\frac{z_j}{\sigma_j},
 \qquad
 h_{j+1}=-\gamma_{j+1}\frac{z_j}{\sigma_j}-c_{\rho,j+1},
 \qquad
 S_{j+1}=\sigma_{j+1},
 \qquad
 \Delta_{j+1}=\frac{h_{j+1}^2}{2\sigma_{j+1}}.
 \label{eq:path-schur-query}
\end{equation}
One reverse pass recovers the current restricted optimum via
\begin{equation}
 x_{U_j,j}^\star=\frac{z_j}{\sigma_j},
 \qquad
 x_{U_j,i}^\star=\frac{z_i}{\sigma_i}
                         -\lambda_{i+1}x_{U_j,i+1}^\star.
 \label{eq:path-response-apply}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Fully charged implicit transport on an endpoint path]
\label{prop:path-implicit-transport-ledger}
Consider any finite exact-real execution of
\cref{eq:center-prox-step,eq:center-update} on endpoint prefixes, with at most
one safe next-vertex admission after each step.  The latter is automatic:
outside a prefix, only its immediate successor can have a negative KKT value.
Store the centered momentum
$w:=v-x_U^\star$ and the response records in
\cref{eq:path-append-ldl}.  During a fixed-face sweep, apply
\cref{eq:path-response-apply}, form $v=x_U^\star+w$ and the point $y$ in
\cref{eq:estimate-center}, and after the proximal step store
$w^+:=v^+-x_U^\star$.  At an admission, append the new factor record,
one zero primal coordinate, and one zero coordinate to $w$.  This represents
exactly $v\leftarrow v+d$:
the old entries of $w$ are unchanged and the new entry is zero.  Thus no
dense optimum-shift vector is materialized.

Let $J$ be the number of admissions,
$\nu_{\rm fin}:=\vol(U_T)$, $n_{\rm fin}:=|U_T|$, and
\begin{equation}
 \mathfrak V_T:=\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\vol(U_t).
 \label{eq:path-transport-swept-volume}
\end{equation}
Cache each exposed path incidence once, perform every response application by
\cref{eq:path-response-apply}, and emit only one terminal certificate and the
terminal safe-envelope sparse vector in one external return stage.  In the
shared eleven-coordinate order, this
implementation has
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 (&C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},C_{\rm rec},
 C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit})\\
 =\bigl(&O(\nu_{\rm fin}),O(J+1),1,0,
 O(\mathfrak V_T+\nu_{\rm fin}),
 O(\mathfrak V_T+\nu_{\rm fin}),
 O(\mathfrak V_T+J+\nu_{\rm fin}),\\
 &O(\nu_{\rm fin}),O(\nu_{\rm fin}),
 O(\mathfrak V_T+n_{\rm fin}),
 \Theta(n_{\rm fin}+1)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:path-transport-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
In particular, the work specific to support expansion---new incidence
exposure, the factor/Schur append and query, admission control, and the two
zero state appends---obeys
\begin{equation}
 C_{\rm exp}^{\rm inc}
 =O\!\left(\sum_t\vol(U_{t+1}\setminus U_t)\right).
 \label{eq:path-transport-new-volume-charge}
\end{equation}
Every old-face recurrence read, response application, envelope maximum,
state write, and terminal validation remains present in the
$\mathfrak V_T$ or $\nu_{\rm fin}$ coordinates of
\cref{eq:path-transport-eleven-vector}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For $i\geq j+2$, a safe envelope supported on $U_j$ has no incident active
neighbor and no source load, so its outside KKT value is
$\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_i}>0$.  Hence only $v_{j+1}$ can be admitted.  Moreover,
if its KKT value at the safe envelope is negative, then its value at
$x_{U_j}^\star$ is no larger, because
$x_{U_j}^\star$ dominates the envelope and the cut row is nonpositive.
The Stieltjes correction is therefore positive, so the restricted optima
remain interior by induction.

Appending one row and column to a tridiagonal $LDL^\top$ factorization gives
\cref{eq:path-append-ldl}.  The last component of the triangular solve is
$z_j/\sigma_j$, and the Schur complement of the old prefix is
$\sigma_{j+1}$, proving \cref{eq:path-schur-query}; back substitution gives
\cref{eq:path-response-apply}.  If $v$ is transported to $v+d$ while the
response optimum changes from $x_U^\star$ to $x_U^\star+d$, then
$(v+d)-(x_U^\star+d)=w$ on the old prefix.  Both transported vectors have new
coordinate $d_{j+1}$, so the new centered coordinate is zero.

Each factor append, Schur query, state append, and boundary-gate decision uses
constant exact-cell work.  The new adjacency list costs its ambient degree.
Summing those charges proves \cref{eq:path-transport-new-volume-charge}.
Each fixed-face step scans the current prefix for the proximal recurrence and
the lower-envelope residual, applies the response in one reverse pass, and
writes the current primal and centered-momentum state; these operations sum
to $O(\mathfrak V_T)$.  A final cached-row validation and materialization cost
$O(\nu_{\rm fin})$, and the sparse output contains $n_{\rm fin}$ cells.
The terminal certificate and vector share the one reported external
interaction stage; their $n_{\rm fin}+1$ emitted cells are separate from the
candidate-state materialization already charged in $C_{\rm mat}$.
The stored path, factors, and two active vectors use $O(\nu_{\rm fin})$
persistent cells; a full streamed-response scratch buffer gives the stated
transient bound.  This accounts for every coordinate in
\cref{eq:path-transport-eleven-vector}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact scope.}
The new statements are exact-real, endpoint-path results for the RPPR
restricted quadratic.  They neither pack the signed defect in
\cref{eq:zero-padded-center-defect} for the original zero-padded recurrence
nor bound $T$: a Schur shock occurring late in
\cref{eq:weighted-schur-ledger} need not have decayed.  Consequently they do
not prove \cref{eq:desired-continuation} or a graph-uniform work theorem.
They do show that on a path the analytical Schur term telescopes and that its
transport need not add an old-prefix materialization charge beyond the fully
reported recurrence/response sweeps.  No floating-point or bit-complexity
claim is made.  Only after a terminal one-sided certificate, with
$\rho=\tau=\epsppr/2$, does \cref{cor:accuracy-volume} convert the RPPR output
to the note-scoped PPR guarantee.

\subsection{A zero-start path can force an accuracy-driven swept-prefix factor}
\label{sec:path-swept-separation}

The path ledger also has an exact lower-bound regime.  The construction below
does not prove the numerically observed separation when $\rho=\tau=q/5$.
Instead, it chooses a much smaller rational accuracy so that the mandatory
support-discovery distance itself is $q^{-2}$.  This gives a clean first
separation for the literal full-prefix implementation, while also showing why
it is not yet the desired soft-order obstruction.

\begin{proposition}[Zero-start accuracy-driven swept-prefix separation]
\label{prop:path-zero-start-swept-separation}
Fix an integer $n\geq2$ and put
\begin{equation}
 q_n:=\frac1n,
 \qquad \alpha_n:=q_n^2,
 \qquad \chi_n:=\frac{1-q_n}{1+q_n},
 \qquad L_n:=n^2,
 \qquad N_n:=L_n+1,
 \label{eq:path-swept-parameters-a}
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}
 \rho_n=\tau_n:=\frac{q_n}{3}\chi_n^{L_n},
 \qquad \epspprinst{n}:=2\rho_n.
 \label{eq:path-swept-parameters-b}
\end{equation}
On the endpoint-seeded path
$P_{N_n}=(v_0,\ldots,v_{N_n})$, use ambient path degrees and initialize the
internally gated transported-center execution at
\begin{equation}
 U_0=\{v_0\},
 \qquad x^{(0)}=v^{(0)}=0.
 \label{eq:path-swept-zero-start}
\end{equation}
At every stage perform
\cref{eq:center-prox-step,eq:center-update}, construct the safe envelope, admit
the complete all-violations batch from \cref{eq:dynamic-gate}, and transport
the center exactly as in \cref{prop:path-implicit-transport-ledger}.  Stop at
the first envelope satisfying \cref{eq:global-certificate}.  This exact-real
execution terminates, and if $T_n$ is its number of fixed-face steps and $J_n$
its number of admissions, then
\begin{equation}
 L_n\leq J_n\leq L_n+1,
 \qquad T_n\geq J_n,
 \qquad
 1+2L_n\leq\nu_{\rm fin}\leq2(L_n+1).
 \label{eq:path-swept-stage-bounds}
\end{equation}
Moreover,
\begin{equation}
 \mathfrak V_{T_n}
 \geq\sum_{j=1}^{L_n}\bigl(1+2(j-1)\bigr)
 =L_n^2=q_n^{-4}
 \geq\frac{\nu_{\rm fin}}{4q_n^2}
 =\frac1{4q_n}\frac{\nu_{\rm fin}}{q_n}.
 \label{eq:path-swept-separation}
\end{equation}
For the literal full-prefix implementation in
\cref{prop:path-implicit-transport-ledger}, the exhaustive resource vector
specializes to
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 (&C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},C_{\rm rec},
 C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit})\\
 =\bigl(&\Theta(\nu_{\rm fin}),\Theta(J_n+1),1,0,
 \Theta(\mathfrak V_{T_n}),\Theta(\mathfrak V_{T_n}),
 \Theta(\mathfrak V_{T_n}),\\
 &\Theta(\nu_{\rm fin}),O(\nu_{\rm fin}),
 \Theta(\mathfrak V_{T_n}),\Theta(\nu_{\rm fin})\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:path-swept-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Thus its charged exact-cell work is $\Omega(q_n^{-4})$, whereas the realized
final-volume product is
$\nu_{\rm fin}/q_n=\Theta(q_n^{-3})$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
First, \cref{eq:path-rppr-lower} at $i=0$ gives
$x_{\rho_n,0}^\star/\sqrt{d_0}\geq q_n-\rho_n>0$, so the seed face lies in
$S_{\rho_n}$ and has an interior restricted optimum.  Every subsequent
strong admission preserves interiority by \cref{eq:positive-expansion}.
The execution is finite.  On any fixed prefix without an admission,
\cref{lem:fixed-face-estimate-center} drives both the primal point and the
estimate center to the interior restricted optimum.  The residual and the
envelope correction therefore converge to zero, so the safe envelope
converges to that optimum.  If the immediate successor is a strict strong
violation at the restricted optimum, it is admitted after finitely many
steps.  Otherwise the successor satisfies the one-sided threshold; equality
is harmless because the envelope lies below the restricted optimum and the
cut row is nonpositive.  The active-coordinate KKT values converge to zero,
and every more distant coordinate has positive KKT slack.  Hence the global
certificate holds after finitely many further steps.  There are only
$N_n+1$ faces, so induction proves termination.

For the terminal safe envelope $\ell_T$, the endpoint-path estimates
\cref{eq:path-solution,eq:path-rppr-lower} give, for every $0\leq i\leq L_n$,
\begin{equation}
 \frac{x_{\rho_n,i}^\star}{\sqrt{d_i}}
 \geq q_n\chi_n^i-\rho_n
 \geq q_n\chi_n^{L_n}-\rho_n
 =2\rho_n.
 \label{eq:path-swept-mandatory-prefix}
\end{equation}
The terminal certificate and \cref{lem:one-sided-certificate} give
$\norm{D^{-1/2}(x_{\rho_n}^\star-\ell_T)}_\infty\leq\tau_n=\rho_n$.
Since $\ell_T$ vanishes outside $U_T$, \cref{eq:path-swept-mandatory-prefix}
forces $v_0,\ldots,v_{L_n}\in U_T$.

Every safe envelope is supported on a path prefix.  Beyond that prefix only
its immediate successor has an active neighbor; every farther nonseed row has
KKT value $\alpha_n\rho_n\sqrt{d_i}>0$.  Thus each all-violations batch has at
most one newly admitted outside vertex (active violations, if any, do not
change the face).  Starting from $v_0$, reaching $v_{L_n}$ takes at least
$L_n$ admissions in distinct fixed-face steps, while the ambient path has
only one additional vertex.  This proves \cref{eq:path-swept-stage-bounds}.

Immediately before the $j$th mandatory admission, the active prefix has
ambient degree volume $1+2(j-1)$.  These $L_n$ distinct steps alone contribute
the sum in \cref{eq:path-swept-separation}.  Since $L_n=q_n^{-2}$ and
$\nu_{\rm fin}\leq2(L_n+1)\leq4L_n$, the remaining inequalities follow.

Finally, this implementation exposes every retained path incidence once and
has one terminal external return.  Every fixed-face step performs a full
proximal recurrence, a full response application, an envelope maximum, and
full active-state writes; bounded path degrees make each of those charges
equivalent to the current face volume.  The factor records and active vectors
occupy $\Theta(\nu_{\rm fin})$ persistent cells, a streamed reverse pass uses
$O(\nu_{\rm fin})$ scratch cells, and the terminal sparse vector and
certificate emit $\Theta(\nu_{\rm fin})$ cells.  Together with one exposure
round per admitted vertex, this proves
\cref{eq:path-swept-eleven-vector}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Limitation of the separation.}
The certified lower-bound factor in \cref{eq:path-swept-separation} is
polynomial in $1/q_n$, but this family deliberately encodes it in the
accuracy:
\begin{equation}
 \log\frac{q_n}{3\rho_n}
 =L_n(-\log\chi_n)
 =\Theta(q_nL_n)
 =\Theta(q_n^{-1}).
 \label{eq:path-swept-log-accuracy}
\end{equation}
Here $-\log\chi_n=2\operatorname{artanh}(q_n)=\Theta(q_n)$ uniformly for
$n\geq2$.
Consequently the result refutes a log-free
$O(\nu_{\rm fin}/q_n)$ charge for this literal swept-prefix implementation,
but not a $\softO(\nu_{\rm fin}/q_n)$ theorem that may hide logarithms in the
accuracy.  It is also far below the RPPR volume-cap benchmark
$1/(\rho_nq_n)$, makes no claim about implicit implementations that avoid the
full-prefix operations defining $\mathfrak V_T$, and does not prove the
observed $\Theta(q^{-3})$ swept work when $\rho=\tau=q/5$.  The latter
constant-ratio support scale is fixed by
\cref{prop:path-constant-ratio-floor};
\cref{thm:path-moving-max-soft-upper} later bounds its stage and swept-volume
powers up to one logarithm, but the exact exponents remain open because the
global envelope correction couples frontier admission to old-coordinate
momentum overshoot.

\subsection{Constant ratio: support scale, nonlocal correction, and a soft chronology bound}
\label{sec:path-constant-ratio}

The constant-ratio regime does have an exact baseline.  It fixes the support
and product-scale floors.  A later moving-maximum energy argument supplies
stage and swept upper bounds up to one logarithm, but does not prove the
sampled exact powers.  The first lemma records why a frontier-only recurrence
cannot settle the remaining question.

For a path-prefix candidate $x$ define normalized coordinates and the global
envelope correction
\begin{equation}
 p_i(x):=\frac{x_i}{\sqrt{d_i}},
 \qquad
 \delta(x):=
 \max_{i\in U_j}
 \left(\frac{\kappa_{\rho,i}(x)}{\alpha\sqrt{d_i}}\right)_+.
 \label{eq:path-constant-ratio-correction}
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[The path admission gate contains the global correction]
\label{lem:path-global-envelope-gate}
Let $U_j=\{v_0,\ldots,v_j\}$ be a proper endpoint prefix whose immediate
successor $v_{j+1}$ is an ambient degree-two vertex.  For any nonnegative
candidate $x$ supported on $U_j$, its safe envelope satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\ell_i}{\sqrt{d_i}}
 =\bigl[p_i(x)-\delta(x)\bigr]_+.
 \label{eq:path-normalized-envelope}
\end{equation}
The complete all-violations gate strongly admits $v_{j+1}$ if and only if
\begin{equation}
 p_j(x)>\delta(x)+\frac{2\alpha(\rho+\tau)}{\eta}.
 \label{eq:path-global-correction-gate}
\end{equation}
In particular, for $\alpha=q^2$ and $\rho=\tau=q/5$, the additive threshold
in \cref{eq:path-global-correction-gate} is
\begin{equation}
 \frac{2\alpha(\rho+\tau)}{\eta}
 =\frac{8q^3}{5(1-q^2)}.
 \label{eq:path-constant-ratio-threshold}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Dividing \cref{eq:safe-envelope} by $\sqrt{d_i}$ gives
\cref{eq:path-normalized-envelope}.  The normalized outside KKT value is
\begin{equation*}
 \frac{\kappa_{\rho,j+1}(\ell)}{\sqrt{d_{j+1}}}
 =\alpha\rho-\frac{\eta}{2}
   \frac{\ell_j}{\sqrt{d_j}}.
\end{equation*}
It is smaller than $-\alpha\tau$ exactly when
$\ell_j/\sqrt{d_j}>2\alpha(\rho+\tau)/\eta$.  The right-hand side is positive, so
\cref{eq:path-normalized-envelope} makes this equivalent to
\cref{eq:path-global-correction-gate}.  Substitution of
$\eta=(1-q^2)/2$ proves \cref{eq:path-constant-ratio-threshold}.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Exact constant-ratio support and product-scale floors]
\label{prop:path-constant-ratio-floor}
Fix $0<q\leq1/4$, set
\begin{equation}
 \alpha=q^2,
 \qquad \rho=\tau=\frac q5,
 \qquad \chi=\frac{1-q}{1+q},
 \qquad
 L_q:=\left\lfloor
 \frac{\log(5/3)}{-\log\chi}
 \right\rfloor,
 \label{eq:path-constant-ratio-parameters}
\end{equation}
and take an endpoint-seeded ambient-degree path with at least $L_q+1$ edges.
Run the exact-real zero-start, internally gated transported-center execution
of \cref{prop:path-zero-start-swept-separation}, now with the parameters in
\cref{eq:path-constant-ratio-parameters}, until its first terminal one-sided
certificate.  If $J_q,T_q,\nu_{\rm fin}$, and $\mathfrak V_{T_q}$ denote its
admissions, fixed-face steps, final degree volume, and swept old-face volume,
then
\begin{align}
 L_q&\leq J_q\leq\frac5q,
 &T_q&\geq J_q,
 \label{eq:path-constant-ratio-JT}\\
 1+2L_q&\leq\nu_{\rm fin}\leq\frac5q,
 &\mathfrak V_{T_q}&\geq L_q^2.
 \label{eq:path-constant-ratio-volume}
\end{align}
Since
\begin{equation}
 L_q\sim\frac{\log(5/3)}{2q}
 \qquad(q\downarrow0),
 \label{eq:path-constant-ratio-radius}
\end{equation}
this proves
\begin{equation}
 J_q=\Theta(q^{-1}),
 \qquad \nu_{\rm fin}=\Theta(q^{-1}),
 \qquad T_q=\Omega(q^{-1}),
 \qquad
 \mathfrak V_{T_q}=\Omega(q^{-2})
 =\Omega(\nu_{\rm fin}/q).
 \label{eq:path-constant-ratio-floors}
\end{equation}

For the named literal full-prefix implementation, with one terminal external
certificate/output return, the exhaustive resource vector is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 (&C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},C_{\rm rec},
 C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit})\\
 =\bigl(&\Theta(\nu_{\rm fin}),\Theta(J_q+1),1,0,
 \Theta(\mathfrak V_{T_q}),\Theta(\mathfrak V_{T_q}),
 \Theta(\mathfrak V_{T_q}),\\
 &\Theta(\nu_{\rm fin}),O(\nu_{\rm fin}),
 \Theta(\mathfrak V_{T_q}),\Theta(\nu_{\rm fin})\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:path-constant-ratio-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The terminal output has note-scoped PPR error at most
$\rho+\tau=2q/5$.  Equations
\cref{eq:path-constant-ratio-JT,eq:path-constant-ratio-volume} do not prove
$T_q=\Theta(q^{-2})$ or $\mathfrak V_{T_q}=\Theta(q^{-3})$ and give no
separation above the realized product scale.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Finite termination follows from the fixed-face contraction and finite-face
argument in the proof of
\cref{prop:path-zero-start-swept-separation}.  At the terminal envelope,
\cref{eq:path-solution,eq:path-rppr-lower} and the definition of $L_q$ give,
for $0\leq i\leq L_q$,
\begin{equation*}
 \frac{x_{\rho,i}^\star}{\sqrt{d_i}}
 \geq q\chi^i-\rho
 \geq\frac{3q}{5}-\frac q5
 =2\rho.
\end{equation*}
The terminal certificate bounds the normalized difference between
$x_\rho^\star$ and the safe envelope by $\tau=\rho$.  Thus the terminal
envelope is positive on $v_0,\ldots,v_{L_q}$, and all those vertices belong
to $U_T$.  Since only the immediate successor of a path prefix can have a
negative outside KKT value, at least $L_q$ singleton admissions in distinct
fixed-face steps are necessary.  This proves the lower bounds for $J_q,T_q$,
and $\nu_{\rm fin}$.

Safe admission gives $\vol(U_T)\leq1/\rho=5/q$, which proves both upper
bounds.  Immediately before the $j$th mandatory admission the prefix volume
is at least $1+2(j-1)$.  Summing these distinct charged steps gives
\begin{equation*}
 \mathfrak V_{T_q}
 \geq\sum_{j=1}^{L_q}\bigl(1+2(j-1)\bigr)=L_q^2.
\end{equation*}
Finally, $-\log\chi=2\operatorname{artanh}(q)\sim2q$, proving
\cref{eq:path-constant-ratio-radius} and the asymptotic consequences.  The
resource vector follows from the same charged literal implementation as
\cref{eq:path-swept-eleven-vector}; bounded path degrees make terminal output
size equivalent to final degree volume.  The accuracy statement is
\cref{cor:accuracy-volume}.
\end{proof}

The correction in \cref{lem:path-global-envelope-gate} is genuinely
nonlocal even on an actual zero-start constant-ratio run.

\begin{proposition}[A zero-start path refutes frontier-only gate logic]
\label{prop:path-frontier-only-gate-fails}
Set
\begin{equation*}
 q=\frac15,
 \qquad \alpha=\rho=\tau=\frac1{25},
 \qquad \eta=\frac{12}{25},
\end{equation*}
and use an endpoint-seeded ambient-degree path with at least six edges.  In
the exact execution of \cref{prop:path-constant-ratio-floor}, stages $1,2,4$
admit $v_1,v_2,v_3$, respectively, while stages $3,5$ do not admit.  At stage
$6$, on $U_3$, the raw normalized frontier candidate and global correction
are
\begin{equation}
 p_3=\frac{145182985951}{10558361328125},
 \qquad
 \delta_6=\frac{14898928}{2462890625},
 \qquad
 p_3-\delta_6
 =\frac{7887224114511}{1024161048828125}.
 \label{eq:path-stage-six-correction}
\end{equation}
Here the maximum defining $\delta_6$ is attained at the old seed $v_0$;
the other three normalized KKT values are negative.  The exact comparisons
are
\begin{equation}
 p_3>\frac1{75}
 \qquad\text{but}\qquad
 p_3-\delta_6<\frac1{75}.
 \label{eq:path-stage-six-suppression}
\end{equation}
Thus the uncorrected frontier would pass the strong admission test for
$v_4$, but the actual safe-envelope all-violations gate does not admit it.

For clarity, the exact first nine stages have the following face/action
chronology.  ``None'' means that every active normalized KKT value is
nonpositive, hence $\delta=0$.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ccll@{}}
\toprule
stage & face before gate & correction maximizer & gate action \\
\midrule
$1$ & $U_0$ & none & admit $v_1$ \\
$2$ & $U_1$ & none & admit $v_2$ \\
$3$ & $U_2$ & none & hold \\
$4$ & $U_2$ & none & admit $v_3$ \\
$5$ & $U_3$ & none & hold \\
$6$ & $U_3$ & $v_0$ & hold \\
$7$ & $U_3$ & $v_0$ & hold \\
$8$ & $U_3$ & $v_0$ & admit $v_4$ \\
$9$ & $U_4$ & $v_2$ & hold \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The run remains on $U_3$ at stage $7$ and admits $v_4$ at stage $8$, where
\begin{equation*}
 \delta_8=\frac{28990137728}{3755908203125},
 \qquad
 p_3-\delta_8
 =\frac{346381814596089}{25604026220703125}>\frac1{75},
\end{equation*}
and the correction maximum is still at $v_0$.  After transporting to $U_4$,
the stage-$9$ positive correction candidates
$\kappa_{\rho,i}/(\alpha\sqrt{d_i})$ at $v_0,v_1,v_2$ are
\begin{equation}
 \frac{154106389088}{18779541015625},
 \quad
 \frac{185836991328}{18779541015625},
 \quad
 \frac{228031410368}{18779541015625},
 \label{eq:path-stage-nine-switch}
\end{equation}
while the remaining two values are negative.  Hence the correction maximizer
has switched from the seed to the old interior vertex $v_2$.  In particular,
the stage-$9$ frontier coordinate also satisfies
\begin{equation*}
 p_4=\frac{2901661181152715177}{591581025829345703125}
 <\delta_9=\frac{228031410368}{18779541015625},
\end{equation*}
so the safe-envelope frontier is zero and the stage-$9$ gate holds.  Thus
neither the raw frontier coordinate nor a correction pinned to one fixed old
coordinate determines the exact admission chronology.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
In normalized path coordinates all coefficients, restricted optima, center
transports, proximal steps, and envelope corrections are rational.  Direct
substitution into
\cref{eq:center-prox-step,eq:center-update,eq:path-global-correction-gate}
gives the stated admission schedule.  At stage $6$ the normalized KKT values
$\kappa_{\rho,i}/\sqrt{d_i}$ are
\begin{equation*}
 \frac{14898928}{61572265625},
 \quad
 -\frac{32479266}{150236328125},
 \quad
 -\frac{4135906804}{3755908203125},
 \quad
 -\frac{5413122}{4736328125}.
\end{equation*}
Dividing their positive maximum by $\alpha=1/25$ proves the middle equality
in \cref{eq:path-stage-six-correction}.  Exact cross multiplication gives
\begin{align*}
 p_3-\frac1{75}
 &=\frac{13214504728}{31675083984375}>0,\\
 p_3-\delta_6-\frac1{75}
 &=-\frac{17304769609592}{3072483146484375}<0,
\end{align*}
which proves \cref{eq:path-stage-six-suppression}.  The stage-$8$ comparison
and the ordering in \cref{eq:path-stage-nine-switch} are again immediate
positive-denominator cross multiplications.  These computations use the
global correction at every stage, not a frontier surrogate.
\end{proof}

The same exact trace also stops the first two pointwise attempts to improve
the moving-maximum estimate itself.  Importantly, the calculation below is
inside the actual projected, globally corrected execution; it does not invoke
the conditional inactive-projection recurrence from
\cref{lem:path-full-face-linearized-roots}.

\begin{proposition}[Monotone moving-correction debt and a $q^{-1}$ error bound fail]
\label{prop:path-monotone-correction-potential-fails}
In the exact $q=1/5$ execution of
\cref{prop:path-frontier-only-gate-fails}, stages $6$ and $7$ are consecutive
held-face steps on the same face $U_3$.  Their full moving corrections are
\begin{equation}
 \delta_6=\frac{14898928}{2462890625},
 \qquad
 \delta_7=\frac{6291729688}{751181640625},
 \qquad
 \delta_7-\delta_6
 =\frac{1747556648}{751181640625}>0.
 \label{eq:path-stage-seven-correction-growth}
\end{equation}
Let
\begin{equation*}
 e_7:=\norm{D_{U_3}^{-1/2}
                   (x^{(7)}-x_{U_3}^{\star})}_\infty .
\end{equation*}
Then the same actual iterate satisfies
\begin{equation}
 e_7=\frac{1373692513944}{1024161048828125},
 \qquad
 \frac{\delta_7}{e_7}
 =\frac{5361340160387}{858557821215}
 =5+\frac{1068551054312}{858557821215}
 >\frac1q.
 \label{eq:path-stage-seven-correction-error-stop}
\end{equation}
Both the proximal candidate and the envelope subtraction are strictly
unclipped at stage $7$.  Consequently the two pointwise statements
\begin{equation*}
 \delta_{t+1}\leq\delta_t
 \quad\hbox{on every held face},
 \qquad
 \delta_t\leq q^{-1}
 \norm{D_{U}^{-1/2}(x^{(t)}-x_U^\star)}_\infty
\end{equation*}
are false for the named exact execution.

The complete $q=1/5$ run has
$J=4$, $T=16$, $\nu_{\rm fin}=9$,
$n_{\rm fin}=5$, and $\mathfrak V_T=114$.  It performs no intermediate
external emission and makes one terminal certificate/output return.  Thus,
using the same named implementation and cost model, substitution into the
already charged literal-path ledger
\cref{eq:path-constant-ratio-eleven-vector} gives
\begin{equation}
 \bigl(\Theta(9),\Theta(5),1,0,
       \Theta(114),\Theta(114),\Theta(114),
       \Theta(9),O(9),\Theta(114),\Theta(9)\bigr)
 \label{eq:path-monotone-correction-stop-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
in the shared eleven-coordinate order.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The stage-$6$ value is
\cref{eq:path-stage-six-correction}.  Direct exact replay of the next
fixed-face step gives the stage-$7$ normalized active KKT vector
\begin{equation*}
 \left(
 \frac{6291729688}{18779541015625},
 \frac{229902132}{3755908203125},
 -\frac{19945416}{61572265625},
 -\frac{5682971052}{18779541015625}
 \right).
\end{equation*}
Its positive maximum divided by $\alpha=1/25$ is the displayed $\delta_7$.
Putting the two corrections over their common denominator proves
\cref{eq:path-stage-seven-correction-growth}.

Solving the four-row restricted system exactly and subtracting its optimum
from the actual stage-$7$ candidate gives normalized error
\begin{equation*}
 \left(
 \frac{1619429295784}{5120805244140625},
 -\frac{18761289036}{52791806640625},
 -\frac{1373692513944}{1024161048828125},
 -\frac{473086325916}{393908095703125}
 \right).
\end{equation*}
Positive-denominator cross multiplication shows that the third absolute
value is maximal, which gives $e_7$.  Reducing $\delta_7/e_7$ gives
\cref{eq:path-stage-seven-correction-error-stop}.  The smallest normalized
candidate coordinate is the frontier coordinate, and it obeys
\begin{equation*}
 p_3(x^{(7)})-\delta_7
 =\frac{51347070933121}{5120805244140625}>0.
\end{equation*}
The raw proximal candidate has this same strictly positive coordinate, so
both the nonnegativity projection and the safe-envelope truncation are
inactive at this stage as claimed.

The exact gate replay admits at stages $1,2,4,8$, holds at all other
nonterminal stages, and certifies at stage $16$.  Hence $J=4$, $T=16$, and
$n_{\rm fin}=5$.  Ambient degrees give $\nu_{\rm fin}=1+2\cdot4=9$, while
the face chronology gives
\begin{equation*}
 \mathfrak V_T=1+3+2\cdot5+4\cdot7+8\cdot9=114.
\end{equation*}
The resource statement is therefore exactly the specialization of

 =\frac{19651905005344050452901946}
 {1629693433860599994659423828125}>0.
 \label{eq:path-two-admission-credit-minimum}
\end{equation}
Stages $9,10,11,12$ are consecutive held checkpoints on $U_4$ and obey
$\Xi_9>\Xi_{10}>\Xi_{11}>\Xi_{12}>0$, so the balance only increases after
stage $9$.  Hence the whole displayed two-admission prefix is ledger-solvent.

Discarding the carried stage-$4$ balance isolates the second-admission block.
Restart with $\widehat{\mathcal C}_7=0$.  Its production, reset, and follow-up
changes remain separate:
\begin{align}
 \Xi_{8^-}-\Xi_7
 &=-\frac{149218476183105431616}
 {14106846430301666259765625}<0,
 \notag\\
 \Xi_{8^+}-(\Xi_{8^-}+\Delta_8)&=-\Delta_8<0,
 \notag\\
 \Xi_9-\Xi_{8^+}
 &=\frac{30987621977208992845824}
 {352671160757541656494140625}>0.
 \label{eq:path-delta-squared-phase-split}
\end{align}
The follow-up is larger than the two realized credits, leaving the exact
deficit
\begin{equation*}
 \Xi_9-(\Xi_7+\Delta_8)
 =\frac{554786577946334102644597007243}
 {11109620138627710163593292236328125}>0.
\end{equation*}
Subsequent fixed-$U_4$ decreases are credited only after they occur.  The
local balance is still negative at stage $11$,
\begin{equation}
 \widehat{\mathcal C}_{11}
 =\Xi_7+\Delta_8-\Xi_{11}
 =-\frac{266914703353796147278415896540763}
 {6943512586642318852245807647705078125}<0,
 \label{eq:path-delta-squared-stage-eleven-stop}
\end{equation}
but first becomes positive at stage $12$,
\begin{equation}
 \widehat{\mathcal C}_{12}
 =\Xi_7+\Delta_8-\Xi_{12}
 =\frac{1384575499982769519202601001412515901}
 {1183348132578517190393991768360137939453125}>0.
 \label{eq:path-delta-squared-stage-twelve-go}
\end{equation}
Thus stage $11$ is the last local ledger STOP and stage $12$ the first local
ledger GO.
The precise cross-state cancellation is
\begin{equation}
 \underbrace{\mathcal C_7}_{\Delta_4-\Xi_7>0}
 +\underbrace{\widehat{\mathcal C}_9}_{<0}
 =\mathcal C_9>0,
 \label{eq:path-two-admission-cross-state-cancellation}
\end{equation}
so the unused earlier realized credit, rather than a future decrease, keeps
the second admission ledger-solvent.

The complete literal run is unchanged:
$J=4$, $T=16$, $\nu_{\rm fin}=9$, $n_{\rm fin}=5$, and
$\mathfrak V_T=114$, with no intermediate external emission and one terminal
certificate/output return.  The complete gate already computes $\delta$; the
path Schur response already computes each $\Delta$.  Updating one retained
scalar balance takes constant exact-cell control work per checkpoint and
removes no scan, response, state, validation, materialization, or output
charge.  Consequently the full vector remains
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 &(C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},C_{\rm rec},
 C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit})\\
 &\quad=\bigl(\Theta(9),\Theta(5),1,0,
       \Theta(114),\Theta(114),\Theta(114),
       \Theta(9),O(9),\Theta(114),\Theta(9)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:path-causal-two-admission-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For any two real numbers, their difference is the positive decrease minus the
positive increase.  Substitution in \cref{eq:causal-recovery-update} and
telescoping proves \cref{eq:causal-recovery-invariant}; no graph or face
geometry is used in this algebraic part.

For the path test, the exact replay and complete gate give the two admissions,
all displayed correction values, and the held chronology.  Exact restricted
solves on $U_2,U_3,U_4$ give \cref{eq:path-two-admission-drops}.  Squaring the
replayed corrections proves the pre-second-admission ordering.  At the frozen
stage-$4$ and stage-$8$ candidates, zero padding does not change the positive
active-residual maximum, so the score is unchanged across each reset.  Direct
substitution gives \cref{eq:path-two-admission-credit-minimum}; exact
positive-denominator comparisons show that it is smaller than every other
positive balance in the displayed prefix.  They also give the strict
stage-$9$--$12$ score order.

Restarting at stage $7$ removes exactly $\mathcal C_7$ and no other term.
Exact squaring gives \cref{eq:path-delta-squared-phase-split}.  Telescoping
the realized stage-$9$--$11$ and stage-$9$--$12$ decreases gives
\cref{eq:path-delta-squared-stage-eleven-stop,eq:path-delta-squared-stage-twelve-go};
the strict score order proves that no earlier reviewed endpoint closes.  The
identity \cref{eq:path-two-admission-cross-state-cancellation} is the
difference of the global and restarted ledgers.  The replay still performs
every operation in the reviewed literal-path ledger, so the resource vector
is unchanged.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact scope of the causal two-admission GO.}
The update \cref{eq:causal-recovery-update} is face-general but conditional:
it says how to audit realized score changes and exact restricted-optimum drops;
it does not prove that the balance is nonnegative on another execution.  If
$\delta$ is not already materialized by the complete gate or if an exact
$\Delta$ requires a response solve, those scans and response operations must
be charged before the constant-size update.  The proved instantiation is a
finite exact-real result for two consecutive later admissions of the named
$q=1/5$ endpoint-path phase and order.  It is neither a pointwise potential
nor an online forecast: a negative block balance remains a STOP until past
decreases actually restore it.  It gives no uniform recovery time,
all-admission telescope, logarithm removal, asymptotic work result, nonpath or
alternate-order theorem, intermediate-output guarantee, finite-precision
bound, or bit-complexity claim.  In particular, the $\delta^2$ production
step decreases here; this does not contradict the Round-018 production STOP
for the different face-error-dependent \textsc{TightPair--Schur} bank.

The maximizer may keep moving, but its value can be controlled without
locating it.  The next theorem gives the first uniform chronology upper bound
for this exact constant-ratio execution.  It preserves one logarithm and
therefore does not prove the sampled power laws.

\begin{theorem}[Moving-maximum soft chronology upper bound]
\label{thm:path-moving-max-soft-upper}
Under the hypotheses and for the exact execution of
\cref{prop:path-constant-ratio-floor}, define
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal E_{\rm gate}(q)
 :=\frac{q^{12}}{50(1+q^2)^2},
 \qquad
 K_q:=\left\lceil
 \frac{\log\!\left(50(1+q^2)^2q^{-10}\right)}
      {-\log(1-q)}
 \right\rceil.
 \label{eq:path-moving-max-block-length}
\end{equation}
Every visited prefix face undergoes at most $K_q$ consecutive fixed-face
steps before either the complete all-violations gate admits its immediate
successor or the global one-sided certificate holds.  Consequently,
\begin{align}
 T_q
 &\leq (J_q+1)K_q
 \leq\left(\frac5q+1\right)K_q
 =\bigO\!\left(q^{-2}\log\frac1q\right),
 \label{eq:path-moving-max-stage-upper}\\
 \mathfrak V_{T_q}
 &\leq\frac5qT_q
 =\bigO\!\left(q^{-3}\log\frac1q\right).
 \label{eq:path-moving-max-volume-upper}
\end{align}
For the named literal full-prefix exact-cell implementation with one terminal
external certificate/output return, the resulting eleven-coordinate upper
ledger is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 (&C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},C_{\rm rec},
 C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit})\\
 =\bigl(&\Theta(q^{-1}),\Theta(q^{-1}),1,0,
 O(q^{-3}\log(1/q)),O(q^{-3}\log(1/q)),
 O(q^{-3}\log(1/q)),\\
 &\Theta(q^{-1}),O(q^{-1}),O(q^{-3}\log(1/q)),
 \Theta(q^{-1})\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:path-moving-max-eleven-upper}
\end{equation}
The bounds hold regardless of how often or where the correction maximizer
switches.  They do not prove $T_q=\Theta(q^{-2})$ or
$\mathfrak V_{T_q}=\Theta(q^{-3})$.  Removal of the logarithm, whether by a
uniform fixed-face argument or by an aggregate potential, and matching global
lower powers remain open.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Write $x_{U}^{\star}$ for the interior restricted optimum on a visited path
prefix $U$.  Interiority holds at the seed face and is preserved by every
strong path admission, as in the proof of
\cref{prop:path-implicit-transport-ledger}.  At every post-transport state,
the weighted Schur ledger and its unweighted telescope imply
\begin{align}
 \mathcal E_U(x,v)
 &\leq \mathcal E_{U_0}(0,0)
       +G_\rho(x_{U_0}^{\star})-G_\rho(x_U^{\star})\notag\\
 &=G_\rho(0)-G_\rho(x_U^{\star})
   +\frac{\alpha}{2}\norm{x_{U_0}^{\star}}_2^2
 \leq q^2.
 \label{eq:path-moving-max-energy-cap}
\end{align}
Indeed, on this endpoint-seeded path the positive part of $c_\rho$ consists
only of the seed entry and has Euclidean norm at most $\alpha$.  Since
$Q\succeq\alpha I$ and feasible vectors are nonnegative,
\begin{equation*}
 G_\rho(0)-G_\rho(x_\rho^\star)
 \leq\frac{\norm{(c_\rho)_+}_2^2}{2\alpha}
 \leq\frac{q^2}{2}.
\end{equation*}
Moreover
$x_{U_0}^{\star}=\alpha(1-\rho)/a$ with $a=(1+q^2)/2$, so its center term is
at most $2q^6\leq q^2/2$ for $q\leq1/4$.  Restricted optimality and
$G_\rho(x_U^\star)\geq G_\rho(x_\rho^\star)$ prove the last inequality in
\cref{eq:path-moving-max-energy-cap}.

Now put
\begin{equation*}
 p:=D_U^{-1/2}x,
 \qquad p^\star:=D_U^{-1/2}x_U^\star,
 \qquad e:=\norm{p-p^\star}_\infty.
\end{equation*}
The normalized active KKT operator is
\begin{equation*}
 \widehat Q_U:=D_U^{-1/2}Q_{UU}D_U^{1/2}.
\end{equation*}
Every row of $\widehat Q_U$ has diagonal magnitude
$a=(1+q^2)/2$ and off-diagonal absolute row sum at most
$\eta=(1-q^2)/2$.  Hence
$\norm{\widehat Q_U}_{\infty\to\infty}\leq a+\eta=1$.  Because
$\kappa_{\rho,U}(x_U^\star)=0$, the moving correction obeys the pointwise
bound
\begin{equation}
 \delta(x)
 \leq\frac{e}{q^2}.
 \label{eq:path-moving-max-delta-energy-a}
\end{equation}
The normalized safe envelope is $[p-\delta(x)\one]_+$, and $p^\star\geq0$;
therefore
\begin{equation}
 \norm{D_U^{-1/2}(\ell-x_U^\star)}_\infty
 \leq (1+q^{-2})e.
 \label{eq:path-moving-max-envelope-error}
\end{equation}
Finally, the quadratic gap in $\mathcal E_U$ is at least
$\alpha\norm{x-x_U^\star}_2^2/2$.  Since every path degree is at least one,
\begin{equation}
 e\leq\frac{\sqrt{2\mathcal E_U(x,v)}}q.
 \label{eq:path-moving-max-delta-energy-b}
\end{equation}

If $\mathcal E_U(x,v)\leq\mathcal E_{\rm gate}(q)$, then
\cref{eq:path-moving-max-envelope-error,eq:path-moving-max-delta-energy-b}
give
\begin{equation*}
 \norm{D_U^{-1/2}(\ell-x_U^\star)}_\infty
 \leq\frac{q^3}{5}=\alpha\tau.
\end{equation*}
Applying the same unit row-sum bound to the active KKT vector shows that every
active row satisfies
$\kappa_{\rho,i}(\ell)/\sqrt{d_i}\geq-\alpha\tau$.
On a path prefix, every exterior row beyond the immediate successor has
normalized KKT value $\alpha\rho>0$.  The immediate successor is therefore
either a strong violation and is admitted by the complete gate, or it too
satisfies the one-sided threshold, in which case
\cref{eq:global-certificate} holds.  This conclusion uses the maximum over
the whole old face through \cref{eq:path-moving-max-delta-energy-a}; it never
assumes that its maximizer is the frontier or the seed.

During $k$ consecutive steps on one fixed face,
\cref{lem:fixed-face-estimate-center} and
\cref{eq:path-moving-max-energy-cap} give
\begin{equation*}
 \mathcal E_U(x_k,v_k)\leq(1-q)^kq^2.
\end{equation*}
The definition of $K_q$ makes the right-hand side at most
$\mathcal E_{\rm gate}(q)$.  Hence a face cannot survive more than $K_q$
steps without admission or termination.  There are $J_q+1$ visited faces and
$J_q\leq1/\rho=5/q$, proving
\cref{eq:path-moving-max-stage-upper}.  Safe support also gives
$\vol(U_t)\leq1/\rho=5/q$ at every stage, which proves
\cref{eq:path-moving-max-volume-upper}.  Since
$-\log(1-q)\geq q$, one has
$K_q=\bigO(q^{-1}\log(1/q))$ uniformly for $0<q\leq1/4$.
Substitution into \cref{eq:path-constant-ratio-eleven-vector}, together with
$J_q,\nu_{\rm fin}=\Theta(q^{-1})$, proves
\cref{eq:path-moving-max-eleven-upper}.
\end{proof}

The candidate uses the zero-start, internally gated, transported-center

\begin{lemma}[Conditional characteristic roots on a full path face]
\label{lem:path-full-face-linearized-roots}
Let the current face be all of $P_m$, and consider consecutive fixed-face
steps of \cref{eq:center-prox-step,eq:center-update} on which the
nonnegativity projection is inactive.  Set
\begin{equation*}
 \begin{aligned}
 a&:=\frac{1+q^2}{2},
 &\eta&:=\frac{1-q^2}{2},
 &P_{\mathrm{row},m}&:=D^{-1}A=P^\top,\\
 \widehat Q&:=D^{-1/2}QD^{1/2}
             =aI-\eta P_{\mathrm{row},m},
 &B&:=I-\widehat Q.
 \end{aligned}
\end{equation*}
For the affine normalized residual states
\[
 r_k^x:=D^{-1/2}(Qx_k-c_\rho),
 \qquad
 r_k^v:=D^{-1/2}(Qv_k-c_\rho),
\]
one fixed-face step obeys
\begin{equation}
 r_{k+1}^x
 =\frac{B}{1+q}(r_k^x+q r_k^v),
 \qquad
 q r_{k+1}^v=r_{k+1}^x-(1-q)r_k^x.
 \label{eq:path-full-face-two-state}
\end{equation}
Consequently, for $k\geq1$,
\begin{equation}
 r_{k+1}^x
 =B\left(
   \frac{2}{1+q}r_k^x
   -\frac{1-q}{1+q}r_{k-1}^x
 \right).
 \label{eq:path-full-face-residual-recurrence}
\end{equation}
Here $P=AD^{-1}$ is the shared column-walk convention.  The eigenvalues of
$P_{\mathrm{row},m}$ are
$\mu_h=\cos(h\pi/m)$, $0\leq h\leq m$.  On the $h$th cosine mode, the two
characteristic roots of \cref{eq:path-full-face-residual-recurrence} are
\begin{equation}
 \zeta_h^\pm
 =(1-q)\cos\!\left(\frac{h\pi}{2m}\right)
  \exp\!\left(\pm\frac{\mathrm i h\pi}{2m}\right).
 \label{eq:path-full-face-linearized-roots}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Inactivity of the projection gives
$x_{k+1}=y_k-(Qy_k-c_\rho)$.  Since
$y_k=(x_k+qv_k)/(1+q)$ and the affine coefficients sum to one,
normalization yields the first identity in
\cref{eq:path-full-face-two-state}.  Multiplying the center update by $q$
gives $qv_{k+1}=x_{k+1}-(1-q)x_k$, which yields its second identity.
Eliminating $r_k^v$ proves
\cref{eq:path-full-face-residual-recurrence}.

For $\theta_h=h\pi/m$, the $h$th eigenvalue of $B$ is
\[
 b_h=\eta(1+\cos\theta_h)
     =(1-q^2)\cos^2(\theta_h/2).
\]
The scalar characteristic polynomial is therefore
\[
 z^2-2(1-q)\cos^2(\theta_h/2)z
 +(1-q)^2\cos^2(\theta_h/2),
\]
whose roots are exactly
\cref{eq:path-full-face-linearized-roots}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Failed lower-bound audit and rigorous GO/STOP.}
An earlier draft promoted the candidate to a logarithmic lower theorem.  Two
independent audits rejected that promotion.  The characteristic roots specify
conditional propagation but not the cosine coefficients of the actual state
upon entry to the full face.  No uniform lower bound on a surviving mode or
anti-cancellation estimate for the moving residual range was derived.
In particular, deleting nonpositive packet fragments is not monotone for
oscillation.  The proposed rapid-front Pascal induction also omitted explicit
base and endpoint-row derivations, and neither projection inactivity nor the
unclipped-envelope margin was proved uniformly through
$k_0=\Theta(q_m^{-1}\log(1/q_m))$.

Thus there is no proved terminal logarithmic block, no asymptotic lower
eleven-vector, and no refutation of a uniform
$K_{\rm face}=O(q^{-1})$ bound.  The existing upper ledger
\cref{eq:path-moving-max-eleven-upper} remains the only rigorous
constant-ratio chronology ledger.  A valid lower-bound GO must derive the
literal full-face entry coefficients, prove the actual zero-start
all-violations trajectory remains in the conditional regime through $k_0$,
and establish a moving-global-residual range lower bound with explicit
anti-cancellation.  The alternative GO is an admission-specific energy,
normalized-error, or space--time potential proving
$K_{\rm face}=O(q^{-1})$, $T=O(q^{-2})$, and
$\mathfrak V_T=O(q^{-3})$.  Either route must retain the full moving global
correction; a frontier or seed surrogate is not admissible.

The exact STOP in
\cref{prop:path-monotone-correction-potential-fails} additionally rules out
treating the correction itself as monotone held-face debt or replacing the
reviewed pointwise factor $q^{-2}$ by the displayed coefficient-one
$q^{-1}$ factor.  It does not rule out a nonmonotone or phase-aware aggregate
argument.  The next explicit attempt
$\Phi_t^{\rm bank}=\delta_t+q^{-1}e_t$ does absorb the stage-$6$--$7$ spike,
but \cref{prop:path-correction-error-bank-potential-fails} refutes its
held-pair monotonicity at stages $9$--$10$.  That finite STOP leaves longer
blocks and cross-face amortization open.  Moreover,
\cref{prop:path-no-constant-coefficient-bank} derives the exact local
coefficient constraints from both pairs and proves that no fixed
$c\geq0$ makes $\delta_t+cq^{-1}e_t$ nonincreasing on both.  This pairwise
incompatibility does not define a global block horizon or exclude a
time-varying, face-dependent, or signed-phase rule.  The canonical endpoint
test in \cref{prop:path-tight-pair-schur-bank} gives one finite positive
reset identity: after squaring the bank, the exact stage-$8$ coefficient
switch and optimum reset are paid by the restricted-optimum drop.  Its raw
linear version fails the same unit-shock comparison.  This keeps held-face
evolution and the frozen-candidate reset separate.  The extension in
\cref{prop:path-tight-pair-local-chain-stop} now evaluates both adjacent
fixed-face steps: the squared-bank-plus-reserve account already rises on the
candidate-producing step $7\to8^-$, falls across the paid reset, rises again
from $8^+$ to $9$, and has positive net stage-$7$--$9$ change.  Both
adjacent-step failures persist throughout the held-pair-feasible coefficient
rectangle.  This closes that pointwise local-chain attempt, not longer
aggregate blocks or a multi-face telescope.  The first tested longer account
in \cref{prop:path-tight-pair-recovery-block} is positive but finite: it
accumulates only the subsequent fixed-$U_4$ squared-bank decreases.  The
reserve is insufficient through stage $12$ and first pays at stage $13$,
giving a rectangle-uniform $7\to13$ endpoint decrease.  This stage-$7$--$13$
single-admission block neither supplies a face-general closing time nor
iterates across admissions.
The causal condition in
\cref{prop:path-causal-two-admission-recovery} supplies the next exact
interface without claiming such a time bound.  Its score $\Xi=\delta^2$ is
observable on every face, and its balance credits only already computed score
decreases and already realized restricted-optimum drops while debiting every
increase.  On the same trace, unused stage-$4$ credit remains solvent through
the next canonical admission at stage $8$; without that cross-state carry, the
stage-$8$ account first recovers at stage $12$.  This is a finite conditional
scalar-ledger GO and an exact cross-state cancellation, not a convergence or
work certificate and not a proof that another graph,
face, order, or admission sequence has nonnegative balance.

\begin{conjecture}[One-sided expanding-subspace acceleration]
\label{conj:one-sided-continuation}
For the recurrence \cref{eq:dynamic-y,eq:dynamic-step,eq:dynamic-gate}, or for
a closely related projected Chebyshev recurrence, universal constants $C,c>0$
exist such that
\begin{equation}
 \norm{D^{-1/2}(x_\rho^\star-\ell^{(t)})}_\infty
 \leq C\exp(-c\sqrt\alpha\,t)+\tau.
 \label{eq:desired-continuation}
\end{equation}
\end{conjecture}

If \cref{conj:one-sided-continuation} holds, then
\begin{equation}
 t=\bigO\!\left(\frac1{\sqrt\alpha}\log\frac1\tau\right),
 \qquad
 \mathcal W
 =\bigO\!\left(
   \frac1{\rho\sqrt\alpha}\log\frac1\tau
 \right).
 \label{eq:conditional-final}
\end{equation}
With $\rho=\tau=\epsppr/2$, this is the sought
$\softO(1/(\sqrt\alpha\epsppr))$ bound.

\paragraph{Why existing analyses do not immediately prove it.}
The nonhomogeneous \LOCCH{} formula of \citet{zhou2024} controls a full signed
inactive residual.  The safe gate controls only the negative part of the KKT
residual at a corrected lower envelope.  Outside the optimal support, positive
KKT slack can be dense and is harmless for the obstacle problem, but it enters
a two-sided residual norm.  Energy contraction alone also does not imply the
uniform active-volume invariant.  Finally, \cref{prop:shock} supplies an
upper perturbation bound only with a possible $1/\alpha$ loss.

\paragraph{A weaker sufficient target.}
It is enough to strengthen \cref{thm:amortized} so that discrete expansion
overhead is charged to newly admitted volume:
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal W_{\mathrm{exp}}
  \leq C\sum_t\vol(U_{t+1}\setminus U_t)
  \leq\frac{C}{\rho},
  \label{eq:new-volume-charge}
\end{equation}
rather than $N_{\mathrm{exp}}/\rho$.  Combining
\cref{eq:new-volume-charge} with the continuous telescoping term already gives
the target up to a logarithm once the number and placement of recurrence
sweeps are controlled.  Proposition
\cref{prop:path-implicit-transport-ledger} proves this charge for the
incremental transport overhead on endpoint paths, but leaves every old-face
recurrence/response sweep in $\mathfrak V_T$ and does not bound $T$.

\section{Implications for the hybrid-solver program}
\label{sec:implications}

The conversation leads to four firm conclusions.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \emph{Acceleration has not vanished spectrally.}
The spider's restricted condition number is never worse than $1/\alpha$.
The extra factor observed in local work comes from the space--time footprint
of repeated scans.

\item \emph{Peak-volume flattening is rigorously feasible.}
RPPR KKT structure supplies a locally checkable, momentum-independent gate
whose working set never exceeds volume $1/\rho$.  This part no longer depends
on a conjectural signed-residual $\ell_1$ invariant.

\item \emph{Naive restart and batching mechanisms are insufficient.}
Short fixed restart blocks lose acceleration; FIFO batch splitting preserves
triangular work; and a path forces accelerated-scale many safe expansions.
Even one endpoint edge forbids carrying a zero fixed-face energy through an
admission without an explicit optimum-shift term.

\item \emph{The open problem is now narrow.}
Exact center transport removes the face-change defect and has a fully charged
new-volume implementation on paths.  Weighted shocks are now exactly an
occupancy charge on the remaining face gain, and the unweighted Schur
telescope alone cannot pay that charge with a factor $\sqrt\alpha$.  One must
still pack the zero-padded cross term or control this occupancy jointly with
the stage count and number of old-prefix sweeps, then
extend the path response beyond one-dimensional append-only geometry.  Until
then, the graph-uniform
$\softO(1/(\sqrt\alpha\epsppr))$ theorem remains open.
The exact zero-start family in
\cref{prop:path-zero-start-swept-separation} additionally shows that a
log-free realized-volume charge is false for the literal full-prefix
implementation, but its certified lower-bound factor is only an accuracy
logarithm and hence does not close this soft-order question.  At the constant
ratio, \cref{prop:path-constant-ratio-floor} fixes
$J,\nu_{\rm fin}=\Theta(q^{-1})$ and only the product-scale lower floor
$\mathfrak V_T=\Omega(\nu_{\rm fin}/q)$; the stage-$6$ obstruction in
\cref{prop:path-frontier-only-gate-fails} shows why the missing chronology
cannot be reduced to the raw frontier coordinate.  The moving-maximum bound
in \cref{thm:path-moving-max-soft-upper} now proves
$T=O(q^{-2}\log(1/q))$ and
$\mathfrak V_T=O(q^{-3}\log(1/q))$ without fixing the maximizer.  It leaves
the matching global lower powers and aggregate logarithm removal open and
therefore does not establish the sampled $\Theta$ laws.  The audited short
full-path candidate proves only the conditional roots in
\cref{lem:path-full-face-linearized-roots} and three finite exact traces; the
face-uniform $O(q^{-1})$ route and a logarithmic fixed-face lower bound are
both open.  The exact stage-$6$--$7$ STOP in
\cref{prop:path-monotone-correction-potential-fails} also shows that the next
upper attempt cannot simply declare the moving correction to be decreasing,
or bound it by $q^{-1}$ times the current normalized face error with unit
coefficient.  The stage-$9$--$10$ STOP in
\cref{prop:path-correction-error-bank-potential-fails} further shows that
adding that same coefficient-one face error as a decreasing bank does not
repair the argument, even though the bank passes the earlier correction
spike.  The exact inequalities in
\cref{prop:path-no-constant-coefficient-bank} strengthen this to every
nonnegative constant coefficient: satisfying the local $U_3$ pair violates
the local $U_4$ pair, or conversely.  They do not analyze a multi-step block
or a global block horizon.  The tight endpoint coefficients do give the
squared, Schur-paid isolated reset in
\cref{prop:path-tight-pair-schur-bank}.  The exact extension in
\cref{prop:path-tight-pair-local-chain-stop} proves why that charge does not
turn the surrounding fixed-face evolution into a pointwise potential: the
production step fails first, the reset passes, the follow-up fails, and their
complete-chain net change is positive.  A later fixed-face recovery does
eventually pay that finite deficit: the telescope in
\cref{prop:path-tight-pair-recovery-block} stops through stage $12$ and first
closes at stage $13$.  Its rectangle-uniform endpoint inequality is one
local block fact and gives no uniform face horizon or repeated-admission
potential.
\end{enumerate}

The gate is compatible with the broader hybrid philosophy: use signed AESP,
Chebyshev, SOR, or another accelerator to generate candidates, but let a
monotone KKT envelope decide which coordinates may be exposed.  A
momentum-free local cleanup remains a proof-safe fallback.  The new result
does not close the separate early-AESP locality promotion gate in the
repository's AESP--\LOCSOR{} note; it instead supplies an alternative route
whose own continuation lemma must be resolved.

\subsection{Recommended proof and falsification sequence}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item On endpoint-source paths, start from the proved
$\rho=\tau=q/5$ support scale and the moving-maximum upper bounds in
\cref{thm:path-moving-max-soft-upper}.  Seek an admission-specific energy,
normalized-error, or space--time potential proving
$K_{\rm face}=O(q^{-1})$, $T=O(q^{-2})$, and
$\mathfrak V_T=O(q^{-3})$; alternatively, close the audited short-path
candidate by deriving its literal entry coefficients, conditional-regime
margin, and anti-cancellation estimate.  Frontier-only logic is refuted, so
either route must retain the moving global correction.  Continue to track the
remaining-gain occupancy separately; the unweighted $q$-scaled shortcut is
already refuted.  Also avoid the two pointwise shortcuts refuted by
\cref{prop:path-monotone-correction-potential-fails}: the actual correction
need not decrease on a held face and need not be at most $q^{-1}$ times the
normalized face error with unit coefficient.  Also avoid treating
$\delta_t+q^{-1}e_t$ as a decreasing held-face bank: it passes stages
$6$--$7$ but fails on consecutive held stages $9$--$10$ by
\cref{prop:path-correction-error-bank-potential-fails}.  More generally, no
fixed $c\geq0$ makes $\delta_t+cq^{-1}e_t$ decrease on both pairs, by
\cref{prop:path-no-constant-coefficient-bank}.  These are pairwise local
constraints, not a bound on any global block horizon.  The first admissible
face-dependent test is the squared \textnormal{\textsc{TightPair--Schur}}
bank in \cref{prop:path-tight-pair-schur-bank}: debit the exact remaining
restricted-optimum drop only at the frozen-candidate reset, and never compare
the raw linear bank directly with objective shock.  Its immediate pointwise
extension is now refuted by
\cref{prop:path-tight-pair-local-chain-stop}: the reserve cannot pay either
adjacent fixed-face increase, even though it pays the reset.  The next upper
test in \cref{prop:path-tight-pair-recovery-block} shows that a literal
post-reset fixed-face telescope first repairs this one block at stage $13$,
but supplies no face-general or multi-admission rule.  The realized-credit
condition in \cref{prop:path-causal-two-admission-recovery} is the first
checkable face-general replacement: it uses $\delta^2$, debits every increase,
and credits only an already observed decrease or already computed
restricted-optimum drop.  It stays solvent across the consecutive stage-$4$
and stage-$8$ admissions because unused earlier credit supplies an exact
cross-state cancellation.  If that credit is discarded, stage $11$ remains a
ledger STOP and stage $12$ is the first local ledger GO.  The next task is to prove a
uniform closing or solvency condition on every visited face, or find the first
exact graph/order where this scalar balance does not recover before the next
admission.  Do not promote the finite two-admission audit to a global
telescope.
\item Extend the proof to trees with bounded branching, isolating whether
simultaneous boundary admissions create an unavoidable $1/\alpha$ shock.
\item Test the zero-padded recurrence on adversarial spiders, stars of stars,
and lollipops.  Record peak volume, newly admitted volume, edge revisits,
$\nu(\ell_t)$, and objective shock separately.
\item If pointwise continuation fails, target the weaker charge
\cref{eq:new-volume-charge}; if that also fails, construct the corresponding
M-matrix counterexample and state the structural condition that restores it.
\end{enumerate}
