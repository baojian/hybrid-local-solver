\begin{abstract}
We describe a deterministic local algorithm for point-source regularized
PageRank, prove its accelerated stage convergence, and give the second-energy
argument controlling total scanned volume. The continuation and two-energy argument originated
in the parallel task \emph{Prove conjecture 2 deterministically}; this task
independently checked it and developed the implementation refinements.
The audited work bound is $\widetilde O(1/(\rho\sqrt\alpha))$ for the
canonical point-source problem. Practical superiority over FISTA remains
unproved. The algorithm and exact-real proof are developed below in repository
notation. The complete earlier audit, bounded-arithmetic extension and
implementation refinements are also preserved with their original sources.
\end{abstract}

\section{Algorithm and the additional energy}
\label{sec:archive-algorithm}
Let $\bm{w}=\bm{D}^{1/2}\one$. The conceptual restriction
$\mathcal A=\{i:d_i\leq1/\rho\}$ is discovered locally; it is never globally
enumerated. All degrees remain original degrees and
$\bm{M}=\bm{Q}_{\mathcal A\mathcal A}$.
On this restriction, $\alpha\bm{I}\preceq\bm{M}\preceq\bm{I}$,
$\bm{M}^{-1}\geq0$, and $\bm{M}\bm{w}\geq\alpha\bm{w}$.

Continuation halves a stage regularizer $r$ from $1/d_v$ down to $\rho$.
A safe baseline $\overline{\bm{x}}\leq\bm{x}_r^*$ has source residual
\[
 \bm{r}_{\mathrm{src}}=\bm{b}-\bm{M}\overline{\bm{x}},\qquad
 \bm{0}\leq\bm{r}_{\mathrm{src}}\leq4\alpha r\bm{w},\qquad
 m_{\mathrm{src}}=\bm{w}^{\top}\bm{r}_{\mathrm{src}}\leq\alpha.
\]
This residual is denoted by $s$ in the archival report; it is distinct from
the shared seed vector $\bm{s}$. Choose a dyadic
$\theta\asymp\sqrt\alpha$, with $\theta\leq1/2$ and $\theta^2\leq\alpha$.
Put $\lambda=\alpha r$ and
\[
 \mathcal K=\{\bm{u}:\bm{0}\leq\bm{u}\leq4r\bm{w},\quad
                \bm{w}^{\top}\bm{u}\leq m_{\mathrm{src}}/\alpha\}.
\]
Starting from $\bm{\xi}=\bm{z}=\bm{0}$, perform
\begin{equation}\label{eq:det-op2-step}
\begin{aligned}
 \bm{y}&=(\bm{\xi}+\theta\bm{z})/(1+\theta),\\
 \bm{q}&=(1-\theta)\bm{z}+\theta\bm{y}
       -(\bm{M}\bm{y}-\bm{r}_{\mathrm{src}}+\lambda\bm{w})/\theta,\\
 \bm{p}&=\operatorname{Proj}_{\mathcal K}^{\rm Euclidean}(\bm{q}),\\
 \bm{\xi}^{+}&=(1-\theta)\bm{\xi}+\theta\bm{p},\qquad
 \bm{z}^{+}=\bm{p}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Projection is scalar thresholding:
$p_i/w_i=\min\{4r,(q_i/w_i-\eta)_+\}$, with the multiplier $\eta\geq0$
chosen to respect the mass cap. Ordinary accelerated energy gives
$K=\widetilde O(\alpha^{-1/2})$ iterations per stage.

The additional estimate uses the analytical comparator
$\bm{t}=\bm{M}^{-1}\bm{r}_{\mathrm{src}}$, which is never computed.
For the projection normal $\bm{n}=\bm{q}-\bm{p}$, Stieltjes signs,
the diffuse source, the box and the cap yield
\[
 (\bm{M}\bm{p}-\bm{r}_{\mathrm{src}})^{\top}\bm{n}\geq0.
\]
This is a special comparator inequality, not general nonexpansiveness of
Euclidean projection in a matrix metric. With $\mu=\theta^2$, the energy
\[
 \mathcal J_2(\bm{\xi})-\mathcal J_2(\bm{t})
       +\frac\mu2\norm{\bm{z}-\bm{t}}_{\bm{M}}^2,\qquad
 \mathcal J_2(\bm{\xi})=\frac12\norm{\bm{M}\bm{\xi}-\bm{r}_{\mathrm{src}}}^2
              +\lambda(\bm{M}\bm{w})^{\top}\bm{\xi},
\]
contracts. It bounds the actual squared response by
$\norm{\bm{M}(\bm{\xi}_k-\bm{\xi}^*)}^2\leq18\alpha^2r$.
Selected signed-flow accounting then proves
\[
 \sum_{k<K}\vol(\supp(\bm{z}_{k+1}))\leq148K/r
\]
for the exact recurrence; the rounded implementation has a proved constant
360 instead of 148. Repeated visits are included even outside the true
optimal support. Shared-scale primal updates, cached matrix responses and
deterministic ordered trees realize this bound without repeatedly scanning
the complete historical primal support.

Terminal PG certification and downward density clipping produce the safe
baseline for the next stage. Summing $1/r$ over the halving schedule gives
$O(1/\rho)$. The complete repair and rounding arguments, accuracy schedules,
and code correspondence are preserved in the full report and package.

superiority over an optimized floating-point FISTA implementation.

desired unconditional locality bound for standard FISTA or for an unchanged
