## Frozen Round 022 target/audit contract (open)

Round 022 freezes the acceptance target for the only currently plausible
graph-uniform route.  This is an **Open** target/audit contract, not a proved
solver theorem.  On every finite simple undirected unweighted no-isolate graph
given by adjacency lists, with a sparse nonnegative seed `s`, `1^T s=1`,
`alpha in (0,1]`, and `eps_ppr in (0,1)`, a deterministic exact-real/algebraic-
cell algorithm must return a finite-support `x_hat` satisfying
`||D^(-1/2)(x_hat-x^0)||_infinity<=eps_ppr`, equivalently the shared degree-
normalized PPR error, together with one note-scoped terminal certificate.
Set `rho=tau=eps_ppr/2`.  For `alpha<1/4`, the candidate is full-space
safeguarded AESP-CD on the one fixed `F_rho`, with dynamic greedy normalized-
KKT exploration, safe lower retraction, cached exposed rows, and the complete
gate; for `alpha>=1/4`, the declared fallback is zero-start monotone RPPR
coordinate descent with `O(1/eps_ppr)` work.  There is no graph preprocessing
and only one terminal external interaction.

Put `V_eps=nnz(s)+2/eps_ppr`,
`W_eps=nnz(s)+O_tilde(1/(sqrt(alpha) eps_ppr))`, and
`k=nnz(x_hat)<=2/eps_ppr`.  In the shared order
`(C_adj,R_adj,R_int,C_pre,C_ctl,C_rec,C_resp,M_pers,M_tmp,C_mat,C_emit)`, the
target vector is
`(O(V_eps),O(V_eps),1,0,O(W_eps),O(W_eps),0,O(V_eps),O(V_eps),O(W_eps),Theta(k+1))`,
with `C_ctl+C_rec+C_mat=O(W_eps)` jointly and soft factors restricted to
logarithms in `1/alpha`, `1/eps_ppr`, and `V_eps`.  Bit complexity and finite
precision are outside this contract.  The terminal bridge must return a safe
lower point `ell` whose complete gate verifies
`kappa_(rho,i)(ell)>=-alpha tau sqrt(d_i)` for every coordinate; this gives
RPPR error at most `tau`, and `rho+tau=eps_ppr` gives the PPR target.

The central missing lemma must apply to the **finite relative-inner
recurrence**, not only ideal exact shifted minimizers.  Round 023 sharpens the
acceptable form: a horizon-uniform or transient-only inflation allowance is
false even for one fixed exact RPPR objective.  The live target is therefore
an actual-finite net exponent
`J_T^fin <= (1-c) q T+B`, with absolute `c>0` and an explicitly declared
soft-logarithmic `B`, or an equivalent windowed estimate.  An allowance called
``polylogarithmic'' only after substituting the terminal
`T=Theta(log(1/eps_ppr))` is not contradicted by the fixed-parameter example.
The accelerated rate-to-gate and full eleven-coordinate vector remain
conditional on this missing net theorem; the terminal bridge, cached-gate
mechanics, and large-`alpha` fallback are now proved independently of it.

Round 024 narrows that missing theorem again.  The support-indexed lower
retraction is discontinuous at support entry and has sharp fixed-positive-face
amplification `1+1/alpha`, so an ambient-norm proof that transfers an exact
trajectory to finite solves only by iterating this Lipschitz factor cannot
retain accelerated inner work.  Conversely, suppose
`supp(x_t)=A=S*(rho)`, `ell_t=x_t`, and `Q_A(x*-x_t)>=0`.  If the solve after
that full center is exact, `x_(t+1)=p(x_t)`, its trial has no correction.  If
instead `x_(t+1)>0` on `A` and is zero off `A`, its adjacent correction is
bounded solely by the newly produced end residual.  Most importantly, if the
nonempty optimal support `A` satisfies
`theta_A=lambda_min(Q_A)/(kappa_A+lambda_min(Q_A))>=c0*q`, direct resolvent
contraction under `||p(ell_t)-x_(t+1)||_2<=xi_t` proves the terminal gate and
the full eleven-coordinate vector without any inflation-packing assumption.
This condition is a posteriori and
uses the unknown optimal face, so it is a structural promised-class theorem,
not a graph-uniform algorithmic certificate.  The remaining graph-uniform
Route-B target is restricted to low-Dirichlet supports `theta_A=o(q)`.

Round 025 removes support entry itself from that low-Dirichlet blocker.  For
the actual finite recurrence, if
`E_t=supp(x_t) minus supp(x_(t-1))`, then the first
retraction violation on every newly positive row obeys
`0<=g_(t,E_t)<=beta_A a_(t-1,E_t)`.  It is exactly zero under exact shifted
solves; under finite solves an entry-dominated common correction is controlled
only by the preceding end residual.  More generally, relative to the
residual-free driver at the same realized states, the positive correction
excess created by that preceding residual has one-stage momentum norm at most
`(1-q)mu_t xi_(t-1)/(q alpha)` under the dual absolute polish.  This is a
one-sided comparison, not trajectory shadowing.

Before the fresh gate, the locally maintained target
`C_end,t<=delta alpha eta_gate^2 q^2`, with
`eta_gate=2 alpha tau/(1+alpha)`, bounds every entry-dominated positive
log-inflation term by `4 delta q`, hence their entire prefix contribution by
`4 delta q T`.  The target costs only a logarithmic refinement of each greedy
inner solve and keeps `V<=1/rho`, `T=O_tilde(1/q)` work at
`O_tilde(1/(rho q))`.  Persistent-row-dominated stages are not covered.

On an exact settled face, a full correction at stage `t` is followed by the
already proved no-correction stage, and the stage-`t+2` collapse driver is
exactly
`Q_A(Q_A+kappa_A I)^(-2)[beta_A(2+beta_A)Q_A-kappa_A I]e_t`.
Its scalar coefficient changes sign at
`lambda/kappa_A=(1+q)^2/((1-q)(3+q))`.  The exact `K_8` mixed-mode stress test
shows that `Q_A e_t>=0` and small `lambda_min(Q_A)` alone do not make the
coordinatewise positive part vanish.  The witness is an algebraic filter
state, not a claimed reachable outer state.  Thus the remaining graph-uniform
target is a windowed high-energy charge for persistent-row mixed-mode partial
corrections.  Round 025 proves no graph-uniform net exponent, solver theorem,
or eleven-coordinate vector.

Round 026 supplies exact actual-finite energy banks for that persistent-row
controller without deleting either finite end residual.  On
`P_t=supp(x_t) intersect supp(x_(t-1))`, put `u_t=Qe_t` and
`d_t=x_t-x_(t-1)`.  Then
`g_(t,P_t)=[beta_A u_(t-1,P_t)-(1+beta_A)u_(t,P_t)]_+`
`<=beta_A[Qd_t]_(+,P_t)`.  Over every window, the persistent normalized
controller has both an L1 bound and the square telescope
`sum_t (G_t^per)^2<=beta_A^2(E_(k-1)^Q-E_l^Q)`; on stages controlled by a
persistent row this also gives
`alpha^2 sum_t Delta_t^2<=beta_A^2(E_(k-1)^Q-E_l^Q)`.  Separately, the common
correction `r_t` and surviving momentum `p_t=beta_A d_t-r_t` each satisfy
their own bound
`<r_t,Qr_t><=beta_A^2<d_t,Qd_t>` and
`<p_t,Qp_t><=beta_A^2<d_t,Qd_t>`.  Their windowed sums have separate copies of
the same error-energy decrease; the two left sides are not added beneath one
copy.

The earlier `K_8` mixed-mode event is now reachable.  One exact dense-seed
instance has the all-time word `N,N,P0,F,N^infinity`, with positive pre-gate
inflation at the partial and full corrections, but
`J_T^fin=log(gamma_2^fin)+log(gamma_3^fin)<2 log 2` for `T>=4`.  It is a
transient collateral pulse, not an additive-term-resistant obstruction.  A
second rational family, for every rational `0<q<=1/100`, has a harmful
persistent full correction with raw defect
`D_2^fin>28q*7/112^2` and same-stage actual energy drop
`E_1^Q-E_2^Q<197q^4*7/112^2`.  Thus direct payment by that same unsplit drop
needs `Omega(q^(-3))=Omega(alpha^(-3/2))`; after the actual potential weight
`mu_E=kappa_A q^2`, it still needs `Omega(q^(-1))`.  This is only a direct
same-drop proof-template STOP.  It does not refute a `q^(-1)`-weighted,
spectrally split, or differently normalized bank, an additive polylogarithmic
allowance, the actual-finite net exponent, a solver theorem, or an
eleven-coordinate vector.  The remaining queue is restricted to such a
`q^(-1)`-weighted, spectrally split, or differently normalized persistent
low-Dirichlet Lyapunov.

Round 027 tests that weighted route at its simplest exact boundary.  On the
actual finite sequence, the lagged Euclidean reserve
`Psi_t^E=Phi_t^fin+((1-q)^2 mu_E/(2q))||e_(t-1)||_2^2` satisfies
`Psi_(t+1)^E<=Psi_t^E-q Phi_t^fin+eta_t`, with the finite shifted-solve error
`eta_t=2 kappa_A xi_t+kappa_A xi_t^2` retained.  However,
`Psi_t^E<=((1+q+q^2)/q)Phi_t^fin`, so its graph-uniform drift is only
`1-q^2/(1+q+q^2)` and does not prove an accelerated stage count.

For the sharper unsplit bank, direct payment of the Round026 `K_8` pulse by
`(A/q)(E_(t-1)^Q-E_t^Q)` forces
`A>14(1-q)kappa_A/197>=6929307/98509850`.  The absorbing bank must be lagged,
`Psi_t^A=Phi_t+(A/q)E_(t-1)^Q`.  On exact uniform-seed `K_2` with `q=1/n`,
`n>=100`, and `rho=tau=1/16`, the first genuinely persistent transition is
stage two, not the entry transition: for every such `A`,
`0<=1-Psi_3^A/Psi_2^A<=(4+2/A_*)q^2`.  This stops a uniform one-step
`1-cq` comparison for the simplest lagged unsplit template.

Neither family is additive-resistant.  The complete `K_2` trajectory obeys
`Psi_t^A/Psi_1^A<=(4/e)exp(-q(t-1))` with initial normalization at most
`1+A/(q(1-q^2))`, only `O(log(q^(-1)))` in the exponent.  Conversely, the
exact `K_8` high-band payment ratio tends to `14641/32256`, so spectral
splitting remains live.  Round 027 proves no windowed nonlinear transfer,
actual-finite net exponent, graph-uniform solver, or eleven-coordinate vector.
The remaining queue is only a windowed spectrally split, nonlinear-transfer,
or differently normalized actual-finite low-Dirichlet Lyapunov.

## Quarantined conditional and numerical scaffolding

- Round-024 endpoint-path sweeps produced the strongest unpromoted numerical
  lead: for `rho=Theta(q)` and horizons of order `1/q`, both floating
  exact-prox and literal finite C2-plus-absolute traces can have normalized
  inflation above one while the support is growing.  Longer runs show that the
  effect is transient: the optimal support settles, inflation saturates or
  falls, and the observed excess is compatible with the allowed
  soft-logarithmic additive term `B`.  The smallest-`q` traces were also
  tolerance-sensitive.  These experiments provide neither an exact family nor
  an arbitrarily long pre-gate net-exponent obstruction, so no ratio, slope, or
  counterexample claim is promoted.
- The withdrawn Round-013 terminal-path logarithmic lower candidate is not a
  theorem, counterexample, or refutation. On a fixed full path face and only
  while the nonnegativity projection is inactive, the normalized two-state
  residual recurrence and its mode roots
  `(1-q) cos(h pi/(2m)) exp(+/- i h pi/(2m))` are correct
  (`lem:path-full-face-linearized-roots`). They do not determine the actual
  full-face entry coefficients or prevent cancellation, and they do not prove
  projection inactivity or an unclipped moving envelope through
  `Theta(q^-1 log(1/q))` steps. The exact `m=2,4,8` checks cover only their
  finite prefixes. No terminal-block lower bound, stage/swept lower vector,
  logarithm necessity, or refutation of the reviewed Round-012 upper bound may
  be inferred.

The main lower-bound frontier is to separate a budgeted row recurrence from a
fully charged persistent response on the same task. The shared model now
requires eleven reported resource coordinates; adjacency access alone is too
weak, and the exact path result separates only intermediate materialization.
The fully exposed endpoint-path product candidate also fails below the Krylov
dimension scale. The resulting `n=Omega_tilde(1/sqrt(alpha))` restriction is
only for fixed-support exact systems with linear-cost matrix--vector access,
not for every local information or support-discovery lower bound. At the
surviving scale `alpha=n^-2`, the named full-vector `DiagSpecPoly(r)` subclass
does meet the product obstruction, but the full budgeted `RowRec` theorem is
still open because supported growing-prefix and arbitrary implicit, rational,
nonlinear, or adaptive response routes are outside that theorem. Exact
ordinary CG on the same endpoint path also takes all `n` steps under the
actual residual certificate and makes a chosen literal growing-prefix
implementation meet, rather than beat, the product scale. That calibration
does not extend the named full-vector obstruction to a recurrence class. In
fact, on that sparse certificate task the named `CertPrefixPoly` class has a
five-site degree-four counteralgorithm and a same-task five-row response, both
with constant exact-cell work and `nu_fin=11`. Tightening the same certificate
to `eps_ppr=1/(10n)` forces full support, but exact singleton coordinate-basis
propagation plus delayed fixed-profile synthesis still gives a linear-work
recurrence, matched by a linear-work same-task response, against a quadratic
product scale. Any next recurrence/response separation must therefore defeat
both constant-prefix residual-slack spreading and sparse-basis cancellation,
by an independently justified trajectory/materialization restriction or a
different same-task family.

## Promotion rule

A direction result enters this ledger only after its assumptions and proof
location are verified. It enters an active manuscript theorem only after its
dependencies are closed or the statement is explicitly narrowed to a correct
conditional/structural version. Measurements remain measurements, and
counterexamples remain attached to the exact algorithm or oracle they refute.
