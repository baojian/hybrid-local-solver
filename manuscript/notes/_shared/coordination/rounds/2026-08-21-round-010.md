`propagate_settle_framework` proved
`prop:double-cycle-anchor-seed-obstruction`. Fix `alpha in (0,1)`, even
`n>=6`, anchor seed `s=e_(u_j)`, and the exact counterrange
`0<rho<(1-alpha)/8`. Under the exact canonical all-violations gate, at most
`f_j,f_(j-1),f_(j+1)` can be admitted before the first batch meeting `W`. If
a canonical batch first meets either distance-two petal, that batch also
contains `w_j` unless a report entered earlier. Hence the anchor counterrange
does not yield an `Omega(n)` report-free petal/attachment epoch or the desired
varying-response witness.

With `t=(1+alpha)/(1-alpha)` and `lambda=1/(2rho)`, a continuing trace reaches
the unique launch face either immediately or after the seed petal alone. The
corrected second launch threshold is

`lambda > 8t+2-3/(2t)`.

The proof then exhausts the optional local faces before a distance-two petal.
The antipodal case `n=6` is separate: `b_3=b_(-3)` and `u_3=u_(-3)` each see
two frontier neighbors, giving thresholds `p_2>4` and `s_2>4`; only for even
`n>=8` do the one-neighbor thresholds become `p_2>8` and `s_2>8`.

The scope/resource anchor remains `rem:double-cycle-feed-trace-ledger`. Let
`P_firstW` denote the complete paid prefix through the first report batch or
termination. The fixed-anchor local proof audit has incremental fresh/stored
vectors

`(O(1),1,0,0,O(1),0,0,0,O(1),0,0)` and
`(0,0,0,0,O(1),0,0,0,O(1),0,0)`

at `eq:double-cycle-anchor-audit-vectors`. They compose with the full paid
prefix by summing additive coordinates and taking memory peaks; neither is a
standalone execution ledger. Auditing all rotations restores the earlier
`Theta(n)` fresh scan/control or stored control entries.

This is an exact-real anchor-seeded canonical chronology STOP only. Relay

### Branch-caterpillar canonical layer reporter: strict-range GO

`adaptive_revisit_control` proved
`thm:branch-caterpillar-canonical-layer-reporter`. Fix `m>=2`,
`alpha in (0,1)`, branch seed `s=e_(b_1)`, and

`0<rho<rho_can(m,alpha)<=rho_cat(m,alpha)<=1/3`.

The analysis-side threshold in
`eq:branch-caterpillar-canonical-threshold` is a finite minimum of exact affine
demand thresholds and may shrink with `m`; it is not constructed or queried
online. The exact canonical all-violations faces are
`U_k=B_(k+1) union A_k union R_k`. For `k<m-1`, the next batch is
`{b_(k+2),a_(k+1),r_(k+1)}`; the final batch is
`{a_m,r_m,r_(m+1)}`. Thus exactly `m` batches reach full support.

The named exact-real `CaterpillarCanonicalLayerDelta` reporter scans and tests
the complete newly live three-row boundary, emits every and only newly strict-
positive label, co-emits positive ties, treats equality as inactive, and
returns one charged certificate reply per batch. No old key survives. Each
long-arm admission appends one arm-transfer cell and changes only the induced
branch-core absorption cell at `b_1`; each side leaf changes one branch cell,
and each nonfinal batch appends one branch-core cell. A balanced transfer tree
therefore needs constant-many updates and at most two parent-coordinate
queries per batch.

In the shared order, `eq:branch-caterpillar-canonical-eleven-vector` is

`(Theta(m),Theta(m),m+1,0,Theta(m),0,`
` O(m log(2+m)),Theta(m),O(m),Theta(m),Theta(m))`.

It charges all row exposure, rounds, exact demand tests, membership/ties,
transfer construction/application, persistent/scratch cells, certificate
replies, terminal recovery, validation, materialization, and exact output.
Total exact-cell work is `O(m log(2+m))`, with `C(S*)=9m+1`.

This is fixed-`m`, family-`rho`, branch-seeded, canonical, exact-real, and
delta-only. It excludes `rho_can<=rho<rho_cat`, other seeds, arbitrary
positive-subset/pre-backbone orders, repeated full-list output, a positive
`m`-uniform `rho` range, finite precision, bit complexity, transfer
conditioning, `kappa=1`, RPPR-to-PPR conversion, and product or graph-uniform
acceleration.

### Zero-start swept-prefix path: log-free implementation STOP

`volume_gated_acceleration` proved
`prop:path-zero-start-swept-separation`. For integer `n>=2`, set

`q_n=1/n`, `alpha_n=q_n^2`, `chi_n=(1-q_n)/(1+q_n)`,
`L_n=n^2`, `N_n=L_n+1`, and
`rho_n=tau_n=(q_n/3)chi_n^(L_n)`.

Run the exact zero-start internally gated transported-center recurrence on the
endpoint-seeded ambient-degree path `P_(N_n)`, with complete all-violations
batches and one terminal external certificate/output. Mandatory prefix
discovery gives

`L_n<=J_n<=L_n+1`, `T_n>=J_n`, and
`1+2L_n<=nu_fin<=2(L_n+1)`.

The distinct pre-admission prefix sweeps imply

`mathfrak V_(T_n)>=L_n^2=q_n^(-4)>=nu_fin/(4q_n^2)`.

For the named literal full-prefix implementation,
`eq:path-swept-eleven-vector` is

`(Theta(nu_fin),Theta(J_n+1),1,0,Theta(mathfrak V_T),`
` Theta(mathfrak V_T),Theta(mathfrak V_T),Theta(nu_fin),`
` O(nu_fin),Theta(mathfrak V_T),Theta(nu_fin))`.

Thus its exact-cell work is `Omega(q_n^(-4))` while the realized final-volume
product is `nu_fin/q_n=Theta(q_n^(-3))`. The certified extra factor is encoded
in accuracy:

`1/q_n=Theta(log(q_n/rho_n))`.

Accordingly this refutes a log-free product charge only for the literal
full-prefix implementation. It does not refute soft order, an implicit
implementation avoiding those full-prefix operations, the sampled
constant-ratio `rho=tau=q/5` chronology, a general product lower bound,
nonpath response, finite precision, bit complexity, or graph-uniform
acceleration.
