### Frozen notched-sun eager-slack obstruction

```text

```

Then `cvol(T)=7n+2`, the exposed `T`--`W` cut consists of the `n` incidences
`u_i w_i`, and its normalized anchor--report block has rank `n`. Keep the
complete shifted ladder from the Round-012 aggregate-flush theorem. During
event `k`, add only the unit nonnegative first-rung fragment `e_(f_k)`. With

```text

g = vartheta^(5/2),
band width = g/2,
```

the exact response column is strictly positive. For fixed `n` and `eta`, its
matching entry is `Theta(vartheta^2)` and every off-matching entry is
`O_(n,eta)(vartheta^3)`. Hence some
`alpha_(n,eta)^eager<1` makes every
`alpha in (alpha_(n,eta)^eager,1)` satisfy

```text
{i : y_i^[k] > g} = {1,...,k},
y_i^[k] <= g-g/2  for i>k.
```

Thus event `k` has exactly one newly crossing label and certifies all other
unreported labels outside the transition band.

The named policy `EagerExactSlack` stores one separately addressed exact slack
for every certified nonnegative stream and unreported label, eagerly adds the
dense response column, tests every resulting slack, and only then returns the

`p sum_(k=1)^n (n-k+1) = p n(n+1)/2`

fragment charge is `rn` or `2rn`, respectively, and external output is
`Theta(rn)`. A cyclic-factor exact implementation matches the dense scale. Its


`(C_adj,R_adj,R_int,C_pre,C_ctl,C_rec,C_resp,M_pers,M_tmp,C_mat,C_emit)`.


At the stop, admitted support, exact numerical support, and scanned rows all

```text

```

These are `eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-prefix-eleven-vector` and

The separate signed-state proposition identifies the exact missing gate

```text

mu_k = min_v g_(k,v)/beta_(k,v).
```

`(ell_rho-Qz)_v = g_(k,v)+beta_(k,v)(z_parent-(x_k)_parent)`.

Every relative-oracle stage separately retains its explicit

online records. This is a sharp obstruction for the imported symmetric
relative-gap interface, not a lower bound on exact settlement, a one-sided
