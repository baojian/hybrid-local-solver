### `aesp_cd_l1_rppr`: actual-finite persistent controller ledger

- Claim-status change: `lem:aesp-cd-persistent-square-ledger` works directly
  on the realized finite recurrence. For
  `P_t=supp(x_t) intersect supp(x_(t-1))`, put
  `e_t=x*-x_t`, `u_t=Qe_t`, and `d_t=x_t-x_(t-1)`.
- Exact persistent identity:
  `g_(t,P_t)=[beta_A u_(t-1,P_t)-(1+beta_A)u_(t,P_t)]_+`
  `<=beta_A[Qd_t]_(+,P_t)`. The previous and current end residuals have
  combined into the exact fixed-row quantities `u_(t-1)` and `u_t`; neither
  residual is discarded.
- For every `2<=k<=ell`, the exact L1 window is
  `sum_(t=k)^ell G_t^per`
  `<=beta_A(1+alpha)m(x_ell-x_(k-1))/2`
  `<=beta_A(1+alpha)/2`.
- The exact square window is
  `sum_(t=k)^ell (G_t^per)^2`
  `<=beta_A^2(E_(k-1)^Q-E_ell^Q)`.
  On the tie-inclusive persistent-dominated class,
  `alpha Delta_t=G_t^per`, hence
  `alpha^2 sum Delta_t^2`
  `<=beta_A^2(E_(k-1)^Q-E_ell^Q)`.
- Scope: these are actual-finite horizon-independent energy banks. They need
  neither an exact shadow trajectory nor the dual stop, but do not control the
  Euclidean collateral cross term or prove `J_T^fin<=(1-c)qT+B`.
