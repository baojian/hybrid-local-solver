## Direction handoffs

- `aesp_cd_l1_rppr`: `prop:aesp-cd-euclidean-packing-fails` uses the exact
  safeguarded recurrence on one edge with seed `1`, `rho=9/20`,
  `alpha=q^2/(1+q^2)`, and `0<q<=1/100`. The optimum has full support. Stage
  one has no correction; stage two is a full collateral correction with
  positive defect. Its normalized collateral charge is asymptotic to
  `(44/9)q`, while the same-step Euclidean log-error decrease is asymptotic to
  `(88/9)q^2`; the first-two-stage prefix has the same `Theta(q^2)` mismatch.
  Thus no `K(q)=o(1/q)`, including no alpha-independent or polylogarithmic
  coefficient, proves the proposed Euclidean-log packing. This does not refute
  multistep-collapse packing, a different potential, a local surrogate, or
  every cumulative normalized-inflation bound.

  `prop:notched-sun-fresh-dense-coded-table-charge` uses the prescribed
  high-cut-rank notched-double-sun structural epoch. For each `n` it chooses an
  `n`-dependent `alpha_n^star > max(alpha_bar_(n,beta),3/4)`. After the full
  event tuple is fixed, it draws mutually independent fresh Gaussian-source
  and hash/sign-target layers. The schedule
  `delta_k=delta/(k(k+1))` is summable and includes all source/target

  caps is `O_gamma(sum_k B_kR_k)`. Source construction, anchor right-hand
  sides/solves, reads, randomness, membership, workspace, validation, and
  output remain explicit. Because each frontier is a singleton, all source

  `O(p_k+r_k)` arithmetic; the obstruction is the table write, not a claimed
