## Direction handoffs

### `aesp_cd_l1_rppr`: realized support-entry shielding

- Claim-status change: `lem:aesp-cd-support-entry-shield` distinguishes newly
  positive rows
  `E_t=supp(x_t) minus supp(x_(t-1))` from persistent rows
  `P_t=supp(x_t) intersect supp(x_(t-1))` for every actual finite stage
  `t>=2`.
- Exact entry inequality:
  `0<=g_(t,E_t)<=beta_A a_(t-1,E_t)`, hence
  `G_t^ent<=beta_A C_end,t-1` and
  `alpha Delta_t=max(G_t^ent,G_t^per)`.
- Consequence: exact shifted solves have zero positive first-entry violation.
  If `G_t^ent>G_t^per` in the finite recurrence, the entire common
  correction is residual-controlled:
  `alpha Delta_t<=beta_A C_end,t-1`.
- Scope: this is a statement about the realized recurrence. It shows that the
  raw support-indexed discontinuity from Round024 does not become an
  uncontrolled entry jump. It gives no control on persistent-row stages.

### `aesp_cd_l1_rppr`: one-sided finite same-state interface

- Claim-status change: `lem:aesp-cd-one-sided-correction-excess` compares the
  actual correction with the residual-free driver at the same realized
  states, without comparing two ambient trajectories.
- Put
  `alpha barDelta_t=kappa_A||D_A^(-1/2)[beta_A s_(t-1)-(1+beta_A)s_t]_+||inf`
  and
  `delta_t=(beta_A/alpha)||D_A^(-1/2)a_(t-1,A)||inf`. Then
  `Delta_t<=barDelta_t+delta_t` and
  `0<=[r_t-barr_t]_+<=delta_t D^(1/2)1` coordinatewise.
- Under `vol(A_t)<=V` and the dual absolute polish at stage `t-1`,
  `||[r_t-barr_t]_+||_2<=beta_A mu_t xi_(t-1)/alpha`, while the corresponding
  momentum excess is at most
  `(1-q)mu_t xi_(t-1)/(q alpha)`.
- Scope: the factor is polynomial and occurs for one following stage. The
  current residual and boundary multiplier can suppress a same-state
  correction, so the result is deliberately one-sided and is neither an
  absolute trajectory-distance estimate nor finite net packing.

### `aesp_cd_l1_rppr`: affordable entry-dominated prefix ledger

- Claim-status change: `cor:aesp-cd-entry-inflation-ledger` controls all
  entry-dominated positive inflation before the fresh gate, independently of
  how many support entries occur.
- Let `eta_gate=2 alpha tau/(1+alpha)` and impose locally at every finite
  shifted solve
  `C_end,t<=delta alpha eta_gate^2 q^2`. On a prefix satisfying
  `H_rho(x_t)>alpha tau`, every entry-dominated stage obeys
  `log max(1,gamma_t^fin)<=4 delta q`, hence
  `sum_(t in T_ent,t<T) log max(1,gamma_t^fin)<=4 delta qT`.
- The per-stage end-mass requirement alone costs at most
  `V[3+tau_cd^(-1)log_+(2(1-alpha)sqrt(V)/(delta alpha eta_gate^2 q^2))]`
  degree work. The same increasing greedy heap run supplies the coordinate
  updates and cached neighbor rekeys; no global residual scan is introduced.
  With `V<=1/rho` and `T=O_tilde(1/q)`, this is
  `O_tilde(1/(rho q))`.
- Scope: choosing an arbitrarily small fixed `delta` makes the entry charge an
  arbitrarily small multiple of `qT`. Persistent-row-dominated inflation is
  excluded, so no net exponent, graph-uniform solver, or eleven-vector follows.

### `aesp_cd_l1_rppr`: post-full high-pass identity and mixed-mode caveat

- Claim-status change: `prop:aesp-cd-post-full-high-pass` identifies the exact
  two-stage collapse driver after a full correction on a settled positive
  face under exact shifted solves.
- If stage `t` is full and `Q_Ae_t>=0`, Round024 gives `r_(t+1)=0`. Put

- Scope: the stress vector is not claimed reachable. It proves that modal sign
