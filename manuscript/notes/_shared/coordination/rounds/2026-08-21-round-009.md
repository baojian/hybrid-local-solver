## Direction handoffs

### Double-cycle independent feed: backbone-seeded canonical STOP

`propagate_settle_framework` proved
`prop:double-cycle-feed-report-obstruction` on the even simple unweighted graph
with backbone and anchor cycles, edges `b_i-u_i`, `b_i-r_i`, `u_i-f_i`, and
`r_i-w_i`, and a perfect matching on `W`. Its degrees are
`(B,A,R,F,W)=(4,4,2,1,2)`, its charged volume is `18n`, and all four sitewise
cut blocks have rank `n`.

For every backbone seed `s=e_(b_j)`, `alpha in (0,1)`, and `rho>0`, the exact
single-seed canonical all-violations execution satisfies the following
invariant before the first petal or report: every active anchor already has
its relay active. If the next batch is report-free, report quietness gives
`q_i<=2`; at a maximum normalized active anchor `M`, the active anchor and
relay equations give

`t(M-q_i) <= (M-2)/2`, with `t=(1+alpha)/(1-alpha)>1`.

Thus `M<=2`, so every inactive petal demand is nonpositive. If an inactive
anchor becomes positive while its relay is also inactive, the exact inactive
demands show that the relay is positive too and the canonical gate co-admits
it. The invariant closes, every report-free prefix is petal-free, and the
first batch meeting `F`, if any, also meets `W`.

The backbone-seed qualifier is essential. For an anchor seed `s=e_(u_j)` and
`0<rho<(1-alpha)/8`, the first petal demand is strictly positive while every
report demand is negative. A report seed violates report exclusion initially,
a petal seed starts with an attachment, and relay seeds are outside the
proposition. The theorem also makes no claim after `W` enters. It is an
exact-real canonical chronology STOP, not a positive-subset, finite-band,
response-direction, reporter/rank/work, finite-precision, or stability result.

The full ledger is `rem:double-cycle-feed-trace-ledger`. In shared order
`(C_adj,R_adj,R_int,C_pre,C_ctl,C_rec,C_resp,M_pers,M_tmp,C_mat,C_emit)`, any
tested prefix retains its complete paid vector `P_k`. Beyond that prefix,
fresh streamed validation has incremental vector

`(Theta(n),1,0,0,Theta(n),0,0,0,O(1),0,0)`,

while validation using already paid retained rows has incremental vector

`(0,0,0,0,Theta(n),0,0,0,O(1),0,0)`.

The total is `P_k` plus the relevant additive coordinates, with the two memory
coordinates taking the larger peak. All face solves, old-row reads, response
queries, state writes, materialization, and task-required emissions remain in
`P_k`; the proposition itself requires no intermediate output.

### Branch-caterpillar kinetic delta reporter: fixed-policy GO

`adaptive_revisit_control` proved
`thm:branch-caterpillar-kinetic-delta-reporter`. Fix `m>=2`, branch seed
`s=e_(b_1)`, `alpha in (0,1)`, and the family-dependent range
`0<rho<rho_cat(m,alpha)<=1/3`. The policy is exact-KKT positive-subset and
backbone-first: it commits the singleton backbone sequence
`b_2,...,b_m`, deliberately deferring positive off-backbone tips, and then
commits arbitrary nonempty subsets of the currently known strict positive
boundary tips. This is not canonical all-violations and does not cover
arbitrary pre-backbone interleavings.

The named exact-real state `CaterpillarKineticDelta` combines the balanced
tridiagonal transfer tree, the separable last Green column, one monotone scalar
backbone update, a strict crossing-key heap, and persistent positive flags. At
each exact restricted checkpoint it retains the exact all-positive live-tip
set, emits only labels that became strict positive since the previous
checkpoint, answers exact membership queries, and validates every proposed
committed label before returning one charged certificate reply. Equality stays
inactive, and a still-live positive label is not re-emitted merely because its
demand changes. Violation persistence makes the suffix flags exact.

For `2m-1<=J<=3m`, one delta/certificate interaction per committed batch plus
one terminal return has the exhaustive vector

`(Theta(m),Theta(m),J+1,0,O(m log(2+m)),0,`
` O(m log(2+m)),Theta(m),O(m),Theta(m),Theta(m))`

at `eq:branch-caterpillar-kinetic-eleven-vector`. This charges every exposed
row, transfer update, named query, heap/control operation, proposed-label
membership test, stored-row validation, persistent and scratch cell, terminal
recovery and candidate write, delta/certificate label, reply header, and exact
final output. The additive exact-cell work is
`O(m log(2+m))=O(C(S*) log(2+C(S*)))`, with `C(S*)=9m+1`. Any speculative or
repeated membership call, extra round/reply, or extra output is additive.

This is a delta-output theorem only. Requiring a complete current positive-tip
list at every checkpoint may itself emit `Theta(m^2)` labels. The GO excludes
canonical all-violations, arbitrary pre-backbone interleavings, other seeds,
an `m`-uniform positive `rho` range, finite precision, bit complexity,
transfer-product conditioning, `kappa=1`, and every product or graph-uniform
accelerated conclusion.

### Weighted-shock occupancy: exact identity and one-shortcut STOP

`volume_gated_acceleration` proved `lem:weighted-shock-occupation`. For
`vartheta=1-q`, remaining face-optimum gain
`H_t=sum_(s=t)^(T-1) Delta_s`, and every nested transported-center execution,

`sum_(t=0)^(T-1) vartheta^(T-1-t) Delta_t`
` = vartheta^(T-1) H_0`
` + q sum_(t=1)^(T-1) vartheta^(T-1-t) H_t`.

The identity is exact summation by parts; the second term is a remaining-gain
occupancy, not the unweighted shock telescope alone.

The terminal one-edge family at `prop:terminal-edge-no-q-packing` sets
`alpha=q^2`, `beta_edge=(1-q^2)/(1+q^2)`, and
`rho=tau=2 beta_edge/(2 beta_edge+5)`. Starting at the exact singleton optimum
and exact estimate center, it performs one stationary old-face step, the exact
all-violations admission with transported center, and one enlarged-face step.
The terminal KKT certificate is strict, and

`T=2, J=1, nu_fin=2, swept volume=3`,

while its only shock `Delta_0>0` gives

`weighted tail / (q sum_t Delta_t)=(1-q)/q`

in `eq:no-q-shock-packing`. Hence no alpha-independent constant `C` proves
`weighted tail <= C q sum_t Delta_t`, even after demanding a terminal
one-sided certificate. The exhaustive terminal implementation vector is

`(O(2),O(2),1,0,O(5),O(5),O(6),O(2),O(2),O(5),Theta(3))`

at `eq:terminal-edge-eleven-vector`; the single terminal return includes one
certificate and the two-coordinate sparse vector. This constant execution is
an analytical packing obstruction only. It does not refute the occupancy
identity, a terminal-accuracy floor, another potential, or a bound involving
`T` or swept volume, and it gives no product-work or other asymptotic work
lower bound.
