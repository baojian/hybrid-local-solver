## Direction handoffs

### `adaptive_revisit_control`

- **Stable result:** `thm:three-arm-spider-token-countdown`.
- **Exact model:** center-seeded unweighted three-arm spider with arbitrary
  positive arm lengths, `alpha in (0,1)`, `rho in (0,1/3)`, and the exact
  canonical RPPR boundary gate. A legal certified block may contain any
  nonempty subset of the currently positive tips.
- **State and conclusion:** the actual common prefixes, one exact affine
  transfer product per arm, and two root aggregates sequentialize every legal
  batch order without an arm-private trajectory or eager untouched-tip rekey.
  Irreversible activation tokens are interference-monotone and
  own-service-progress with `kappa=1`.
- **Ledger:** adjacency exposure, every forward response/checkpoint update,
  terminal settlement/recovery, three-tip control tests, state writes, peak
  resident cells, and exact listed output are separate and total
  `O(C(S*))=O(1/rho)` exact-cell work and linear exact-cell state.
- **Boundary:** exactly one degree-three branch vertex. The theorem excludes
  two branching vertices, cycles, finite precision and bit complexity,
  RPPR-to-PPR accuracy conversion, and any graph-uniform accelerated-arm
  conclusion.

### `two_rung_direct_theory`

- **Stable result:** `def:direct-fixed-attachment-reporter`,
  `thm:direct-fixed-attachment-cycle-reporter`, and
  `rem:direct-fixed-attachment-scope`.
- **Exact model:** an SPD Stieltjes simple-cycle core with nonnegative load;
  pairwise-disjoint supplied certified pendant trees all attach at one fixed
  core vertex `p`. Still-exterior coordinates remain fixed at zero while the
  reporter returns exact upward crossings of `tau` and `B*tau`.
- **State and conclusion:** cumulative Schur data reduce to two scalars and
  `x_k=x_0+z_k g` for one fixed Green column and nondecreasing `z_k`. `FACR(p)`
  stores static affine crossing lists and two pointers, so no current boundary
  key is globally rekeyed.
- **Ledger:** supplied-certificate verification and adjacency scans, forest
  elimination and final recovery, width-two cycle factorization and Green
  construction/application, stored boundary-row reads, sorting, membership,
  queries, exact validation, final materialization, memory, and streamed output
  total `O(V_fin+|R| log(2+|R|)+Z)` exact-cell work and `O(V_fin)` memory.
- **Boundary:** absorption-only with supplied closure certificates, one fixed
  attachment, and exterior coordinates held at zero. Varying attachments,
  post-repair exterior updates, word/bit complexity, finite precision, and
  numerical stability remain open.

### `volume_gated_acceleration`

- **Stable result:** `prop:one-edge-zero-shock-fails`.
- **Exact model:** endpoint-source edge, `0<alpha<1`, `rho=tau=r`, and
  `0<r<(1-alpha)/(3+alpha)`, under the displayed exact zero-padded safe-gated
  recurrence.
- **Counterexample:** the old-face state is its exact minimizer with zero
  momentum, the other endpoint is the unique safe admission, and the first
  enlarged-face step retains objective gap
  `(2 eta^2/a) Delta_B>0`. Therefore a fixed-face-exact energy cannot satisfy
  a universal shock-free pointwise carryover inequality.
- **Ledger:** the old-face residual/envelope check, cut-edge gate and admission,
  two history zero-writes, two-coordinate enlarged recurrence and persistent
  state, validation, and optional two-coordinate output are all constant and
  explicitly charged.
- **Boundary:** not a cumulative-shock, multistep-transport, or newly
  admitted-volume obstruction, and not an asymptotic work lower bound.
