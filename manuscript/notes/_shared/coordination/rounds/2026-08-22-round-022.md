## Frozen target/audit contract

This contract is the acceptance test for a future theorem. It is not itself a
solver result.

- **Input/model:** every finite simple undirected unweighted graph with no
  isolates, given by adjacency lists; sparse nonnegative seed `s` with
  `1^T s=1`; `alpha in (0,1]`; `eps_ppr in (0,1)`; deterministic exact-real or
  algebraic-cell arithmetic.
- **Output/accuracy:** return a finite-support vector `x_hat` and one
  note-scoped certificate such that
  `||D^(-1/2)(x_hat-x^0)||_infinity<=eps_ppr`, equivalently the shared
  degree-normalized PPR error. Set `rho=tau=eps_ppr/2`.
- **Route:** for `alpha<1/4`, use full-space safeguarded AESP-CD on the one
  fixed objective `F_rho`, with dynamic greedy normalized-KKT exploration,
  safe lower retraction, cached exposed rows, and the complete gate. For
  `alpha>=1/4`, use the declared zero-start monotone RPPR coordinate-descent
  fallback with `O(1/eps_ppr)` work. No graph preprocessing is allowed, and
  there is one terminal external interaction.
- **Terminal bridge:** return a safe lower point `ell` whose complete gate
  verifies `kappa_(rho,i)(ell)>=-alpha tau sqrt(d_i)` for every coordinate.
  This yields RPPR error at most `tau`; `rho+tau=eps_ppr` yields the PPR target.
- **Resource target:** put `V_eps=nnz(s)+2/eps_ppr`,
  `W_eps=nnz(s)+O_tilde(1/(sqrt(alpha) eps_ppr))`, and
  `k=nnz(x_hat)<=2/eps_ppr`. In the shared order
  `(C_adj,R_adj,R_int,C_pre,C_ctl,C_rec,C_resp,M_pers,M_tmp,C_mat,C_emit)`,
  require
  `(O(V_eps),O(V_eps),1,0,O(W_eps),O(W_eps),0,O(V_eps),O(V_eps),O(W_eps),Theta(k+1))`,
  with `C_ctl+C_rec+C_mat=O(W_eps)` jointly. Soft factors may contain only
  logarithms in `1/alpha`, `1/eps_ppr`, and `V_eps`. Finite precision and bit
  complexity are excluded.
- **Central proof obligation:** for
  `q=sqrt(alpha/(1-alpha))`, the **finite relative-inner recurrence** must
  satisfy `Phi_T<=exp(-c q T+I_T)Phi_0` with `I_T` polylogarithmic, or an
  equivalent `-Omega(qT)` net exponent. An exact-shifted-minimizer recurrence
  is insufficient. Finite-inner reset robustness, rate-to-gate integration,
  cached heap/gate implementation, the complete eleven-vector, terminal
  bridge, and large-`alpha` fallback must all be proved and charged.

The independent contract audit selected Route B only in this qualified sense:
its fixed full-space objective avoids Route A's changing restricted objective
and explicit face-reset interface. It did not certify the missing packing,
implementation, or terminal seams.

## Direction handoffs

### Hybrid Route A: exact settled reset/drop boundary

- Stable authority:
  `prop:path-three-settled-reset-drop-obstruction`,
  `lem:settled-reset-optimum-drop-bound`,
  `eq:settled-actual-shock-drop-bound`, and
  `eq:branch-caterpillar-literal-reset-product-count`.
- On endpoint-seeded `P_3`, set `rho=3/10`, `alpha=s/(1+s)`,
  `q_r=sqrt(alpha/(1-alpha))`, and `0<s<1/12`. Exact settlement on the first
  face gives a positive admission demand; settlement on the enlarged face
  gives a negative remaining boundary demand. Thus the canonical exact trace
  has exactly one admission.
- At that settled zero-momentum admission,
  `B_1/Delta_1=3/(4q_r^2)+O(1)`. Consequently neither an alpha-uniform
  `O(Delta_1)` payment nor an `O(Delta_1/q_r)` payment can bound the declared
  observable KKT reset budget.
- This is not an obstruction of the same size for the actual estimate shock.
  The analytical settled shock satisfies
  `Sigma_es<2 Delta_1`; the `Theta(1/alpha)` loss diagnoses looseness of the
  observable KKT upper budget. That budget enters the imported relative-inner
  stage work only through a logarithm, so no accelerated-work lower bound
  follows.
- The surviving general result for settled zero-momentum safe expansions is
  `sum_j B_j<=((3-5alpha)/(alpha(1-alpha)))sum_j Delta_j`. Its
  `Theta(1/alpha)` order is sharp on `P_3`, but it says nothing about the
  nonsettled displacement term present in the Round021 trajectory.
- A stronger nested exact-settlement/lower-center telescope remains
  conditional and has coefficient `Theta(alpha^-2)`; its order and residual
  premises are not proved for the actual nonsettled greedy execution and it is
  not promoted as a Route-A closure.
- If the existing conservative two-product register is invoked without any
  sharing after all `m` canonical caterpillar admissions, it reads exactly
  `2 sum_(j=1)^m V_j=6m^2+12m-6` stored row cells. This is only the literal
  named-interface cost. Product sharing, another representation, or another
  potential may improve it; it is not a class lower bound.

### Full-space Route B: scalar-ledger insufficiency and finite late inflation

- Stable authority: `prop:aesp-cd-ledger-only-insufficient`,
  `eq:aesp-cd-fixed-operator-relation`,
  `prop:aesp-cd-p4-late-inflation`, `eq:aesp-cd-p4-metrics`, and
  `eq:aesp-cd-p7-scaffolding`.
- The abstract self-similar sequence satisfies the current safe chain,
  start-mass, correction-mass, collapse, defect, and defective-contraction
  ledgers. With `N=ceil(q^-2)` two-step cycles, it has
  `I_(2N)=Omega(q^-1)` while `log(Phi_0/Phi_(2N))=O(1)`. Therefore those
  scalar ledgers alone cannot imply
  `I_T=O(q^2 T+log(Phi_0/Phi_T))` or soft-logarithmic inflation packing.
- The abstraction has no fixed Stieltjes operator `Q`, RPPR objective,
  boundary multipliers, or exact proximal map. It is not an RPPR trajectory
  and does not refute Route B. A valid proof must use the actual active-row
  relation `Qe_t=kappa_A s_t` and the inactive-row complementarity coupled
  through the same fixed operator over multiple stages.
- On endpoint-seeded `P_4` with `q=1/8`, `alpha=1/65`, and `rho=7/40`, the
  iterate support equals the optimal support from stage 7 onward, but positive
  inflation continues through stage 498. Through `T=500`, it has 135
  nonzero corrections, 44 positive-inflation stages, and
  `I_500 approximately 1.0236455928394953`.
- The exact endpoint-seeded `P_7` corroboration has fixed support after stage
  9, positive inflation through stage 796, 122 corrections, 39 positive
  stages, and `I_800 approximately 7.2945227043682557`.
- These finite traces refute only attribution of positive inflation one-for-
  one to support additions and the assumption that it disappears after final
  support discovery. They prove no infinite periodicity, exponent or
  polylogarithmic packing obstruction, finite-inner robustness, work theorem,
  or eleven-resource vector. Ideal exact shifted solves cannot be treated as
  a free implementation.
