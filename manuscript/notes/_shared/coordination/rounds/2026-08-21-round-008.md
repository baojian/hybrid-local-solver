### Canonical petal-cycle chronology: STOP

`propagate_settle_framework` proved
`prop:petal-cycle-relay-first-obstruction` on the even simple unweighted graph
with anchor and petal cycles, edges `u_i f_i`, two-edge stems `u_i r_i w_i`,
and a perfect matching on the reports. The degrees are `(4,3,2,2)`,
`cvol(V)=15n`, and the raw `AF`, `AR`, and `RW` blocks each have rank `n`.

For every `alpha in (0,1)`, `rho>0`, and single-seed orbit, let `U` be a
positive canonical face with `W intersect U=empty` and `R subseteq U`, and let
its next canonical all-violations batch `B` also satisfy
`B intersect W=empty`. Report quietness gives `q_i<=2`; the relay equation
gives the anchor cap `p_i<=4+8t`. If a nonseed petal were inactive, its anchor
equation would require
`p_(i-1)+p_(i+1)>8+16t`, contradicting the two neighboring caps. Thus all but
at most the petal paired with an anchor or relay seed are already in `U`.
Positive `A,F,R,W` seed loads require respectively
`rho<1/4,1/3,1/2,1/2`; a positive report seed violates report exclusion and a
nonpositive seed load gives the zero solution. If a report enters with or
before the last relay, or in the first post-last-relay batch, the desired
epoch has already failed. Otherwise at least `n-1` petals precede or accompany
the last relay.

The isolated threshold reversal is nevertheless exact: a relay crosses at
`p_i>4`, while a still-isolated petal crosses at `p_i>6`. The global balance,
not a collapsed local threshold or low-rank cut, stops the chronology.

The shared vector order is
`(C_adj,R_adj,R_int,C_pre,C_ctl,C_rec,C_resp,M_pers,M_tmp,C_mat,C_emit)`.
Beyond a complete paid prefix `P_U`, fresh streamed validation has incremental
vector

`(Theta(n),1,0,0,Theta(n),0,0,0,O(1),0,0)`,

while validation from already paid retained rows has incremental vector

`(0,0,0,0,Theta(n),0,0,0,O(1),0,0)`.

The full trace prefix retains every earlier adjacency/interaction,
preprocessing, gate, recurrence/response, memory, materialization, validation,
and output charge. An actual implementation adds its own numerical trace
ledger. The proposition itself emits no intermediate batch or vector.

Scope: exact-real KKT chronology for the canonical all-violations gate only.
Arbitrary positive-subset policies can defer currently positive petals. This
is not a reporter, response-rank, scan/output, finite-band, finite-precision,
bit-complexity, or stability lower bound.

### Branch-caterpillar eager keys: representation-specific STOP

`adaptive_revisit_control` proved
`prop:branch-caterpillar-eager-rekey-obstruction`. For each fixed `m>=2`, the
branch vertices `b_1,...,b_m` form a path; the endpoints have two nonempty
pendant arms and every internal branch has one, so every branch degree is
three. The single seed is `s=e_(b_1)`, `|V|=3m+1`, and
`C(V)=9m+1`. For fixed `m` and `alpha in (0,1)`, the explicit threshold
`0<rho<rho_cat(m,alpha)<=1/3` makes the exact-KKT positive-subset singleton
order

`b_2,...,b_m; a_1,...,a_m; r_1,...,r_(m+1)`

legal. It is not the canonical all-violations batch, which may co-admit the
deliberately deferred positive leaves, and no positive lower bound on
`rho_cat(m,alpha)` uniform in `m` is claimed. The order reaches the whole true
support and an `m`-vertex SPD tridiagonal core.

`EagerTipKey` retains one separately addressed exact cell for every live tip
KKT demand and rewrites every cell whose value changed before the next reply.
Each of the `m` long-arm admissions changes all `m+1` deferred leaf keys, so
the representation performs at least `m(m+1)` old-key writes. The first
long-arm append is already the failed linear activation-countdown step.

A literal sequential implementation has full eleven-vector

`(Theta(m),Theta(m),3m+1,0,Theta(m^2),0,Theta(m^2),Theta(m),O(m),Theta(m),Theta(m))`.

Here `C_ctl` contains eager key indexing, tests, and writes; `C_resp` separately
contains the chosen repeated tridiagonal sweeps; there is no preprocessing or
recurrence direction; persistent and scratch peaks are separate; terminal
materialization, stored-row validation, singleton certificate replies, and the
exact output are charged. If the external task requests the complete positive
tip list at every checkpoint, repeated list output changes the last coordinate
to `C_emit=Theta(m^2)`.

`rem:branch-caterpillar-implicit-transfer-comparator` is the essential open
comparator. A balanced product tree over constant-size affine transfer cells
supports one append/update or named-tip demand query in
`O(log(2+m))` exact response work with `O(m)` retained cells. With `q` named
queries, it costs `O((m+q)log(2+m))+O(m)` terminal recovery. It represents the
dense optimum shift without eager coordinate or key rewrites, but it is not a
charge-comparable dynamic all-positive-tip reporter.

Scope: fixed-`m`, branch-seeded, exact-real, positive-subset chronology and the
named literal `EagerTipKey` state only. Lazy affine, sign-persistence,
kinetic/group, and on-demand implicit reporters, canonical all-violations
orders, finite precision, bit complexity, and transfer-product conditioning
remain open.

### Transported endpoint-path center: structural GO

`volume_gated_acceleration` proved the exact identities in
`prop:transported-schur-ledger`. For a safe expansion `U+ = U union B`, with
`d=x_(U+)*-x_U*` and exact Schur gain `Delta_B`,

`E_(U+)(x,v+d)=E_U(x,v)+Delta_B`.

The gains telescope as restricted optimal-value drops, while their weighted
appearance in the accelerated recurrence need not decay if a shock arrives
late. The original zero-padded recurrence still has an uncontrolled signed
cross term.

`prop:path-implicit-transport-ledger` gives the exact-real endpoint-prefix
implementation. It stores centered momentum `w=v-x_U*` and an append-only
tridiagonal `LDL^T` response. At admission, one factor record, one zero primal
cell, and one zero centered-momentum cell represent the dense transport without
rewriting the old prefix. Let `J` be the number of admissions,
`nu_fin=vol(U_T)`, `n_fin=|U_T|`, and
`mathfrak V_T=sum_(t=0)^(T-1) vol(U_t)`. Its exhaustive vector is

`(O(nu_fin),O(J+1),1,0,`
` O(mathfrak V_T+nu_fin),O(mathfrak V_T+nu_fin),`
` O(mathfrak V_T+J+nu_fin),O(nu_fin),O(nu_fin),`
` O(mathfrak V_T+n_fin),Theta(n_fin+1))`.

The one external interaction is the terminal certificate/output stage. The
terminal candidate is charged to `C_mat`; the certificate plus exact sparse
output cells are charged separately to `C_emit`. The factor append, Schur
query, gate/control, and zero-state appends specific to expansion cost
`O(sum_t vol(U_(t+1) setminus U_t))`. Every old-face recurrence read, response
application, envelope scan, state write, final validation, and materialization
remains in the displayed `mathfrak V_T` or `nu_fin` coordinates.

The cancellation identity applies to a general safe interior face expansion;
the charged implementation scope is internally gated exact-real endpoint-path
RPPR with `R_int=1` and one terminal external certificate/output stage.
Intermediate external emissions are excluded. There is no bound on `T`, the
late weighted shock tail, or `mathfrak V_T`; no theorem for the literal
zero-padded recurrence, branching/cyclic/general graphs, graph-uniform
accelerated work, finite precision, or bit complexity. PPR conversion occurs
only after a terminal one-sided certificate with
`rho=tau=eps_ppr/2`.
