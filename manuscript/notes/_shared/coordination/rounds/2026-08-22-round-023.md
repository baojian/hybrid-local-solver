## Direction handoffs

### `aesp_cd_l1_rppr`: exact fixed-operator tail

- Claim-status change: `prop:aesp-cd-p4-infinite-inflation` upgrades the finite
  Round022 `P_4` observation to a theorem for every horizon.
- Evidence pointer: from stage 44, the exact shifted-minimizer recurrence has
  the eleven-stage word `F N N F N^6 P_0`. The harmful phases are
  `47+11j`, and each satisfies `log gamma>=315/33280`. Thus
  `I_(44+11N)>=(315/33280)N` and `I_T=Theta(T)`.
- Simultaneous convergence: at word boundaries,
  `||e_(55+11j)||_infinity<=(11/500)||e_(44+11j)||_infinity`.
  Positive-density harmful inflation therefore is not a convergence
  obstruction on this instance.
- Work/accuracy qualifiers: this is one exact fixed RPPR objective with
  `q=1/8`, `alpha=1/65`, `rho=7/40`, optimal support `{0,1,2}`, and ideal exact
  shifted solves. It refutes horizon-uniform fixed-support/fixed-parameter and
  transient-only inflation bounds. It does not refute accuracy-logarithmic or
  polylogarithmic terminal dependence, a bound
  `I_T<=(1-c)qT+B`, small-`q` scaling, finite-inner robustness, or an
  end-to-end solver. No eleven-vector is attached to the exact cone.
- Exact certificate: the active shifted map `M`, partial-row retraction `R_0`,
  and four-ray cone verify every phase, unique-row/cap decision, and boundary
  inequality with a strict rational margin. The word map sends the cone
  strictly into itself, and exact monomial intervals give the defect,
  potential, and inflation floor.

### `aesp_cd_l1_rppr`: finite-inner and terminal contract

- Exact residual interface: `lem:aesp-cd-finite-inner-identities` puts the end
  residual `a_t` into the active fixed-row equation, next start mass, and next
  collapse. The safe nested chain survives increasing greedy finite solves,
  but the exact identities cannot be reused with `a_t` erased.
- Relative-stop obstruction: `prop:aesp-cd-c2-residual-stop` gives a scalar
  quadratic satisfying Catalyst C2 at equality while retaining nonzero
  residual with residual/displacement ratio `Theta(sqrt(q))`. This is a
  logical interface STOP, not a fixed-RPPR rate lower bound.
- Dual stop: `cor:aesp-cd-dual-inner-stop` requires both C2 and
  `C_end<=mu_t xi/sqrt(V)`. It gives shifted-solve Euclidean error at most
  `xi`, with additional degree work at most
  `V[3+tau_cd^(-1)log_+(2V/xi)]`; for the safeguarded RPPR envelope,
  `V<=1/rho`.
- Conditional rate: if the **actual finite** trajectory satisfies
  `J_T^fin<=(1-c)qT+B`, `cor:aesp-cd-conditional-finite-acceptance` chooses
  computable `T` and `xi` and reaches the finite potential threshold. Neither
  C2 nor the exact invariant cone proves this assumption.
- Unconditional terminal bridge: the fresh complete unshifted KKT gate at a
  safe lower point gives degree-normalized RPPR error at most `tau`; the
  Stieltjes bias comparison adds at most `rho`, so
  `rho=tau=eps_ppr/2` reaches the shared PPR target. This correctness statement
  is independent of the rate used to reach the gate.
- Conditional accelerated resource vector: for `alpha<1/4`, additionally
  assume `c>=c_0>0` and soft-logarithmic declared `B`. Put
  `V_eps=nnz(s)+2/eps_ppr`,

  shared order the vector is
  `(O(V_eps),O(V_eps),1,0,O(W_eps),O(W_eps),0,O(V_eps),O(V_eps),O(W_eps),Theta(k+1))`,

  the actual-finite net exponent.
