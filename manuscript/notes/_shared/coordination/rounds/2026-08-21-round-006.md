## Direction handoffs

### `propagate_settle_framework`

- **Proved result.** For even `n>=4`, the matched-report sun has anchor,
  petal, and report degrees `4,1,2`; moreover
  `Q_AW=-(vartheta/sqrt(8))I` has rank `n`. For every `alpha in (0,1)` and
  `rho>0`, however, any canonical single-seed RPPR prefix with every face
  report-free contains at most one `F` petal. Report quietness bounds every
  anchor by `4 alpha rho/vartheta`; a positive nonseed petal would require its
  anchor to exceed `2 alpha rho/vartheta`, and the exact nonseed anchor
  equation contradicts that band and the neighboring upper bounds.
- **Seed and batch split.** Every nonpositive shifted seed load gives the zero
  optimum. A positive report seed violates `W intersect U=empty` initially; a
  positive petal seed is the sole initial `F` exception; and a positive anchor
  seed can admit only its matched petal while the next face stays report-free.
  Both the current face and committed next batch are required to avoid `W`.
- **Ledger.** `rem:matched-report-sun-trace-ledger` preserves the complete
  eleven-coordinate prefix
  `(C_adj,R_adj,R_int,C_pre,C_ctl,C_rec,C_resp,M_pers,M_tmp,C_mat,C_emit)`.
  It distinguishes a fresh streamed `Theta(n)` row audit from reuse of
  already charged persistent records, includes all face/Schur/response work in
  the prefix, and makes scan, materialization, and emitted-label charges depend
  on the implementation and task rather than on co-admission alone.
- **Scope and redistribution.** This is only a canonical-trace legality
  obstruction. It is not a reporter, response-rank/work, finite-band,
  finite-precision, or numerical-stability lower bound. The next legal-trace
  candidate should attenuate each report through a bounded-degree stem with an
  active relay and an unadmitted designated endpoint.

### `adaptive_revisit_control`

- **Proved result.** On the unweighted double-Y seeded at branch vertex `o`,
  with `alpha in (0,1)` and `rho in (0,1/3)`, four actual pendant-prefix
  affine records couple through a scalar Schur core before the other branch
  `h` is admitted and through a fixed SPD rank-two core afterward. Every legal
  certified batch order updates the same irreversible common state, with no
  private replay or eager materialized boundary rekey.
- **Forced rank-two regime.** With
  `zeta=(1-alpha)/(1+alpha)` and
  `rho_link=zeta/[3(3+zeta)]`, the initial exact violation at `h` is positive
  exactly when `rho<rho_link`. Violation persistence then forces every legal
  order to admit `h` and enter the rank-two phase.
- **Ledger and countdown.** The theorem separately charges adjacency
  (`C_adj<=vol(S*)+8`), gate/control, response construction, update and query,
  state writes, persistent and transient cells, canonical recovery,
  validation, and exact output. Fixed exact cell-operation templates give an
  irreversible activation-token countdown with `kappa=1` and total
  `O(C(S*))=O(1/rho)` exact-real work for every legal batch order. Terminal
  certification, recovery, validation, and output are explicit paid phases.
- **Scope and redistribution.** The theorem fixes the branch seed `s=e_o`
  and one two-vertex core. Other seeds, a growing branch backbone, cycles,
  finite precision, bit complexity, RPPR-to-PPR conversion, a graph-uniform
  accelerated arm, and an independent fully charged Phase-I bound remain
  open.

### `local_solver_oracle_hierarchy`

- **Common task.** On endpoint `P_n`, `n>=8`, set `alpha=n^-2` and
  `eps_ppr=1/10`. Both arms must emit an explicit sparse vector whose exact
  final support-plus-boundary verifier confirms
  `||D^(-1/2)(b-Q*x_hat)||_infinity<=alpha/10` after one solve/output
  interaction.
- **Counteralgorithm.** The five normalized nonzero coordinates
  `alpha*(60,40,24,12,4)/11` have six possibly nonzero scaled residuals
  `alpha*(1-50*alpha,1-41*alpha,1-25*alpha,1-13*alpha,
  1-5*alpha,1-alpha)/11`, all strictly between zero and `alpha/10`.
  Endpoint Krylov triangularity puts this vector in `K_5(Q,b)`, so an
  admissible degree-four `CertPrefixPoly` execution constructs it with four
  constant-prefix row actions.
- **Same-task response and ledger.** A five-row append-only `LDL^T` response
  emits the identical vector. Equations
  `eq:certificate-prefix-poly-resource-vector` and
  `eq:certificate-path-response-resource-vector` report all eleven resources:
  adjacency, adjacency adaptivity, interaction, preprocessing, control,
  recurrence, response, persistent memory, scratch, materialization, and
  emission. Every coordinate is `O(1)`, preprocessing is zero, the output has
  five cells, and the realized final exposed set `[6]` has `nu_fin=11`.
- **Scope and redistribution.** This refutes both
  `Omega(nu_fin/sqrt(alpha))` and ambient

  class/task. It does not weaken the exact full-vector `DiagSpecPoly(r)`

  finite-precision consequence.
