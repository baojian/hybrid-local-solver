## Direction handoffs

### `aesp_cd_l1_rppr`: lower-retraction shadowing STOP

- Claim-status change: `prop:aesp-cd-retraction-shadowing-stop` proves that the
  support-indexed lower retraction is discontinuous at a support entry and has
  sharp fixed-positive-support amplification `1+1/alpha`.
- Exact witness: on endpoint-seeded `P_2`, put
  `lambda=(1+alpha)/2`, `nu=(1-alpha)/2`, `rho=nu/4`,
  `a=(alpha/8,0)`, and `a^eta=(alpha/8,eta)`.  The negative row-one lower
  residual is excluded from `supp(a)`, so `L(a)=a`; once `eta>0`, it enters
  and sets `Delta=nu/8+(lambda/alpha)eta`, which clips both coordinates and
  gives `L(a^eta)=0`.  The limiting jump is `alpha/8`.
- Fixed-face witness: the explicit full-support points in
  `eq:aesp-cd-retraction-sharp-amplification` keep the same unique controlling
  row and remain unclipped, attaining the factor `1+1/alpha`; row absolute
  sum one gives the matching upper bound.
- Scope: a proof that iterates only this ambient factor over `T` stages needs
  exponentially small uniform perturbations and inserts a
  `V T^2 log(1/alpha)` term into the certified inner-work ledger.  This stops
  that black-box proof template only.  It is not instability of the actual
  finite recurrence, a direct-packing counterexample, or a solver lower bound.

### `aesp_cd_l1_rppr`: full-correction separation

- Claim-status change: `lem:aesp-cd-full-correction-separation` now states its
  face and stage indices explicitly: `A=S*(rho)`, `supp(x_t)=A`,
  `ell_t=x_t`, and `Q_A(x*-x_t)>=0`; exactness applies to the solve producing
  `x_(t+1)=p(x_t)` after that full center.
- Exact map: with `H_A=Q_A+kappa_A I`,
  `M_A=kappa_A H_A^(-1)`, and
  `S_A=(1+beta_A)M_A-beta_A I`, one has
  `S_A>=q beta_A I` spectrally, `S_A>=0` entrywise, and
  `S_A Q_A=Q_A S_A`.  Hence the next exact trial is already a lower
  certificate and has no correction.
- Finite interface: if the next finite output remains positive on `A` and zero
  off it, its end residual `a_(t+1)=H_A[p(x_t)-x_(t+1)]` gives
  `alpha Delta_(t+1)<=2(1-q)||D_A^(-1/2)a_(t+1)||_infinity`
  `<=2(1-q)C_end,t+1`.  The constant follows from
  `M_A D_A^(1/2)1<=(1-q^2)D_A^(1/2)1` and
  `(1+beta_A)(1-q^2)=2(1-q)`.
- Scope: exact full corrections are separated and a finite adjacent correction
  is residual-only.  Partial-correction density, cumulative inflation, and the
  actual-finite net exponent remain uncontrolled.

### `aesp_cd_l1_rppr`: high-Dirichlet promised class

- Claim-status change: `thm:aesp-cd-high-dirichlet-branch` closes the
  nonempty-optimal-face class
  `theta_A=lambda_min(Q_A)/(kappa_A+lambda_min(Q_A))>=c0*q` without any
  inflation-packing assumption.
- Direct recurrence: every finite safeguarded stage satisfying
  `||p(ell_t)-x_(t+1)||_2<=xi_t` obeys
  `||x*-x_(t+1)||_2<=(1-theta_A)||x*-x_t||_2+xi_t`.
  With `eta_gate=2 alpha tau/(1+alpha)`,
  `xi_t<=c0 q eta_gate/2`, and
  `T>=(c0 q)^(-1)log(2/eta_gate)`, the fresh gate is at most `alpha tau` and
  the RPPR error is at most `tau`.
- Promised-class resource vector: for `alpha<1/4`, `0<eps_ppr<=1`, and
  `rho=tau=eps_ppr/2`, put
  `V_eps=nnz(s)+2/eps_ppr`,
  `W_eps=nnz(s)+O_tilde(1/(sqrt(alpha) eps_ppr))`, and
  `k<=2/eps_ppr`.  In the shared order the vector is
  `(O(V_eps),O(V_eps),1,0,O(W_eps),O(W_eps),0,O(V_eps),O(V_eps),O(W_eps),Theta(k+1))`,
  with `C_ctl+C_rec+C_mat=O(W_eps)` jointly.
- Scope: the vector is unconditional on the a-posteriori promised structural
  class, but `A` is unknown and the condition is not algorithmically
  certified.  This is not a graph-uniform solver theorem.  Finite precision
  and bit complexity remain outside the exact-real/algebraic-cell contract.
