\begin{abstract}
This note independently reconstructs and studies the exact one-step Lyapunov
master form for a clipped accelerated proximal recurrence.  It separates
proved identities, finite exact decisions, structural sufficient conditions,
and open graph-family claims.  At registration time no general nonpositivity
theorem is asserted.
\end{abstract}

\input{../../tex/shared/source_aligned_problem}

\section{Question and claim boundary}

The target is a proof that the master form is nonpositive on a nontrivial
infinite graph family, or a precise structural condition or counterexample.
Finite enumeration and floating sampling remain measured evidence unless
converted into exact arguments.
