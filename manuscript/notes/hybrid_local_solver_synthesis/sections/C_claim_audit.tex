\subsection{Statements deliberately left conditional or parameterized}

The note does not assert any of the following without an added theorem:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item a graph-uniform bound on the AESP constant \(R\);
\item monotonicity of the original weighted residual mass across Catalyst
      outer iterations;
\item a graph-uniform \(\Lambda_t=\mathcal O(1/\eps)\) bound for FIFO
      AESP-LOCAPPR;
\item local accelerated work for plain optimal SOR;
\item a valid transfer of Catalyst momentum into LocCH;
\item a graph-independent
      \(\softO(1/(\rho\sqrt\alpha))\) RPPR work theorem from an arbitrary
      Catalyst handoff;
\item active-set identification in RPPR without a nondegeneracy or
      strict-complementarity hypothesis;
\item acceptance of a TB-Science proposal before reviewer feedback and
      frontier-agent calibration.
\end{itemize}
