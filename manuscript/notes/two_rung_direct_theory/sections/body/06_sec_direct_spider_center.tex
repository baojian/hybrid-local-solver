\section{A branching obstruction: the spider center echo}
\label{sec:direct-spider-center}

The path certificate depends on equal interior rank weights. It fails at a
branch even before different waves return from positive depth. Consider a
symmetric spider with center $c$, $q\geq3$ arms of length at least two, and
seed $s=e_c$. The center has degree $q$, while every first arm vertex
$v_i$ has degree two. In normalized residual coordinates, put
\begin{equation}
 \zeta:=\frac{\alpha}{q},
 \qquad
 w_q:=\frac{q}{q+1},
 \qquad
 \beta_\lambda:=\frac23\eta
 =\frac{2\lambda}{3(1+\lambda^2)}.
 \label{eq:direct-spider-parameters}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Exact center-only prefix]
\label{prop:direct-spider-center-prefix}
Suppose the first $t$ optimal-SOR pushes are all at the center. Immediately
after them,
\begin{align}
 y_c^{(t)}&=(-\lambda^2)^t\zeta,
 \label{eq:direct-spider-center-residual}\\
 y_{v_i}^{(t)}&=eta\bigl(1-(-\lambda^2)^t\bigr)\zeta,
 \qquad i=1,\ldots,q.
 \label{eq:direct-spider-arm-residual}
\end{align}
All other residuals vanish. Consequently, the center and each first arm
vertex have ranks
\begin{align}
 p_c^{(t)}&=w_q\lambda^{2t}\zeta,
 \label{eq:direct-spider-center-rank}\\
 p_{v_i}^{(t)}&=\beta_\lambda
 \bigl(1-(-\lambda^2)^t\bigr)\zeta.
 \label{eq:direct-spider-arm-rank}
\end{align}
Thus, while the displayed entries remain above the spreading gate, refreshed
ranking continues to push the center exactly while
\begin{equation}
 w_q\lambda^{2t}>
 \beta_\lambda\bigl(1-(-\lambda^2)^t\bigr).
 \label{eq:direct-spider-center-condition}
\end{equation}
An exact score tie only delegates the next operation to the implementation's
tie rule.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Initially $y_c^{(0)}=\zeta$. A center push multiplies its residual by
$-\lambda^2$ and sends $\lambda y_c$ to each degree-two arm target. Therefore
\begin{equation*}
 y_{v_i}^{(t)}
 =\lambda\zeta\sum_{j=0}^{t-1}(-\lambda^2)^j
 =\frac{\lambda}{1+\lambda^2}
   \bigl(1-(-\lambda^2)^t\bigr)\zeta.
\end{equation*}
This proves the residual formulas. Multiply their magnitudes by the rank
weights $q/(q+1)$ and $2/3$ from \cref{eq:direct-path-rank}. No deeper arm
coordinate has received a packet, so comparing the displayed ranks proves
\cref{eq:direct-spider-center-condition}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The branch consumes a full accelerated revisit budget]
\label{cor:direct-spider-center-echo}
For every fixed $q\geq3$ and $Bq\epsppr<1/4$, the spreading gate does not
truncate the center-only prefix for all sufficiently small $\alpha$. As
$\alpha\downarrow0$, the number $T_q$ of consecutive center pushes before an
arm first has at least the center's rank satisfies
\begin{equation}
 T_q
 =\Theta\!\left(\frac1{\sqrt\alpha}\right),
 \qquad
 T_q\sqrt\alpha
 \longrightarrow
 \frac14\log\!\left(\frac{2q-1}{q+1}\right)
 \label{eq:direct-spider-center-asymptotic}
\end{equation}
away from the immaterial exact-tie parameters. In particular, about half of
these operations push a negative center residual, and their charged work is
\begin{equation}
 (q+1)T_q=\Theta\!\left(\frac q{\sqrt\alpha}\right).
 \label{eq:direct-spider-center-work}
\end{equation}
Moreover, the second center operation is already a negative-residual revisit
whenever
\begin{equation}
 \lambda>\frac{2(q+1)}{3q}
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \sqrt\alpha<\frac{q-2}{5q+2}.
 \label{eq:direct-spider-immediate-negative}
\end{equation}
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Write $x_t:=\lambda^{2t}$. At odd $t$, the arm term in
\cref{eq:direct-spider-center-condition} is
$\beta_\lambda(1+x_t)$. Its crossover is therefore
\begin{equation*}
 x_t=\frac{\beta_\lambda}{w_q-\beta_\lambda}
 \longrightarrow\frac{q+1}{2q-1}.
\end{equation*}
At even $t$ the arm term is $\beta_\lambda(1-x_t)$ and its crossover is
strictly later. Since
$-\log\lambda=2\operatorname{arctanh}(\sqrt\alpha)
\sim2\sqrt\alpha$, solving $\lambda^{2t}$ at the odd crossover proves
\cref{eq:direct-spider-center-asymptotic}; parity and an exact tie change
$T_q$ by only $O(1)$. The center sign alternates, giving the work statement.
The limiting odd crossover is at least $1/2$, whereas the spreading threshold
divided by $\zeta$ is $Bq\epsppr<1/4$. Hence, for sufficiently small
$\alpha$, neither the center prefix nor the first competitive arm is removed
by the gate.

After the first center push, compare
$w_q\lambda^2\zeta$ with the arm rank
$(2/3)\lambda\zeta$. The center is still larger precisely under the first
inequality in \cref{eq:direct-spider-immediate-negative}. Substitution of
$\lambda=(1-\sqrt\alpha)/(1+\sqrt\alpha)$ gives the equivalent second
inequality.
\end{proof}

This result is an obstruction to extending the path proof, not yet a lower
bound against two-rung SOR. The exposed degree volume after the first push is
$\Theta(q)$, so \cref{eq:direct-spider-center-work} exactly saturates the
desired $\mathcal V_{\rm exp}/\sqrt\alpha$ scale at one branch. A global
theorem must show that many branch echoes do not multiply. It must also treat
literal batching separately: a top-fraction batch can move several tied arms
after the center operation, so batching may reduce this particular refreshed
obstruction rather than merely introduce staleness. The calculation suggests
a branch-aware alternative---rank a center together with the aggregate score
of its forward children, or use the sign guard from
\cref{cor:direct-path-sign-guarded-batches}---but no graph-uniform claim for
either modification is made here.
