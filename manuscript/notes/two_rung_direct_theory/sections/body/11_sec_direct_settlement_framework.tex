\section{A settlement-factor framework beyond one edge}
\label{sec:direct-settlement-framework}

For an exact push at $u$, define its residual map
\begin{equation}
 P_u:=I-\frac{Qe_ue_u^\mathsf T}{Q_{uu}},
 \qquad r^+=P_ur.
 \label{eq:direct-exact-residual-map}
\end{equation}
For a no-repeat coordinate sequence
$\mathcal O=(u_1,\ldots,u_m)$, define the note-scoped settlement factor
\begin{equation}
 \chi_{\rm set}(r;\mathcal O)
 :=\frac{
   \norm{D^{-1/2}P_{u_m}\cdots P_{u_1}r}_\infty
 }{\norm{D^{-1/2}r}_\infty}.
 \label{eq:direct-settlement-factor}
\end{equation}
This quantity is trajectory-specific; it is not a condition number or a
stopping rule.

\begin{proposition}[One-pass settlement certificate]
\label{prop:direct-one-pass-certificate}
Suppose the switch residual satisfies
\begin{equation*}
 \norm{D^{-1/2}r^{\rm sw}}_\infty\leq B\tau.
\end{equation*}
If a no-repeat sequence
$\mathcal O$ consists of legal live exact pushes and
\begin{equation}
 B\chi_{\rm set}(r^{\rm sw};\mathcal O)\leq1,
 \label{eq:direct-one-pass-condition}
\end{equation}
then the terminal phase ends after that sequence with work at most
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{u\in\mathcal O}(1+d_u).
 \label{eq:direct-one-pass-work}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Apply \cref{eq:direct-settlement-factor} and the switch bound. The work
display is the declared charge, and no coordinate occurs twice.
\end{proof}

On one edge, \cref{lem:direct-edge-settlement-ratio} proves that the
one-coordinate sequence chosen by the ranked exact phase has asymptotic
factor at most $R_\star$. On the endpoint path,
\cref{thm:direct-path-two-rung} proves that one parity sweep plus at most one
outer push suffices. Beyond paths, the same idea has three distinct failure
modes.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
 \item \textbf{Multi-neighbor interference.} A pushed coordinate sends a
 packet to every neighbor. On a path the two incoming packets give exactly
 \cref{eq:direct-path-terminal-ratio}; at a branch they can reactivate
 several already settled directions. The delayed-reflection note proves
 that backward neighbor traffic can be larger than self-reflection on a
 spider arm.
 \item \textbf{Rank staleness.} The measured top-fraction batch is chosen
 from one snapshot. A proof must compare the live charged sequence with a
 refreshed settlement order, or bound the price of coordinates whose score
 becomes stale but remains above the gate.
 \item \textbf{Spreading-wave overlap.} A short terminal rung is not enough.
 The optimal-SOR phase itself must expose the relevant region while charging
 each layer only $\widetilde O(1/\sqrt\alpha)$ times in aggregate. Existing
 FIFO spider results show that this is false for some schedules; the
 charge-aware ranked order requires its own proof or counterexample.
\end{enumerate}

The refreshed endpoint-path target is closed by
\cref{thm:direct-path-two-rung}, and the measured literal batching is closed
by \cref{thm:direct-path-literal-measured-regime}.  The proposed
bounded-overlap theorem without an additional boundary condition is now
refuted by \cref{thm:direct-three-vertex-lower-bound}: the three-vertex path
has bounded degree, no merging, constant explored volume, singleton fresh
batches, and nevertheless costs $\Omega(\alpha^{-3/2})$.

The corrected structural statement has two proved branches.  A causal
corridor event DAG pays gapless propagation by geometric attenuation, while a
uniform positive supersolution pays all revisits by a final-region Dirichlet
gap.  A third ingredient is necessary at a reached boundary: it must rule out
or explicitly eliminate the reflecting slow mode
\cref{eq:direct-three-vertex-macro-spectrum}.  Maximum degree, batch
staleness, and explored volume alone cannot do this.

These results deliberately use explored volume rather than replacing it by
$1/\epsppr$. Such a replacement is a separate locality lemma and is not
currently available under the note-scoped unregularized residual. They also
do not identify this problem with the repository's
$1/(\rho\sqrt\alpha)$ persistent-support RPPR lower bound, which belongs to a
different accuracy convention and oracle model.
