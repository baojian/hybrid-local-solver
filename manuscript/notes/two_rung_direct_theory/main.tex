\begin{abstract}
The measured two-rung policy first applies optimally over-relaxed coordinate
pushes above a coarse residual gate and then applies exact Gauss--Seidel
pushes at the terminal gate. This note begins a direct proof program for that
literal method. The empirical ranking key is proved to be a graph-universal
factor-two approximation to the exact one-step objective decrease per charged
operation; no residual-band hypothesis is needed. A refreshed frontier
version consequently has a rigorous fixed-region contraction, while the
literal top-fraction batching exposes a precise staleness obligation.

The complete dynamics on the two-vertex graph are then solved in closed
form. Optimal SOR creates a critically damped, opposite-sign residual pair.
For every band factor below $3.33019\ldots$, a sufficiently developed
spreading phase is followed by at most one exact push. In particular, the
measured factor $2.5$ is certified on this model even where the
self-reflection-only rule rejects it.

The endpoint-path dynamics admit a stronger exact description. A causal
parity-wave schedule produces a signed two-level plateau, uses exactly
$\lfloor(L+1)^2/4\rfloor$ spreading pushes through depth $L$, and the fully
refreshed absolute-residual rank realizes precisely this event set without a
sign gate. Every negative waiting packet has a larger positive certificate
on its northeast dependency chain. The terminal exact rung then pushes each
high-parity coordinate at most once and possibly one new boundary coordinate.
Adding a live positive-sign guard extends the same charged-work theorem to
literal snapshot batches of arbitrary size on the path. This proves an
$\widetilde O(\mathcal V_{\rm exp}/\sqrt\alpha)$ explored-volume theorem on
the first nontrivial graph family for refreshed two-rung SOR.  The guard is
genuinely necessary for arbitrary batches: full-frontier batching executes a
negative endpoint in its third batch.  For $\alpha>1/49$, including both
measured values, a partial-layer invariant removes the guard from the actual
top-$1/32$ policy in both rungs.  A separate legal-interface construction
shows that static parent closure is false, so an all-$\alpha$ extension must
use trajectory history rather than corner geometry alone.

Branching breaks the path certificate in an exactly quantifiable way. On a
$q$-arm symmetric spider, refreshed ranking performs
$\Theta(1/\sqrt\alpha)$ consecutive center pushes before touching an arm,
about half on negative residual, and spends $\Theta(q/\sqrt\alpha)$ work at
that one branch. This saturates rather than violates the desired per-volume
scale. Grouping each symmetric radial shell into one block removes the echo
and reduces the complete spider dynamics exactly to the endpoint path,
proving the same accelerated explored-volume bound. More generally, radial
aggregation on every level-regular rooted tree is proved to be a symmetric
variable-coupling chain after a shell-energy rescaling. Its local impedance
identifies the endpoint path as the unique homogeneous critically matched
tree.  For every homogeneous branching factor $g>1$, finite explored balls
have a depth-independent Dirichlet gap.  A retrospective final-region ledger
then pays all spreading and exact-rung revisits and proves
$\widetilde O_g(\mathcal V_{\rm exp}/\sqrt\alpha)$ work for any fixed band
factor.  A positive supersolution extends this theorem to variable
level-regular profiles; in fact the same gap holds on fully nonsymmetric
rooted trees with uniformly positive forward branching, provided the
individual-coordinate rank is refreshed after every charge.  Long unary
corridors are the sharp obstruction to this gap proof.

Finally, unrestricted graph-uniform acceleration is false.  On the
center-seeded three-vertex path the literal top-$1/32$ policy has singleton
batches yet requires at least $\alpha^{-3/2}/1408$ spreading work at fixed
accuracy, while its explored degree volume is four.  The exact macrocycle
combines $\Theta(1/\sqrt\alpha)$ center echoes with a
$1-\Theta(\alpha)$ slow mode.  This establishes a structural boundary:
expansion or a causal corridor wave accelerates, whereas a reached reflecting
leaf need not. These claims are not identified with the separate RPPR
lower-bound accuracy namespace.  The obstruction also determines a minimal
repair.  Permanently Schur-deflating a certified closed pendant forest
changes only its attachment diagonals, costs linear work in the forest, and
erases every reflected trajectory inside it.  On a reached star this solves
the entire problem in $O(\mathcal V)$ work, whereas settling the leaves while
retaining the old center pivot still needs $\Omega(1/\alpha)$ steps.  Splicing
this boundary operation into the radial wave proves the accelerated
explored-volume bound for finite symmetric spiders of every arm length,
including those whose true leaves are reached.  Exact forest response also
has an exact preconditioning interpretation: every eliminated forest mode
becomes a unit generalized eigenvalue and all nontrivial conditioning is
confined to the Schur core.  Peeling to the graph $2$-core is the canonical
maximal version of this reduction.
On the minimal cyclic-core family, a further online case is now closed.
If every newly certified forest component attaches at one fixed cycle vertex,
all core corrections remain on one fixed Green column.  A kinetic reporter
sorts each boundary row's two gate-crossing scalars once and thereafter uses
only one response scalar and two advancing pointers.  Its total work is
linear in final charged volume up to sorting and reported crossings, with no
global boundary rekey.  This conclusion is specific to a fixed attachment;
varying attachment vertices can create new Green directions and remain open.
\end{abstract}
