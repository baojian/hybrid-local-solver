\section{A certified best-of-strategies portfolio}
\label{sec:two-stage-race}

No single Stage~I engine dominates on every graph.  This can be handled
without choosing a heuristic winner in advance.

\begin{definition}[Interruptible certified lane]
A lane is interruptible if its state can be paused after any charged work
quantum and later resumed, and if it eventually returns a semantically valid
PPR output.  A numerical lane may return directly.  A mass-completion lane
includes its APPR/SOR cleanup, and a set-only lane includes its certified
fixed-envelope Stage~II.  Let $W_j$ be the total charged work until lane $j$
returns on the current instance.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Fair certificate race]
\label{thm:two-stage-certificate-race}
Run $m$ interruptible engines in round-robin work quanta, with $m$ fixed.
Stop at the first certified PPR output.  Then the total work is at most
\begin{equation}
 W_{\rm race}\leq m\min_{1\leq j\leq m}W_j+O(m),
 \label{eq:two-stage-race-work}
\end{equation}
and correctness is that of the winning lane.  If direct APPR is included,
the race terminates on every finite graph and never loses more than a constant
factor relative to direct APPR at the same semantic target.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Before lane $j$ receives $W_j$ work, the fair schedule has spent at most
$mW_j+O(m)$ total quanta.  Choose the minimizing engine.  Every returned
output carries an independent semantic certificate.  Direct APPR supplies a
finite $W_j$ on every finite graph.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Recommended constant-size portfolio.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.2em]
\item \textbf{Direct APPR.}  Universal numerical fallback with
  $1/\alpha$ worst-case work.
\item \textbf{Direct priority SOR.}  The same observable residual certificate
  with Gauss--Southwell scheduling; useful when its ordering wins.
\item \textbf{Safe active-set AESP-CD.}  Fixed-envelope accelerated obstacle
  solves, restarted after certified batches; a numerical gap/key certificate
  returns early, a mass certificate invokes APPR/SOR cleanup, and a genuinely
  set-only screen invokes the fixed linear Stage~II.
\item \textbf{Structural response.}  Tree, unicyclic, cactus, bounded-block,
  or fixed-port reporter selected after local structure is certified.
\item \textbf{Potential or mass handoff lane.}  AESP burn-in followed by
  momentum-free SOR, or a safely retracted lower checkpoint followed by
  APPR/SOR, as discussed in
  \cref{sec:two-stage-alternative-handoff}.
\end{enumerate}

The race is a theorem about charged execution, not a license to run dense
global preprocessing for every lane.  Each paused engine pays for its own
state and exposures.  Sharing an adjacency cache can improve constants but is
not used in \cref{eq:two-stage-race-work}.

\paragraph{Deterministic diagnostic.}
The experiment-directory script \path{portfolio_benchmark.py}
tests six graph families, three values of $\alpha$, two error targets, and
seven lanes (252 rows).  On the 30 cases where direct APPR does nonzero work,
the total-work ratios below use direct APPR at the same semantic target as
denominator.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lcc@{}}
\toprule
Lane & median ratio & wins / 30 \\
\midrule
Direct priority SOR & $1.00$ & $2$ \\
Hard-capped Green screen or APPR & $3.13$ & $0$ \\
APPR envelope plus forced tail & $5.08$ & $0$ \\
Oracle-gap envelope plus forced tail & $5.48$ & $0$ \\
Priority envelope plus forced tail & $4.93$ & $0$ \\
Tuned priority envelope plus forced tail & $5.07$ & $0$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

All numerical handoff points already satisfy the target; their largest
observed error is $0.62\epsppr$.  The Green screen is the only genuinely
set-only lane: it completes its capped ball in 10 of 36 cases and falls back
safely in the rest, but it still loses every nonzero equal-accuracy
comparison.  These data do not prove a lower bound.  They do rule out using
the current forced-tail experiments as evidence for an end-to-end speedup and
motivate racing full certified lanes rather than stopping at the first set.

Inside a set-only lane, a second constant-size race may run Chebyshev/CG,
accelerated gradient, and SOR on the same principal system $Q_Eu=b_E$.
Every lane must use an independently valid residual or energy certificate;
the first certified $\tau$-accurate output wins.  Chebyshev or accelerated
gradient supplies the worst-case $O(\alpha^{-1/2})$ iteration guarantee,
while SOR can win on favorable ordered supports without weakening it.
