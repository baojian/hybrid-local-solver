\begin{abstract}
We isolate the strict two-stage part of a much longer AESP-CD analysis for
single-source personalized PageRank.  Stage~I returns only a certified
coordinate set; all changing-face state is discarded.  Stage~II solves one
ordinary principal PageRank system.  A containing RPPR envelope of volume
$V$ gives total work
$W_{\rm disc}+\widetilde O(V/\sqrt\alpha)$, while an exact structural tail
uses the full semantic budget $\rho=\varepsilon$ and may reuse the verifier's
factorization.  Exact support is unnecessary: a Green envelope, a direct
boundary-leakage certificate, or a low-energy inner RPPR face is enough.
A threshold-batched active-set algorithm now closes the arbitrary-graph
target in an exact-real randomized word model; deterministic finite-precision
complexity remains open.  In saved large-graph experiments, conservative
fresh Stage~II solves preserve
2 APPR-proposal, 5 SOR-proposal, 8 exact-batch, 14 tree-message, and 35
structural-Green fair-race wins.  This short core is the theorem spine; the
changing-face machinery belongs in a companion audit.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem and the strict handoff}

Let $G$ be a finite simple undirected graph without isolates, with adjacency
$A$ and degree matrix $D$.  For one seed $v$ and $0<\alpha\leq1$, define
\begin{equation}
 Q=\alpha I+\frac{1-\alpha}{2}
   (I-D^{-1/2}AD^{-1/2}),
 \qquad b=\alpha D^{-1/2}e_v .
 \label{eq:strict-q}
\end{equation}
Then $Q$ is symmetric Stieltjes, $\alpha I\preceq Q\preceq I$, and
$QD^{1/2}\one=\alpha D^{1/2}\one$.  Full PPR is $x^0=Q^{-1}b$ and the target
is
\begin{equation}
 \norm{D^{-1/2}(\widehat x-x^0)}_\infty\leq\varepsilon.
 \label{eq:strict-target}
\end{equation}
Classical APPR is the universal local baseline \citep{andersen2007using};
accelerated sparse PPR motivates the product target
\citep{martinezrubio2023accelerated,huang2025accelerated}; and RPPR supplies
the support screen \citep{fountoulakis2026complexity}.
At regularization $\rho$, RPPR is the obstacle problem
\begin{equation}
 x_\rho^\star=\argmin_{x\geq0}
 \left\{\frac12x^\top Qx-(b-\alpha\rho D^{1/2}\one)^\top x\right\},
 \qquad S^\star=\supp(x_\rho^\star).
 \label{eq:strict-rppr}
\end{equation}
Inverse positivity and the constant-vector identity give
\begin{equation}
 0\leq x_\rho^\star\leq x^0,
 \quad
 \norm{D^{-1/2}(x^0-x_\rho^\star)}_\infty\leq\rho,
 \quad
 \vol(S^\star)\leq1/\rho.
 \label{eq:strict-bridge}
\end{equation}
If $S^\star$ is nonempty, it is connected and contains $v$.  Indeed, an
active component $C$ not containing the only positive load would satisfy
$Q_Cx_C=c_{\rho,C}<0$; inverse positivity contradicts $x_C>0$.  This rooted
support invariant is the main structural gain from the point-source scope.

The original strict interface is deliberately set-only.  Stage~I returns a
certified $E\supseteq S^\star$; Stage~II ignores its numerical point and solves
\begin{equation}
 Q_Eu_E=b_E,
 \label{eq:strict-stage2}
\end{equation}
then zero-pads $u_E$.  No obstacle momentum or changing-face response is
continued.
Section~\ref{sec:random-core} later widens the set-only certificate to a
low-energy inner face, which need not contain $S^\star$.

\begin{lemma}[Envelope linearization]
\label{lem:strict-linearization}
For every $E\supseteq S^\star$, the zero extension $\widetilde u_E$ of the
solution to \cref{eq:strict-stage2} obeys
\begin{equation*}
 0\leq\widetilde u_E\leq x^0,
 \qquad
 \norm{D^{-1/2}(x^0-\widetilde u_E)}_\infty\leq\rho.
\end{equation*}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Outside $E$, \cref{eq:strict-bridge} and $x_{\rho,i}^\star=0$ give
$0\leq x_i^0\leq\rho\sqrt{d_i}$.  Put $w=x_E^0-u_E$.  The full and principal
PPR equations give $Q_Ew=-Q_{E\bar E}x_{\bar E}^0\geq0$, so inverse
positivity gives $0\leq u_E\leq x_E^0$.  Since
$x_{\bar E}^0\leq\rho D_{\bar E}^{1/2}\one$ and $Q_{E\bar E}\leq0$,
\begin{equation*}
 Q_Ew\leq-Q_{E\bar E}\rho D_{\bar E}^{1/2}\one
 \leq Q_E\rho D_E^{1/2}\one.
\end{equation*}
The last inequality is the restriction of
$QD^{1/2}\one=\alpha D^{1/2}\one$.  Multiplying by $Q_E^{-1}\geq0$ and
combining with the outside bound proves the claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Strict two-stage composition]
\label{thm:strict-composition}
If Stage~I certifies $E\supseteq S^\star$ in charged work $W_{\rm disc}$ and
Stage~II solves \cref{eq:strict-stage2} to Euclidean error $\tau$, then the
output is $(\rho+\tau)$-accurate.  For $\tau>0$, total work is
\begin{equation}
 W_{\rm disc}+O\!\left(
   \frac{\vol(E)}{\sqrt\alpha}
   \log\frac{C}{\tau}
 \right).
 \label{eq:strict-composition-work}
\end{equation}
If Stage~II is exact, replace the iterative term by its structural
factorization/backsolve cost and take $\rho=\varepsilon$, $\tau=0$; no
terminal half-budget is needed.  If an exact verifier already factors $Q_E$, the
ordinary-PPR right-hand side reuses the factors and costs only one additional
backsolve: $O(w^2\vol(E))$ at elimination width $w$, and $O(\vol(E))$ on a
tree or unicyclic graph.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine \cref{lem:strict-linearization} with the terminal error.  The spectrum
of every principal $Q_E$ lies in $[\alpha,1]$, so Chebyshev, CG, or accelerated
gradient gives \cref{eq:strict-composition-work}; one matrix application costs
$O(\vol(E))$.  Exact elimination has no terminal error.  The RPPR and PPR
systems have the same matrix and different right-hand sides, proving factor
reuse.
\end{proof}

If the exact RPPR verifier point $p_E$ is available, it is also a certified
warm start: $b_E-Q_Ep_E=\alpha\rho D_E^{1/2}\one$, the correction is
nonnegative, and its initial objective gap is at most
$\alpha\rho^2\vol(E)/2\leq\alpha\rho/2$.  This can reduce polishing, but it
does not justify forcing Stage~II when $p_E$ already meets the semantic target.

\section{Seven safe Stage-I choices}

Exact support is a KKT statement.  If an exact face solve $p_A$ is positive
on $A$ and every exterior key
$(b-\alpha\rho D^{1/2}\one-Q\widetilde p_A)_j$ is nonpositive, then
$A=S^\star$.  For a single source already contained in $A$, only
$\partial A$ must be read: every farther row has zero coupling to $A$ and
strictly negative load $-\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_j}$.  With finite arithmetic,
primal and dual error intervals replace exact comparisons; a zero margin is
not a uniform finite-precision contract.
The following options are therefore intentionally heterogeneous.

\midrule

Exact positive batches & exact RPPR face & always correct where structural
refits apply; cumulative face volume can be quadratic \\
Tree thresholds & exact face without rejected-row probes & unconditional

Random threshold batches & low-energy inner RPPR face & unconditional

\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

The leakage option is exact-support-free.  Let $u_E=Q_E^{-1}b_E$ and suppose
$\widehat u_E$ has normalized coordinate error at most $\eta$.  For
$j\in\partial E$, set
\begin{equation*}
 \widehat\ell_j=\frac{-Q_{jE}\widehat u_E}{\sqrt{d_j}},
 \qquad c=\frac{1-\alpha}{2}.
\end{equation*}
If
\begin{equation}
 \widehat\ell_j+c\frac{\deg_E(j)}{d_j}\eta\leq\alpha\delta
 \quad(j\in\partial E),
 \label{eq:strict-leakage-gate}
\end{equation}
then $0\leq x^0-\widetilde u_E\leq\delta D^{1/2}\one$.  Indeed the padded
principal residual vanishes except on the boundary, where
\cref{eq:strict-leakage-gate} bounds it by
$\alpha\delta D^{1/2}\one$; multiply by $Q^{-1}\geq0$.  The computable choice
$\eta=\norm{b_E-Q_E\widehat u_E}_2/\alpha$ follows from strong convexity.

The SOR/AESP safety fact is short.  If $0\leq z\leq x_\rho^\star$ and an
inactive row has $(c_\rho-Qz)_i>0$, then $i\in S^\star$: otherwise Stieltjes
signs give $(c_\rho-Qz)_i\leq(c_\rho-Qx_\rho^\star)_i\leq0$.  Thus positive
residuals may be admitted without false support.  Solving each current face
exactly and admitting all positive exterior rows is also monotone, since for
$B=A\cup P$,
\begin{equation*}
 x^B-(x^A,0)=Q_B^{-1}g_B\geq0.
\end{equation*}
It terminates at global KKT, but a path may add only one vertex per refit.

Every optional lane is interruptible and is scheduled one charged quantum at
a time against direct APPR.  If it certifies and finishes after $W$ work, the
combined charge is at most $2W$; if it fails or APPR finishes first, the charge
is at most $2W_{\rm APPR}$.  Thus proposal failures never invalidate the
unconditional baseline.

For a point-source tree, eliminate every active descendant subtree into the
affine response
$x_u=(\beta_ux_{p(u)}+B_u)/A_u$ with $A_u>0$.  An inactive child contributes
the parent value at which its KKT key becomes positive; an active child
propagates its minimum descendant threshold through the same positive-slope
map.  One handled heap per vertex therefore names a strictly positive
frontier row or certifies global KKT.  Each admission changes one record along
its root path and each admitted adjacency list is scanned once, giving
\begin{equation}
 \widetilde O\!\left(
   \vol(S^\star)+\sum_{i\in S^\star}\operatorname{dist}(v,i)
 \right)
 =\widetilde O\!\left(
   \frac{1+\vol(S^\star)}{\sqrt\alpha}
 \right).
 \label{eq:strict-tree-work}
\end{equation}
With $\rho=\varepsilon$, this closes the desired
$\widetilde O(1/(\varepsilon\sqrt\alpha))$ strict two-stage bound on trees.

Exact support is not required on general graphs.  The case $\alpha=1$ returns
the seed coordinate immediately; for $0<\alpha<1$, point-source Green decay
gives
$x_i^0/\sqrt{d_i}\leq
2((1-\sqrt\alpha)/(1+\sqrt\alpha))^{\operatorname{dist}(v,i)}$.
Expose the corresponding accuracy ball under a hard volume cap, then solve
one principal system.  Completion is correct; cap failure discards the lane
and resumes direct APPR.  On a certified width-$w$ envelope the successful
branch costs $O(w^3\vol(E))$, and on trees/unicyclic graphs $O(\vol(E))$.

\section{Conservative literal-two-stage measurements}

All rows below use 1,024--2,047 vertex deterministic path, cycle, star,
binary-tree, broom, and grid families.  ``Early'' returns the exact RPPR
verifier point.  ``Literal'' additionally runs and charges a fresh ordinary
PPR face solve; it does not take credit for factor reuse.  ``Fair'' charges a
one-for-one race with direct APPR.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
Lane & certified / nontrivial & early raw / fair & literal raw / fair \\
\midrule
APPR proposal & $28/19$ & $9/4$ & $4/2$ \\
SOR proposal & $75$ & $8/5$ & $5/5$ \\
Exact batches & $75$ & $43/13$ & $33/8$ \\
Tree messages & $60/51$ & $--$ & $27/14$ \\
Structural Green & $56$ & $--$ & $50/35$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The literal column deliberately refactors $Q_E$.  Retaining the verifier's
tree/unicyclic factors and charging the implemented structural backsolve
model changes the APPR, SOR, and exact-batch raw/fair counts to respectively
$6/2$, $7/5$, and $37/9$.  Thus reuse recovers several standalone cases and
one additional fully charged batch case; it does not manufacture a universal
advantage for the proposal lanes.

On the 51 nontrivial promised-tree rows, a literal three-way round-robin race
of direct APPR, threshold messages, and structural Green still has 23 strict
wins even after charging three times the first successful speculative lane.
Structural Green supplies 21 of those winners and threshold messages supply
the other two.  Thus the two set-only mechanisms are genuinely complementary
without requiring an offline oracle selector.

Every literal Stage~II output was compared with a full sparse PPR solve and
met \cref{eq:strict-target}.  The strongest general lesson is not that exact
support always wins.  APPR/SOR proposals are useful optional lanes; repeated
refits remain vulnerable to serial paths; tree thresholds are a genuine
single-source structural closure; and Green truncation is the cleanest
support-free set-only screen.

\section{What remains open}

The strict two-stage reduction removes all need to carry momentum across a
face change, and \cref{thm:random-core-two-stage} closes the arbitrary-graph
existence theorem in the exact-real randomized word model.  The remaining
questions are stronger: deterministic finite-precision and coefficient-bit
bounds, a practical local realization of the randomized SDD primitive, and a
persistent reporter that improves on rebuilding a fresh face solver.  The
older Green, leakage, APPR, SOR, and response lanes remain useful deterministic
or practical competitors.  Direct APPR remains the unconditional
finite-arithmetic fallback, rather than the only graph-uniform existence
proof.

The single-source restriction is substantive: it yields one connected rooted
support and the radius bound behind \cref{eq:strict-tree-work}.  It is not a
free reduction for sparse sources.  Linear PPR superposition can multiply
work by as much as $\operatorname{nnz}(s)$, and RPPR supports do not
superpose.  The correct paper claim is therefore a point-source randomized
theorem, not an additive-$\operatorname{nnz}(s)$ sparse-source theorem.
