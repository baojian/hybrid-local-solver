\section{The ladder}
\label{sec:ladder-ladder}

\begin{corollary}[Three classes, one instance; \statusproveddraft]
\label{cor:ladder-ladder}
Fix $0<\epsppr\le1/16$, $\alpha\in(0,3/4)$, and the centre-seeded star
\eqref{eq:ladder-hard-instance}. All classes use the identical primitive
\eqref{eq:ladder-primitive}, fully charged work model, plain output
$\widehat{\bm{z}}=\bm{z}$ (equivalently
$\widehat{\bm{x}}=\bm{D}^{-1/2}\bm{z}$), and semantic guarantee
\eqref{eq:ladder-guarantee}. The following proved bounds hold.
\end{corollary}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
Class & Axiom dropped & Star work & Lower / upper source \\
\midrule
$\Mpos$ & --- & $\Theta(1/(\alpha\epsppr))$
    & \Cref{thm:ladder-monotone-lower-bound} / \APPR{} itself \\
$\Mpm$ & step positivity & $\Theta(1/(\alpha\epsppr))$, no gain
    & \Cref{thm:ladder-monotone-lower-bound} / same \\
$\Rpm$ & $\bm{r}\ge\bm{0}$
    & $\Omega(1/(\sqrt\alpha\,\epsppr))$
    & \Cref{thm:ladder-signed-lower-bound} \\
& & $O(\log(1/\alpha)/(\sqrt\alpha\,\epsppr))$
    & \Cref{thm:ladder-signed-upper-bound} \\
elimination & $\delta=\omega r_u$, $\omega\in(0,2)$
    & $\Theta(1/\epsppr)$
    & \eqref{eq:ladder-output-floor} /
      \Cref{prop:ladder-elimination} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The step-sign axiom buys nothing: $\Mpos$ and $\Mpm$ have the same proved
order. Dropping residual nonnegativity improves the proved lower-bound scale
by $\sqrt\alpha$, while the best proved signed-relaxation upper bound carries
one additional $\log(1/\alpha)$ factor. Dropping dissipativity permits exact
star elimination in output-linear work. Therefore the table is a proved
lower/upper ladder, not a proof that the optimum of $\Rpm$ has an exact
$\Theta(1/(\sqrt\alpha\,\epsppr))$ order.

On the dyadic grid with $\alpha\le1/16$, the scan-work statements have the
explicit normalizations
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
    \Mpm:&\quad W_{\mathrm{adj}}\alpha\epsppr\ge0.0397,\\
    \Rpm:&\quad W_{\mathrm{adj}}\sqrt\alpha\epsppr
        \ge\frac{3}{128\sqrt{1+\alpha}}\ge0.0227,\\
    \text{elimination}:&\quad
        W_{\mathrm{adj}}\epsppr=2m\epsppr=\tfrac14,
        \qquad \Work=\Theta(1/\epsppr).
\end{aligned}
    \label{eq:ladder-constants}
\end{equation}
The measured scan-work member frontiers sit at $0.087$ for $\Mpm$ and
$0.083$ to $0.094$ for $\Rpm$ (\statusmeasured;
\Cref{sec:ladder-verification}), so the experiments suggest small constant
slack. They do not remove the logarithm from the proved $\Rpm$ upper bound.
Consequently, the best proved class-cost ratios are bracketed rather than
identified exactly: elimination versus $\Rpm$ lies between
$\Omega(1/\sqrt\alpha)$ and
$O(\log(1/\alpha)/\sqrt\alpha)$, with the analogous logarithmic uncertainty
between $\Mpm$ and $\Rpm$.

\paragraph{Reading the two axioms.}
The condition $\bm{r}\ge\bm{0}$ is the \emph{anti-acceleration} axiom. It is
what forces settling to be paid at the seed's own multiplicative rate
$\kappa_{+}=\Theta(\alpha)$ of \Cref{lem:ladder-kappa-step}, instead of the
additive fast-mode rate $\Theta(\sqrt\alpha)$ of
\Cref{lem:ladder-displacement}. Dissipative one-hop relaxation is the
\emph{anti-output-linearity} axiom. It prevents an algorithm from directly
writing the closed form \eqref{eq:ladder-elimination-output}; its lower-bound
signature is the fast-mode cap. The auxiliary output-side analogue is the
column-mass threshold of \Cref{prop:ladder-escape}, beyond which an oracle map
can produce a correct answer without a push. In the fully charged Problem~1
model, applying that map and emitting the answer are still charged.
