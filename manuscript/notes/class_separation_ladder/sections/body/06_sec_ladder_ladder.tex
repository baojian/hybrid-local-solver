\section{The ladder}
\label{sec:ladder-ladder}

\begin{corollary}[Three classes, one instance; \statusproveddraft]
\label{cor:ladder-ladder}
Fix $\epsppr\le1/16$, $\alpha\in(0,3/4)$, and the centre-seeded star
\eqref{eq:ladder-hard-instance}. All classes use the identical primitive
\eqref{eq:ladder-primitive}, work charge $d_{u}$, plain output
$\widehat z=z$, and guarantee \eqref{eq:ladder-guarantee}. Then the following
holds.
\end{corollary}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
Class & Axiom dropped & Star work & Lower / upper source \\
\midrule
$\Mpos$ & --- & $\Theta(1/(\alpha\epsppr))$
    & \Cref{thm:ladder-monotone-lower-bound} / \APPR{} itself \\
$\Mpm$ & step positivity & $\Theta(1/(\alpha\epsppr))$, no gain
    & \Cref{thm:ladder-monotone-lower-bound} / same \\
$\Rpm$ & $r\ge0$ & $\Theta(1/(\sqrt\alpha\,\epsppr))^{\dagger}$
    & \Cref{thm:ladder-signed-lower-bound} /
      \Cref{thm:ladder-signed-upper-bound} \\
elimination & $\delta=\omega r_{u}$, $\omega\in(0,2)$
    & $\Theta(1/\epsppr)$
    & \eqref{eq:ladder-output-floor} /
      \Cref{prop:ladder-elimination} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Each step from $\Mpos$ to elimination drops exactly one axiom and buys exactly
one factor of $\sqrt\alpha$, except the step-sign axiom, which buys nothing.
The dagger records the one place where the table is not fully proved. The lower
bound of the $\Rpm$ row is proved, but the matching upper bound
\eqref{eq:ladder-signed-upper} carries a factor $\log(1/\alpha)$, and removing
it rests on the \statusmeasured{} semantic-stop constant of
\Cref{thm:ladder-signed-upper-bound}; read without that measurement, the row is
$\Theta$ up to $\log(1/\alpha)$.
On the dyadic grid with $\alpha\le1/16$ the explicit constants are
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
    \Mpm:&\quad \Work\cdot\alpha\cdot\epsppr\ \ge\ 0.0397,\\
    \Rpm:&\quad \Work\cdot\sqrt\alpha\cdot\epsppr\ \ge\
        \frac{3}{128\sqrt{1+\alpha}}\ \ge\ 0.0227,\\
    \text{elimination}:&\quad
        \Work\cdot\epsppr\ \le\ \tfrac38+\epsppr
        \quad\text{against the universal floor }
        \Work\cdot\epsppr\ \ge\ \epsppr m=\tfrac18 .
\end{aligned}
    \label{eq:ladder-constants}
\end{equation}
Measured member frontiers sit at $0.087$ for $\Mpm$ and $0.083$ to $0.094$
for $\Rpm$ (\statusmeasured; \Cref{sec:ladder-verification}), so both lower
bounds are tight to a small constant rather than to an order. The separation
ratios on the single instance are $\Mpm/\Rpm=\Theta(1/\sqrt\alpha)$, again free
of a logarithm only under the semantic stop, and
$\Rpm/\text{elimination}=\Theta(1/\sqrt\alpha)$, which is unconditional.

\paragraph{Reading the two axioms.}
The condition $r\ge0$ is the \emph{anti-acceleration} axiom. It is what forces
settling to be paid at the seed's own multiplicative rate
$\kappa_{+}=\Theta(\alpha)$ of \Cref{lem:ladder-kappa-step}, instead of the
additive fast-mode rate $\Theta(\sqrt\alpha)$ of
\Cref{lem:ladder-displacement}. Dissipative one-hop relaxation is the
\emph{anti-output-linearity} axiom. It is what stops an algorithm from simply
writing a linear functional of what it has already seen --- the eliminator's
closed form \eqref{eq:ladder-elimination-output} --- and its price is exactly
the fast-mode cap. Its shadow on the output side is the column-mass threshold
of \Cref{prop:ladder-escape}, beyond which even a one-hop \emph{output} map is
linear-powerful enough to print the answer at $\Work=0$; by
\Cref{prop:ladder-transport} the entire output-map question on a radius-one
instance collapses to that single scalar.
