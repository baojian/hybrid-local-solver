\section[Rung three: elimination]
{Rung three: elimination pays $\Theta(1/\epsppr)$}
\label{sec:ladder-elimination}

\begin{proposition}[Exact elimination on the star; \statusproveddraft]
\label{prop:ladder-elimination}
On the centre-seeded $K_{1,m}$ of \eqref{eq:ladder-hard-instance}, scan $c$
at adjacency cost $m$, discovering $d_c=m$ and the leaf set; scan each leaf at
cost $1$ to certify $d_\ell=1$; then form the sparse mass-coordinate output
\begin{equation}
    \widehat z_c=\frac{\galpha}{1-\calpha^2},
    \qquad
    \widehat z_\ell=\frac{\calpha\widehat z_c}{m}
    \quad(\ell\in\mathcal F),
    \qquad
    \widehat{\bm{x}}=\bm{D}^{-1/2}\widehat{\bm{z}}.
    \label{eq:ladder-elimination-output}
\end{equation}
Then $\widehat{\bm{z}}=\bm{\pi}$ and
$\widehat{\bm{x}}=\bm{x}_0^*$ exactly, so $\errop=0$. Its accounting is
\begin{equation}
    W_{\mathrm{adj}}=2m,
    \qquad
    W_{\mathrm{emit}}=\Theta(m),
    \qquad
    \Work=\Theta(m)=\Theta(1/\epsppr).
    \label{eq:ladder-elimination-work}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Row $\ell$ of $\bm{H}\widehat{\bm{z}}$ is
$\widehat z_\ell-\calpha\widehat z_c/m
=0=(\galpha\bm{e}_c)_\ell$. Row $c$ is
$\widehat z_c-\calpha\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal F}\widehat z_\ell
=\widehat z_c(1-\calpha^2)=\galpha$. Since $\bm{H}$ is nonsingular,
$\widehat{\bm{z}}=\bm{\pi}$ by \eqref{eq:ladder-H-conjugation}. The two scan
phases inspect $2m$ adjacency entries. Computing the closed forms, reading or
storing the discovered degrees and labels, converting the $m+1$ coordinates,
and emitting their labels and values use $O(m)$ additional word operations.
The output floor gives the matching $\Omega(m)$ full-work bound.
\end{proof}

Combined with the universal output floor \eqref{eq:ladder-output-floor},
elimination is $\Theta(1/\epsppr)$ on this instance. By
\Cref{thm:ladder-signed-lower-bound}, every $\Rpm$ member is asymptotically
slower by at least an $\Omega(1/\sqrt\alpha)$ factor; the proved SOR upper
bound leaves a logarithmic gap in the best-member ratio.

\begin{remark}[What elimination gives up, and what this means for the target]
\label{rem:ladder-elimination-scope}
Within the one-hop state framework of \Cref{sec:ladder-scope}, what
\Cref{prop:ladder-elimination} abandons is the axiom
$\delta=\omega r_u$ with $\omega\in(0,2)$: its writes are not constrained to
be dissipative residual relaxations. On a radius-one instance, one-hop
\emph{locality} itself never binds, so locality is not what is being traded
here; on general graphs, elimination gives that up as well. The consequence
for the project's target is the one that matters most in this note:
\Cref{thm:ladder-signed-lower-bound} is a \emph{class} bound, not an
information barrier, and it therefore lends no support to
$1/(\sqrt\alpha\,\epsppr)$ as a necessary cost of the semantic PPR problem.
The graph-uniform open target in Problem~1 stands untouched by it.
\end{remark}
