\section{Rung three: elimination pays $\Theta(1/\epsppr)$}
\label{sec:ladder-elimination}

\begin{proposition}[Exact elimination on the star; \statusproveddraft]
\label{prop:ladder-elimination}
On the centre-seeded $K_{1,m}$ of \eqref{eq:ladder-hard-instance}: scan $c$
at cost $m$, discovering $d_{c}=m$ and the leaf set; scan each leaf at cost
$1$ to certify $d_{l}=1$; then write
\begin{equation}
    \widehat z_{c}=\frac{\galpha}{1-\calpha^{2}},
    \qquad
    \widehat z_{l}=\frac{\calpha\widehat z_{c}}{m}
    \quad(l\in L).
    \label{eq:ladder-elimination-output}
\end{equation}
Then $\widehat z=\pi$ exactly, so $\errop=0$, and
\begin{equation}
    \Work=3m+1\ \le\ \frac{3}{8\epsppr}+1 .
    \label{eq:ladder-elimination-work}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Row $l$ of $H\widehat z$ is
$\widehat z_{l}-\calpha\widehat z_{c}/m=0=(\galpha e_{c})_{l}$. Row $c$ is
$\widehat z_{c}-\calpha\sum_{l}\widehat z_{l}
=\widehat z_{c}(1-\calpha^{2})=\galpha$. Since $H$ is nonsingular,
$\widehat z=\pi$ by \eqref{eq:ladder-H-conjugation}. The work is $m$ for the
centre scan, $m$ for the leaf scans, and $m+1$ output writes.
\end{proof}

Combined with the universal output floor \eqref{eq:ladder-output-floor},
elimination is $\Theta(1/\epsppr)$ on this instance, and it beats every member
of $\Rpm$ by $\Theta(1/\sqrt\alpha)$ by
\Cref{thm:ladder-signed-lower-bound}.

\begin{remark}[What elimination gives up, and what this means for the target]
\label{rem:ladder-elimination-scope}
Within the one-hop state framework of \Cref{sec:ladder-scope}, what
\Cref{prop:ladder-elimination} abandons is the axiom
$\delta=\omega r_{u}$ with $\omega\in(0,2)$: its writes are non-dissipative
one-hop updates. On a radius-one instance, one-hop \emph{locality} itself
never binds, so locality is not what is being traded here; on general graphs
elimination gives that up as well. The consequence for the project's target is
the one that matters most in this note:
\Cref{thm:ladder-signed-lower-bound} is a \emph{class} bound, not an
information barrier, and it therefore lends no support to
$1/(\sqrt\alpha\,\epsppr)$ as a \emph{necessary} cost of the semantic problem
of \Cref{subsec:shared-source-aligned-problem}. The output-linear target
stands untouched by it.
\end{remark}
