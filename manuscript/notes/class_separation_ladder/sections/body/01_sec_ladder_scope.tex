\section{Scope, claim status, and the push-scale setting}
\label{sec:ladder-scope}

\subsection*{What this note does and does not claim}

This note fixes one instance, one state, one primitive, one work charge, one
output map, and one accuracy guarantee, and then proves how much charged work
is needed by each of three algorithm classes that differ from one another by
exactly one axiom. It is a \emph{class} comparison. It is not an information
lower bound, and \Cref{sec:ladder-elimination} exhibits, on the same instance,
an algorithm outside the classes that beats every member of the strongest one
by $\Theta(1/\sqrt{\alpha})$. Nothing in this note argues that
$1/(\sqrt\alpha\,\epsppr)$ is a necessary cost of the semantic problem.

\paragraph{Accuracy namespace.}
Throughout, $\epsppr$ is the degree-normalized solution accuracy of the shared
model in \Cref{subsec:shared-source-aligned-problem}. It is never identified
with the \APPR{} activation threshold $\epsappr$, with an objective gap
$\epsobj$, or with a proximal fixed-point tolerance $\epspg$. The one place
where a bridge to $\epsappr$ is used is
\Cref{sec:ladder-manuscript-relation}, and it is stated there explicitly.

\paragraph{Claim status.}
Statements carry one of the following labels.
\statusproveddraft{} means a complete argument is written out in this note but
has not been independently audited; it asserts no repository promotion.
\statusimported{} means the argument is complete in a named source package and
is used here without being re-derived. \statusmeasured{} means numerics only.
\statusopen{} means open. Provenance for every statement, together with an
auditor's checklist, is in \Cref{sec:ladder-verification}.

\subsection*{Push-scale notation}

The shared model of \Cref{subsec:shared-source-aligned-problem} is used
verbatim; this subsection only introduces note-scoped abbreviations for
objects it already defines, and redefines nothing. Put
\begin{equation}
    \calpha:=\frac{1-\alpha}{1+\alpha},
    \qquad
    \galpha:=\frac{2\alpha}{1+\alpha},
    \qquad
    H:=I-\calpha AD^{-1},
    \label{eq:ladder-push-scale-constants}
\end{equation}
so that $1-\calpha=\galpha$ and $1+\calpha=2/(1+\alpha)$. Conjugating the
shared operator $Q$ of \eqref{eq:shared-pagerank-matrices} gives
\begin{equation}
    D^{1/2}QD^{-1/2}=\frac{1+\alpha}{2}\,H,
    \qquad\text{hence}\qquad
    H\pi=\galpha\,e_{v}
    \label{eq:ladder-H-conjugation}
\end{equation}
for the single-seed specialization $s=e_{v}$ of \eqref{eq:shared-seed}. Write
\begin{equation}
    \pr(h):=\galpha H^{-1}h,
    \qquad\text{so}\qquad
    \pr(e_{v})=\pi .
    \label{eq:ladder-pr-operator}
\end{equation}
The symmetric form used in \Cref{sec:ladder-signed-lower-bound} is
\begin{equation}
    \Hsym:=I-\calpha D^{-1/2}AD^{-1/2}=\frac{2}{1+\alpha}\,Q,
    \qquad
    \galpha I\preceq\Hsym\preceq\frac{2}{1+\alpha}I,
    \label{eq:ladder-Hsym}
\end{equation}
the spectral bracket following from $\alpha I\preceq Q\preceq I$.

\subsection*{State, primitive, and charged work}

An algorithm maintains a pair $(z,r)$ with
\begin{equation}
    z^{0}=0,
    \qquad
    r:=\galpha e_{v}-Hz,
    \qquad\text{so}\qquad
    r^{0}=\galpha e_{v},
    \qquad
    e:=\pi-z=H^{-1}r .
    \label{eq:ladder-state}
\end{equation}
The one-hop primitive is, for $u\in V$ and $\delta\in\R$,
\begin{equation}
    \push(u,\delta):\quad
    z_{u}\mathrel{+}=\delta,
    \qquad
    r_{u}\mathrel{-}=\delta,
    \qquad
    r_{w}\mathrel{+}=\frac{\calpha\,\delta}{d_{u}}\ \ (w\in\mathcal N(u)),
    \label{eq:ladder-primitive}
\end{equation}
charged $\work=d_{u}$: one adjacency-list scan of $u$, the manuscript's unit.
By construction $\push(u,\delta)$ preserves the invariant
$z+H^{-1}r=\pi$ exactly and changes $z$ only at its base vertex; the
neighbour split in \eqref{eq:ladder-primitive} is forced and the scalar
$\delta$ is the only freedom. Total charged work of a run is
$\Work=\sum_{t}d_{u_{t}}$ plus the output writes accounted in
\Cref{sec:ladder-classes}. The policy choosing $(u_{t},\delta_{t})$ is
arbitrary --- adaptive, randomized, or batched. Every bound below is
\emph{pathwise}, so a randomized policy obeys it with probability one.

\subsection*{Semantic guarantee}

The output is a finite-support vector $\widehat z$, coordinates not listed
being zero, and must satisfy the shared degree-normalized error target
\begin{equation}
    \errop(\widehat z)
    :=\max_{u\in V}\frac{\abs{\pi_{u}-\widehat z_{u}}}{d_{u}}
    \le\epsppr .
    \label{eq:ladder-guarantee}
\end{equation}

\subsection*{Dictionary to the mass scale}

The monotone results were first proved in the mass scale of
\file{sections/appr_lower_bound.tex}, where the state is the \APPR{} pair
$(p,r_{\mathrm{mass}})$. The translation is
\begin{equation}
    z=p,
    \qquad
    r=\galpha\,r_{\mathrm{mass}},
    \qquad
    \delta=\alpha\,\eta,
    \qquad
    \pi-z=H^{-1}r=\pr(r_{\mathrm{mass}}).
    \label{eq:ladder-mass-dictionary}
\end{equation}
Under it, lazy \APPR{} push at $u$ (mass amount $\xi=(r_{\mathrm{mass}})_{u}$)
is $\push(u,\omega r_{u})$ with $\omega=(1+\alpha)/2$; full non-lazy push is
$\omega=1$; and Gauss--Seidel SOR with $\omega>1$ overshoots $r_{u}$ past
zero. Feasibility of the monotone class, $\eta\le
2(r_{\mathrm{mass}})_{u}/(1+\alpha)$, is exactly $\delta\le r_{u}$.

\subsection*{Five facts used throughout}

\begin{lemma}[Elementary facts; \statusproveddraft]
\label{lem:ladder-facts}
For every connected $G$, every $\alpha\in(0,1)$ and every $h\in\R^{n}$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(F\arabic*)},leftmargin=3em]
    \item\label{it:ladder-f1}
        $H^{\trans}\one=\galpha\one$, hence
        $\sum_{u}(H^{-1}r)_{u}=\norm{r}_{1}/\galpha$ whenever $r\ge0$;
    \item\label{it:ladder-f2}
        $H^{-1}\ge0$ entrywise;
    \item\label{it:ladder-f3}
        $(H^{-1})_{vv}=\pi_{v}/\galpha$;
    \item\label{it:ladder-f4}
        $\max_{u}\pr(h)_{u}/d_{u}\le\max_{u}h_{u}/d_{u}$ for $h\ge0$;
    \item\label{it:ladder-f5}
        $\abs{H^{-1}h}\le H^{-1}\abs{h}$ entrywise.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
\ref{it:ladder-f1}: $H^{\trans}\one=\one-\calpha D^{-1}A\one
=\one-\calpha D^{-1}d=(1-\calpha)\one=\galpha\one$, and then
$\sum_{u}(H^{-1}r)_{u}=\inner{H^{-\trans}\one}{r}=\norm{r}_{1}/\galpha$ for
$r\ge0$.
\ref{it:ladder-f2}: $\calpha AD^{-1}\ge0$ has column sums $\calpha<1$, so the
Neumann series $\sum_{k\ge0}(\calpha AD^{-1})^{k}$ converges to $H^{-1}$ and
is nonnegative.
\ref{it:ladder-f3}: take the $v$-coordinate of
$\pi=\galpha H^{-1}e_{v}$, which is \eqref{eq:ladder-H-conjugation}.
\ref{it:ladder-f4}: $Hd=d-\calpha A\one=d-\calpha d=\galpha d$, so
$\pr(d)=d$; if $h\le td$ coordinatewise then $\pr(h)\le t\,\pr(d)=td$
by \ref{it:ladder-f2}.
\ref{it:ladder-f5}: immediate from \ref{it:ladder-f2}.
\end{proof}
