\subsection{What a certified admission can pay}

Same-point replay can be amortized when it is launched from a monotone lower
checkpoint, rather than from an arbitrary momentum state.

\begin{lemma}[Boundary-margin admission gain]
\label{lem:boundary-admission-gain}
Let $A$ be a locked face with positive restricted optimum $x_A^\star$, and
let a batch $S$ enlarge it to $B=A\cup S$.  Assume the new restricted optimum
is positive.  Write
\begin{equation*}
 g_S^\star:=c_S-Q_{SA}x_A^\star>0,
 \qquad
 R:=Q_{SS}-Q_{SA}Q_{AA}^{-1}Q_{AS}.
\end{equation*}
Then the restricted optimum gain is
\begin{equation}
 \Delta_{A\to B}
 :=F_A^\star-F_B^\star
 =\frac12\langle g_S^\star,R^{-1}g_S^\star\rangle
 \geq\frac12\norm{g_S^\star}_2^2.
 \label{eq:boundary-admission-gain}
\end{equation}
If the replay checkpoint satisfies $0\leq x\leq x_A^\star$ coordinatewise,
then its observable same-point boundary residual
$g_S(x)=c_S-Q_{SA}x$ obeys $0<g_S(x)\leq g_S^\star$.  Consequently, along
nested admissions launched from such lower checkpoints,
\begin{equation}
 \sum_j\norm{g_j(x_j)}_2^2
 \leq2\sum_j\Delta_j
 =2(F_{A_0}^\star-F_{A_J}^\star).
 \label{eq:boundary-admission-telescope}
\end{equation}
In particular, batches with certified residual norm at least $\zeta$ occur at
most $2(F_{A_0}^\star-F_{A_J}^\star)/\zeta^2$ times.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Block elimination of the restricted quadratic gives the Schur formula in
\cref{eq:boundary-admission-gain}.  For the normalized Laplacian,
$0\prec R\preceq Q_{SS}\preceq I$, hence $R^{-1}\succeq I$.  Since the
off-diagonal block $Q_{SA}$ is nonpositive, $x\leq x_A^\star$ gives
$g_S(x)\leq g_S^\star$.  Squaring, summing, and telescoping the nested
restricted optimum values proves \cref{eq:boundary-admission-telescope}.
\end{proof}

This ledger does not apply to an arbitrary accelerated point: a momentum
boundary shock can be large while the true restricted-optimum gain is
arbitrarily small.  A safe protocol must therefore return to a maintained
lower-shadow checkpoint before using this payment.  Without a residual
margin, support monotonicity alone bounds only the number of batches by the
final support size and can charge quadratic cumulative face volume.  If
accelerated epochs are restarted only when exposed volume doubles, the
doubling restarts are at most logarithmic and their replay volumes sum to at
most twice the final volume; smaller intervening admissions still need
\cref{eq:boundary-admission-telescope} or remain in a monotone growth phase.

\begin{corollary}[Conditional admission-work bound]
\label{cor:conditional-admission-work}
Let $V$ be the final exposed volume and suppose one replay scan/solve on a
face of volume $W$ costs at most $C_{\rm res}W$.  Classify as doubling every
batch with $\vol(A_{j+1})\geq2\vol(A_j)$.  Require every other batch to obey
the observable volume-normalized margin
\begin{equation*}
 \norm{g_j(x_j)}_2^2\geq\eta\vol(A_{j+1}).
\end{equation*}
Under the lower-checkpoint hypotheses of
\cref{lem:boundary-admission-gain}, total replay work satisfies
\begin{equation}
 W_{\rm adm}
 \leq2C_{\rm res}\left(V+\frac{\Delta_{\rm tot}}\eta\right).
 \label{eq:conditional-admission-work}
\end{equation}
If an admission can also discard $O(\bigl(\vol(A_j)/q\bigr))$ preceding
accelerated work, the stronger margin
$\norm{g_j}^2\geq\eta\vol(A_j)/q$ is required to pay that work by the same
argument.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The volumes of the doubling destinations form a geometric sequence and sum
to at most $2V$.  For nondoubling batches,
\cref{eq:boundary-admission-telescope} gives
$\sum_j\vol(A_{j+1})\leq2\Delta_{\rm tot}/\eta$.  Multiply the two sums by
$C_{\rm res}$.
\end{proof}

The margin is necessary for this interface.  Without it, one may attach
arbitrarily many coordinates with geometrically tiny positive optimum gains.
The gain sum stays finite while single-coordinate admissions make cumulative
replay volume $\Theta(V^2)$, or $\Theta(V^2/q)$ if each admission discards a
full accelerated window.  Volume doubling pays only its geometric
subsequence; it cannot pay the small batches between doublings.
