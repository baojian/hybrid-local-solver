\begin{abstract}
This note asks whether a componentwise entrance analysis can replace the
conservative spectral warmup used to prevent support retraction after a face
change.  The first target is an endpoint path and the second is a spider.
We first reconstruct the exact entrance condition in the project's
source-aligned variables.  An exact rational screen then separates two
questions that had been conflated: one stable-face proximal solve can be safe
on the realized path traces while still being insufficient uniformly over the
whole nonnegative residual cone.  In fact, supplied full paths give exact
cone witnesses against one, two, and three proximal solves, while a 16-arm
star gives an exact witness against four.  A leaf-seeded family then turns the
star obstruction into a literal reachable trajectory: every fixed number of
post-lock pure-prox solves fails for all sufficiently large stars.  Conversely,
an explicit logarithmic warmup makes every later trigger kernel entrywise
nonnegative on the full star.  An exact residual decomposition sharpens the
reachable lower bound to logarithmic order, so this repeated-solve policy has
matching $\Theta(\log B)$ warmup scale.  A strict entrywise upper margin is
available on finite horizons, whereas every strict connected proper face has
an oscillatory Perron obstruction to permanent safety under the same global
momentum.  Tuning momentum to the face Perron mode repairs that obstruction
under an explicit spectral-ratio and Perron-spread warmup.  These results still
do not supply an end-to-end local-work theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Question and claim boundary}

The target is a proved constant or logarithmic post-lock warmup bound under a
fully stated changing-face recurrence and charged-work model.  A failed proof,
growing exact family, or spider obstruction will be retained as a refuted
route rather than promoted to an accelerated local-solver theorem.

The graph is a finite undirected endpoint path with ambient degrees, and all
variables below use the source-aligned PageRank parameter
\(q=\sqrt{\alpha/(1-\alpha)}\).  A supplied fixed face is not a local
discovery algorithm.  Every proximal solve, face sweep, inverse application,
certificate query, and state write must be charged by any algorithmic
corollary.  This note presently proves no \(\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}\) stopping
or end-to-end work theorem.

\subsection{Exact reconstruction of the Fable entrance gate}
\label{sec:gate-reconstruction}

Let \(\widetilde Q=D^{1/2}QD^{1/2}\),
\(\widetilde c=\alpha(s-\rho d)\), and
\(\kappa=1-2\alpha\), as in the exploratory Fable recurrence.  On a fixed
positive face \(S\), define the lower state residual
\begin{equation}
 Y_t=\widetilde c-\widetilde Qx_t
 \quad\hbox{and}\quad
 \mathcal M_S=\kappa D_S
 (\widetilde Q_{SS}+\kappa D_S)^{-1}.
 \label{eq:path-lock-hat-residual-map}
\end{equation}
If a stage has no retraction, exact obstacle optimality gives
\begin{equation}
 Y_{t+1}=\mathcal M_Su_t,
 \qquad
 u_t=(1+\beta)Y_t-\beta Y_{t-1}.
 \label{eq:path-lock-trigger-recurrence}
\end{equation}
The stage does not trigger if and only if \(u_t\geq0\) componentwise on its
support.  Conjugating by \(D^{1/2}\),
\begin{equation}
 D_S^{-1/2}\mathcal M_SD_S^{1/2}
 =\kappa(Q_{SS}+\kappa I)^{-1}.
 \label{eq:path-lock-normalized-map}
\end{equation}
Thus this is not the transported-center residual wave of the sibling
\texttt{path\_terminal\_modal\_block} note.  The two recurrences share the
weighted path cosine basis, but neither one's entry profile can be imported
into the other without a new calculation.

For completeness, split the normalized residual as
\(D^{-1/2}Y_t=L_t\phi+h_t\), where \(\phi>0\) is the Perron direction of the
normalized map.  Fable's sharp sufficient entrance test is
\begin{equation}
 \left|(1+\beta)h_t-\beta h_{t-1}\right|
 \leq \beta L_{t-1}\phi
 \quad\hbox{componentwise},
 \qquad
 L_t\geq(1-q_S)L_{t-1}>0.
 \label{eq:path-lock-sharp-entrance}
\end{equation}
The absolute values make
\eqref{eq:path-lock-sharp-entrance} stronger than the exact trigger
\(u_t\geq0\).  The conservative certified warmup controls it by one global
norm; the present target is the true componentwise condition.

\subsection{One prox solve certifies the first momentum stage}
\label{sec:first-momentum-safe}

There is a useful graph-uniform fact hidden by the conservative modal norm.
It certifies entrance to momentum, but not the whole momentum tail.

\begin{proposition}[First-momentum entrance; proved here]
\label{prop:first-momentum-safe}
Let \(0<\alpha\leq1/5\), let \(S\) be any supplied face, and let
\(0\leq\beta\leq1\).  Suppose a stable-face pure proximal solve starts from
a state residual \(Y\geq0\) and produces \(Y^+=\mathcal M_SY\).  Then the
immediately following momentum trigger is nonnegative:
\begin{equation}
 (1+\beta)Y^+-\beta Y\geq0
 \quad\hbox{componentwise}.
 \label{eq:first-momentum-safe}
\end{equation}
Consequently that first momentum stage has no retraction.  The claim applies
to every endpoint-path prefix and every spider face, without a spectral-gap
or Perron-spread constant.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Write \(S_S=D_S^{-1/2}A_{SS}D_S^{-1/2}\geq0\).  Direct substitution gives
\begin{equation}
 \overline{\mathcal M}_S
 :=D_S^{-1/2}\mathcal M_SD_S^{1/2}
 =\frac{2(1-2\alpha)}{1-\alpha}(3I-S_S)^{-1}.
 \label{eq:first-momentum-neumann-map}
\end{equation}
Since \(\lVert S_S\rVert_2\leq1\), the Neumann series
\begin{equation}
 (3I-S_S)^{-1}=\frac13\sum_{r\geq0}(S_S/3)^r
 \label{eq:first-momentum-neumann-series}
\end{equation}
is entrywise nonnegative and has diagonal at least \(1/3\).  The scalar
coefficient in \eqref{eq:first-momentum-neumann-map} is at least \(3/2\)
exactly when \(\alpha\leq1/5\).  Hence
\(\overline{\mathcal M}_S\geq\frac12I\) entrywise.  With
\(\sigma=\beta/(1+\beta)\leq1/2\), this gives
\(\overline{\mathcal M}_S-\sigma I\geq0\).  Positive diagonal conjugation
preserves entrywise signs, so
\[
 (1+\beta)Y^+-\beta Y
 =(1+\beta)(\mathcal M_S-\sigma I)Y\geq0.
\]
The exact trigger equivalence in
\eqref{eq:path-lock-trigger-recurrence} completes the proof.
\end{proof}

This proposition reduces the actual entrance warmup from Fable's large
norm-certified \(J_S\) to one solve if the goal is only to launch one safe
momentum stage.  It does not establish
\eqref{eq:path-lock-sharp-entrance}, and
\cref{prop:path-lock-cone-witnesses} shows why the conclusion cannot simply
be iterated forever.

\begin{proposition}[Every fixed safe burst with reset loses acceleration;
proved here]
\label{prop:alternating-reset-slow}
Fix \(L\geq1\).  On the full connected face, consider a cycle comprising one
pure proximal stage followed by \(L\) consecutive momentum stages, and then
reset momentum.  Assume those \(L\) stages have already been certified safe.
On the Perron residual mode, one cycle has exact multiplier
\begin{equation}
 \eta_{L,q}=\bigl(1+(L+1)q\bigr)(1-q)^{L+1}.
\label{eq:alternating-reset-multiplier}
\end{equation}
For every fixed \(L\),
\begin{equation}
 1-\eta_{L,q}=\binom{L+2}{2}q^2+O_L(q^3).
 \label{eq:fixed-burst-expansion}
\end{equation}
Thus a constant-factor reduction of a nonzero Perron component needs
\(\Theta_L(q^{-2})=\Theta_L(\alpha^{-1})\) cycles.  A bounded safe burst
followed by reset is not an accelerated algorithm.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
On the full face the Perron eigenvalue of \(\mathcal M\) is
\(m_0=1-q^2\), and the global momentum is
\(\beta=(1-q)/(1+q)\).  Normalize the incoming Perron residual to
\(p_0=1\).  The pure proximal stage gives \(p_1=m_0\), and each safe
momentum stage obeys
\[
 p_{j+1}=m_0\bigl((1+\beta)p_j-\beta p_{j-1}\bigr).
\]
The characteristic polynomial is
\[
 \lambda^2-2(1-q)\lambda+(1-q)^2=(\lambda-(1-q))^2.
\]
The two initial values therefore give
\(p_j=(1+jq)(1-q)^j\).  Taking \(j=L+1\) proves
\eqref{eq:alternating-reset-multiplier}; Taylor expansion at \(q=0\)
proves \eqref{eq:fixed-burst-expansion} and the fixed-\(L\) rate claim.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A cone-uniform shortcut is already false}
\label{sec:cone-screen}

Suppose a supplied full face receives an arbitrary nonnegative residual
column \(y\).  After \(J\geq1\) stable-face pure proximal solves, the two
entrance residuals are \(\mathcal M^{J-1}y\) and
\(\mathcal M^Jy\).  The first trigger matrix is therefore
\begin{equation}
 U_0^{(J)}=
 \mathcal M^{J-1}\bigl((1+\beta)\mathcal M-\beta I\bigr),
 \label{eq:path-lock-trigger-kernel-initial}
\end{equation}
and subsequent trigger matrices obey
\begin{equation}
 U_{k+1}^{(J)}=
 \mathcal M\bigl((1+\beta)U_k^{(J)}-\beta U_{k-1}^{(J)}\bigr),
 \qquad U_{-1}^{(J)}=\mathcal M^{J-1}.
 \label{eq:path-lock-trigger-kernel-recurrence}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Exact cone witnesses; proved here]
\label{prop:path-lock-cone-witnesses}
A theorem asserting permanent no-triggering after one stable-face proximal
solve for \emph{every} nonnegative residual is false, even on the full
endpoint path.  On \(P_4\), with \(q=1/8\), \(J=1\), the endpoint diagonal
of \(U_2^{(1)}\) is
\begin{equation}
 (U_2^{(1)})_{00}=-\frac{541}{48000}<0.
 \label{eq:path-lock-one-warmup-witness}
\end{equation}
The analogous two-solve assertion is also false: on \(P_8\), with
\(q=1/16\), \(J=2\),
\begin{equation}
 (U_3^{(2)})_{00}
 =-\frac{151523312832478779015383634375}
 {28857779215644070278768053190656}<0.
 \label{eq:path-lock-two-warmup-witness}
\end{equation}
In each case the nonnegative entrance column \(y=e_0\) realizes the negative
trigger coordinate.

Three solves are not cone-uniform either.  On \(P_{46}\), with
\(q=1/92\), exact rational elimination gives
\begin{equation}
 (U_4^{(3)})_{00}<0.
 \label{eq:path-lock-three-warmup-witness}
\end{equation}
The verifier prints the full reduced fraction; its decimal value is about
\(-1.0542862762\times10^{-5}\).  The smaller sizes through \(P_{44}\) do not
replace this witness: finite endpoint effects delay the first negative
diagonal until size 46 in the screened family.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For a full endpoint path all entries of \(\widetilde Q\), \(D\),
\(\mathcal M\), and \(\beta=(1-q)/(1+q)\) are rational.  Exact Gaussian
elimination followed by
\eqref{eq:path-lock-trigger-kernel-initial} and
\eqref{eq:path-lock-trigger-kernel-recurrence}
gives the two displayed fractions and the exact negative sign in
\eqref{eq:path-lock-three-warmup-witness}.  Multiplication by \(e_0\)
selects the first column, so its endpoint is negative and the exact trigger
condition fails.
\end{proof}

The witnesses are deliberately scoped.  They refute a proof that forgets the
actual face-entry profile and uses only \(Y\geq0\); they do not show that
\(e_0\) is reached by the endpoint-seeded changing-face RPPR execution.  They
also do not rule out a different absolute constant.  The exact finite screen
over \(q=1/(2n)\) found the first horizon-passing values \(J=2\) on
\(P_4,P_6\) and \(J=3\) on \(P_8,P_{10},P_{12},P_{14},P_{16},P_{20}\),
through 32 or 64 momentum stages as applicable.  This passing table is
\emph{Measured} finite exact arithmetic, not an all-time theorem.

\section{The spider junction: exact coupling and an obstruction}
\label{sec:spider-junction}

Let a supplied spider have center \(c\), \(B\geq3\) arms, and arm lengths
\(\ell_a\).  Write \(v_{a,r}\) for distance \(r\) along arm \(a\), and let
\(\delta_{a,r}\) be its ambient degree.  Thus the center degree is \(B\), an
internal arm degree is two, and a full-arm leaf degree is one.

\begin{proposition}[Exact center equation and star obstruction; proved here]
\label{prop:spider-center-and-star}
In normalized coordinates, put \(R=(3I-S_S)^{-1}\).  For \(z=Rf\), the
center and arm equations are exactly
\begin{align}
 3z_c-\sum_{a:v_{a,1}\in S}
   \frac{z_{a,1}}{\sqrt{B\delta_{a,1}}}&=f_c,
 \label{eq:spider-center-row}\\
 3z_{a,r}
 -\frac{z_{a,r-1}}{\sqrt{\delta_{a,r}\delta_{a,r-1}}}
 -\mathbf 1_{\{r<\ell_a,\ v_{a,r+1}\in S\}}
  \frac{z_{a,r+1}}{\sqrt{\delta_{a,r}\delta_{a,r+1}}}
 &=f_{a,r},
 \label{eq:spider-arm-row}
\end{align}
where \(z_{a,0}=z_c\) and \(\delta_{a,0}=B\).  Moreover, cone-uniform
permanent safety after four pure proximal solves is false on spiders.  On the
16-arm unit-length star, with \(q=1/34\), the leaf diagonal of the second
consecutive momentum trigger is
\begin{equation}
 (U_1^{(4)})_{11}
 =-\frac{17140927690425}{914326479306752}<0.
 \label{eq:star-four-warmup-witness}
\end{equation}
The same star already has a negative \(U_1^{(J)}\) leaf diagonal for each
\(J=1,2,3\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Equations \eqref{eq:spider-center-row}--\eqref{eq:spider-arm-row} are the
rows of \((3I-S_S)z=f\), using the normalized edge weight
\(1/\sqrt{d_id_j}\).  They are the only junction coupling; the residual map
is \(\overline{\mathcal M}=2(1-q^2)R\).

For the star, \(S\) has eigenvalues \(1,-1,0\), with the zero eigenspace of
dimension \(B-1\).  Hence the three residual-map eigenvalues are
\[
 m_+=1-q^2,\qquad m_-=(1-q^2)/2,\qquad
 m_z=2(1-q^2)/3.
\]
For a leaf coordinate and any scalar function \(F\) on these eigenvalues,
spectral projection gives
\begin{equation}
 (F(\overline{\mathcal M}))_{11}
 =\left(1-\frac1B\right)F(m_z)
  +\frac1{2B}\bigl(F(m_+)+F(m_-)\bigr).
 \label{eq:star-leaf-mixture}
\end{equation}
From \eqref{eq:path-lock-trigger-kernel-recurrence},
\[
 F_J(m)=m^J\bigl((1+\beta)^2m-\beta(2+\beta)\bigr)
\]
is the scalar polynomial for \(U_1^{(J)}\).  Substituting
\(B=16\), \(q=1/34\), and \(J=4\) into
\eqref{eq:star-leaf-mixture} gives
\eqref{eq:star-four-warmup-witness}.  The exact values for \(J=1,2,3\) are,
respectively,
\[
 -\frac{22143}{152320},\qquad
 -\frac{14394765}{171051008},\qquad
 -\frac{17615977995}{395469930496}.
\]
Positive diagonal conjugation leaves diagonal entries unchanged, so these
are also the hat-coordinate trigger entries printed by the verifier.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A reachable family and a logarithmic repair}
\label{sec:reachable-star-family}

The preceding diagonal witness used an arbitrary nonnegative entrance
column.  The next theorem closes that reachability gap for a concrete
leaf-seeded schedule.  A second theorem gives a logarithmic upper mechanism
at the level of full-face trigger kernels.

\begin{theorem}[No universal constant warmup on reachable stars; proved here]
\label{thm:reachable-star-no-constant}
For every fixed integer $J\geq1$, there is an arm count $B_J$ such that for
every $B\geq B_J$ the following holds.  Let $G_B$ be the unit $B$-star, seed
leaf one, and set
\begin{equation}
 q_B=\frac{1}{2(B+1)},\qquad
 \alpha_B=\frac{q_B^2}{1+q_B^2},\qquad
 \rho_B=\frac{1}{4B}.
 \label{eq:reachable-star-parameters}
\end{equation}
Starting from zero, run exact obstacle-prox stages with $\beta=0$ until the
full face first appears, run exactly $J$ further full-face pure-prox stages,
and then use $\beta_B=(1-q_B)/(1+q_B)$.  The seeded
leaf of the second momentum trigger is negative.  Consequently no
graph-size-independent constant $J$ can certify this exact policy.

This is not a solver lower bound.  It does not exclude a $q$-dependent or
adaptive warmup, a different momentum rule, or a finite-inner method with
explicit margins.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Put $t=(1-\alpha_B)/2$, $\kappa=1-2\alpha_B$, and
$R_B=\kappa/t=2-1/[2(B+1)^2]$.  By symmetry, retain three coordinates:
the center $c$, the seeded leaf $s$, and one common value $o$ for every
other leaf.  In the scaled variable $X=(t/\alpha_B)x$, the shifted Hessian is
$tK_B$, where
\begin{equation}
 K_B=
 \begin{pmatrix}
  3B&-1&-(B-1)\\
  -1&3&0\\
  -1&0&3
 \end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 \frac{\widetilde c}{\alpha_B}
 =\left(-\frac14,1-\frac{1}{4B},-\frac{1}{4B}\right)^{\!\top}.
 \label{eq:reachable-star-three-class}
\end{equation}

While only $c,s$ are positive, write $Z_n=BX_{n,c}$ and $S_n=X_{n,s}$.
The exact two-class recurrence is
\begin{align}
 r_{c,n}&=-\frac14+R_BZ_{n-1},&
 r_{s,n}&=1-\frac{1}{4B}+R_BS_{n-1},
 \label{eq:reachable-star-rhs}\\
 Z_n&=\frac{B(3r_{c,n}+r_{s,n})}{9B-1},&
 S_n&=\frac{r_{c,n}+3Br_{s,n}}{9B-1},
 \label{eq:reachable-star-restricted-recurrence}
\end{align}
starting at zero.  The inactive-other KKT condition is exactly $Z_n\leq1/4$.
Taking $B\to\infty$ in these rational functions gives
\begin{equation}
 (Z_1,Z_2,Z_3)\longrightarrow
 \left(\frac1{36},\frac{13}{108},\frac{25}{108}\right),
 \qquad
 Z_4^{\rm res}\longrightarrow\frac{329}{972}>\frac14.
 \label{eq:reachable-star-admission-limits}
\end{equation}
All inequalities are strict.  Rational continuity therefore implies that,
for every sufficiently large $B$, the first three outputs have support
$\{c,s\}$ and the fourth output admits all other leaves.

For the fourth, full-face solve put $W_4=BX_{4,o}$.  The limiting three
equations give
\begin{equation}
 Z_4\longrightarrow\frac{151}{432},\qquad
 S_4\longrightarrow\frac{65}{81},\qquad
 W_4\longrightarrow\frac{43}{1296},
 \label{eq:reachable-star-full-state}
\end{equation}
so all three classes are strictly positive.  Let
$Y_4=\widetilde c-\widetilde Qx_4$.  Exact shifted optimality gives
$Y_4=\kappa D(x_4-x_3)$.  Hence, in normalized coordinates,
\begin{equation}
 \alpha_B^{-1}D^{-1/2}Y_4
 \longrightarrow \frac{16}{81}e_{\mathrm{seed}}
 \quad\hbox{in }\ell_2.
 \label{eq:reachable-star-residual-limit}
\end{equation}
Indeed the seeded entry tends to
$R_B(S_4-S_3)\to16/81$, the center is $O(B^{-1/2})$, and the combined norm
of the other leaves is $O(B^{-1/2})$.  The residual is strictly positive for
all sufficiently large $B$; subsequent pure-prox stages preserve positivity.

The normalized full-face residual map has eigenvalues
\begin{equation}
 m_+=1-q_B^2,\qquad m_z=\frac23(1-q_B^2),\qquad
 m_- =\frac12(1-q_B^2),
 \label{eq:reachable-star-three-modes}
\end{equation}
where the leaf-contrast eigenspace for $m_z$ has multiplicity $B-1$.  After
$J$ pure-prox stages, the scalar polynomial of the second momentum trigger is
\begin{equation}
 F_J(m)=m^J\left((1+\beta_B)^2m-\beta_B(2+\beta_B)\right).
 \label{eq:reachable-star-second-trigger-polynomial}
\end{equation}
The first trigger is nonnegative by \cref{prop:first-momentum-safe}, since
$\alpha_B\leq1/5$.  The seeded coordinate in
\eqref{eq:reachable-star-residual-limit} is asymptotically entirely leaf
contrast.  Therefore, for every fixed $J$,
\begin{equation}
 \alpha_B^{-1}(U_1^{(J)}Y_4)_s
 \longrightarrow
 \frac{16}{81}F_J(2/3)
 =-\frac{16}{243}\left(\frac23\right)^J<0.
 \label{eq:reachable-star-negative-limit}
\end{equation}
Strict convergence proves the claimed threshold $B_J$.
\end{proof}

The exact three-class verifier supplies a finite guard for the asymptotic
argument.  At $B=50$, $J=6$, $q=1/102$, and $\rho=1/200$, the first negative
trigger is the seeded leaf at stage $11=J+5$, with value
\begin{equation}
 -\frac{147824969167474143070830622648108443644686306420711}
 {5876713986025144219355264603664195883039449023398155386880}.
 \label{eq:reachable-star-b50-witness}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Matching logarithmic reachable-star lower bound; proved here]
\label{thm:reachable-star-logarithmic-lower}
There is an absolute $B_0$ such that, for every $B\geq B_0$ and every integer
\begin{equation}
 1\leq J\leq
 \min\left\{B,
 \left\lfloor\log_{3/2}\!\left(\frac{9B}{6400}\right)\right\rfloor
 \right\},
 \label{eq:reachable-star-logarithmic-lower-range}
\end{equation}
the literal trajectory of \cref{thm:reachable-star-no-constant} reaches the
full face at $x_4$, survives the first momentum stage, and its seeded-leaf
normalized residual at the second momentum trigger satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \alpha_B^{-1}(U_1^{(J)}Y_4)_s\leq-\frac{2}{B}.
 \label{eq:reachable-star-logarithmic-lower-margin}
\end{equation}
The integer range becomes nonempty at $B=1067$.  Together with
\cref{thm:star-logarithmic-warmup}, this gives a $\Theta(\log B)$ warmup
scale for this exact pure-prox-then-global-momentum policy.  It remains a
policy-specific statement, not a lower bound for all local solvers.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Write the exact normalized entrance residual as
\[
 \alpha_B^{-1}D^{-1/2}Y_4
 =\left(C_B/\sqrt B,S_B,O_B,\ldots,O_B\right).
\]
Direct substitution of the three-class recurrence gives, for every $B\geq2$,
\begin{equation}
 0<C_B<1,\qquad
 0<S_B+(B-1)O_B<1,\qquad
 S_B-O_B\geq\frac{3}{20}.
 \label{eq:reachable-star-residual-margins}
\end{equation}
These are exact rational inequalities.  For example, the last left-hand
side is
\[
 \frac{(2B+1)(2B+3)}{96(B+1)^8(9B-1)^3}
 \begin{aligned}[t]
 (&3456B^9+20376B^8+47328B^7+56134B^6+38516B^5\\
  &+18173B^4+6724B^3+1423B^2+23B-1).
 \end{aligned}
\]
After subtracting $3/20$, clearing the positive denominator, and putting
$B=b+2$, every coefficient is nonnegative and the constant coefficient is
positive.  The other two bounds have the same coefficient certificate; the
exact verifier checks all three symbolic polynomials.

Let $F_+,F_z,F_-$ denote \eqref{eq:reachable-star-second-trigger-polynomial}
at the three modes in \eqref{eq:reachable-star-three-modes}.  Exact star
spectral projectors give
\begin{align}
 T_{B,J}:={}&\alpha_B^{-1}(U_1^{(J)}Y_4)_s\notag\\
 ={}&\frac{(F_+-F_-)C_B}{2B}
 +\frac{(F_++F_-)(S_B+(B-1)O_B)}{2B}
 +\left(1-\frac1B\right)(S_B-O_B)F_z.
 \label{eq:reachable-star-exact-seed-trigger}
\end{align}
With $m_0=1-q_B^2$, scalar simplification yields
\begin{align*}
 F_+&=m_0^J\frac{(1-q_B)(1+3q_B)}{(1+q_B)^2},\\
 F_-&=-m_-^J\left(\frac{1-q_B}{1+q_B}\right)^2,\\
 F_z&=-\left(\frac23\right)^Jm_0^J
 \frac{(1-q_B)(1-5q_B)}{3(1+q_B)^2}.
\end{align*}
Thus $|F_+|,|F_-|\leq1$.  For $B\geq4$ and $J\leq B$,
$m_0^J\geq3/4$ and the final rational factor in $F_z$ is at least $1/10$.
Using \eqref{eq:reachable-star-residual-margins} in
\eqref{eq:reachable-star-exact-seed-trigger} gives
\begin{equation}
 T_{B,J}\leq\frac{2}{B}
 -\frac{9}{1600}\left(\frac23\right)^J.
 \label{eq:reachable-star-quantitative-trigger-bound}
\end{equation}
The range \eqref{eq:reachable-star-logarithmic-lower-range} makes the second
term at least $4/B$.  The support chronology holds for all sufficiently
large $B$ by the strict limits in
\eqref{eq:reachable-star-admission-limits}--\eqref{eq:reachable-star-full-state};
absorbing that fixed threshold into $B_0$ proves the claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Logarithmic cone-uniform star warmup; proved here]
\label{thm:star-logarithmic-warmup}
Let $B\geq2$, $0<q\leq1/2$, and let a supplied full $B$-star face receive any
nonnegative residual $y$.  If
\begin{equation}
 J\geq3,\qquad
 \left(\frac23\right)^{J-1}\leq\frac{1}{8(B-1)},
 \label{eq:star-logarithmic-condition}
\end{equation}
then $J$ exact pure-prox solves followed by
$\beta=(1-q)/(1+q)$ make every later trigger nonnegative.  In particular one
may take
\begin{equation}
 J_B=\max\left\{3,
 1+\left\lceil\log_{3/2}(8(B-1))\right\rceil\right\}.
 \label{eq:star-logarithmic-warmup}
\end{equation}
This is an exact fixed-full-face theorem, not a finite-inner margin theorem.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $m_0=1-q^2$, $\theta=1-q$, and let $u_k(m)$ be the scalar trigger
kernel, with
\begin{align*}
 u_{-1}(m)&=m^{J-1},\\
 u_0(m)&=m^{J-1}((1+\beta)m-\beta),\\
 u_{k+1}(m)&=m((1+\beta)u_k(m)-\beta u_{k-1}(m)).
\end{align*}
On the Perron mode,
\begin{equation}
 u_k(m_0)=m_0^{J-1}\theta^{k+1}
 \left(1+\frac{q(k+1)}{1+q}\right)>0.
 \label{eq:star-perron-trigger}
\end{equation}
For $m=\delta m_0$, $\delta\in\{2/3,1/2\}$, the characteristic roots are
$\theta\sqrt\delta\,e^{\pm i\phi_\delta}$ with
$\cos\phi_\delta=\sqrt\delta$.  Direct solution gives
\begin{equation}
 u_k(\delta m_0)=(\delta m_0)^{J-1}
 (\theta\sqrt\delta)^{k+1}
 \left(\cos((k+1)\phi_\delta)
 +A_\delta(q)\sin((k+1)\phi_\delta)\right),
 \label{eq:star-oscillatory-trigger}
\end{equation}
where
\[
 A_\delta(q)=
 \frac{\delta(1+q)-1}{(1+q)\sqrt{\delta(1-\delta)}}.
\]
For the two displayed values of $\delta$ and $q\leq1/2$,
$|A_\delta(q)|\leq1$.  Thus, for every $k\geq-1$,
\begin{equation}
 \frac{|u_k(\delta m_0)|}{u_k(m_0)}
 \leq2\delta^{J-1+(k+1)/2}
 \leq2\delta^{J-1}.
 \label{eq:star-mode-domination}
\end{equation}

Write $F_+=u_k(m_+)$, $F_z=u_k(m_z)$, and $F_-=u_k(m_-)$.  Conditions
\eqref{eq:star-logarithmic-condition} and $J\geq3$ imply
$|F_-|\leq F_+/2$ and
$|F_z|\leq F_+/[4(B-1)]$.  The four entry types of
$u_k(\overline{\mathcal M})$ are
\begin{align*}
 cc &: (F_++F_-)/2,\\
 c\ell &: (F_+-F_-)/(2\sqrt B),\\
 \ell\ell &: (1-1/B)F_z+(F_++F_-)/(2B),\\
 \ell\ell' &: -F_z/B+(F_++F_-)/(2B).
\end{align*}
The two bounds make each expression nonnegative.  Hence every trigger kernel
is entrywise nonnegative, and multiplication by $y\geq0$ proves permanent
full-face safety.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Strict full-star kernel margin; proved here]
\label{cor:star-logarithmic-kernel-margin}
Under the hypotheses of \cref{thm:star-logarithmic-warmup}, strengthen the
warmup conditions to
\begin{equation}
 J\geq4,\qquad
 \left(\frac23\right)^{J-1}\leq\frac{1}{16(B-1)}.
 \label{eq:star-strict-margin-condition}
\end{equation}
Then, for every $k\geq-1$ and all star coordinates $i,j$,
\begin{equation}
 [u_k(\overline{\mathcal M})]_{ij}
 \geq\frac{a_k}{4B},
 \qquad
 a_k:=u_k(m_0)>0.
 \label{eq:star-strict-kernel-margin}
\end{equation}
Consequently an entrance residual $y\geq0$ has trigger margin at least
$a_k\lVert y\rVert_1/(4B)$ in every coordinate.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Equations \eqref{eq:star-mode-domination} and
\eqref{eq:star-strict-margin-condition} give
$|F_-|\leq a_k/4$ and $|F_z|\leq a_k/[8(B-1)]$.  Substitution in the four
entry types in the preceding proof gives respective lower bounds
$3a_k/8$, $3a_k/(8\sqrt B)$, $a_k/(4B)$, and at least $a_k/(4B)$.
\end{proof}

The margin in \cref{cor:star-logarithmic-kernel-margin} is useful but not
uniform in time or input scale: $a_k\to0$.  On a fixed finite horizon $T$,
an absolute finite-inner schedule can compare its accumulated trigger error
with $\min_{k\leq T}a_k\lVert y\rVert_1/(4B)$.  Permanent finite-inner safety
instead requires a relative tolerance, a finite window, or a restart rule.

\begin{proposition}[Conditional finite-inner full-star window; proved here]
\label{prop:star-finite-inner-window}
Let $B\geq2$, $0<q\leq1/2$, and let a reset admit the final full unit $B$-star from a normalized trigger
$g\geq0$, and put $G=\lVert g\rVert_1>0$ and $m_0=1-q^2$.  Suppose the
shifted solve returns normalized right-hand-side residual $\bar a_t$.
Choose $J$ as in \eqref{eq:star-strict-margin-condition}, fix a momentum
horizon $0\leq k\leq T$, and set
\begin{equation*}
 a_{\min}:=\min_{0\leq k\leq T}u_k(m_0),
 \qquad
 h:=\frac{a_{\min}m_0G}{96B}.
\end{equation*}
If the admission and $J$ warmup solves have
$\lVert\bar a_t\rVert_2\leq\xi_{\rm w}$, the momentum-window solves have
$\lVert\bar a_t\rVert_2\leq\xi_{\rm m}$, and
\begin{align}
 \xi_{\rm w}&\leq
 \min\left\{
  \frac{m_0G}{24B},
  \frac{hq}{16(J+1)},
  \frac{m_0G}{24B(J+1)}\left(\frac{3m_0}{4}\right)^J
 \right\},
 \label{eq:star-finite-warm-tolerance}\\
 \xi_{\rm m}&\leq\frac{hq^2}{8},
 \label{eq:star-finite-momentum-tolerance}
\end{align}
then every trigger through the stated window is strictly positive.  To keep
the approximate primal state on the full face, it is additionally sufficient
to enforce the observable per-stage guard
\begin{equation}
 \lVert\bar a_t\rVert_2\leq\frac{\lVert u_t\rVert_1}{24B}.
 \label{eq:star-finite-primal-guard}
\end{equation}
This is a one-admission, final-full-face finite-window theorem, not a
changing-proper-face convergence result.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The four exact entry types of the normalized full-star map are
\[
 M_{cc}=\frac{3m_0}{4},\quad
 M_{c\ell}=\frac{m_0}{4\sqrt B},\quad
 M_{\ell\ell}=m_0\left(\frac23+\frac{1}{12B}\right),\quad
 M_{\ell\ell'}=\frac{m_0}{12B}.
\]
Thus the exact admission output $y_0=Mg$ obeys
$\min_i(y_0)_i\geq m_0G/(12B)$ and
$\lVert y_0\rVert_1\geq m_0G/12$.  A finite solve instead gives
$y_0^{\rm fin}=Mg+(I-M)\bar a_0$, and
$\lVert(I-M)\bar a_0\rVert_2\leq\lVert\bar a_0\rVert_2$.  The first and
third bounds in \eqref{eq:star-finite-warm-tolerance} preserve positivity
through admission and pure warmup; the minimum row sum of $M$ is at least
$3m_0/4$.

By \cref{cor:star-logarithmic-kernel-margin}, the ideal $k$th momentum
trigger is at least $a_km_0G/(48B)$ in every coordinate.  The scalar Green
kernel satisfies $|g_r|\leq(r+1)(1-q)^r$, so the accumulated trigger error is
at most
\[
 \frac{8(J+1)}q\xi_{\rm w}+\frac4{q^2}\xi_{\rm m}\leq h.
\]
This leaves at least half the ideal minimum margin.  Finally, the exact primal
increment is $M u_t/\kappa$, whose minimum coordinate is at least
$m_0\lVert u_t\rVert_1/(12B\kappa)$, while the solve error is at most
$m_0\lVert\bar a_t\rVert_2/\kappa$.  This proves
\eqref{eq:star-finite-primal-guard} and the claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Strict proper faces defeat permanent global momentum]
\label{prop:proper-face-global-momentum-stop}
Let the ambient graph be connected and let $A\subsetneq V$ be a nonempty
connected fixed face.  For any finite number $J$ of pure-prox warmup solves,
the global choice $\beta=(1-q)/(1+q)$ is not permanently cone-safe on $A$:
for every nonzero entrance residual $y\geq0$, some later exact fixed-face
trigger has a negative coordinate.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Let $S_A=D_A^{-1/2}A_{AA}D_A^{-1/2}$.  The strict Dirichlet face has Perron
eigenvalue $\lambda_1<1$ and a positive Perron vector $\phi_1$.  With
$m_0=1-q^2$, the corresponding residual-map eigenvalue is
\[
 m_1=\delta_1m_0,
 \qquad \delta_1=\frac{2}{3-\lambda_1}<1.
\]
The scalar momentum characteristic roots are
$(1-q)\sqrt{\delta_1}\,e^{\pm i\varphi_1}$ with
$\cos\varphi_1=\sqrt{\delta_1}$.  Hence $u_k(m_1)$ is a nonzero damped
sinusoid and is negative for some $k$; multiplying by $m_1^{J-1}$ changes
only its amplitude.  Since
\[
 \langle\phi_1,u_k(\overline{\mathcal M})y\rangle
 =u_k(m_1)\langle\phi_1,y\rangle
\]
and the second factor is positive, that trigger cannot be componentwise
nonnegative.  The negative time may diverge as $\lambda_1\uparrow1$, so the
claim does not exclude finite windows or face-adapted momentum.
\end{proof}

The preceding obstruction is specific to using the full-graph momentum
parameter on a Dirichlet face.  Tuning momentum to the current face restores
permanent exact cone safety, at the cost of face spectral information.

\begin{theorem}[Face-tuned permanent cone safety; proved here]
\label{thm:face-tuned-permanent-safety}
Let $A$ be a finite connected fixed face.  Write the eigenvalues of its
normalized residual map as $1>\mu_1$, and, in dimension at least two,
$\mu_1>\mu_2\geq\cdots>0$.  Let $\phi_1>0$ be its unit Perron vector, and put
\begin{equation*}
 \gamma:=\min_i(\phi_1)_i,\qquad
 q_{\mathrm{face}}:=\sqrt{1-\mu_1},\qquad
 \beta_{\mathrm{face}}:=\frac{1-q_{\mathrm{face}}}{1+q_{\mathrm{face}}},\qquad
 \delta_i:=\frac{\mu_i}{\mu_1}.
\end{equation*}
For $i\geq2$, define
\begin{equation*}
 A_i:=\frac{\delta_i(1+q_{\mathrm{face}})-1}
 {(1+q_{\mathrm{face}})\sqrt{\delta_i(1-\delta_i)}},\qquad
 C_\star:=\max_{i\geq2}\sqrt{1+A_i^2},\qquad
 \delta_\star:=\delta_2,
\end{equation*}
In dimension one, omit $C_\star,\delta_\star$ and regard the following
condition as vacuous.  For an integer $J\geq1$, if
\begin{equation}
 C_\star\delta_\star^{J-1}\leq\frac{\gamma^2}{2},
 \label{eq:face-tuned-warmup-condition}
\end{equation}
then $J$ pure-prox solves followed by $\beta_{\mathrm{face}}$ give, for every
$k\geq-1$,
\begin{equation}
 [u_k(\overline{\mathcal M}_A)]_{ij}
 \geq\frac12u_k(\mu_1)(\phi_1)_i(\phi_1)_j
 \geq\frac{\gamma^2}{2}u_k(\mu_1)>0.
 \label{eq:face-tuned-kernel-margin}
\end{equation}
Hence every nonzero entrance residual $y\geq0$ has a permanently positive
exact trigger tail.  The warmup depends on the face spectral ratio and Perron
spread; it is not graph-uniform.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The Perron scalar is
\[
 u_k(\mu_1)=\mu_1^{J-1}(1-q_{\mathrm{face}})^{k+1}
 \left(1+\frac{q_{\mathrm{face}}(k+1)}{1+q_{\mathrm{face}}}\right)>0.
\]
For a lower mode, the same calculation as
\eqref{eq:star-oscillatory-trigger} gives
\[
 \frac{|u_k(\mu_i)|}{u_k(\mu_1)}
 \leq\sqrt{1+A_i^2}\,
 \delta_i^{J-1+(k+1)/2}
 \leq C_\star\delta_\star^{J-1}.
\]
By Cauchy--Schwarz, the absolute value of each entry of the lower-mode
spectral remainder is at most the last ratio times $u_k(\mu_1)$.  The Perron
rank-one entry is at least $\gamma^2u_k(\mu_1)$, so
\eqref{eq:face-tuned-warmup-condition} proves
\eqref{eq:face-tuned-kernel-margin}.
\end{proof}

All quantities in \cref{thm:face-tuned-permanent-safety} belong to the current
face, but computing or certifying them must be charged.  Full eigendata are
not necessary for a conditional accelerated window: pure-prox probes and an
observable trigger gate suffice.

\begin{theorem}[Collatz-bracketed gated accelerated window]
\label{thm:collatz-gated-window}
Let $\overline{\mathcal M}_A$ be the positive symmetric normalized residual
map of a fixed connected face, with $0<\mu_i\leq\mu_1<1$.  From an anchor
$z^0>0$, form pure-prox probes $z^{s+1}=\overline{\mathcal M}_Az^s$ and
\begin{equation*}
 L_s:=\min_i\frac{z_i^{s+1}}{z_i^s},
 \qquad U_s:=\max_i\frac{z_i^{s+1}}{z_i^s}.
\end{equation*}
Then $L_s\uparrow$, $U_s\downarrow$, and $L_s\leq\mu_1\leq U_s$.  Suppose a
probe gives $0<L\leq\mu_1\leq U<1$ and
\begin{equation*}
 U-L\leq\frac{1-U}{16}.
\end{equation*}
Put $q_U=\sqrt{1-U}$, $Q=\sqrt{1-L}$, and
$\beta_L=(1-Q)/(1+Q)$.  Restart at the same anchor, so the momentum-window
initial history is $y_{-1}=y_0=z$.  If the coordinatewise residual gate
accepts every stage of the following fixed-face momentum window, then its exact residuals
satisfy
\begin{equation}
 \norm{y_n}_2
 \leq3(n+1)\left(1-\frac{3q_U}{8}\right)^n\norm{z}_2.
 \label{eq:collatz-gated-window-rate}
\end{equation}
Thus, after the bracket is certified, the accepted window is face-accelerated
without computing $\mu_2$ or any eigenvector.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Collatz gives the bracket, while applying the positive map once more proves
its monotonicity.  The bracket condition gives
$q_U\leq Q\leq\sqrt{17/16}\,q_U$.  On an eigenmode $\mu\leq L$, the momentum
roots have modulus at most $1-Q$.  For $L\leq\mu\leq U$, the larger root is
at most
\begin{equation*}
 \frac{U+\sqrt{U(U-L)}}{1+Q}
 \leq1-\frac{3q_U}{8}.
\end{equation*}
The divided-difference bound for the restarted scalar recurrence is
$3(n+1)$ times this common root modulus to power $n$.  Orthogonal spectral
decomposition proves \cref{eq:collatz-gated-window-rate}.
\end{proof}

With coordinate observation error bars
$|\widehat y_t-y_t|\leq\eta_t$, first require every denominator lower bound
$\widehat y_{t,i}-\eta_{t,i}$ to be strictly positive.  Certify the probe
ratios by replacing each numerator with $\widehat y_{t+1,i}\mp\eta_{t+1,i}$
and each denominator with $\widehat y_{t,i}\pm\eta_{t,i}$, and intersect all
certified brackets accumulated so far; the raw inexact ratios need not be
monotone.  Accept
momentum only when
\begin{equation*}
 (1+\beta)\widehat y_t-\beta\widehat y_{t-1}
 \geq((1+\beta)\eta_t+\beta\eta_{t-1})\one;
\end{equation*}
otherwise restart.  A boundary admission must abort the old trial, return to
the unchanged checkpoint $x$, set $x_{\rm prev}=x$, certify the same-point
boundary residual, and replay a $\beta=0$ solve on the enlarged face.  Only
after a primal/error certificate places that output in the relative interior
may the protocol clear its history and rebuild the Collatz bracket.  The
probe hitting time and number of admission replays remain explicit work terms;
the theorem bounds only a fully accepted exact window.  If the error also
perturbs the shifted solve, a forced-recurrence/Green-convolution tolerance
schedule is additionally required; the gate alone proves safety, not a
finite-inner accelerated rate.

The center creates no extra factor in the one-stage entrance theorem of
\cref{prop:first-momentum-safe}; that theorem already includes every arm
count.  It does create charged work.  For a center-containing prefix face
with retained length \(r_a\in\{0,\ldots,\ell_a\}\) on arm \(a\), its exact
ambient-degree volume is
\begin{equation}
 \vol(S)=B+\sum_{a:r_a>0}
 \left(2r_a-\mathbf 1_{\{r_a=\ell_a\}}\right).
 \label{eq:spider-prefix-volume}
\end{equation}
Thus the center row alone scans \(B\) neighbors, and a full spider sweep
costs \(2\sum_a\ell_a\).  These are adjacency-list sweep charges only.  Each
warmup or momentum stage additionally applies a resolvent; factorization,
inverse-oracle, state-write, and repeated-solve costs cannot be replaced by
the sweep count and must be charged by any algorithmic theorem.

For the full unit star this cost is explicit.  If $f_c,f_1,\ldots,f_B$ are
normalized coordinates and $z=(3I-S)^{-1}f$, then
\[
 z_c=\frac{3f_c}{8}+\frac{\sum_i f_i}{8\sqrt B},\qquad
 z_i=\frac{f_i}{3}+\frac{f_c}{8\sqrt B}
       +\frac{\sum_j f_j}{24B}.
\]
One application and its explicit output cost $\Theta(B)$.  Therefore the
literal repeated-resolvent policy certified above costs
$\Theta(B\log B)$ on the full star.  The $O(1)$ three-class verifier exploits
symmetry and is not an implementation-cost bound for arbitrary residuals.

\section{Open path and spider targets}
\label{sec:path-spider-targets}

The first live path target is now sharper: characterize the residual produced
by one stable-face proximal solve after an actual endpoint-prefix admission,
then prove or refute that
\eqref{eq:path-lock-sharp-entrance} or the weaker exact trigger remains valid
for the whole segment after a graph-size-independent number of solves.  A
constant must be proved on the realized profile, not on the full nonnegative
cone unless the latter is genuinely true.

The graph-uniform first-stage result survives the spider junction, but
\cref{thm:reachable-star-no-constant} rules out every fixed warmup on a
reachable leaf-seeded star family, and
\cref{thm:reachable-star-logarithmic-lower,thm:star-logarithmic-warmup}
identify its matching logarithmic scale.  The strict-margin corollary supports
finite horizons, while \cref{prop:proper-face-global-momentum-stop} rules out
permanent global-momentum safety on strict proper faces.
\Cref{thm:face-tuned-permanent-safety} repairs the exact tail, and
\cref{prop:star-finite-inner-window} supplies a finite-inner final-face window.
The live route is therefore a charged changing-face implementation of the
face-tuned restart gate, rather than another global warmup constant.
