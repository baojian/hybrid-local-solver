\subsection{Activation tokens: a tight mergeable countdown}

The abstract condition has a concrete realization when transport work is
represented by irreversible common-state events rather than by a smooth
numerical error.

\begin{lemma}[Irreversible activation-token countdown]
\label{lem:activation-token-countdown}
For policy $P_j$, let $T_j$ be a finite token set with positive weights
$w_j(t)$.  Suppose every certified shared state $z$ records a consumed set
$H_j(z)\subseteq T_j$ such that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=*]
 \item every legal block $z\mapsto z^+$ satisfies
 $H_j(z)\subseteq H_j(z^+)$;
 \item every nonterminal $P_j$ block of charge $a$ consumes new tokens
 $N=H_j(z^+)\setminus H_j(z)$ with
 $a\leq\sum_{t\in N}w_j(t)$;
 \item $P_j$ has a legal next block until the common terminal certificate;
 and
 \item
 $\sum_{t\in T_j\setminus H_j(z_0)}w_j(t)\leq\kappa_j C_j$,
 where $C_j$ is its standalone work from $z_0$.
\end{enumerate}
Then
\begin{equation}
 \Phi_j(z):=\sum_{t\in T_j\setminus H_j(z)}w_j(t)
 \label{eq:activation-token-potential}
\end{equation}
is an interference-monotone, $\kappa_j$-tight mergeable countdown for
\Cref{thm:mergeable-countdown}.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Monotonicity of $H_j$ makes $\Phi_j$ nonincreasing under every legal block.
For an own block,
\[
 \Phi_j(z)-\Phi_j(z^+)
 =\sum_{t\in H_j(z^+)\setminus H_j(z)}w_j(t)\geq a.
\]
The last hypothesis is exactly $\kappa_j$-tightness.
\end{proof}

The important restriction is that these are events in the common certified
state.  Tokens for an arm-private simulated trajectory would merely hide a
branch and would not establish interference monotonicity.

\begin{theorem}[A $\kappa=1$ shared-state countdown on endpoint paths]
\label{thm:path-token-countdown}
Consider single-seed RPPR on an endpoint-seeded path
$P_N=(v_0,\ldots,v_N)$, and suppose
\[
 S^\star(\rho)=U_J:=\{v_0,\ldots,v_J\}.
\]
Require the common checkpoint representation to store, for every admitted
prefix $U_k$, the exact forward Schur records described below.  Every legal
support expansion, regardless of which arm requests it, appends the unique
new record; support-preserving blocks leave the records unchanged.  Once the
terminal prefix is certified, the common verifier enters a terminalization
phase: reverse-recovery blocks are legal, while numerical policy updates that
could overwrite recovered output are not.

Then the exact path-Schur arm has an irreversible activation-token countdown
with $\kappa=1$.  Its declared standalone work and initial countdown are
\begin{equation}
 C_{\rm path}=\Phi_{\rm path}(z_0)\leq2C(S^\star(\rho)),
 \label{eq:path-token-work}
\end{equation}
up to deletion of any seed-initialization token already paid at $z_0$.
Consequently
\begin{equation}
 C_{\rm path}\leq\frac4\rho,
 \label{eq:path-token-rppr}
\end{equation}
and a uniform work-fair shared-state portfolio containing this arm terminates
on the promised path class in at most
$2K C(S^\star)+\Delta\leq4K/\rho+\Delta$ declared work.  No reset or
arm-private numerical iterate is required.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Write
\[
 a_i:=Q_{v_i v_i},
 \qquad
 \beta_i:=-Q_{v_{i-1}v_i}>0\quad(i\geq1),
 \qquad
 \lambda_i:=(\ell_\rho)_{v_i}.
\]
The common representation stores the forward elimination records
\begin{align}
 \delta_0&=a_0,& \eta_0&=\lambda_0,\notag\\
 \delta_i&=a_i-\frac{\beta_i^2}{\delta_{i-1}},&
 \eta_i&=\lambda_i+\frac{\beta_i\eta_{i-1}}{\delta_{i-1}}.
 \label{eq:path-common-schur-record}
\end{align}
Positive definiteness gives $\delta_i>0$.  At prefix $U_k$, the unique
boundary residual has the sign of
$\eta_{k+1}=\lambda_{k+1}+\beta_{k+1}\eta_k/\delta_k$.
Thus it is positive for $k<J$ and nonpositive at $k=J$ unless $J=N$.
The next forward block is therefore always legal until the exact terminal
prefix is found.  The terminal solution is then recovered once in reverse:
\begin{equation}
 x_{v_J}=\frac{\eta_J}{\delta_J},
 \qquad
 x_{v_i}=\frac{\eta_i+\beta_{i+1}x_{v_{i+1}}}{\delta_i}
 \quad(i=J-1,\ldots,0).
 \label{eq:path-common-backsolve}
\end{equation}

Let $w_i:=c(v_i)=1+d_{v_i}$.  Give every $v_i\in S^\star$ one forward
token $f_i$ and one reverse token $b_i$, both of weight $w_i$.  The forward
token is consumed when $v_i$ is admitted or initialized and its record is
appended; the reverse token is consumed when
\eqref{eq:path-common-backsolve} recovers that coordinate.  If the current
prefix is $U_k$ and $H$ is the reverse-recovered set, the remaining-token
countdown is
\begin{equation}
 \Phi_{\rm path}(U_k,H)
 =\sum_{i=k+1}^{J}w_i
  +\sum_{v_i\in S^\star\setminus H}w_i,
 \label{eq:path-token-potential}
\end{equation}
with the already initialized seed token handled identically on both sides.
Any compatible block either leaves this quantity unchanged, advances the
prefix, or recovers output.  A path-arm forward or reverse block has declared
charge exactly the token it consumes, so
\Cref{lem:activation-token-countdown} applies with $\kappa=1$ and initial
value at most $2C(S^\star)$.

The recurrence and backsolve use constant arithmetic per path vertex; the
declared charge dominates this work and its adjacency access by an absolute
factor.  Finally,
$C(S^\star)\leq2\vol(S^\star)\leq2/\rho$, proving
\eqref{eq:path-token-rppr}.  Apply
\Cref{thm:mergeable-countdown} with uniform shares for the portfolio bound.
\end{proof}

This theorem is the clean positive answer for serializable fronts: preserve
an activation-once transport representation in the common state, and charge
forward discovery and terminal recovery exactly once.  The next result shows
that the first genuine branch is still serializable if the common state stores
an implicit affine response rather than eagerly materializing all current
coordinate values.

\begin{theorem}[Three-arm common-state countdown]
\label{thm:three-arm-spider-token-countdown}
Let $Y(N_1,N_2,N_3)$ be the unweighted tree with center $o$ and three
internally disjoint arms
\begin{equation*}
 o-v_{a,1}-\cdots-v_{a,N_a},
 \qquad a\in\{1,2,3\},\qquad N_a\geq1.
\end{equation*}
Use the single seed $s=e_o$, fix $\alpha\in(0,1)$ and
$\rho\in(0,1/3)$, and run the exact canonical RPPR boundary gate of
\Cref{thm:absorption-safe-switch}.  A legal certified block may admit any
nonempty subset of the currently positive boundary tips.  The common state
stores the actual three admitted prefixes, the affine transfer records below,
and a terminal output phase; it stores no arm-private support or numerical
trajectory.

In exact real-cell arithmetic, there is an exact response arm such that every
legal certified block order, including orders committed by other policies,
preserves its irreversible activation-token countdown with $\kappa=1$.
Writing $S^\star:=S^\star(\rho)$ and
$C(S^\star)=\sum_{v\in S^\star}(1+d_v)$, its resource coordinates obey
\begin{align}
 \mathcal C_{\rm adj}
 &\leq \vol(S^\star)+6,
 &
 \mathcal C_{\rm resp}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 \notag\\
 \mathcal C_{\rm set/rec}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 &
 \mathcal C_{\rm ctl}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 \label{eq:spider-resource-ledger}\\
 \mathcal C_{\rm state\text{-}write}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 &
 \mathcal M_{\rm state}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 \qquad
 \mathcal C_{\rm out}=\card{S^\star}.
 \notag
\end{align}
Here response work includes every forward record and exact checkpoint update;
settlement/recovery is the one terminal materialization pass, not an
intermediate full-vector solve; control includes all three tip tests after
each committed expansion; state writes and peak resident cells are separate;
and output counts exact listed coordinates.  There is no ambient-graph
preprocessing.  For a fixed cell-operation convention, the arm's declared
standalone work $C_Y$ and initial token mass are equal and satisfy
\begin{equation}
 \Phi_Y(z_0)=C_Y=O(C(S^\star))=O(1/\rho),
 \label{eq:spider-token-work}
\end{equation}
after deleting initialization tokens already paid at $z_0$.  Therefore a
uniform work-fair common-state portfolio containing this arm finishes on this
three-arm family in at most $K C_Y+\Delta$ declared work.

The statement is structural.  It does not cover two branching vertices,
arbitrary trees, cycles, finite precision, or bit complexity, and it does not
assert a conversion from $\rho$ to $\epsppr$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Put
\begin{equation*}
 \lambda_v:=(\ell_\rho)_v,
 \qquad
 \sigma_v:=Q_{vv},
 \qquad
 \beta_{a,i}:=-Q_{v_{a,i-1},v_{a,i}}>0,
 \qquad v_{a,0}:=o.
\end{equation*}
The condition $\rho<1/3$ is exactly
$\lambda_o=\alpha(1-3\rho)/\sqrt3>0$, so the seed initialization required by
\Cref{thm:absorption-safe-switch} is valid.  Every region reached from $o$ is
the union of three arm prefixes.  Since the exact gate terminates at the true
support, write
\begin{equation}
 S^\star
 =\{o\}\cup
  \bigcup_{a=1}^3\{v_{a,1},\ldots,v_{a,J_a}\},
 \label{eq:spider-support-prefixes}
\end{equation}
for some $0\leq J_a\leq N_a$.

We first give the common exact response.  Introduce a harmless positive ghost
coupling
\begin{equation*}
 \widehat\beta_{a,i+1}
 :=\begin{cases}
     \beta_{a,i+1},&i<N_a,\\
     1,&i=N_a,
   \end{cases}
\end{equation*}
and the affine transfer cell
\begin{equation}
 M_{a,i}
 :=
 \begin{pmatrix}
  \sigma_{v_{a,i}}/\widehat\beta_{a,i+1}
    &-\beta_{a,i}/\widehat\beta_{a,i+1}
    &-\lambda_{v_{a,i}}/\widehat\beta_{a,i+1}\\
  1&0&0\\
  0&0&1
 \end{pmatrix}.
 \label{eq:spider-transfer-cell}
\end{equation}
For an admitted prefix of length $k\geq1$, set
\begin{equation}
 P_{a,k}:=M_{a,k}\cdots M_{a,1}
 =
 \begin{pmatrix}
  A_{a,k}&B_{a,k}&C_{a,k}\\
  D_{a,k}&E_{a,k}&F_{a,k}\\
  0&0&1
 \end{pmatrix}.
 \label{eq:spider-transfer-product}
\end{equation}
The arm equations say exactly that
\begin{equation*}
 \begin{pmatrix}x_{v_{a,k+1}}\\x_{v_{a,k}}\\1\end{pmatrix}
 =P_{a,k}
  \begin{pmatrix}x_{v_{a,1}}\\x_o\\1\end{pmatrix},
\end{equation*}
where the current restricted endpoint condition is the ghost value
$x_{v_{a,k+1}}=0$.  The coefficient $A_{a,k}$ is the positive determinant of
the arm-prefix principal matrix divided by
$\prod_{i=1}^k\widehat\beta_{a,i+1}$, so it is nonzero.  Consequently
\begin{align}
 p_{a,k}&:=-B_{a,k}/A_{a,k},
 &q_{a,k}&:=-C_{a,k}/A_{a,k},\notag\\
 r_{a,k}&:=D_{a,k}p_{a,k}+E_{a,k},
 &s_{a,k}&:=D_{a,k}q_{a,k}+F_{a,k}
 \label{eq:spider-affine-arm-response}
\end{align}
give the exact affine readouts
\begin{equation*}
 x_{v_{a,1}}=p_{a,k}x_o+q_{a,k},
 \qquad
 x_{v_{a,k}}=r_{a,k}x_o+s_{a,k}.
\end{equation*}
For $k=0$, use $p_{a,0}=q_{a,0}=0$ and regard the arm endpoint as $o$, so
$r_{a,0}=1$ and $s_{a,0}=0$.

At the common prefix vector $k=(k_1,k_2,k_3)$, store only the two root
aggregates
\begin{align}
 \Delta(k)
 &:=Q_{oo}-\sum_{a:k_a>0}\beta_{a,1}p_{a,k_a},\notag\\
 \eta(k)
 &:=\lambda_o+\sum_{a:k_a>0}\beta_{a,1}q_{a,k_a}.
 \label{eq:spider-root-aggregates}
\end{align}
Here $\Delta(k)>0$ is the root Schur complement, and the canonical root value
is exactly
\begin{equation}
 x[U(k)]_o=\eta(k)/\Delta(k).
 \label{eq:spider-root-readout}
\end{equation}
If $k_a<N_a$, the exact KKT violation at the next tip is
\begin{equation}
 g_a(k)
 :=(\ell_\rho-Qx[U(k)])_{v_{a,k_a+1}}
 =\lambda_{v_{a,k_a+1}}
  +\beta_{a,k_a+1}
    \bigl(r_{a,k_a}x[U(k)]_o+s_{a,k_a}\bigr).
 \label{eq:spider-tip-readout}
\end{equation}
Thus all current boundary certificates are obtained by three constant-size
readouts.  No stored value on an untouched arm is rekeyed when the root value
changes.

Appending $v_{a,k_a+1}$ left-multiplies one product,
$P_{a,k_a+1}=M_{a,k_a+1}P_{a,k_a}$, replaces one pair $(p,q)$ in
\cref{eq:spider-root-aggregates}, and reevaluates
\cref{eq:spider-root-readout,eq:spider-tip-readout}.  This is constant exact
arithmetic and a constant number of cell writes.  A legal batch contains at
most one tip from each arm.  It may be processed in any order: every tip was
positive before the batch, and violation persistence
\eqref{eq:rppr-violation-persistence} keeps the unprocessed tips positive.
The final products and the two root sums are independent of that
sequentialization.  Hence the representation follows the actual committed
common prefixes under every legal certified batch order.

Once all three readouts are nonpositive or their arms are exhausted, the
exact KKT gate has certified the terminal support.  The verifier then forbids
support-changing numerical blocks and enters an irreversible output phase.
Compute the root once; on each nonempty arm compute
$x_{v_{a,1}}=p_{a,J_a}x_o+q_{a,J_a}$ and apply the stored cells
\cref{eq:spider-transfer-cell} outward.  This recovers and writes each exact
coordinate once.  The three-tip check that discovers terminality is always
part of the preceding initialization or append block; that block also writes
the terminal-phase bit.  There is therefore no paid, tokenless terminal-check
block.

We now audit resources.  Every admitted vertex is scanned once.  In addition,
at most one terminal exterior tip per arm is exposed, and each has degree at
most two.  This proves the adjacency bound in
\cref{eq:spider-resource-ledger}.  Every append performs one constant-size
product update, one root-sum update, and at most three tip tests.  Every local
transfer cell is written once and retained until recovery, and recovery and
output touch each support vertex once.  These facts prove all remaining
coordinates in \cref{eq:spider-resource-ledger}.  In particular, the ledger
does not hide an intermediate materialized canonical vector.  This is an
exact-cell count only; coefficient bit lengths and numerical stability are
outside the claim.

For completeness, define the tokens explicitly.  Let
\begin{equation*}
 \mathcal X_Y
 :=S^\star\cup\bigl(\partial S^\star\cap
       \{v_{a,i}:a\in\{1,2,3\},\ i\geq1\}\bigr).
\end{equation*}
The token set contains an exposure token $e_v$ for every $v\in\mathcal X_Y$,
a forward response token $f_v$ for every $v\in S^\star$, and a terminal
recovery/output token $b_v$ for every $v\in S^\star$.  Fix the implementation's
cell-operation templates: an exposure executes its full adjacency scan and
boundary-record write, a forward append executes the fixed $3$-by-$3$
product/root template and tests all three tip slots (including an exhaustion
flag), and a recovery executes its fixed state/output template.  Give
$e_v,f_v,b_v$ exactly the charges of those respective templates.  Thus these
are the arm's actual declared operations, not padded analytical credits.  Call
the resulting token set $T_Y$; its remaining total from $z_0$ is exactly the
standalone declared charge $C_Y$.

The consumed set $H_Y(z)$ is defined by events in the common state: first
exposure of a label, committed admission and response append, and irreversible
terminal recovery/output.  A support-preserving block leaves it unchanged;
every certified expansion or terminal block only adds events.  An own forward
block chooses a positive tip from \cref{eq:spider-tip-readout}, consumes its
new $f_v$ and any newly exposed $e_v$ tokens, and charges no more than their
weight.  An own terminal block consumes a new $b_v$ and likewise charges no
more than its weight.  The arm always has one of these legal blocks until the
full exact output certificate holds.  Tokens consumed by another policy are
consumed in the same committed prefixes and can only help; no simulated
private order appears in $H_Y$.

Therefore
\begin{equation*}
 \Phi_Y(z):=\sum_{t\in T_Y\setminus H_Y(z)}w(t)
\end{equation*}
satisfies \Cref{lem:activation-token-countdown}.  By the definition of the
declared cell charges, its initial mass equals the standalone declared work,
so $\kappa=1$.  Finally $\card{\mathcal X_Y}\leq\card{S^\star}+3$,
$C(S^\star)\leq2\vol(S^\star)\leq2/\rho$, and $\rho<1/3$ absorbs the constant
exterior-tip term.  This proves \cref{eq:spider-token-work}; the portfolio
bound is \Cref{thm:mergeable-countdown}.
\end{proof}

The minimal degree-three branch therefore does not furnish a legal-order
counterexample.  Its three response directions meet only through one scalar
root value, so one fixed-size affine product per arm is enough.  The next
theorem tests the first larger interface rather than extrapolating this scalar
argument: two adjacent branching vertices require a rank-two core.

\begin{theorem}[Double-Y rank-two common-state countdown]
\label{thm:double-y-two-core-token-countdown}
Let $\mathsf{DY}(N_{o,1},N_{o,2};N_{h,1},N_{h,2})$ be the unweighted tree
with adjacent degree-three vertices $o$ and $h$ and four pendant arms
\begin{equation*}
 u-v_{u,a,1}-\cdots-v_{u,a,N_{u,a}},
 \qquad u\in\{o,h\},\quad a\in\{1,2\},\quad N_{u,a}\geq1.
\end{equation*}
Use the single seed $s=e_o$, fix $\alpha\in(0,1)$ and
$\rho\in(0,1/3)$, and run the exact canonical RPPR gate of
\Cref{thm:absorption-safe-switch}.  Require every committed support expansion,
including one requested by another policy, to maintain the common response
state below and to include that update in its declared charge.  Support-
preserving blocks may not overwrite these records, and the terminal phase may
not overwrite recovered coordinates.

In exact real-cell arithmetic, one affine transfer product per actual pendant
prefix and a core Schur interface of dimension at most two give an exact
response arm for every legal certified boundary-batch order.  Its irreversible
activation-token countdown remains $\kappa=1$.  Before $h$ is admitted the
interface dimension is one; after $h$ is admitted its core matrix is positive
definite of rank two.  In particular, writing
\begin{equation}
 \zeta:=\frac{1-\alpha}{1+\alpha},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\rm link}(\alpha):=\frac{\zeta}{3(3+\zeta)},
 \label{eq:double-y-link-threshold}
\end{equation}
every legal order enters the rank-two phase whenever
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm link}(\alpha)$.

For arbitrary $0<\rho<1/3$, with $S^\star:=S^\star(\rho)$ and
$C(S^\star)=\sum_{v\in S^\star}(1+d_v)$, the implementation has the separately
charged resource coordinates
\begin{align}
 \mathcal C_{\rm adj}
 &\leq \vol(S^\star)+8,
 &
 \mathcal C_{\rm gate\text{-}ctl}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 \notag\\
 \mathcal C_{\rm resp\text{-}build}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 &
 \mathcal C_{\rm resp\text{-}upd}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 \notag\\
 \mathcal C_{\rm resp\text{-}qry}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 &
 \mathcal C_{\rm state\text{-}write}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 \label{eq:double-y-resource-ledger}\\
 \mathcal M_{\rm persistent}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 &
 \mathcal M_{\rm transient}
 &=O(1),
 \notag\\
 \mathcal C_{\rm rec}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 &
 \mathcal C_{\rm val}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 \qquad
 \mathcal C_{\rm out}=\card{S^\star}.
 \notag
\end{align}
There is no ambient preprocessing.  Adjacency includes closure discovery from
the seed, gate/control includes every fixed-slot membership and positivity
test, response construction/update/query are distinct coordinates, validation
reads the stored rows after recovery, and output counts exact listed
coordinates.  Reading and initializing the single seed costs $O(1)$ and is
included in the paid initial state.  If $C_{\rm DY}$ is the sum of these declared cell-operation
templates, then, after removing initialization events already paid at the
initial common state $z_0$,
\begin{equation}
 \Phi_{\rm DY}(z_0)=C_{\rm DY}
 =O(C(S^\star))=O(1/\rho).
 \label{eq:double-y-token-work}
\end{equation}
Thus a uniform work-fair portfolio containing this arm terminates on the
double-Y family within $K C_{\rm DY}+\Delta$ declared work.

The theorem is an exact RPPR result on this promised tree family.  It does not
cover a longer backbone of branching vertices, arbitrary trees, cycles,
finite precision, bit complexity, or an RPPR-to-PPR accuracy conversion.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Put $\lambda_v:=(\ell_\rho)_v$ and $\beta_0:=-Q_{oh}>0$.  Since
$d_o=d_h=3$,
\begin{equation*}
 \lambda_o=\frac{\alpha(1-3\rho)}{\sqrt3}>0,
 \qquad
 \lambda_h=-\alpha\rho\sqrt3,
 \qquad
 \beta_0=\frac{1-\alpha}{6}.
\end{equation*}
Thus the seed initialization satisfies
\Cref{thm:absorption-safe-switch}.  Every reached region is connected.  Before
$h$ is admitted it consists of $o$ and two prefixes on the $o$-arms; afterward
it consists of $o,h$ and four pendant prefixes.  The true support has the same
form because the exact gate terminates at $S^\star$.

For each admitted pendant prefix, use the ghost-coupled transfer cells and
products of
\cref{eq:spider-transfer-cell,eq:spider-transfer-product}, with the relevant
branch vertex $u\in\{o,h\}$ in place of the spider root.  Denote the resulting
affine readouts by
\begin{equation*}
 x_{v_{u,a,1}}=p_{u,a,k}x_u+q_{u,a,k},
 \qquad
 x_{v_{u,a,k}}=r_{u,a,k}x_u+s_{u,a,k},
\end{equation*}
and use $p=q=0$, $r=1$, $s=0$ at an empty prefix.  Positive definiteness of
every arm-prefix principal matrix makes each division in these readouts valid.
For the two branch vertices store
\begin{align}
 \Delta_u(k)
 &:=Q_{uu}-\sum_{a:k_{u,a}>0}
      \beta_{u,a,1}p_{u,a,k_{u,a}},
 &
 \eta_u(k)
 &:=\lambda_u+\sum_{a:k_{u,a}>0}
      \beta_{u,a,1}q_{u,a,k_{u,a}},
 \label{eq:double-y-branch-aggregates}
\end{align}
where $\beta_{u,a,i}:=-Q_{v_{u,a,i-1},v_{u,a,i}}$ and
$v_{u,a,0}:=u$.

While $h$ is inactive, the exact core and central boundary readouts are
\begin{equation}
 x_o=\frac{\eta_o}{\Delta_o},
 \qquad
 g_h:=\lambda_h+\beta_0x_o.
 \label{eq:double-y-scalar-core}
\end{equation}
Once $h$ is admitted, eliminating the four current pendant prefixes leaves
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal K(k):=
 \begin{pmatrix}
  \Delta_o(k)&-\beta_0\\
  -\beta_0&\Delta_h(k)
 \end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 \mathcal K(k)\binom{x_o}{x_h}=\binom{\eta_o(k)}{\eta_h(k)}.
 \label{eq:double-y-rank-two-core}
\end{equation}
This is a principal Schur complement and hence is positive definite and rank
two.  With
$\Theta(k):=\Delta_o(k)\Delta_h(k)-\beta_0^2>0$, its exact readout is
\begin{equation}
 x_o=\frac{\Delta_h\eta_o+\beta_0\eta_h}{\Theta},
 \qquad
 x_h=\frac{\beta_0\eta_o+\Delta_o\eta_h}{\Theta}.
 \label{eq:double-y-core-readout}
\end{equation}
For every active branch vertex $u$, the next pendant-tip violation is
\begin{equation}
 g_{u,a}(k)
 =\lambda_{v_{u,a,k_{u,a}+1}}
  +\beta_{u,a,k_{u,a}+1}
   \bigl(r_{u,a,k_{u,a}}x_u+s_{u,a,k_{u,a}}\bigr).
 \label{eq:double-y-tip-readout}
\end{equation}
There are three live slots before $h$ is admitted---two $o$-tips and $h$---and
at most four afterward.  The implementation computes these constant-size
readouts when needed; it stores no materialized boundary keys.

The displayed threshold genuinely reaches both branch vertices.  At the
initial region $\{o\}$, $Q_{oo}=(1+\alpha)/2$.  Equation
\eqref{eq:double-y-scalar-core} and $\beta_0=(1-\alpha)/6$ give
\begin{equation*}
 g_h(\{o\})
 =\frac{\alpha}{\sqrt3}
   \left[-3\rho+\frac{\zeta}{3}(1-3\rho)\right]>0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \rho<\frac{\zeta}{3(3+\zeta)}.
\end{equation*}
Hence $h\in S^\star$ in this range.  If another positive tip is selected
first, violation persistence
\eqref{eq:rppr-violation-persistence} keeps $h$ positive; finiteness of the
tree forces its eventual admission under every legal order.  For larger
$\rho<1/3$, the same representation remains valid whether or not $h$ belongs
to $S^\star$; if it does not, the core stays scalar.

Appending a pendant tip left-multiplies only its own transfer product, changes
one pair in \eqref{eq:double-y-branch-aggregates}, and reevaluates the scalar or
rank-two core.  Admitting $h$ changes the active-core bit, initializes
$(\Delta_h,\eta_h)=(Q_{hh},\lambda_h)$, and exposes its two pendant-tip slots.
Every operation uses constant exact arithmetic and constant state writes.  A
legal batch contains at most one current tip from each active arm and may also
contain $h$ before the rank-two phase.  Sequentialize only those actually
committed vertices.  Every member was positive before the batch and remains
positive by \eqref{eq:rppr-violation-persistence}; the final prefix products,
branch sums, and core bit depend only on the committed support.  This is not a
private replay of a different order.

At most four exterior boundary rows are ever live at termination.  If $h$ is
active they are four pendant tips, each of degree at most two; if $h$ is
inactive they are at most two such tips and the degree-three vertex $h$.
Scanning each admitted or boundary row once therefore costs at most
$\vol(S^\star)+8$.  Every admission constructs one local transfer cell,
updates one product and one constant-size core, queries at most four slots, and
writes only permanent prefix/core records.  This proves all forward coordinates
of \eqref{eq:double-y-resource-ledger} and gives linear persistent state and
constant transient workspace.

The fixed-slot check in the initialization or last append that finds no
positive violation also writes the irreversible terminal-phase bit.  Recover
the one- or two-vertex core once and traverse each stored pendant product
outward once.  Then make one charged pass over the stored bounded-degree rows
to validate active positivity and equations and the terminal boundary
inequalities, and make one separate exact output pass.  Nonboundary inactive
vertices have no active neighbor and satisfy their KKT interval automatically.
These operations prove the recovery, validation, and output coordinates in
\eqref{eq:double-y-resource-ledger}; no row is rediscovered and no intermediate
canonical vector is materialized during the forward phase.

It remains to verify the countdown without analytical padding.  Let
\begin{equation*}
 \mathcal X_{\rm DY}:=S^\star\cup\partial S^\star.
\end{equation*}
Give every $v\in\mathcal X_{\rm DY}$ one exposure token with weight equal to
its full adjacency/row-record template.  Give every $v\in S^\star$ separate
forward-response, recovery, validation, and output tokens whose weights are
exactly the corresponding fixed cell-operation templates, including gate,
control, state-write, and core-query work where it occurs.  The common consumed
set records first row exposure, committed admission and response update,
irreversible recovery, completed validation, and exact output.  Events already
paid during initialization are removed from both the token set and
$C_{\rm DY}$.

Every compatible block only adds common events.  An own forward or terminal
block charges no more than the exact weights of the new tokens it consumes and
one is always legal until the full validated output certificate holds.  An
expansion performed by another policy invokes the same mandatory common
updater and consumes the same permanent events, so it can only decrease the
remaining mass; there is no catch-up replay.  Thus
\Cref{lem:activation-token-countdown} applies.  By construction the initial
mass is exactly the standalone declared work, proving $\kappa=1$.  Finally
$\card{\mathcal X_{\rm DY}}\leq\card{S^\star}+4$, all degrees are at most three,
and
$C(S^\star)\leq2\vol(S^\star)\leq2/\rho$.  This proves
\eqref{eq:double-y-token-work} and the portfolio bound.
\end{proof}

The adjacent second branch therefore increases the exact response interface
from one scalar to one fixed rank-two Schur core, but does not break the
activation-once countdown.  The next unresolved tree has a growing branch-core
backbone rather than this constant two-vertex interface.

\begin{proposition}[A growing branch core forces global rekeys in an eager tip array]
\label{prop:branch-caterpillar-eager-rekey-obstruction}
Fix $\alpha\in(0,1)$ and an integer $m\geq2$.  Let
$\mathsf{BC}_m$ be the unweighted branch caterpillar with branch path
$b_1-\cdots-b_m$, single seed $s=e_{b_1}$, and the following pendant arms:
$b_1-a_1-\cdots-a_m$ and $b_1-r_1$, one leaf $r_i$ at every
$b_i$ for $2\leq i<m$, and two leaves $r_m,r_{m+1}$ at $b_m$.
Thus every $b_i$ has degree three, both endpoint branches have two nonempty
pendant arms, and
\begin{equation}
 |V(\mathsf{BC}_m)|=3m+1,
 \qquad C(V(\mathsf{BC}_m))=9m+1.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-size}
\end{equation}
For each fixed $m$ there is an explicit number
$\rho_{\rm cat}(m,\alpha)>0$, defined in
\eqref{eq:branch-caterpillar-reach-threshold} below.  For every
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm cat}(m,\alpha)$, the exact canonical RPPR KKT gate with the
positive-subset policy permitted by \Cref{thm:absorption-safe-switch} may
follow the legal singleton order
\begin{equation}
 b_2,b_3,\ldots,b_m;\qquad
 a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_m;\qquad
 r_1,r_2,\ldots,r_{m+1}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-legal-order}
\end{equation}
In particular, the response core genuinely reaches dimension $m$ and the
true support is the whole tree.  This is not the canonical all-violations
batch rule: that rule may co-admit the deliberately deferred positive leaves.
No positive lower bound on $\rho_{\rm cat}(m,\alpha)$ uniform in $m$ is
claimed.  The threshold is an analysis-side reach condition, not a table
constructed or queried by the online execution.

Consider the named representation \emph{EagerTipKey}: at every canonical
checkpoint $U$ it retains a separately addressed exact-real cell
\begin{equation*}
 K_v=(\ell_\rho-Qx[U])_v
 \qquad(v\in\partial U),
\end{equation*}
and, before the next gate reply, it overwrites every such cell whose exact
value changed.  Shared lazy tags, affine formulas, sign-only flags, and
on-demand recomputation are not EagerTipKey states.  Along
\eqref{eq:branch-caterpillar-legal-order}, each of the $m$ long-arm admissions
changes all $m+1$ deferred leaf keys.  Hence EagerTipKey incurs at least
$m(m+1)$ old-key writes and cannot have an $O(C(S^\star))$ activation-once
countdown.

A literal sequential implementation that scans each row once, solves the
current tridiagonal branch response by a linear exact sweep at each
checkpoint, refreshes the eager array internally, emits one constant-size
singleton certificate per expansion and one terminal certificate, then
materializes, validates, and emits the exact solution once has the exhaustive
resource vector
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_m^{\rm eager})
 :=\bigl(&C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},
 C_{\rm rec},C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit}
 \bigr)\\
 =\bigl(&\Theta(m),\Theta(m),3m+1,0,\Theta(m^2),0,
 \Theta(m^2),\Theta(m),O(m),\Theta(m),\Theta(m)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-eager-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The $\Omega(m^2)$ eager-key work in $C_{\rm ctl}$ persists even if exact core
coordinates are supplied for free; the displayed $C_{\rm resp}$ is the
separate cost of the chosen repeated linear tridiagonal sweeps.  If the
external interface instead requires the complete positive-tip label list at
every checkpoint, then its repeated list itself adds
$C_{\rm emit}=\Theta(m^2)$.
Here the cached boundary-key array is a control data structure: indexing,
testing, and persistent key-cell overwrites belong to $C_{\rm ctl}$, whereas
the response arithmetic that computes the exact values belongs to
$C_{\rm resp}$.

This is only a representation-specific exact-cell obstruction.  It is not a
lower bound for implicit or lazy response states, kinetic/group reporters,
sign-only persistence records, arbitrary RPPR algorithms, or finite-precision
implementations.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Put $B_i:=\{b_1,\ldots,b_i\}$,
$A_t:=\{a_1,\ldots,a_t\}$, and $A_0:=\varnothing$.  For a connected
$U\ni b_1$ and a nonseed boundary vertex $v$, define
\begin{align}
 \Gamma_{U,v}
 &:=-Q_{vU}Q_{UU}^{-1}b_U,
 &
 H_{U,v}
 &:=\sqrt{d_v}-Q_{vU}Q_{UU}^{-1}D_U^{1/2}\one.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-affine-demand}
\end{align}
The principal matrix $Q_{UU}$ is an irreducible Stieltjes matrix.  Its inverse
is strictly positive, $b_U$ is positive at $b_1$, and $v$ has an edge into
$U$.  Therefore $\Gamma_{U,v}>0$ and $H_{U,v}>0$.  Direct substitution of
$\ell_\rho=b-\alpha\rho D^{1/2}\one$ gives the exact affine demand
\begin{equation}
 (\ell_\rho-Qx[U])_v
 =\Gamma_{U,v}-\alpha\rho H_{U,v}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-demand-line}
\end{equation}

Let
\begin{equation*}
 \mathcal P_m:=
 \{(B_i,b_{i+1}):1\leq i<m\}
 \cup\{(B_m\cup A_t,a_{t+1}):0\leq t<m\}
 \cup\{(B_m,r_i):1\leq i\leq m+1\}
\end{equation*}
and set
\begin{equation}
 \rho_{\rm cat}(m,\alpha)
 :=\min\left\{
  \frac13,
  \min_{(U,v)\in\mathcal P_m}
  \frac{\Gamma_{U,v}}{\alpha H_{U,v}}
 \right\}>0.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-reach-threshold}
\end{equation}
For $0<\rho<\rho_{\rm cat}(m,\alpha)$,
\eqref{eq:branch-caterpillar-demand-line} is strictly positive for every pair
in $\mathcal P_m$, while $\rho<1/3$ makes the degree-three seed load positive.
Consequently the backbone and then the long arm in
\eqref{eq:branch-caterpillar-legal-order} are legal singleton admissions.
All $r_i$ are already positive at $B_m$ and remain positive under every later
admission by violation persistence
\eqref{eq:rppr-violation-persistence}.  They may therefore be admitted in the
displayed singleton order.  The absorption theorem puts every admitted vertex
in $S^\star$; since the order admits every graph vertex,
$S^\star=V(\mathsf{BC}_m)$.  Eliminating the current pendant prefixes leaves
an SPD tridiagonal system on $b_1,\ldots,b_m$, so the active branch-core
dimension is exactly $m$ after the first phase.  The vertex and charge counts
in \eqref{eq:branch-caterpillar-size} follow from
$|E|=3m$ and $C(V)=|V|+2|E|$.

Fix $1\leq t\leq m$, let $U=B_m\cup A_{t-1}$, and append $v=a_t$.
Block elimination gives
\begin{equation}
 x[U\cup\{v\}]_U-x[U]_U
 =-Q_{UU}^{-1}Q_{Uv}\,x[U\cup\{v\}]_v.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-dense-shift}
\end{equation}
The new coordinate is strictly positive.  The set $U$ is connected, so
$Q_{UU}^{-1}$ is strictly positive; moreover $-Q_{Uv}$ has one positive
entry at the parent of $v$.  Hence the vector in
\eqref{eq:branch-caterpillar-dense-shift} is strictly positive in every old
coordinate.  In particular, every branch value $x_{b_i}$ increases strictly.
For the deferred leaf attached to $b_i$,
\begin{equation*}
 K_{r_i}=(\ell_\rho)_{r_i}-Q_{r_i b_i}x_{b_i}
          =(\ell_\rho)_{r_i}+(-Q_{r_i b_i})x_{b_i},
\end{equation*}
with the same formula for $r_{m+1}$ at $b_m$.  Thus all $m+1$ leaf keys
change strictly after each of the $m$ long-arm admissions.  A literal eager
array has no shared indirection by definition, so it must perform at least
$m(m+1)$ distinct persistent-cell overwrites.

For the displayed implementation, first-exposure bounded-degree scans and
their sequential batches cost $\Theta(m)$.  There are $3m$ singleton
expansions after the initialized seed and one terminal interaction.  Eager
key testing, indexing, and overwriting cost $\Theta(m^2)$; the lower bound
above is matched by scanning the live array.  Re-solving a tridiagonal core of
length at most $m$ at each checkpoint costs $\Theta(m^2)$ response arithmetic,
separately from those writes.  Stored rows, core records, and keys use
$\Theta(m)$ persistent cells, and a linear sweep uses $O(m)$ scratch.  There
is no preprocessing or recurrence direction.  One terminal recovery,
stored-row validation, final vector materialization, all singleton replies,
and the exact $3m+1$-cell output are linear.  These charges prove
\eqref{eq:branch-caterpillar-eager-eleven-vector}.

Finally, the ordinary bounded-degree activation and exposure tokens consumed
by the first long-arm append have $O(1)$ weight, whereas that block already
forces $m+1$ old-key writes.  Pairwise append--key tokens can pay the block,
but the $m(m+1)$ such tokens have quadratic initial mass.  Thus the first
long-arm append is an explicit failure point for the linear activation-token
template, and any own-service countdown paying the entire eager trace has
quadratic initial mass by telescoping.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The implicit transfer route remains open]
\label{rem:branch-caterpillar-implicit-transfer-comparator}
Eliminating each actual pendant prefix changes only the diagonal and load at
its attachment branch.  The remaining branch system is tridiagonal.  A
current core of length $t$ has equations
\begin{equation*}
 \Delta_i x_i-\beta_{i-1}x_{i-1}-\beta_i x_{i+1}=\eta_i,
 \qquad x_0=x_{t+1}=0,
\end{equation*}
where $\beta_0$ and the last $\beta_t$ may be any positive ghost couplings.
Thus the
constant-size cells
\begin{equation*}
 T_i=
 \begin{pmatrix}
  \Delta_i/\beta_i&-\beta_{i-1}/\beta_i&-\eta_i/\beta_i\\
  1&0&0\\0&0&1
 \end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 \begin{pmatrix}x_{i+1}\\x_i\\1\end{pmatrix}
 =T_i\begin{pmatrix}x_i\\x_{i-1}\\1\end{pmatrix},
\end{equation*}
encode the exact response.  Positive definiteness makes the endpoint
coefficient used to solve $x_1$ from $x_{t+1}=0$ nonzero.  A balanced product
tree over the $T_i$ supports one attachment-cell update in
$O(\log(2+m))$ exact operations.  A branch append replaces the old ghost cell
and appends one new ghost cell, again in $O(\log(2+m))$ work.  The total
product determines $x_1$, and a prefix-product query then determines any named
$x_i$ and its attached tip demand in $O(\log(2+m))$ work.  The tree retains
$O(m)$ cells.

This represents the dense shift
\eqref{eq:branch-caterpillar-dense-shift} without rewriting every coordinate
or key.  With $q$ internally requested exact tip values, its response work is
$O((m+q)\log(2+m))$ plus $O(m)$ terminal recovery; every requested value must
still be charged, and an externally emitted scalar reply adds one output
cell.  Querying every live tip at every checkpoint of the witness has
$q=\Theta(m^2)$, and emitting every complete positive-tip list has quadratic
output by itself.

The point-query comparator alone is not a dynamic all-positive-tip reporter.
In particular, the proposition does not rule out lazy affine keys,
persistence flags for already positive tips, or a kinetic/group minimum
structure.  It only closes the literal eager exact-key route and keeps the
append-only tridiagonal response distinct from repeated core solves.  The
next two theorems give charged delta reporters for one explicit
backbone-first positive-subset policy and for the actual canonical
all-violations trace on a stricter fixed-family parameter range.  Arbitrary
pre-backbone positive-subset interleavings and the remaining canonical range
stay open.  Transfer-product conditioning and finite-precision stability are
not claimed.
\end{remark}
