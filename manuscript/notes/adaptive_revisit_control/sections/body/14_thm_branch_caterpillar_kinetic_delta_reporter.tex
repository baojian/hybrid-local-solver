\begin{theorem}[A kinetic delta reporter for the backbone-first caterpillar]
\label{thm:branch-caterpillar-kinetic-delta-reporter}
Fix $\alpha\in(0,1)$ and $m\geq2$, use the branch seed $s=e_{b_1}$ on
$\mathsf{BC}_m$, and assume
\begin{equation*}
 0<\rho<\rho_{\rm cat}(m,\alpha)\leq\frac13.
\end{equation*}
Consider the following exact KKT positive-subset policy class
$\mathfrak P_m^{\rm bf}$.  Starting from $B_1=\{b_1\}$, its first $m-1$
committed batches are the singletons
$\{b_2\},\ldots,\{b_m\}$.  Once $B_m$ has been reached, every committed batch
may be an arbitrary nonempty subset of the currently known strict positive
boundary tips.  In particular, the side leaves may be interleaved arbitrarily
with the sequentially exposed long-arm frontier.  This is not the canonical
all-violations policy: during the backbone phase it deliberately defers any
positive off-backbone tip.

There is a named exact-real reporter, \emph{CaterpillarKineticDelta}, with the
following interface at every exact restricted checkpoint $U$ of every order in
$\mathfrak P_m^{\rm bf}$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=*]
 \item it retains the exact set
 \begin{equation*}
  \mathcal K(U):=
  \{v\in\partial U:(\ell_\rho-Qx[U])_v>0\};
 \end{equation*}
 \item it returns only the delta labels that became strict positive since the
 preceding checkpoint, and supports an exact membership query for every live
 tip; and
 \item it accepts a proposed batch only after checking that every proposed
 label is a live member of $\mathcal K(U)$, then returns one charged
 certificate reply for that committed batch.
\end{enumerate}
The reporter never re-emits a still-live positive label merely because its
exact demand changed.  Violation persistence is the proof carried by its
positive flag.  At initialization its delta is $\mathcal K(B_1)$.  The
displayed interface tests membership once for every label in the next
proposed committed batch.  If a caller requests $q_{\rm spec}$ additional or
repeated speculative membership tests, they add $q_{\rm spec}$ to
$C_{\rm ctl}$ and add the corresponding separately requested interaction and
reply cells to $R_{\rm int}$ and $C_{\rm emit}$; none is free in the vector
below.

It uses the balanced tridiagonal transfer state from
\Cref{rem:branch-caterpillar-implicit-transfer-comparator}, one exact kinetic
minimum heap, and $O(m)$ retained cells.  If $J$ is the number of committed
batches after seed initialization, then
\begin{equation*}
 2m-1\leq J\leq3m.
\end{equation*}
With one external delta/certificate interaction per committed batch and one
terminal return, its exhaustive resource vector is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_m^{\rm kin})
 :=\bigl(&C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},
 C_{\rm rec},C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit}
 \bigr)\\
 =\bigl(&\Theta(m),\Theta(m),J+1,0,
 O(m\log(2+m)),0,O(m\log(2+m)),\\
 &\Theta(m),O(m),\Theta(m),\Theta(m)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-kinetic-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Consequently its additive exact-cell work is
\begin{equation}
 O(m\log(2+m))
 =O\!\left(C(S^\star)\log(2+C(S^\star))\right),
 \qquad C(S^\star)=9m+1.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-kinetic-total}
\end{equation}

This is an output-delta theorem.  If the external task instead requests the
complete current positive-tip list at every checkpoint, then the prescribed
witness can require $\Theta(m^2)$ emitted labels, independently of the
reporter's internal work.  The theorem does not cover the canonical
all-violations policy, an arbitrary interleaving before $B_m$, another seed,
an $m$-uniform positive range of $\rho$, finite precision, bit complexity,
or transfer-product conditioning.  It supplies neither a $\kappa=1$ linear
activation countdown nor a graph-uniform accelerated arm.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Put $a=(1+\alpha)/2$, $h=(1-\alpha)/2$, and
$\beta=h/3$.  During the backbone phase the active set is exactly $B_i$.
Every branch vertex has ambient degree three, so $Q_{B_iB_i}$ is the leading
$i$-by-$i$ tridiagonal matrix with diagonal $a$ and off-diagonal $-\beta$.
Define its leading continuants by
\begin{equation*}
 \mathcal D_0=1,\qquad \mathcal D_1=a,\qquad
 \mathcal D_i=a\mathcal D_{i-1}-\beta^2\mathcal D_{i-2}.
\end{equation*}
Every $\mathcal D_i$ is positive because $Q_{B_iB_i}$ is SPD.  The last
Green column has the exact separable form
\begin{equation}
 (Q_{B_iB_i}^{-1})_{b_jb_i}=u_j\upsilon_i,qquad
 u_j:=\frac{\mathcal D_{j-1}}{\beta^{j-1}},qquad
 \upsilon_i:=\frac{\beta^{i-1}}{\mathcal D_i}
 \quad(1\leq j\leq i).
 \label{eq:branch-prefix-green-factorization}
\end{equation}
This follows directly by expanding the corresponding tridiagonal cofactor.

Write $x^{(i)}:=x[B_i]$.  Appending the strict positive backbone tip
$b_{i+1}$ and using block elimination gives
\begin{equation}
 x^{(i+1)}_{B_i}-x^{(i)}_{B_i}
 =\beta x^{(i+1)}_{b_{i+1}}Q_{B_iB_i}^{-1}e_{b_i}
 =\Delta\theta_i\,(u_1,\ldots,u_i)^\top,
 \qquad
 \Delta\theta_i:=\beta\upsilon_i x^{(i+1)}_{b_{i+1}}>0.
 \label{eq:branch-prefix-one-parameter-update}
\end{equation}
Let $\theta_1=0$ and
$\theta_{i+1}=\theta_i+\Delta\theta_i$.  For every $j\leq i$,
\begin{equation}
 x^{(i)}_{b_j}=\xi_j+u_j\theta_i,
 \qquad
 \xi_j:=x^{(j)}_{b_j}-u_j\theta_j.
 \label{eq:branch-prefix-affine-coordinate}
\end{equation}

Every boundary tip $v$ exposed during this phase has one active parent
$b_j$.  Put $\gamma_v:=-Q_{vb_j}>0$ and store the exact crossing key
\begin{equation}
 \theta_v^\times
 :=\frac{-(\ell_\rho)_v/\gamma_v-\xi_j}{u_j}.
 \label{eq:branch-tip-crossing-key}
\end{equation}
For every later backbone prefix at which $v$ is still outside,
\begin{equation}
 (\ell_\rho-Qx[B_i])_v
 =\gamma_vu_j(\theta_i-\theta_v^\times).
 \label{eq:branch-tip-kinetic-demand}
\end{equation}
Thus $v$ is a strict violation exactly when
$\theta_v^\times<\theta_i$.  CaterpillarKineticDelta inserts the pair
$(\theta_v^\times,v)$ when $v$ is first exposed.  At $B_1$ it performs the
same strict minimum drain at $\theta_1$, producing the declared initialization
delta.  After each backbone append it updates $\theta_i$ and repeatedly
removes the minimum key while
that strict inequality holds, inserting each removed label into the
persistent positive set.  Equality is retained in the heap because the gate
requires a strict positive demand.  Hence the heap reports every and only
new strict positive tip during the entire backbone phase.  The certified
backbone tip is among those labels and is removed from the positive set when
its singleton is committed.

At $B_m$, the definition of $\rho_{\rm cat}(m,\alpha)$ includes the pairs
$(B_m,a_1)$ and $(B_m,r_i)$ for every $1\leq i\leq m+1$.  Therefore every
remaining boundary tip is already in the persistent positive set.  After
this checkpoint a committed side leaf has no child, while committing $a_t$
exposes only $a_{t+1}$.  Its demand at the base face
$B_m\cup A_t$ is strict positive by the same definition of
$\rho_{\rm cat}$.  Any side leaves already committed were themselves strict
positive expansions, so the Schur positivity and violation-persistence
identity \eqref{eq:rppr-violation-persistence} can only increase the new arm
tip's demand.  If $a_t$ and side leaves occur in one allowed batch, compute the
same final restricted point by sequentializing $a_t$ first and those
already-positive leaves afterward; this is an equality of the final support,
not a replay used by the online reporter.  One exact named transfer query
therefore certifies and flags
$a_{t+1}$.  Every older positive flag remains valid until its vertex is
committed.  This proves that the retained set is exactly $\mathcal K(U)$ for
every allowed suffix order, and it also proves legality and finite
termination.  The long arm forces at least $m$ suffix batches, while at most
all $3m$ nonseed vertices are committed separately, giving the claimed range
for $J$.

It remains to account for the implementation.  Every bounded-degree row is
exposed and retained once; recognizing its branch, arm, or leaf role is
charged with that scan and with control membership, not preprocessing.  The
backbone and long-arm fronts force $\Theta(m)$ adaptive adjacency rounds.
There are $O(m)$ transfer-cell appends or attachment updates and $O(m)$ named
tip/endpoint queries.  Each touches $O(\log(2+m))$ stored product-tree cells
and is charged to $C_{\rm resp}$.  A multi-vertex batch applies these updates
only to its actual committed members; it neither replays an unchosen order nor
materializes an intermediate full response.  There is no hidden bulk subtree
query.
The kinetic heap inserts and removes each tip at most once; every root test,
child comparison, exact crossing comparison, membership lookup, committed
batch check, and final stored-row validation is charged to $C_{\rm ctl}$.
These operations total $O(m\log(2+m))$.

The retained graph records, transfer products, continuants, heap, positive
flags, and batch membership use $\Theta(m)$ cells.  Heap paths need only
$O(\log(2+m))$ scratch, while one terminal response recovery and validation
buffer is allowed $O(m)$ scratch.  The terminal tridiagonal/pendant recovery
arithmetic is included in $C_{\rm resp}$; writing its exact candidate is
charged separately as $C_{\rm mat}=\Theta(m)$.  Each nonseed label is emitted
at most once as a new-positive delta and once in a committed-batch
certificate; the $J+1$ constant-size reply headers and the final
$3m+1$-coordinate exact output are all charged to
$C_{\rm emit}=\Theta(m)$.  No iterative recurrence is used.  These charges
prove \eqref{eq:branch-caterpillar-kinetic-eleven-vector} and
\eqref{eq:branch-caterpillar-kinetic-total}.
\end{proof}

The canonical all-violations policy does not preserve the backbone-only
prefixes used in the preceding theorem: sufficiently light side tips enter
as soon as their parents are reached.  On an explicit fixed-family parameter
range, however, that interleaving is simpler rather than harder.  Every live
boundary is the next graph-distance layer, so no negative key survives from
one checkpoint to the next.

For $0\leq k<m$, put
\begin{equation}
 \begin{split}
 R_k&:=\{r_1,\ldots,r_k\},\qquad R_0:=\varnothing,\\
 U_k^{\rm can}&:=B_{k+1}\cup A_k\cup R_k,\\
 \mathcal F_k^{\rm can}
 &:={}
 \begin{cases}
  \{b_{k+2},a_{k+1},r_{k+1}\},&0\leq k<m-1,\\
  \{a_m,r_m,r_{m+1}\},&k=m-1.
 \end{cases}
 \end{split}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-canonical-layers}
\end{equation}
Thus $U_0^{\rm can}=\{b_1\}$,
$\partial U_k^{\rm can}=\mathcal F_k^{\rm can}$, and
$U_k^{\rm can}\cup\mathcal F_k^{\rm can}=U_{k+1}^{\rm can}$ for
$k<m-1$, while the last union is $V(\mathsf{BC}_m)$.  Let
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal P_m^{\rm can}:=
 \{(U_k^{\rm can},v):0\leq k<m,\ v\in\mathcal F_k^{\rm can}\}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-canonical-pairs}
\end{equation}
and, using \eqref{eq:branch-caterpillar-affine-demand}, define the
analysis-side threshold
\begin{equation}
 \rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)
 :=\min\left\{
  \rho_{\rm cat}(m,\alpha),
  \min_{(U,v)\in\mathcal P_m^{\rm can}}
  \frac{\Gamma_{U,v}}{\alpha H_{U,v}}
 \right\}>0.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-canonical-threshold}
\end{equation}
This finite threshold is a reach condition, not a table built or queried by
the online execution.  It may shrink with $m$.

\begin{theorem}[Canonical layer-delta reporting on the branch caterpillar]
\label{thm:branch-caterpillar-canonical-layer-reporter}
Fix $m\geq2$, $\alpha\in(0,1)$, branch seed $s=e_{b_1}$, and
\begin{equation*}
 0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)
 \leq\rho_{\rm cat}(m,\alpha)\leq\frac13.
\end{equation*}
The exact canonical all-violations RPPR gate on $\mathsf{BC}_m$ has precisely
the faces $U_0^{\rm can},\ldots,U_{m-1}^{\rm can}$ and the $m$ committed
batches $\mathcal F_0^{\rm can},\ldots,\mathcal F_{m-1}^{\rm can}$ in
\eqref{eq:branch-caterpillar-canonical-layers}, followed by the full support.

There is a named exact-real reporter,
\emph{CaterpillarCanonicalLayerDelta}, that at every canonical checkpoint
enumerates the entire current boundary, computes and strictly tests every
exact demand, emits every and only newly strict-positive label, and returns
one charged certificate reply for the canonical batch.  Positive ties are
all emitted in the same batch; equality is nonpositive and remains outside.
In the stated strict parameter range equality never occurs on the displayed
trace.  Every emitted label is consumed immediately, so no old boundary key
or positive label survives a checkpoint.

With one external delta/certificate interaction per committed batch and one
terminal return, its exhaustive resource vector in the shared order is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_m^{\rm can})
 :=\bigl(&C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},
 C_{\rm rec},C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit}
 \bigr)\\
 =\bigl(&\Theta(m),\Theta(m),m+1,0,\Theta(m),0,
 O(m\log(2+m)),\\
 &\Theta(m),O(m),\Theta(m),\Theta(m)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-canonical-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Consequently its additive exact-cell work is
\begin{equation}
 O(m\log(2+m))
 =O\!\left(C(S^\star)\log(2+C(S^\star))\right),
 \qquad C(S^\star)=9m+1.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-canonical-total}
\end{equation}

This is a fixed-$m$, branch-seeded, family-$\rho$, exact-real canonical
all-violations and delta-interface theorem.  It makes no claim for
$\rho_{\rm can}\leq\rho<\rho_{\rm cat}$, another seed, arbitrary
positive-subset or pre-backbone orders, a positive $m$-uniform range of
$\rho$, finite precision, bit complexity, transfer-product conditioning,
$\kappa=1$, RPPR-to-PPR conversion, or product-scale or graph-uniform
accelerated work.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For every $(U,v)\in\mathcal P_m^{\rm can}$, the set $U$ is connected,
contains the seed, and $v$ has an edge into $U$.  The Stieltjes argument in
\eqref{eq:branch-caterpillar-affine-demand} therefore gives
$\Gamma_{U,v}>0$ and $H_{U,v}>0$.  By
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-demand-line,eq:branch-caterpillar-canonical-threshold},
every $v\in\mathcal F_k^{\rm can}$ is a strict boundary violation at
$x[U_k^{\rm can}]$.

Now induct on $k$.  The seed load is positive because
$\rho<\rho_{\rm can}\leq1/3$, so the initialized canonical face is
$U_0^{\rm can}=\{b_1\}$.  The tree boundary of this face is exactly
$\mathcal F_0^{\rm can}$, and every member is strict positive, so the
canonical all-violations gate admits the whole set.  If the current face is
$U_k^{\rm can}$, its full boundary is exactly
$\mathcal F_k^{\rm can}$ and the same threshold argument makes every member
strict positive.  The next canonical batch is therefore that entire set.
Equation \eqref{eq:branch-caterpillar-canonical-layers} gives the next face,
and the final batch gives the whole graph.  This proves the exact chronology,
$S^\star=V(\mathsf{BC}_m)$, and the assertion that every boundary vertex is
newly live at its checkpoint.  Its label may have been revealed by the
preceding layer's row scan, but its own boundary row is first scanned and
charged only when it becomes live.  Strict comparison handles the zero
convention, while the all-violations rule includes every positive tie.

It remains to implement the exact tests and response without dense rewrites.
At $U_k^{\rm can}$, eliminate the active long-arm prefix $A_k$ into one
diagonal/load absorption pair at $b_1$, and eliminate each closed side leaf
$r_i$, $i\leq k$, into the cell at $b_i$.  The surviving system on
$B_{k+1}$ is SPD and tridiagonal.  Store its affine transfer cells in the
balanced product tree of
\Cref{rem:branch-caterpillar-implicit-transfer-comparator}, together with one
affine product for the active long-arm prefix.

Every vertex of $\mathcal F_k^{\rm can}$ has one active parent.  One named
branch query gives the last branch value used by the next branch and its side
leaf, and one named arm query gives the active arm-tip value; at $k=0$ both
reduce to the seed value.  The branch endpoint solve also recovers $x_{b_1}$,
which is the input to the arm-product readout, so no third core query is
hidden.  Hence at most two charged transfer queries compute
all three exact demands at a checkpoint.  The reporter scans the at-most-three
charged boundary rows, retains each row after its first exposure, performs
strict sign comparisons, and emits the three positive labels.

After commitment, the side leaf changes only the diagonal/load cell at its
branch, the long-arm vertex appends one arm-transfer cell and changes only the
induced branch-core absorption cell at $b_1$, and,
except in the last batch, the new branch vertex appends one tridiagonal cell.
The final batch instead contributes two leaf absorptions at $b_m$.  Thus a
batch performs only $O(1)$ product-tree appends or point updates, each costing
$O(\log(2+m))$ exact response operations.  Applying these constant-size
updates in a fixed internal order is Schur-complement associativity for the
one committed support; it neither replays another gate order nor emits an
intermediate face.  The side-leaf absorption changes the fixed-prefix
continuants and Green factors used in
\eqref{eq:branch-prefix-green-factorization}, but the balanced product tree
localizes that change to one point update.  Since the complete boundary is
consumed, the kinetic heap
from \Cref{thm:branch-caterpillar-kinetic-delta-reporter} is empty after every
checkpoint and requires no repair.

Every bounded-degree row is exposed and retained once, over $\Theta(m)$
adaptive adjacency work and rounds.  There are exactly $m$ committed batches,
so the specified external interface has $R_{\rm int}=m+1$.  Boundary
enumeration, membership, strict comparisons, tie handling, batch checks, and
the final stored-row validation use $\Theta(m)$ control operations.  The
$O(1)$ transfer updates and named queries per batch give
$O(m\log(2+m))$ response work, including $O(m)$ terminal recovery.  No
preprocessing or iterative recurrence is used.

Stored rows, arm/core products, absorption cells, and membership records use
$\Theta(m)$ persistent cells.  Forward queries need logarithmic scratch; one
terminal recovery and validation buffer gives the reported $O(m)$ peak.
Writing the terminal candidate costs $C_{\rm mat}=\Theta(m)$.  The $3m$
delta labels, $m$ constant-size certificate replies, one terminal header, and
the exact $(3m+1)$-coordinate output give
$C_{\rm emit}=\Theta(m)$.  These charges prove
\eqref{eq:branch-caterpillar-canonical-eleven-vector} and
\eqref{eq:branch-caterpillar-canonical-total}.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Continuous numerical countdowns cannot pay activation]
\label{prop:no-continuous-activation-countdown}
Fix $\alpha\in(0,1)$.  On the one-edge graph there are legal exact RPPR
activations of fixed charge two whose before-and-after canonical states
converge to one another.  Hence no countdown jointly continuous in
$(\rho,x)$ can decrease by the full activation charge uniformly over RPPR
instances.  In particular, an exact obstacle-energy countdown needs an
unbounded multiplier near the activation breakpoint.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Retain $a=(1+\alpha)/2$ and $h=(1-\alpha)/2$.  For $0<\rho<h$, the shifted
load is $\ell_\rho=(\alpha(1-\rho),-\alpha\rho)$ and the root checkpoint is
\[
 x^-=(\alpha(1-\rho)/a,0).
\]
Its boundary demand at $v$ is
\[
 g_v=(\ell_\rho-Qx^-)_v=\frac{\alpha(h-\rho)}a>0.
\]
The Schur complement is $a-h^2/a=\alpha/a$, so the legal exact expansion
satisfies
\begin{equation}
 x_v^+=h-\rho,
 \qquad
 x_o^+-x_o^-=\frac{h(h-\rho)}a.
 \label{eq:vanishing-activation-jump}
\end{equation}
Thus $\|x^+-x^-\|\to0$ as $\rho\uparrow h$, although scanning and activating
$v$ always costs $c(v)=2$.  Joint continuity would force
$\Phi(\rho,x^-)-\Phi(\rho,x^+)\to0$, contradicting a uniform own-service
decrease of two.  The exact obstacle-energy drop is
\[
 \frac12\frac{g_v^2}{\alpha/a}
 =\frac{\alpha(h-\rho)^2}{2a}.
\]
An energy scaling that pays charge two must therefore be at least
$4a/[\alpha(h-\rho)^2]$, which diverges.
\end{proof}

The discontinuous support tokens in
\Cref{thm:path-token-countdown,thm:three-arm-spider-token-countdown} are not a
technical convenience; the proposition shows why a support jump must be
recorded explicitly.  Raw causal debts do not repair this issue either: an
exact neighbor push can create new exact-after-exact backflow from zero, and
a fresh over-relaxed push can create delayed-reflection magnitude from zero.
Neither raw magnitude is interference monotone without additional transport
aggregation.
