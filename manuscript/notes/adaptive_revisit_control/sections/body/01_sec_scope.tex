The repository-wide residual convention remains open.  This note uses only
the document-scoped unregularized residual
\begin{equation}
 r(\widehat x):=b-Q\widehat x,
 \qquad
 \max_{u\in V}\frac{\abs{r_u(\widehat x)}}{\sqrt{d_u}}
 \leq g,
 \qquad g:=\alpha\epsppr.
 \label{eq:note-gate}
\end{equation}
It is the verifier gate of the measured ladder notes, not an adopted
repository stopping rule.  No conversion from $\epsppr$ to $\rho$,
$\epsappr$, $\epsobj$, or $\epspg$ is asserted.

The measured row-operation charge is
\begin{equation}
 c(u):=1+d_u.
 \label{eq:charge}
\end{equation}
This differs from the manuscript's pure adjacency-scan charge $d_u$ by at
most a factor two because the shared model has $d_u\geq1$.  Theorems below
state which convention they use.  Frontier enumeration, ranking, state
copying, and scheduler arithmetic are not hidden inside \eqref{eq:charge};
where they matter, they are reported separately.
When discussing a future accelerated arm, $\mathsf V_{\rm loc}(I)$ denotes
its explicitly defined local degree-volume ledger on instance $I$; it is not
the reserved vertex set $V$.
