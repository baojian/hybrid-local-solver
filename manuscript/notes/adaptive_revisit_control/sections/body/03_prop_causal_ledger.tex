\subsection{A causal four-way refinement}

Label a paid operation \emph{exact} if $\omega_t=1$ and \emph{spreading} if
$\omega_t>1$.  For every revisit of $u$, inspect the most recent earlier paid
operation on the same $u$.  For $a,b\in\{\mathrm e,\mathrm s\}$, let
$\mathsf{Rev}_{a\to b}$ be the total charge of revisits whose previous same-coordinate
mode was $a$ and whose current mode is $b$.  This ledger requires only two
bits of state per touched coordinate: whether it has been visited and the
mode of its most recent paid visit.

\begin{proposition}[Causal revisit ledger]
\label{prop:causal-ledger}
Every paid run satisfies
\begin{equation}
 W=C(S)+\mathsf{Rev}_{\mathrm e\to\mathrm e}
       +\mathsf{Rev}_{\mathrm e\to\mathrm s}
       +\mathsf{Rev}_{\mathrm s\to\mathrm e}
       +\mathsf{Rev}_{\mathrm s\to\mathrm s},
 \label{eq:causal-ledger}
\end{equation}
and therefore
\begin{equation}
 \varrho_{\rm rev}
 =1+\frac{\mathsf{Rev}_{\mathrm e\to\mathrm e}
       +\mathsf{Rev}_{\mathrm e\to\mathrm s}
       +\mathsf{Rev}_{\mathrm s\to\mathrm e}
       +\mathsf{Rev}_{\mathrm s\to\mathrm s}}{C(S)}.
 \label{eq:causal-ratio}
\end{equation}
Moreover, in exact sequential arithmetic, every charged revisit counted in
$\mathsf{Rev}_{\mathrm e\to\mathrm e}
 +\mathsf{Rev}_{\mathrm e\to\mathrm s}$ certifies that at
least one off-coordinate operation altered that coordinate's residual after
its previous exact operation.  In the shared graph model, such an operation
must occur at a neighbor.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Every paid operation is either the first paid visit to its coordinate or is
in exactly one of the four previous/current-mode classes.  The first visits
sum to $C(S)$, proving \eqref{eq:causal-ledger}.

For the causal statement, write $r=b-Q\widehat x$.  A row operation on $u$
has the form
\begin{equation}
 \widehat x^+=\widehat x+\omega\frac{r_u}{Q_{uu}}e_u,
 \qquad
 r^+=r-\omega\frac{r_u}{Q_{uu}}Qe_u.
 \label{eq:row-update}
\end{equation}
If $\omega=1$, then $r_u^+=0$.  Before a later paid revisit, $r_u$ must
again cross the live guard.  Operations on a coordinate $v$ change $r_u$
only through $Q_{uv}$.  In the shared PageRank matrix, $Q_{uv}\neq0$ for
$u\neq v$ exactly when $u$ and $v$ are adjacent.  Thus some intervening
neighbor operation generated the new residual.
\end{proof}

The quantity
\begin{equation}
 B_{\rm nbr}:=
 \frac{\mathsf{Rev}_{\mathrm e\to\mathrm e}
       +\mathsf{Rev}_{\mathrm e\to\mathrm s}}{C(S)}
 \label{eq:backflow-share}
\end{equation}
is therefore a certified neighbor-backflow revisit share.  By contrast,
$\mathsf{Rev}_{\mathrm s\to\mathrm e}
 +\mathsf{Rev}_{\mathrm s\to\mathrm s}$ mixes neighbor
backflow with the deterministic self-reflection left by over-relaxation.  The
four-way ledger does not prove which $\omega$ is optimal, but it separates a
real causal event that the scalar ratio erases.
