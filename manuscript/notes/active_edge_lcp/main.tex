\begin{abstract}
This note audits an obstacle/linear-complementarity route to the project's
second open problem (OP2).  In the shared normalization, regularized
PageRank is exactly the nonnegative quadratic
\[
 \min_{\vx\geq0}\ \phi(\vx)
 :=\tfrac12\vx^\trans\mQ\vx-\vc^\trans\vx,
 \qquad
 \vc=\vb-\alpha\rho\mD^{1/2}\one .
\]
Its KKT system is the symmetric nonsingular M-matrix LCP
$\vx\geq0$, $\vw=\mQ\vx-\vc\geq0$, and
$x_iw_i=0$.  This reduction has no sign or scaling slack.

The strongest positive result proved here is an exact safe-pivot theorem.
Starting from zero, solve the current principal face exactly and activate any
outside coordinate with negative complementarity slack.  Every activated
coordinate belongs to the unknown optimum support; after a batch activation,
the new exact face solution is positive and increases coordinatewise.  Thus
there are no false activations or deletions and at most $|\mathcal S_\rho^*|$ pivots.
Only graph-boundary coordinates can violate.  A local minimum-norm KKT
subgradient gives an objective-gap certificate in one charged terminal scan.

These facts do not prove OP2.  On endpoint paths, the exact faces are nested
prefixes and a solve or boundary refresh that scans every current face pays
quadratic cumulative volume although terminal volume is linear.  We isolate
the missing theorem as an active-edge continuation contract: all discovery,
changing-face numerical work, boundary rekeying and certification,
materialization, state traffic, and output must be amortized within
$\softO(\vol(\mathcal S_\rho^*)\sqrt{\kappa(\mQ)})$.  Since
$\kappa(\mQ)\leq1/\alpha$ and $\vol(\mathcal S_\rho^*)\leq1/\rho$, that contract would imply
the desired $\softO(1/(\rho\sqrt\alpha))$ bound with logarithmic objective
accuracy.  No audited primary source supplies the contract.  Wei--Yang's 2026
local active-set method is closest, but explicitly solves each SDD face from
scratch; global SDD, obstacle, block-pivot, and bound-QP solvers either require
a supplied face or operate on the full problem.

Finally, a four-vertex rational graph refutes the proposed invariant
$0\leq\vx_k\leq\vx_\rho^*$ for ordinary or orthant-projected face CG.  CG
started from an exact old face stays strictly feasible and decreases the
quadratic, yet overshoots one optimum coordinate at iteration three.
Projection is inactive, so it cannot repair the failure.  This is a precise
algorithmic obstruction, not a lower bound for all accelerated active-set
methods.
\end{abstract}

\section{Exact obstacle and LCP reduction}
\label{sec:reduction}

Define
\begin{equation}
 \vc:=\vb-\alpha\rho\mD^{1/2}\one,
 \qquad
 \phi(\vx):=\frac12\vx^\trans\mQ\vx-\vc^\trans\vx .
 \label{eq:c-and-phi}
\end{equation}
The off-diagonal entries of $\mQ$ are nonpositive, and
$\alpha\mI\preceq\mQ\preceq\mI$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact obstacle reduction]
\label{thm:reduction}
The RPPR problem is equivalent, with the same optimum value and unique
optimum, to
\begin{equation}
 \min_{\vx\geq\vzero}\phi(\vx).
 \label{eq:obstacle}
\end{equation}
Its KKT system is
\begin{equation}
 \vx\geq\vzero,
 \qquad
 \vw:=\mQ\vx-\vc\geq\vzero,
 \qquad
 x_iw_i=0\quad(i\in[n]).
 \label{eq:lcp}
\end{equation}
Moreover, $\mQ$ is a symmetric nonsingular M-matrix (a Stieltjes matrix) and
a K-matrix, so \cref{eq:lcp} has a unique solution.  In the regime
\cref{eq:nonzero-regime}, $v\in\mathcal S_\rho^*$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For any $\vx$, replacing it by $|\vx|$ leaves the diagonal quadratic and
weighted $\ell_1$ terms unchanged.  Because $Q_{ij}\leq0$ for $i\neq j$,
the off-diagonal quadratic contribution cannot increase, and because
$\vb\geq\vzero$, $-\vb^\trans|\vx|\leq-\vb^\trans\vx$.  Thus
$F_\rho(|\vx|)\leq F_\rho(\vx)$, and uniqueness from strong convexity makes
$\vx_\rho^*\geq\vzero$.  On the nonnegative orthant,
\[
 \alpha\rho\norm{\mD^{1/2}\vx}_1
 =\alpha\rho\one^\trans\mD^{1/2}\vx,
\]
so $F_\rho(\vx)=\phi(\vx)$ there.  This proves \cref{eq:obstacle}.

At a lower bound $x_i\geq0$, stationarity and the normal cone give
$w_i=(\nabla\phi(\vx))_i\geq0$ when $x_i=0$ and $w_i=0$ when $x_i>0$.
These are exactly \cref{eq:lcp}; in particular the slack sign is positive,
not negative.  The matrix $\mQ$ is symmetric positive definite and has
nonpositive off-diagonals.  Hence it is Stieltjes.  Every principal minor is
positive, so it is also a P-matrix; together with the Z-sign pattern this is a
K-matrix.  Strict convexity, or the P-matrix LCP theorem, gives uniqueness.

Finally,
\[
 c_v=\frac{\alpha}{\sqrt{d_v}}(1-\rho d_v)>0.
\]
If $(\vx_\rho^*)_v=0$, then
$w_v=\sum_{j\neq v}Q_{vj}(\vx_\rho^*)_j-c_v<0$, contradicting
\cref{eq:lcp}.  Thus $v\in\mathcal S_\rho^*$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Degree-coordinate boundary key]
\label{rem:boundary-key}
Set $y_i=x_i/\sqrt{d_i}$.  Once the seed is active, every inactive
$j\neq v$ satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \frac{w_j}{\sqrt{d_j}}
 =\alpha\rho-\frac{1-\alpha}{2d_j}
   \sum_{i\in\mathcal N(j)\cap\supp(\vx)}y_i.
 \label{eq:boundary-key}
\end{equation}
If $j$ is not on the graph boundary of the support, the sum is empty and the
slack is strictly positive.  Detecting violations is therefore a dynamic
active-edge problem: admitted coordinates increase and monotonically lower
the keys of their inactive neighbors, but a dense old-face correction may
change every cut-edge contribution.
\end{remark}

\section{What a supplied active set gives}
\label{sec:supplied}

For $S\subseteq[n]$, let $\mQ_{SS}$ denote the principal matrix and extend
vectors on $S$ by zero outside $S$.  Principal interlacing gives
\begin{equation}
 \alpha\mI\preceq\mQ_{SS}\preceq\mI,
 \qquad
 \kappa(\mQ_{SS})\leq1/\alpha.
 \label{eq:principal-spectrum}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Supplied exact-support CG]
\label{thm:supplied-cg}
Suppose $S=\mathcal S_\rho^*$ is supplied.  Run ordinary exact-arithmetic CG on
\begin{equation}
 \mQ_{SS}\vy=\vc_S
 \label{eq:face-system}
\end{equation}
from any $\vy_0$, and after the last iteration return
$\widehat\vx_S=[\vy_k]_+$ and zero outside $S$.  If
$\Delta_S:=\tfrac12\norm{\vy_0-(\vx_\rho^*)_S}_{\mQ_{SS}}^2$, then
\begin{equation}
 F_\rho(\widehat\vx)-F_\rho(\vx_\rho^*)
 \leq \frac{4\Delta_S}{\alpha}
 \left(\frac{\sqrt\kappa-1}{\sqrt\kappa+1}\right)^{2k},
 \qquad \kappa:=\kappa(\mQ_{SS}).
 \label{eq:supplied-cg-rate}
\end{equation}
Consequently a supported-row implementation has charged work
\begin{equation}
 \bigO\!\left(
   \frac{\vol(\mathcal S_\rho^*)}{\sqrt\alpha}
   \log\frac{\Delta_S}{\alpha\epsobj}
 \right),
 \label{eq:supplied-work}
\end{equation}
including one final projection and output scan.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Every optimum coordinate on its exact support is positive, so
$\mQ_{SS}(\vx_\rho^*)_S=\vc_S$.  The standard CG energy estimate
\citep{hestenes1952methods} is
\[
 \norm{\vy_k-(\vx_\rho^*)_S}_{\mQ_{SS}}
 \leq2q^k\norm{\vy_0-(\vx_\rho^*)_S}_{\mQ_{SS}},
 \quad q=\frac{\sqrt\kappa-1}{\sqrt\kappa+1}.
\]
Euclidean orthant projection is nonexpansive relative to the positive vector
$(\vx_\rho^*)_S$.  Since $\mQ_{SS}\preceq\mI$ and
$\mQ_{SS}\succeq\alpha\mI$,
\begin{align*}
 \phi([\vy_k]_+)-\phi((\vx_\rho^*)_S)
 &=\tfrac12\norm{[\vy_k]_+-(\vx_\rho^*)_S}_{\mQ_{SS}}^2\\
 &\leq\tfrac12\norm{[\vy_k]_+-(\vx_\rho^*)_S}_2^2\\
 &\leq\frac1{2\alpha}
       \norm{\vy_k-(\vx_\rho^*)_S}_{\mQ_{SS}}^2.
\end{align*}
This proves \cref{eq:supplied-cg-rate}.  A principal matrix-vector product
scans only rows in $S$ and costs $O(\vol(S))$.  The estimate
$q\leq\exp(-2/(\sqrt\kappa+1))$ and \cref{eq:principal-spectrum} give the
iteration and work bound.
\end{proof}

For the zero start, no graph-dependent polynomial is hidden in the logarithm:
since $F_\rho(\vzero)=0$ and
$\min f\geq-\norm{\vb}^2/(2\alpha)\geq-\alpha/2$, the initial RPPR gap is at
most $\alpha/2$.  The ordinary $\sqrt\kappa=O(1/\sqrt\alpha)$ factor therefore
enters exactly through the supplied principal solve.  What is hidden by
\cref{eq:supplied-work} is the cost of finding and maintaining $S$.

\section{Safe exact active-edge discovery}
\label{sec:safe-discovery}

For $U\subseteq[n]$ with $\mQ_{UU}$ nonempty, define the exact face-stationary
vector
\begin{equation}
 x_i^U=
 \begin{cases}
  (\mQ_{UU}^{-1}\vc_U)_i,&i\in U,\\
  0,&i\notin U,
 \end{cases}
 \qquad
 \vw^U:=\mQ\vx^U-\vc.
 \label{eq:face-vector}
\end{equation}
The empty face means $\vx^\emptyset=\vzero$ and $\vw^\emptyset=-\vc$.
We call a face \emph{reachable} if it arises from the empty face by repeatedly
adding only coordinates with strictly negative current slack.

\begin{theorem}[Safe batched K-LCP pivots]
\label{thm:safe-pivots}
Let $U$ be reachable.  Then
\begin{equation}
 U\subseteq\mathcal S_\rho^*,
 \qquad
 \vzero<\vx^U_U\leq(\vx_\rho^*)_U.
 \label{eq:reachable-invariant}
\end{equation}
Every coordinate $j\notin U$ with $w_j^U<0$ belongs to
$\mathcal S_\rho^*\setminus U$.  If $J$ is any nonempty collection of such coordinates,
then $U':=U\cup J$ is reachable and
\begin{equation}
 \vx^{U'}_{U'}>\vzero,
 \qquad
 \vx^{U'}_U\geq\vx^U_U.
 \label{eq:batch-monotonicity}
\end{equation}
If no outside slack is negative, $\vx^U=\vx_\rho^*$.  Consequently this exact
outer loop makes at most $|\mathcal S_\rho^*|$ pivots, has nested support, and never
activates a false coordinate or deletes a coordinate.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The empty-face slack at the seed is $w_v^\emptyset=-c_v<0$, so the first
pivot admits $v\in\mathcal S_\rho^*$.  Its scalar principal solve is positive.

Assume the invariant holds at a reachable $U$.  Restricted stationarity of
$\vx^U$ and the optimum equations on $\mathcal S_\rho^*$ give
\begin{equation}
 \mQ_{UU}\bigl((\vx_\rho^*)_U-\vx^U_U\bigr)
 =-\mQ_{U,\mathcal S_\rho^*\setminus U}(\vx_\rho^*)_{\mathcal S_\rho^*\setminus U}
 \geq\vzero.
 \label{eq:face-comparison}
\end{equation}
The inverse of a principal Stieltjes matrix is nonnegative
\citep[Chapter~6]{berman1994nonnegative}, so
$\vx^U_U\leq(\vx_\rho^*)_U$.

Now take $j\notin\mathcal S_\rho^*$.  Since $(\vx_\rho^*)_j=0$ and $w_j^*\geq0$,
\begin{align}
 w_j^U-w_j^*
 &=\mQ_{jU}\bigl(\vx^U_U-(\vx_\rho^*)_U\bigr)
   -\mQ_{j,\mathcal S_\rho^*\setminus U}(\vx_\rho^*)_{\mathcal S_\rho^*\setminus U}
 \geq0.
 \label{eq:no-false-violation}
\end{align}
Both terms are nonnegative because the coordinate differences in the first
are nonpositive and all displayed off-diagonal matrix entries are
nonpositive.  Hence $w_j^U\geq0$; a negative violator must lie in $\mathcal S_\rho^*$.

For a batch $J$ of negative violators, block elimination in the enlarged
principal equations gives
\begin{align}
 \mH_J\vx^{U'}_J&=-\vw^U_J,
 &\mH_J&:=\mQ_{JJ}-\mQ_{JU}\mQ_{UU}^{-1}\mQ_{UJ},
 \label{eq:schur-pivot}\\
 \vx^{U'}_U-\vx^U_U&=-\mQ_{UU}^{-1}\mQ_{UJ}\vx^{U'}_J.
 \label{eq:old-correction}
\end{align}
The Schur complement $\mH_J$ is again Stieltjes, so
$\mH_J^{-1}\geq0$.  Since $-\vw_J^U>\vzero$,
$\vx_J^{U'}>\vzero$; \cref{eq:old-correction} and the Z-sign pattern make the
old correction nonnegative.  This proves \cref{eq:batch-monotonicity} and
closes the induction.  If there is no negative outside slack, inside
stationarity and positivity give complementarity and outside nonnegativity;
uniqueness in \cref{thm:reduction} identifies the optimum.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Boundary-only discovery]
\label{cor:boundary-only}
At every nonempty reachable face, a negative violation can occur only in
$\partial U$.  Exposing every newly admitted vertex's adjacency list once costs
$\vol(U)$, and creates at most $\vol(U)$ boundary-candidate incidences.  No
adjacency list of a false boundary candidate need be scanned.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
For $j\notin U\cup\partial U$ with $j\neq v$, sparsity gives
$w_j^U=-c_j=\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_j}>0$.  The seed is already in every nonempty
reachable face.  The incidence counts follow by charging each exposed edge to
its endpoint in $U$.
\end{proof}

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{Exact active-edge LCP skeleton (not yet an OP2 implementation)}
\label{alg:skeleton}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\STATE $U\gets\emptyset$, $\vx^U\gets\vzero$.
\WHILE{a certified reporter returns $j\notin U$ with $w_j^U<0$}
  \STATE Choose a nonempty reported batch $J\subseteq\{j:w_j^U<0\}$.
  \STATE Continue the principal state from $U$ to $U\cup J$ and obtain
         $\vx^{U\cup J}$.
  \STATE Expose the adjacency lists of newly admitted vertices and update
         boundary keys.
  \STATE $U\gets U\cup J$.
\ENDWHILE
\STATE Materialize a feasible approximation and certify \cref{eq:op2-gap}.
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

The theorem validates the outer logic of \cref{alg:skeleton}.  It says
nothing about how cheaply an exact or sufficiently accurate principal state
can be continued, how a negative boundary key is located after a dense old
correction, or how ambiguous finite-precision signs are resolved.

\section{A local objective-gap certificate}
\label{sec:certificate}

For a feasible $\vx\geq\vzero$, set $\vw=\mQ\vx-\vc$ and define
\begin{equation}
 g_i(\vx):=
 \begin{cases}
  w_i,&x_i>0,\\
  \min\{w_i,0\},&x_i=0.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:minimal-subgradient}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[KKT subgradient certificate]
\label{thm:kkt-certificate}
The vector $\vg(\vx)$ is the minimum-Euclidean-norm element of the
subdifferential of $\phi+I_{\R_+^n}$ at $\vx$, and
\begin{equation}
 F_\rho(\vx)-F_\rho(\vx_\rho^*)
 \leq\frac{\norm{\vg(\vx)}_2^2}{2\alpha}.
 \label{eq:gap-certificate}
\end{equation}
Thus $\norm{\vg(\vx)}_2\leq\sqrt{2\alpha\epsobj}$ is a sufficient OP2
stopping rule.  If $\vx$ is stored on $U$ containing the seed, one scan of
the rows in $U$ computes all positive-support residuals and all possibly
negative outside entries, at work $O(\vol(U))$ plus $O(|U|)$ state and output
words.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
At $x_i>0$ the normal cone is zero.  At $x_i=0$ it is $(-\infty,0]$, so the
smallest magnitude in $w_i+(-\infty,0]$ is zero when $w_i\geq0$ and $w_i$
when $w_i<0$.  This proves \cref{eq:minimal-subgradient}.  Strong convexity
gives, for every subgradient $\vg$,
\[
 (\phi+I)(\vy)\geq(\phi+I)(\vx)+\vg^\trans(\vy-\vx)
       +\frac\alpha2\norm{\vy-\vx}_2^2.
\]
Minimizing the right side over $\vy\in\R^n$ and taking
$\vy=\vx_\rho^*$ yields \cref{eq:gap-certificate}.  For locality, a sparse
row scan accumulates $\mQ\vx$ on $U\cup\partial U$.  Every farther nonseed
coordinate has positive slack by \cref{rem:boundary-key}, so its minimum-norm
subgradient entry is zero.
\end{proof}

The certificate explicitly charges the terminal scan.  It also avoids a
minimum positive coordinate or strict-complementarity assumption.  Combined
with the shared source conversion, objective gap $\epsobj$ yields the usual
degree-normalized PageRank accuracy
$\rho+\sqrt{2\epsobj/\alpha}$; this is a consequence, not a replacement for
the OP2 objective target.

\section{The fully charged active-edge contract}
\label{sec:contract}

We now isolate the weakest aggregate guarantee used by the end-to-end proof.
Let $E$ be the total number of distinct active-row incidences exposed, plus
the boundary-candidate and degree words generated from them.  Let the work
ledger be
\begin{equation}
 \begin{split}
 W={}&C_{\mathrm{exp}}+C_{\mathrm{false}}+C_{\mathrm{upd}}+C_{\mathrm{bnd}}
      +C_{\mathrm{num}}\\
    &+C_{\mathrm{state}}+C_{\mathrm{mat}}+C_{\mathrm{emit}}.
 \end{split}
 \label{eq:resource-ledger}
\end{equation}
The meanings are fixed in \cref{tab:ledger}; none may be suppressed as an
oracle call.

\begin{definition}[Margin-free active-edge continuation contract]
\label{def:active-edge-contract}
An active-edge primitive satisfies the contract if, for every canonical OP2
instance and $0<\epsobj<\alpha/2$, when coupled to the safe outer skeleton it
has the following properties.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(C\arabic*)]
\item \emph{Sound discovery.}  Every admitted coordinate lies in $\mathcal S_\rho^*$.
Any speculative activation is restored and its entire cost is included in
$C_{\mathrm{false}}$.
\item \emph{Complete reporting.}  It either returns a certified negative
outside slack for the current face or returns a feasible $\widehat\vx$ with
$\norm{\vg(\widehat\vx)}_2\leq\sqrt{2\alpha\epsobj}$.
No positive lower bound on an unknown complementarity margin is assumed;
ambiguous signs are refined or bypassed within the ledger.
\item \emph{Exploration bound.}  Counting candidate words as above,
\begin{equation}
 E\leq\softO(\vol(\mathcal S_\rho^*)).
 \label{eq:exploration-contract}
\end{equation}
\item \emph{Aggregate continuation bound.}  Across the entire nested
sequence, including all items in \cref{eq:resource-ledger},
\begin{equation}
 W\leq\softO\!\left(
 E\sqrt{\kappa(\mQ)}\log\frac1{\epsobj}
 \right).
 \label{eq:aggregate-contract}
\end{equation}
The soft factors may be polylogarithmic in $n$, $1/\alpha$, $1/\rho$, and a
declared failure probability, but not polynomial in $1/\epsobj$ or in an
unstated sign margin.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional OP2 theorem]
\label{thm:conditional-op2}
If a primitive satisfying \cref{def:active-edge-contract} exists, then
\cref{alg:skeleton} is a graph-uniform local algorithm satisfying OP2 with
fully charged work
\begin{equation}
 W=\softO\!\left(
   \frac{1}{\rho\sqrt\alpha}\log\frac1{\epsobj}
 \right).
 \label{eq:conditional-op2-work}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Soundness and \cref{thm:safe-pivots} keep every exact outer face in
$\mathcal S_\rho^*$.  Complete reporting and \cref{thm:kkt-certificate} give the
objective gap.  The shared support-volume theorem gives
$\vol(\mathcal S_\rho^*)\leq1/\rho$, while
$\kappa(\mQ)\leq1/\alpha$.  Substitution into
\cref{eq:exploration-contract,eq:aggregate-contract} proves
\cref{eq:conditional-op2-work}.  Every access and output item appears in
\cref{eq:resource-ledger}, so no boundary scan, preprocessing, or
materialization is uncharged.
\end{proof}

The mathematical outer loop already makes $C_{\mathrm{false}}=0$.  The central
missing lemma is therefore \cref{eq:aggregate-contract}: maintain a changing
Stieltjes principal response and locate all relevant decreases of
\cref{eq:boundary-key} in aggregate active-volume times the fixed-face
$\sqrt\kappa$ scale.  It is acceptable for a construction to have logarithmic
rekeying or rebuilding levels; it is not acceptable to rescan every prefix.

\begin{proposition}[Terminal volume is insufficient]
\label{prop:terminal-insufficient}
There is a family of canonical endpoint-seeded path instances whose exact
safe-pivot faces are all prefixes $U_t=\{1,\ldots,t\}$, whose terminal volume
is $\Theta(n)$, and whose cumulative face volume is
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{t=1}^n\vol(U_t)=\Theta(n^2).
 \label{eq:path-prefix-sum}
\end{equation}
Thus a method that scans or materializes every current face once per pivot is
not controlled by terminal volume.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Fix an endpoint-seeded path $P_n$ and any $\alpha\in(0,1)$.  At $\rho=0$,
$\mQ^{-1}\vb$ is strictly positive because the inverse of the irreducible
Stieltjes matrix is positive.  By continuity, for all sufficiently small
$\rho>0$ the unconstrained shifted solution $\mQ^{-1}\vc$ remains positive,
so it is the obstacle optimum and $\mathcal S_\rho^*=[n]$.  On a proper prefix, the only
outside graph-boundary vertex is the next path vertex.  The current face is
not terminal, so \cref{thm:safe-pivots} implies that this unique boundary
vertex has negative slack and is admitted next.  Hence every prefix occurs.
For $t<n$, $\vol(U_t)=2t-1$, and $\vol(U_n)=2n-2$, proving
\cref{eq:path-prefix-sum}.
\end{proof}

This is a representation/implementation obstruction, not a class lower
bound.  A persistent elimination record solves the same issue on paths; the
arbitrary-graph response and boundary interface is the unresolved part.

\section{Projected and face CG: exact verdict}
\label{sec:cg-verdict}

Exact restricted solutions enjoy the one-sided invariant in
\cref{thm:safe-pivots}.  It is tempting to replace each exact face solve by CG
and assume its intermediate iterates inherit
$\vzero\leq\vx_k\leq\vx_\rho^*$.  The following exact example disproves that
assumption.

\begin{theorem}[Four-vertex coordinatewise overshoot]
\label{thm:cg-counterexample}
Let the graph have vertices $\{0,1,2,3\}$ and edges
\begin{equation}
 \{01,12,13,23\},
 \label{eq:counterexample-graph}
\end{equation}
with degrees $(1,3,2,2)$, seed $0$, $\alpha=1/5$, and $\rho=1/1000$.
Let $U=\{0,1,2\}$ and start ordinary full-face CG from the exact
$U$-restricted solution, extended by zero.  Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item the obstacle optimum is strictly positive on all four coordinates;
\item the first three CG iterates are strictly positive and the quadratic
energy decreases at every iteration;
\item the third iterate exceeds the optimum in coordinate $2$ by the exact
positive amount
\begin{equation}
 \frac{30473}{32130000};
 \label{eq:overshoot}
\end{equation}
\item orthant-projected CG follows the identical trajectory through this
iteration because every projection is inactive.
\end{enumerate}
Therefore feasibility, support monotonicity, objective monotonicity, and
within-face conjugacy do not imply coordinatewise monotonicity below the
optimum.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Use degree coordinates $\vy=\mD^{-1/2}\vx$.  Direct substitution into
\cref{eq:c-and-phi} gives
\begin{equation}
 \mH:=\mD^{1/2}\mQ\mD^{1/2}=\frac35\mD-\frac25\mA,
 \qquad
 \vh:=\mD^{1/2}\vc=\frac15(\eunit{0}-\rho\vd).
 \label{eq:H-h}
\end{equation}
Exact rational elimination gives
\begin{align}
 \vy^*&=
 \left(\frac{6983}{17000},\frac{1983}{17000},
       \frac{983}{17000},\frac{983}{17000}\right),
 \label{eq:counter-optimum}\\
 \vy_0&=
 \left(\frac{24953}{63000},\frac{1987}{21000},
       \frac{983}{31500},0\right).
 \label{eq:counter-start}
\end{align}
The positivity of $\vy^*$ makes it the unconstrained and obstacle optimum.
Running the ordinary Euclidean CG recurrence in the original $\vx$
coordinates gives step sizes
\begin{equation}
 \frac53,\quad\frac{225}{74},\quad\frac{259}{120},\quad\frac{150}{119},
 \label{eq:counter-steps}
\end{equation}
and the first three degree-coordinate iterates
\begin{align*}
 \vy_1={}&\left(\frac{24953}{63000},\frac{1987}{21000},
  \frac{983}{31500},\frac{983}{23625}\right),\\
 \vy_2={}&\left(\frac{24953}{63000},\frac{259877}{2331000},
  \frac{65861}{1165500},\frac{10813}{194250}\right),\\
 \vy_3={}&\left(\frac{388057}{945000},\frac{4363}{37800},
  \frac{111079}{1890000},\frac{36371}{630000}\right).
\end{align*}
Every displayed entry is positive, while
\[
 (y_3)_2-(y^*)_2
 =\frac{111079}{1890000}-\frac{983}{17000}
 =\frac{30473}{32130000}>0.
\]
Positive diagonal scaling preserves coordinatewise order between $\vx$ and
$\vy$.  Standard exact CG line minimization decreases the quadratic, and the
verification script checks the corresponding rational energy values and
$\mQ$-conjugacy.  The fourth step reaches $\vy^*$.  Since no coordinate is
negative before the overshoot, orthant projection changes nothing.
\end{proof}

The exact script \texttt{verify\_counterexample.py} also prints transformed
residual sign patterns
\[
 0{+}{+}0,\qquad {+}{+}{-}0,\qquad {-}{+}{-}0,\qquad0000,
\]
so no fixed residual-sign invariant survives either.  The distinctions are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Objective monotonicity} holds in the example by exact line
minimization.
\item \emph{Coordinatewise monotonicity} fails at iteration three.
\item \emph{Support monotonicity} holds in this example because all new-face
iterates are positive; it is too weak to certify safe values.
\item \emph{Residual-sign monotonicity} fails by the exact sign trace.
\item \emph{Conjugacy} holds only while the fixed-face recurrence is
unmodified.  If an orthant projection clips a coordinate, the actual
displacement is no longer the CG step $a_k\vp_k$ and the short residual and
conjugacy recurrences require a restart or reorthogonalization.
\end{itemize}

The theorem refutes only the ordinary/project-after-step route and any proof
that assumes its one-sided invariant.  It does not rule out accelerated
obstacle solvers with different estimate sequences, certified envelopes, or
persistent response representations.

\section{Algorithmic synthesis and exact blocker}
\label{sec:synthesis}

The route now separates cleanly into three layers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Outer mathematical logic (proved):} the K-LCP pivot from zero is
nested, support-safe, and boundary-local by \cref{thm:safe-pivots}.
\item \emph{Fixed-face numerical acceleration (proved with supplied face):}
CG contributes $\sqrt\kappa=O(1/\sqrt\alpha)$ and logarithmic accuracy by
\cref{thm:supplied-cg}.
\item \emph{Changing-face implementation (open):} compose the two without
paying $\sum_t\vol(U_t)$, refreshing every affected boundary key, or assuming
a minimum sign margin.  This is exactly \cref{def:active-edge-contract}.
\end{enumerate}

A plausible implementation maintains an implicit Schur response for the old
face, a Krylov or multilevel correction space for the new block, and a
monotone boundary-key data structure driven by \cref{eq:boundary-key}.
However, a new block can cause a dense positive correction on old
coordinates; every such change contributes to all incident cut keys.  Energy
telescoping pays for correction magnitude, but no proved conversion currently
pays for locating every newly negative key.  Conversely, a heap pays only if
all key decrements can be generated without replaying the dense correction.
This dense-response/all-negative-reporting interface is the central missing
lemma.

The required theorem can be weakened in useful ways without changing the
proof of \cref{thm:conditional-op2}: a structural graph condition may replace
graph-uniform \cref{eq:aggregate-contract}; a randomized reporter may fail
with declared probability $\delta$; or a cumulative explored-volume bound
may replace $\vol(\mathcal S_\rho^*)$.  What cannot be replaced by terminal volume alone
is cumulative continuation and boundary work.

\section{Final claim ledger}
\label{sec:final-ledger}

\begin{description}[style=nextline,leftmargin=2.9cm]
\item[\statussource]
The canonical nonnegative optimum and support-volume bound; standard CG
energy convergence; the primary-source results and limitations summarized in
\cref{sec:literature}.

\item[\statusproved]
The exact LCP reduction; supplied-support CG work and final projection; safe
batched true-support pivots; boundary-only violations; the local KKT
objective certificate; endpoint-prefix insufficiency; and the exact
four-vertex face-CG counterexample.

\item[\statusconditional]
\Cref{thm:conditional-op2}: the margin-free aggregate active-edge contract
implies fully charged
$\softO(1/(\rho\sqrt\alpha))$ work with logarithmic objective accuracy.

\item[\statusempirical]
None.  The counterexample audit is exact rational computation, not floating-
point measurement.

\item[\statusrefuted]
Terminal volume as a standalone cumulative-work argument; and the invariant
$\vzero\leq\vx_k\leq\vx_\rho^*$ for ordinary/orthant-projected face CG.
Neither refutation is a lower bound for all local obstacle algorithms.

\item[\statusopen]
Prove \cref{eq:aggregate-contract}, or a weaker structural version sufficient
for OP2: persistent changing-face solve state plus complete boundary
violation reporting, with every update, scan, word, and sign refinement
charged and no supplied support.
\end{description}

The next falsifiable target is a single-block expansion theorem: after adding
$J$ to $U$, update an implicit representation of
\cref{eq:schur-pivot,eq:old-correction} and report all newly negative keys in
\cref{eq:boundary-key} at amortized
$\softO((\vol(J)+\text{new boundary incidences})\sqrt\kappa)$ work.  A proof
must survive a dense old-coordinate correction; a counterexample should show
that even an implicit response must communicate with too many cut keys.
