\section{Falsifiable conclusion and next proof step}
\label{sec:terminal-modal-conclusion}

Exact orthogonality and the even-mode band remove spatial and temporal
cancellation.  The finite-$q$ correction theorem proves the proper-prefix
chronology, so the optimum formula gives
\eqref{eq:terminal-modal-source-transform} unconditionally for $m\ge64$,
while the ideal-sign lemma separates the packet from its correction.  The
position and velocity targets are proved on the growing band in
\cref{lem:terminal-modal-position-profile,lem:terminal-modal-velocity-profile}.
The correlated stopped ledger proves the early binomial-window half-retention
and hence the literal nonlinear regime for $qk<3/50$.  The folded-source
completion proves every interior static two-step row uniformly from $m=64$;
the five moving-frontier rows have strictly positive joint first-order
limits.  Their combination proves the static $21q^3/80$ target for all
sufficiently large $m$.  The late position lower bound then gives
\eqref{eq:terminal-modal-unclipped-target} and raw positivity through
\eqref{eq:terminal-modal-horizon}.  Exact modal anti-cancellation yields the
unconditional asymptotic terminal block
\eqref{eq:terminal-modal-log-unconditional}.

The result is deliberately narrow: it proves a lower block only for the
named exact-real recurrence.  It does not cover other implementations, face
schedules, local accelerated algorithms, or nonlocal response primitives,
and the frontier proof supplies no explicit finite cutoff.
