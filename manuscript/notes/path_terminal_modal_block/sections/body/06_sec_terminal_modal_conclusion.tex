\section{Falsifiable conclusion and next proof step}
\label{sec:terminal-modal-conclusion}

Exact orthogonality and the even-mode band remove spatial and temporal
cancellation.  The finite-$q$ correction theorem proves the proper-prefix
chronology, so the optimum formula gives
\eqref{eq:terminal-modal-source-transform} unconditionally for $m\ge64$,
while the ideal-sign lemma separates the packet from its correction.  Two
uniform statements remain:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item prove the two scalar bounds
 \eqref{eq:terminal-modal-source-position-target} and
 \eqref{eq:terminal-modal-source-velocity-target}
 by signed summation of the explicit source traces;
 \item bound the positive weighted mass of the static directed-velocity
 remainder in \cref{lem:terminal-modal-directed-packet}, then combine it with
 an early/late position lower bound to prove
 \eqref{eq:terminal-modal-unclipped-target} and raw positivity through
 \eqref{eq:terminal-modal-horizon}.
\end{enumerate}

Failure of any interface falsifies this route.  Success proves only a lower
block for the named exact-real recurrence, not for other implementations or
local accelerated algorithms.
