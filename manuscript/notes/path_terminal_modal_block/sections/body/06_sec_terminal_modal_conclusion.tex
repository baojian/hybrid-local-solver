\section{Falsifiable conclusion and next proof step}
\label{sec:terminal-modal-conclusion}

Exact orthogonality and the even-mode band remove spatial and temporal
cancellation.  Conditional on the open chronology, the optimum formula gives
\eqref{eq:terminal-modal-source-transform}, while the ideal-sign lemma
separates the packet from its correction.  Three uniform statements remain:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item prove the proper-prefix chronology in
       \cref{lem:terminal-modal-admission-chronology};
 \item conditional on that chronology, prove the two scalar bounds
 \eqref{eq:terminal-modal-source-position-target} and
 \eqref{eq:terminal-modal-source-velocity-target}
 by signed summation of the explicit source traces;
 \item prove \eqref{eq:terminal-modal-unclipped-target} and raw positivity
 through \eqref{eq:terminal-modal-horizon}.
\end{enumerate}

Failure of any interface falsifies this route.  Success proves only a lower
block for the named exact-real recurrence, not for other implementations or
local accelerated algorithms.
