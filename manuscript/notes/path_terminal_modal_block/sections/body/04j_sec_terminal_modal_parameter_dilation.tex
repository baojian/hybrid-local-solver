\section{A parameter-dilated terminal family}
\label{sec:terminal-modal-parameter-dilation}

The choice $q=1/(16m)$ is not needed by the proper-prefix chronology itself.
This section records a second, separately scoped family in which
\begin{equation}
 q=\frac1{64m},\qquad \rho=\tau=\frac q5,
 \label{eq:terminal-modal-dilated-parameters}
\end{equation}
and shows that the late nonlinear bridge begins earlier.  This does not
replace the named family elsewhere in the note: in particular, the two modal
entry-profile estimates were normalized using $16mq=1$ and must be audited
again before the terminal lower bound is asserted for
\eqref{eq:terminal-modal-dilated-parameters}.

\begin{lemma}[Chronology survives parameter dilation; proved here]
\label{lem:terminal-modal-dilated-chronology}
Let $m\ge64$ and use \eqref{eq:terminal-modal-dilated-parameters}.  Every
proper-prefix chronology conclusion of
\cref{lem:terminal-modal-admission-chronology}, the first full-face
average-residual sign in
\cref{lem:terminal-modal-first-full-average-sign}, and the entry correction
bound
\begin{equation}
 c(j)<-\frac{13}{100}q^3\qquad(0\le j\le m)
 \label{eq:terminal-modal-dilated-entry-correction}
\end{equation}
remain valid.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The finite correction proof uses the parameter choice only through
\[
 q\le\frac1{1024},\qquad nq\le\frac1{16},\qquad
 \chi^n\ge\frac78,\qquad \delta^{-(n-2)}\le\frac{16}{15}.
\]
For \eqref{eq:terminal-modal-dilated-parameters} all four inequalities are
strengthened: $q\le1/4096$, $nq\le1/64$,
$\chi^n\ge1-2nq\ge31/32$, and
$\delta^{-(n-2)}\le64/63$.  Thus every scalar, total-variation, stopped
kernel, base, and mass estimate in
\cref{lem:terminal-modal-finite-correction-sign} applies without changing a
constant.  This proves the proper-prefix chronology.

The one-row extension used in
\cref{lem:terminal-modal-first-full-average-sign} has the same hypotheses,
with $n=m+1$ only in the final target and source indices still at most $m$;
its base and mass losses decrease when $q$ and $mq$ decrease.  Hence its two
strict average-residual bounds survive.  Finally, the proof of
\cref{lem:terminal-modal-entry-correction-margin} uses the same final-prefix
ledger, $\delta^{m-1}>15/16$, and the broad frontier bounds from
\cref{lem:terminal-modal-frontier-bounds}.  These hypotheses are again
strengthened, so \eqref{eq:terminal-modal-dilated-entry-correction} follows.
\end{proof}

The full optimum has a much larger position scale in this family.  Put
\begin{equation}
 x=2m\operatorname{artanh}q
   =\frac{\operatorname{artanh}q}{32q}.
 \label{eq:terminal-modal-dilated-x}
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\min_jp_{m+1}^\star(j)}q=\operatorname{csch}x-\frac15,
 \qquad
 \frac{\max_jp_{m+1}^\star(j)}q=\operatorname{coth}x-\frac15.
 \label{eq:terminal-modal-dilated-optimum-extrema}
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[Dilated late bridge; proved here]
\label{lem:terminal-modal-dilated-late-bridge}
Assume the still-open static certificate
\begin{equation}
 \norm{u_+}_{1,D}\le\frac{21}{80}q^3
 \label{eq:terminal-modal-dilated-static-assumption}
\end{equation}
for the family \eqref{eq:terminal-modal-dilated-parameters}.  If the literal
iteration agrees with the linear full-face candidate through time $k-1$, then
for every $k$ with
\begin{equation}
 t=qk\ge\frac1{40},
\end{equation}
the time-$k$ raw candidate is strictly positive and the safe-envelope
subtraction is unclipped.  Therefore, conditional on
\eqref{eq:terminal-modal-dilated-static-assumption}, the only remaining
nonlinear interval is $qk<1/40$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Since $q\le1/4096$,
\[
 \frac1{32}<x\le\frac1{32(1-q^2)}
 \le\frac1{32(1-4096^{-2})}=:x_0.
\]
The elementary estimates
\[
 \tanh(1/32)\ge\frac1{32}-\frac1{3\cdot32^3}>\frac5{161}
\]
and, for $x_0<1/7$,
\[
 \sinh x_0\le x_0+\frac{x_0^3}{5}<\frac{20}{639}
\]
give the rational bounds
\begin{equation}
 \min_jp_{m+1}^\star(j)>\frac{127}{4}q,
 \qquad
 \max_jp_{m+1}^\star(j)<32q.
 \label{eq:terminal-modal-dilated-optimum-bounds}
\end{equation}
The focused verifier checks both strict rational comparisons.

At full entry, chronology gives $p_0\ge0$ and nonnegative transported
proper-prefix velocity.  The proper-prefix formula has
$p_m^\star(j)<q$, so the entry errors
$e_0=p_0-p_{m+1}^\star$ and $f_0=v_0-p_{m+1}^\star$ obey
\[
 e_0\ge-32q\one,\qquad f_0\ge-q\one.
\]
The exact position propagator and the row masses
$K_k\one=\one$, $J_k\one=k\one$, and $L\one=\one$ therefore yield
\begin{equation}
 p_k\ge q\left\{\frac{127}{4}-(32+t)\delta^k\right\}\one.
 \label{eq:terminal-modal-dilated-position-lower}
\end{equation}

On the other hand, the directed alias bound and
\eqref{eq:terminal-modal-dilated-static-assumption} give for the safe
subtraction size
\begin{equation}
 \Delta_k\le\frac{21}{80}q\,e^{-t}(1+32t),
 \label{eq:terminal-modal-dilated-envelope-upper}
\end{equation}
because $k/(2m)=32t$.  Combining the last two displays, it is enough that
\begin{equation}
 \frac{127}{4}>
 e^{-t}\left\{32+t+\frac{21}{80}(1+32t)\right\}.
 \label{eq:terminal-modal-dilated-scalar-comparison}
\end{equation}
The right side decreases for $t\ge0$.  At $t_0=1/40$, use
$e^{-t_0}<1-t_0+t_0^2/2=3121/3200$.  The remaining exact slack is
\begin{equation}
 \frac{127}{4}-\frac{3121}{3200}\frac{12999}{400}
 =\frac{70121}{1280000}>0.
\end{equation}
This proves the strict late comparison and the claimed linear-to-literal
stitch, exactly as in
\cref{lem:terminal-modal-late-position-envelope}.
\end{proof}

The dilation is not a source-free cure for the early kernel.  Finite screens
show that the separate quantity $\mathcal T_kd/q^3$ can exceed $0.1$ near
the far endpoint, even though the correlated half-retention expression
$-\mathcal H_kc-\mathcal T_kd$ remains positive.  These measurements are not
used above.  A proof of the dilated early interval must therefore preserve
that correlation, just as for the named family.
