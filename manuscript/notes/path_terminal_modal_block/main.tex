\title{A Terminal Logarithmic Block for a Transported-Center Path Execution}

\begin{abstract}
We revisit one quarantined terminal-face candidate for a transported-center
accelerated RPPR execution on the endpoint path $P_m$.  Its parameters are
$q=1/(16m)$, $\alpha=q^2$, and $\rho=\tau=q/5$.  On a projection-inactive
full face, the normalized residual is an exactly solvable damped cosine wave.
We give the literal degree-weighted cosine coefficients, keep the stationary
mode separate from residual range, and prove a phase-uniform
anti-cancellation bound over a band of even modes.

The measured full-face entry is close to the binomial packet
\[
 g_j=-\frac{q^2}{2}(1-q)^m2^{-m}\binom mj.
\]
This packet has about $\sqrt m$ even cosine coefficients of order $q^3$.
The proper-prefix chronology is proved for every $m\ge64$ by a finite-$q$
moving-source cancellation bound, so the exact sparse changing-face source
follows unconditionally in the asymptotic parameter range.  On the full face,
an exact directed split retains the packet's outward entry momentum, proves
the literal entry-position correction nonpositive, and reduces every positive
residual to one static directed-velocity remainder with no factor-$k$ or
reflection loss.  A final-prefix refinement proves $c<-13q^3/100$
coordinatewise while retaining the exact two-row frontier defect and the
separate degree-one endpoint; this does not separately control the velocity
remainder over the early window.  That remainder is exactly a local average of an explicit
final-prefix vector, and its early propagation has a finite binomial-window
kernel.  The position and velocity entry profiles are proved on the growing
band $H_m=\Theta(\sqrt{m/\log m})$.  A correlated finite stopped ledger
proves projection inactivity and zero safe subtraction through $qk<3/50$.
For the late range, an exact folded-source theorem proves every interior
static two-step row from $m=64$ onward, while a moving-frontier expansion
gives five strictly positive $q^{-4}$ boundary limits.  Hence all sufficiently
large members satisfy the static $21q^3/80$ directed-remainder certificate.
The late position lower bound then preserves projection inactivity and an
unclipped global envelope through
$\lfloor(8q)^{-1}\log(1/q)\rfloor$.  Exact modal anti-cancellation therefore
forces a terminal block of length $\Omega(q^{-1}\log(1/q))$ for all
sufficiently large $m$.  The frontier argument does not provide an explicit
cutoff at the level of the first-order proof; a separate finite transfer
ledger gives the conservative explicit threshold $m\ge4096$ for the full
static cone and nonlinear regime.  Positivity of the propagation kernels and
that all-time nonlinear regime also prove that the continued iterate already
has degree-normalized RPPR semantic error below $\tau=q/5$ after
$\lceil15/(4q)\rceil$ full-face steps for every $m\ge4096$.  Thus the
logarithm belongs to the prescribed one-sided certificate, not to semantic
accuracy on this family.  The theorem concerns only the named exact-real
recurrence; it gives
no lower bound for other recurrences, face schedules, local accelerated
methods, or implicit response algorithms.
\end{abstract}
