\begin{abstract}
We revisit one quarantined terminal-face candidate for a transported-center
accelerated RPPR execution on the endpoint path $P_m$.  Its parameters are
$q=1/(16m)$, $\alpha=q^2$, and $\rho=\tau=q/5$.  On a projection-inactive
full face, the normalized residual is an exactly solvable damped cosine wave.
We give the literal degree-weighted cosine coefficients, keep the stationary
mode separate from residual range, and prove a phase-uniform
anti-cancellation bound over a band of even modes.

The measured full-face entry is close to the binomial packet
\[
 g_j=-\frac{q^2}{2}(1-q)^m2^{-m}\binom mj.
\]
This packet has about $\sqrt m$ even cosine coefficients of order $q^3$.
The proper-prefix chronology is proved for every $m\ge64$ by a finite-$q$
moving-source cancellation bound, so the exact sparse changing-face source
follows unconditionally in the asymptotic parameter range.  On the full face,
an exact directed split retains the packet's outward entry momentum, proves
the literal entry-position correction nonpositive, and reduces every positive
residual to one static directed-velocity remainder with no factor-$k$ or
reflection loss.  A final-prefix refinement proves $c<-13q^3/100$
coordinatewise while retaining the exact two-row frontier defect and the
separate degree-one endpoint; this does not separately control the velocity
remainder over the early window.  That remainder is exactly a local average of an explicit
final-prefix vector, and its early propagation has a finite binomial-window
kernel.  A quantified entry approximation on only
$H_m=\Theta(\sqrt{m/\log m})$ modes, together with uniform projection
inactivity and an unclipped global safe-envelope subtraction, forces at least
$\frac18q^{-1}\log(1/q)$ terminal steps for all sufficiently large $m$.
The position entry profile is proved on that growing band by a uniform
source-to-continuum estimate whose three limiting absolute constants sum
strictly below the required threshold.  A second uniform continuum estimate
proves the velocity entry profile by retaining the exact cancellation among
the base, derivative trace, and final endpoint.  The terminal regime is the
sole remaining missing lemma.  Its first average residual is strictly
negative, and a rational early--late reduction proves position/envelope
dominance after $qk=3/50$ conditional on one static local-average target;
the earlier interval reduces to the finite bound
$\mathcal L_ku\le q^3/16$, which remains open.  A
finite floating screen supports the remaining interfaces but does not prove
them.  Thus
this note supplies a rigorous conditional route
and a falsification interface; it does not prove that the logarithm is
necessary, and it gives no lower bound for other recurrences or implicit
response algorithms.
\end{abstract}
