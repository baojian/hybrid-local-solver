\section{A coarse deterministic prefix and a signed coordinate finish}
\label{sec:revisit-deterministic-signed}
The preceding excess potential has a signed version. This gives a literal
deterministic accelerated-prefix / coordinate-tail algorithm whose prefix
does not have to be below PPR or the RPPR optimum. Unlike appending GS after
the earlier fine clipping rule, this handoff uses a coarser objective target
and can leave a nonempty tail.

\begin{lemma}[Signed residual excess pays for coordinate delivery]
\label{lem:revisit-signed-excess}
Let $0<\rho<\sigma$ and initialize $R=b-Qx$ from any sparse vector.
Define
\[
 \mathcal M_\rho(x)=\sum_i\omega_i[|R_i|-\alpha\rho\omega_i]_+.
\]
While $|R_i|>\alpha\sigma\omega_i$, update
\begin{equation}
 x_i\gets x_i+a_i,\qquad
 a_i=\operatorname{sign}(R_i)
            \frac{|R_i|-\alpha\rho\omega_i}{Q_{ii}}.
 \label{eq:revisit-signed-coordinate}
\end{equation}
This tail terminates at normalized semantic error at most $\sigma$, with
\begin{equation}
 W_{\rm tail}\leq
 \frac{Q_{ii}\mathcal M_\rho(x_{\rm in})}
      {\alpha^2(\sigma-\rho)}.
 \label{eq:revisit-signed-tail-work}
\end{equation}
All support and boundary initialization, output, and label bookkeeping are
charged separately. If the input is nonnegative, all iterates remain so.
Rounding the magnitude of each density increment downward on a grid at
most $\alpha(\sigma-\rho)/(2Q_{ii})$ only doubles the bound.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The selected residual is set to
$\operatorname{sign}(R_i)\alpha\rho\omega_i$. Its own potential
contribution decreases by $Q_{ii}\omega_i|a_i|$. The function
$[|r|-c]_+$ is one-Lipschitz, so the total increase at neighbors is at
most $((1-\alpha)/2)\omega_i|a_i|$. Consequently
\[
 \mathcal M_\rho(x+a_i e_i)
 \leq\mathcal M_\rho(x)-\alpha\omega_i|a_i|.
\]
Every selected row therefore spends more than
$\alpha^2(\sigma-\rho)d_i/Q_{ii}$ of this nonnegative potential.
This proves the work bound and finite termination. At termination,
$|R|\leq\alpha\sigma\omega$, so
$|Q^{-1}R|\leq Q^{-1}|R|\leq\sigma\omega$.

If $x\geq0$ and $R_i<0$, Stieltjes signs and $b\geq0$ imply
$-R_i\leq Q_{ii}x_i$. The negative increment has magnitude less than
$x_i$, so it cannot cross zero. Positive increments also preserve
nonnegativity. A magnitude rounded toward zero retains at least half of
the ideal increment, keeps the selected residual on its original side of
the reserve, and has the same potential decrease per unit actual movement.
This proves the rounded statement.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[A coarse RPPR gap controls the thresholded residual]
\label{lem:revisit-gap-to-excess}
For $0<r_*<\rho$ and any $x\geq0$ with RPPR gap at $r_*$ at most $G$,
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal M_\rho(x)\leq\frac{G}{2\alpha(\rho-r_*)}.
 \label{eq:revisit-gap-to-excess}
\end{equation}
The same bound holds for signed $x$ if its gap is measured by the full
composite objective $F_{r_*}(x)=f(x)+\alpha r_*\|D^{1/2}x\|_1$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $u=x^*_{r_*}$. Its residual belongs to
$[0,\alpha r_*\omega]$, and KKT gives
$\|Q(x-u)\|_2^2\leq\|x-u\|_Q^2\leq2G$.
Thus
\[
 [|b-Qx|_i-\alpha\rho\omega_i]_+
 \leq[|Q(x-u)|_i-\alpha(\rho-r_*)\omega_i]_+.
\]
For $z\geq0$ and $a>0$, $(z-a)_+\leq z^2/(4a)$.
Multiply the displayed inequality by $\omega_i$, sum, and apply that
scalar bound to obtain \cref{eq:revisit-gap-to-excess}.
For signed $x$, the composite KKT subgradient at $u$ gives the same
quadratic lower bound $F_{r_*}(x)-F_{r_*}(u)\geq\|x-u\|_Q^2/2$.
The remaining inequalities are unchanged.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Deterministic accelerated prefix with signed GS delivery]
\label{thm:revisit-deterministic-gs}
For point-source semantic accuracy $0<\epsppr<1$, there is a deterministic
local algorithm returning
\[
 0\leq\widehat x\leq x^*_{\epsppr/3}\leq x_0^*,\qquad
 \|D^{-1/2}(\widehat x-x_0^*)\|_\infty\leq\epsppr,
 \qquad \vol(\supp\widehat x)\leq3/\epsppr.
\]
With $L_\alpha=1+\log(2/\alpha)$, its exact-real adjacency work is
$O(L_\alpha/(\epsppr\sqrt\alpha))$, and all local operations cost
\[
 O\!\left(\frac{L_\alpha}{\epsppr\sqrt\alpha}
       \log\!\left(2+\frac{L_\alpha}{\epsppr\sqrt\alpha}\right)\right).
\]
A separately rounded implementation has the same soft local work scale
and bounded integer encodings. The output residual is nonnegative and is
at least $\alpha(\epsppr/3)\omega_i$ on every positive output coordinate.
The result is semantic; no ACL residual upper bound is asserted for this
final clipped output.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Construction and exact-real work]
Set
\[
 \sigma=\epsppr/3,\qquad \rho=\sigma/2,\qquad
 r_*=\rho/2=\epsppr/12,\qquad G=\alpha^2\theta\rho.
\]
Read $d_v$. If $\sigma d_v\geq1$, return zero, which is accurate by
the maximum principle. If $\alpha=1$, return
$[x_0^*-\sigma\omega]_+=x_\sigma^*$, using only the seed coordinate;
its error is $\sigma$ and its support volume is below $1/\sigma$.

Run the orthant continuation to $r_*$, then freeze the same state until
its Euclidean accelerated energy is at most $G$. No clipping or reset is
performed at this handoff. The initial frozen energy is at most
$9\alpha r_*$, so the number of frozen steps is at most
\[
 N\leq1+\theta^{-1}\log\frac9{2\alpha\theta}.
\]
The kinetic incidence bound is
$44(N+2/\theta)/r_*$. This is
$O(L_\alpha/(\epsppr\sqrt\alpha))$ and includes the complete prefix
history. Materialize that history and initialize the actual residual with
one charged scan of its support. By \cref{lem:revisit-gap-to-excess},
$\mathcal M_\rho(x)\leq G/(\alpha\rho)=\alpha\theta$.

Apply \cref{eq:revisit-signed-coordinate} with activation threshold
$\alpha\sigma\omega$. By \cref{lem:revisit-signed-excess}, its work is
at most $6/(\epsppr\sqrt\alpha)$ and its final point $x^{\rm t}$
satisfies $|x^{\rm t}-x_0^*|\leq\sigma\omega$.
Return
\[
 \widehat x=[x^{\rm t}-2\sigma\omega]_+.
\]
Then $0\leq\widehat x\leq x_0^*$ and
$x_0^*-\widehat x\leq3\sigma\omega$.
There is also a stronger order certificate. Put
$n=\widehat x-(x^{\rm t}-2\sigma\omega)\geq0$.
At a positive output coordinate $n_i=0$, so Stieltjes signs give
\[
 (Q\widehat x)_i
   \leq (Qx^{\rm t})_i-2\alpha\sigma\omega_i,
 \qquad (b-Q\widehat x)_i\geq\alpha\sigma\omega_i.
\]
At a zero output coordinate the residual is nonnegative directly.
The positive-part Stieltjes comparison therefore gives
$\widehat x\leq x^*_\sigma$: on any set where the output exceeded that
optimum, its RPPR gradient would be nonpositive and the optimum's gradient
nonnegative, contradicting positive definiteness as in
\cref{lem:revisit-source-envelope}. This also proves the stated support
volume bound. A residual lower bound is distinct from the ACL upper bound.

Use a deterministic label map and an active queue in the tail. Every newly
exposed label comes from a charged row, and every residual update and queue
change is charged to that incidence. The retained prefix history, the new
tail history, initialization, and final clipping all fit the sum of the
two incidence budgets, before label-map logarithms. The prefix reporter
has the same fully charged realization as in
\cref{sec:revisit-completion}. This proves the
claimed total work.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Separately rounded realization]
Use the directed-rounded prefix of \cref{sec:revisit-rounding} at final
regularizer $r_*$, retaining $h\leq\min\{1/16,\theta\}$ and choosing
$\Gamma=256h/\theta\leq\min\{\alpha^2r_*,G/2\}$.
At the freeze its energy is at most $13\alpha r_*$. Choose $q$ by
integer doubling until $2^q\geq13/(\alpha\theta)$ and run $q/\theta$
frozen steps. Their actual gap is at most $G$. The prefix incidence
constant is $60$, and its existing scalar rebase passes add only parameter
logarithms.

Refine the common stored density grid for the tail to be at most
$\alpha(\sigma-\rho)/(2Q_{ii})$, and round magnitudes toward zero.
Its potential argument only doubles the tail work. Exact residual
numerators use the fixed input denominators and the fixed density grid;
each comparison uses only the individual degree. The total weighted
absolute coordinate movement is at most
$\mathcal M_\rho(x_{\rm in})/\alpha\leq\theta$, so together with the
prefix's primal mass bound this also bounds all actual tail densities.
No denominator grows with the number of tail steps. Final clipping uses
the fixed input denominator of $\epsppr$, so all integers have the same
type of logarithmic bit bound as in \cref{sec:revisit-rounding}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{An optional observable early handoff.}
At any fully charged prefix checkpoint one may compute
$\mathcal M_\rho$ directly and hand off when it is at most
$\alpha\theta$, without waiting for the conservative objective bound.
For the orthant prefix, use only $O(\log(1/\epsppr)+\log(1/\alpha))$
checkpoints, such as geometric regularizer levels and frozen blocks of
$1/\theta$ steps. Repeated historical materializations then add only
logarithmic overhead. For an old AESP prefix, the same signed-tail lemma
gives the instance-wise bound
\[
 B_J+\frac{Q_{ii}\mathcal M_\rho(x_J)}
                 {\alpha^2(\sigma-\rho)},
\]
plus explicitly charged initialization and output. Proving that unchanged
AESP reaches this threshold with accelerated local prefix work remains
\textbf{Open}. This is a falsifiable alternative handoff target, not a
proof of its original confinement quantity $\Lambda_J$.

In particular, a signed composite-AESP endpoint at regularizer $r_*$ with
$F_{r_*}$ gap at most $G$ qualifies directly by
\cref{lem:revisit-gap-to-excess}. It needs no lower retraction before the
coordinate tail. Although such a tail may contain negative coordinates,
the residual certificate and final clipping proof above still apply and
give the same nonnegative, lower-RPPR output. This removes a handoff
requirement; it does not establish locality of the preceding AESP stages.
