\section{Orthant projection and a regularizer that changes every step}
Write $\vomega=\mD^{1/2}\one$, so $\mQ\vomega=\alpha\vomega$.
For $\lambda>0$ put
\[
 J_\lambda(\vx)=f(\vx)+\lambda\vomega^\trans\vx.
\]
On the nonnegative orthant this equals $F_r$ when $\lambda=\alpha r$.
Choose a dyadic $\theta\in(0,1/2]$ by halving $1/2$ until
$\theta^2\leq\alpha$, and set
\begin{equation}
 \mu=\theta^2,\qquad \chi=1-\theta,\qquad
 \eta=1-\theta/2,\qquad \alpha/4\leq\mu\leq\alpha.
 \label{eq:revisit-parameters}
\end{equation}
Starting with $\vx_0=\vz_0=\vzero$, the recurrence is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \vy_k&=(\vx_k+\theta\vz_k)/(1+\theta),\\
 \vq_k&=\chi\vz_k+\theta\vy_k
          -(\mQ\vy_k-\vb+\lambda_k\vomega)/\theta,\\
 \vz_{k+1}&=[\vq_k]_+,\\
 \vx_{k+1}&=\chi\vx_k+\theta\vz_{k+1}.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:revisit-recurrence}
\end{equation}
Thus both actual vectors are nonnegative. The primal support is nested
within a run, but it is not assumed to lie in any RPPR optimal support.
The projection is on the kinetic vector $\vz$; it is not the ordinary
FISTA proximal update on the primal iterate.

\begin{proposition}[Equivalent accelerated step with a retention floor]
\label{prop:revisit-floor-form}
Set $\vx_{-1}=\vx_0=\vzero$ and
$\beta=\chi/(1+\theta)$. The primal trajectory is exactly
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \vy_k&=\vx_k+\beta(\vx_k-\vx_{k-1}),\\
 \vx_{k+1}&=\max\{\chi\vx_k,
              (\mI-\mQ)\vy_k+\vb-\lambda_k\vomega\}.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:revisit-floor-form}
\end{equation}
The maximum is coordinatewise. Its kinetic vector is recovered by
$\theta\vz_{k+1}=\vx_{k+1}-\chi\vx_k\geq\vzero$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Use $\theta\vz_k=\vx_k-\chi\vx_{k-1}$ in the first line of
\cref{eq:revisit-recurrence}. Also
$\chi\vx_k+\theta\vq_k
 =\vy_k-(\mQ\vy_k-\vb+\lambda_k\vomega)$.
Adding $\chi\vx_k$ to $\theta[\vq_k]_+$ is the displayed maximum.
\end{proof}

This form locates the change relative to standard projected acceleration:
the lower obstacle is $\chi\vx_k$ rather than zero. The positive increments
above this common decay floor are precisely the coordinates charged by
the kinetic work theorem. Implementing the formula with full historical
primal scans would lose that local work guarantee.

\begin{lemma}[Orthant sector in the PageRank metric]
\label{lem:revisit-orthant}
For any $\vh\geq\vzero$, let $\vt=\mQ^{-1}\vh$. For any $\vq$, set
$\vp=[\vq]_+$ and $\vn=\vq-\vp$. Then
\[
 (\vp-\vt)^\trans\mQ\vn=(\mQ\vp-\vh)^\trans\vn\geq0.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $n_i\ne0$, then $n_i<0$ and $p_i=0$. All other coordinates of
$\vp$ are nonnegative and all off-diagonal entries of $\mQ$ are
nonpositive, so $(\mQ\vp-\vh)_i\leq0$. Every summand is nonnegative.
\end{proof}

The imported accelerated comparison identity says the following.
For a one-smooth, $\mu$-strongly convex quadratic $\varphi$ in a Hilbert
metric, take the first two and last lines of
\cref{eq:revisit-recurrence}, with the gradient of $\varphi$ in that metric,
and write $\vp$ for the next kinetic vector. If
$\langle\vp-\vu,\vq-\vp\rangle\geq0$, then
\begin{equation}
 \varphi(\vx^+)-\varphi(\vu)+\frac\mu2\|\vp-\vu\|^2
 \leq\chi\left(\varphi(\vx)-\varphi(\vu)
                          +\frac\mu2\|\vz-\vu\|^2\right).
 \label{eq:revisit-imported-comparison}
\end{equation}
The comparison vector need not minimize $\varphi$. In particular the
quantity on the right is allowed to be negative. The omitted additional
term in the source identity is nonpositive. Its assumptions hold in the
Euclidean metric for $J_\lambda$ and in the $\mQ$ metric for the
auxiliary quadratic below; the orthant sector supplies the latter case.

For the rest of this note, the analytical comparator is fixed:
$\vt:=\vx_0^*=\mQ^{-1}\vb$. In particular,
$\vomega^\trans\vt=1$ and $\vt\geq\vzero$.
Define
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal A_\lambda(\vx)
   &:=\tfrac12\|\mQ\vx-\vb\|_2^2
                           +\alpha\lambda\vomega^\trans\vx,\\
 B_k&:=\mathcal A_{\lambda_k}(\vx_k)
       -\mathcal A_{\lambda_k}(\vt)
                       +\tfrac\mu2\|\vz_k-\vt\|_{\mQ}^2.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:revisit-bank}
\end{equation}
Its $\mQ$-metric gradient is precisely
$\mQ\vx-\vb+\lambda\vomega$, because
$\mQ^{-1}\vomega=\vomega/\alpha$. Its metric Hessian is $\mQ$.

\begin{theorem}[Cooling bank without resets]
\label{thm:revisit-cooling-bank}
Let $\lambda_0=\alpha/d_v$ and let
$\eta\lambda_k\leq\lambda_{k+1}\leq\lambda_k$. Then the actual recurrence
satisfies, for every $k$,
\begin{align}
 B_{k+1}&\leq\chi B_k+\alpha(\lambda_k-\lambda_{k+1}),
                 &B_k&\leq\alpha\lambda_k,
                 \label{eq:revisit-cooling-bank}\\
 \|\mQ\vx_k-\vb\|_2^2
   +\mu\|\vz_k-\vt\|_{\mQ}^2&\leq4\alpha\lambda_k,
                 &\vomega^\trans\vx_k&\leq2.
                 \label{eq:revisit-response-mass}
\end{align}
No box, mass constraint, intermediate repair, or support confinement is
assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply \cref{eq:revisit-imported-comparison} with the current $\lambda_k$,
the $\mQ$ metric, comparator $\vt$, and
\cref{lem:revisit-orthant}. Changing the parameter afterward changes the
bank by exactly
\[
 \alpha(\lambda_{k+1}-\lambda_k)
                     (\vomega^\trans\vx_{k+1}-1)
 \leq\alpha(\lambda_k-\lambda_{k+1}),
\]
since the new primal vector is nonnegative. This proves the recursion.
At initialization,
\[
 B_0=\tfrac12\|\vb\|_2^2-\alpha\lambda_0
                                  +\tfrac\mu2\vt^\trans\mQ\vt\leq0,
\]
because $\|\vb\|_2^2=\alpha^2/d_v=\alpha\lambda_0$ and
$\mu\vb^\trans\mQ^{-1}\vb\leq\|\vb\|_2^2$.
If $B_k\leq\alpha\lambda_k$, the recursion gives
\[
 B_{k+1}\leq\alpha\{(1+\chi)\lambda_k-\lambda_{k+1}\}
              \leq\alpha\lambda_{k+1},
\]
since $1+\chi=2\eta$. Finally
$\mathcal A_{\lambda_k}(\vt)=\alpha\lambda_k$.
All three terms in $B_k+\alpha\lambda_k$ are nonnegative, so dropping
either the mass term or the other two terms proves
\cref{eq:revisit-response-mass}.
\end{proof}

For the actual schedule put $r_k=\lambda_k/\alpha$ and
$\vu_k=\vx_{r_k}^*$. These comparison optima are never computed.
The RPPR residual $\vb-\mQ\vu_k$ is nonnegative, bounded above by
$\lambda_k\vomega$, and has weighted mass at most $\alpha$.
Its squared Euclidean norm is consequently at most $\alpha\lambda_k$.
The squared triangle inequality gives
\begin{equation}
 \|\mQ(\vx_k-\vu_k)\|_2^2\leq10\alpha\lambda_k.
 \label{eq:revisit-optimum-response}
\end{equation}
