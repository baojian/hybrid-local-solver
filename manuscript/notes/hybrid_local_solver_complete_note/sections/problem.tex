Let $G=(V,E)$ be an undirected graph with adjacency matrix $A$ and degree
matrix $D$. Define
\[
Q=\frac{1+\alpha}{2}I-\frac{1-\alpha}{2}D^{-1/2}AD^{-1/2}.
\]
The symmetric PageRank quadratic is
\[
f(x)=\frac12x^TQx-b^Tx,
\qquad b=\alpha D^{-1/2}s.
\]
The minimizer gives the personalized PageRank vector through
$\pi=D^{1/2}x^\star$.

The local work model charges a coordinate update at vertex $u$ by $d_u$ and
parallel active-set updates by $\vol(S)=\sum_{u\in S}d_u$.
