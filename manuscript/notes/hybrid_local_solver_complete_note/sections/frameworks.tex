The note studies two complementary frameworks.

\paragraph{Locally evolving-set methods.}
Local iterative solvers maintain an active set and exploit graph locality. Their
cost is controlled by active volume and residual progress.

\paragraph{AESP.}
AESP applies Catalyst-style acceleration outside local inner solves. The shifted
systems have improved conditioning and provide accelerated outer convergence.

The empirical motivation is that early AESP iterations often reduce error
rapidly before local volume becomes expensive. This motivates switching to a
monotone local tail.
