The central open problem is proving a graph-independent early AESP locality theorem.

Current rigorous routes are:

\begin{enumerate}
\item prove an early locality bound for AESP;
\item develop a confinement theorem inspired by $\ell_1$-regularized PageRank;
\item use cumulative boundary charging rather than uniform support bounds;
\item design safeguarded accelerated local tails.
\end{enumerate}

The desired theorem
\[
\widetilde{\mathcal O}(1/(\sqrt\alpha\eps))
\]
remains a research target rather than an established result.
