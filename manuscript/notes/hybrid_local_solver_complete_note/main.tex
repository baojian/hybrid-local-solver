\title{Acceleration versus Locality in $\ell_1$-Regularized\\
Personalized PageRank\\[0.3em]
\large Proof Attempts, Obstructions, Lower Bounds, and Tree Waves}
\author{Baojian Zhou}
\date{August 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
This note reconstructs the full research path toward a hybrid local solver
with degree-weighted work
$\softO(1/(\rho\sqrt{\alpha}))$ for $\ell_1$-regularized personalized
PageRank.  A slightly over-regularized accelerated warmup followed by a
one-sided local Gauss--Seidel tail gives the desired tail complexity.  The
difficulty is to accelerate the warmup without allowing momentum to destroy
locality.  We prove that a matched generalized-Bregman proximal subproblem is
individually $1/\rho$-local, but also prove an exact linear face-change defect
that prevents uniform exponent-two Bregman acceleration.  Ordinary Euclidean
proximal centers preserve locality when they are lower subsolutions, but
unrestricted extrapolation introduces a cumulative deactivation flux.  We
derive an exact active-volume inequality in terms of this flux.  A proposed
graph-uniform flux bound is then stress-tested on finite rooted trees: an
exact radial obstacle recurrence produces outward traveling shells and
rapidly growing normalized flux.  A period-11 growing wave is proved for the
outward limiting recurrence; finite-tree computations provide reproducible
certificates of the same mechanism.  Separately, we prove a matching
$\Omega(1/(\rho\sqrt{\alpha}))$ lower bound for persistent-support one-hop
local methods.  The resulting conclusion is precise: the monotone local tail
and several conditional hybrid theorems survive, while unconditional locality
of classical inertial warmup does not.  Safeguarded centers, wave-triggered
restarts, or a narrower oracle are required for the next complete theorem.
\end{abstract}
