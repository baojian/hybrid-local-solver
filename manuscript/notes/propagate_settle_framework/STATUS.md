## Exact question and contract

- **Question:** Can spread-then-settle solvers report every finite-band boundary
  crossing on nonequitable cyclic cores without repeated full-boundary work?
- **Model:** For the shared SPD Stieltjes PageRank matrix `Q`, a fixed load `ell`, and nested regions `U`, exact settlement is the Dirichlet map `E_U^ell`. The RPPR specialization uses `ell_rho = b - alpha rho D^(1/2) 1` and the exact support-safe boundary gate; no general `rho`--`eps_ppr` identification is assumed (`sec:scope`, `sec:rppr-gate`).
- **Accuracy namespace:** Exact settlement and exact RPPR KKT signs are the strong model. The finite-resolution model uses demand error `alpha tau_gate / 4`, final solve error `tau_sol`, and proves degree-normalized PPR error at most `rho + tau_gate + tau_sol` (`thm:finite-resolution-continuation`).
- **Access and charged work:** Adjacency scans and row operations are charged by `cvol(U) := sum_{u in U}(1+d_u)`, equivalent within factor two to degree volume. With bounded scans and a supplied width-`w` ordering, fresh settlement costs `O((w+1)^2 R_set cvol(U_K))`, where `R_set = sum_k cvol(U_k)/cvol(U_K)` (`thm:propagate-settle-work`, `thm:rppr-revisit-bound`). All exact cyclic-trace audits use the full eleven-coordinate vector `(C_adj,R_adj,R_int,C_pre,C_ctl,C_rec,C_resp,M_pers,M_tmp,C_mat,C_emit)` and preserve every prefix charge; fresh row validation and reuse of already charged stored records have separate vectors (`rem:notched-sun-rppr-trace-ledger`, `rem:matched-report-sun-trace-ledger`, `rem:active-relay-matched-sun-trace-ledger`, `rem:petal-cycle-relay-trace-ledger`, `rem:double-cycle-feed-trace-ledger`). For either fixed anchor or relay seed, the complete prefix through first report, termination, or absent initialization is still paid; only the constant-radius proof audit has the local `O(1)` fresh/stored vectors in `eq:double-cycle-anchor-audit-vectors` and `eq:double-cycle-relay-audit-vectors`.
- **Intended result:** Construct a certified finite-band reporter with `C_resp^(alpha tau) = O_tilde((1+cvol(S*(rho)))/sqrt(alpha))`; setting `rho = tau_gate = tau_sol = eps_ppr/3` would yield `O_tilde(1/(eps_ppr sqrt(alpha)))` work (`prob:finite-resolution-response`).
