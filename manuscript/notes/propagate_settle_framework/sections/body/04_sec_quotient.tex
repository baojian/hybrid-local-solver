\section{The propagate--settle quotient principle}
\label{sec:quotient}

Let
\begin{equation}
 U_0\subseteq U_1\subseteq\cdots\subseteq U_K
 \label{eq:nested-regions}
\end{equation}
be regions discovered online.  Starting from $x^{-1}$, a general exact
propagate--settle process forms an arbitrary provisional point $y^k$ with
\begin{equation}
 \supp(y^k-x^{k-1})\subseteq U_k,
 \qquad
 x^k:=\mathcal E_{U_k}^\ell(y^k).
 \label{eq:propagate-settle-epoch}
\end{equation}
The provisional update may include many coordinate pushes and any residual
split used only for scheduling.

\begin{theorem}[Region-sequence quotient]
\label{thm:region-sequence-quotient}
Every process satisfying
\cref{eq:nested-regions,eq:propagate-settle-epoch} obeys
\begin{equation}
 x^k=\mathcal E_{U_k}^\ell(x^{-1})
 \qquad(0\leq k\leq K).
 \label{eq:canonical-settled-trajectory}
\end{equation}
Consequently, two exact propagate--settle algorithms with the same initial
outside state and the same region sequence have identical settled iterates
and identical post-settlement boundary residuals.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Induct on $k$.  The update from $x^{k-1}$ to $y^k$ is supported in $U_k$, so
trajectory erasure gives
$x^k=\mathcal E_{U_k}^\ell(x^{k-1})$.  For $k=0$, $x^{-1}$ and $x^0$ agree
outside $U_0$, giving the display.  For $k>0$, the induction hypothesis and
nested absorption give
\[
 x^k
 =\mathcal E_{U_k}^\ell
  \mathcal E_{U_{k-1}}^\ell(x^{-1})
 =\mathcal E_{U_k}^\ell(x^{-1}).
\]
The boundary residual is a deterministic function of the settled point.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Where acceleration can enter]
\label{cor:discovery-only-acceleration}
For an exactly settled algorithm, changing $\omega$, ranking, batching, or
the fresh/debt representation while preserving the same discovered regions
cannot improve the settled objective values or terminal point.  A genuine
gain must change at least one of:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=*]
 \item which regions are discovered;
 \item how soon they are discovered;
 \item the charged work of discovering them; or
 \item the accuracy and work of an inexact settlement.
\end{enumerate}
\end{corollary}

This corollary explains both the empirical success and the theoretical
limit of two rungs.  The over-relaxed phase is useful only because it changes
the frontier seen before delivery.  If delivery were placed immediately
after every spread update on the same coordinate or fixed region, the
absorption law would reduce the method to ordinary exact updates.
