\section{Typed propagation and debt aggregation}
\label{sec:typed-propagation}

An implementation can realize one propagation epoch with three logical
states: unseen, forward-fresh, and processed.  An arrival from processed to
unseen may expose new frontier; an arrival into a processed coordinate is
stored as backward debt; and non-tree traffic is assigned to the current
cyclic settlement block.  These labels affect scheduling, not the residual
identity.

\begin{lemma}[One-scan causal epoch]
\label{lem:one-scan-causal-epoch}
Suppose an epoch discovers $U$ and satisfies:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=*]
 \item each adjacency list in $U$ is scanned at most $C$ times during
 propagation;
 \item every deferred packet is added algebraically to the net residual on
 $U\cup\partial U$ rather than executed as a separate row operation; and
 \item settlement is performed once on the net interior residual.
\end{enumerate}
Then propagation costs $O(C\vol(U))$.  If $G[U]$ is a forest, propagation
plus exact settlement and boundary update cost $O((C+1)\vol(U))$; under a
width-$w$ ordering the cost is
$O((C+(w+1)^2)\vol(U))$.  The bound is independent of the number of forward
and backward causal paths contributing to a residual entry.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The first claim is direct adjacency-list accounting.  Residual updates are
linear, so accumulating packets and applying their sum produces the same
right-hand side as applying them separately.  One forest elimination scans
each induced and boundary edge only constantly many times.  General sparse
elimination costs $O(\vol(U)+w^2|U|)$, which is
$O((w+1)^2\vol(U))$ because the graph has no isolated vertices.
\end{proof}

The lemma closes the \emph{accounting} half of the causal idea.  The remaining
mathematical obligation is threshold progress: the labels must discover a
region whose exact settlement either certifies the current target or exposes
the next admissible frontier without rescanning processed adjacency lists.
On trees and bounded articulation blocks, directed Schur responses provide
that mechanism.  In a thick cyclic core, the corresponding compressed
interface is still open.
