\section{The algorithm}
\label{sec:note-algorithm}
\subsection{A continuation stage}
At regularization $r$, maintain a sparse baseline and diffuse source:
\[
 \bm{0}\le\overline{\bm{x}}\le\bm{x}_r^*,\qquad
 \bm{h}=\bm{b}-\bm{Q}\overline{\bm{x}},\qquad
 \bm{0}\le\bm{h}\le4\alpha r\bm{w}.
\]
Set $\lambda_r=\alpha r$. The unknown correction
$\bm{\xi}^*=\bm{x}_r^*-\overline{\bm{x}}$ belongs to
\[
 \mathcal K_r=\{\bm{z}:\bm{0}\le\bm{z}\le4r\bm{w},\quad
                       \bm{w}^{\mathsf T}\bm{z}\le1\}.
\]
This follows from inverse positivity, $\bm{Q}\bm{w}=\alpha\bm{w}$,
and the residual source bound. The analytical inverse is not evaluated.
Choose a dyadic $\theta$ by halving $1/2$ until $\theta^2\le\alpha$;
then $\sqrt\alpha/2<\theta\le\sqrt\alpha$. Let $a=1-\theta$.
Starting with $\bm{\xi}_0=\bm{z}_0=\bm{0}$, repeat
\begin{align}
 \bm{y}_k&=\frac{\bm{\xi}_k+\theta\bm{z}_k}{1+\theta},\nonumber\\
 \bm{q}_k&=a\bm{z}_k+\theta\bm{y}_k
       -\frac{\bm{Q}\bm{y}_k-\bm{h}+\lambda_r\bm{w}}{\theta},\nonumber\\
 \bm{z}_{k+1}&=\operatorname{Proj}_{\mathcal K_r}(\bm{q}_k),
       \qquad
 \bm{\xi}_{k+1}=a\bm{\xi}_k+\theta\bm{z}_{k+1}.
 \label{eq:note-update}
\end{align}
The projection is Euclidean. Its densities have the explicit form
\[
 \frac{z_{k+1,i}}{w_i}
 =\min\{4r,(q_{k,i}/w_i-\eta)_+\},\qquad \eta\ge0.
\]
Use $\eta=0$ when the mass cap is satisfied; otherwise find the common
threshold giving mass one. Deterministic balanced trees store ordered keys
and degree-weighted sums, locate the appropriate finite breakpoint interval,
and solve its affine mass equation exactly.

\subsection{Outer schedule and certified repair}
Initialize $r_0=1/d_v$ and $\overline{\bm{x}}=\bm{0}$.
For $j=1,2,\ldots$, set $r_j=\max\{\rho,r_0/2^j\}$.
Choose $\delta_j=\alpha r_j/2$ at intermediate stages; at the final stage,
halve that value until $\delta_j^2\le\epsobj\rho/2$.
Set $\tau_j=\alpha^3\delta_j^2/2$ and run
\eqref{eq:note-update} until the maintained scalar $a^k\le\tau_j$.
Materialize $\widetilde{\bm{x}}=\overline{\bm{x}}+\bm{\xi}_k$, then set
\[
 \overline{\bm{x}}^{+}=[\widetilde{\bm{x}}-\delta_j\bm{w}]_+.
\]
The repair lemma gives safety, a nonnegative source bounded by
$2\alpha r_j\bm{w}$, and objective error at most $2\delta_j^2/r_j$.
Because $r_{j+1}\ge r_j/2$, the next stage's source bound follows.
Stop after the repaired stage at $r_j=\rho$.

\paragraph{Sparse execution.}
Shared scaling handles the decay of old correction coordinates. Cached
responses change through the emitted momentum coordinates and their neighbors.
An unexposed vertex has negative raw projection value and cannot be selected.
An inactive boundary record does not cause a recursive scan of its row.
All first and repeated scans, source refreshes, threshold queries, and discarded
state are charged. A dense execution of \eqref{eq:note-update} has the same
optimization recurrence but does not establish the local-work theorem.

\paragraph{Implemented refinements.}
The package uses controlled dyadic rounding, deterministic AVL maps, a tighter
source-energy schedule, and certified stopping checks with a deterministic
fallback horizon. Its terminal projected-gradient step permits a less
conservative stage tolerance. Optional adaptive regularization and mass-scaled
precision preserve their separately proved guarantees; neither is needed for
the exact-real recurrence above.
