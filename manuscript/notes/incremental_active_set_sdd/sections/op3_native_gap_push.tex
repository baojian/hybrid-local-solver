\section{An implemented local producer with a weaker uniform bound}
\label{sec:op3-native-gap-push}

This section closes the native-producer interface at a deliberately weaker
rate. It supplies an executable, source-free reference algorithm, not the
conjectured inverse-accuracy work. In particular, its cubic bound does not
improve the source's nearly quadratic active-set bound in \cref{sec:source}.

\begin{theorem}[Paid gap-threshold local push; \statusproved]
\label{thm:op3-native-gap-push}
On the original connected simple unweighted graph with at least two
vertices, let $0<a\leq1$, $0<e<1$, $\gamma=(1-a)/(1+a)$,
$\bar a=1-\gamma$, and $\lambda=e/2$. There is a deterministic local
exact-real word algorithm returning $x\geq0$ such that
\[
 0\leq r=e_v-(D-\gamma A)x\leq e d,
 \qquad \operatorname{vol}(\operatorname{supp}x)<2/e.
\]
Its total work and allocated words are
$O(1/(\bar a e)+e^{-2})$. Original rows are read once, only when their
vertex first becomes positive; the total original row volume is below
$2/e$. Every discovered degree is queried once. Live graph records,
cached original incidences and output occupy $O(1+e^{-1})$ words.
Neither ambient size nor a supplied support is needed. The last space
claim concerns graph state; it is not a count of Python scalar objects or
a bit-complexity assertion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Initialize $x=0$, $r=e_v$. Keep every discovered label in a sorted
dynamic pointer array, with a stable record containing its original degree,
potential, residual, activation flag, queue flag and next pointer. A record
is queued exactly when $r_i>e d_i$. On removing such a record, make the
update
\[
 h=(r_i-\lambda d_i)/d_i>e/2,\qquad
 x_i\gets x_i+h,\qquad r_i\gets\lambda d_i,\qquad
 r_j\gets r_j+\gamma h\quad(j\sim i).
\]
If this is its first update, first read and cache its original row as
stable pointers to neighbor records, creating missing records and querying
their degrees. Enqueue a neighbor when it crosses the threshold, unless
already queued. An intrusive FIFO uses these existing record fields.

The displayed changes preserve the original residual identity and
nonnegativity. Every previously pushed coordinate has residual at least
$\lambda d_i$ forever: its own push resets to that value, and other
pushes can only increase it. The total mass satisfies
\[
 m:=\sum_i r_i=1-\bar a\sum_i d_i x_i\leq1;
 \qquad \Delta m=-\bar a d_i h<-\bar a(e/2)d_i.
\]
Thus the sum $P$ of the degrees of all pushed rows, including repeated
updates, is below $2/(\bar a e)$. This proves finite termination because
all original degrees are at least one. If $S$ is the set already pushed,
then $\lambda\operatorname{vol}(S)\leq m<1$ after its first push.
Before a first activation, the newly legal residual exceeds
$e d_i>\lambda d_i$, so the old active residuals and that new residual
already imply $\lambda\operatorname{vol}(S\cup\{i\})<m\leq1$.
This bound holds \emph{before} allocating a degree-sized row buffer.
Hence a huge outside degree cannot trigger a large unbudgeted row read.

Every nonzero residual is at the seed or a neighbor of a pushed vertex.
All such labels have records. Residual increases update the queue, and
the just-pushed coordinate is below threshold. A queued coordinate cannot
fall below threshold before its own turn. Therefore an empty queue
certifies the desired inequalities on all vertices, including undiscovered
vertices whose residual is zero. The positive support is exactly $S$.

For an additional comparison used only by the audit, let $u$ be the
exact obstacle with load $b=e_v-\lambda d$. Inductively $x\leq u$.
Indeed the new value at a pushed coordinate is
$(b_i+\gamma\sum_{j\sim i}x_j)/d_i$, which is at most $u_i$ by
the KKT inequality $d_i u_i\geq b_i+\gamma\sum_{j\sim i}u_j$.
The algorithm never computes this comparison vector.

It remains to charge the representation. The number $R$ of discovered
labels is at most $1+\operatorname{vol}(S)$. Binary lookup of each
first-read adjacency entry costs $O(\log(2+R))$; there are
$\operatorname{vol}(S)$ such entries. Array capacities double, with
total initialization and copying $O(R)$ and total reserved slots $O(R)$
even counting old arrays. Sorted insertions shift at most $O(R^2)$
pointers in total. Records do not move, so all repeated neighbor updates
use their cached pointers in constant work and require no dictionary
lookup. Queue operations, arithmetic and cached row visits cost $O(P)$.
The final sorted-record scan and output cost $O(R)$.
These bounds give $O(1/(\bar a e)+e^{-2})$ work. Graph-state buffers
and stable records reserve $O(R+\operatorname{vol}(S))$ words; counting
constant-size scalar temporaries conservatively gives total allocated
words $O(1/(\bar a e)+e^{-2})$ as well. All costs hold deterministically.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A source-free alpha-uniform local reference; \statusproved]
\label{cor:op3-cubic-local-reference}
For every original target $0<\alpha\leq1$ and $0<\epsappr<1$, a
deterministic local algorithm returns original ACL output with support
volume below $4/\epsappr$ in $O(\epsappr^{-3})$ exact-real word work
and total allocated words. It reads original rows of total volume below
$4/\epsappr$, once per row, with $O(1+\epsappr^{-1})$ live graph state.
The bound has no dependence on target teleportation or ambient size.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Use \cref{thm:op3-native-gap-push} at
\[
 e=\epsappr/2,\quad B=4/\epsappr,\quad
 a_0=\frac{\epsappr^2}{16+\epsappr^2},\quad
 a_{\rm eff}=\max\{\alpha,a_0\}.
\]
These parameters satisfy the native premises of
\cref{thm:op3-arithmetic-alpha-floor}. The producer is deterministic,
so no probability budget is needed. Moreover
\[
 1-\gamma_{\rm eff}\geq1-\gamma_0
 =\frac{\epsappr^2}{8+\epsappr^2}\geq\epsappr^2/9,
 \qquad P<36\epsappr^{-3}.
\]
The wrapper costs $O((1+B)\log(2+B))$, returns the same potential on
proper support and adds one constant on full support. All rows and
degrees it needs are already cached, so it issues no additional original
graph queries. Its paid maps and temporary arrays use $O(1+B)$ live
words. Finally emit $(1-\gamma_\alpha)Dx$. The native obstacle load
here is $\epsappr/4$; no containment in the different
$\epsappr/2$ obstacle is claimed.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact implementation audit.}
\path{native_gap_push.py} implements the stable map, queue, first-activation
volume check, cached updates and arithmetic wrapper. The full audit checks
826 complete original ACL outputs and 3,848 intermediate states, with
12,452 original residual rows, 3,022 pushes and 3,773 degree-weighted
updates. Dense obstacles and full-graph residuals are validators only.
Three private hubs of degree through $2^{1024}$ are queried only for
their degree; only the seed leaf row is read. The exact symbolic update
bound is checked for 1,024 dyadic accuracies. Results, parameter records
and source/backend hashes are in \path{NATIVE_GAP_PUSH_AUDIT.json}.
Its two-vertex stress case still needs 1,744 native pushes at
$\epsappr=1/8$: avoiding the slow full-support constant mode during
the native computation is a concrete next improvement. General OP3 and
an inverse-linear arbitrary-graph producer remain \statusopen.
