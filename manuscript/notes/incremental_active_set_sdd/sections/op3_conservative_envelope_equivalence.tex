\section{A conservative formulation of the algorithmic ACL target}
\label{sec:op3-conservative-envelope-equivalence}

This section isolates a remaining discovery problem without a teleportation
parameter. The reductions concern the fully charged ACL output target
with the standard original support-volume guarantee. They do not assert
reuse of the particular exact nested systems in the literal OP3 question.

\begin{lemma}[Proper conservative obstacle and a quantitative comparison;
\statusproved]
\label{lem:op3-conservative-torsion-comparison}
Let $0<e<1$ and $\operatorname{vol}(G)>4/e$. Set
$\lambda=e/2$, $\delta=e/8$, $b=e_v-\lambda d$, and let $u_0$ be
the nonnegative obstacle minimizer for $L=D-A$ with load $b$.
This minimizer exists uniquely, has proper support $S_0$, and satisfies
\[
 V_0:=\operatorname{vol}(S_0)<2/e,\qquad
 H_0:=\max_i u_{0,i}\leq|S_0|\leq V_0.
\]
For $0<t\leq1$, let $u_t$ be the obstacle for
$M_t=L+tA=D-(1-t)A$ with the same load. Then $0\leq u_t\leq u_0$.
If $S_0$ is nonempty and $w$ solves
$L_{S_0S_0}w_{S_0}=d_{S_0}$ with zero extension, then
\[
 \|w\|_\infty\leq V_0|S_0|\leq V_0^2,
 \qquad
 0\leq u_0-u_t\leq tH_0w,
 \qquad
 \|u_0-u_t\|_\infty<8t/e^3.
\]
The final inequality also holds when $S_0$ is empty. In particular,
at $t=e^4/128$, every $u_{0,i}>\delta$ has $u_{t,i}>0$.
The support and torsion solve are comparison objects, not algorithm inputs.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Here $\lambda\operatorname{vol}(G)>2$, so
\cref{prop:op3-conservative-sandpile-mapping} gives existence and
uniqueness. Full-support stationarity would imply
$0=\one^TLu_0=\one^Tb<0$, which is impossible. The conservative
original residual is nonnegative: positive-coordinate KKT gives
$r_i=\lambda d_i$, and a zero coordinate gives
$r_i=(e_v)_i+\sum_{j\sim i}u_{0,j}\geq0$. Summing residuals gives
one. If $S_0$ is nonempty, connectedness supplies a positive cut
residual outside it, so $\lambda V_0<1$. The conservative proper-support
maximum bound gives $H_0\leq|S_0|$. Empty support satisfies both bounds.

The same-load maximum principle applies to the positive-target operator:
$M_tu_0=Lu_0+tAu_0\geq b$. A positive maximum of $u_t-u_0$ would
have $u_{t,i}>0$ and $(M_t(u_t-u_0))_i\leq0$, contradicting
strict diagonal dominance by $td_i$. Thus $u_t\leq u_0$.

The original proper Dirichlet matrix $L_{S_0S_0}$ is positive definite
and an M-matrix. Indeed every component of $S_0$ has a cut edge, and
the zero-extended Laplacian energy vanishes only at zero. Its inverse
is nonnegative. For a strict positive superlevel $H$ of its torsion
solution, $H\subseteq S_0$, and summing its rows gives
\[
 \sum_{i\in H,\ j\notin H,\ j\sim i}(w_i-w_j)
 =\sum_{i\in H}d_i\leq V_0.
\]
Every cut term is positive, so every edge difference is at most $V_0$.
Follow a simple path from a maximum to the first zero coordinate;
it has at most $|S_0|$ edges. This proves the torsion bound.

On $S_0$ the conservative obstacle is stationary, while positive-target
KKT holds as an inequality even where $u_t$ vanishes. Therefore
\[
 L(u_0-u_t)\leq tAu_t\leq tH_0d\quad\hbox{on }S_0.
\]
Both obstacles vanish outside $S_0$. Multiplying by the positive
Dirichlet inverse proves the coordinate comparison. The bounds
$H_0\leq V_0<2/e$ and $\|w\|_\infty\leq V_0^2$ give
$\|u_0-u_t\|_\infty<8t/e^3$. If $u_0=0$, monotonicity gives
$u_t=0$ directly. At $t=e^4/128$ the error is below
$e/16=\delta/2$, proving strict containment of significant coordinates.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Conservative significant-envelope target]
\label{def:op3-conservative-envelope-target}
On an original graph promised to have volume greater than $4/e$, the
input consists of its point seed, $e\in(0,1)$ and failure budget $p$.
For the conservative obstacle at $\lambda=e/2$, return a list $U$
containing $\{i:u_{0,i}>e/8\}$, with original volume at most $C/e$
on success, for an absolute constant $C$. The desired work and total
allocated words on every branch are
\[
 e^{-1}\operatorname{polylog}(2+e^{-1}+p^{-1}),
\]
with success probability at least $1-p$. Graph access, labels, failed
searches and output are charged; no ambient preprocessing is provided.
This target remains \statusopen.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Equivalence of the capped algorithmic targets;
\statusproved]
\label{thm:op3-conservative-envelope-equivalence}
The conservative target in
\cref{def:op3-conservative-envelope-target} is equivalent to the
existence of a graph-uniform local ACL producer with original output
volume $O(1/e)$ on success and all-branch work and total allocated words
\[
 e^{-1}\operatorname{polylog}(2+e^{-1}+\alpha^{-1}+p^{-1}).
\]
The forward reduction in fact removes target $\alpha$ even from the
logarithms, using the source-backed supplied theorem already proved in
this note. The equivalence concerns these explicit resource and volume
contracts; it is not a proof that either desired producer exists.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First assume the conservative finder. Pay the gate at $B=4/e$.
On its complete branch use \cref{cor:op3-small-whole-graph}.
On its larger-graph branch call the finder with confidence budget $p/2$.
Guard its output length, distinctness and original degree volume before
reading any new row, rejecting a malformed or oversized result. These
guards cost $O((1+e^{-1})\log(2+e^{-1}))$ after a length cap.

On a good finder execution, same-load monotonicity gives
$u_\alpha\leq u_0$ for the original target obstacle. Thus its supplied
set contains every target coordinate above $e/8$, with exactly the
required load $\lambda=e/2$. Apply
\cref{thm:op3-alpha-independent-supplied-envelope} with fresh confidence
budget $p/2$. Its output has original ACL accuracy $e$ and volume
below $2/e$. A union bound gives failure probability at most $p$.
The gate, guards, capped finder and capped supplied computation give
the stated work on bad branches as well. An incorrect small envelope
need not give correct output, but its numerical/degree caps still apply;
that event was already charged to finder failure. The forward bound
contains no target-parameter logarithm.

Conversely, suppose the ACL producer exists with successful output
volume at most $C_A/\eta$ at residual accuracy $\eta$. On a promised
large graph, set
\[
 \lambda=e/2,\qquad t=e^4/128,\qquad a=t/(2-t),
\]
and call that producer at lazy parameter $a$, accuracy $\eta=\lambda$
and failure budget $p$. Its physical potential $x\geq0$ satisfies
$M_tx\geq e_v-\lambda d=b$. If the interface returns probabilities,
convert each emitted coordinate by $x_i=p_i/(td_i)$ using paid original
degree replies. A positive maximum principle proves $x\geq u_t$:
at a hypothetical positive maximum of $u_t-x$, positive-coordinate
KKT for $u_t$ and the supersolution inequality for $x$ contradict
strict diagonal dominance of $M_t$.

\Cref{lem:op3-conservative-torsion-comparison} now implies that
$U=\operatorname{supp}(x)$ contains every conservative coordinate
above $e/8$. Its volume is at most $2C_A/e$. Guard word count, positive
format, distinct labels and original volume as above; the good output
passes. The producer's all-branch work is of the required order because
$\eta^{-1}=2/e$ and $\log(a^{-1})=O(\log(2+e^{-1}))$.
The conversion and output guards add only the same local logarithmic
work. No computation of $u_0$, $S_0$, $u_t$ or the torsion vector was
used by this reduction.
\end{proof}

An advertised high-probability work bound can be capped at its declared
primitive budget, counting cap failure in the confidence split. An
expected-only premise does not automatically give this all-branch,
polylogarithmic-confidence contract. Likewise, output cardinality alone
does not replace the original support-volume premise. These are part
of the precise target being reduced.

\begin{proposition}[The output obstruction persists on arbitrarily large graphs;
\statusproved]
\label{prop:op3-large-ambient-leaf-lower-bound}
For $0<e\leq1/16$, let $m=\lfloor1/(8e)\rfloor$ and attach $m$
degree-one leaves and a path of any length $N>2/e$ to a point-seeded
center. The graph has volume greater than $4/e$.
For every $0<\alpha\leq1/3$, any nonnegative original ACL output of
accuracy $e$ must emit all $m\geq1/(16e)$ leaves. Every conservative
significant envelope at $\lambda=e/2$, $\delta=e/8$ must also contain
these leaves. Thus the inverse-accuracy output scale survives the
small-component gate. The first conclusion is a residual-certificate
lower bound; no unchanged semantic star formula is imported after
adding the path.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The original center degree is $d_c=m+1$, with
$ed_c\leq1/8+e\leq3/16<1/3$. The seed residual upper bound and
nonnegative neighbor potentials imply $x_c\geq1/d_c-e$.
If a leaf were omitted, its residual would be $\gamma x_c$, where
$\gamma=(1-\alpha)/(1+\alpha)\geq1/2$. But
\[
 \gamma(1/d_c-e)>e,
\]
equivalently $ed_c<\gamma/(1+\gamma)$, contradicting that leaf's
original residual bound. The path length never enters these two rows.

The conservative obstacle exists uniquely by the volume premise.
Its seed is positive because $1-\lambda d_c>0$. Seed stationarity
gives $u_{0,c}\geq1/d_c-\lambda$, and the leaf obstacle equation gives
$u_{0,\ell}=\max\{0,u_{0,c}-\lambda\}$. Hence
$u_{0,\ell}\geq1/d_c-e>e/8$. Every leaf is significant.
Finally $m\geq1/(16e)$ in this parameter range. Each emitted label
costs at least one word; a randomized algorithm with constant success
probability has the same lower bound in expectation.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{What is implemented and what remains open.}
\path{conservative_envelope_reduction.py} independently checks
conservative KKT, proper support, torsion, quantitative parameter
comparison and the large-ambient leaf inequalities. Its reverse-direction
test uses the implemented \emph{cubic} ACL producer to obtain an actual
local envelope. Its forward supplied numerical callback is an explicitly
labelled dense validator. Original target residuals and support are then
checked in the full graph. Neither this finite composition nor the
equivalence supplies a fast conservative finder or literal nested reuse.
The new remaining target is the algorithm in
\cref{def:op3-conservative-envelope-target}; source and backend hashes
and all counts are recorded in \path{CONSERVATIVE_ENVELOPE_REDUCTION_AUDIT.json}.

The full run checks 7,299 original target outputs in 7.501 seconds,
including 1,902 conservative comparisons and actual weak-producer
envelopes, 1,237 Dirichlet torsion systems and 5,706 same-load target
comparisons. The forward interface uses 586 explicit dense numerical
validators. The large-ambient leaf bounds are checked at 1,021 dyadic
accuracies, with 3,063 positive-target parameter cases. These are exact
checks of the reduction and its source-interface separation, not timing
evidence for the missing fast finder.
