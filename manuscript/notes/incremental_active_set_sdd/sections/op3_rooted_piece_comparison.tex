\section[Rooted-piece spectral comparison]{A repair using rooted pieces}
\label{sec:op3-rooted-piece-comparison}

\paragraph{Source issue and scope.}
The following discussion concerns the actual definitions in
\citet[Definitions 5.4/5.7 and Lemma 5.9, PDF pp.\ 22--23]{chen2021diffusion},
arXiv:2105.14629v2. Their local stretch sums contributions of all
original edges over an entire forest component. The following elementary
obstruction shows that this particular intermediate claim cannot be
imported literally. It does not refute their final spectral-constructor
theorem or their diffusion result.

\begin{proposition}[Literal component-stretch obstruction; \statusproved]
\label{prop:op3-component-stretch-star}
On a unit star with $m$ leaves, every rooted spanning subforest with
at most $j$ components, $1\leq j\leq m+1$, has maximum component local
stretch at least $m-j+1$ under the source definition. The minimum
possible value is exactly $m-j+1$.
Consequently an $O(m\log n\log\log n/j)$ bound on that quantity,
uniform in $m$ and $j$, is false.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A forest with $h\leq j$ components deletes $h-1$ star edges. The
component containing the center retains $m-h+1\geq m-j+1$ edges.
Each retained edge routes to itself and contributes one to that
component's local stretch. Choosing exactly $j-1$ deletions, the center
as its component root, and each isolated leaf as its own root attains
equality: every deleted edge moves entirely into the core and contributes
zero forest stretch. Put $m=2^{2k}$ and $j=2^k$. Since $n=m+1$ and
$\log n\log\log n=O(k^2)$, the ratio of the lower bound to
$m\log n\log\log n/j$ tends to infinity.
\end{proof}

On this family the original graph is itself a tree and hence an exact
spectral preconditioner. The obstruction is to the intermediate
whole-component quantity. A component can contain many smaller tree
pieces meeting at the same root. We next prove the comparison that is
needed when those pieces have bounded stretch separately.

\paragraph{Rooted pieces.}
Let $G$ be a supplied connected positive-weight graph, and let $F$ be a
rooted spanning subforest with root set $C$. A collection $\mathcal B$
of rooted pieces partitions the edges of $F$ into connected subtrees.
Each piece contains exactly its forest component's root. Distinct pieces
may intersect only at their common root; every nonroot forest vertex
belongs to one piece. Singleton-root components have no nonempty piece.
No restriction on the number of pieces sharing a root is imposed.

Use original conductances $c_e>0$ and resistances $r_e=1/c_e$.
An original edge in $F$ routes to itself. A nonforest edge with endpoints
in the same component routes along their unique forest path. Otherwise
it routes from its first endpoint to its root, across a new core edge,
and from the second root to its endpoint. Denote this route by $P_F(e)$.
It intersects at most two rooted pieces, with a connected path segment
in each. Let $G_C$ aggregate all new core-edge conductances by root pair.

\begin{theorem}[Spectral comparison from rooted-piece loads; \statusproved]
\label{thm:op3-rooted-piece-spectral-comparison}
Suppose $\kappa\geq1$ and every $B\in\mathcal B$ satisfies
\[
 \sum_{e\in E(G)} c_e\,r(P_F(e)\cap B)\leq\kappa.
\]
Let $H_0$ contain the forest edges scaled by $\kappa$, together with
the core graph $G_C$, and set $Q=3H_0$ by scaling all conductances.
Then
\[
 L(G)\preceq L(Q)\preceq21\kappa L(G).
\]
Given the forest, roots and a valid supplied $\kappa$, forming $Q$ costs
$O(n+m\log(2+m))$ exact-real word work and $O(n+m)$ allocated words,
including parallel-edge aggregation and output. This does not construct
the bounded-load forest or sparsify its core.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $\mathcal E_J(\bm x)=\sum_{ab\in E(J)}c_{ab}(x_a-x_b)^2$.
For a forest path segment $P$,
\[
 (x_{\rm start}-x_{\rm end})^2
 \leq r(P)\sum_{f=ab\in P}c_f(x_a-x_b)^2.
\]
Each original edge difference splits into at most two such segments
and one core difference. Applying the three-term square bound and
then summing, the load bound for each edge-disjoint piece gives
\[
 \mathcal E_G(\bm x)
 \leq3\mathcal E_{G_C}(\bm x)+3\kappa\mathcal E_F(\bm x)
 =3\mathcal E_{H_0}(\bm x).
\]
This includes retained tree edges and same-root nonforest edges; their
routes merely use fewer segments.

For the reverse direction, expand every core difference into its
underlying original edge difference and its two endpoint-to-root
differences. Conductance aggregation is an exact sum over those original
edges. The same path inequality and the same piece loads imply
\[
 \mathcal E_{G_C}(\bm x)
 \leq3\mathcal E_G(\bm x)+3\kappa\mathcal E_F(\bm x).
\]
As $F$ is an original subforest and $\kappa\geq1$,
\[
 \mathcal E_{H_0}(\bm x)
 \leq3\mathcal E_G(\bm x)+4\kappa\mathcal E_F(\bm x)
 \leq7\kappa\mathcal E_G(\bm x).
\]
These inequalities hold for every real $\bm x$, including constant
vectors in the common nullspace, and prove the claimed Loewner bounds.

For construction, label forest components and their roots by one DFS.
Scan all original edges once. Retain and scale forest edges; move each
cross-root edge into a root-pair record and discard other nonforest
edges. A comparison sort and one merge scan aggregate the root pairs.
All input scans, map arrays, edge records, sorting buffers, copies and
emitted edges fit the stated work and storage bounds. No route path or
Laplacian matrix is formed by this construction. The supplied load
guarantee is an assumption, not a free numerical certificate.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact audit and the remaining constructor.}
\texttt{rooted\_piece\_spectral\_comparison.py} checks all 31 connected
atlas graphs through five vertices, every spanning forest, every choice
of one root per component, and three positive-weight profiles, including
conductances $2^{-80}$ and $2^{80}$. It passes 19,854 comparisons,
59,562 exact PSD certificates and 149,178 route-piece checks; 26,022
parallel core records are aggregated. The audit also passes 5,116
small-star component certificates and 65 asymptotic parameter cases.
All dense matrices and enumerated path tables are explicitly validators.
They do not implement the missing bounded-load forest construction.

The decomposition and the corrected common-offset routing argument are
now supplied by
\cref{sec:op3-weighted-tree-ownership,sec:op3-weighted-corridor-routing}.
They retain separate rooted pieces even when a forest component contains
several of them. Low-stretch-tree selection and core-sparsifier confidence
still require source composition. The full supplied recurrence remains
\statusconditional, and local OP3 remains \statusopen.
