\section[Computable local gap certificate]{A computable objective-gap certificate with local graph access}
\label{sec:op3-local-gap-certificate}

\paragraph{Status.}
The following \statusproved{} drafts replace the unknown-optimum acceptance
test in \cref{thm:op3-diffusion-accuracy-bridge} by an implemented certificate.
The graph, physical point seed, original degrees and ACL namespace are as
in that theorem. Candidate production remains a separate \statusopen{}
work obligation. Every row of the positive candidate support, including
false positives later clipped away, is charged here.

Put $\mu=\bar\alpha$, $\ell=1+\gamma$ and $g=Mw-b$ for a feasible
candidate $w\geq0$. These are note-scoped curvature and smoothness symbols;
$\mu D\preceq M\preceq\ell D$. Define
\begin{equation}
 C(w):=\sum_i c_i(w),\qquad
 c_i(w)=
 \begin{cases}
  g_i^2/(2\mu d_i),&g_i\leq\mu d_iw_i,\\
  g_iw_i-\mu d_iw_i^2/2,&g_i>\mu d_iw_i.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:op3-local-gap-certificate}
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[Computable gap sandwich; \statusproved]
\label{lem:op3-local-gap-sandwich}
For every feasible $w$, the exact optimum $u$ satisfies
\begin{equation}
 E(w)-E(u)\leq C(w)\leq\frac{\ell}{\mu}\bigl(E(w)-E(u)\bigr).
 \label{eq:op3-gap-sandwich}
\end{equation}
Every summand is nonnegative, and $C(w)=0$ if and only if $w=u$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Minimize the separable quadratic lower model
\[
 E(w)+g^\top(z-w)+\frac\mu2\|z-w\|_D^2,\qquad z\geq0.
\]
Its minimizer is $z_i=\max\{0,w_i-g_i/(\mu d_i)\}$, and its improvement
over $E(w)$ is exactly \cref{eq:op3-local-gap-certificate}. Since this is
a global lower model for $E$, its minimum is at most $E(u)$, giving
the first inequality. In particular, the formula handles positive
gradients on small positive candidate coordinates; replacing them all
by zero would not be a valid gap certificate.

Let $t=\mu/\ell\in(0,1]$ and $p=z-w$. The point $w+tp$ is feasible.
Smoothness and $\ell t=\mu$ give
\[
 E(w+tp)\leq E(w)+t g^\top p+\frac{\ell t^2}{2}\|p\|_D^2
 =E(w)-tC(w).
\]
Optimality of $u$ proves the second inequality. The lower model includes
$z=w$, so $C(w)\geq0$, also clear termwise from the displayed cases.
Strict convexity and the sandwich characterize zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Local candidate acceptance; \statusproved]
\label{thm:op3-local-gap-certificate}
Let $U=\{i:w_i>0\}$ be supplied as a list of distinct labels and positive
values. The value $C(w)$ and the clipped vector
$\widehat u_i=\max\{0,w_i-\delta\}$, $\delta=\epsappr/8$, can be computed
with original degree and adjacency access in
\[
 O\bigl((1+\vol(U))\log(2+\vol(U))\bigr)
\]
exact-real word work and $O(1+\vol(U))$ allocated words. Only rows in
$U$ are read. If
\begin{equation}
 C(w)\leq\frac{\mu\delta^2}{2},
 \label{eq:op3-computable-acceptance}
\end{equation}
the clipped vector satisfies the original ACL certificate and has support
contained in the exact obstacle support.

If $\epsappr d_v<1$, a feasible candidate satisfying
\begin{equation}
 E(w)\leq E(u)/(1+\eta_C),\qquad
 \eta_C=\frac{\bar\alpha^3\delta^2}{1+\gamma},
 \label{eq:op3-source-accuracy-for-certificate}
\end{equation}
is guaranteed to pass \cref{eq:op3-computable-acceptance}.
Only logarithms of $1/\alpha$ and $1/\epsappr$ occur in
$\log(1/\eta_C)$. These statements do not bound the number of candidates
or the sum of their support volumes.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At a zero coordinate outside $U\cup N(U)\cup\{v\}$,
$g_i=\lambda d_i>0$ and $c_i(w)=0$. To compute the other summands, emit
one seed record, one record $(i,w_i,-d_iw_i,d_i)$ per positive candidate
coordinate, and one residual contribution $\gamma w_i$ at label $j$ for
each original incidence $i\sim j$ with $i\in U$. Sort by vertex label
and sum contributions within each group. The resulting residual is
$r_i=\mathbf1_{i=v}-d_iw_i+\gamma\sum_{j\sim i}w_j$, and
$g_i=\lambda d_i-r_i$. Query a group's original degree if it was not
already read as a positive candidate coordinate.

There are at most $1+|U|+\vol(U)$ records and at most that many groups.
A bottom-up merge sort with one reusable buffer charges every comparison
and copy in the stated bound, without a constant-time dictionary assumption
or any ambient preprocessing. Degree queries cost one per distinct group;
adjacency entries cost exactly $\vol(U)$. The grouped certificate and
clipped output also use linear work and space. When $U$ is empty, only
the seed degree is needed. These charges apply to failed certificates too.

The left inequality in \cref{eq:op3-gap-sandwich} and
\cref{lem:op3-diffusion-energy-scale} imply $\|w-u\|_\infty\leq\delta$
under \cref{eq:op3-computable-acceptance}. The clipping argument of
\cref{thm:op3-diffusion-accuracy-bridge} then applies unchanged.
For \cref{eq:op3-source-accuracy-for-certificate}, the right inequality
in the sandwich and $|E(u)|\leq1/(2\bar\alpha)$ give
\[
 C(w)\leq\frac{1+\gamma}{\bar\alpha}
       \frac{\eta_C}{1+\eta_C}|E(u)|
 \leq\frac{\bar\alpha\delta^2}{2}.
\]
The stated logarithmic tolerance follows directly.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Audit and remaining obligation.}
\texttt{local\_gap\_certificate.py} implements the record emission,
buffered merge sort and grouped certificate. A row guard forbids access to
every coordinate outside the positive candidate support. Independent
original matrices check both sides of the gap sandwich, every computed
residual, and each accepted original ACL output. There are 16,506 candidate
checks across 1,233 original graph cases: 12,807 pass, 3,699 correctly
fail the sufficient test, and all 11,574 candidates meeting
\cref{eq:op3-source-accuracy-for-certificate} pass. Four implicit stars,
with up to $10^{30}$ leaves, inspect only one leaf row and two degrees.
The center's huge adjacency list is neither supplied nor read.

The exact record is \texttt{LOCAL\_GAP\_CERTIFICATE\_AUDIT.json}.
Candidates and original exact optima in the audit are reference work.
An arbitrary candidate can have expensive false-positive rows, and a
sequence of individually local scans can have quadratic cumulative volume.
The theorem therefore resolves a certificate interface, while the generic
OP3 producer and cumulative-work questions remain \statusopen.
