\section{Sparse residual epochs and their refresh obstruction}
\label{sec:op3-coarse-residual-epochs}

\paragraph{Status.}
The following identities and family are \statusproved{} as drafts awaiting
independent review. The corresponding residual-epoch state machine is
implemented and exactly audited. They refine the certified coarse solver
but do not remove its rank factor. The obstruction below concerns this
fixed absolute-budget representation, not all OP3 algorithms.

\begin{lemma}[Two-coordinate residual update; \statusproved]
\label{lem:op3-sparse-residual-epoch}
During an epoch with fixed ports and a fixed certified vector $\bm t_P$,
write $\bm g=\bm f-\bm K\bm t_P$. Suppose an ordinary admitted vertex $j$
has unique old active parent $i$, whose old conditional response is
$u_i=\zeta_i+\bm a_i^\top\bm u_P$, with conditional variance $g_{ii}$.
Put $\tau=d_j-\gamma^2g_{ii}$ and $c=-\lambda d_j+\gamma\zeta_i$.
Then
\begin{align}
 \bm K_{\rm new}&=\bm K-\frac{\gamma^2}{\tau}\bm a_i\bm a_i^\top,
 &\bm f_{\rm new}&=\bm f+\frac{\gamma c}{\tau}\bm a_i,
 \label{eq:op3-epoch-sparse-matrix}\\
 \bm g_{\rm new}&=\bm g+
 \frac{\gamma}{\tau}(\gamma t_i-\lambda d_j)\bm a_i.
 \label{eq:op3-epoch-sparse-residual}
\end{align}
The residual increment is nonnegative and supported on at most two ports.
Only the upper acceptance budgets at these ports need checking; all other
coordinate inequalities persist while $\bm t_P$ is unchanged.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
First eliminate the old interiors. The bordered physical system is
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}\bm K&-\gamma\bm a_i\\
 -\gamma\bm a_i^\top&\tau\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 \begin{pmatrix}\bm f\\c\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Eliminating $j$ gives \cref{eq:op3-epoch-sparse-matrix}; subtracting its
action on the fixed $\bm t_P$ gives \cref{eq:op3-epoch-sparse-residual}.
The old named conditional query supplies $\bm a_i,g_{ii},\zeta_i$ in the
paid hierarchy work, without a whole inverse. Its row has at most two
nonnegative port coefficients. Positivity of the full original system
gives $\tau\geq\bar\alpha d_j>0$.
The publication admission has true gate excess greater than
$\epsappr d_j/20$; the certified parent error is at most $\delta$.
Thus $\gamma t_i-\lambda d_j>\epsappr d_j/20-\gamma\delta>0$.
The last inequality uses $d_j\geq1$ and \cref{eq:op3-coarse-delta}.
This proves the support and monotonicity of the residual change.
\end{proof}

The optional epoch algorithm uses this sparse update and retains the old
port vector if every changed upper bound remains valid. Otherwise it
refreshes the complete certified coarse solution. A cycle birth uses the
full sparse assembly and solve. Matrix/load updates, residual resets,
failed certificates and complete vector writes are all charged.

\begin{lemma}[An admitted retained-parent leaf forces refresh; \statusproved]
\label{lem:op3-forced-leaf-refresh}
Suppose $j$ has original degree one, its parent $i$ is retained,
$\gamma\geq1/2$, and $\bar\alpha d_i\leq1$. Every such publication
admission makes the old fixed port vector fail its absolute residual
certificate, regardless of the signed errors left by its last solve.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Here $\bm a_i$ is a unit coordinate, $g_{ii}=0$ and $\tau=1$.
Also $\delta=\epsappr/(256\gamma)$. With
$B_i=\delta\bar\alpha d_i$ the residual increment obeys
\[
 \Delta g_i>\gamma\epsappr\left(\frac1{20}-\frac1{256}\right)
 =\frac{59\gamma\epsappr}{1280},\qquad
 2B_i\leq\frac{\epsappr}{128\gamma}.
\]
Their ratio is greater than $59\gamma^2/10\geq59/40>1$.
Since the old certificate has $g_i\geq-B_i$, its updated value exceeds
$B_i$ and necessarily fails.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[A source-valid global-refresh cost; \statusproved]
\label{prop:op3-clique-leaf-refresh}
For each $k\geq3$, take a $k$-clique with $t\geq k$ pendant leaves at
each core vertex and core seed $v=0$. Set $d=k-1+t$ and
\[
 \bar\alpha=\frac1{4d},\quad \alpha=\frac1{8d-1},\quad
 \epsappr=\frac1{8d^2},\quad \lambda=\frac1{16d^2}.
\]
List seed core neighbors before its leaves, and use FIFO readiness and the
first-discovered insertion parent. The certified publication algorithm
admits the entire clique before any leaf and then admits all $kt$ leaves.
All $k$ core vertices are retained ports throughout these leaf admissions.
The fixed absolute-budget epoch algorithm refreshes at every leaf, so
writing its complete refreshed port vectors costs at least $k^2t$ words.
For $t=k$, this is $\Omega(k^3)$ work whereas
$1/\epsappr=\Theta(k^2)$ and $\operatorname{cvol}(U)=\Theta(k^2)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
We have $d\geq5$, $\gamma=1-1/(4d)\geq19/20$, and
$\epsappr d_v<1$. The initial exact seed value is $1/d-\lambda$;
its first certified lower publication exceeds $1/(2d)$ even after
subtracting $2\delta$. It queues every core neighbor, since
$\gamma/(2d)>\vartheta d=11/(160d)$, and queues the seed's leaves too.
The chosen incidence order places all core neighbors first. Later
publications only append to the FIFO queue, so the entire clique is
admitted before a leaf.

On that core-only face put $L_0=d-\gamma(k-1)$. Direct clique elimination
gives each nonseed core value
\[
 u_i=\frac{\gamma/(d+\gamma)-\lambda d}{L_0}.
\]
Since $L_0\leq d$ and $d/(d+1)\geq5/6$, this is at least
$35/(48d^2)>4\epsappr$. The seed value is larger. At producer quietness
every core lower publication is consequently greater than $3\epsappr$.
It queues every pendant leaf, since $3\gamma\epsappr>\vartheta$.
Monotonicity retains readiness until each leaf is admitted.

The insertion parent of every core vertex is the seed. At the first
triangle, all three vertices are marked by the seed or an extra-edge
endpoint. Every further core vertex becomes an extra-edge endpoint too.
Thus all core vertices are permanent ports before any leaf admission.
Now $\bar\alpha d=1/4$, so \cref{lem:op3-forced-leaf-refresh} applies to
every one of the $kt$ leaves. Each full refresh writes $k$ coordinates.
The exact charged volume is $k(k+3t)$ and, for $t=k$, the inverse tolerance
is $8(2k-1)^2$. The resulting $k^3$ mandatory writes exceed both scales
by a growing polynomial factor.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Interpretation and evidence.}
This is \statusrefuted{} for the explicit global-vector refresh shortcut:
sparse matrix and residual updates alone imply graph-uniform OP3 work.
It is not a lower bound for implicit coarse representations, different
publication policies or arbitrary local algorithms. In particular, the
proved linear-rank algorithm and its structural corollaries remain valid.
\texttt{coarse\_residual\_epochs.py} checks the new sparse residual identities,
accepted reuse events, failed epochs, original ACL output and the canonical
family against independent original equations. Its family reference
eliminates pendant leaves separately and checks the full original equations;
the smallest cases additionally use full dense original-face solves.
The exact execution evidence is in \texttt{COARSE\_RESIDUAL\_EPOCH\_AUDIT.json}.
Adaptive coordinate certificates, implicit coarse updates, incremental
partition maintenance and a reporter avoiding all-component scans remain
separate \statusopen{} obligations.

\begin{proposition}[A true geometric envelope need not be a supersolution; \statusproved]
\label{prop:op3-coarse-geometric-supersolution}
Suppose the obstacle support is the entire finite graph, and let $P$ contain
the seed and at least one other vertex. Put
$\bm d_{\rm eff}=\bm d_P-\bm M_{PI}\bm M_{II}^{-1}\bm d_I$.
For the exact physical coarse solution $\bm u_P$ and its true coordinate
upper envelope $\bm H=\eta\bm u_P+h\one$, with the publication constants,
\begin{equation}
 (\bm f-\bm K\bm H)_i
 =\bigl((\eta-1)\lambda-h\bar\alpha\bigr)(d_{\rm eff})_i
 \qquad(i\in P\setminus\{v\}).
 \label{eq:op3-geometric-supersolution-gap}
\end{equation}
In particular, when $\gamma>1/3$ this envelope fails the sufficient
supersolution test $\bm K\bm H\geq\bm f$ at every nonseed port.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Since the support is the whole graph, $\bm M\one=\bar\alpha\bm d$.
Eliminating interiors gives $\bm K\one=\bar\alpha\bm d_{\rm eff}$,
while the retained physical seed gives
$\bm f=\bm e_v-\lambda\bm d_{\rm eff}$.
Subtracting $\bm K(\eta\bm u_P+h\one)$ proves
\cref{eq:op3-geometric-supersolution-gap}. The effective degrees are
positive by inverse positivity. Here $\eta-1=1/4$ and
$h=\lambda/(8\gamma)$, so the coefficient is positive exactly when
$2\gamma>\bar\alpha$, equivalently $\gamma>1/3$.
\end{proof}

For a triangle with $\alpha=1/3$ and $\lambda=1/20$, the exact response
is $(1/2,1/10,1/10)$ and its envelope is $(51/80,11/80,11/80)$.
The supersolution residual is $(-19/80,1/80,1/80)$, although the envelope
is strictly above the true vector at every coordinate. The exact audit
\nolinkurl{COARSE_GEOMETRIC_SUPERSOLUTION_AUDIT.json} verifies the identity
through independent original Schur elimination, including the equality
case $\gamma=1/3$. Thus geometric coordinate domination does not itself
justify this supersolution certificate; tighter coupled upper bounds may
still be useful. On this full-support face,
the shift $\bm u_P+(\lambda/\bar\alpha)\one$ does have the positive
coarse source $\bm e_v$; recovering small physical values then requires
resolving cancellation against that shift. This identifies another
certificate obligation, not an impossibility result for adaptive bands.
