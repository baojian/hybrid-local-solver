\section[Certified sparse coarse solves]{Certified sparse coarse responses with linear rank dependence}
\label{sec:op3-certified-coarse-sdd}

\paragraph{Status and distinction.}
This section gives a \statusproved{} construction, as a draft awaiting
independent review, that replaces the exact dense inverse by a certified
approximate solution of the sparse coarse SDD system. The published
supplied-SDD solver and the height-bounded top-tree balancing algorithm
are explicit \statussource{} imports. The application state machine,
sparse assembly, acceptance certificate and original ACL repair are
implemented and exactly audited. The audit substitutes a dense coarse
reference provider for the source solver; it is not an implementation of
that solver's fast numerical algorithm. No exact RPPR conclusion or OP2
rate is asserted. Graph-uniform OP3 remains \statusopen{}.

\begin{lemma}[Sparse Schur residual certifies all coordinates; \statusproved]
\label{lem:op3-coarse-error-certificate}
Let $U$ be a positive reached face, $P$ its permanent ports, $I=U\setminus P$,
and $\bm K,\bm f$ the \emph{physical} Schur matrix and load on $P$.
The matrix $\bm K$ is SDDM, has $O(1+|P|+r_U)$ nonzeros, and satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \bm K\one\geq\bar\alpha\bm d_P,\qquad
 K_{ii}\leq d_i.
 \label{eq:op3-coarse-rowsum}
\end{equation}
If a supplied vector $\bm t_P$ passes the exact sparse certificate
\begin{equation}
 |\bm f-\bm K\bm t_P|\leq\delta\bar\alpha\bm d_P,
 \label{eq:op3-coarse-accept}
\end{equation}
then its exact conditional extension through the tree pieces satisfies
$|t_i-u_i^U|\leq\delta$ on every $i\in U$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write the conditional extension as
\[
 \bm u_I=\bm\zeta+\bm H\bm u_P,\qquad
 \bm H=-\bm M_{II}^{-1}\bm M_{IP}\geq\bm0.
\]
Every row sum of $\bm M_{UU}$ is at least $\bar\alpha d_i$.
Multiplying $\bm M_{II}\one+\bm M_{IP}\one\geq\bm0$ by the
nonnegative inverse gives $\bm H\one\leq\one$.
The assertion is vacuous when $I$ is empty. Consequently
\[
 \bm K\one=\bm M_{PP}\one+\bm M_{PI}\bm H\one
 \geq\bm M_{PP}\one+\bm M_{PI}\one
 \geq\bar\alpha\bm d_P.
\]
Schur elimination preserves positive definiteness and nonpositive
off-diagonal entries and reduces the diagonal, proving the other claims
in \cref{eq:op3-coarse-rowsum}. There are $O(1+|P|)$ one/two-port pieces
and $r_U$ extra edges. Each contributes at most a two-coordinate matrix;
duplicate-port corrections affect only diagonals. Sparse assembly thus
uses $O(1+|P|+r_U)$ entries and operations, before dictionary factors.
This assembly retains original degrees and restores every extra edge.

Inverse positivity and \cref{eq:op3-coarse-rowsum,eq:op3-coarse-accept}
give
\[
 |\bm t_P-\bm u_P^U|
 \leq\bm K^{-1}|\bm f-\bm K\bm t_P|
 \leq\delta\bm K^{-1}\bm K\one=\delta\one.
\]
The nonnegative substochastic rows of $\bm H$ propagate this same bound
to every interior coordinate, with no factor depending on path depth.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[A certified supplied coarse solve; \statusproved{} using \statussource]
\label{lem:op3-certified-sparse-solve}
For a positive face, a vector satisfying \cref{eq:op3-coarse-accept} can
be obtained with expected
\begin{equation}
 \widetilde O\!\left((1+|P|+r_U)
       \log\frac{2}{\delta\bar\alpha^2}\right)
 \label{eq:op3-certified-coarse-source-work}
\end{equation}
word work, including preprocessing, candidate certification and independent
retries. Here $0<\delta<1$ and all matrices supplied to the source solver
are already assembled from locally read active data.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The physical seed $v$ is always retained. Every interior physical load is
negative. Since
$\bm f=\bm b_P-\bm M_{PI}\bm M_{II}^{-1}\bm b_I$,
we have $f_i\leq0$ for $i\ne v$ and $f_v\leq1$. Positivity of the face
and \cref{lem:op3-response-size} imply
\[
 0<\|\bm u_P^U\|_{\bm K}^2
   =\bm f^\top\bm u_P^U\leq u_v^U\leq1/\bar\alpha.
\]
If the relative energy error is at most
$\eta_{\rm sdd}=\delta\bar\alpha^2/2$, Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
 |(\bm K(\bm t_P-\bm u_P^U))_i|
 \leq\sqrt{K_{ii}}\|\bm t_P-\bm u_P^U\|_{\bm K}
 \leq\frac{\sqrt{d_i}\eta_{\rm sdd}}{\sqrt{\bar\alpha}}
 \leq\delta\bar\alpha d_i.
\]
The last step uses original unweighted degrees $d_i\geq1$ and
$0<\bar\alpha<1$. This is a sufficient energy accuracy for the explicit
coordinate certificate, not an assumption that a relative spectral
approximation alone controls original residuals.

The supplied weighted-SDD theorem of \citet{koutis2011nearly},
Theorem~4.6 (local arXiv v4 PDF, p.~11), gives expected
$\widetilde O(m\log n\log(1/\eta_{\rm sdd}))$ work.
The constant-success good-chain event is in Lemma~4.5 on pp.~10--11;
Lemma~4.3 on p.~9 applies the chain to a supplied SDD matrix.
Use independent source attempts with a fixed constant failure parameter
in that construction, accept only after \cref{eq:op3-coarse-accept}, and
discard rejected workspaces. A successful energy-accuracy attempt passes.
Each attempt costs the displayed source amount in expectation and succeeds
with probability bounded below by a constant. The renewal identity gives
the same expected bound for the accepted solve, including failed attempts
and their allocations. Fresh randomness after each adaptively chosen face
makes this a conditional expected bound given that face's history.
No arbitrary high-probability interface is attributed to Theorem~4.6.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Certified finite-width producer.}
Use the constants $\lambda,h,\eta=5/4,\vartheta,C$ of
\cref{sec:op3-coarse-publications} and fix
\begin{equation}
 \delta=\min\{h/16,\epsappr/64\}.
 \label{eq:op3-coarse-delta}
\end{equation}
Initially return zero if $\epsappr d_v\geq1$; this directly certifies ACL
without reading the seed row. Otherwise admit the seed. At each admission
update only local metadata and the component summaries, assemble the sparse
physical coarse system, and obtain a certified approximate port vector.
There is no maintained dense inverse, Green promotion or temporary port.
Retain this vector until the next admission.

On vertex $i$'s single permanent home edge put the producer row
\begin{equation}
 t_i-(\eta\ell_i+h-\delta).
 \label{eq:op3-approximate-producer-row}
\end{equation}
The existing reporter uses shifted key $\eta\ell_i+h-\delta-C$ and the
current approximate shifted port values. A positive selected row is followed
by a named query for $t_i$ and publication $\ell_i^{\rm new}=t_i-\delta$.
The normalized hull score is used only for its sign and label.
All cached delivery, parent-buffer and monotone ready-queue rules are as
before; the inverse-free admission rule uses a queued vertex whose lower
signal exceeds $\vartheta d_i$.

\begin{lemma}[Safe publications and downward ACL recovery; \statusproved]
\label{lem:op3-approximate-publications-repair}
This producer implements \cref{def:op3-publications}. It admits only
strictly positive original obstacle gates. When it is quiet and the ready
queue is empty, recover $\bm t_U$ once and return
\begin{equation}
 \widehat{\bm u}_U=\max\{\bm0,\bm t_U-\delta\one\},\qquad
 \widehat{\bm u}_{\bar U}=\bm0,\qquad
 \widehat{\bm p}=\bar\alpha\bm D\widehat{\bm u}.
 \label{eq:op3-coarse-final-repair}
\end{equation}
The output is an original ACL $\epsappr$-approximation and, in the nonzero
case, is strictly positive at every admitted vertex.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The preceding certificate lemma gives $|t_i-u_i^U|\leq\delta$. A positive
row therefore yields a true lower publication with
\[
 \ell_i^{\rm new}>\eta\ell_i^{\rm old}+h-2\delta
 \geq\eta\ell_i^{\rm old}+7h/8.
\]
It meets both the first-publication threshold and geometric growth rule.
After that publication, $u_i^U\leq\ell_i^{\rm new}+2\delta
\leq\eta\ell_i^{\rm new}+h$. Quietness of all rows certifies
$u_i^U\leq t_i+\delta\leq\eta\ell_i+h$ everywhere.
Old publications remain lower bounds because exact positive faces increase
under admissions. Thus the recipient's original residual argument applies
without requiring the actual exact face as algorithmic state.
The reporters allow arbitrary real port query values; their positive
homogeneous denominators depend on the exact structural maps and loads,
not on a sign assumption for approximate port values.

At termination put $\bm e=\bm u^U-\widehat{\bm u}$.
Clipping gives $0\leq e_i\leq2\delta$ on $U$. For active coordinates,
\[
 \widehat{\bm r}_U=\lambda\bm d_U+\bm M_{UU}\bm e_U,
 \qquad
 (\lambda-2\gamma\delta)d_i\leq\widehat r_i
       \leq(\lambda+2\delta)d_i.
\]
Both bounds lie within $[0,\epsappr d_i]$. Exterior residuals decrease
under downward repair and were already at most $3\epsappr d_i/4$.
All residuals stay nonnegative because exterior values are nonnegative
weighted sums of the returned active coordinates.

The initial nonzero seed value is $1/d_v-\epsappr/2>\epsappr/2$.
For a later admitted vertex $j$, its original old gate excess is greater
than $(\vartheta-\lambda)d_j=\epsappr d_j/20$. Its full Schur admission
pivot is positive and at most $d_j$, so its new exact value exceeds
$\epsappr/20$. Monotonicity preserves this lower bound. Finally,
$2\delta\leq\epsappr/32<\epsappr/20$ makes every repaired active value
strictly positive. Thus the actually scanned support equals the returned
positive support; there is no uncharged discarded exploration.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Expected linear-rank ACL work; \statusproved]
\label{thm:op3-certified-linear-rank}
On every finite simple connected unweighted graph with at least two
vertices and for $0<\epsappr<1$, the preceding local degree/adjacency
algorithm terminates almost surely and returns an original ACL
$\epsappr$-approximation. In the
exact-real word model its expected work satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \mathbb E W=\widetilde O\!\left(
       \frac{1+\mathbb E r}{\epsappr}\right),
 \label{eq:op3-expected-linear-rank}
\end{equation}
where $r$ is the cycle rank of its possibly random returned support
(set $r=0$ for the zero output). The hidden factors are logarithmic in
$1/\epsappr$ and $1/\alpha$. In particular, if the exact
$\lambda=\epsappr/2$ obstacle positive support has cycle rank at most a
fixed $R$, expected work is
$\widetilde O((1+R)/\epsappr)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every accepted solve is certified, so the preceding lemmas prove correctness
for every finite accepted trajectory. At most $|U|$ faces are reached and
each solve terminates almost surely by independent constant-success retries.
The original mass bound gives
$V=\operatorname{cvol}(U)<4/\epsappr$. Let
$L=\log(2+V)$, let $b$ count cycle-birth admissions, and let $p$ be final
permanent-port count. As before $b\leq r$ and $p=O(1+r)$, pathwise.

With $K_{
\rm pub}=1+\log_+(32\gamma/(\bar\alpha\epsappr))$, the fully charged
discovery, metadata, source rebuild, producer, named-query, cached-delivery
and final-recovery work outside coarse solves is
\[
 O\!\left(1+V\left[(1+b)L^4+
 K_{\rm pub}\bigl(L^4+(1+p)L^2\bigr)\right]\right).
\]
This is the previous publication ledger with the dense inverse terms
removed. Each new face additionally assembles and certifies a matrix with
$O(1+p_t+r_t)$ nonzeros and applies the supplied solver.
Since $\epsappr/256\leq\delta\leq\epsappr/64$,
\cref{eq:op3-certified-coarse-source-work} is
$\widetilde O(1+p_t+r_t)$ with the stated uniform logarithms.
Conditional expectation at each reached face and the tower rule bound all
such work by $\widetilde O(\mathbb E[V(1+r)])$.
The same bound absorbs the deterministic pathwise charges above, proving
\cref{eq:op3-expected-linear-rank}. No random realized $r$ is treated as a
deterministic expected-work bound. Finally, every admitted vertex lies in
the fixed obstacle support; cycle rank is monotone under subgraphs, so
$r\leq R$ whenever the stated sufficient condition holds.

Only current whole root/port vectors and the current sparse system are kept.
Outside the source solver workspace, retaining immutable application
histories uses $O(1+V(1+b+K_{\rm pub})L^3+p+r)$ words. All source workspace
allocations, including failed attempts, are charged in expected work and
discarded between attempts/faces. No sharper worst-case workspace bound
for the imported solver is claimed; expected peak storage is bounded by
the expected total word work. No ambient graph structure is supplied to
either imported primitive.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Audit boundary and remaining question.}
\texttt{certified\_coarse\_publications.py} constructs sparse coarse
matrices and checks their original-matrix Schur identities independently.
Its testing provider sends two oppositely signed unacceptable perturbations
before a certified nonzero perturbation. Every original-coordinate error
band, producer sign, lower signal, positive admission and final repaired
residual is checked against independent exact faces. No dense inverse is
stored, and publication-only events leave the coarse matrix, load and
certified port vector unchanged. The exhaustive evidence is recorded in
\texttt{CERTIFIED\_COARSE\_PUBLICATION\_AUDIT.json}; it does not test the
unimplemented source solver's numerical performance. The remaining general
OP3 question is how to avoid the aggregate rank-dependent coarse work and
component-query work while retaining these paid original-coordinate
certificates.
