\section{Branching forces cubic work for the current group order}
\label{sec:op3-group-branching-obstruction}

The shared star clique does not imply small response diversity on trees.
The following obstruction applies to the implemented first-eligible-group
rule, with its explicit matrix between current groups. Earlier fast tree
methods remain available; this is not a lower bound on OP3 computation.

\begin{theorem}[Cubic grouped frontier on a finite binary tree; \statusproved]
\label{thm:op3-grouped-binary-cubic}
For every integer $R\geq1$, take the complete binary tree of depth
$D=4R+8$, with root seed and all original degrees, and set
\[
 \lambda=\frac1{64\,2^R},\qquad e=2\lambda,\qquad
 t=1-\gamma=\frac1{48R},\qquad \alpha=\frac1{96R-1}.
\]
The producer in \cref{thm:op3-frontier-response-groups} admits every vertex
through depth $R$ in breadth-first order. While processing depth $R$
alone, it executes at least $2^{3R-3}$ shared matrix-entry updates.
Its eventual original support volume is $\Theta(2^R)=\Theta(e^{-1})$,
and the graph's ambient volume exceeds $4/e$. Thus this representation and
order incur $\Omega(e^{-3})$ work even though
$\alpha^{-1}=\Theta(\log(1/e))$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $S_r$ be the complete root ball of depth $0\leq r\leq R$. Its
original volume is $v_r=3\cdot2^{r+1}-4$. The root has degree two and
every other vertex reached in this argument has original degree three;
the true degree-one leaves remain at depth $D$.

We first show that the exact face solution on $S_r$ is positive and makes
every next-level vertex eligible. All the following vectors are constant
on a level, with zero value outside $S_r$. Write $L_r$ for the original
Dirichlet Laplacian, $A_r$ for the within-ball adjacency, and
$M_r=L_r+tA_r$. The conservative torsion $w^0=L_r^{-1}d$ has the exact
radial values
\[
 w^0_\ell=3(r+1-\ell)
       -4\bigl(2^{-\ell}-2^{-(r+1)}\bigr)
 \leq3(r+1-\ell),\qquad 0\leq\ell\leq r.
\]
The formula follows by direct substitution into the root, interior and
last-level equations; all original degrees are retained. Positivity also
follows from inverse positivity. With $w=M_r^{-1}d$, the inequality
$M_rw^0=d+tA_rw^0\geq d$ gives $0<w\leq w^0$.

Put $q=(1-3t)/2$ and define
\[
 y_\ell=\tfrac12(q^\ell-q^{r+1}),\qquad y_{r+1}=0.
\]
For a nonroot row, the homogeneous coefficient satisfies
\[
 3-\frac\gamma q-2\gamma q
 =-\frac{3t^2(5-3t)}{1-3t}<0.
\]
The subtracted constant contributes $-3tq^{r+1}/2$, so these rows of
$M_ry$ are nonpositive, including the last level by its zero extension.
The root row is $1-\gamma q-tq^{r+1}\leq1$. Thus $M_ry\leq e_v$,
and $g=M_r^{-1}e_v\geq y>0$.

Since $r\leq R$, Bernoulli's inequality gives
\[
 q^r=2^{-r}\left(1-\frac1{16R}\right)^r
 \geq\frac{15}{16}\,2^{-r}.
\]
Also $1-q\geq1/2$, and summing the geometric differences yields
\[
 y_\ell=\tfrac12(1-q)\sum_{j=\ell}^r q^j
 \geq\frac{15(r+1-\ell)}{64\,2^r}.
\]
Consequently the damped face solution $z=g-\lambda w$ satisfies
\[
 z_\ell\geq y_\ell-\lambda w^0_\ell
 \geq\frac{3(r+1-\ell)}{16\,2^R}>0.
\]
Every next-level vertex has one admitted parent and original degree three.
Its residual is at least
\[
 \gamma z_r\geq\frac{47}{256\,2^R}
 >\frac9{128\,2^R}=3(\lambda+\kappa),
 \qquad \kappa=\lambda/2.
\]
Thus all next-level gates are strict. Partial subsequent positive
admissions can only increase their transformed loads, by the exact Schur
invariant already proved.

In this tree each admitted row discovers its two children and encounters
no other unadmitted old neighbor. It creates one fresh sibling group and
causes no partition split. Each existing sibling group is exhausted before
groups later in the live list: at the start of a level all its vertices
are eligible by the complete-ball calculation, and they remain eligible.
Their original degrees agree, so the heap's label tie-break only orders
the two siblings. Induction gives breadth-first order through depth $R$.

At the start of that level, $2^{R-1}$ nonempty sibling groups remain.
Each of its $2^R$ pivots creates a new child group and removes at most one
empty parent group, so the number of nonempty groups never drops below
$2^{R-1}$. The implementation updates every unordered pair including
diagonal pairs, at least $\tfrac12(2^{R-1})^2$ entries per pivot. The
level total is therefore at least $2^{3R-3}$. Its admitted volume is
already $v_R$, while the general positive-pivot bound gives eventual
$V<1/\lambda=64\,2^R$. Finally the ambient volume is
$2^{D+2}-4>4/e$, and the displayed $\alpha$ gives the asserted logarithmic
inverse-teleportation scale. The argument constrains this implementation,
not a compressed low-rank matrix or a different elimination order.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Finite and symbolic audits.}
\path{frontier_group_branching.py} checks 1,024 symbolic parameter,
ambient-volume and uniform gate certificates. Independent radial systems
check 156 conservative/damped faces, 234 Green/torsion systems and 78
rational subsolution certificates, with 728 original-degree radial rows.
These small quotient systems are validators, not a hidden producer solver.

The actual group producer executes six capped prefixes, $R=1,\ldots,6$,
through 246 total positive pivots. All lifted prefix coordinates match
independent radial face solutions, and 126 final sibling groups are checked.
Every prefix is stopped with a further legal vertex and is explicitly
recorded as a nonterminal computation, not a completed ACL solve. For
$R=6$, original admitted volume is 380, only 255 labels are discovered,
and the final frontier has 64 sibling groups. The run makes 91,519 total
shared matrix updates, including 79,552 while processing depth six, against
the proved lower bound 32,768 for that level. Its finite ambient tree has
$2^{33}-1$ vertices and is accessed through a private lazy oracle, never
materialized. Results and hashes are in
\path{FRONTIER_GROUP_BRANCHING_AUDIT.json}. General OP3 remains
\statusopen.
