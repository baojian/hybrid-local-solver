\section{A bounded core sparsifier and the weighted constructor}
\label{sec:op3-bounded-core-sparsifier}

\paragraph{Purpose.}
The resistance estimates in \cref{sec:op3-ordinary-solve-resistances}
now close the core-sampling obligation left by
\cref{thm:op3-supplied-tree-constructor}. The graph is supplied,
connected, loopless and positively weighted. All edge records, randomness,
searches, copies, guards and allocated words are charged. The matrix
concentration inequality remains the explicit primary-source result from
\citet[Corollary 5.2, PDF p.~29]{tropp2012tails}; neither dense effective
resistances nor spectral tests are a production primitive.

\begin{theorem}[Capped arbitrary-weight sparsification; \statusproved]
\label{thm:op3-bounded-core-sparsifier}
Given a supplied graph $C$ on $r\geq2$ vertices, with $m_C$ edge records,
positive weight ratio $\chi_C$, and $0<p_C<1$, a procedure returns
\textnormal{FAIL} or a connected weighted graph $S$. With probability
at least $1-p_C$, it returns a graph satisfying
\[
 C\preceq S\preceq2C.
\]
With $\mathcal L_C=\log(2+r+m_C+1/p_C)$, every branch has bounded
word work and total allocated words
\[
 O\bigl(m_C[\mathcal L_C^6+\log\chi_C]\bigr),
\]
using the checked low-stretch-tree source. The output has
$O(r\log(2+r/p_C))$ edge records. If the output is not
\textnormal{FAIL}, it is always connected and its weights satisfy
\[
 \sum_{e\in S}c_e\leq2\sum_{e\in C}c_e,
 \qquad c_{\min}(S)\geq\frac{3c_{\min}(C)}{2q},
\]
where the charged draw count is $q=O(r\log(2+r/p_C))$.
These output-size, connectivity and weight conclusions hold even when
the spectral estimate is wrong. The one-vertex graph returns the empty
graph directly.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Run \cref{cor:op3-resistance-near-linear} with joint failure budget
$p_C/4$. Form scores $s_e=2c_e\widehat R_e$ and $t=\sum_es_e$.
Abort if any score is nonpositive or $t>4(r-1)$. On the good estimator
event, $c_eR_e\leq s_e\leq4c_eR_e$, so these guards never abort,
because $\sum_e c_eR_e=r-1$.
Let
\[
 \ell=\lceil\log_2(8r/p_C)\rceil,
 \qquad q=\text{the smallest power of two at least }
               \max\{1,27t\ell\}.
\]
Both quantities are found by paid doubling. The score guard implies
$q\leq\max\{1,216(r-1)\ell\}$ on every branch, before any sample
buffer is allocated. Use the capped fair-bit sampler with probabilities
$p_e=s_e/t$, $q$ draws, and total cap-failure budget $p_C/2$.
Abort if any draw fails. Give each sampled copy of edge $e$ conductance
$3c_e/(2q p_e)$ and aggregate copies of the same original edge using
an explicitly allocated multiplicity array.

Condition on any good estimate vector and consider the ideal independent
categorical draws to which the capped draws couple. On the space
orthogonal to constants, each unscaled sampled proof matrix has expectation
identity and norm $c_eR_e/p_e\leq t$. The source matrix Chernoff bound,
with relative error $1/3$, gives
\[
 \Pr\{C\not\preceq S\text{ or }S\not\preceq2C\}
 \leq2(r-1)e^{-q/(27t)}\leq p_C/4.
\]
This is the same source inequality as in
\cref{prop:op3-spectral-confidence-wrapper}, now with a directly requested
estimator confidence and an implemented capped sampler. Union with the
estimator event and the cap event gives total failure at most $p_C$.
We condition on the estimate vector for the ideal sampling analysis,
not on successful termination of the capped sampler.

After assembling $S$, scan its weights and abort if their sum exceeds
twice the input weight sum. Also check connectivity by union by size
and abort if disconnected. Neither guard rejects the good spectral event:
taking traces of $S\preceq2C$ gives the first condition, while
$S\succeq C$ implies connectivity. Every selected edge has at least
$3c_{\min}(C)/(2q)$ weight because $p_e\leq1$; aggregation only
increases that bound. Thus both weight conclusions hold on every
nonaborted branch. Connectivity checking requires only $O(r)$ parent
and size words and $O(\log(2+r))$ work per selected edge.

All score scans and prefix construction are linear. The per-draw bit
cap is $O(\log(2+qm_C/p_C))=O(\mathcal L_C)$, and each draw uses
$O(\mathcal L_C^2)$ word work. Multiplicity arrays, every fair bit,
CDF comparison, output record and guard are charged. Since
$q=O(r\mathcal L_C)$ and $r\leq m_C+1$, this sampling work is
absorbed by the displayed resistance-estimation bound. No guard
performs a spectral comparison. The argument also bounds all failed
attempts, rather than discarding their work.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sparse-core construction from a supplied tree; \statusproved]
\label{thm:op3-supplied-tree-sparse-core}
Let $G$ be supplied on $n\geq2$ vertices with $m$ positive edge records,
weight ratio $\chi_w$, a supplied spanning tree of total stretch $W$, and
$1\leq j\leq n$. Put $\kappa=256W/j$ and $0<p_Q<1$.
There is a procedure returning \textnormal{FAIL} or a rooted forest
with a connected core on at most $j$ roots. With probability at least
$1-p_Q$, its Laplacian satisfies
\[
 G\preceq Q\preceq\frac{10752W}{j}G.
\]
The core has $O(j\log(2+j/p_Q))$ edge records on every nonaborted
branch. With $\mathcal L=\log(2+n+m+1/p_Q)$, bounded work and all
allocated words are
\[
 O\bigl(m[\mathcal L^6+\log\chi_w]\bigr).
\]
In this work bound only the core's own low-stretch tree is selected by
the checked source; the original tree is the supplied one. Every
nonaborted output is connected and, if $q$ is its core draw count,
\[
 c_{\min}(Q)\geq\frac{3c_{\min}(G)}{\max\{1,q\}},
 \qquad \sum_{e\in Q}c_e\leq9\kappa\sum_{e\in G}c_e.
\]
For a one-root core take $q=0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply \cref{thm:op3-supplied-tree-constructor}. Its result has the form
$Q_0=3\kappa F+3C$ and satisfies
$G\preceq Q_0\preceq21\kappa G$. The unscaled core $C$ is the
quotient of the original graph by the components of the retained
forest, so it is connected unless it has just one vertex. Its edges
are sums of original cross-component conductances. For a nonempty core,
therefore,
\[
 c_{\min}(C)\geq c_{\min}(G),\qquad
 \sum_{e\in C}c_e\leq\sum_{e\in G}c_e,
 \qquad \chi_C\leq m\chi_w.
\]
For a single root, set $S=C$ empty and $q=0$ directly. Otherwise
use \cref{thm:op3-bounded-core-sparsifier} on $C$, and return
$Q=3\kappa F+3S$. On its good event,
$Q_0\preceq Q\preceq2Q_0$, which gives the claimed quality
$42\kappa=10752W/j$. The core-size bound follows because it has at
most $j$ vertices. Relabel its supplied roots into an explicit array,
then lift returned edges back using that array; all maps and scans are
paid. The initial routing work and the core sparsification work fit the
displayed bound, using $m_C\leq m$ and $\log\chi_C\leq\log(m\chi_w)$.

The forest has one root per component. A returned connected core therefore
makes $Q$ connected even off the good spectral event. Forest weights
are at least $3\kappa c_{\min}(G)$, and $\kappa\geq1$.
For $q\geq1$, core weights after scaling by three are at least
$9c_{\min}(G)/(2q)$. These facts prove the lower weight bound, including
the forest-only case $q=0$. The retained forest is a subset of the
original supplied tree, and the core weight guard gives
\[
 \sum_{e\in Q}c_e
 \leq3\kappa\sum_{e\in G}c_e+6\sum_{e\in G}c_e
 \leq9\kappa\sum_{e\in G}c_e.
\]
These deterministic bounds do not use successful resistance estimates.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The arbitrary-weight constructor contract; \statusproved]
\label{cor:op3-weighted-constructor-contract}
Use the Abraham--Neiman source tree on the original supplied graph,
after paid aggregation of parallel edges. For $1\leq j<n$, the
preceding procedure gives, with failure at most $p_Q$,
\[
 G\preceq Q\preceq C_0\frac{m\mathcal L^2}{j}G,
 \qquad m_{\rm core}\leq C_1j\mathcal L,
\]
for universal constants, at work
$O(m[\mathcal L^6+\log\chi_w])$ and the same bound on all allocated
words. For $j=n$, apply the whole-graph sparsifier directly, with an
empty forest and constant spectral quality. This imposes no polynomial
weight-ratio assumption in the current vertex count.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The checked arbitrary-weight tree result gives $W=O(m\mathcal L^2)$.
Include its construction, sorting, path-index and output costs, and
substitute in \cref{thm:op3-supplied-tree-sparse-core}. Parallel
aggregation preserves the Laplacian exactly and increases the weight
ratio by at most a factor $m$. The whole-graph branch is
\cref{thm:op3-bounded-core-sparsifier} itself. The one-vertex input is
handled directly without a resistance estimate or a division by zero.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Relation to the supplied recursion.}
This discharges the missing weighted-constructor hypothesis on input
families whose $\log\chi_w$ is bounded by a polynomial in the global
parameter $L$ from \cref{thm:op3-conditional-supplied-recursion}.
The simultaneous recursive range theorem gives $\log\chi_w=O(L^2)$
on successful recursive executions. Final composition must retain the
explicit call, depth, work and allocation caps on failed executions;
no per-current-core polynomial weight-ratio assumption may be substituted.
The conditional theorem remains a reusable contract statement. A complete
source-backed recurrence needs this final capped-execution reconciliation.

\paragraph{Implementation and audit.}
The implementation is \texttt{bounded\_core\_sparsifier.py}; source and
backend hashes are saved in \texttt{BOUNDED\_CORE\_SPARSIFIER\_AUDIT.json}.
Its production pipeline composes the ordinary resistance sketch with
capped sampling. Separate weighted atlas and routed-core fixtures identify
dense resistance and PSD providers as validators. Deliberate bad estimates
exercise score, total-weight and connectivity guards, while a binary CDF
boundary exercises a capped-sampler abort. Legitimate seeded sampling
aborts are recorded as allowed outcomes, not misreported as wrong answers
or omitted from work. The low-stretch tree algorithm is an explicit
source dependency and is not implemented by these audits. General OP3
and local support discovery remain \statusopen.

The final audit records 182 valid-estimator sampling attempts: 155 returned
graphs pass both exact PSD bounds, and 27 report allowed capped-sampler
aborts. Returned weighted fixtures include ratio $2^{160}$ and omit four
low-leverage input edges in total. Three complete sketch-to-sampler runs
use 450 actual resistance groups, and nine supplied-tree/core compositions
through 256 vertices pass their exact spectral comparisons; six have a
nonempty core. Score, sampler, weight and connectivity failures are also
forced explicitly. The complete run took 149.180 seconds. These are finite
algebra and implementation checks, not empirical proofs of the confidence.
