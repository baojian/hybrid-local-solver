\section{The fourth-power comparison scale is necessary for support containment}
\label{sec:op3-conservative-parameter-obstruction}

The reverse reduction in
\cref{thm:op3-conservative-envelope-equivalence} calls an arbitrary ACL
producer at a very small comparison parameter. The following obstruction
shows that its fourth power of accuracy cannot uniformly be improved by
that support-extraction rule. It is not a computational lower bound for OP3.

\begin{theorem}[A significant endpoint disappears at fourth-power damping;
\statusproved]
\label{thm:op3-fourth-power-support-obstruction}
For every integer $N\geq4$, take the original path on $0,\ldots,8N$,
seeded at $N$, and set
\[
 \lambda=\frac{2N}{8N^2+1},\qquad e=2\lambda,\qquad
 \delta=e/8=\lambda/4,\qquad t_N=N^{-4}.
\]
Its volume is $16N>4/e$. The conservative obstacle $u_0$ at load
$e_N-\lambda d$ has $u_{0,0}=e/4>\delta$. Nevertheless, at physical
damping $t_N$ there is a nonnegative original ACL output $z$ of accuracy
$\lambda$ and original support volume $4N-2<1/\lambda$ with $z_0=0$.
The exact same-load obstacle $u_t$ also has $u_{t,0}=0$ for every
$t_N\leq t\leq1$. Moreover,
\[
 16<\frac{t_N}{e^4}
 =\frac{(8N^2+1)^4}{256N^8}<17,
 \qquad
 \|u_0-u_{t_N}\|_\infty\geq e/4
 \geq\frac{t_N}{68e^3}.
\]
Thus the $t/e^3$ uniform comparison dependence is sharp in its accuracy
power, and a comparison rule using $t=\omega(e^4)$ cannot guarantee that
the support of an arbitrary accuracy-$\lambda$ ACL output contains every
conservative coordinate above $e/8$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The conservative obstacle has the following explicit zero extension:
\[
 u_{0,i}=\begin{cases}
 N-\lambda(4N^2-i^2),&0\leq i\leq N,\\
 (2N-i)\bigl(1-\lambda(2N+i)\bigr),&N\leq i\leq2N,\\
 0,&i\geq2N.
 \end{cases}
\]
The formulas agree at $N$ and are positive precisely on
$S_0=\{0,\ldots,2N-1\}$. Substitution gives
$(Lu_0)_i=(e_N)_i-\lambda d_i$ there, including the original degree-one
endpoint $0$. The only nontrivial outside KKT row is $2N$, where
$u_{0,2N-1}=\lambda(1+1/(2N))\leq2\lambda$.
All remaining outside rows have zero potential and zero incoming flux.
These are exactly the obstacle KKT conditions; uniqueness follows from
the large-volume premise. In particular $u_{0,0}=\lambda/2=2\delta$.

Now impose zero Dirichlet values at both $0$ and $2N$, and let
$T=\{1,\ldots,2N-1\}$. Every original degree on $T$ is two.
Write $L_T$ and $A_T$ for the original Dirichlet Laplacian and internal
adjacency. The positive, symmetric unconstrained solution on $T$ and
its torsion vector are
\[
 v_i=\frac{\min\{i,2N-i\}}2-\lambda i(2N-i),\qquad
 w_i=i(2N-i),
 \qquad L_Tv=b_T,\quad L_Tw=2\one.
\]
Both identities follow by second differences, including the seed kink.
For $1\leq i\leq N$, the coefficient of $i$ in
$v_i=\lambda i^2+(1/2-2N\lambda)i$ is positive. Symmetry therefore
gives $\max v=v_N\leq N/3$. Also
\[
 \frac{v_i}{w_i}
 =\frac{1}{2\max\{i,2N-i\}}-\lambda
 \geq\frac{v_1}{w_1}\geq\frac1{9N^2}.
\]
For the last inequality, $v_1>\lambda\geq2/(9N)$ and $w_1<2N$.

Define nonnegative vectors
$h=L_T^{-1}A_Tv$ and $k=L_T^{-1}A_Th$, and set
$z=v-t_Nh+t_N^2k$ on $T$, zero elsewhere.
Nonnegativity of the Dirichlet inverse and $A_Tv\leq2\max(v)\one$
give $h\leq(N/3)w$. Consequently $v-t_Nh>0$ for $N\geq4$,
so $z$ is positive exactly on $T$. A direct cancellation yields
\begin{equation}
 (L_T+t_NA_T)z=b_T+t_N^3A_Tk.
 \label{eq:op3-path-certificate-cancellation}
\end{equation}

To check the omitted endpoint, first sum the explicit values of $v$:
\[
 \sum_{i\in T}v_i
 =\frac{N^2(8N^2+7)}{6(8N^2+1)}\geq N^2/6,
 \qquad
 v_1-\lambda=\frac1{2(8N^2+1)}\leq\frac1{16N^2}.
\]
The first row of $L_T^{-1}$ is $(2N-j)/(2N)$.
Symmetry of $A_Tv$ implies
$h_1=\frac12\sum_i(A_Tv)_i=\sum_i v_i-v_1$.
Since $v_1\leq3/(8N)$, this gives $h_1\geq N^2/8$ for $N\geq3$.
Moreover $\max h\leq N^3/3$, so $k\leq(N^3/3)w$ and
$k_1\leq2N^4/3$. It follows that
\[
 z_1-\lambda
 \leq-\frac1{16N^2}+\frac2{3N^4}<0\qquad(N\geq4).
\]
The same bound holds at $2N-1$ by symmetry. Thus the omitted original
endpoint has residual $(1-t_N)z_1<\lambda$, and row $2N$ has residual
$(1-t_N)z_{2N-1}<\lambda\leq2\lambda$. Every farther outside residual
is zero. On $T$, \cref{eq:op3-path-certificate-cancellation} gives
\[
 r_i=2\lambda-t_N^3(A_Tk)_i,\qquad
 0\leq t_N^3(A_Tk)_i\leq\frac2{3N^7}<2\lambda,
\]
where $\max k\leq N^5/3$ was used. This proves all original ACL
inequalities $0\leq r\leq\lambda d$, including the smaller degree
at the omitted endpoint. Its support volume is $2|T|=4N-2<1/\lambda$.

The candidate is a nonnegative supersolution for the same-load positive
obstacle. The maximum principle gives $u_{t_N}\leq z$, hence
$u_{t_N,0}=0$. Same-load monotonicity preserves that zero for larger
$t$. The exact obstacles at those parameters are themselves valid
accuracy-$\lambda$ ACL outputs, so this is an obstruction for arbitrary
output support throughout that range, not only for the displayed $z$.

Finally $t_N/e^4=16(1+1/(8N^2))^4$, which lies between $16$ and $17$
for $N\geq4$ and tends to $16$. The endpoint difference is $e/4$,
giving the displayed sensitivity lower bound. Along this sequence
$e\downarrow0$, any $t(e)=\omega(e^4)$ eventually exceeds $t_N$,
provided it lies in the parameter range $(0,1]$. Its exact obstacle
therefore omits a significant conservative coordinate. A producer that
deliberately emits extra envelope coordinates is not ruled out.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact audit and scope.}
\path{conservative_parameter_obstruction.py} validates the explicit
conservative KKT and target ACL rows, all displayed bounds, and the
cancellation identity. Its two Dirichlet inverses use prefix sums:
for a right-hand side $q$ of length $K$, the first increment is
$a_1=(K+1)^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^K(K+1-j)q_j$, followed by
$x_i=x_{i-1}+a_i$ and $a_{i+1}=a_i-q_i$. The computed final endpoint
is checked to be zero. These are audit-only computations, not an
unimplemented primitive in a claimed local algorithm.

The full run checks 135 paths through $N=4096$, with 193,071 original
conservative and 193,071 original positive-target residual rows, in
14.235 seconds. Six independent dense Dirichlet solves and 15 small
whole-graph obstacle solves check the prefix certificates, the safe
$e^4/128$ containment, and omission at $N^{-4}$ and larger parameters.
Hashes and exact examples are in
\path{CONSERVATIVE_PARAMETER_OBSTRUCTION_AUDIT.json}.
The result constrains the parameter comparison and support-extraction
rule. It neither refutes the nearly inverse-accuracy ACL target nor
provides a lower bound on incremental numerical work.
