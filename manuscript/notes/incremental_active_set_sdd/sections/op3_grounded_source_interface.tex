\section[Grounding, range and pruning]{Grounded source objectives, exact range loss, and certified edge pruning}
\label{sec:op3-grounded-source-interface}

\paragraph{Status and matrix scope.}
These \statusproved{} drafts concern supplied grounded quadratic matrices
and a physical unweighted-graph witness. They do not implement or remove
the source's recursive numerical assumptions. A source interface must match
the objective class as well as its advertised accuracy and work.

Consider $K=\operatorname{diag}(h)+\sum_e c_e a_ea_e^\top$, where
$a_e=e_i-e_j$, $c_e>0$, $h_i\geq\mu>0$. Let $f$ be a load with a known
bound $\sum_i\max\{f_i,0\}\leq B$, $B>0$, and minimize
$E_K(x)=x^\top Kx/2-f^\top x$ over $x\geq0$.
Write $U_B=B/\mu$ and $T(x)_i=\min\{x_i,U_B\}$.
For the physical OP3 objective one may use $h_i=\bar\alpha d_i$,
$\mu=\bar\alpha$, $B=1$, $f=e_v-\lambda d$.
For a supplied restricted face, the diagonal grounding additionally
includes $\gamma$ times its original cut degree.

\begin{lemma}[A two-piece source VWF embedding; \statusproved]
\label{lem:op3-capped-grounding-vwf}
The optimum $u$ satisfies $0\leq u_i\leq U_B$. Replace its quadratic
vertex term by
\begin{equation}
 q_i(x)=
 \begin{cases}
  h_i x^2/2-f_i x,&0\leq x\leq U_B,\\
  h_iU_Bx-h_iU_B^2/2-f_i x,&x\geq U_B.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:op3-capped-grounding-vwf}
\end{equation}
Each $q_i$ is a size-two vertex weighting function in the source's
Definition 3.2. For the source-form objective
$E_{\mathrm{cap}}(x)=\sum_e c_e(a_e^\top x)^2/2+\sum_i q_i(x_i)$,
\begin{equation}
 E_K(T(x))\leq E_{\mathrm{cap}}(x)\leq E_K(x),\qquad x\geq0,
 \quad \min E_{\mathrm{cap}}=\min E_K.
 \label{eq:op3-capped-grounding-equivalence}
\end{equation}
Thus boxing any source-form relative-energy candidate preserves its
approximation guarantee for the original grounded objective. Constructing
the source instance on a supplied graph costs $O(n+m)$ words and work.
This construction does not supply a locally discovered graph.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $f_+$ denote the coordinatewise positive part. For an optimum $u$,
$r=f_+-Ku$ is nonnegative: on positive coordinates it equals $f_+-f$,
and on zero coordinates it equals $f_++\sum_j c_{ij}u_j$.
Summing gives $\sum_i h_i u_i\leq\sum_i(f_+)_i\leq B$, so $u_i\leq U_B$.

The derivative $q_i'(x)=h_i\min\{x,U_B\}-f_i$ is continuous,
nondecreasing, concave and constant on its final ray; $q_i(0)=0$.
These are precisely the source VWF conditions. Beyond $U_B$ its slope
is $h_iU_B-f_i\geq B-f_i\geq0$. Boxing cannot increase a vertex term,
and $|T(x)_i-T(x)_j|\leq|x_i-x_j|$ cannot increase an edge term.
On the box the capped and original objectives agree. Finally
\[
 E_K(x)-E_{\mathrm{cap}}(x)
 =\frac12\sum_i h_i(x_i-U_B)_+^2\geq0.
\]
This proves the two inequalities and equality of minima. There are two
constant-size pieces per vertex and the same supplied edges.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Source distinction.}
An unmodified positive quadratic vertex penalty is not a VWF under
Chen--Peng--Wang's Definition 3.2, PDF p.\ 14: its derivative is not
constant on a final ray \citep{chen2021diffusion}.
\Cref{lem:op3-capped-grounding-vwf} supplies the missing objective mapping.
It does not discharge Assumption 3.15 or make the source construction local.

\begin{proposition}[Exponential Schur scale on a fully positive physical face;
 \statusproved]
\label{prop:op3-positive-face-range-loss}
There is a simple unweighted graph on $n+1$ vertices, with
$\alpha=1/3$, $\epsappr=1/(24n)$, and every original obstacle coordinate
strictly positive, whose exact constrained-Schur energy has a local
quadratic piece with a nonzero edge coefficient at most
\begin{equation}
 \frac12\left(\frac2{11}\right)^{n-2}.
 \label{eq:op3-exponentially-small-coupling}
\end{equation}
Thus polynomial numerical range in only the original graph and requested
parameters is not inherited by arbitrary exact Schur elimination.
This is not a trace of the source algorithm or an OP3 lower bound.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Take a path $1,\ldots,n$ and a hub $0$ adjacent to every path vertex;
the hub is the physical seed. The degrees are $n$ at the hub, two at
path endpoints, and three at internal path vertices. Set
$\gamma=\bar\alpha=1/2$ and $\lambda=1/(48n)$.
For $G=M^{-1}$, its nonnegative Neumann expansion gives
$G_{00}\geq1/n$ and $G_{i0}\geq\gamma/(d_i n)\geq1/(6n)$ for $i>0$.
Since $M\one=\bar\alpha d$, the unconstrained solution is
$u=Ge_0-(\lambda/\bar\alpha)\one$. It is positive, with
$u_i\geq1/(8n)$ for every path vertex, and hence is the obstacle optimum.
Its full original volume is $4n-2$.

Retain ports $\{0,1,n\}$ and eliminate $\{2,\ldots,n-1\}$.
The interior principal matrix is tridiagonal with diagonal three and
adjacent entries $-1/2$. Let $\Delta_k$ be its size-$k$ determinant:
$\Delta_0=1$, $\Delta_1=3$, and
$\Delta_k=3\Delta_{k-1}-\Delta_{k-2}/4$.
The successive ratios are at least $11/4$, by induction, so
$\Delta_k\geq(11/4)^k$.
The positive endpoint coupling in the Schur matrix is
\[
 c_{1n}=\frac{(1/2)^{n-1}}{\Delta_{n-2}}
 \leq\frac12\left(\frac2{11}\right)^{n-2}.
\]
For example this follows from the endpoint cofactor of the interior
tridiagonal inverse; the hub is retained and is not another eliminated
route between the endpoints. All eliminated coordinates are strictly
positive at the physical optimum. Continuity therefore gives a neighborhood
of its port values where constrained elimination equals this quadratic
Schur formula. All original input scales and $1/\epsappr$ are polynomial
in $n$, while the displayed nonzero coefficient is exponentially small.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Small total edge weight can be pruned with an absolute budget;
 \statusproved]
\label{lem:op3-grounded-edge-pruning}
For the supplied grounded matrix $K$ above, remove edges of total weight
$W$ by subtracting their Laplacian terms, and call the result $\widetilde K$.
Grounding and the load remain unchanged. If $u,\widetilde u$ are their
obstacle optima, then
\begin{equation}
 0\leq E_K(\widetilde u)-E_K(u)\leq\frac{WB^2}{2\mu^2},
 \qquad
 \|\widetilde u-u\|_\infty^2\leq\frac{WB^2}{\mu^3}.
 \label{eq:op3-pruning-accuracy}
\end{equation}
More generally, let $w\geq0$ have error at most $\zeta$ for the pruned
objective. Its boxed version $T(w)$ has original energy error at most
$\zeta+WB^2/(2\mu^2)$. In particular, $W\leq\mu^3\delta^2/(2B^2)$
and $\zeta\leq\mu\delta^2/4$ imply
$\|T(w)-u\|_\infty\leq\delta$.
This controls approximation, not exact support preservation.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The pruned matrix still has the same $h_i\geq\mu$ and is positive
definite. Both optima lie in $[0,U_B]^n$ by
\cref{lem:op3-capped-grounding-vwf}. Write $R=K-\widetilde K$.
For any vector $z$ in that box, $0\leq z^\top Rz\leq WU_B^2$.
Optimality in the pruned objective therefore gives
\[
 E_K(\widetilde u)-E_K(u)
 \leq\tfrac12\widetilde u^\top R\widetilde u-\tfrac12u^\top Ru
 \leq WU_B^2/2.
\]
The original constrained quadratic gap is at least
$\mu\|\widetilde u-u\|_2^2/2$, proving the first claim.
Boxing decreases the pruned quadratic objective, so the same comparison
with an additional $\zeta$ applies to $T(w)$. Its gap and strong convexity
give the final bound. Two vertices already permit a support change:
take both shunts $\mu$, one small edge $c<\mu$, and
$f=(1,-c/(2\mu))$. The second optimum coordinate is positive before
deletion and zero afterwards.
\end{proof}

For a supplied $m$-edge matrix, deleting every edge smaller than
$\mu^3\delta^2/(2mB^2)$ respects the displayed combined budget.
The scan costs $O(n+m)$, and the logarithm of the resulting edge threshold
depends only logarithmically on $m$, $1/\mu$, $1/\delta$ and $B$.
This observation addresses tiny edge coefficients. It does not compress
arbitrary vertex-weighting functions, control recursive shifted breakpoints,
or supply a cumulative local-work argument. The exact range witness is
therefore not an obstruction to certified approximate pruning.

\paragraph{Audit.}
\texttt{grounded\_pruning\_range.py} verifies original KKT positivity on
hub-paths through 257 physical vertices, determinant and coupling identities,
and independent dense Schur elimination through 17 vertices. Grounded
weighted atlas cases check exact energy and coordinate bounds, row-margin
preservation, boxed approximate candidates, and the capped VWF inequality.
Two designed cases verify strict support changes after pruning.
\texttt{GROUNDED\_PRUNING\_RANGE\_AUDIT.json} records all cases and hashes.
All full matrices and exact solves here are validators or supplied inputs;
no source algorithm's speed or graph discovery is implemented by this audit.
