\section[Mixed additive accuracy]{Compression, refinement and acceleration with additive budgets}
\label{sec:op3-mixed-additive-oracles}

\paragraph{Scope.}
The global compressor incurs an explicit additive error. A normalized
proximal objective may have zero optimum or an arbitrarily small negative
optimum, so an additive budget must not be converted to a relative one
using an unstated positive gap margin. This section keeps the two errors
separate throughout the supplied numerical interface. The statements are
\statusproved{} drafts awaiting independent review. The accelerated
convergence inequality remains the same explicit CPW Theorem 8.3 source
import. Source Algorithm 6's elimination-before-refinement/compression
schedule is context, not a fast recursive solver imported by these proofs.

For a feasible-origin objective $\Phi$ with $\Phi(0)\leq0$ and finite
attained minimum $\Phi^*$, call a feasible output $q$ a
$(\beta,\eta)$ output when
\begin{equation}
 \Phi(q)\leq\Phi^*/\beta+\eta,\qquad \beta\geq1,\quad\eta\geq0.
 \label{eq:op3-mixed-oracle-contract}
\end{equation}
The parameter $\eta$ is an absolute objective error. It is distinct from
both $\epsappr$ and $\rho$, and from the scalar compression error $\Xi$.

\begin{lemma}[Compression preserves a mixed contract; \statusproved]
\label{lem:op3-mixed-compression-embedding}
Suppose the implemented compressor gives
$E(x)\geq\widehat E(x)\geq2E(x/2)-2\Xi$ on the feasible domain,
as in \cref{cor:op3-global-vwf-energy-embedding}.
If $w$ is a $(\beta,\zeta)$ output for $\widehat E$, then
\[
 E(w/2)\leq E^*/(2\beta)+\zeta/2+\Xi.
\]
If $E$ is the exact reduced objective of a supplied forest, its original
reconstruction at $w/2$ has this same energy. In particular, a
$(2,\xi)$ compressed oracle with $\Xi\leq\xi/2$ gives a
$(4,\xi)$ output for the uncompressed objective.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The lower compression inequality gives
$E(w/2)\leq\widehat E(w)/2+\Xi$, and
$\widehat E^*\leq E^*$. Apply the mixed contract. Scaling by one half
preserves each domain $[L_i,\infty)$ because $L_i\leq0$.
The forest recovery theorem preserves the reduced energy exactly.
No division by $|E^*|$ occurs, including when $E^*=0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Refinement with a retained additive error; \statusproved]
\label{thm:op3-mixed-relative-refinement}
Suppose a $(\beta,\xi)$ oracle is supplied for every exact residual
instance of $\Phi$, with fixed $\beta\geq1$ and $\xi\geq0$.
Starting at $a_0=0$, call it on
$R_a(z)=\Phi(a+z)-\Phi(a)$ and form $a+z$.
A paid guard may keep $a$ if its energy would increase, and the canonical
common shift of \cref{prop:op3-vwf-canonical-input-gap} may be applied
before that guard in the graph/VWF setting. For every $J$,
\begin{equation}
 \Phi(a_J)-\Phi^*\leq
 (1-1/\beta)^J\bigl(\Phi(0)-\Phi^*\bigr)+\beta\xi.
 \label{eq:op3-mixed-refinement-recurrence}
\end{equation}
For desired relative $\delta>0$ and additive $\zeta\geq0$, choose
$\xi=\zeta/\beta$ and the least $J$ with
$(1-1/\beta)^J\leq\delta/(1+\delta)$.
Then $a_J$ is a $(1+\delta,\zeta)$ output.
The call count is $O(1+\beta\log(1+1/\delta))$; it has no dependence
on the unknown absolute optimum or on $\log(1/\zeta)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The residual minimum is $-g$, where $g=\Phi(a)-\Phi^*\geq0$.
Its oracle gives $R_a(z)\leq-g/\beta+\xi$, hence the next gap is at
most $(1-1/\beta)g+\xi$. Canonicalization only decreases the candidate
energy. If the guard rejects an increasing candidate, that candidate gap
exceeds $g$ but is still bounded by the displayed upper bound; keeping
$g$ therefore preserves the inequality. Summing the geometric recurrence
gives \eqref{eq:op3-mixed-refinement-recurrence}. Initially
$\Phi(0)-\Phi^*\leq|\Phi^*|$, because $\Phi(0)\leq0$.
The chosen $J$ and $\xi$ then give
$\Phi(a_J)-\Phi^*\leq\delta|\Phi^*|/(1+\delta)+\zeta$, which is
the claimed mixed contract. The same calculation includes zero initial
gap. For $\beta=1$ one call suffices; for $\beta>1$, use
$1-1/\beta\leq\exp(-1/\beta)$ for the call bound.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Implemented residual and guard work.}
On a supplied graph with $m$ edges and total explicit vertex piece count
$S$, residual construction uses
$f_i(a_i+t)-f_i(a_i)+(\mathcal Ga)_it$ on $t\geq L_i-a_i$.
It copies all translated splits and coefficients and computes the sparse
graph product in $O(m+S)$ work and $O(S)$ newly allocated words.
Both vertex value anchors and the original objective guard are charged.
Binary search per vertex costs at most linear total piece work.
Each accepted candidate, failed guard, common-shift scan and output vector
is charged. Excluding the supplied oracle's own work and allocations,
$J$ iterations therefore cost $O(J(m+S))$ work and $O(JS)$ new words.
No graph access is classified as local merely because the original
objective arose from a local-diffusion question.

\begin{theorem}[An accelerated mixed tolerance without a gap floor; \statusproved]
\label{thm:op3-mixed-proximal-accuracy}
Under the matrix and convexity hypotheses of
\cref{thm:op3-generic-relative-proximal}, give the algorithm $\eta>0$.
Choose the least dyadic $T$ with $T^2\geq256\kappa$ and set
\begin{equation}
 \delta_{\rm rel}=\frac1{66\cdot2^{30}\kappa^3},\qquad
 \zeta=\frac{\eta}{2^{32}\kappa}.
 \label{eq:op3-mixed-proximal-policy}
\end{equation}
At every source accelerated center $x$, require a feasible output obeying
\begin{equation}
 E_x(q)\leq E_x^*/(1+\delta_{\rm rel})+\zeta,
 \qquad E_x=\Phi_x-\Phi_x(0).
 \label{eq:op3-mixed-normalized-proximal-contract}
\end{equation}
Starting at zero, the output satisfies
\[
 \Phi(y_T)-\Phi^*\leq\frac{\Delta+\eta}{32},
 \qquad\Delta=\Phi(0)-\Phi^*.
\]
All centers have $\mathcal H$ seminorm squared at most
$64\kappa^2(\Delta+\eta)$ and all feasible iterate gaps are at most
$2\kappa(\Delta+\eta)$. Neither $\Delta$ nor a positive gap floor is
needed to set the policy. If $\Phi(0)\leq0$, the output in particular
satisfies the weaker reusable $(2,\eta)$ contract.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $D=\Delta+\eta>0$ and $e_0=D/(2^{30}\kappa)$ for analysis only.
The gap-distance inequality gives $\|u\|_{\mathcal H}^2\leq2\kappa\Delta
\leq2\kappa D$. As in the generic proof,
$3\sum_{j=1}^Tj\sqrt{2e_0}<\sqrt{\kappa D}/8$.
Every already justified source prefix $k\geq2$ therefore has gap at
most $8\kappa D/(k+1)^2$. At the first center $x=0$, the mixed
contract gives $\Phi(q)\leq\Phi(0)+\zeta$, so its gap is at most $D$.
All previous feasible gaps are thus at most $2\kappa D$. The exact
extrapolation identity bounds $\|x\|_{\mathcal H}^2\leq64\kappa^2D$.

Since $\Phi_x\geq\Phi$, one has
$\Phi_x(0)-\Phi_x(q)\leq\|x\|_{\mathcal H}^2/2+\Delta
\leq33\kappa^2D$. This difference is allowed to be negative.
Rearranging \eqref{eq:op3-mixed-normalized-proximal-contract} gives
\begin{align*}
 0\leq e_x&\leq\delta_{\rm rel}\bigl(\Phi_x(0)-\Phi_x(q)\bigr)
                +(1+\delta_{\rm rel})\zeta\\
 &\leq\frac{D}{2^{31}\kappa}+
          \frac{\eta}{2^{31}\kappa}\leq e_0.
\end{align*}
This closes the induction and yields final gap at most $D/32$.
The source seminorm inequality has the same limiting justification as
in \cref{thm:op3-generic-relative-proximal}. When $\Delta=0$, $D=\eta$
still makes the argument valid and permits small nonzero model errors;
the proof does not require exact oracle output in that case.
If $\Phi(0)\leq0$, then $\Delta\leq-\Phi^*$, so
$\Phi(y_T)\leq31\Phi^*/32+\eta/32\leq\Phi^*/2+\eta$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A compatible numerical recursion schedule; \statusproved]
\label{cor:op3-mixed-recursive-budgets}
Consider one invocation of \cref{thm:op3-mixed-proximal-accuracy}.
Suppose every supplied inner graph has a valid forest/core decomposition,
and a recursive $(2,\eta_{\rm child})$ oracle is available on compressed
coarse instances. Exactly eliminate each inner forest once. Refine the
resulting coarse objective using $\beta=4$ and per-call error $\xi=\zeta/4$.
For each refinement residual use total compression error at most $\zeta/8$
and request the recursive oracle with
\begin{equation}
 \eta_{\rm child}=\frac\zeta4
                 =\frac{\eta_{\rm parent}}{2^{34}\kappa_{\rm parent}}.
 \label{eq:op3-mixed-child-additive-budget}
\end{equation}
Then refinement and exact recovery provide
\eqref{eq:op3-mixed-normalized-proximal-contract}.
Each inner solve uses $O(1+\log(2+\kappa))$ recursive calls. Along a
path of depth $d$, the logarithm of the inverse additive budget increases
by exactly $34d\log2+\sum_{\ell<d}\log\kappa_\ell$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The compression lemma gives a $(4,\zeta/4)$ coarse residual output:
the two additive terms are at most $\zeta/8$ each.
The refinement theorem gives the requested relative and additive pair,
and original forest recovery preserves energy. The relative target in
\eqref{eq:op3-mixed-proximal-policy} has logarithm
$O(1+\log(2+\kappa))$. Iterating
\eqref{eq:op3-mixed-child-additive-budget} gives the last statement.
Geometric refinement and the accelerated weighted error sum already charge
their repeated calls, so no guessed global call count is used as an error
divisor. This is a conditional numerical interface: existence, construction
cost, coefficient ranges and size reduction of the recursive graphs and
oracles remain explicit hypotheses.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Dense validator and exact precision control.}
The implemented compressed-forest refinement audit
eliminates a supplied forest, exports its root functions, constructs exact
residuals, calls the unchanged global compressor, applies the paid energy
guard and reconstructs the original coordinates. It uses a dense exact
coarse policy solely as a validator. The policy is proved below, and its
matrix work is separate from the supplied fast-oracle hypotheses.
Reference coarse candidates are rounded upward to a dyadic grid, which
preserves lower-bound feasibility; the grid is refined until the mixed
energy inequality passes exactly. This replaces an initial reference
choice whose rational denominators grew beyond the decimal serializer's
limit. The failed serialization was not a failed mathematical inequality.
Dyadic integer rounding and its backtracking are reference work, not a
production bit-complexity or floating-point guarantee.

\begin{lemma}[A finite forward-piece reference policy; \statusproved]
\label{lem:op3-vwf-forward-quadratic-policy}
For a connected supplied graph and finite-piece VWFs with nonnegative
total final slope, the following dense policy reaches an exact minimum
in at most one plus the total number of scalar splits bound-QP calls.
Start at the original lower vector. Form the quadratic pieces containing
the current vector, continue them quadratically to the right, and minimize
that quadratic subject to the current vector as lower bound. Advance the
piece indices to the new vector, using right-continuous ties, and repeat
until no index changes. The temporary lower bounds introduce no spurious
original optimality constraints.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every quadratic matrix is the connected Laplacian plus nonnegative diagonal
curvature. When some curvature is positive it is positive definite.
Otherwise all selected pieces are affine final rays, so the total linear
slope is nonnegative. The dense positive-face obstacle reference can also
solve this latter bound QP: its total load is nonpositive, and with zero
residual on the current face the inactive residuals cannot all be strictly
positive. Thus strict face growth never requests the singular full
Laplacian; every solved proper principal submatrix is positive definite.
The resulting bound QP has the usual feasible complementary gradient.

New iterates never decrease, by their imposed lower bound. At a piece
advance, concavity of the scalar derivative implies that the newly selected
piece derivative at the previous vector is no greater than the old
quadratic continuation. At a coordinate already above its original lower
bound, the old quadratic gradient is zero by induction, so the new gradient
there is nonpositive. If that coordinate stays at its temporary lower
bound in the next solve, neighbor increases only decrease its gradient,
because Laplacian off-diagonals are nonpositive. Bound-QP optimality requires
a nonnegative gradient there, hence it must be zero. If the coordinate
increases, ordinary complementary optimality again makes it zero.
Coordinates at their original bound need only a nonnegative gradient.
This proves original-bound KKT for every quadratic model.

Each nonterminal iteration advances at least one piece index, and no index
decreases. After at most the total split count such advances, the quadratic
and original nonlinear derivatives agree at the output. The original
feasible complementary gradient is therefore exact, so convexity gives a
global minimum. All dense solves and piece-policy bookkeeping remain
validator costs; this bound is not a fast coarse-oracle implementation.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Remaining obligations.}
The new mixed contracts remove one unknown-optimum error-budget obstacle.
They do not establish the sizes, weight ranges or construction costs of
future recursive preconditioners. Compression size still depends explicitly
on its largest split, curvature and requested additive budget. The supplied
graph recurrence and unknown-support graph discovery must both be proved
before inferring original OP3 work. General OP3 remains \statusopen{}.
