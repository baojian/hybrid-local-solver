\section{A paid exact frontier elimination reference}
\label{sec:op3-exact-frontier}

The following implementation makes the frontier algebra in
\path{FRONTIER_ELIMINATION_PROBE.md} executable, including original graph
access and fill generation. It is a reference for testing representations.
Its worst-case cubic bound is weaker than the source quadratic bound in
\cref{sec:source}; no improvement of general OP3 is claimed.

\begin{theorem}[Exact local frontier with a fill ledger; \statusproved]
\label{thm:op3-exact-frontier}
Let the original graph be finite, connected, simple and unweighted, with
at least two vertices. For $0<\alpha\leq1$ and $0<e<1$, put
\[
 \gamma=\frac{1-\alpha}{1+\alpha},\quad \bar\alpha=1-\gamma,
 \quad M=D-\gamma A,\quad \lambda=e/2,\quad \kappa=e/4.
\]
There is a deterministic local exact-real word algorithm returning $x\geq0$
with
\[
 0\leq e_v-Mx\leq(3e/4)d,\qquad
 V:=\operatorname{vol}(\operatorname{supp}x)<2/e.
\]
It reads each admitted original row once, or skips all rows if $\gamma=0$,
queries each discovered degree once, and materializes the final physical
potentials in one reverse pass. Let $p_i$ be the number of live effective
neighbors just before eliminating admitted vertex $i$, after exposing its
original row, and put $\Phi=\sum_i(p_i+1)^2$. Its work and total allocated
words are
\[
 O\bigl((1+V+\Phi)\log(2+V)\bigr)
 \subseteq O\bigl((1+V)^3\log(2+V)\bigr),
\]
with $O((1+V)^2)$ live words. These bounds include original adjacency and
degree access, all fill and transformed-load updates, failed admission
checks, history and final output $\bar\alpha D x$. They have no dependence
on target $\alpha$ or ambient size in this arithmetic model. No bit or
floating-point complexity guarantee is asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $b=e_v-\lambda d$. For an admitted set $S$, let $x^S$ be the exact
face solution $M_{SS}x^S_S=b_S$, zero outside $S$. Initially $S$ is empty.
For each known unadmitted label $j$, maintain the exact quantities
\[
 h_j=b_j-M_{jS}x^S_S,
 \qquad s_j=M_{jj}-M_{jS}M_{SS}^{-1}M_{Sj}.
\]
After each completed pivot, the stored edges between two unadmitted
vertices $j,k$ contain only their accumulated fill conductance
$M_{jS}M_{SS}^{-1}M_{Sk}\geq0$. Original edges between two unadmitted
vertices have not yet been read and are not included in this stored fill.
This distinction avoids assuming that the whole frontier graph is supplied.

Known labels are queued exactly when $h_j>\kappa d_j$; a queued flag and
intrusive FIFO prevent duplicate insertions. On admitting $i$, expose its
original row once. A newly encountered label gets one degree query and
initial data $s_j=d_j$, $h_j=b_j$. It could not have an original edge to
an earlier admitted vertex, since that earlier row would already have
discovered it. Add conductance $\gamma$ to every still-unadmitted original
neighbor, combining it with any stored fill on that pair. Skip original
edges back to eliminated vertices, whose contributions are already in the
Schur state. If $\gamma=0$, no original row is needed.

Let $w_{ij}>0$ be the resulting effective conductances and $\sigma=s_i>0$.
Store $z_i=h_i/\sigma>0$ and the coefficients $w_{ij}/\sigma$, then make
the exact scalar Schur updates
\[
 s_j\gets s_j-w_{ij}^2/\sigma,\qquad
 h_j\gets h_j+w_{ij}z_i,\qquad
 w_{jk}\gets w_{jk}+w_{ij}w_{ik}/\sigma\quad(j<k).
\]
Remove all incident pairs of $i$. The Schur formula proves the stated
invariant inductively; positive definiteness of $M$ gives all positive
pivots and remaining diagonals. Loads only increase, so a queued vertex
stays eligible until removed. No old potential coordinate is written in
these discovery updates.

For completeness, every admission is safe before its original row buffer
is allocated. The enlarged face solution has new coordinate $z_i>0$ and
old coordinates
\[
 x^{S\cup\{i\}}_S
 =x^S_S+M_{SS}^{-1}(-M_{Si})z_i\geq x^S_S\geq0.
\]
Its original residual is $\lambda d$ on the enlarged face and is
nonnegative outside, because outside coordinates are zero and all physical
couplings are nonpositive. Since the new vector is nonzero,
\[
 \lambda\operatorname{vol}(S\cup\{i\})
 \leq\sum_j(e_v-Mx^{S\cup\{i\}})_j
 =1-\bar\alpha\sum_jd_jx^{S\cup\{i\}}_j<1.
\]
Thus checking $\lambda(V+d_i)<1$ before allocating or reading that row
always succeeds in the exact model. A huge outside degree is only a single
degree response unless it passes this certified admission gate. Also, if
$u$ is the full $\lambda$ obstacle, then
$M_{SS}u_S\geq b_S-M_{S\bar S}u_{\bar S}\geq b_S$ and inverse
positivity gives $x^S\leq u$. This comparison vector is never computed.

There are fewer than $2/e$ admissions. The number $R$ of discovered labels
is at most $1+V$, because every label except the seed first appears in an
admitted original row. At termination every known unadmitted load is at
most $\kappa d_j$. Its residual is $\lambda d_j+h_j\leq3e d_j/4$.
Every undiscovered vertex has no original neighbor in $S$ and is not the
seed, so its residual is zero. Active residuals equal $\lambda d_j$.
Reverse the saved pivot history, setting
\[
 x_i=z_i+\sum_j(w_{ij}/\sigma_i)x_j,
\]
where later admitted values have already been reconstructed and never
admitted values remain zero. This is exact back substitution, giving the
face solution just certified. Emit its physical and probability records in
one paid pass.

Here are the data-structure costs, including historical allocations.
Use an insertion-only AVL map for labels, with stable vertex records and
indices; a second AVL map indexes unordered pairs of these indices. Each
pair has two intrusive incidence-list records, so retiring all incident
edges costs $O(p_i)$. An inactive pair key is retained in the map: it has
an eliminated endpoint and will never be used again. There are at most
$R(R-1)/2$ historical pair records, and both maps have logarithmic lookup,
insertion and balancing cost. All original row entries, whose total is at
most $V$, use the label map. Each pivot allocates $O(1+p_i)$ gathered
neighbor and lifting records, updates $p_i$ loads and diagonals, and makes
$\binom{p_i}{2}$ pair updates, each charged to the pair map. AVL recursive
frames, constant-size temporaries, queue operations, discarded buffers and
dynamic history capacities are included in the total-allocation bound.
The reverse pass costs $O(1+\sum_i(1+p_i))$. These bounds yield the stated
work and allocation formula. Since $p_i<R\leq1+V$ and $|S|\leq V$,
$\Phi=O((1+V)^3)$. Historical pairs and all saved lifting coefficients
occupy $O((1+V)^2)$ live words. No dense inverse, scan of all original
vertices or repeated full face solve is part of the algorithm.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Cubic work of explicit pair expansion; \statusproved]
\label{prop:op3-explicit-frontier-cubic}
For every integer $m\geq4$, there is an original graph of volume $18m$
with $\alpha=1/3$ and $e=1/(4m)$ on which the above explicit frontier
representation makes at least $\binom m3$ fill-pair updates. Its admitted
original volume is $\Theta(m)$, while the ambient volume exceeds $4/e$.
This is a representation-specific obstruction, not an OP3 work lower bound.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Take the path $0,\ldots,8m$, attach $m$ leaves at the seed $0$, and retain
all original degrees. The seed degree is $m+1$, and its first face value is
\[
 x_0=\frac{1-\lambda(m+1)}{m+1}
 =\frac{7m-1}{8m(m+1)},\qquad \lambda=\frac1{8m}.
\]
It is eligible. Each attached leaf then has transformed load
\[
 h=-\lambda+\tfrac12x_0
 =\frac{5m-3}{16m(m+1)}>
 \kappa=\frac1{16m}.
\]
All $m$ leaves are therefore queued and remain eligible. Eliminating the
seed creates a positive clique among them; positive fill updates never
remove an edge between surviving leaves. If $k$ of these leaves remain,
eliminating one has at least $k-1$ other leaf neighbors and explicitly
updates at least $\binom{k-1}{2}$ pairs. Summing over the $m$ leaf pivots
gives $\sum_{k=1}^m\binom{k-1}{2}=\binom m3$, independent of their
queue order. The seed and all leaves contribute $2m+1$ to $V$, while
\cref{thm:op3-exact-frontier} gives $V<8m$. The graph's $18m$ volume is
greater than $4/e=16m$. The first clique is rank one; implicit algebraic
representations and the existing fast tree results are not ruled out.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Implemented audit; Measured.}
\path{frontier_exact.py} implements both AVL maps, stable records,
intrusive incidence lists and queue, original row access, Schur updates and
one final reconstruction. The full audit checks 7,305 original ACL outputs,
44,149 final residual rows and 12,986 exact prefix states. Independent
dense face, inverse-column and obstacle calculations are validators only;
they are absent from the producer. Prefix checks also verify AVL balance,
pair-list consistency, queue eligibility and the original-volume guard.
The audit records 6,349 actual pivots, 60,747 fill updates and 12,900 load
publications. Exact gate ties are checked on both initial and final states.

Additional profiles include endpoint paths, cycles, seeded sparse regular
graphs, signed labels larger than a machine integer, and two-vertex graphs
with $\alpha$ through $2^{-1024}$. The latter use two pivots and the same
529 charged units at all four tested parameter scales; this is an arithmetic
observation, not a bit bound. Three private huge hubs, with degrees through
$2^{1024}$, expose only their seed leaf row. The $m=64$ star-and-tail
profile makes 45,760 fill updates despite admitted volume 131. Parameters,
source hash and the full cost ledger are in \path{FRONTIER_EXACT_AUDIT.json}.
The next missing primitive is an implicit representation that also pays for
transformed-load threshold reporting under adaptive positive admissions.
General OP3 remains \statusopen.
