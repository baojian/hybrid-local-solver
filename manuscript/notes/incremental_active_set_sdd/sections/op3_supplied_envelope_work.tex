\section{The complete work reduction from a supplied envelope}
\label{sec:op3-supplied-envelope-work}

\begin{theorem}[Paid significant-envelope preparation and solve; \statusproved]
\label{thm:op3-supplied-envelope-work}
Let $G$ be a connected simple unweighted graph on at least two vertices,
accessed by original degree and adjacency-list queries, with point seed
$v$, $\alpha\in(0,1]$, $\epsappr\in(0,1)$ and $p\in(0,1)$.
Let $u$ be the original obstacle solution for
$M=D-\gamma A$, $b=e_v-(\epsappr/2)d$ and
$\gamma=(1-\alpha)/(1+\alpha)$. Suppose distinct labels for a set
$U\supseteq\{i:u_i>\epsappr/8\}$ are supplied. Write
$V=\operatorname{vol}_G(U)$ for its original degree volume.
There is a supplied algorithm using
\[
 (1+V)\operatorname{polylog}
 (2+V+\alpha^{-1}+\epsappr^{-1}+p^{-1})
\]
exact-real word work and total allocated words on every execution.
It returns \textnormal{FAIL} or a nonnegative sparse potential $x$;
with probability at least $1-p$ its output obeys
\[
 0\leq e_v-Mx\leq\epsappr d,\qquad
 \operatorname{supp}(x)\subseteq\operatorname{supp}(u),\qquad
 \operatorname{vol}_G(\operatorname{supp}x)<2/\epsappr.
\]
The only adjacency lists read are those of $U$. A preliminary one-degree
shortcut can return without reading even those lists. Outside degree
queries are unnecessary, except for the seed query before the shortcut.
The output approximation is $\bar\alpha Dx$, with original degrees and
$\bar\alpha=1-\gamma$. The algorithm does not discover $U$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First use \cref{prop:op3-sharp-seed-only-gate}. If
$\epsappr d_v\geq\gamma/(1+\gamma)$, return the potential
$x_v=\max\{0,d_v^{-1}-\epsappr\}$ and zero elsewhere.
If it is positive, the exact obstacle is positive at the seed because
$b_v=1-(\epsappr/2)d_v>0$; moreover $d_v<1/\epsappr$.
Thus the shortcut also has the claimed support and volume bounds.
It includes $\alpha=1$. Otherwise
\[
 \epsappr d_v<\frac\gamma{1+\gamma}<\frac12,
 \qquad \gamma>\epsappr.
\]
Here $b_v>0$ forces $u_v>0$ by its original KKT row, and
$d_vu_v\geq(Mu)_v=b_v$ gives
$u_v\geq d_v^{-1}-\epsappr/2>3\epsappr/2$.
Consequently every valid envelope contains the seed.

Sort its labels by a paid merge sort and use binary search for membership.
Read and store every original degree first, then scan each original row
in $U$ exactly once. There are $V$ entries, and $|U|\leq V$ because
all original degrees are positive. During these scans construct the
induced graph, recording an edge only at its smaller endpoint. This
costs $O((1+V)\log(2+V))$ comparisons, arithmetic, reads and copied
or allocated words. No outside adjacency list is needed to recognize
an induced edge. If an optional volume limit is supplied, the degree
pass can reject an oversized set before reading any row.

Find the seed component $C$ of the induced graph by a paid traversal.
Every other component has load $-(\epsappr/2)d_i<0$ at all vertices,
so its restricted energy is nonnegative on the nonnegative orthant,
with unique minimum at zero. Discard these components. On $C$ let
$d_i^{\mathrm{in}}$ be induced degree, and form
\[
 K=\gamma L_{G[C]}+\operatorname{diag}(h),\qquad
 h_i=d_i-\gamma d_i^{\mathrm{in}}
     =\bar\alpha d_i+\gamma(d_i-d_i^{\mathrm{in}}).
\]
This is exactly the original principal matrix $M_{CC}$, with
$h_i\geq\bar\alpha d_i\geq\bar\alpha$. There are no edges from
$C$ to another induced component, so its induced degrees agree with
those computed in $U$. In particular, replacing original degrees with
induced degrees in the diagonal would be incorrect.

Apply the size-two capped grounding embedding with positive load mass
at most one, $\mu=\bar\alpha$, and cap $U_B=1/\bar\alpha$.
Write $f_i=(e_v)_i-(\epsappr/2)d_i$ on $C$ and put
\[
 \delta=\epsappr/8,\qquad t=\bar\alpha\delta^2/2,\qquad
 B_0=1/(2\bar\alpha),\qquad
 Z=256(1+V+\bar\alpha^{-1}+\epsappr^{-1})^4.
\]
These are computable using the supplied parameters and degree pass.
Here $-E_C^*\leq B_0$: on the original nonpositive sublevel,
the quadratic lower bound and positive load mass at most one give
this standard scale, also directly by completing the square at the
seed and dropping other nonnegative terms. The cap lemma preserves
the minimum. Its terminal slopes $T_i=h_iU_B-f_i$ are nonnegative.
Every initial range in the complete supplied theorem is bounded by $Z$:
\[
 \begin{split}
 R_0&\leq\max\{1,\bar\alpha^{-1}\},\qquad
 S_0=\sum_i T_i\leq V/\bar\alpha+(\epsappr/2)V,\\
 C_0&=\sum_i h_i\leq V,\qquad W_0\leq V,\qquad
 c_0^{-1}=\gamma^{-1}<\epsappr^{-1},\\
 B_0+1&\leq1+(2\bar\alpha)^{-1},\qquad
 t^{-1}=128/(\bar\alpha\epsappr^2).
 \end{split}
\]
The edge minimum is used only when $|C|\geq2$; a singleton is minimized
by the scalar piece scan. The bounds for total curvature and terminal
mass refer to the two-piece embedding, whose affine tail has zero
curvature. Since $\bar\alpha^{-1}\leq\alpha^{-1}$, the logarithm
of $Z$ has the claimed parameter dependence. The preceding gate removes
a possible extra $\log(1/(1-\alpha))$ from the edge minimum.

The supplied input size is $O(1+V)$. Invoke
\cref{cor:op3-complete-supplied-absolute} with gap $t$, confidence $p$
and these range bounds. All its failed executions have the stated
work and allocation caps. On a good execution, box the feasible
output to $[0,U_B]$ without increasing the original energy gap.
Strong convexity $K\succeq\bar\alpha I$ then makes its coordinate
error from the restricted optimum at most $\delta$.
Extend by zero and subtract $\delta$ coordinatewise with truncation
at zero. \Cref{cor:op3-significant-envelope-ACL} gives the original
ACL certificate, support containment and strict volume bound.
All boxing, clipping, normalization, label recovery and output words
cost $O(1+V)$ and are included. No ambient graph-size quantity occurs.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{What remains conditional.}
A local procedure that pays
$\epsappr^{-1}\operatorname{polylog}(2+\alpha^{-1}+\epsappr^{-1}+p^{-1})$
work and supplies such a $U$ of original volume $O(1/\epsappr)$ would
combine with this theorem to produce the corresponding original ACL
output. If the finder is randomized, split confidence between finding
and solving, and cap the supplied degree volume before the row pass.
This is a precise sufficient local-search contract; no such arbitrary-graph
finder is proved here. It also makes no promise of literally retaining
every exact nested face of the original active-set realization.

\paragraph{Measured construction checks.}
The exact audit \texttt{supplied\_envelope\_work.py} checks 6,177
preparations on 2,059 physical inputs. It verifies 258 principal-matrix
identities and numerical reference compositions, 1,806 range quantities,
and 29,223 original residual rows. It exercises 41 discarded off-seed
coordinates and 159 positive cut-grounding coordinates. Three privately
represented stars have outside hub degrees $2^{20}$, $2^{80}$ and
$2^{1024}$; the valid one-leaf envelope reads no hub row and has a
parameter bound independent of that ambient size. Three oversized
envelopes are rejected by the degree pass before any adjacency row.
Dense obstacle solutions and feasible objective perturbations are
identified validators, not implementations of the full VWF solver or
an envelope finder. The durable source and backend hashes are in
\path{SUPPLIED_ENVELOPE_WORK_AUDIT.json}; the full run took 2.256 seconds.
