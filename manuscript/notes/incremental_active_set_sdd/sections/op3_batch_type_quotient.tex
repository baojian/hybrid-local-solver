\section{Complete eligible batches and exact original twin quotients}
\label{sec:op3-batch-type-quotient}

The individual grouped update in \cref{thm:op3-frontier-response-groups}
can repeat its small matrix work once per physical vertex. The following
construction instead takes every currently eligible member in one batch.
It computes exact original twin classes from rows that this same batch
has already paid to expose. A supplied global type partition is unnecessary.

\begin{theorem}[Batched quotient frontier; \statusproved]
\label{thm:op3-batch-type-quotient}
On the original connected simple unweighted graph, fix
$0<\alpha\leq1$, $\lambda>0$ and $\kappa\geq0$. A local deterministic
exact-real word algorithm returns $x\geq0$ such that
\[
 0\leq e_v-Mx\leq(\lambda+\kappa)d,
 \qquad (e_v-Mx)_i=\lambda d_i\quad\text{if }x_i>0,
 \qquad M=D-\gamma A.
\]
Its original positive-support volume is $V<1/\lambda$, and it reads
exactly those positive rows once. On any graph, let $t$ be the total
number of consumed batch classes and $f$ the maximum number of completed
frontier groups after a nonempty batch. Its work and total allocated words
are $O((1+V+t(t+f)^2)\log(2+V))$, with $f\leq2^t-1$; take $f=0$
when there is no batch. If the ambient graph has at most $k$
uncolored neighborhood types, its total work and allocated words are
\begin{equation}
 O\left((1+V+(1+k)^3)\log(2+V)\right),
 \label{eq:op3-batch-type-work}
\end{equation}
with $O(1+V+(1+k)^2)$ live words. It receives neither $k$ nor a type
partition and has no target-teleportation factor in this arithmetic model.
All degree queries, full rows, sorting, tries, heap changes, quotient
factors, group refinement, failed gates, retained history and final output
are charged. Setting $\kappa=0$ returns the exact physical obstacle;
setting $(\lambda,\kappa)=(\epsappr/2,\epsappr/4)$ returns original ACL
output with upper residual margin $3\epsappr/4$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Keep the individual-degree heaps and stable original-label dictionary
from \cref{thm:op3-frontier-response-groups}. A completed frontier group
$t$ has common original adjacency into the admitted set $S$, common
transformed load offset $q_t$, and shared fill matrix $C$, so
\[
 h_i=q_t-\lambda d_i,\qquad
 (M/M_{SS})_{ij}=M_{ij}-C_{g_i,g_j}.
\]
The diagonal convention includes $C_{g_i,g_i}$. Initially only the seed
is known, in a group with $q=1$ and $C=0$; newly discovered groups have
base $q=0$ and zero prior fill.

\emph{The batch and its row budget.}
At the beginning of a round take every known member with
$q_{g_i}>(\lambda+\kappa)d_i$. Repeated minimum extraction from each
heap finds exactly this set $B$, without scanning its ineligible members.
Let $H$ be the original Schur block on $B$. It is an SPD M-matrix, so
$z=H^{-1}h_B>0$. The old face correction is nonnegative as well.
Thus the enlarged restricted solution is positive on $S\cup B$, has
residual $\lambda d$ there and nonnegative residual everywhere else.
The unit-source mass identity gives
\[
 \lambda\vol(S\cup B)\leq\sum_i(e_v-Mx^{S\cup B})_i
 =1-\bar\alpha\sum_i d_i x_i^{S\cup B}<1.
\]
This certifies the whole batch's row volume before any of its rows are
read. Mark and remove the batch members, then read their original rows
once. Store the row entries and discover their new labels and degrees.

\emph{Paid full-row classes.}
Partition $B$ by the original uncolored twin relation, refined by old
response-group identity and original degree. This relation is transitive:
for three distinct pairwise consecutive twins, the three mutual adjacency
bits agree and all external adjacency bits agree. Each class is either
a clique or an independent set. Its full open rows are equal in the
independent case, and its full closed rows are equal in the clique case.
Sort each paid original row, form open and closed keys with the two
metadata fields, and insert them into separate tries with AVL child maps.
Merge equal keys by union by size. This constructs exactly the desired
classes, with no row query at an unadmitted vertex. The total key length
is $O(|B|+\vol(B))$; sorting, trie steps and union-find paths cost this
quantity times $O(\log(2+V))$, including all temporary allocations.

Write $a=1,\ldots,\tau$ for these classes, $n_a$ for their sizes,
$d_a$ for their original degrees, $g_a$ for their old group and $c_a$
for their internal clique bit. All future couplings are constant on a
class because its members are twins in the \emph{original graph}.
The row-constant quotient of $H$ is
\begin{align*}
 \overline H_{aa}&=d_a-\gamma c_a(n_a-1)-n_a C_{g_a,g_a},\\
 \overline H_{ab}&=-n_b(\gamma A_{ab}+C_{g_a,g_b}),\qquad a\ne b.
\end{align*}
Its symmetric form is $K=N\overline H$, $N=\operatorname{diag}(n_a)$.
Indeed, if $T$ is the class-indicator matrix, then $HT=T\overline H$
and $K=T^THT\succ0$. Original adjacency bits $A_{ab}$ are obtained
by binary search in the already sorted representative rows. The producer
factors $K$ once by explicit rational LDL elimination. It never forms
an inverse or a matrix indexed by all physical batch members.

\emph{Future refinement and Schur updates.}
Traverse the cached batch rows once more. A touched unadmitted vertex
records the indices of adjacent batch classes. Sort and deduplicate
these indices. A trie keyed by its old group and this signature creates
one new group per distinct key. Move only touched old members from their
heaps; untouched members stay in their original heap without a copy or
traversal. Fresh labels use a separate empty-prior-group key. Empty old
groups leave the live list but retain their identity for reconstruction.
Every moved member is paid by an original incidence in this batch.

Let $f$ be the number of resulting live groups. For each such group $t$,
let $q_t^0,C_{tu}^0$ denote its inherited offset and matrix entries;
for a fresh group these are zero. Put
\[
 W_{at}=C^0_{g_a,t}+\gamma\mathbf1_{\{a\text{ adjacent to }t\}},
 \qquad h_a=q_{g_a}-\lambda d_a.
\]
Exact twins make the displayed adjacency a whole-class bit. Solve
\[
 z=K^{-1}Nh,\qquad Y_{\cdot t}=K^{-1}N W_{\cdot t},
 \quad t=1,\ldots,f,
\]
using the one retained factor within this transaction, and update
\begin{equation}
 q_t=q_t^0+\sum_a n_a W_{at}z_a,
 \qquad C_{tu}=C_{tu}^0+\sum_a n_a W_{at}Y_{au}.
 \label{eq:op3-batch-quotient-update}
\end{equation}
These are the exact physical Schur identities. Rebuild only the $f$ by
$f$ scalar matrix, assigning new dense indices to stable group objects.
No heap membership is traversed to change such an index. The algebra
costs $O(\tau^3+\tau^2 f+\tau f^2+f^2)$; adjacency searches and the
smaller bookkeeping terms fit the same expression with the logarithm
in \cref{eq:op3-batch-type-work}. The number of old groups is at most
$f+\tau$: an old group with remaining members has a distinct future
descendant, and every other old group contributes a consumed class.

\emph{One reverse reconstruction.}
Save each class's physical member list, old parent group, $z_a$, row
$Y_{a\cdot}$, and the future group identities. Also save the split and
fresh-discovery records. Initially every group aggregate is zero.
In reverse transaction order use
\[
 x_a=z_a+\sum_t Y_{at}\,s_t,
 \qquad s_t=\sum_{j\text{ in the historical future group }t}x_j.
\]
Assign this value to every original member of class $a$. Then undo the
future splits by adding child aggregates to their old parents and clearing
the children; clear the fresh groups, whose members were previously
unknown. Finally add $n_a x_a$ to each old batch parent group. The same
historical-membership induction as in the individual grouped lift proves
all these sums and all reconstructed coordinates. It never expands a
historical future group. Scalar matrix entries and old forward $q$ values
need not be retained. At termination all remaining loads are at most
$\kappa d$, while unseen residuals are zero. This proves the original
certificate, and for $\kappa=0$ the full strictly convex obstacle KKT system.

\emph{Adaptive and global type bounds.}
Completed groups have distinct nonzero adjacency columns into the admitted
set: refinement preserves distinct old prefixes, while a fresh column has
zero old prefix and a nonzero new signature. Each consumed exact twin
class contributes only one adjacency bit to an unadmitted vertex. Thus
$f\leq2^t-1$, without any promise on the ambient graph. Summing the
transaction costs with $\sum_j\tau_j=t$ and at most $t$ rounds gives
$O(t(t+f)^2)$ algebra and group bookkeeping. The paid row, sorting, trie
and heap terms contribute $O((1+V)\log(2+V))$ as before.

For accounting only, separate the physical seed from the rest of its
original type. There are at most $k+1$ resulting source-colored types.
Every nonseed type's remaining members are discovered together by the
first admitted adjacent row, and no completed frontier group splits them.
They have identical original degrees. Consequently either all of a known
type is eligible in a round or none is. Every nonempty round therefore
consumes at least one whole type, and a type can occur in a batch only
once. The exact full-row partition may merge accounting types, so
\[
 \#\text{rounds}\leq k+1,
 \qquad \sum_j\tau_j\leq k+1,
 \qquad f_j\leq k\quad\text{after the seed round}.
\]
In particular, $\sum_j\tau_j^3$, $\sum_j\tau_j^2f_j$,
$\sum_j\tau_jf_j^2$ and $\sum_jf_j^2$ are all $O((1+k)^3)$.
All original rows total $V$; labels, row sorts, trie keys, heap insertions
and incidence-driven moves cost $O((1+V)\log(2+V))$. This includes
unsuccessful terminal gates and all phase lists, not only the quotient
arithmetic. The bound holds on every execution since there is no randomness.

For live space, all retained physical member/row records and heap
capacities use $O(1+V)$ words. The saved numerical lift coefficients total
$\sum_j\tau_j f_j=O((1+k)^2)$; split/fresh signatures fit the same
bound. The old and new scalar matrices, current quotient factor and
coupling arrays have quadratic type size. Current sort, trie and union-find
buffers use $O(1+V)$ live words and can be discarded after the transaction.
Old group heaps are not duplicated; geometric heap capacities are bounded
by total paid insertions. This proves the stated live bound. Exact scalar
bit lengths and floating-point stability are outside this word model.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Improved structural ACL and canonical RPPR work; \statusproved]
\label{cor:op3-batch-type-canonical}
Without a supplied type partition, the batched producer gives original ACL
work/allocation
\[
 O\left((1+\epsappr^{-1}+(1+k)^3)\log(2+\epsappr^{-1})\right).
\]
With $\lambda=\rho$ and $\kappa=0$, followed by
\cref{thm:op3-rational-canonical-materialization}, it emits dyadic canonical
RPPR output of objective gap at most $\epsobj$ in
\[
 O\left((1+\rho^{-1}+(1+k)^3)\log(2+\rho^{-1})
       +(1+\rho^{-1})\log(2+\epsobj^{-1})\right).
\]
The original four-type family in \cref{prop:op3-four-type-output-lower-bound}
continues to match the ACL accuracy power up to logarithms. These are
structural results, not a resolution of arbitrary-graph OP2 or OP3.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proposition}[Two limits of this exact quotient; \statusproved]
\label{prop:op3-batch-type-limits}
Induced-batch twins cannot replace full-original-row twins in this
construction. Also, the implemented batched quotient still has a cubic
accuracy cost on a finite binary-tree family; the obstruction concerns
its dense quotient factorization, not the graph problem.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Take the path $0,1,2,3,4$, physical seed $2$, $\alpha=1/3$,
$\lambda=1/64$ and $\kappa=1/128$. The first batch is the seed, and
its next eligible batch is $\{1,3\}$. Both members have original degree
two and identical empty neighborhoods within that batch. They are not
original twins: their future neighbors are respectively $0$ and $4$.
After the seed pivot the Schur block is
\[
 H=\begin{pmatrix}2-c&-c\\-c&2-c\end{pmatrix},\qquad c=\gamma^2/2.
\]
The final frontier fill obeys $C_{00}-C_{04}=\gamma^2/2>0$, since the
contrast eigenvalue of $H$ is two. A one-class constant quotient loses
this contrast and does not give the required original Schur matrix.

For the second claim use the finite binary trees and parameters of
\cref{thm:op3-grouped-binary-cubic}: depth $4R+8$,
$\lambda=1/(64\,2^R)$, $\kappa=\lambda/2$ and
$\alpha=1/(96R-1)$. That proof's original radial gate inequality makes
all of the next level eligible after each completed ball through level
$R-1$. The batch policy therefore consumes one full level in each round
through level $R$. All $2^R$ vertices in that last batch have distinct
full original rows because their two future children differ; there are
no edges within the level. Thus its exact twin quotient has order $2^R$.
The implemented dense LDL routine executes exactly $\binom{2^R}{3}$
off-diagonal inner products in that factorization alone, giving
$\Omega(\epsappr^{-3})$ work. Here final support volume is
$\Theta(\epsappr^{-1})$ by the same positive-ball and mass bounds, and
inverse teleportation is only logarithmic in inverse accuracy. Existing
fast tree algorithms are unaffected by this representation-specific cost.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Implemented exact audit; Measured.}
\texttt{BATCH\_TYPE\_QUOTIENT\_AUDIT.json} records 7,728 original ACL
outputs and 7,298 exact physical obstacles, checking 88,759 final original
residual rows. Independent validators check 40,641 prefix states,
100,668 face/inverse-column solves, 37,149 reverse coordinates,
27,098 historical group sums and 25,618 distinct nonzero frontier-column
bounds. Mixed clique/independent quotient blow-ups contribute 1,687
global type checks. Signed gate offsets and teleportation reach $2^{-1024}$;
five arbitrary signed-label cases and private huge hubs also pass.

Four-type blow-ups with multiplicities $4,16,64$ keep four batches,
five consumed classes and the same quotient-work count, while paid
original volume grows from $120$ to $32,640$. Four private double-stars
with up to 256 near leaves and $2^{1024}$ unread remote leaves use exactly
two batches and two quotient classes. These are charged-operation
diagnostics, not claims of a floating-point speed improvement.
The six binary-tree executions are explicitly nonterminal prefixes,
not ACL outputs. They check 27 level batches and 246 independently lifted
coordinates. At $R=6$, the last quotient has order 64 and executes 41,664
off-diagonal LDL inner products. Reference graph partitions, full original
obstacles, dense inverses, radial solves and historical member snapshots
are validator work; they are never passed to the producer.
