\section[Diffusion accuracy and ACL]{A feasible diffusion objective guarantee implies ACL accuracy}
\label{sec:op3-diffusion-accuracy}

\paragraph{Status and interface.}
The following \statusproved{} drafts concern finite simple connected
unweighted graphs, the physical point seed, original degrees, and exact-real
arithmetic. They convert a feasible objective approximation to an original
ACL certificate. They neither construct the candidate locally nor bound its
production work. General OP3 remains \statusopen.

Write $\gamma=(1-\alpha)/(1+\alpha)$, $\bar\alpha=1-\gamma$,
$M=D-\gamma A$, $\lambda=\epsappr/2$, and $b=e_v-\lambda d$.
Let $u$ minimize
\[
 E(x)=\tfrac12x^\top Mx-b^\top x,\qquad x\geq0,
 \qquad E_*:=E(u).
\]
The original residual is $r=e_v-Mu$. If $\epsappr d_v\geq1$, the zero
output already has an original ACL residual certificate. Thus the
nontrivial case below assumes $\epsappr d_v<1$.

\begin{lemma}[Known objective scale and coordinate error; \statusproved]
\label{lem:op3-diffusion-energy-scale}
In the nontrivial case,
\begin{equation}
 -\frac1{2\bar\alpha}\leq E_*< -\frac{\epsappr}{8}.
 \label{eq:op3-diffusion-energy-scale}
\end{equation}
For every feasible $w\geq0$,
\begin{equation}
 E(w)-E_*\geq\tfrac12(w-u)^\top M(w-u)
 \geq\frac{\bar\alpha}{2}\sum_i d_i(w_i-u_i)^2.
 \label{eq:op3-diffusion-energy-error}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Complementarity gives $u^\top Mu=b^\top u$ and
$E_*=-b^\top u/2$. The original residual is nonnegative, so
$\bar\alpha\sum_i d_i u_i\leq1$ by
\cref{lem:op3-inactive-residual-mass}. Since $d_v\geq1$,
$b^\top u=u_v-\lambda\sum_i d_i u_i\leq u_v\leq1/\bar\alpha$.
For the other bound, the feasible singleton
$x=(b_v/d_v)e_v$ has energy $-b_v^2/(2d_v)$.
Here $b_v=1-\epsappr d_v/2>1/2$ and $1/d_v>\epsappr$.
Optimality proves \cref{eq:op3-diffusion-energy-scale}.

Expand $E(w)-E(u)$ about $u$. The linear term is
$(Mu-b)^\top(w-u)\geq0$ by feasibility and complementarity.
Finally $M=\gamma L+\bar\alpha D\succeq\bar\alpha D$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Multiplicative energy to original ACL; \statusproved]
\label{thm:op3-diffusion-accuracy-bridge}
Set $\delta=\epsappr/8$ and $\eta=\bar\alpha^2\delta^2$.
Suppose a candidate $w\geq0$ satisfies the negative-energy convention
\begin{equation}
 E(w)\leq\frac{E_*}{1+\eta}.
 \label{eq:op3-diffusion-relative-interface}
\end{equation}
Define $\widehat u_i=\max\{0,w_i-\delta\}$.
Then $\widehat u$ has support contained in the exact obstacle support and
\begin{equation}
 0\leq e_v-M\widehat u\leq\epsappr d.
 \label{eq:op3-diffusion-original-certificate}
\end{equation}
Consequently $\bar\alpha D\widehat u$ is an ACL approximation in the
project's original namespace. The requested relative accuracy has only
logarithmic parameter size:
$\log(1/\eta)=O(1+\log(1/\alpha)+\log(1/\epsappr))$.
This is an accuracy implication, not a local complexity theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By the assumption and \cref{lem:op3-diffusion-energy-scale},
\[
 E(w)-E_*\leq\frac{\eta}{1+\eta}|E_*|
 \leq\frac{\eta}{2\bar\alpha}
 =\frac{\bar\alpha\delta^2}{2}.
\]
Equation~\eqref{eq:op3-diffusion-energy-error} and $d_i\geq1$ imply
$|w_i-u_i|\leq\delta$ for every $i$. Downward clipping therefore gives
$0\leq u_i-\widehat u_i\leq2\delta$ and $\widehat u_i=0$ wherever
$u_i=0$. Put $e=u-\widehat u$ and $\widehat r=e_v-M\widehat u=r+Me$.
At a positive exact coordinate, $r_i=\lambda d_i$ and
\[
 (\lambda-2\gamma\delta)d_i\leq\widehat r_i
 \leq(\lambda+2\delta)d_i.
\]
These bounds lie in $[0,\epsappr d_i]$. At an exact zero coordinate,
$\widehat u_i=e_i=0$ and
$\widehat r_i=\mathbf1_{i=v}+\gamma\sum_{j\sim i}\widehat u_j\geq0$;
also $\widehat r_i=r_i-\gamma\sum_{j\sim i}e_j\leq r_i\leq\lambda d_i$.
This proves \cref{eq:op3-diffusion-original-certificate}. The logarithmic
statement follows from $\alpha\leq\bar\alpha\leq2\alpha$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{What the conversion does and does not resolve.}
The negative-energy convention matches Definitions 3.7--3.8 of
Chen--Peng--Wang \citep{chen2021diffusion}. The source's Assumption 3.15
(PDF p.\ 17) controls all encountered nonzero numbers; Claim 8.21's proof
(p.\ 52) uses it in selecting a proximal tolerance. The known initial
energy scale above does not establish that assumption for intermediate
eliminations or residual problems. Nor does an approximate candidate's
small support certify that its computation read only a local graph.
This theorem imports no source algorithm. Its audit obtains candidates
from an exact reference solution and halving, which are validation costs.

\subsection{A separate curvature diagnostic for local flow claims}
\label{sec:op3-diffusion-path-curvature}

\begin{proposition}[Unit edges do not give unit support curvature;
 \statusproved]
\label{prop:op3-flow-path-curvature}
For each integer $m\geq3$, a unit-weight path with $m+2$ vertices and
unit sink capacities has a diffusion optimum with exactly $m$ positive
coordinates and strictly inactive next coordinate. Nevertheless the
smallest Dirichlet curvature on that positive support is at most
$6/((m+1)(2m+1))$. The same quantity times two is still less than the
minimum edge weight. Thus a minimum-edge-weight lower bound for this
curvature is \statusrefuted, under either the $L$ or $2L$ Hessian convention.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Number the path $0,\ldots,m+1$, take source
$\Delta=(m+1/2)e_0$, and minimize
$\tfrac12x^\top Lx+(\one-\Delta)^\top x$ over $x\geq0$.
The vector $x_i=(m-i)^2/2$ for $i<m$ and zero otherwise has zero
gradient on its positive coordinates, gradient $1/2$ at $m$, and gradient
$1$ at $m+1$. It is an optimum by convex KKT conditions.
On $S=\{0,\ldots,m-1\}$, put $z_i=m-i$.
The principal matrix $K=L_{SS}$ has diagonal $(1,2,\ldots,2)$ and
adjacent entries $-1$. Thus $z^\top Kz=m$, whereas
$z^\top z=m(m+1)(2m+1)/6$. The Rayleigh principle proves the bound,
and $12/((m+1)(2m+1))<1$ for $m\geq3$.
\end{proof}

This checks one proposed parameter interpretation, not an algorithmic
lower bound for OP3 or a refutation of an unspecified source parameter.
The original Fountoulakis--Wang--Yang running-time theorem retains a
Dirichlet-curvature factor \citep{fountoulakis2020pnorm}.
The weighted-flow statement's parameter meaning requires reconciliation
before importing it as an OP3 certificate \citep{yang2023weighted}.

\paragraph{Exact audit.}
\texttt{diffusion\_accuracy\_bridge.py} checks all connected graph-atlas
graphs of orders two through five, every physical seed, three teleportation
parameters and three ACL tolerances. Its 1,233 original graph cases give
11,574 feasible-candidate original-residual certificates, including 3,291
clipped false-positive coordinates. Seven path instances check the full
KKT conditions and exact Rayleigh quotients. The record
\texttt{DIFFUSION\_ACCURACY\_BRIDGE\_AUDIT.json} distinguishes reference
work from any proposed local implementation.
