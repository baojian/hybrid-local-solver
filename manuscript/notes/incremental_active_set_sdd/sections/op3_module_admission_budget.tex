\section[Residual-paid union updates]{A causal residual budget for union-response updates}
\label{sec:op3-module-admission-budget}

\paragraph{Status and measured interface.}
The lemmas below are \statusproved{} drafts awaiting independent review.
They bound a particular curve-update charge along legal original obstacle
admissions; they are not a complete local graph solver. All degrees are
original full degrees, including every as-yet unadmitted vertex. A freshly
constructed full response on an arbitrary supplied subset is a validator,
not a free local update. General OP3 remains \statusopen.

\begin{lemma}[Residual mass pays for common inactive inputs; \statusproved]
\label{lem:op3-inactive-residual-mass}
Let $u$ be any restricted obstacle response for physical seed $v$ and
load $e_v-\lambda d$, extended by zero outside the restricted set. Its
original residual $r=e_v-Mu$ satisfies
\begin{equation}
 r\geq0,\qquad \sum_i r_i
   =1-\bar\alpha\sum_i d_i u_i\leq1.
 \label{eq:op3-inactive-residual-mass}
\end{equation}
Consequently, if $L$ inactive nonseed vertices each have original residual
strictly larger than $\lambda D$, then $L\lambda D<1$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At a positive coordinate, obstacle equality gives $r_i=\lambda d_i>0$.
At a zero coordinate, including one outside the restricted set,
$r_i=\mathbf1_{i=v}+\gamma\sum_{j\sim i}u_j\geq0$.
Sum and use $\one^\top M=\bar\alpha d^\top$. Nonnegativity of the
remaining residuals proves the last assertion. This uses the original
ambient identity for analysis; computing its full sum is not an algorithmic
operation being supplied for free.
\end{proof}

Consider the following fixed finite graph. Its core is the complete join
of a universal vertex $w$ with the disjoint union of two stars $A,B$.
The centers are $a,b$. Star $A$ has $k$ old leaves with distinct private-leaf
counts $1,\ldots,k$, and $T$ further leaves, called twins, with no private
leaves. Each twin has exactly the two original neighbors $w,a$, hence
original degree two. The old leaves of $B$ may also have private leaves.
The physical seed is $w$ or $a$. The current core grows by strictly positive
original gates. For every reached core vertex its private leaves are
handled by their exact obstacle kernel, whether zero or positive.

Let $s$ be the number of reached old leaves in $A$ just before a twin
admission, and let $L$ count all still-unadmitted twins, including that one.
The response of the current $A$ uses original degrees throughout.

\begin{lemma}[Genuine events under a legal twin admission; \statusproved]
\label{lem:op3-twin-event-budget}
Immediately after admitting this twin, the $A$ response has at most
$2s+2$ genuine breakpoints. If $s>0$, it has fewer than $2/(\lambda L)$
breakpoints. This counts actual events, including coincidences, rather
than treating every admitted identical twin as a new event.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Each old leaf and its private-leaf group contribute at most two events.
All reached twins have the same singleton kernel
$(h-2\lambda)_+/2$ and are mutually nonadjacent, so their aggregate
contributes a single event, independently of their multiplicity. The
center, if present, contributes one event. Sum and the order-preserving
join transforms create no additional events, proving $2s+2$.
This also holds when the center has not yet entered and $A$ is a union.

If $s>0$, let $q_*$ be the largest private-leaf count among those $s$
old leaves. Distinct positive integer counts imply $q_*\geq s$.
Their positivity implies
\[
 \gamma(u_w+u_a)>\lambda(2+q_*).
\]
Indeed, the eliminated private-leaf kernel has strictly positive slope
after its birth, whose input is exactly $\lambda(2+q_*)$; no old leaf
is the physical seed. Each unadmitted twin has original residual
$\gamma(u_w+u_a)$. Thus \cref{lem:op3-inactive-residual-mass} gives
$L\lambda(2+q_*)<1$. It follows that
$2s+2<2/(\lambda L)$. The newly admitted twin changes no old leaf count
in this step, so the same event bound applies to the new response.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Harmonic cumulative union-stream charge; \statusproved]
\label{prop:op3-harmonic-union-stream}
At every twin admission for which both sides are present, suppose the
old and new $A$ responses and the unchanged $B$ response are available.
Update their union by the least expensive of these three known operations:
remove old $A$ from the old sum and add new $A$; add $B$ to new $A$; or
add new $A$ to $B$. If $J$ such updates occur, the total number of streamed
genuine breakpoints is at most
\begin{equation}
 2J+\frac{2}{\lambda}H_J,
 \qquad H_J=\sum_{j=1}^J\frac1j,
 \label{eq:op3-harmonic-union-stream}
\end{equation}
with $H_0=0$. In particular, this part of the update work and all its
persistent allocations are
$O(\lambda^{-1}\log(2+\lambda^{-1})\log(2+N))$ exact-real words.
This prices the union operation, not construction of its supplied inputs,
module recognition, reporters or all operations of an outer local solver.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
By \cref{lem:op3-module-summand-removal}, the three stream lengths are
$k_{A,\mathrm{old}}+k_{A,\mathrm{new}}$, $k_B$ and
$k_{A,\mathrm{new}}$, respectively. Their minimum is at most
$k_{A,\mathrm{new}}$. The preceding lemma bounds it by
$2+2/(\lambda L)$ in all cases, including $s=0$.
The successive values of $L$ at these updates are distinct positive
integers: every twin is admitted once and the graph is fixed. For any
$J$ such integers their reciprocal sum is at most $H_J$, giving
\cref{eq:op3-harmonic-union-stream}. It is unnecessary to replace this
by a logarithm of the unknown ambient twin count $T$.

Each measured twin remains positive and has original degree two, so the
usual original support-volume bound gives $2\lambda J<1$. Therefore
$J=O(1/\lambda)$ and $H_J=O(\log(2+1/\lambda))$.
Each streamed hinge costs $O(\log(2+N))$ operations and copied nodes;
fixed overhead contributes $O(J\log(2+N))$. This proves the displayed
word bound without asserting that any unavailable input curve is free.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact audit and consequence.}
\texttt{module\_admission\_cost.py} keeps all future degrees fixed, admits
only strictly positive original gates, checks every original core and
private-leaf equation, and verifies the full residual-mass identity at
each step. It executes all three union-update routes and checks complete
affine identities with no redundant knots. Original-degree event bounds,
the twin residual budget and \cref{eq:op3-harmonic-union-stream} are checked
separately. Full response construction and candidate scans belong to the
reference ledger and are not charged as an implemented local solver.
The exact record is \texttt{MODULE\_ADMISSION\_COST\_AUDIT.json}.

This family therefore does not establish a superlinear cumulative
union-rebuild obstruction at the OP3 scale. A single expensive update or
an arbitrary-field curve with many events is insufficient evidence.
The useful general question is whether numerical response complexity can
be associated with original residual mass on the still-unadmitted
boundary. The special twin identity and distinct pendant-count argument
above do not provide that association for arbitrary modules or graphs.
