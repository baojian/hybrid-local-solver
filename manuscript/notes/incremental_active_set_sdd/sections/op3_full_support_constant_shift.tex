\section{An arithmetic full-support repair for the alpha floor}
\label{sec:op3-full-support-constant-shift}

\begin{lemma}[Oscillation without a proper-support premise; \statusproved]
\label{lem:op3-global-potential-oscillation}
Under the nonnegative-residual hypotheses of
\cref{lem:op3-superlevel-potential-cap}, write
$\operatorname{osc}(x)=\max_i x_i-\min_i x_i$.
On a connected graph with $n$ vertices,
\[
 \operatorname{osc}(x)\leq(n-1)/\gamma.
\]
This holds on full support too. Original degree-normalized neighborhood
averages $a_i=(Ax)_i/d_i$ obey
$\operatorname{osc}(a)\leq\operatorname{osc}(x)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Connect a maximizing vertex to a minimizing one by a simple path of at
most $n-1$ edges, and use the proved bound of $1/\gamma$ on each edge
difference. Every $a_i$ is an average of nonnegative-neighbor coordinate
values, so it belongs to $[\min_jx_j,\max_jx_j]$. All original degrees
are positive, and the asserted oscillation inequality follows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A purely arithmetic full-support floor wrapper;
\statusconditional]
\label{thm:op3-arithmetic-alpha-floor}
Under the native producer, graph and volume hypotheses of
\cref{thm:op3-small-alpha-floor}, the same effective parameter
$a_{\mathrm{eff}}=\max\{\alpha,\epsappr/(4B+\epsappr)\}$ suffices
with total work and allocated words
\[
 \mathcal W(a_{\mathrm{eff}},\epsappr/2,p/2,B)
 +O((1+B)\log(2+B)).
\]
There is no second numerical solve. On a native good execution, proper
support returns the same potential. Full support returns the native
potential plus one nonnegative constant, computed from its original
target residual. Success probability is at least $1-p/2\geq1-p$.
The extra work has no logarithmic dependence on target $1/\alpha$ in
the exact-real word model. Every output still has original support
volume at most $B$ on the good event.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the same native call and paid word, format, degree-volume and
support-closure checks as in the earlier wrapper. The unchanged-parameter,
empty-support and proper-support branches have already been proved.
Suppose instead that $\alpha<a_0=\epsappr/(4B+\epsappr)$ and the
native positive support is the full graph. Its volume is at most $B$
by the degree pass; all its rows and original degrees have been
materialized by the closure scan. Define
\[
 \rho_i=\frac{(e_v-M_{\gamma_\alpha}x)_i}{d_i},\qquad
 m=\min_i\rho_i,\qquad c=\frac{m}{\bar\alpha},\qquad
 y=x+c\one.
\]
Reject a negative $m$ on a bad native execution. On a good native
execution, with $\Delta=\gamma_\alpha-\gamma_0\geq0$,
\[
 \rho_i=\frac{(r_0)_i}{d_i}+\Delta\frac{(Ax)_i}{d_i}\geq0,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{osc}(\rho)
 \leq\frac{\epsappr}{2}
      +\frac{\Delta(n-1)}{\gamma_0}
 \leq\frac{\epsappr}{2}
      +\frac{(1-\gamma_0)B}{\gamma_0}
 =\epsappr.
\]
The first inequality uses the native original ACL certificate. The
second uses \cref{lem:op3-global-potential-oscillation}; positive
original degrees give $n-1\leq B$. The floor identity is the one
already proved in \cref{thm:op3-small-alpha-floor}.

Since $M_{\gamma_\alpha}\one=\bar\alpha d$ with $\bar\alpha>0$,
adding $c$ changes every degree-normalized original residual by exactly
$-\bar\alpha c=-m$. Hence
\[
 y\geq x>0,\qquad
 0\leq\frac{(e_v-M_{\gamma_\alpha}y)_i}{d_i}
      =\rho_i-m\leq\epsappr.
\]
It follows that $\bar\alpha Dy$ is the requested original ACL output.
The shift cannot add an unknown vertex, because this branch has already
proved that the entire ambient graph is precisely the positive support.

Compute the target residual from the stored rows using binary lookup
in the sorted support map. There are at most $B$ entries, each lookup
costs $O(\log(2+B))$, and the remaining degree divisions, minimum,
constant shift, label recovery and output copies cost $O(1+B)$.
All allocations and guards, including a negative-minimum rejection,
are charged on every execution. A very large $c$ still takes one word
in the stipulated arithmetic model; bit complexity and floating-point
cancellation are not claimed. Only the native producer is randomized,
so its failure budget $p/2$ is sufficient and the old fallback confidence
budget is unused slack. No source numerical algorithm is invoked by
this correction.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A general reserved residual margin; \statusproved]
\label{cor:op3-general-residual-margin-floor}
The same wrapper argument applies to a native accuracy $e_0$ with
$0<e_0<\epsappr$. Write $h=\epsappr-e_0$ and use
$a_0=h/(2B+h)$. Then $(1-\gamma_0)B/\gamma_0=h$.
Proper support increases its normalized residual upper bound by at
most $h$, while full support increases normalized residual width by at
most $h$ before the exact constant correction. Thus both branches
give target accuracy $e_0+h=\epsappr$, with the same charged maps,
degree pass, closure scan and arithmetic repair. The producer's work
and volume assumptions remain required.
\end{corollary}

\paragraph{What this improves and leaves open.}
The constant-shift wrapper strengthens the previous full-component
fallback without changing its volume premise or its proper-support
branch. For $B=O(1/\epsappr)$, the native computation can again be
restricted to $a\geq\Theta(\epsappr^2)$. Establishing the required
near-linear native work remains \statusopen. The full-support
counterexample to a uniform maximum bound remains valid: the correction
uses bounded oscillation while allowing a large common constant.

\paragraph{Implemented arithmetic audit.}
The exact script \texttt{small\_alpha\_constant\_shift.py} implements
the correction on materialized original rows. Its native inputs include
exact obstacles, nonconstant seed perturbations and common positive
shifts, all verified against the native ACL contract. The original
target residual, its normalized width and the final mass
$\bar\alpha\sum_i d_i y_i\leq1$ are checked exactly, including target
$\alpha=2^{-1024}$. The native producer remains an explicitly supplied
validator; there is no second solve even in the production-side repair.
Durable source/backend hashes and counts appear in
\path{SMALL_ALPHA_CONSTANT_SHIFT_AUDIT.json}.

The full run checks 3,243 original target certificates, including 1,554
constant corrections and 608 proper-support transfers, in 2.069 seconds.
It verifies 1,081 global oscillation bounds and 1,554 final mass bounds;
three invalid native residual minima are rejected. The largest computed
shift numerator has 1,069 bits, illustrating the distinction between the
stated word model and bit complexity.
