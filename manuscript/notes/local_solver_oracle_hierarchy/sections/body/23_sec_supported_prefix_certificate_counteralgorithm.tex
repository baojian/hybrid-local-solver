\section{The actual sparse certificate defeats supported-prefix polynomial
work}
\label{sec:supported-prefix-certificate-counteralgorithm}

The full-vector task in \cref{eq:path-exact-output-task} is deliberately
stronger than the project's sparse PPR task.  This distinction is decisive on
the same endpoint path.  The companion
\texttt{evolving\_support\_cg} note proves in
Theorem~\texttt{thm:endpoint-path-exact-cg-certificate} that ordinary exact CG
at $\alpha=n^{-2}$ does not satisfy its actual residual certificate before
step $n$.  The proof and its literal ledger are imported here rather than
repeated.  They do not say that every supported polynomial must follow the CG
Galerkin residual.
