\section{An exhaustive resource vector rather than a scalar oracle}
\label{sec:resource-vector}

A capability name is not a work bound.  For an execution $\mathcal E$, record
the vector
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C(\mathcal E):=\bigl(&
 C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},
 C_{\rm rec},C_{\rm resp},\\
 &M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit}
 \bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:resource-vector}
\end{equation}
Its coordinates have the following note-scoped meanings.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{0.14\textwidth}X@{}}
\toprule
Coordinate & Charged resource \\
\midrule
$C_{\rm adj}$ & Every degree reply and returned adjacency incidence, including
repeated reads.  A complete scan of $i$ costs $d_i$. \\
$R_{\rm adj}$ & Sequential batches of algorithm-chosen adjacency queries whose
choice depends on earlier graph replies.  Query work remains charged in
$C_{\rm adj}$. \\
$R_{\rm int}$ & Externally ordered online input/reply stages, such as a gate
that requests one certificate before deciding whether to continue.  This is
not an adjacency-adaptivity lower bound. \\
$C_{\rm pre}$ & Graph-dependent arithmetic performed before the seed is known
or before online continuation begins.  Seed-oblivious preprocessing is
reported rather than hidden in an amortization convention. \\
$C_{\rm ctl}$ & Online arithmetic and data-structure work that is neither a
row-recurrence action nor response computation: control flow, queues, heaps,
gates, bookkeeping, aggregation, and certificate logic belong here. \\
$C_{\rm rec}$ & Exposed-row actions, supported matrix--vector products,
diagonal actions, scalar combinations, orthogonalization, and restart work
used to generate recurrent numerical directions. \\
$C_{\rm resp}$ & Construction, update, application, and query computation for
factors, elimination messages, Schur maps, graph-dependent preconditioners,
sketches, or other implicit response objects.  Computing a response query is
charged here; emitting the resulting reply is charged in $C_{\rm emit}$. \\
$M_{\rm pers}$ & Peak graph-dependent state retained across online stages,
including recurrence vectors, factor records, fill, tables, sketches, and
cached keys. \\
$M_{\rm tmp}$ & Peak transient workspace in addition to persistent state,
including scratch vectors and temporary factor/application buffers.  Freeing
it after one operation does not remove this peak-space charge. \\
$C_{\rm mat}$ & Intermediate cell reads and writes whose purpose is to keep
every coordinate of a current active vector explicitly materialized.  Factor
and message writes are instead charged to $C_{\rm resp}$. \\
$C_{\rm emit}$ & Words or algebraic cells emitted as required scalar replies
and final output.  Arithmetic used to compute or verify them remains in
$C_{\rm ctl}$, $C_{\rm rec}$, or $C_{\rm resp}$. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

Every online arithmetic operation must be assigned to $C_{\rm ctl}$,
$C_{\rm rec}$, or $C_{\rm resp}$; preprocessing arithmetic belongs to
$C_{\rm pre}$.  Both persistent and transient peak space are reported.  Thus
the vector does not silently drop online control work, scratch space, or the
difference between computing and emitting a query answer.  Bit precision,
random bits, and failure probability are additional clauses.  A scalar
total-work bound may be formed only after every coordinate of
\cref{eq:resource-vector} is bounded or assigned an explicit weight.

This ledger gives a semantic division between recurrence and response.  A
\emph{row-recurrence action} produces a new active-space vector from the load,
previous active-space vectors, scalar linear combinations, fixed diagonal
maps, and charged applications of exposed rows.  Momentum, conjugate
directions, masking, restart, and reorthogonalization may all be allowed and
charged in $C_{\rm rec}$.  A graph-dependent linear map not generated by those
actions is a \emph{response object}: its construction or preprocessing,
persistent and transient representation, and every application are charged in
$C_{\rm pre}$ or $C_{\rm resp}$, $M_{\rm pers}$ and $M_{\rm tmp}$, and
$C_{\rm resp}$, respectively.  Thus an algorithm may have both nonzero
recurrence and response coordinates.  In particular, the word
\emph{preconditioned} does not decide its class.

For budgets $B_{\rm pre},B_{\rm resp},B_{\rm mem}$, let
$\mathsf{RowRec}[B_{\rm pre},B_{\rm resp},B_{\rm mem}]$ denote executions of
the preceding form with
\[
 C_{\rm pre}\leq B_{\rm pre},\qquad
 C_{\rm resp}\leq B_{\rm resp},\qquad
 M_{\rm pers}+M_{\rm tmp}\leq B_{\rm mem}.
\]
This is a budgeted family, not a single black-box first-order oracle.  A future
lower bound must additionally specify support masking, allowed fixed diagonal
maps, precision, and the charged cost of a row action.  Those choices may
change the theorem and are deliberately not smuggled into the name.

\begin{lemma}[A free exposed-system inverse oracle collapses recurrence
iterations; \ProvedStatus]
\label{lem:free-preconditioner-collapse}
Let $T\succ0$ and $h$ be completely exposed.  In exact arithmetic, suppose a
purported recurrence class permits the update
\begin{equation}
 z_{t+1}=z_t+P_t(h-Tz_t)
 \label{eq:preconditioned-residual-step}
\end{equation}
and simultaneously places no charge or restriction on graph-dependent
construction, persistent encoding, or application of $P_t$.  Then it solves
$Tz=h$ exactly in one update.  Consequently no lower bound on recurrence
iteration count or condition-number dependence \emph{after complete exposure}
can hold for that class.  The conclusion does not remove adjacency exposure,
preprocessing, response, memory, workspace, control, emission, or bit costs.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Choose $P_0=T^{-1}$.  From any $z_0$,
\[
 z_1=z_0+T^{-1}(h-Tz_0)=T^{-1}h.
\]
Constructing, encoding, and applying this inverse may each be expensive.  The
lemma proves only that those three capabilities cannot all be free
simultaneously; it does not prove that each one is independently necessary in
every valid lower-bound model.
\end{proof}

The lemma rules out a common but invalid definition: linear span plus a free
arbitrary preconditioner oracle.  An inverse hidden behind that oracle has the
power of persistent response.  The lemma is not an adjacency-query,
preprocessing, memory, or output lower bound.
