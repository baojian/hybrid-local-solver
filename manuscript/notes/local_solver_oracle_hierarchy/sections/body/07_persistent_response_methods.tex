\subsection{Persistent response methods}

Let $\mathsf{Response}$ contain adjacency-access methods that may retain
directed elimination messages, factor records, Schur complements, terminal
responses, or certified approximations to them.  A response method can leave
the current old-face vector implicit and perform one reverse materialization
at final output.

Its charge must include:

\begin{itemize}
\item construction and update of every response record;
\item fill, separator, or hierarchy storage;
\item all old-face reads and response applications;
\item boundary reporting, interval refinement, and rekeying;
\item final output and verification.
\end{itemize}

Allowing response state does not make an algorithm free or automatically
local.  Exact fill can be quadratic on cyclic expanders, and an eager boundary
demand array can require quadratic updates.  Those are lower bounds for the
named representations, not for all compressed or matrix-free response
methods.
