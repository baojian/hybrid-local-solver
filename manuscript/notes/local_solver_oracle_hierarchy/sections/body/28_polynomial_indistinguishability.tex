\subsection{Polynomial indistinguishability}

For a fixed-support linear-span class, use two locally indistinguishable
Stieltjes systems whose relevant Green-function values require polynomial
degree $\Omega(1/\sqrt\alpha)$ to separate.  The construction must preserve
the local output and accuracy convention and must not reveal the answer through
degrees or boundary labels.  On a sparse residual-certificate task it must
also rule out the constant-prefix slack-spreading mechanism in
\cref{thm:five-site-supported-prefix-counteralgorithm}; exact-CG
nontermination alone is not such a degree lower bound.  The tighter path task
adds a second obstruction: even when the certificate forces full support, the
broad class can propagate singleton coordinate directions and synthesize a
global profile in linear work.  A future class restriction must exclude this
mechanism explicitly and independently, or the graph family must defeat it.
