\label{sec:scope}

The shared matrix $Q$ fixes the numerical operator discussed below.  The note
does not adopt a repository-wide residual or convert between APPR, PPR, and
RPPR tolerances.  Whenever $\epsppr$ appears below, it has only the note-scoped
meaning
\begin{equation}
 \norm{D^{-1/2}(\widehat x-x^0)}_\infty\leq\epsppr.
 \label{eq:note-ppr-error}
\end{equation}
Explicit output means a word-RAM list of vertex labels and numerical values;
one listed coordinate costs at least one word operation.  The information and
capacity statements specialize to a single seed $s=e_v$ and do not choose an
implementable stopping certificate.

For the still-open computational separation, let $S_{\rm fin}$ denote the
final exposed active set and define the distinct final charged-volume symbol
\begin{equation}
 \nu_{\rm fin}:=\vol(S_{\rm fin})=\sum_{i\in S_{\rm fin}}d_i.
 \label{eq:final-charged-volume}
\end{equation}
This is not the vertex set $V$ and is not total work: repeated reads,
recurrence, response, control, materialization, and emission remain additional
ledger charges.
