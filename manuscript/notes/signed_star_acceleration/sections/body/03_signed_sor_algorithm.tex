\section{The explicit signed-SOR algorithm}
\label{sec:signed-star-algorithm}

Set
\begin{equation}
  t:=\sqrt\alpha,
  \qquad
  \lambda:=\frac{1-t}{1+t},
  \qquad
  \omega_\star:=1+\lambda^2
  =\frac{2(1+\alpha)}{(1+\sqrt\alpha)^2}.
  \label{eq:signed-star-optimal-parameters}
\end{equation}
For $\alpha\in(0,1)$ we have $0<\lambda<1$ and
$1<\omega_\star<2$.  Direct algebra gives the two identities
\begin{equation}
  1-\omega_\star=-\lambda^2,
  \qquad
  c_\alpha\omega_\star=2\lambda.
  \label{eq:signed-star-sor-identities}
\end{equation}
The negative sign in the first identity is the mechanism unavailable to a
nonnegative-residual method: every processed coordinate reflects a smaller,
opposite-sign residual.

\paragraph{Promised-star algorithm.}
Initialize $p=0$ and $r=\gamma_\alpha e_o$.  Define
\begin{equation}
  k_\star:=\left\lceil\frac{2}{\sqrt\alpha}\right\rceil.
  \label{eq:signed-star-predetermined-stop}
\end{equation}
Execute exactly $k_\star$ alternating blocks, starting with the center:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,label=\arabic*.]
\item On a center block, apply
      $p_o\leftarrow p_o+\omega_\star r_o$ and update the residual by
      \eqref{eq:signed-star-coordinate-primitive}.
\item On a leaf block, apply the same SOR relaxation once to every leaf.
      Leaf order is irrelevant because distinct leaves are not adjacent.
\item After block $k_\star$, output the explicit vector $p$.
\end{enumerate}

No unknown solution, semantic-error oracle, or adaptive tolerance appears in
the stopping rule.  The algorithm uses only $\alpha$ and the promised graph
structure.  If the star promise must be checked, scan the center and all its
leaves once; this costs $O(m)$ and does not change the result below.

\subsection{Two-state residual dynamics}

Star symmetry is preserved.  Write
\begin{equation}
  R:=r_o,
  \qquad
  T:=\sum_{\ell\in L}r_\ell=m r_\ell.
  \label{eq:signed-star-aggregate-residual}
\end{equation}
Here $T$ is the aggregate leaf residual, not the per-leaf value.  A center
block with pre-block center residual $R$ performs
\begin{equation}
  R\leftarrow-\lambda^2R,
  \qquad
  T\leftarrow T+2\lambda R,
  \label{eq:signed-star-center-block}
\end{equation}
where the $R$ on the right is the pre-block value.  A leaf block with
pre-block aggregate $T$ performs
\begin{equation}
  T\leftarrow-\lambda^2T,
  \qquad
  R\leftarrow R+2\lambda T.
  \label{eq:signed-star-leaf-block}
\end{equation}
Both block types cost exactly $m$: the center block makes one degree-$m$
relaxation, and the leaf block makes $m$ degree-one relaxations.

The critically damped recurrence generated by
\eqref{eq:signed-star-center-block}--\eqref{eq:signed-star-leaf-block} has a
polynomial prefactor.  A residual-only analysis sees that prefactor and pays a
logarithm.  The next section shows that the semantic error combines the two
layers with opposite signs and cancels precisely the harmful part.
