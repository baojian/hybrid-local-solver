\section{The mass-scale PageRank system and the hard star}
\label{sec:signed-star-model}

The shared source-aligned definition uses the symmetric variable $x^0$ and
the PPR mass vector $\pi=D^{1/2}x^0$.  For local propagation it is convenient
to write the equivalent mass-scale system explicitly.  Put
\begin{equation}
  c_\alpha:=\frac{1-\alpha}{1+\alpha},
  \qquad
  \gamma_\alpha:=\frac{2\alpha}{1+\alpha}=1-c_\alpha,
  \qquad
  H:=I-c_\alpha AD^{-1}.
  \label{eq:signed-star-mass-operator}
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}
  H\pi=\gamma_\alpha s,
  \qquad
  H=\frac{2}{1+\alpha}
      D^{1/2}QD^{-1/2}.
  \label{eq:signed-star-mapping}
\end{equation}
Thus this is only a diagonal change of variables and a positive scalar
rescaling of the shared system; it is not a different PageRank convention.
Because $H$ is a nonsingular $M$-matrix, $H^{-1}$ is entrywise nonnegative.

For a maintained mass iterate $p$, define the exact mass residual and semantic
error by
\begin{equation}
  r:=\gamma_\alpha s-Hp=H(\pi-p),
  \qquad
  \mathcal E(p):=\max_{u\in V}
      \frac{|p_u-\pi_u|}{d_u}.
  \label{eq:signed-star-residual-error}
\end{equation}
The theorem target is $\mathcal E(p)\le\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}$.

\subsection{The exact residual certificate and its scale}

Let $d=D\mathbf 1$ be the degree vector.  Since
$AD^{-1}d=A\mathbf1=d$,
\begin{equation}
  Hd=(1-c_\alpha)d=\gamma_\alpha d,
  \qquad
  H^{-1}d=\frac{d}{\gamma_\alpha}.
  \label{eq:signed-star-degree-eigenvector}
\end{equation}
Consequently, positivity of $H^{-1}$ gives the sufficient certificate
\begin{equation}
  |r|\le\gamma_\alpha\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}d
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  |\pi-p|=|H^{-1}r|
  \le H^{-1}|r|
  \le\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}d.
  \label{eq:signed-star-certificate}
\end{equation}
The factor $\gamma_\alpha$ is essential.  The unscaled test
$\max_u|r_u|/d_u\le\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}$ would imply only
$\mathcal E(p)\le\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}/\gamma_\alpha$.  Later we will show
that even the correctly scaled sufficient certificate can be substantially
stronger than the semantic target on the star.

\subsection{The center-seeded star}

Let $G=K_{1,m}$ have center $o$, leaf set $L$, and seed $s=e_o$.  Hence
\begin{equation}
  d_o=m,
  \qquad d_\ell=1\quad(\ell\in L),
  \qquad \operatorname{vol}(V)=2m.
  \label{eq:signed-star-degrees}
\end{equation}
Solving the two balance equations gives
\begin{equation}
  \pi_o=\frac{1+\alpha}{2},
  \qquad
  \sum_{\ell\in L}\pi_\ell=\frac{1-\alpha}{2},
  \qquad
  \pi_\ell=\frac{1-\alpha}{2m}.
  \label{eq:signed-star-ppr}
\end{equation}
Indeed, if $P_L:=\sum_\ell\pi_\ell$, then
$\pi_o=\gamma_\alpha+c_\alpha P_L$ and
$P_L=c_\alpha\pi_o$.

For the complexity separation fix
\begin{equation}
  0<\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}\le\frac1{16},
  \qquad
  m:=\left\lfloor\frac{1}{8\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}}\right\rfloor.
  \label{eq:signed-star-size}
\end{equation}
Since $1/(8\varepsilon_{\rm ppr})\ge2$,
\begin{equation}
  \frac{1}{16\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}}
  \le m\le
  \frac{1}{8\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}},
  \qquad
  \varepsilon_{\rm ppr}m\le\frac18.
  \label{eq:signed-star-size-bounds}
\end{equation}
The center coordinate alone is therefore nontrivial: any semantically
accurate output must satisfy
\begin{equation}
  p_o\ge\pi_o-\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}m
  \ge\frac12-\frac18=\frac38.
  \label{eq:signed-star-center-required}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Charged primitive}

A coordinate relaxation at $u$ changes
\begin{equation}
  p_u\leftarrow p_u+\delta,
  \qquad
  r\leftarrow r-\delta He_u,
  \label{eq:signed-star-coordinate-primitive}
\end{equation}
and costs $d_u$, representing the scan of the $u$th adjacency list.  Repeated
relaxations are charged repeatedly.  An explicit final output pays one write
per listed coordinate.  The SOR specialization takes
$\delta=\omega r_u$ because $H_{uu}=1$.
