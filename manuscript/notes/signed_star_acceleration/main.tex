\title{Signed Local Acceleration on a Star:\\
Exact SOR, Matching Class Bounds, and Failure Modes of Pure Local Acceleration}

\begin{abstract}
This note completely resolves one promised-family case of the accelerated
local PageRank question.  On the center-seeded star
$K_{1,m}$ with $m=\lfloor1/(8\varepsilon_{\rm ppr})\rfloor$, optimal signed
SOR, stopped after the predetermined number
$\lceil2/\sqrt\alpha\rceil$ of alternating center/leaf blocks, has exact
degree-normalized semantic error
\[
  \frac{\lambda^k}{2m}
  \bigl(1+\alpha+2k\sqrt\alpha\bigr),
  \qquad
  \lambda=\frac{1-\sqrt\alpha}{1+\sqrt\alpha},
\]
and therefore uses
$O(1/(\sqrt\alpha\,\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}))$ charged work including output.
The proof identifies a cancellation that is invisible to the usual residual
certificate and removes its apparent $\log(1/\alpha)$ overhead.  A matching
energy/fast-mode lower bound applies to every adaptive dissipative signed
one-coordinate relaxation, giving an exact
$\Theta(1/(\sqrt\alpha\,\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}))$ class result on this star.
For comparison, when $0<\alpha\leq1/16$, maintaining a nonnegative residual
forces $\Omega(1/(\alpha\varepsilon_{\rm ppr}))$ work on the same family.  We then
give a detailed, accuracy-separated account of why standard FISTA can lose
locality on a leaf-seeded RPPR star, why literal safe active-set acceleration
can pay a repeated-prefix tax on paths, and why a conservative certificate can
misdiagnose an already accurate signed iterate.  These are sharply scoped
special-case and algorithm-specific results, not a graph-uniform local-solver
theorem.
\end{abstract}
