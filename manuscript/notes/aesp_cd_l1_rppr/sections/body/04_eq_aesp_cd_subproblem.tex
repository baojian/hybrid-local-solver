\section{AESP outer framework}

At outer iteration $t$, the proposed method approximately minimizes
\begin{equation}
  F_{\rho,t}(z):=F_\rho(z)
  +\frac{\kappa_{\mathrm A}}{2}
       \norm{z-y^{(t-1)}}_2^2,
  \qquad \kappa_{\mathrm A}:=1-2\alpha,
  \label{eq:aesp-cd-subproblem}
\end{equation}
with AESP-scoped momentum
\begin{equation}
  \beta_{\mathrm A}
  :=\frac{\sqrt{1-\alpha}-\sqrt\alpha}
          {\sqrt{1-\alpha}+\sqrt\alpha}.
  \label{eq:aesp-cd-momentum}
\end{equation}
The inner initialization is $z_t^{(0)}=y^{(t-1)}$.
