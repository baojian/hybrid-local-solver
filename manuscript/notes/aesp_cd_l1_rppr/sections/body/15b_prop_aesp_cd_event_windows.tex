\subsection{Event windows and explicit epoch restarts}

The surviving target is therefore a window inequality that combines the
contracting cross-normalized bank with low endpoint progress or a compatible
mean/volume normalization.  It must not rely on high-band decrease alone and
must not commute a spectral projector with coordinatewise positive part.

There is an exact event-level payment of precisely this type.  It is not yet
a static Lyapunov closure, because installing its endpoint reserve with a
fixed $q^{-1}$ coefficient would restore the slow low-mode drift exposed by
the reachable $K_2$ family.

\begin{proposition}[Cross-normalized contract-or-spend window; proved here]
\label{prop:aesp-cd-cross-normalized-contract-spend}
Under the exact settled-optimal-face hypotheses of
\cref{prop:aesp-cd-cross-normalized-bank}, put
\begin{equation}
 K_I:=M_A^2\left(I+(\kappa_{\mathrm A}+\alpha)Q_A^{-1}\right),
 \qquad
 \mathcal S_t:=\langle e_t,K_Ie_t\rangle.
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-cross-normalized-spend-bank}
\end{equation}
If $k\leq\ell$ and $L=\ell-k+1$, then every realized exact correction
pattern satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal B_{\ell+1}
 \leq2(1-q)^L\mathcal B_k
 +\frac{2\beta_{\mathrm A}^2}{q}
   (\mathcal S_{k-1}-\mathcal S_\ell).
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-cross-normalized-contract-spend}
\end{equation}
Moreover, with $m_0=1-q^2$ and
$\mu_{\mathcal E}=\kappa_{\mathrm A}q^2$,
\begin{equation}
 \mu_{\mathcal E}\mathcal B_{\ell+1}
 \leq2(1-q)^L\mu_{\mathcal E}\mathcal B_k
 +\frac{2m_0^3\beta_{\mathrm A}^2}{q}
  \left(\norm{e_{k-1}}_2^2-\norm{e_\ell}_2^2\right).
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-cross-normalized-low-spend}
\end{equation}
For $L=\lceil\log(4)/q\rceil$, the first term on either right-hand side is
at most one half of its starting bank.  Thus every such window either
contracts or spends a telescoping low-progress resource, including the mean
reset in \cref{prop:aesp-cd-kn-cross-bank-stop}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The Stieltjes matrices $Q_A^{-1}$ and $M_A$ are entrywise nonnegative and
commute, so $K_I$ is positive semidefinite and entrywise nonnegative.  On an
actual safe chain, $0\leq r_t\leq\beta_{\mathrm A}d_t$ coordinatewise and
$e_{t-1}=e_t+d_t$ with $e_t,d_t\geq0$.  Consequently
\begin{align*}
 \mathcal R_t
 &=\langle r_t,K_Ir_t\rangle
 \leq\beta_{\mathrm A}^2\langle d_t,K_Id_t\rangle\\
 &\leq\beta_{\mathrm A}^2(\mathcal S_{t-1}-\mathcal S_t).
\end{align*}
Iterate \eqref{eq:aesp-cd-cross-normalized-forcing}.  Cauchy--Schwarz and
the geometric sum give
\[
 \left(\sum_{t=k}^{\ell}(1-q)^{(\ell-t)/2}\sqrt{\mathcal R_t}\right)^2
 \leq\frac1q\sum_{t=k}^{\ell}\mathcal R_t.
\]
Squaring with $(a+b)^2\leq2a^2+2b^2$ and telescoping proves
\eqref{eq:aesp-cd-cross-normalized-contract-spend}.

On a $Q_A$ eigenmode $\lambda$, the eigenvalue of $K_I$ is
\[
 k_I(\lambda)=m(\lambda)^2
 \left(1+\frac{\kappa_{\mathrm A}+\alpha}{\lambda}\right).
\]
It is decreasing in $\lambda$, and
$\mu_{\mathcal E}k_I(\alpha)=m_0^3$.  Therefore
\[
 \mu_{\mathcal E}\mathcal R_t
 \leq m_0^3\beta_{\mathrm A}^2\norm{d_t}_2^2
 \leq m_0^3\beta_{\mathrm A}^2
 (\norm{e_{t-1}}_2^2-\norm{e_t}_2^2).
\]
The same unrolling proves \eqref{eq:aesp-cd-cross-normalized-low-spend}.
\end{proof}

A natural split variant would pay the low part of the forcing with the high
energy drop and a fixed multiple of the starting low bank.  Even the
coefficient $6/5$ is too small on an actual full-support path trajectory.

\begin{proposition}[$P_{24}$ stops the $6/5$ low-start payment]
\label{prop:aesp-cd-p24-low-start-stop}
Take the endpoint-seeded path $P_{24}$ with $q=1/24$ and $\rho=1/92$.  Let
\begin{equation*}
 \lambda_c:=\frac{\kappa_{\mathrm A}}
 {\beta_{\mathrm A}(2+\beta_{\mathrm A})},
 \qquad
 P_\ell:=\one_{Q\leq\lambda_c},
 \qquad P_h:=I-P_\ell,
\end{equation*}
and let $\mathcal B_t^\ell$ and $\mathcal R_t^\ell$ be the restrictions of
\cref{eq:aesp-cd-cross-normalized-bank,eq:aesp-cd-cross-normalized-forcing}
to $P_\ell$.  Put
$\mathfrak E_t^h:=\langle P_he_t,QP_he_t\rangle$.  The exact safeguarded
trajectory has full support throughout the window $[57,93)$ and correction
word $P N^{35}$.  It satisfies the rigorous rational-interval certificate
\begin{equation}
 \frac{
   \mu_{\mathcal E}\sum_{t=57}^{92}\mathcal R_t^\ell
   -(\mathfrak E_{56}^h-\mathfrak E_{92}^h)}
  {\mu_{\mathcal E}\mathcal B_{56}^\ell}
 \geq\frac{3628783833}{3006550000}
 >\frac65.
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-p24-low-start-stop}
\end{equation}
Consequently, the coefficient-$6/5$ inequality obtained by reversing
\cref{eq:aesp-cd-p24-low-start-stop} fails even on a settled path face.  This
does not stop the unsplit endpoint payment in
\cref{eq:aesp-cd-cross-normalized-low-spend} or a larger/adaptive event
allowance.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The registered checker uses exact rational obstacle-prox iterates.  It encloses
the explicit normalized-path cosine basis using Machin's formula for $\pi$,
alternating rational remainders, and outward rounding to a $10^{-28}$ rational
grid.  In particular, it certifies that exactly ten modes lie below
$\lambda_c$, and proves the deliberately looser rational bounds
\begin{align*}
 \mu_{\mathcal E}\sum_{t=57}^{92}\mathcal R_t^\ell
 &>\frac{181459359}{10^{15}},\\
 \mathfrak E_{56}^h-\mathfrak E_{92}^h
 &<\frac{2016735}{10^{17}},\\
 \mu_{\mathcal E}\mathcal B_{56}^\ell
 &<\frac{1503275}{10^{13}}.
\end{align*}
Their quotient gives the first inequality in
\cref{eq:aesp-cd-p24-low-start-stop}; its exact excess over $6/5$ is
$20923833/3006550000>0$.
\end{proof}

The preceding endpoint payment is exact but should not be frozen into a
static reserve.  A Moreau-Hessian weighting instead records only correction
events and admits a clean explicit epoch restart across nested faces.  The
restart is essential: on $K_2$ with $q=1/100$, expansion from one vertex to
both vertices has
\begin{equation*}
 M_{\{1\}}=\frac{3333}{5000},
 \qquad (M_{\{1,2\}})_{11}=\frac{29997}{40000},
 \qquad
 \frac{(M_{\{1,2\}})_{11}}{M_{\{1\}}}=\frac98.
\end{equation*}
Thus even the improved velocity weight is not raw face-monotone.

\begin{proposition}[Moreau-Hessian event bank and epoch restart; proved here]
\label{prop:aesp-cd-moreau-epoch-restart}
On a fixed face $A$, define
\begin{equation}
 W_A:=Q_AM_A=\kappa_{\mathrm A}(I-M_A),
 \qquad
 \mathcal C_A(e,u):=\langle e,W_Ae\rangle
 +c\langle u,M_Au\rangle,
 \quad c:=\kappa_{\mathrm A}+\alpha.
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-event-bank}
\end{equation}
On every correction-free exact stage,
$\mathcal C_A(e_{t+1},u_{t+1})\leq(1-q)\mathcal C_A(e_t,u_t)$.
A correction $r_t$ has forcing norm
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal R_t^{\mathcal C}
 =\left\langle r_t,
   (\kappa_{\mathrm A}M_A^2+\alpha M_A^3)r_t\right\rangle
 \leq m_0^3\norm{r_t}_2^2,
 \qquad m_0=1-q^2.
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-event-forcing}
\end{equation}

Assume $q\leq1/\sqrt2$.  Consider an explicit epoch protocol with nested
faces $A_0\subset A_1\subset\cdots\subset A_J$.  A boundary expansion keeps
the primal point unchanged, pads it by zero, and explicitly restarts momentum
by setting $x_{\mathrm{prev}}:=x$.  It then runs at least
\begin{equation*}
 L_j\geq\left\lceil\frac{\log8}{q}\right\rceil
\end{equation*}
exact stages on the fixed new face before another expansion.  Suppose each
face has a positive restricted optimum and, after the boundary expansion
solve, every shifted-solve output stays in the relative interior of that
declared face until the next epoch boundary.  Thus no admission or support
retraction changes the active face inside an epoch, and the fixed-face
residual recurrence applies.  Let $\mathcal C_j$ be the bank immediately
before expansion $j$, let
\begin{equation*}
 \Delta_j:=\mathcal F_{A_j}^\star-\mathcal F_{A_{j+1}}^\star\geq0
\end{equation*}
be the restricted quadratic optimum gain, and let
$Y_j=\sum_{t\text{ in epoch }j}\norm{r_t}_2^2$.  Then the next epoch endpoint
satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \sqrt{\mathcal C_{j+1}}
 \leq\frac12\sqrt{\mathcal C_j}
 +\frac1{\sqrt2}\sqrt{\Delta_j}
 +\sqrt{\frac{m_0^3Y_j}{q}}.
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-epoch-one-step}
\end{equation}
Consequently, with $w_j=2^{-(J-1-j)}$,
\begin{equation}
 \sqrt{\mathcal C_J}
 \leq2^{-J}\sqrt{\mathcal C_0}
 +\sqrt{\sum_{j<J}w_j\Delta_j}
 +\sqrt{\frac{2m_0^3}{q}\sum_{j<J}w_jY_j}.
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-epoch-net}
\end{equation}
Each correction belongs to one half-open epoch, and the unweighted nested-face
gains telescope.  This is a theorem for the displayed restart protocol, not
for the unchanged per-stage automatic-admission trajectory.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
On a $Q_A$ eigenmode $\lambda$, the quadratic weights in
\eqref{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-event-bank} equal $\lambda m$ and $cm$.  They are the
weights in \eqref{eq:aesp-cd-cross-normalized-bank} multiplied by the positive
scalar $\lambda m$.  Thus the modal positive-semidefinite remainder already
proved in \cref{prop:aesp-cd-cross-normalized-bank} gives the same
correction-free factor $1-q$.  The forcing state $(M_Ar_t,M_Ar_t)$ has kernel
\[
 M_A^3(Q_A+cI)=\kappa_{\mathrm A}M_A^2+\alpha M_A^3.
\]
Its scalar eigenvalue decreases with $\lambda$ and equals $m_0^3$ at
$\lambda=\alpha$, proving \eqref{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-event-forcing}.  At
$\lambda=1$ it equals
$m_0^3(2+q^2)/[8(1+q^2)]$, so this forcing metric has condition number
$8(1+q^2)/(2+q^2)$ rather than the $q^{-2}$ spread of the inverse-$Q$ bank.

At a restart, $u=qe$ and $cq^2=\alpha$, so
\begin{equation*}
 \mathcal C_A^{\rm rst}(e)
 =\langle e,D_Ae\rangle,
 \qquad
 D_A:=W_A+\alpha M_A
 =\kappa_{\mathrm A}I-(\kappa_{\mathrm A}-\alpha)M_A.
\end{equation*}
Also $\mathcal C_A^{\rm rst}(e)\leq2\langle e,W_Ae\rangle
\leq2\mathcal C_A(e,u)$.  If $A\subset B$, the inverse Schur complement gives
$(M_B)_{AA}\succeq M_A$.  Since $\kappa_{\mathrm A}\geq\alpha$,
$(D_B)_{AA}\preceq D_A$.  Put
$h=x_B^\star-(x_A^\star,0)$.  Restricted quadratic optimality gives
$\Delta_{AB}=\langle h,Q_Bh\rangle/2$, while $D_B\preceq2Q_B$.  The triangle
inequality in the $D_B$ norm therefore proves the restart-transfer bound
\begin{equation*}
 \sqrt{\mathcal C_B^{\rm rst}((e_A,0)+h)}
 \leq\sqrt{\mathcal C_A^{\rm rst}(e_A)}+2\sqrt{\Delta_{AB}}.
\end{equation*}

Within the new fixed face, iterate the root form of the modal contraction and
use Cauchy--Schwarz on the correction convolution.  Its norm is at most
$\sqrt{m_0^3Y_j/q}$.  Since
$(1-q)^{L_j/2}\leq1/\sqrt8$, combining the factor-$2$ restart comparison and
the face-gain bound gives \eqref{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-epoch-one-step}.  Iteration
gives the geometric convolution.  Finally, $\sum_jw_j<2$ and
Cauchy--Schwarz separately on the two event sequences give
\eqref{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-epoch-net}.
\end{proof}

The correction-square term in \cref{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-event-forcing} is a
safe but potentially very loose envelope.  Keeping the cross term gives an
exact signed event increment and removes the geometric $1/q$ loss from the
fixed-face convolution.

\begin{proposition}[Signed correction increment and refined epoch ledger]
\label{prop:aesp-cd-moreau-signed-event}
Under the exact fixed-settled-face safe-chain hypotheses, put
$\vartheta=1-q$, $d_t=x_t-x_{t-1}$, and define the uncorrected and retained
center advances
\begin{equation*}
 s_t^0:=\beta_{\mathrm A}d_t,
 \qquad s_t:=s_t^0-r_t=\ell_t-x_t.
\end{equation*}
Let
\begin{align}
 \mathcal K_A
 &:=M_A^3(Q_A+cI)
   =\kappa_{\mathrm A}M_A^2+\alpha M_A^3,\notag\\
 \mathcal J_A
 &:=\mathcal K_A-c\vartheta M_A^2
   =cqM_A^2(\vartheta I+qM_A),
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-signed-kernels}
\end{align}
and
\begin{equation}
 \Xi_t:=-\langle r_t,\mathcal K_A(s_t+s_t^0)\rangle
       +2\langle r_t,\mathcal J_Ae_t\rangle.
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-signed-increment}
\end{equation}
Then the actual exact next state satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_{t+1}\leq\vartheta\mathcal C_t+\Xi_t.
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-signed-recursion}
\end{equation}
Both kernels in \cref{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-signed-kernels} are positive
semidefinite and entrywise nonnegative.  With
$\mathcal S_t^{\mathcal J}:=\langle e_t,\mathcal J_Ae_t\rangle$,
\begin{equation}
 [\Xi_t]_+
 \leq\beta_{\mathrm A}
   (\mathcal S_{t-1}^{\mathcal J}-\mathcal S_t^{\mathcal J}).
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-signed-payment}
\end{equation}
Consequently, for $L=\ell-k+1$,
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_{\ell+1}
 \leq\vartheta^L\mathcal C_k
 +\sum_{t=k}^{\ell}\vartheta^{\ell-t}[\Xi_t]_+,
 \qquad
 \sum_{t=k}^{\ell}[\Xi_t]_+
 \leq\beta_{\mathrm A}
 (\mathcal S_{k-1}^{\mathcal J}-\mathcal S_\ell^{\mathcal J}).
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-signed-window}
\end{equation}

For the explicit epoch-restart protocol of
\cref{prop:aesp-cd-moreau-epoch-restart}, replace $Y_j$ by the within-epoch
discounted signed mass
\begin{equation*}
 X_j:=\sum_{t\text{ in epoch }j}
 \vartheta^{b_{j+1}-1-t}[\Xi_t]_+,
\end{equation*}
where $b_{j+1}$ is the next epoch boundary.  Then
\begin{align}
 \sqrt{\mathcal C_{j+1}}
 &\leq\frac12\sqrt{\mathcal C_j}
   +\frac1{\sqrt2}\sqrt{\Delta_j}+\sqrt{X_j},
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-signed-epoch}\\
 \sqrt{\mathcal C_J}
 &\leq2^{-J}\sqrt{\mathcal C_0}
   +\sqrt{\sum_{j<J}w_j\Delta_j}
   +\sqrt{2\sum_{j<J}w_jX_j}.
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-signed-net}
\end{align}
Thus only harmful signed correction increments, rather than every correction
square, enter the refined ledger.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For fixed $e_t$, let $F_t(s)$ be the next Moreau bank generated by center
advance $s$.  Exact solution gives
$e_{t+1}=M_A(e_t-s)$ and $u_{t+1}=e_{t+1}-\vartheta e_t$.
Expanding the two quadratic terms in \cref{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-event-bank}
gives
\begin{equation*}
 F_t(s)=F_t(0)+\langle s,\mathcal K_As\rangle
 -2\langle s,\mathcal J_Ae_t\rangle.
\end{equation*}
Subtract $F_t(s_t^0)$ from $F_t(s_t^0-r_t)$ to obtain
\cref{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-signed-increment}.  The correction-free modal theorem
gives $F_t(s_t^0)\leq\vartheta\mathcal C_t$, proving
\cref{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-signed-recursion}.

The displayed factorizations show that $\mathcal K_A,\mathcal J_A$ are
positive semidefinite and entrywise nonnegative.  Since
$0\leq r_t\leq\beta_{\mathrm A}d_t$ and $e_t,d_t\geq0$ coordinatewise,
\begin{align*}
 [\Xi_t]_+
 &\leq2\langle r_t,\mathcal J_Ae_t\rangle
 \leq2\beta_{\mathrm A}\langle d_t,\mathcal J_Ae_t\rangle\\
 &\leq\beta_{\mathrm A}
 \left(\langle e_t+d_t,\mathcal J_A(e_t+d_t)\rangle
       -\langle e_t,\mathcal J_Ae_t\rangle\right),
\end{align*}
which is \cref{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-signed-payment}.  Linear iteration proves
\cref{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-signed-window}.  Combining its first inequality with
the same-point restart transfer and
$\vartheta^{L_j/2}\leq1/\sqrt8$ proves
\cref{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-signed-epoch}.  The weighted net inequality follows
from Cauchy--Schwarz and $\sum_jw_j<2$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Any isolated correction is absorbed by a quiet window]
\label{cor:aesp-cd-moreau-pulse-reset}
Under the same exact fixed-face hypotheses, every partial or full correction
obeys
\begin{equation*}
 \mathcal C_{t+1}<8\mathcal C_t.
\end{equation*}
If the following $L$ stages have no correction, then
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_{t+L+1}<8(1-q)^L\mathcal C_t.
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-pulse-reset}
\end{equation}
In particular, $L=\lceil\log(16)/q\rceil$ gives strict one-half contraction.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Entrywise nonnegativity of $\mathcal J_A$ and $e_t,s_t\geq0$ give
\begin{equation*}
 F_t(s_t)\leq F_t(0)+\langle s_t,\mathcal K_As_t\rangle.
\end{equation*}
The full-reset modal bound gives $F_t(0)<2\mathcal C_t$, while
$\mathcal K_A\preceq m_0^3I$.  Since $s_t\leq\beta_{\mathrm A}d_t$ and
$u_t=qe_t-(1-q)d_t$, the lower modal weights in $\mathcal C_t$ imply
\begin{equation*}
 m_0^3\norm{s_t}_2^2
 \leq6(1-q)^2(1+q^2)\mathcal C_t<6\mathcal C_t.
\end{equation*}
This proves the factor eight.  Apply the correction-free factor $1-q$ for the
following stages and use $(1-q)^L\leq e^{-qL}$.
\end{proof}

The signed increment is substantially sharper than correction-square mass,
but it can still be positive on a legal reachable trajectory and its
Stieltjes payment is asymptotically tight.

\begin{proposition}[Reachable positive signed events and tight payment]
\label{prop:aesp-cd-p3-positive-signed-event}
On the endpoint-seeded path $P_3$ with $q=1/8$ and $\rho=1/16$, the exact
support sequence through stage four is
\begin{equation*}
 \varnothing,\quad\{0,1\},\quad\{0,1,2\},\quad
 \{0,1,2\},\quad\{0,1,2\},
\end{equation*}
and the stage word is $\mathsf N\mathsf N\mathsf N\mathsf F\mathsf N$.
The full correction at $t=3$ has
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\Xi_3}{\mathcal C_3}
 =\frac{938073510129372371755}{3462704548150418915328}
 >\frac14,
 \qquad
 \frac{\Xi_3}{q\mathcal C_3}>2,
 \qquad
 \mathcal C_4>\mathcal C_3.
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-p3-positive-signed-event}
\end{equation}

On the reachable $K_8$ family of
\cref{prop:aesp-cd-k8-q-bank-stop}, the stage-two full correction satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \lim_{q\downarrow0}\frac{\Xi_2}{q\mathcal C_2}=2,
 \qquad
 \lim_{q\downarrow0}
 \frac{\Xi_2}{\beta_{\mathrm A}
 (\mathcal S_1^{\mathcal J}-\mathcal S_2^{\mathcal J})}=1.
 \label{eq:aesp-cd-k8-signed-sharpness}
\end{equation}
At $q=1/100$, the first ratio is exactly
\begin{equation*}
 \frac{10021978673028809851221}{5135269638022709000000}
 \approx1.95160,
\end{equation*}
while the second is approximately $0.98582$.  Hence neither signed
nonpositivity nor an extra graph-uniform factor $q$ in
\cref{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-signed-payment} follows from the actual common-cap
controller.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The registered $P_3$ audit replays the legal exact obstacle-prox trajectory
and evaluates all four terms of the Moreau bank as rational numbers.  Its
full-correction cap ratio is $134592/34351>1$, and direct subtraction gives
\cref{eq:aesp-cd-p3-positive-signed-event}.

For the $K_8$ family, use its constant and source-orthogonal modal weights
$8$ and $8/7$.  The low-mode stage-one and stage-two errors obey
$E_{1,0}=a_0m_0$, $E_{2,0}=E_{1,0}s_0$, with $a_0=1/112$, while the high
amplitudes carry an additional $q^2$ factor.  As $q\downarrow0$,
\begin{equation*}
 \frac{\mathcal C_2}{a_0^2q^2}\longrightarrow16,
 \qquad
 \frac{\Xi_2}{a_0^2q^3}\longrightarrow32,
 \qquad
 \frac{\mathcal S_1^{\mathcal J}-\mathcal S_2^{\mathcal J}}
 {a_0^2q^3}\longrightarrow32.
\end{equation*}
The high-mode contributions are lower order.  These limits prove
\cref{eq:aesp-cd-k8-signed-sharpness}; exact substitution at $q=1/100$ gives
the displayed rational calibration.
\end{proof}

This refinement is not yet a complete event-packing theorem.  If
$L=\lceil\log(2)/q\rceil$ and a block fails to contract from
$\mathcal C_k$ to $3\mathcal C_k/4$, then
\cref{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-signed-window} forces
\begin{equation*}
 \mathcal S_{k-1}^{\mathcal J}-\mathcal S_\ell^{\mathcal J}
 >\frac{\mathcal C_k}{4\beta_{\mathrm A}}.
\end{equation*}
However, the exact modal comparison
$q\mathcal J_A\preceq W_A$ permits
$\mathcal S^{\mathcal J}/\mathcal C=\Theta(1/q)$ on the lowest mode.  The
generic two-account argument therefore allows $O(1/q)$ bad blocks, or
$O(1/q^2)$ stages.  Closing the accelerated exponent now requires a
controller-specific theorem showing that positive $\Xi_t$ events are sparse,
followed by quiet reset windows, or spectrally separated; the common-cap
inequality $0\leq r_t\leq\beta_{\mathrm A}d_t$ alone is insufficient.

The event ledger avoids both registered normalization traps.  On the
full-support $K_2$ tail, $\Delta_j=Y_j=0$, so every
$\Theta(1/q)$-stage epoch contracts the bank by a constant without a static
low reserve.  On the complete-graph family of
\cref{prop:aesp-cd-kn-cross-bank-stop}, the stage-two correction satisfies
$m_0^3\norm{r_2}_2^2/(q\mathcal C_2)<1/16$, so an otherwise quiet epoch still
contracts.  What remains open is an event-packing theorem that absorbs the
weighted terms in \eqref{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-epoch-net}, plus finite-inner and
automatic-admission transfer.
