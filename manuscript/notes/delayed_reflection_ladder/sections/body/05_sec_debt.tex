\section{An exact delayed-debt representation}
\label{sec:debt}

The self-reflection can be separated algebraically without changing the
linear-system residual.  Maintain a fresh component $\varphi$ and a delayed
component $h$ satisfying
\begin{equation}
 r=b-Qx=\varphi+h.
 \label{eq:split-invariant}
\end{equation}
Initially $x=0$, $\varphi=b$, and $h=0$.

\begin{definition}[Fresh over-relaxed push]
\label{def:fresh-push}
At coordinate $u$, let $\sigma:=\varphi_u$ and set
\begin{align}
 x^+&=x+\omega\frac{\sigma}{Q_{uu}}e_u,\notag\\
 \varphi_u^+&=0,
 &h_u^+&=h_u+(1-\omega)\sigma,\notag\\
 \varphi_v^+&=\varphi_v-\omega\frac{\sigma Q_{vu}}{Q_{uu}}
 \quad(v\neq u),
 &h_v^+&=h_v\quad(v\neq u).
 \label{eq:fresh-push}
\end{align}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Debt-only exact cleanup]
\label{def:debt-cleanup}
At coordinate $u$, let $\delta:=h_u$ and set
\begin{align}
 x^+&=x+\frac{\delta}{Q_{uu}}e_u,\notag\\
 h_u^+&=0,
 &\varphi_u^+&=\varphi_u,\notag\\
 \varphi_v^+&=\varphi_v-\frac{\delta Q_{vu}}{Q_{uu}}
 \quad(v\neq u),
 &h_v^+&=h_v\quad(v\neq u).
 \label{eq:debt-cleanup}
\end{align}
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Split-residual correctness]
\label{lem:split-correctness}
Both \cref{def:fresh-push,def:debt-cleanup} preserve
\cref{eq:split-invariant} exactly.  If a fresh push starts from $h_u=0$ and
its new debt is cleaned immediately, the combined operation is the ordinary
exact push from \cref{lem:immediate-collapse}.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For the fresh push, summing the two updated components gives
\[
 \varphi^++h^+
 =\varphi+h-\omega\frac{\sigma}{Q_{uu}}Qe_u
 =b-Qx^+.
\]
The debt-cleanup calculation is identical with amplitude $\delta$ and
$\omega=1$.  The last statement follows by summing the two increments to
$x_u$.
\end{proof}

This representation makes self-reactivation optional at the scheduler level:
$h_u$ need not make $u$ eligible during a spreading epoch.  Correctness is
not the difficulty.  The difficulty is deciding which neighbor-generated
arrivals belong to the advancing frontier and which are backward debt.
