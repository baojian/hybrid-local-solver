\section{The conditional accelerated theorem}
\label{sec:conditional}

The preceding RPPR theorem reaches the exact product scale under an
output-sensitive discovery lemma.  For an unregularized residual-threshold
ladder without the RPPR volume path, the following more generic statement
retains the dynamic-range logarithm.

\begin{assumption}[Certified causal expansion]
\label{assump:causal-expansion}
For a problem-dependent volume bound $V_{\rm loc}$, the algorithm executes
paired epochs on regions $S_k$ satisfying $\vol(S_k)\leq V_{\rm loc}$.  In
each epoch its causal spreading phase scans every adjacency list of $S_k$ at
most $C_0$ times, all deferred traffic is included in one exact block
correction, and a supplied elimination ordering has width at most $w$.  The
verified live threshold decreases by
$b_\alpha=\lambda_\alpha^{-s}$ for some fixed $s\in\{1,2\}$.
\end{assumption}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional bounded-width matching scale]
\label{thm:conditional-work}
Under \cref{assump:causal-expansion}, crossing dynamic range $R$ costs
\begin{equation}
 \Work
 =O\!\left(
  (w+1)^2V_{\rm loc}\bigl(K_s(R,\alpha)+1\bigr)
 \right)
 =O\!\left(
   \frac{(w+1)^2 V_{\rm loc}}{\sqrt\alpha}(1+\log R)
  \right).
 \label{eq:conditional-work}
\end{equation}
In particular, $V_{\rm loc}=O(1/\epsppr)$ gives
$\widetilde O(1/(\epsppr\sqrt\alpha))$, while a safe RPPR envelope with
$V_{\rm loc}=O(1/\rho)$ gives
$\widetilde O(1/(\rho\sqrt\alpha))$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The spreading pass costs $O(C_0V_{\rm loc})$.  By
\cref{thm:width-resolution}, the exact cleanup and boundary update cost
$O((w+1)^2V_{\rm loc})$.  Multiply their sum by the exact epoch count in
\cref{cor:rung-count}.  The two specializations substitute the stated volume
bounds; they do not identify $\epsppr$ with $\rho$.
\end{proof}

On a spider every induced region is a forest, so $w=1$ and
\cref{cor:forest-pair-locality} removes the triangular inward-cleanup cost.
The unresolved spider statement is now narrower: prove that the online
spreading rule discovers a certified region and advances its threshold after
only $O(1)$ scans of that region.  Backward traffic itself is no longer the
obstruction once it is resolved as one tree block.
