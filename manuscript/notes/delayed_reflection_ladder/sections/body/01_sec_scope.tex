The repository-wide residual convention remains open.  In this note only,
\begin{equation}
 r(x):=b-Qx,
 \qquad
 \norm{D^{-1/2}r(x)}_\infty\leq\alpha\epsppr
 \label{eq:note-gate}
\end{equation}
is the unregularized terminal certificate.  The RPPR symbol $\rho$ is used
only when invoking the separate support-volume scale
$\vol(S^\star(\rho))\leq1/\rho$.  These two accuracy namespaces are not
identified.

The source comparisons used here are narrow.  The PageRank/RPPR formulation
is the shared convention above, aligned with Fountoulakis and
Mart\'{i}nez-Rubio (2026), PDF page 3
\citep{fountoulakis2026complexity}.  Adaptive restart supplies general
motivation for clearing unstable momentum \citep{odonoghue2015adaptive}, but
does not provide the graph-local work lemma sought here.  AESP supplies the
intended accelerated active-volume scale \citep{huang2025accelerated}; its residual,
inner map, and work convention are not silently transferred to R-LSOR.
