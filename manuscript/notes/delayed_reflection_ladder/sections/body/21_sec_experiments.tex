\section{Decisive experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

Real-graph totals should follow, not precede, the following adversarial
checks.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
 \item Sweep $\alpha$ below the benchmark range and compare fixed
 $b\in\{2,3\}$ against $b_{\rm self}(\alpha)$ and
 $b_{\rm next}(\alpha)$.  Record both charged work and reactivation
 multiplicity.
 \item On the long spider, plot pushes by arm depth and epoch.  The acceptance
 signal is a strip of width $O(1)$ around the moving frontier, not merely a
 smaller total on one finite graph.
 \item Ablate three states separately: self-debt tagging, backward-arrival
 deferral, and cyclic-core handling.  \Cref{prop:backflow} predicts that the
 first without the second cannot remove triangular work.
 \item Fit the small-$\alpha$ work exponent at fixed output scale.  The target
 is $\alpha^{-1/2}$ up to logarithms; an improved constant at
 $\alpha\in\{0.025,0.04\}$ is not sufficient evidence.
 \item Recompute the full verifier-owned residual after every final exact
 rung.  No split-state or stale-frontier condition substitutes for the actual
 certificate.
\end{enumerate}
