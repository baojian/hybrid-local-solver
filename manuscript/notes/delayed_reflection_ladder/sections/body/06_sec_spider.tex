\section{Spider audit: self-debt is not enough}
\label{sec:spider}

The active manuscript's long-spider construction uses the same
$\lambda_\alpha$ parameterization.  On every degree-two arm interior, an
optimal-SOR push of residual $\sigma$ leaves
$-\lambda_\alpha^2\sigma$ at the pushed vertex and sends
$\lambda_\alpha\sigma$ to each of its two neighbors.  FIFO scheduling turns
these packets into a triangular inward traversal and costs
$\Omega(1/(\alpha\epsppr))$.

\begin{proposition}[Neighbor backflow dominates self-debt]
\label{prop:backflow}
Fix an interior arm coordinate and $0<\alpha<1$.  After its optimal-SOR push,
the magnitude sent to its already processed parent is strictly larger than
the magnitude of its own self-reflection:
\begin{equation}
 \lambda_\alpha|\sigma|
 >\lambda_\alpha^2|\sigma|.
 \label{eq:backflow-dominates}
\end{equation}
Consequently, for any live threshold strictly between these two values, a
policy that suppresses only tagged self-debt still activates the parent.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For $0<\alpha<1$, one has $0<\lambda_\alpha<1$.  The displayed inequality is
immediate.  The parent-directed packet is a neighbor arrival and therefore
belongs to $\varphi$ under \cref{def:fresh-push}; suppressing $h$ alone does
not suppress it.
\end{proof}

This proposition identifies the required extra state.  During a causal
spreading epoch, arrivals to newly discovered coordinates may remain fresh,
while arrivals to already processed coordinates must be deferred as backward
debt or summarized by a directed-edge message.  The next section proves that
once a region has been discovered, all of this debt can be summarized exactly
in linear work on a tree and in elimination-width-sensitive work on a sparse
cyclic region.

The audit also limits what an eight-phase empirical ladder can establish.
The active manuscript proves, under a diffusive-annulus assumption, that an
$m$-stage vertex-push method resolving spider depth $L$ pays at least
$\Omega(kL^2/m)$.  Thus a fixed number of ordinary diffusive rungs does not
yield the target bound.  An alpha-scaled number of lightweight causal epochs,
or a nonbacktracking/elimination state that invalidates the annulus
assumption, is necessary.
