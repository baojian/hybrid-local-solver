\subsection{Settled reset budgets: a sharp path obstruction and a
conditional telescope}
\label{sec:settled-reset-budget-amortization}

Round~021 leaves two logically different questions.  The first is whether an
\emph{additive} sum of observable reset budgets can be paid by the objective
drops at face admissions.  The second is whether the work of the accelerated
stages, where an initial budget enters only through an oracle logarithm, can
still be accelerated.  The following three-vertex example gives a sharp STOP
for the first question at coefficients smaller than order $1/\alpha$.  It does
not answer the second question.

Let $P_3$ be the path $0$--$1$--$2$, seeded at $0$, and use the ambient
degrees $(1,2,1)$.  Fix
\begin{equation}
 \rho=\frac3{10},\qquad
 q_{\rm r}:=t,\qquad s:=t^2,
 \qquad \alpha:=\frac{s}{1+s},
 \qquad 0<s<\frac1{12},
 \label{eq:path-three-reset-parameters}
\end{equation}
so $q_{\rm r}=\sqrt{\alpha/(1-\alpha)}$ is the proof-local reset scale, not
the reserved bare FISTA symbol.  Put
\begin{equation}
 A_s:=1+2s,\qquad E_s:=1+8s+8s^2,
 \label{eq:path-three-reset-polynomials}
\end{equation}
and use degree-scaled coordinates $p=D^{-1/2}x$.  Let
$U_0=\{0\}$ and $U_1=\{0,1\}$.

\begin{proposition}[A settled $P_3$ reset needs an order-$1/\alpha$ drop
coefficient]
\label{prop:path-three-settled-reset-drop-obstruction}
Under \cref{eq:path-three-reset-parameters}, exact settlement on $U_0$ gives
\begin{equation}
 p_0^{(0)}=\frac{2s(1-\rho)}{A_s},
 \qquad
 g_0:=\frac{(\ell_\rho-Qx^{(0)})_1}{\sqrt{d_1}}
 =\frac{s(1-12s)}{20(1+s)A_s}>0.
 \label{eq:path-three-first-settlement-demand}
\end{equation}
Thus the exact all-violations gate admits vertex $1$.  Exact settlement on
$U_1$ gives
\begin{equation}
 p_0^{(1)}=\frac{4s\{A_s(1-\rho)-\rho\}}{E_s}>0,
 \qquad
 p_1^{(1)}=\frac{2s\{1-\rho(3+4s)\}}{E_s}>0,
 \label{eq:path-three-second-settlement}
\end{equation}
whereas the remaining normalized boundary demand is
\begin{equation}
 g_1:=\frac{(\ell_\rho-Qx^{(1)})_2}{\sqrt{d_2}}
 =\frac{s\{1-4\rho(1+s)A_s\}}{(1+s)E_s}<0.
 \label{eq:path-three-terminal-demand}
\end{equation}
Hence this is a complete one-admission canonical trace.

At the $U_0\to U_1$ admission, retain the settled primal point and zero
momentum.  The reset quantities in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-shock-budget} then satisfy
\begin{equation}
 D_1^2=0,\qquad K_1^2=2g_0^2,\qquad
 B_1=\frac{(3-2s)g_0^2}{\alpha}.
 \label{eq:path-three-reset-budget}
\end{equation}
For the degree-scaled Hessian
$H=D^{1/2}QD^{1/2}$, the new-coordinate Schur pivot and exact restricted
optimum drop are
\begin{equation}
 S_1=\frac{E_s}{2(1+s)A_s},
 \qquad
 \Delta_1:=F_{\rho,U_0}(x^{(0)})-F_{\rho,U_1}(x^{(1)})
 =\frac{(2g_0)^2}{2S_1}
 =\frac{4(1+s)A_sg_0^2}{E_s}.
 \label{eq:path-three-schur-drop}
\end{equation}
Consequently
\begin{align}
 \frac{B_1}{\Delta_1}
 &=\frac{E_s(3-2s)}{4A_ss}
 =\frac{3}{4s}+\frac{2(2+s-2s^2)}{A_s}
 =\frac{3}{4q_{\rm r}^2}+O(1),
 \notag\\
 q_{\rm r}\frac{B_1}{\Delta_1}
 &=\frac{3}{4q_{\rm r}}+O(q_{\rm r}).
 \label{eq:path-three-reset-drop-ratio}
\end{align}
Thus no $\alpha$-uniform constant, and no coefficient
$O(1/q_{\rm r})$, can bound even this one settled reset budget by its exact
face-optimum drop as $q_{\rm r}\downarrow0$.

The separation concerns the \emph{observable upper budget} $B_1$, not the
actual analytical settled shock.  Indeed, for any settled admission,
$\operatorname{Env}(\bar x)\leq F_\rho(\bar x)$ and strong convexity give
\begin{equation}
 \Sigma^{\rm es}
 \leq\left(1+\frac{\mu_{\rm E}}\alpha\right)\Delta<2\Delta.
 \label{eq:settled-actual-shock-drop-bound}
\end{equation}
Thus the path diagnoses looseness in this locally observable KKT reset
formula; it does not show that the underlying face shock itself is
$\Theta(1/\alpha)$ times the drop.

This is a reset-budget obstruction for the declared interface, not a work
lower bound.  In the imported relative inner oracle, the initial estimate
budget occurs inside a logarithm.  The factor
$B_1/\Delta_1=\Theta(q_{\rm r}^{-2})$ therefore contributes only an
$O(\log(1/q_{\rm r}))$ change to that stage bound by itself.  The proposition
does not refute accelerated total work, another potential, a nonadditive
packing, or a different reset representation.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
In degree-scaled coordinates the $U_1$ Hessian and shifted load are
\begin{equation*}
 H_{U_1U_1}=\frac1{1+s}
 \begin{pmatrix}A_s/2&-1/2\\-1/2&A_s\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 c_{U_1}=\frac{s}{1+s}
 \begin{pmatrix}1-\rho\\-2\rho\end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation*}
The scalar solve on $U_0$ gives $p_0^{(0)}$.  The missing-coordinate
degree-scaled demand is $2g_0$; direct substitution with $\rho=3/10$ gives
\cref{eq:path-three-first-settlement-demand}.  The determinant of the
displayed $2$-by-$2$ matrix is $E_s/[4(1+s)^2]$, and its exact solve gives
\cref{eq:path-three-second-settlement}.  Since
$1-\rho(3+4s)=1/10-6s/5$, both coordinates are positive precisely throughout
the stated range.  Substitution of $p_1^{(1)}$ in the row at vertex $2$
gives \cref{eq:path-three-terminal-demand}; its numerator is already negative
at $s=0$ and decreases for $s>0$.

The minimum-norm KKT vector at the padded settled point vanishes on $U_0$
and has raw $x$-coordinate magnitude $\sqrt2g_0$ at vertex $1$.  This proves
$K_1^2=2g_0^2$ and $D_1^2=0$.  Substituting
$\mu_{\rm E}=\alpha(1-2\alpha)/(1-\alpha)$ in the reset definition gives
\begin{equation*}
 B_1=\left(\frac1{2\alpha}+\frac{\mu_{\rm E}}{\alpha^2}\right)K_1^2
 =\frac{(3-2s)g_0^2}{\alpha}.
\end{equation*}
Eliminating the old coordinate in $H$ gives the displayed $S_1$.  The new
degree-scaled linear demand is $2g_0$, so the standard quadratic Schur identity
gives \cref{eq:path-three-schur-drop}.  Division and the exact decomposition
in \cref{eq:path-three-reset-drop-ratio} finish the proof.
\end{proof}

The previous coefficient is not merely an artifact of that path.  A general
settled expansion retains an order-$1/\alpha$ upper bound, and the path shows
that its order cannot be improved for this reset formula.

\begin{lemma}[Sharp-order settled reset/drop bound]
\label{lem:settled-reset-optimum-drop-bound}
Fix $0<\alpha<1/2$.  Let $U\subset U^+$ be a safe face expansion, let $x_U$
be the exact restricted minimizer, and zero-pad it to $\bar x$ on $U^+$ with
zero momentum.  Suppose the minimum-norm KKT vector $\kappa$ at $\bar x$ is
supported on admitted violating coordinates, so $\bar x-\kappa$ is a feasible
nonnegative point on the same positive-orthant quadratic piece.  Put
\begin{equation*}
 K^2:=\norm{\kappa}_2^2,\qquad
 \Delta:=F_{\rho,U}(x_U)-F_{\rho,U^+}(x_{U^+}),\qquad
 B:=\left(\frac1{2\alpha}+\frac{\mu_{\rm E}}{\alpha^2}\right)K^2.
\end{equation*}
Then
\begin{equation}
 \Delta\geq\frac{K^2}{2},
 \qquad
 B\leq
 \frac{3-5\alpha}{\alpha(1-\alpha)}\,\Delta.
 \label{eq:settled-reset-optimum-drop-bound}
\end{equation}
Consequently the same coefficient bounds sums over any finite collection of
such settled zero-momentum expansions.  Its order is
$\Theta(1/\alpha)=\Theta(q_{\rm r}^{-2})$, and
\Cref{prop:path-three-settled-reset-drop-obstruction} makes that order sharp.
No conclusion about the nonsettled displacement term $D^2$ follows.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The RPPR quadratic piece has Hessian $Q\preceq I$.  The feasible KKT step
therefore gives
\begin{equation*}
 F_{\rho,U^+}(\bar x-\kappa)-F_{\rho,U^+}(\bar x)
 =-K^2+\frac12\langle\kappa,Q\kappa\rangle
 \leq-\frac{K^2}{2}.
\end{equation*}
The new restricted optimum is no worse than this trial point, proving the
first inequality.  Since
\begin{equation*}
 2\left(\frac1{2\alpha}+\frac{\mu_{\rm E}}{\alpha^2}\right)
 =\frac{3-5\alpha}{\alpha(1-\alpha)},
\end{equation*}
the second follows.  Summation is immediate.  On the path,
$q_{\rm r}^2=\alpha/(1-\alpha)=s$ and
$B_1/\Delta_1\sim3/(4q_{\rm r}^2)$.
\end{proof}

There is also a face-general algebraic telescope, but only under a stronger
settlement-and-order interface that the actual Round--021 nonsettled policy
does not establish.  We state it to fix both its scope and its correct
coefficient.

\begin{proposition}[Conditional nested reset-budget telescope]
\label{prop:conditional-nested-reset-budget-telescope}
Let $U_0\subset\cdots\subset U_K$, $K\geq2$, be nested faces of one
full-space RPPR objective.  Put $x_0=0$, and for $1\leq k\leq K$ let $x_k$
be the exact settled minimizer on $U_k$ with
\begin{equation}
 d_k:=x_k-\iota_{k-1}x_{k-1}\geq0,
 \qquad
 \Delta_k:=F_{\rho,U_k}(\iota_{k-1}x_{k-1})
             -F_{\rho,U_k}(x_k)\geq0.
 \label{eq:conditional-reset-face-drops}
\end{equation}
For this proposition only, set
\begin{equation}
 q_{\rm r}:=\sqrt{\frac{\alpha}{1-\alpha}},\quad
 \beta:=\frac{1-q_{\rm r}}{1+q_{\rm r}},\quad
 a:=\frac{1-q_{\rm r}}{q_{\rm r}},\quad
 \mu:=\mu_{\rm E},\quad L:=L_{\rm A},\quad
 A_0:=\frac1{2\alpha}+\frac{\mu}{\alpha^2}.
 \label{eq:conditional-reset-parameters}
\end{equation}
For every $1\leq k<K$, assume that
$y_k:=x_k+\beta d_k$ is a lower certificate on $U_{k+1}$, that the next
greedy shifted evolution remains ordered between its proximal warm start and
$x_{k+1}$, and that its live reset residual $r_k^y$ satisfies the declared
interface identities
\begin{equation}
 \kappa_{k+1}(\iota_kx_k)=r_k^y+\beta Q\iota_kd_k,\qquad
 \norm{r_k^y}_2^2\leq2L\Delta_{k+1}.
 \label{eq:conditional-reset-interface}
\end{equation}
Use the carried estimate point
$v_k^{\rm es}=x_k+a d_k$, and define
\begin{equation*}
 K_{k+1}:=\norm{\kappa_{k+1}(\iota_kx_k)}_2,\qquad
 D_{k+1}:=\norm{\iota_kv_k^{\rm es}-\iota_kx_k}_2,\qquad
 B_{k+1}:=A_0K_{k+1}^2+\mu D_{k+1}^2.
\end{equation*}
Then, for every $\eta>0$,
\begin{equation}
 B_{k+1}\leq
 2A_0(1+\eta)L\Delta_{k+1}
 +\frac2\alpha\left[
  A_0(1+\eta^{-1})\beta^2+\mu a^2\right]\Delta_k.
 \label{eq:conditional-reset-local-bound}
\end{equation}
Optimizing the common $\eta$ and summing the interior reset budgets gives
\begin{align}
 \sum_{k=1}^{K-1}B_{k+1}
 &\leq \mathcal A_{\rm rst}(\alpha)
       \sum_{j=1}^{K}\Delta_j
 =\mathcal A_{\rm rst}(\alpha)
   \{F_\rho(0)-F_{\rho,U_K}(x_K)\},
 \notag\\
 \mathcal A_{\rm rst}(\alpha)
 &:=2A_0\left(\sqrt L+\frac\beta{\sqrt\alpha}\right)^2
   +\frac{2\mu a^2}{\alpha}
 =\Theta(q_{\rm r}^{-4})=\Theta(\alpha^{-2})
 \quad(q_{\rm r}\downarrow0).
 \label{eq:conditional-reset-global-telescope}
\end{align}
The scaling is $\Theta(q_{\rm r}^{-4})$, not
$\Theta(q_{\rm r}^{-3})$.  This proposition is conditional on every displayed
settlement, lower-center, ordered-greedy, and residual identity.  Those
premises have not been proved for the Round--021 two-nonsettled trajectory or
for all canonical caterpillar faces, so the result is not a graph-uniform
accelerated-rate theorem.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Strong convexity at the settled point and
\cref{eq:conditional-reset-face-drops} give
$\norm{d_k}_2^2\leq2\Delta_k/\alpha$.  Using
$Q\preceq I$ in \cref{eq:conditional-reset-interface} yields
\begin{equation*}
 K_{k+1}\leq\sqrt{2L\Delta_{k+1}}
  +\beta\sqrt{\frac{2\Delta_k}{\alpha}},
 \qquad
 D_{k+1}^2=a^2\norm{d_k}_2^2
 \leq\frac{2a^2\Delta_k}{\alpha}.
\end{equation*}
Apply
$(u+v)^2\leq(1+\eta)u^2+(1+\eta^{-1})v^2$ and substitute in $B_{k+1}$ to
obtain \cref{eq:conditional-reset-local-bound}.  Each of the partial sums
$\sum_{k=1}^{K-1}\Delta_k$ and
$\sum_{k=1}^{K-1}\Delta_{k+1}$ is at most
$\sum_{j=1}^{K}\Delta_j$.  The latter telescopes because zero padding does
not change the common full-space objective.  Choosing
$\eta=\beta/\sqrt{\alpha L}$ gives the displayed square.  Finally,
$A_0=\Theta(q_{\rm r}^{-2})$, $\beta=\Theta(1)$,
$\alpha^{-1/2}=\Theta(q_{\rm r}^{-1})$, and the first term therefore has
order $q_{\rm r}^{-4}$; the remaining term has only order
$q_{\rm r}^{-2}$.
\end{proof}

For the literal canonical caterpillar interface, there is a separate exact
representation charge.  If the existing conservative two-product reset
register is invoked after every one of the $m$ canonical admissions, without
sharing the KKT and proximal-start products, then
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal V_j=\vol(\widehat U_j)=6j+3\quad(1\leq j<m),
 \qquad \mathcal V_m=6m,
 \qquad
 2\sum_{j=1}^{m}\mathcal V_j=6m^2+12m-6.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-literal-reset-product-count}
\end{equation}
This is the exact stored-row read count of that \emph{literal} no-sharing
interface.  At a settled zero-momentum checkpoint the two products may be
algebraically shareable, and another implementation may represent or reuse
them differently.  Therefore
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-literal-reset-product-count} is not a necessary
cost, an algorithm-class lower bound, or a graph-uniform obstruction.

The combined Round--022 conclusion is deliberately narrow.  Additive
$O(1)$ and $O(1/q_{\rm r})$ reset/drop packing are refuted, while the general
settled $O(1/\alpha)$ coefficient survives and is sharp in order.  The only
current multi-reset telescope has the stronger conditional interface above
and coefficient $\Theta(\alpha^{-2})$.  Because actual stage work depends on
the reset budgets logarithmically, neither fact settles accelerated work.
Route~A still needs a nonadditive or logarithmic reset ledger valid on the
actual nonsettled greedy trajectory, with candidate exposure and every
product charged.
