\subsection{A second nonsettled admission and two-stage continuation}
\label{sec:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-continuation}

Round~020 stopped after proving that one nonsettled face transition can be
followed by one relative-accuracy stage.  We now use the order structure of
the \emph{greedy} imported AESP--CD oracle to certify the next canonical
batch from that stage's actual output.  This order argument is specific to
the safe-center sandwich for the greedy normalized-KKT rule in
\path{thm:aesp-cd-safe-center}; an arbitrary point satisfying only the
relative stopping inequality is not an admissible substitute.

Keep the parameters in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-auxiliary-parameters} and put
\begin{equation}
 \rho_2(m,\alpha):=
 \begin{cases}
 \dfrac{1+\beta_{\rm A}}{138+3\beta_{\rm A}},&m=2,\\[7pt]
 \dfrac{1+\beta_{\rm A}}{354+3\beta_{\rm A}},&m>2,
 \end{cases}
 \qquad
 \beta_{\rm A}=
 \frac{1-\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}{1+\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}},
 \quad
 \sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}=\sqrt{\frac{\alpha}{1-\alpha}}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-threshold}
\end{equation}
Throughout this subsection
\begin{equation}
 m\geq2,
 \qquad 0<\alpha<\frac12,
 \qquad
 0<\rho<\min\{\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha),\rho_2(m,\alpha)\}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-range}
\end{equation}
This range implies the Round--020 condition $\rho<1/30$.

Let $\widehat x^{\rm ns}$, $y^{\rm ns}$, and $y^+=\iota_0y^{\rm ns}$ be
the first nonsettled endpoint and its zero-padded extrapolated center from
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-nonsettled-state,%
eq:branch-caterpillar-first-nonsettled-auxiliary-state,%
eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-auxiliary-append}.  Although $y^+$ is a
strict lower center on $\widehat U_1$, it is zero on
$b_2,a_1,r_1$.  Hence its three demands toward $\mathcal F_1$ are all
$-\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_v}<0$: the center itself does \emph{not} certify the
second batch.

\begin{lemma}[Safe first-face center and strict greedy endpoint]
\label{lem:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-safe-center}
Let $x_0$ and $x_1$ be the exact restricted RPPR points on
$\widehat U_0$ and $\widehat U_1$.  Then
\begin{equation}
 \frac{(y^+)_{b_1}}{(x_0)_{b_1}}
 =\frac{2(1+2\chi_{\rm A})}
        {3(1+\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}})}<1,
 \qquad 0\leq y^+<x_1.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-center-ratio}
\end{equation}
The point $y^+$ is a lower certificate for the principal face
$\widehat U_1$.  If $z_1$ is the output obtained by initializing at the
standard proximal warm start $u_{\widehat U_1}(y^+)$ and applying the exact
greedy normalized-KKT coordinate updates until the imported relative rule
holds, then
\begin{equation}
 y^+\leq u_{\widehat U_1}(y^+)\leq z_1
 \leq p_1(y^+)<x_1
 \quad\hbox{coordinatewise}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-safe-order}
\end{equation}
Here $p_1(y^+)$ is the exact shifted minimizer on $\widehat U_1$.
In particular, $z_1$ is strictly nonsettled.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The first exact seed-face point has denominator $a_0$, whereas
$\widehat x^{\rm ns}$ has denominator $a_0+\kappa_{\rm A}=3h_0$.
Using $1+\beta_{\rm A}=2/(1+\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}})$ and
$\alpha=\chi_{\rm A}/(1+\chi_{\rm A})$ gives the displayed ratio.  Its
strict inequality is equivalent to
$(4\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}+1)(\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}-1)<0$.
Principal-face monotonicity gives $x_0\leq x_1|_{\widehat U_0}$, and the
three new coordinates of $x_1$ are positive, proving $y^+<x_1$.

The only positive coordinate of $y^+$ is $b_1$.  Its lower residual is
strictly positive because $(y^+)_{b_1}<(x_0)_{b_1}$ and $x_0$ satisfies the
one-coordinate KKT equality.  Thus $y^+$ is a principal-face lower
certificate.  The imported safe-center theorem now gives
$y^+\leq u\leq z_1\leq p_1(y^+)\leq x_1$ for the exact greedy trajectory.
Strictness at the last comparison follows from
\begin{equation*}
 x_1-p_1(y^+)
 =(Q_{\widehat U_1\widehat U_1}+\kappa_{\rm A}I)^{-1}
   \kappa_{\rm A}(x_1-y^+)>0.
\end{equation*}
The inverse is entrywise positive because the principal Stieltjes graph is
connected and the right-hand side is strictly positive.  This proves every
claim.
\end{proof}

The useful point is that the proximal warm start, rather than the center,
already contains enough first-layer mass to open the next batch.

\begin{lemma}[Greedy first-face output certifies the second batch]
\label{lem:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-admission}
Let $u_1:=u_{\widehat U_1}(y^+)$.  If $m>2$, its normalized demands toward
$\mathcal F_1=\{b_3,a_2,r_2\}$ are
\begin{align}
 \frac{(\ell_\rho-Qu_1)_{b_3}}{\sqrt{d_{b_3}}}
 &=\alpha\left[
   \frac{(1+\beta_{\rm A})(1-3\rho)}{324}
   -\frac{13\rho}{12}\right]>0,
 \notag\\
 \frac{(\ell_\rho-Qu_1)_{a_2}}{\sqrt{d_{a_2}}}
 &=\alpha\left[
   \frac{(1+\beta_{\rm A})(1-3\rho)}{144}
   -\frac{9\rho}{8}\right]>0,
 \notag\\
 \frac{(\ell_\rho-Qu_1)_{r_2}}{\sqrt{d_{r_2}}}
 &=\alpha\left[
   \frac{(1+\beta_{\rm A})(1-3\rho)}{108}
   -\frac{5\rho}{4}\right]>0.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-margins}
\end{align}
If $m=2$, then $\mathcal F_1=\{a_2,r_2,r_3\}$ and
\begin{align}
 \frac{(\ell_\rho-Qu_1)_{a_2}}{\sqrt{d_{a_2}}}
 &=\alpha\left[
   \frac{(1+\beta_{\rm A})(1-3\rho)}{72}
   -\frac{5\rho}{4}\right]>0,
 \notag\\
 \frac{(\ell_\rho-Qu_1)_{r_2}}{\sqrt{d_{r_2}}}
 =\frac{(\ell_\rho-Qu_1)_{r_3}}{\sqrt{d_{r_3}}}
 &=\alpha\left[
   \frac{(1+\beta_{\rm A})(1-3\rho)}{108}
   -\frac{5\rho}{4}\right]>0.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-terminal-margins}
\end{align}
Every boundary demand is increasing in its unique parent coordinate.
Therefore the greedy order
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-safe-order} implies that the
same three demands at $z_1$ are strictly positive.  One paid complete-batch
interaction consequently commits exactly $\mathcal F_1$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
In degree-scaled coordinates, write
\begin{equation*}
 t:=\frac{(y^+)_{b_1}}{\sqrt{d_{b_1}}}
 =\frac{(1+\beta_{\rm A})\alpha(1-3\rho)}{9h_0}.
\end{equation*}
Because the shift cancels at its center and $L_{\rm A}=4h_0$, the three new
warm-start coordinates are
\begin{equation*}
 \frac{(u_1)_{b_2}}{\sqrt{d_{b_2}}}
 =-\frac{\alpha\rho}{4h_0}+\frac{t}{12},\quad
 \frac{(u_1)_{a_1}}{\sqrt{d_{a_1}}}
 =-\frac{\alpha\rho}{4h_0}+\frac{t}{8},\quad
 \frac{(u_1)_{r_1}}{\sqrt{d_{r_1}}}
 =-\frac{\alpha\rho}{4h_0}+\frac{t}{4}.
\end{equation*}
Substitution into the unique-parent boundary rows gives the displayed
formulas.  For $m>2$, the $b_3$ inequality is restrictive and is equivalent
to $\rho<\rho_2(m,\alpha)$; for $m=2$, either terminal-leaf inequality is
restrictive and has the same equivalence.  The other expressions are then
strictly positive.  Finally, a tree-boundary demand is affine increasing in
its one parent, so $z_1\geq u_1$ preserves all three strict signs.
\end{proof}

Put
\begin{equation}
 d_1:=z_1-\iota_0\widehat x^{\rm ns}>0,
 \qquad
 y_1:=z_1+\beta_{\rm A}d_1,
 \qquad
 v_1^{\rm es}:=z_1+
   \left(\frac1{\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}-1\right)d_1.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-momentum}
\end{equation}
The inequality is coordinatewise: on $b_1$ it follows from
$z_1\geq y^+>\iota_0\widehat x^{\rm ns}$, while the three other coordinates
of $z_1$ are positive.  Thus $y_1$ is a genuinely extrapolated center.

After the second interaction, zero-pad $z_1,d_1,y_1,v_1^{\rm es}$ to
$\widehat U_2$.  Compute and charge the old-face proximal array at $y_1$
before the interaction.  Since the padded new coordinates make no
contribution to an old row, the enlarged-face proximal array retains those
old cells and satisfies
\begin{equation}
 u_{\widehat U_2}(\iota_1y_1)_v
 =\frac{(\ell_\rho-Q\iota_1y_1)_v}{L_{\rm A}}>0
 \qquad(v\in\mathcal F_1).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-auxiliary-append}
\end{equation}
Indeed $y_1\geq z_1$ and the certified boundary demands at $z_1$ are
strict.  Hence the center, momentum, and estimate arrays each append three
zeros and the proximal array appends three positive cells: exactly twelve
new coordinate records.

The second enlarged-face estimate proof is again restarted explicitly.  Put
\begin{align}
 K_2^2
 &:=\norm{\kappa_{\rho,\widehat U_2}(\iota_1z_1)}_2^2,
 &D_2^2
 &:=\norm{\iota_1v_1^{\rm es}-\iota_1z_1}_2^2,
 \notag\\
 B_2
 &:=\frac{K_2^2}{2\alpha}
   +\mu_{\rm E}D_2^2
   +\frac{\mu_{\rm E}}{\alpha^2}K_2^2.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-shock-budget}
\end{align}

\begin{lemma}[Computable reset at the second nonsettled admission]
\label{lem:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-shock-reset}
The two stored-row products at the padded primal and extrapolated center,
together with exact norm accumulators, compute the warm start above and the
observable scalars $K_2^2,D_2^2,B_2$.  They satisfy
\begin{equation}
 0<\Psi_2(\iota_1z_1,\iota_1v_1^{\rm es})\leq B_2.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-shock-bound}
\end{equation}
Incremental to the paid candidate rows and ordinary stage-state writes, this
second reset operation has vector
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{1\to2}^{\rm NS})=
 \bigl(2\mathcal V_2,2,0,0,O(1),\Theta(\mathcal V_2),0,
 \Theta(C_2),O(C_2),12+\Theta(1),0\bigr),
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-transition-vector}
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation*}
 \mathcal V_2:=\vol(\widehat U_2)
 =\begin{cases}12,&m=2,\\15,&m>2,\end{cases}
 \qquad
 C_2=C(\widehat U_2)
 =\begin{cases}19,&m=2,\\22,&m>2.\end{cases}
\end{equation*}
No response, optimum query, or external emission is included.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The proof of
\Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-shock-reset} applies verbatim with
$\bar x=\iota_1z_1$, $\bar v=\iota_1v_1^{\rm es}$, and the restricted
optimum $x_2$.  Strict positivity follows because $\bar v$ is zero on
$\mathcal F_1$ whereas $x_2$ is strictly positive there.  Strong convexity
gives
$F_{\rho,\widehat U_2}(\bar x)-F_{\rho,\widehat U_2}(x_2)
 \leq K_2^2/(2\alpha)$ and
$\norm{\bar x-x_2}_2\leq K_2/\alpha$; the triangle inequality then yields
the displayed upper bound.  One product forms the minimum-norm KKT vector at
$\bar x$, and the other forms the proximal start at $\iota_1y_1$.  Three
appends in each of four arrays plus the norm/reset scalars give the
materialization coordinate.  The retained-face counts give the stated
$\mathcal V_2$ and $C_2$.
\end{proof}

Define \texttt{SecondNonsettled-BC-AESP}$_{0\to2}^{+2}$ to perform the
Round--020 first shifted stage and admission, but to lock both subsequent
fixed-face oracles to the exact greedy normalized-KKT AESP--CD rule.  Its
first greedy output is $z_1$ above; it scans the live rows in $\mathcal F_1$,
certifies and commits that exact batch, executes the second reset, and then
runs exactly one relative-accuracy greedy stage on $\widehat U_2$ from the
carried extrapolated center $\iota_1y_1$.  It then retires the accelerated
arena and builds the native response directly on the retained rows of
$\widehat U_2$.

For the second stage put
\begin{equation}
 L_2^{\rm rel}:=2+\frac34\log_+\!\left(
 \frac{16(1-\alpha)\mathcal V_2}
      {\delta_{\rm A}(1-2\alpha)}\right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-two-nonsettled-oracle-log}
\end{equation}
Together with $L_1^{\rm rel}$ from
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-oracle-log}, define
\begin{align}
 \mathfrak A_{\rm 2NS}
 &:=\mathcal V_0+2\mathcal V_1+\mathcal V_1L_1^{\rm rel}
    +2\mathcal V_2+\mathcal V_2L_2^{\rm rel},
 \notag\\
 \mathfrak D_{\rm 2NS}&:=C_2+\mathfrak A_{\rm 2NS},
 &\mathfrak H_{\rm 2NS}
 &:=C_2+(1+\log(2+C_2))\mathfrak A_{\rm 2NS}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-two-nonsettled-budgets}
\end{align}
Every warm start, stored-row product, greedy coordinate row, KKT update,
stopping test, state access, and oracle logarithm is included.

\begin{theorem}[Two nonsettled admissions, two continuations, and rate STOP]
\label{thm:branch-caterpillar-two-nonsettled-continuation}
Under \cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-range}, the named greedy
policy commits $\mathcal F_0$ and $\mathcal F_1$ from two strictly nonsettled
points, performs a genuine extrapolated relative-accuracy stage after each
admission, and reaches the native handoff at $\widehat U_2$.  Immediately
before the native response build its complete prefix vector is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm 2NS}^{\rm pref})=\bigl(&
 \mathcal V_2+O(\mathfrak A_{\rm 2NS}),
 3+O(\mathfrak A_{\rm 2NS}),2,0,O(\mathfrak H_{\rm 2NS}),
 O(\mathfrak A_{\rm 2NS}),0,\\
 &O(C_2),O(C_2),O(\mathfrak D_{\rm 2NS}),6+\Theta(2)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-two-nonsettled-prefix-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The exposed, numerical, and admitted arenas are exactly $\widehat U_2$;
only the labels of $\mathcal F_2$ are known when $m>2$.  No row beyond the
two live certificate batches is exposed.

The paid native suffix has vector
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm 2NS}^{\rm post})=\bigl(&
 \vol(S^\star\setminus\widehat U_2),m-2,m-1,0,
 O(C(S^\star)),0,\\
 &O(C(S^\star)+(m-2)\log(2+m)),
 \Theta(C(S^\star)),O(C(S^\star)),\Theta(\card{S^\star}),\\
 &\card{S^\star\setminus\widehat U_2}
  +\card{S^\star}+\Theta(m-1)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-two-nonsettled-post-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Consequently
\begin{equation}
 R_{\rm int}^{\rm total}=m+1,
 \qquad
 R_{\rm adj}^{\rm total}=m+1+O(\mathfrak A_{\rm 2NS}),
 \qquad
 W_{\rm total}=O\!\left(
  \mathfrak H_{\rm 2NS}+C(S^\star)\log(2+C(S^\star))\right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-two-nonsettled-total-work}
\end{equation}
The structural first-exposure part of $R_{\rm adj}$ is exactly $m+1$.

This is a finite two-admission/two-continuation GO, not an accelerated-rate
theorem.  In particular, $B_2$ is a fresh observable reset with no proved
inequality against $B_{\rm ns}$, and the first greedy stage is used only for
its safe order and gate signs.  The argument neither amortizes the two
estimate shocks nor proves a multi-face contraction or speedup.  The exact
$\rho_2$ margin, greedy-policy restriction, live-row exposures, four charged
transition products, both oracle logarithms, and native suffix remain
visible.  All fixed-family, strict-$\rho_{\rm can}$, exact-real, branch-seed,
delta/certificate, non-PPR, non-finite-precision, and non-bit restrictions
remain in force.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The first admission, reset, and continued stage are the Round--020
construction.  The additional greedy restriction invokes
\Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-safe-center}; its warm start
and every later greedy iterate stay ordered below $p_1(y^+)$.
\Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-admission} therefore supplies
the paid second certificate from the actual nonsettled output $z_1$.  The
seed row and the two live boundary batches expose exactly $\widehat U_2$ in
three structural rounds and degree volume $\mathcal V_2$.  The two resets
and two fixed-face relative stages are bounded by
$\mathfrak A_{\rm 2NS}$; their recurrence, heap/control, state, and memory
charges give
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-two-nonsettled-prefix-eleven-vector}.  The strict
momentum in \cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-second-nonsettled-momentum} makes the
second carried center genuinely extrapolated.

After the second reply, every row of $\widehat U_2$ is retained and no native
response on that face has been credited.  The reviewed direct suffix at
$q=2$ therefore gives
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-two-nonsettled-post-eleven-vector}.  The
structural and interaction partitions are
\begin{equation*}
 3+(m-2)=m+1,
 \qquad
 2+(m-1)=m+1.
\end{equation*}
Adding the explicit numerical adjacency rounds, summing additive work, and
taking sequential memory peaks proves
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-two-nonsettled-total-work}.  Since neither the
safe-order argument nor the second KKT reset compares $B_2$ with
$B_{\rm ns}$, the stated rate STOP is exact.
\end{proof}
