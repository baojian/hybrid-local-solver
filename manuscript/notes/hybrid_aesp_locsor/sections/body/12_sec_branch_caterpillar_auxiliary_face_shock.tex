\subsection{Sparse auxiliary appends and the first exact estimate shock}
\label{sec:branch-caterpillar-auxiliary-face-shock}

The dense fresh-array term in
\Cref{prop:branch-caterpillar-fresh-auxiliary-shock} is an interface cost,
not a mathematical necessity.  We now test the smallest favorable
accelerated-state transition: the old face is exactly settled and its
momentum is zero.  Even there, one part of the state carries sparsely and one
part provably does not carry with zero shock.

The definitions below are exactly the composite/AESP definitions imported
from \path{aesp_cd_l1_rppr}.  In that note,
\path{eq:aesp-cd-subproblem} and
\path{eq:aesp-cd-relative-parameters} give the shift, smoothness, and
condition ratio; the paragraph following
\path{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-envelope} gives the envelope parameters.  Thus
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \kappa_{\rm A}&:=1-2\alpha,
 &L_{\rm A}&:=2(1-\alpha),
 &\chi_{\rm A}&:=\frac{\alpha}{1-\alpha},\\
 \mu_{\rm E}&:=\kappa_{\rm A}\chi_{\rm A}
 =\frac{\alpha(1-2\alpha)}{1-\alpha}.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-auxiliary-parameters}
\end{equation}
The imported \path{eq:aesp-cd-smoothed-start}, on a fixed face $U$ at
center $y$, is
\begin{equation}
 u_U(y):=\prox_{g_{\rho,U}/L_{\rm A}}
 \left(y-\frac{\nabla f_{t,U}(y)}{L_{\rm A}}\right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-auxiliary-proximal-start}
\end{equation}
The estimate point and potential are the exact objects in
\emph{eq:aesp-cd-momentum-states} and
\emph{eq:aesp-cd-potential-defect}.  To make their face dependence
explicit, define the restricted envelope
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{Env}_k(y):=
 \min_{w\in\mathbb R^{\widehat U_k}}
 \left\{F_{\rho,\widehat U_k}(w)
       +\frac{\kappa_{\rm A}}2\norm{w-y}_2^2\right\}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-restricted-envelope}
\end{equation}
and the exact analytical estimate certificate
\begin{equation}
 \Psi_k(x,v):=
 \operatorname{Env}_k(x)-F_{\rho,\widehat U_k}(x_k)
 +\frac{\mu_{\rm E}}2\norm{v-x_k}_2^2.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-restricted-estimate-certificate}
\end{equation}
The quantity $\Psi_k$ is an analysis certificate because it contains the
unknown restricted optimum.  It is not a free online scalar.

At a settled zero-momentum checkpoint put the previous and current primal
points equal to $x_k$.  The AESP center is then $y_k=x_k$, the estimate point
is $v_k^{\rm es}=x_k$, the explicit momentum is zero, and
$u_{\widehat U_k}(x_k)=x_k$.  Consequently
\begin{equation}
 \Psi_k(x_k,x_k)=0.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-old-zero-estimate}
\end{equation}
The coordinate records proposed for enlarged-face initialization are therefore
the direct zero-padding of the imported momentum identities,
\begin{equation}
 y_{k+1}^{\rm init}=\iota_kx_k,
 \qquad
 d_{k+1}^{\rm init}=\iota_k0=0,
 \qquad
 v_{k+1}^{{\rm es},{\rm init}}=\iota_kx_k.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-settled-auxiliary-state-append}
\end{equation}
This is an exact algebraic transfer of three arrays.  It is not yet a claim
that the old estimate-sequence proof continues on the enlarged objective.

\begin{lemma}[Exact proximal append at a settled canonical face]
\label{lem:branch-caterpillar-settled-proximal-append}
Fix $m\geq2$, $0<\alpha<1/2$, and
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)$.  For every $0\leq k<m$, zero-pad the
settled center to $y^+:=\iota_kx_k$.  Then the standard composite proximal
start on the enlarged face is
\begin{equation}
 u_{\widehat U_{k+1}}(y^+)
 =\iota_kx_k+
   \frac1{L_{\rm A}}\sum_{v\in\mathcal F_k}g_{k,v}e_v,
 \qquad g_{k,v}>0.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-settled-proximal-append}
\end{equation}
Thus every old proximal coordinate is retained verbatim, but the three new
proximal coordinates are strictly positive.  Appending zeros to the old
proximal array is not the imported warm-start map.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
At the raw center the quadratic shift cancels, exactly as in the imported
\emph{lem:aesp-cd-center-cancel}.  Every coordinate of $x_k$ on
$\widehat U_k$ is strictly positive, so its restricted KKT equality is
\[
 (Qx_k-b)_i=-\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_i}
 \qquad(i\in\widehat U_k).
\]
Soft thresholding in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-auxiliary-proximal-start} therefore returns
$(x_k)_i$ on every old coordinate.  A new $v\in\mathcal F_k$ has center
coordinate zero and only its retained parent contributes to the old-face
product.  Its positive soft-thresholded value is
\[
 \frac{[b_v-(Q\iota_kx_k)_v-\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_v}]_+}
      {L_{\rm A}}
 =\frac{g_{k,v}}{L_{\rm A}}.
\]
The imported strict canonical chronology gives $g_{k,v}>0$ for all three
vertices, proving the formula.
\end{proof}

The proximal writes are not the first obstruction to sparse transport: there
are only three of them.  The first obstruction to carrying the old
accelerated certificate unchanged is the face change itself.  Define the
exact objective shock and analytical estimate shock
\begin{align}
 \Delta_k^{\rm face}
 &:=F_{\rho,\widehat U_{k+1}}(\iota_kx_k)
    -F_{\rho,\widehat U_{k+1}}(x_{k+1}),
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-objective-face-shock}\\
 \Sigma_k^{\rm es}
 &:=\Psi_{k+1}(\iota_kx_k,\iota_kx_k).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-estimate-face-shock}
\end{align}

\begin{proposition}[Positive analytical estimate shock under zero padding]
\label{prop:branch-caterpillar-zero-estimate-carry-fails}
Under the hypotheses of
\Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-settled-proximal-append}, every canonical
admission satisfies
\begin{align}
 \Delta_k^{\rm face}
 &=\frac12\norm{x_{k+1}-\iota_kx_k}_{
                 Q_{\widehat U_{k+1}\widehat U_{k+1}}}^2>0,
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-objective-face-shock-identity}\\
 \Sigma_k^{\rm es}
 &=\operatorname{Env}_{k+1}(\iota_kx_k)
   -F_{\rho,\widehat U_{k+1}}(x_{k+1})
   +\frac{\mu_{\rm E}}2
      \norm{\iota_kx_k-x_{k+1}}_2^2
 \notag\\
 &\geq\frac{\mu_{\rm E}}2
      \sum_{v\in\mathcal F_k}(x_{k+1})_v^2>0.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-estimate-face-shock-positive}
\end{align}
Hence the old exact certificate
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-old-zero-estimate} cannot be reused as a zero
certificate on the new face.  A correct interface must either restart the
estimate-sequence proof at $\widehat U_{k+1}$ or explicitly introduce and
charge an upper bound for the positive analytical shock
$\Sigma_k^{\rm es}$.  The proposition is not a lower bound on the work of
computing that bound and is not an obstruction to another accelerated
potential.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
On the positive orthant the restricted RPPR objective is a quadratic with
linear term.  The enlarged restricted optimum is strictly positive on
$\mathcal F_k$ and satisfies its full KKT equality.  Expanding the quadratic
about $x_{k+1}$ gives the first identity.  It is strict because
$\iota_kx_k$ is zero on $\mathcal F_k$ whereas $x_{k+1}$ is positive there.

The envelope term minus the new optimum is nonnegative because the envelope
minimizes the unshifted objective plus a nonnegative quadratic.  The remaining
squared-distance term is bounded below by its three new-coordinate summands.
Since $\mu_{\rm E}>0$ and those coordinates are strictly positive, the
estimate shock is strict.  Together with
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-old-zero-estimate}, this rules out unchanged
zero-shock carry.
\end{proof}

To separate coordinate transport from this analytical reset, define
\texttt{SettledAuxAppend} as the following representation-level operation.
Incremental to an already paid zero-padding of the numerical vector and to
already retained candidate rows, it retains every old auxiliary cell,
appends three zeros to each explicit center, momentum, and estimate-point
array, appends the three positive values in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-settled-proximal-append} to the proximal array,
and writes one estimate-reset epoch marker.  With one explicit record for
each of those four named arrays, this is exactly twelve new auxiliary
coordinate records and no old auxiliary-coordinate write.  It reads no old
row, applies no old-face product, makes no adjacency query, and invokes no
response.  Its incremental vector is
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{k\to k+1}^{\rm aux})=
 \bigl(0,0,0,0,O(1),O(1),0,
 \Theta(\mathcal C_{k+1}^{\rm can}),O(1),12+\Theta(1),0\bigr).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-settled-auxiliary-transition-vector}
\end{equation}
Here the persistent coordinate is the peak after transition, not a new
linear allocation.  The constant recurrence work computes the three
retained-row demands and divides by $L_{\rm A}$; the materialization
coordinate contains the twelve appends and the scalar reset record.  If the
candidate rows are not retained, their separate first-exposure increment is
the one stated in
\Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-transition}.  If an
implementation instead materializes a numerical value of
$\Sigma_k^{\rm es}$, every response, solve, query, and write used to obtain it
must be added; none is credited in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-settled-auxiliary-transition-vector}.

For completeness, the first nonzero checkpoint gives a concrete paid audit.

\noindent
The policy \emph{FirstAuxShock-BC-AESP}$_{1\to2}$ first runs the existing
response-native \texttt{FirstLayer-BC}$_1$ execution, materializes a paid
numerical copy of $x_1$, and initializes one explicit center, momentum,
estimate-point, and proximal record per coordinate consistently with that
settled state.  It scans the three rows in $\mathcal F_1$ and commits that
exact strict canonical batch.  It then zero-pads the numerical point, applies
\texttt{SettledAuxAppend}, records the reset marker rather than
claiming a numerical value for the analytical shock, and stops before the
first enlarged-face numerical update.  Thus it audits an actual state
transition but performs no accelerated stage.

\begin{theorem}[First-layer auxiliary append and exact shock STOP]
\label{thm:branch-caterpillar-first-auxiliary-shock-handoff}
Fix $m\geq2$, $0<\alpha<1/2$, and
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)$.  The prefix
\texttt{FirstAuxShock-BC-AESP}$_{1\to2}$ reaches
$U_{\rm adm}=U_{\rm num}=E_{\rm row}=\widehat U_2$, carries every old
coordinate of its explicit accelerated arrays without rewrite, appends the
twelve exact auxiliary records above, and resets only the estimate
certificate.  Its complete vector is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm aux,1\to2}^{\rm pref})=
 \bigl(&9+\nu_1,3,2,0,O(1),O(1),O(1),
 O(C_2),O(C_2),\Theta(C_2),6+\Theta(2)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-auxiliary-prefix-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The response coordinate is the already charged constant-size native response
that settled $x_1$; it does not compute $x_2$ or
$\Sigma_1^{\rm es}$.  The six output labels and two constant-size replies
are the first two canonical delta/certificate interactions.

Discarding the audit arrays by one epoch pointer and directly building the
native response on $\widehat U_2$ gives the post vector
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm aux,1\to2}^{\rm post})=
 \bigl(&\vol(S^\star\setminus\widehat U_2),m-2,m-1,0,
 O(C(S^\star)),0,\\
 &O(C(S^\star)+(m-2)\log(2+m)),
 \Theta(C(S^\star)),O(C(S^\star)),\Theta(\card{S^\star}),\\
 &\card{S^\star\setminus\widehat U_2}
  +\card{S^\star}+\Theta(m-1)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-auxiliary-post-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The total interaction and first-exposure round counts are both exactly
$m+1$, and total exact-cell work is
\begin{equation}
 O\!\left(C(S^\star)\log(2+C(S^\star))\right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-auxiliary-total-work}
\end{equation}

This theorem is a response-assisted, settled-state transition audit.  Its GO
is that an explicitly dense old auxiliary arena need not be repopulated:
only a constant number of new vector cells are written.  Its STOP is that the
imported proximal array is not an all-zero append and the old zero analytical
estimate certificate is false on the enlarged face.  Because the execution
uses exact native settlement before the tested admission, resets the
estimate proof, runs no accelerated stage after the append, and hands off to
the native response, it proves no acceleration speedup, no subquadratic
automatically exploring prefix, no amortization of
$\Sigma_k^{\rm es}$, and no lower bound for other representations or
potentials.  All fixed-family, strict-range, exact-real, branch-seed,
delta-interface, non-PPR, and non-finite-precision restrictions remain.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The vector for \texttt{FirstLayer-BC}$_1$ is
$(9,2,1,0,O(1),0,O(1),O(1),O(1),\Theta(1),\Theta(1))$.
The additional scan of $\mathcal F_1$ costs $\nu_1$ adjacency cells and one
round; the exact $x_1$ demands certify one additional interaction with three
delta labels and one reply.  Initializing the four constant-size arrays and
the paid primal copy, then applying \texttt{SettledAuxAppend}, costs constant
recurrence/control work and $\Theta(C_2)$ state/materialization cells, with no
new response.  This proves
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-auxiliary-prefix-eleven-vector} and, by the
two preceding results, the exact state and reset claims.

After the second reply, rows through $\widehat U_2$ are retained and no
response on that face has been credited.  The reviewed direct suffix at
$q=2$ therefore gives
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-auxiliary-post-eleven-vector}.  The round
partitions are
\[
 3+(m-2)=m+1,
 \qquad
 2+(m-1)=m+1.
\]
Summing additive coordinates and taking sequential memory peaks gives
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-auxiliary-total-work}.
\end{proof}
