\subsection{An on-demand first-layer margin and a numerical gate splice}
\label{sec:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-splice}

The result below does not maintain an eager array of future margins.  It
computes one scalar certificate only when the actual execution is already at
$\widehat U_1$, from the retained constant-size response and one paid scan of
the three currently live boundary rows.  This distinction matters: the scan
exposes rows in $\mathcal F_1$, but it neither admits those vertices nor lets
the numerical method use their coordinates.

Let
\begin{equation}
 \xi_1:=a_0^2-\frac{11h_0^2}{18}>0.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-response-denominator}
\end{equation}
The four restricted equations at $\widehat U_1$ give
\begin{align}
 (x_1)_{b_1}
  &=\frac{\alpha(a_0-3\rho)}{\sqrt3\,\xi_1},
 \notag\\
 (x_1)_{b_2}
  &=\frac{h_0(x_1)_{b_1}/3-\alpha\rho\sqrt3}{a_0},
 &
 (x_1)_{a_1}
  &=\frac{h_0(x_1)_{b_1}/\sqrt6-\alpha\rho\sqrt2}{a_0},
 \notag\\
 (x_1)_{r_1}
  &=\frac{h_0(x_1)_{b_1}/\sqrt3-\alpha\rho}{a_0}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-response-formula}
\end{align}
Define the scan-derived scalar
\begin{equation}
 \underline\mu_1:=
 \begin{cases}
  \min\left\{
   (x_1)_{b_2}-\dfrac{3\alpha\rho\sqrt3}{h_0},
   (x_1)_{a_1}-\dfrac{2\alpha\rho\sqrt2}{h_0}
       \right\},&m>2,\\[7pt]
  \min\left\{
   (x_1)_{b_2}-\dfrac{\alpha\rho\sqrt3}{h_0},
   (x_1)_{a_1}-\dfrac{\alpha\rho\sqrt2}{h_0}
       \right\},&m=2.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-local-margin}
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[Exact on-demand certificate for the first-layer margin]
\label{lem:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-certificate}
Fix $m\geq2$, $0<\alpha<1/2$, and
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)$.  At the actual checkpoint
$\widehat U_1$, scan each row in $\mathcal F_1$ once and retain it.  The local
incidences, degrees, $\alpha$, $\rho$, and
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-response-formula} are an exact-real,
locally checkable certificate that
\begin{equation}
 0<\underline\mu_1=\mu_1.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-equality}
\end{equation}
No value for another checkpoint is computed or retained.  Without the stated
strict-range promise, the same local routine fails closed whenever its
computed minimum is nonpositive; the lemma makes no conclusion in that case.

Put
\begin{equation}
 \nu_1:=\vol(\mathcal F_1)
 =\begin{cases}6,&m>2,\\3,&m=2.\end{cases}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-boundary-volume}
\end{equation}
Incremental to the already paid state of
\texttt{FirstLayer-BC}$_1$, one batched implementation of this on-demand
certificate has the complete vector
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\mu_1})
 =\bigl(\nu_1,1,0,0,O(1),0,O(1),O(1),O(1),O(1),0\bigr).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-certificate-vector}
\end{equation}
The response coordinate pays the constant-size retained-response readout and
algebraic cross-check in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-response-formula}; control pays the
degree, parent, strict-sign, minimum, and certificate checks; persistent and
materialization cells pay the three retained rows and the constant-size
certificate transcript.  There is no external interaction or emission.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
On $\widehat U_1=\{b_1,b_2,a_1,r_1\}$ the diagonal of $Q$ is $a_0$ and its
three off-diagonal entries incident to $b_1$ are
$-h_0/3$, $-h_0/\sqrt6$, and $-h_0/\sqrt3$.  The shifted loads at
$b_1,b_2,a_1,r_1$ are respectively
\[
 \frac{\alpha(1-3\rho)}{\sqrt3},\qquad
 -\alpha\rho\sqrt3,\qquad
 -\alpha\rho\sqrt2,\qquad
 -\alpha\rho.
\]
Eliminating the last three coordinates gives the coefficient
$a_0-11h_0^2/(18a_0)$ at $b_1$ and proves
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-response-formula}.

For any unseeded boundary vertex $v$ with active parent $p$, its load is
$-\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_v}$ and
$\beta_{1,v}=h_0/\sqrt{d_vd_p}$.  Therefore
\begin{equation}
 \frac{g_{1,v}}{\beta_{1,v}}
 =(x_1)_p-\frac{\alpha\rho}{h_0}d_v\sqrt{d_p}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-ratio}
\end{equation}
If $m>2$, the scanned boundary is $\{b_3,a_2,r_2\}$, with degree/parent
pairs $(3,b_2),(2,a_1),(1,b_2)$.  The $r_2$ ratio strictly exceeds the
$b_3$ ratio, leaving exactly the first line of
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-local-margin}.  If $m=2$, the
boundary is $\{a_2,r_2,r_3\}$; all three vertices have degree one and the
two $r$ ratios coincide, giving the second line.  The imported strict-range
chronology makes all three exact demands positive, so their minimum ratio is
strictly positive.  This proves
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-equality}.

The four rows of $\widehat U_1$ were paid by
\texttt{FirstLayer-BC}$_1$.  The three boundary rows have total degree
$\nu_1$ and can be scanned in one additional batched round.  All remaining
arithmetic and storage is constant size.  Assigning each operation as stated
proves
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-certificate-vector}.
\end{proof}

We now use the certificate in one deliberately auditable numerical splice.
It is useful as an interface witness, not as an efficiency claim: the exact
constant-size response needed to certify $\underline\mu_1$ could itself
decide the same boundary.  We nevertheless retire that response before the
handoff, so the coordinate state actually carried into the next gate is the
signed numerical endpoint, not a relabeled exact settlement.

Put
\begin{align}
 \mathcal V_1&:=\vol(\widehat U_1)=9,
 &C_1&:=C(\widehat U_1)=13,
 &C_2&:=C(\widehat U_2)=16+\nu_1,
 \notag\\
 L_1^{\rm rel}
 &:=2+\frac34\log_+\!\left(
  \frac{16(1-\alpha)^2\mathcal V_1}
       {\delta_{\rm A}(1-2\alpha)(1-\alpha)}
                         \right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-oracle-log}
\end{align}
Let $F_{\rho,\widehat U_1}$ denote $F_\rho$ restricted to vectors supported
on $\widehat U_1$, with all other coordinates fixed to zero.  For an
explicitly materialized signed start $z^{(0)}$ on that face, let
\begin{equation}
 \Delta_{1,0}:=
 F_{\rho,\widehat U_1}(z^{(0)})
 -F_{\rho,\widehat U_1}(x_1)\geq0
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-initial-gap}
\end{equation}
and define the integer cap
\begin{equation}
 T_1(z^{(0)}):=
 \begin{cases}
  0,&\Delta_{1,0}=0,\\[2pt]
  1+\left\lfloor
   \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}
   \log_+\!\left(
    \dfrac{4\Delta_{1,0}}
          {\alpha\underline\mu_1^2}
               \right)
     \right\rfloor,&\Delta_{1,0}>0.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-stage-cap}
\end{equation}
Thus the zero-gap branch invokes neither the logarithm nor a numerical stage.
For the named nontrivial instantiation below we use $z^{(0)}=0$.  Then
\begin{equation}
 \Delta_{1,0}
 =\frac12 x_1^TQ_{\widehat U_1\widehat U_1}x_1>0,
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-zero-start-gap}
\end{equation}
so at least one genuine numerical stage is run.

\begin{definition}[Margin-certified first-layer numerical splice]
\label{def:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-policy}
The policy \texttt{MarginCert-BC-}\allowbreak
\texttt{AESP}$_1(z^{(0)})$ performs the following operations.
\begin{enumerate}
 \item Run \texttt{FirstLayer-BC}$_1$ to the actual checkpoint
       $\widehat U_1$, and run the single on-demand certificate in
       \Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-certificate}.  Store
       $\underline\mu_1$, the exact restricted optimum-value scalar, and the
       local certificate transcript.  Retire the exact response arena by an
       epoch pointer; do not call its point the accelerated state.
 \item On the fixed numerical face $\widehat U_1$, run exactly
       $T_1(z^{(0)})$ relative-accuracy composite Catalyst stages with
       AESP--CD inner solves, using the same relative oracle as
       \Cref{thm:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-prefix}.  Every warm-start
       row and repeated coordinate row is rescanned and charged.  Coordinates
       in $\mathcal F_1$ remain identically zero in this numerical arena.  The
       accelerated target here is the restricted point $x_1$, not the full
       RPPR minimizer; no claim that $\widehat U_1$ contains $S^\star$ is used.
 \item Materialize the signed endpoint $z^{(T_1)}$ and locally recompute its
       exact restricted objective gap from the retained four face rows and
       optimum-value scalar.  Store the strict certificate
       \[
        F_{\rho,\widehat U_1}(z^{(T_1)})
        -F_{\rho,\widehat U_1}(x_1)
       <\frac{\alpha\underline\mu_1^2}{2}.
       \]
       Put
       $\Delta_{1,T_1}:=
       F_{\rho,\widehat U_1}(z^{(T_1)})-
       F_{\rho,\widehat U_1}(x_1)$ and
       $r_1^{\rm cert}:=\sqrt{2\Delta_{1,T_1}/\alpha}
       <\underline\mu_1$.  If $\mu_{1,v}:=g_{1,v}/\beta_{1,v}$ is the
       corresponding one of the three stored ratios, certify
       \begin{equation}
        (\ell_\rho-Qz^{(T_1)})_v
        \geq\beta_{1,v}(\mu_{1,v}-r_1^{\rm cert})
        \geq\beta_{1,v}(\underline\mu_1-r_1^{\rm cert})>0.
        \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-positive-interval}
       \end{equation}
       For each of the three stored boundary rows, compute the signed demand
       $(\ell_\rho-Qz^{(T_1)})_v$ and its certified positive lower interval.
       Stop before the second external interaction, carrying the signed
       endpoint, the scalar certificate, the paid rows, and the common
       admitted checkpoint $\widehat U_1$.
 \item In the post phase, use those three certified signed demands for the
       canonical all-violations interaction, emit and commit
       $\mathcal F_1$, then build and exactly settle the native response on
       $\widehat U_2$.  Retire the signed arena and continue
       \emph{CaterpillarCanonicalLayerDelta} from this actual checkpoint.
\end{enumerate}
The zero-gap branch in step 2 has $z^{(0)}=x_1$ by strong convexity and runs
zero stages.  It is correct but is not called a numerical acceleration.  The
named zero-start instance has positive gap by
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-zero-start-gap} and is the genuinely
numerical comparison witness.
\end{definition}

Immediately before the second interaction, its four arenas are
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 U_{\mathrm{adm}}&=\widehat U_1,
 &U_{\mathrm{num}}&=\widehat U_1,
 &\supp(z^{(T_1)})&\subseteq\widehat U_1,\\
 E_{\mathrm{row}}&=\widehat U_2,
 &E_{\mathrm{lab}}&=
 \begin{cases}
  \widehat U_2,&m=2,\\
  \widehat U_3,&m>2.
 \end{cases}
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-arenas}
\end{equation}
Here $U_{\mathrm{num}}$ is the allocated signed face; its actual support may
be smaller.  The rows in $\mathcal F_1=\widehat U_2\setminus\widehat U_1$
are scanned lookahead only and have zero numerical coordinates.  When $m>2$,
the labels in $\widehat U_3\setminus\widehat U_2$ are names returned by those
rows; their own rows are unscanned.  No lookahead vertex is admitted before
the certified external interaction.

Because this interface has already performed a paid internal prequery of
$\mathcal F_1$, it is not asserted to satisfy the stricter endpoint clause in
\Cref{def:branch-caterpillar-canonical-checkpoint-prefix}.  The post vector
below is proved directly from the stored rows and the native reporter state;
it is not obtained by silently invoking that older conversion theorem.

Define the charged numerical budgets
\begin{align}
 \mathfrak A_1&:=T_1(z^{(0)})\mathcal V_1L_1^{\rm rel},
 &\mathfrak D_1&:=C_2+\mathfrak A_1,
 \notag\\
\mathfrak H_1&:=C_2+
  \bigl(1+\log(2+C_1)\bigr)\mathfrak A_1.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-budgets}
\end{align}

\begin{theorem}[Capped numerical gate at the actual first-layer checkpoint]
\label{thm:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-handoff}
Fix $m\geq2$, $0<\alpha<1/2$, and
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)$.  The policy in
\Cref{def:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-policy} certifies the
complete batch $\mathcal F_1$ and returns the exact RPPR minimizer.  Its
pre-handoff phase ends at the actual common checkpoint $\widehat U_1$ before
the second external interaction.  In the shared order it has the complete
prefix vector
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm MC1}^{\rm pref})=\bigl(&
 9+\nu_1+O(\mathfrak A_1),
 3+O(\mathfrak A_1),1,0,O(\mathfrak H_1),
 O(\mathfrak A_1),O(1),\\
 &O(C_2),O(C_1),O(\mathfrak D_1),\Theta(1)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-prefix-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The prefix emission is exactly the first three delta labels and one
constant-size certificate reply; the on-demand margin and all numerical
certificates are internal control/state and emit no cell.  In particular,
\begin{equation}
 B_{\rm MC1}^{\rm full}=O(\mathfrak H_1).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-prefix-work}
\end{equation}

After the handoff, the stored rows of $\mathcal F_1$ are not scanned again.
One batched continuation has the complete post vector
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm MC1}^{\rm post})=\bigl(&
 \vol(S^\star\setminus\widehat U_2),m-2,m,0,
 O(C(S^\star)),0,\\
 &O(C(S^\star)+(m-1)\log(2+m)),
 \Theta(C(S^\star)),O(C(S^\star)),\\
 &\Theta(\card{S^\star}),
 \card{S^\star\setminus\widehat U_1}
 +\card{S^\star}+\Theta(m)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-post-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The $m-2$ adjacency rounds are zero when $m=2$.  The $m$ interactions are
the certified $\mathcal F_1$ batch, the remaining $m-2$ batches, and the
terminal return.  The emission coordinate contains every still-unemitted
positive label once, every exact output coordinate once, one reply for every
remaining batch, and the terminal reply.

All additive and round coordinates sum across the handoff.  Releasing the
constant-size numerical/certificate epoch after the $\mathcal F_1$ response
update gives total persistent and temporary peaks
$\Theta(C(S^\star))$ and $O(C(S^\star))$.  Therefore
\begin{equation}
 W_{\rm total}
 =O\!\left(
   \mathfrak H_1+C(S^\star)\log(2+C(S^\star))
       \right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-total-work}
\end{equation}
The exact margin-dependent expression is authoritative.  It contains
\begin{equation*}
 \log_+\!\left(
 \frac{4\Delta_{1,0}}{\alpha\underline\mu_1^2}
             \right)
 \quad\text{when }\Delta_{1,0}>0,
 \qquad\text{and}\qquad
 L_1^{\rm rel}=2+\frac34\log_+\!\left(
  \frac{144(1-\alpha)}{\delta_{\rm A}(1-2\alpha)}\right)
\end{equation*}
with the full constants fixed in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-oracle-log,eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-stage-cap}.
The second expression retains its explicit
$\log(1/(1-2\alpha))$ upper-end nonuniformity.  Neither factor is hidden by a
claim uniform in the margin or as $\alpha\uparrow1/2$.

This is an exact-real, fixed-family, branch-seeded, fixed-face numerical
gate witness at $k=1$.  Its signed state really supplies the carried
pre-gate coordinate vector in the positive-gap branch, but its one-time
margin certificate was obtained from a separately charged exact
constant-size response.  Hence it is not a speedup over
\texttt{FirstLayer-BC}$_1$, not an automatically exploring accelerated
prefix, and not a graph-uniform locality result.  It proves no claim for
another checkpoint, range, seed, policy, repeated complete-positive-list
interface, finite precision, bit complexity, PPR conversion, or arbitrary
graph.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For $\Delta_{1,0}=0$, strong convexity gives $z^{(0)}=x_1$ and the policy runs
zero stages.  Suppose $\Delta_{1,0}>0$.  By construction,
$T_1(z^{(0)})$ is an integer satisfying the strict stage inequality
\begin{equation*}
 T_1(z^{(0)})>
 \frac{2}{\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}
 \log_+\!\left(
  \frac{4\Delta_{1,0}}
       {\alpha\underline\mu_1^2}
             \right).
\end{equation*}
The imported relative Catalyst estimate and
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-equality} therefore give
\[
 F_{\rho,\widehat U_1}(z^{(T_1)})
 -F_{\rho,\widehat U_1}(x_1)
 <\frac{\alpha\mu_1^2}{2}.
\]
This invokes only the generic strongly convex outer rate on the restricted
objective.  The certified-envelope corollary's separate conclusion that a
restricted solve equals the full optimum is neither assumed nor needed here.
This also covers the case where the logarithm is zero: then $T_1=1$ and the
strict exponential contraction improves the already sufficient
$4\Delta_{1,0}\leq\alpha\mu_1^2$ bound.  Strong convexity and
\Cref{prop:branch-caterpillar-signed-gate-margin} make all three signed
boundary demands strictly positive.  Hence the carried signed state and its
certificate select exactly the actual canonical batch $\mathcal F_1$.

The imported fixed-envelope relative oracle charges at most
$\mathcal V_1L_1^{\rm rel}$ degree work per stage, including every warm start
and repeated row scan.  One adjacency round per charged row scan gives the
$O(\mathfrak A_1)$ additions to the first two coordinates.  Proximal,
gradient, and outer recurrences cost $O(\mathfrak A_1)$ in $C_{\rm rec}$;
heap, queue, strict-gap, signed-demand, interval, and outer-stage logic costs
$O(\mathfrak H_1)$ in $C_{\rm ctl}$.  The exact first-layer settlement and
one-time response readout remain separately visible as $C_{\rm resp}=O(1)$.
Every explicit numerical state read/write and full four-coordinate stage
materialization is covered by $O(\mathfrak D_1)$ in $C_{\rm mat}$.  The
common records, seven scanned rows, numerical face, heap, certificate
transcript, and temporary vectors give the displayed memory coordinates.
There is no preprocessing, no external AESP interaction, and no numerical
emission.  Adding the vectors from
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-prefix-eleven-vector,eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-certificate-vector}
proves
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-prefix-eleven-vector}.

At the second interaction, consume the already scanned $\mathcal F_1$ rows,
emit its three labels once, and pay the exact response build and settlement
on $\widehat U_2$.  Schur associativity gives the native reporter state at
that actual checkpoint; no earlier face is replayed.  The subsequent live
boundary rows partition $S^\star\setminus\widehat U_2$, giving the first two
post coordinates.  Removing the already paid first layer and pre-scanned
second layer from the reviewed canonical reporter ledger gives every other
coordinate in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-post-eleven-vector}.
The stated sum and peak rules now prove
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-total-work}.  Exact terminal
settlement returns $x^\star(\rho)$.
\end{proof}
