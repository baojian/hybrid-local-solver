\subsection{A full-envelope capped comparison witness}
\label{sec:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-prefix}

The preceding corollary cannot be discharged from the phrase ``any finite
valid composite Catalyst burn-in.''  That phrase specifies correctness of
each stage but neither a stopping time nor an execution budget.

\begin{proposition}[Uncapped finite burn-in does not imply a prefix bound]
\label{prop:branch-caterpillar-uncapped-prefix-obstruction}
Fix $m\geq2$, $0<\alpha<1/2$, and
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)$.  There is no upper bound on
$B_J^{\rm full}$, in terms of only $m,\alpha$, and $\rho$, that holds for
every finite valid composite Catalyst burn-in admitted by
\cref{alg:rppr-hybrid}.  This remains true while the common canonical state
is held at $\widehat U_0=\{b_1\}$ and no gate is queried.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Take any reachable valid prefix before the first canonical gate query.  At
each subsequent outer center, solve the strongly convex composite Catalyst
subproblem exactly.  Its exact minimizer satisfies every permitted inner
correctness condition, so the resulting Catalyst stage is valid; repeat this
construction for any prescribed finite number $N$ of stages.  Keep every
numerical or exposed nonseed label outside the common admission arena, so the
canonical checkpoint remains $\widehat U_0$ throughout.  Independently of the
inner solve's other nonnegative charges, each declared stage performs at least
one outer-stage advance and correctness-control operation.  Hence the appended
suffix has $\Delta C_{\rm ctl}=\Omega(N)$ and therefore increases
$B_J^{\rm full}$ by $\Omega(N)$.  Since $N$ is arbitrary, validity plus
finiteness is not a work bound.  This is an algorithm-specification
obstruction, not a lower bound for a capped policy.
\end{proof}

We next give one capped policy for which the missing premise is provable.  It
uses the reviewed certified-envelope AESP--CD result in the project note
\texttt{aesp\_cd\_l1\_rppr}, stable labels
\texttt{thm:aesp-cd-relative-oracle} and
\texttt{cor:aesp-cd-certified-envelope}.  We restate the execution details
needed by the present ledger.

Put
\begin{align}
 C_\star&:=C(S^\star)=9m+1,
 &\mathcal V_\star&:=\vol(S^\star)=6m,
 \notag\\
 \chi_{\rm A}&:=\frac{\alpha}{1-\alpha},
 &\delta_{\rm A}&:=
   \frac{\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}{2-\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}},
 \notag\\
 T_\star&:=\left\lceil
   \frac{2\log 4}{\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}\right\rceil,
 &L_\star&:=2+\frac34\log_+\!\left(
   \frac{16(1-\alpha)^2\mathcal V_\star}
        {\delta_{\rm A}(1-2\alpha)(1-\alpha)}
                         \right),
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-parameters}\\
 \mathfrak D_\star&:=C_\star+T_\star\mathcal V_\star L_\star,
 &\mathfrak H_\star&:=
  \bigl(1+\log(2+C_\star)\bigr)\mathfrak D_\star.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-budgets}
\end{align}
Here $\log_+(z):=\max\{0,\log z\}$.  The quantities are finite precisely in
the present composite range $0<\alpha<1/2$.

\begin{definition}[Capped full-envelope policy]
\label{def:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-zero-policy}
The policy \texttt{Full-BC-AESP}$_0$ acts on the promised
$\mathsf{BC}_m$ instance as follows.
\begin{enumerate}
 \item Read the sparse seed and make one paid outward traversal from $b_1$.
       Verify the fixed-family incidences, expose and index every vertex and
       row of $U:=V(\mathsf{BC}_m)$, and store its labels and degrees.  Do not
       treat the first returned incidences as a free row cache: every later
       AESP row scan is queried and charged again.  In the common
       canonical arena retain only the paid checkpoint record
       $\widehat U_0=\{b_1\}$.  All other exposed labels belong to a
       distinguishable numerical-envelope arena and are not admissions.
 \item From zero, run exactly $T_\star$ strongly convex composite Catalyst/AESP
       stages on the restricted objective over $U$, with
       $\kappa_{\rm A}=1-2\alpha$ and
       $\beta_{\rm A}=(\sqrt{1-\alpha}-\sqrt\alpha)/
       (\sqrt{1-\alpha}+\sqrt\alpha)$.  At each center $y$, materialize the
       proximal warm start
       \[
        u_U(y)=\prox_{g_{\rho,U}/[2(1-\alpha)]}\!\left(
          y-\frac{\nabla f_{t,U}(y)}{2(1-\alpha)}\right),
       \]
       and repeatedly update an exact maximizer of
       $|\kappa_{t,U,i}(z)|/\sqrt{d_i}$.  Rekey the updated row and its
       neighbors until the locally maintained
       relative certificate
       \[
        C_{t,U}(z)^2\leq
        \delta_{\rm A}(1-2\alpha)(1-\alpha)\norm{z-y}_2^2
       \]
       holds.  Here $C_{t,U}$ is the degree-weighted minimum shifted KKT mass.
       Maintain both sides of this test incrementally; no uncharged global
       certificate scan is permitted.
 \item Invoke no canonical gate, restricted settlement, Schur response, or
       external reply.  After stage $T_\star$, retire the numerical envelope,
       iterates, momentum, heap, and certificates by one epoch pointer, retain
       only the common seed checkpoint, and hand off before the first canonical
       gate query.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Fully charged capped AESP prefix on the caterpillar]
\label{thm:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-prefix}
Fix $m\geq2$, $0<\alpha<1/2$, and
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)$.  The execution in
\Cref{def:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-zero-policy} is a
canonical-checkpoint prefix in the sense of
\Cref{def:branch-caterpillar-canonical-checkpoint-prefix}, with $k=0$.  In the
shared exhaustive order it obeys
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm env}^{\rm pref})
 =\bigl(&O(\mathfrak D_\star),O(\mathfrak D_\star),0,O(C_\star),
 O(\mathfrak H_\star),O(\mathfrak D_\star),0,\\
 &O(C_\star),O(C_\star),O(\mathfrak D_\star),0\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-prefix-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Consequently,
\begin{equation}
 B_{\rm env}^{\rm full}
 =O(\mathfrak H_\star)
 =\softO\!\left(\frac{C(S^\star)}{\sqrt\alpha}\right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-prefix-work}
\end{equation}
The soft order hides only the displayed logarithms in $2+C_\star$,
$1/\alpha$, and $1/(1-2\alpha)$.  It is pointwise over
$0<\alpha<1/2$, not uniform as $\alpha\uparrow1/2$; the exact multivariate
bound $O(\mathfrak H_\star)$ is authoritative.  The numerical endpoint
additionally obeys
\begin{equation}
 F_\rho(x^{(T_\star)})-F_\rho(x^\star)
 \leq\frac12\bigl(F_\rho(0)-F_\rho(x^\star)\bigr),
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-half-gap}
\end{equation}
but the handoff neither uses nor transports this progress.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The imported strict-range canonical theorem gives
$S^\star=V(\mathsf{BC}_m)$, $\mathcal V_\star=6m$, and $C_\star=9m+1$.
Therefore the initial traversal constructs a certified envelope without an
optimal-support oracle.  It is nevertheless a full final-support exposure:
every returned adjacency incidence is charged in $C_{\rm adj}$, graph-family
validation and index construction before the online AESP continuation are
charged in $C_{\rm pre}$, seed reads and traversal scheduling are charged in
$C_{\rm ctl}$, label/degree index writes are charged in $C_{\rm pre}$ and the
memory peaks, and the explicit common seed checkpoint is charged in
$C_{\rm mat}$.

For one relative-accuracy stage, the reviewed local-oracle theorem bounds the
sum of degrees of all warm-start and repeated coordinate-row scans by
$\mathcal V_\star L_\star$; its PageRank contraction parameter is exactly
$2/3$, which gives the factor $3/4$ in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-parameters}.  Repeated reads of an
old row are counted again.  One adjacency-query round per row scan gives
$R_{\rm adj}=O(\mathfrak D_\star)$.  Exact proximal-coordinate arithmetic,
neighbor-gradient arithmetic, and the outer momentum recurrence cost
$O(\mathfrak D_\star)$ and are assigned to $C_{\rm rec}$.
Heap, queue, stopping-test, family, and outer-stage control cost
$O(\mathfrak H_\star)$ after charging a logarithm per touched key.  The
proximal warm start, stage iterate, center, momentum, and checkpoint vectors
are each materialized at most a constant number of times per stage, so
$C_{\rm mat}=O(C_\star+T_\star\card{S^\star})
 =O(\mathfrak D_\star)$.  Every explicit iterate and gradient state-cell read
or write made by a coordinate update is separately charged within this same
$O(\mathfrak D_\star)$ materialization bound; recurrence arithmetic
remains in $C_{\rm rec}$ and heap/key state and logic in $C_{\rm ctl}$.  All
exact KKT mass and displacement norms are updated with those charged local
changes.

There is no Phase-I response primitive or row settlement, so $C_{\rm resp}=0$;
there is no canonical gate or external stage, so $R_{\rm int}=C_{\rm emit}=0$.
The fixed envelope, a constant number of full vectors, their KKT keys, and the
heap require $O(C_\star)$ persistent and temporary cells.  This accounts for
every coordinate in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-prefix-eleven-vector}, including
all excess exposure (there is none beyond $S^\star$), every internal
certificate, and both memory peaks.  Summing the seven additive coordinates
gives the first equality in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-prefix-work}.  Since
$T_\star=O(1/\sqrt\alpha)$ and $C_\star=\Theta(\mathcal V_\star)$, the second
follows.

Every exposed nonseed label remains in the numerical arena, so the common
canonical state is still exactly $\widehat U_0$ and no earlier face exists to
replay.  Thus the policy meets the existing checkpoint interface.  Finally,
the reviewed relative Catalyst estimate is
\[
 F_\rho(x^{(T)})-F_\rho(x^\star)
 \leq2\left(1-\frac{\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}{2}\right)^T
       \bigl(F_\rho(0)-F_\rho(x^\star)\bigr).
\]
The definition of $T_\star$ and $1-u\leq e^{-u}$ prove
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-half-gap}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Product work for the named capped policy]
\label{cor:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-product}
Run \texttt{Full-BC-AESP}$_0$ and then the response in
\Cref{thm:branch-caterpillar-composite-response-handoff} at $k=0$.  The
concatenated exact-real execution returns $x^\star(\rho)$ and has
\begin{equation}
 W_{\rm total}
 =O\!\left(\mathfrak H_\star
      +C_\star\log(2+C_\star)\right)
 =\softO\!\left(\frac{C(S^\star)}{\sqrt\alpha}\right)
 =\softO\!\left(\frac1{\rho\sqrt\alpha}\right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-total-work}
\end{equation}
This soft-order conclusion has the same
$\log(1/(1-2\alpha))$ nonuniformity as
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-prefix-work}; it is not uniform as
$\alpha\uparrow1/2$.
Its additive and round coordinates sum exactly at the existing interface,
$R_{\rm int}^{\rm total}=m+1$, and releasing the numerical prefix arena gives
total persistent and temporary peaks $O(C(S^\star))$.  This is a
fixed-family, full-envelope, zero-checkpoint witness.  It proves no bound for
an uncapped or automatically exploring Catalyst/AESP prefix, commits no
Phase-I canonical batch, uses no numerical-energy carryover, and supplies no
claim for another seed, policy, range, graph, precision model, or PPR
accuracy.
\end{corollary}
