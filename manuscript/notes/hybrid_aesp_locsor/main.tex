\begin{abstract}
This note formalizes a hybrid local method suggested by two complementary
lines of work on personalized PageRank. The locally evolving-set framework
shows how Gauss--Seidel/SOR, gradient descent, Chebyshev, and heavy-ball
iterations can be localized, but its accelerated variants face
momentum-localization errors. The AESP framework instead places Catalyst
acceleration outside short monotone local solves and exhibits particularly
fast early-stage convergence. The resulting design principle is to use AESP
as an early residual/objective compressor and then reset momentum and finish
with a direct local method.

For unregularized PageRank, we prove finite convergence from every signed
handoff, objective-gap and weighted-residual-mass tail bounds, and a master
handoff inequality. Combining the direct AESP amortization with the SOR tail
gives an unconditional instance-wise
$\bigO(R/(\alpha^{3/4}\epsppr))$ inner-plus-tail bound, parameterized by the
run-dependent AESP ratio $R$. A sharper trajectory analysis yields
$\softO(1/(\sqrt\alpha\epsppr))$ under an early-locality condition; explicit
support-envelope and no-percolation conditions are given, but the premise is
not known for every graph. A KKT-slack charging lemma provides a third,
boundary-sensitive proof route.

For $\ell_1$-regularized PageRank, Catalyst is naturally compatible with the
weighted soft-thresholding term. We prove that the unit-step proximal map is a
$(1-\alpha)$ contraction in degree-weighted $\ell_\infty$ and $\ell_1$ norms,
and that its fixed-point residual divided by $\alpha$ certifies solution
error. Thus a finite composite Catalyst burn-in followed by momentum-free
ISTA is unconditionally convergent. On the center-seeded three-arm spider, a
stronger structural handoff is fully charged: retire the signed numerical
state, build the exact affine response on the actual irreversibly admitted
prefixes in one common-state pass, and finish with incremental work
$\bigO(C(S^\star(\rho)))=\bigO(1/\rho)$. Thus every fully charged finite
gate-compatible Phase-I prefix of cost $B_J^{\rm full}$ gives total work
$B_J^{\rm full}+\bigO(C(S^\star(\rho)))$ on this family. This includes the note's
composite Catalyst/AESP construction in its stated range $\alpha<1/2$. The
same paid conversion extends to the branch-seeded unweighted double-Y: the
four actual pendant prefixes determine a scalar core before the second branch
is admitted and a positive-definite rank-two Schur core afterward, so no
private Phase-I order is replayed.  Its total is likewise only
$B_J^{\rm full}+\bigO(C(S^\star(\rho)))$; product scale remains conditional on
an independent bound for the fully charged prefix.  On the fixed-$m$
branch-seeded caterpillar with $m\geq2$, $\alpha\in(0,1)$, and the strict canonical range
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)$, a fully charged Phase-I prefix ending at
any actual canonical checkpoint also has a paid support-only handoff.  One
direct traversal builds the current arm absorptions, tridiagonal branch cells,
and balanced transfer tree without replaying the preceding layers; the
remaining canonical response costs
$\bigO(C(S^\star)\log(2+C(S^\star)))$.  The unconditional total is only this
post term plus $B_J^{\rm full}$.  A responder-native local policy now supplies
an actual nonzero checkpoint without exposing the promised full support: it
scans only the seed and the first live boundary, commits the first canonical
three-label layer, retains the exact constant-size response at
$\widehat U_1$, and resumes the canonical reporter from that state.  Its
prefix and suffix have complete eleven-coordinate ledgers and its exact total
is $O(C(S^\star)\log(2+C(S^\star)))$, hence is pointwise
$\softO(C(S^\star)/\sqrt\alpha)$ with no
$\log(1/(1-2\alpha))$ singularity.  This is a response-native RPPR execution,
not an accelerated-burn-in theorem.  The older full-envelope, zero-checkpoint
AESP construction remains a separately charged comparison witness: it runs a
fixed $O(1/\sqrt\alpha)$ number of relative-accuracy stages after full support
exposure and has logarithmic nonuniformity as $\alpha\uparrow1/2$.  An exact
margin lemma explains why its signed relative-accuracy state cannot simply be
declared a canonical gate state: a parent-coordinate error must be smaller
than the minimum exact demand-to-coupling ratio, adding a margin-dependent
accuracy logarithm unless exact settlement or a new one-sided certificate is
paid.  At the actual first-layer checkpoint, that ratio is exceptionally
local: one paid scan of the three live boundary rows gives the exact sharp
$\mu_1$.  The capped \texttt{MarginCert-BC-AESP}$_1$ witness then carries a
genuine signed AESP endpoint into the next certified gate, with complete
prefix/post ledgers and both the margin logarithm and per-stage
$\log(1/(1-2\alpha))$ factor explicit.  Its separately charged exact
constant-size margin response makes it a comparison witness, not an
acceleration speedup.  The remaining open problem is a
graph-independent local-work bound
from an arbitrary accelerated warm start, not correctness of the hybrid
itself.
The response dependence at the first layer is not intrinsic to sign
certification.  A local lower retraction of any signed trial point produces a
nonnegative subsolution below the exact restricted point.  Three positive
boundary demands at that lower point therefore certify the exact canonical
batch without computing any coordinate of the restricted response, its
optimum value, or $\mu_1$.  This strictly removes the response coordinate from
the incremental gate certificate.  An adaptive two-face witness can use the
same test to commit the first layer without exact settlement and then certify
the second layer, but exact validation of the candidate degrees forces their
rows to be exposed before each interaction.  Thus its
pre-second-interaction prefix is response-free, not row-unexposed; the post
phase builds and charges the native response on $\widehat U_2$.  Its observed
stopping times retain the margin and $\log(1/(1-2\alpha))$ dependence.  It
remains a comparison witness rather than a speedup.
The lower point itself can nevertheless cross every canonical admission
without a whole-vector zero reset.  Principal Stieltjes face monotonicity
shows that zero-padding a certified lower point remains below the next
restricted solution.  Taking the coordinatewise maximum of this transported
anchor and each new local lower retraction preserves every old lower
coordinate and still yields conservative boundary demands.  The named
\texttt{TransportLower-BC-AESP}$_{0:q-1}$ policy makes this invariant
operational for every fixed $2\leq q\leq m$, with a complete response-free
prefix ledger and a separately charged native response suffix.  It restarts
all AESP auxiliary state and pays a full old-face sweep at every sign test, so
it transports neither estimate-sequence energy nor a speedup.
The repeated diagnostic sweep is not intrinsic.  The exact
\texttt{ImplicitLowerHeap} stores the raw lower residual and a max-heap for
its normalized negative part; a local coordinate write rekeys only its closed
neighborhood, while a complete caterpillar boundary query reads one heap
maximum and three cached parent coordinates.  The resulting
\texttt{ImplicitLowerHeap-BC-AESP}$_{0:q-1}$ prefix has complete update,
exposure, validation, prefix, and suffix ledgers and performs no old-face
sweep at a sign query.  It still explicitly materializes the transported
anchor, initializes a fresh raw product, and restarts every AESP auxiliary on
each enlarged face.  Those admission/restart terms are quadratic when
$q=m$, so this is an exact diagnostic improvement rather than a speedup or
accelerated-energy transfer.
The raw-product and anchor-copy parts of that shock are also
representation-specific.  The named
\texttt{FaceCarry}\allowbreak\texttt{LowerHeap-}\allowbreak
\texttt{BC-AESP}$_{0:q}$ policy
zero-pads the successful signed numerical endpoint, so every old residual
and heap key survives literally and only the three new keys are inserted.
Successful lower points are represented by append-only range-minimum tags and
per-coordinate flush-before-write markers, avoiding every eager old-anchor
copy.  This exact transition works through the full-support expansion and has
complete prefix/post ledgers.  It still restarts explicitly dense AESP
auxiliaries on every numerical face; their declared interface writes
$q(3q-1)/2$ coordinate records.  Thus the remaining restart term is
quadratic at $q=m$, and the construction is neither a speedup nor a scalar
energy/potential transport theorem.
At a settled canonical checkpoint, however, the dense fresh-array restart is
not forced by the vector algebra.  The old center, zero momentum, and
estimate-point coordinates may be retained and zero-extended.  The standard
composite proximal warm start has exactly three mandatory positive new
entries, one for each strict canonical demand, and no old entry changes.  One
explicit record of each named auxiliary therefore needs only twelve new
coordinate writes.  The analytical estimate certificate does not carry with
zero shock: its old value is zero, whereas the enlarged-face value is
strictly positive.  Thus a valid transition must reset that certificate or
explicitly account for the positive face shock.  A concrete first-layer
audit gives complete prefix/post ledgers, but it begins from an exact native
response and proves no accelerated speedup or class lower bound.
\end{abstract}

\section{A fully charged composite handoff on the three-arm spider}
\label{sec:three-arm-composite-handoff}

This section composes a finite gate-compatible Phase-I prefix with the
proved structural response theorem in the adaptive-revisit-control project
note (stable label \texttt{thm:three-arm-spider-}\allowbreak
\texttt{token-countdown}). We use that theorem with all of its restrictions:
a center-seeded unweighted three-arm spider,
$\alpha\in(0,1)$, $\rho\in(0,1/3)$, the exact canonical RPPR boundary gate,
and exact real-cell arithmetic. We do not reprove its affine countdown.

\begin{definition}[Fully charged gate-compatible prefix]
\label{def:three-arm-gate-compatible-prefix}
Let a finite Phase-I prefix have numerical output $x_J$, momentum state, and
fully charged work $B_J^{\rm full}$. This includes the composite Catalyst/AESP
construction of this note when its parameters are valid. The charge includes
seed and input reads, every adjacency exposure (including excess exposure),
repeated old-row reads, inner recurrence work, outer and queue control, gate
queries and certificate verification, exact restricted canonical settlement
or Schur-response updates, any checkpoint materialization or recovery, and all
state reads and writes. Let $M_J^{\rm pers}$ and $M_J^{\rm scr}$ be its
separately reported peak persistent and scratch cell counts, including the
exact gate state.
The superscript distinguishes this all-resource ledger from the inner-only
$B_J$ in \cref{eq:BJ}; when $B_J$ already denotes the fully charged prefix,
set $B_J=B_J^{\rm full}$ below.

The prefix is \emph{gate-compatible} if it also maintains one common
irreversible admission set
\begin{equation}
 U_J=\{o\}\cup
 \bigcup_{a=1}^3\{v_{a,1},\ldots,v_{a,k_a}\},
 \qquad 0\leq k_a\leq N_a,
 \label{eq:three-arm-handoff-prefixes}
\end{equation}
with the three prefix lengths (and any admitted-label log) stored as paid
common records. Every committed batch was a nonempty subset of the positive
tips at the
exact canonical RPPR boundary gate, whose common gate state was canonically
settled after that batch. Its certificate was checked and charged when
committed. Signed trial coordinates, momentum coordinates, and merely
exposed labels outside $U_J$ are not admissions. A prefix that never invokes
the gate is covered by taking $U_J=\{o\}$.
\end{definition}

The distinction in the last sentence is essential. Numerical support may be
nonnested and signed; the certified admission set is the only irreversible
support state passed to the response phase.

\begin{theorem}[Common-state Phase-I-to-affine-response handoff]
\label{thm:three-arm-composite-response-handoff}
Let $Y(N_1,N_2,N_3)$ be the center-seeded unweighted three-arm spider. Fix
$\alpha\in(0,1)$ and $\rho\in(0,1/3)$, and let any finite Phase-I prefix
satisfy \Cref{def:three-arm-gate-compatible-prefix}. For the particular
composite Catalyst/AESP construction in \cref{sec:composite-hybrid}, also
assume its stated range $\alpha\in(0,1/2)$. After discarding all numerical
momentum, there is a deterministic common-state conversion followed by the
exact affine activation-token response that returns $x^\star(\rho)$ and obeys
the following bound, where
$C(S):=\sum_{v\in S}(1+d_v)$:
\begin{align}
 W_{\rm post}
 &\leq c\,C(S^\star(\rho)),
 \notag\\
 W_{\rm total}
 &\leq B_J^{\rm full}+c\,C(S^\star(\rho))
 \leq B_J^{\rm full}+\frac{2c}{\rho},
 \label{eq:three-arm-composite-work}
\end{align}
for an absolute constant $c$ under a fixed exact-cell operation convention.
The post-handoff resource coordinates satisfy
\begin{align}
 \mathcal C_{\rm adj}^{\rm post}
   &\leq 2\vol(S^\star)+O(1),
 \notag\\
 \mathcal C_{\rm conv}^{\rm post}
   +\mathcal C_{\rm resp}^{\rm post}
   +\mathcal C_{\rm ctl}^{\rm post}
   &=O(C(S^\star)),
 \notag\\
 \mathcal C_{\rm set/rec}^{\rm post}
   +\mathcal C_{\rm val}^{\rm post}
   &=O(C(S^\star)),
 \notag\\
 \mathcal C_{\rm state\text{-}write}^{\rm post}
   &=O(C(S^\star)),
 \notag\\
 \mathcal M_{\rm persistent}^{\rm post}
   &=O(C(S^\star)),
 \notag\\
 \mathcal M_{\rm scratch}^{\rm post}
   &=O(1),
 \notag\\
 \mathcal C_{\rm out}^{\rm post}
   &=\card{S^\star}.
 \label{eq:three-arm-composite-resources}
\end{align}
If the retired prefix arena remains resident, total peak persistent state is
$M_J^{\rm pers}+O(C(S^\star))$; an arena/epoch pointer may instead release it
without enumerating its cells. Across the two sequential phases, total peak
scratch is $\max\{M_J^{\rm scr},O(1)\}$. There is no ambient-graph
preprocessing.

The terminal vector is exact and therefore also satisfies
\begin{equation}
 R_{\mathrm{fp},\alpha,\rho}(x^\star(\rho))=0.
 \label{eq:three-arm-terminal-proximal}
\end{equation}
Thus it meets every positive note-scoped proximal residual tolerance. The
claim has no RPPR-to-PPR accuracy conversion and no finite-precision or bit
complexity consequence. It is not a graph-uniform accelerated solver.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Gate compatibility and the absorption-safe support theorem imply
$U_J\subseteq S^\star(\rho)$. The set in
\cref{eq:three-arm-handoff-prefixes} records the actual common prefixes, not a
private policy trajectory. At the switch, change one phase/epoch flag so that
$x_J$, its momentum, queues, and nonadmitted caches can never again influence
the solver. No cell-by-cell clearing or scan of excess exposure is required;
its work and residence were already recorded in $B_J^{\rm full}$,
$M_J^{\rm pers}$, and $M_J^{\rm scr}$.

Make one conversion pass over the common admitted set. Starting at the center,
walk each actual arm to its stored prefix length; no membership search,
sorting, or historical admission order is needed. Read a
compatible cached row or scan each visited vertex once, construct the missing
local transfer cell and its prefix product, and retain the resulting affine
record. Then form the two root aggregates and evaluate the three current tip
slots. This costs $O(1+d_v)$ for each $v\in U_J$, plus constant work for at
most one exterior tip on each arm. It writes $O(\card{U_J})$ cells and uses
constant transient scratch. The resulting state is exactly the common affine
state of the cited three-arm countdown theorem at $U_J$.

This pass does not replay the Catalyst iterates or any historical batch order.
It constructs a canonical response from the actual committed set. In
particular, if two or three symmetric tips were certified in one batch, they
remain one common batch. The fixed-size records may be written in any order
inside that batch because exact violation persistence keeps every unprocessed
tip legal and the final prefix products depend only on the committed prefix
lengths.

Starting from this reachable common state, the suffix of the proved
activation-token response admits only new positive tips, appends each new
record once, and queries all three tips after every expansion. At terminality
it performs one canonical recovery and writes every coordinate of
$x^\star(\rho)$ once. A simultaneous exact KKT/proximal validation uses the
stored rows and at most the three terminal exterior tips; allowing a fresh
validation traversal gives the conservative adjacency bound in
\cref{eq:three-arm-composite-resources}. Thus conversion, remaining response,
control, state, validation, recovery, and output are all
$O(C(S^\star))$.

The prefix and response phases never delete an admitted vertex. Conversely,
the numerical Catalyst state is discarded rather than transported, so this
argument retains no estimate-sequence energy and needs no shock-free
carryover. Retaining any part of that numerical energy would require an
explicit expansion/transport shock and is outside the theorem.

Finally, $C(S^\star)\leq2\vol(S^\star)\leq2/\rho$ in the shared RPPR model.
This proves \cref{eq:three-arm-composite-work}. Exact optimality gives the
fixed-point identity in \cref{eq:three-arm-terminal-proximal}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Product work only from an independent prefix bound]
\label{cor:three-arm-composite-product-conditional}
Under \Cref{thm:three-arm-composite-response-handoff}, if a separate theorem
proves the fully charged prefix bound
\begin{equation}
 B_J^{\rm full}=\softO\!\left(
   \frac{C(S^\star(\rho))}{\sqrt\alpha}
 \right),
 \label{eq:three-arm-independent-prefix-bound}
\end{equation}
then
\begin{equation}
 W_{\rm total}
 =\softO\!\left(
   \frac{C(S^\star(\rho))}{\sqrt\alpha}
 \right)
 =\softO\!\left(\frac1{\rho\sqrt\alpha}\right).
 \label{eq:three-arm-conditional-product-work}
\end{equation}
Without \cref{eq:three-arm-independent-prefix-bound}, the proved statement is
only $B_J^{\rm full}+O(C(S^\star))$. In particular, the theorem does not use
raw correction counts or the refuted Euclidean-log packing of safeguarded
AESP collateral clipping.
\end{corollary}

\section{A fully charged composite handoff on the double-Y}
\label{sec:double-y-composite-handoff}

We next compose the same kind of paid Phase-I prefix with the reviewed
double-Y response in the adaptive-revisit-control note (stable label
\texttt{thm:double-y-two-core-token-countdown}).  Its graph is the unweighted
tree $\mathsf{DY}(N_{o,1},N_{o,2};N_{h,1},N_{h,2})$ with adjacent
degree-three branch vertices $o,h$ and two nonempty pendant paths at each
branch.  Throughout this section the single seed is $s=e_o$,
$\alpha\in(0,1)$, $\rho\in(0,1/3)$, the boundary gate and restricted
settlement are the exact canonical RPPR ones, and arithmetic uses exact real
cells.  These are precisely the hypotheses of the imported response theorem;
we do not extrapolate it to another seed or a growing branch core.

\begin{definition}[Fully charged double-Y gate-compatible prefix]
\label{def:double-y-gate-compatible-prefix}
Let a finite Phase-I prefix execution $\mathcal E_J$ have numerical output
$x_J$ and signed momentum or other numerical state.  In the shared exhaustive
order, report
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_J):=\bigl(&
 C_{\rm adj}^J,R_{\rm adj}^J,R_{\rm int}^J,C_{\rm pre}^J,C_{\rm ctl}^J,
 C_{\rm rec}^J,C_{\rm resp}^J,\\
 &M_{\rm pers}^J,M_{\rm tmp}^J,C_{\rm mat}^J,C_{\rm emit}^J\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:double-y-composite-prefix-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Under the fixed exact-cell operation convention, define its fully charged
additive work by
\begin{equation}
 B_J^{\rm full}:=
 C_{\rm adj}^J+C_{\rm pre}^J+C_{\rm ctl}^J+C_{\rm rec}^J
 +C_{\rm resp}^J+C_{\rm mat}^J+C_{\rm emit}^J.
 \label{eq:double-y-composite-prefix-full-work}
\end{equation}
This sum includes seed and input reads, adjacency and excess exposure, every
repeated old-row read, inner recurrence work, outer and queue control,
membership and gate queries, certificate computation and emitted replies,
exact restricted settlement and Schur work, checkpoint materialization or
recovery, and all state reads and writes, assigned to the additive coordinate
matching their purpose.  The round counts $R_{\rm adj}^J,R_{\rm int}^J$ and
the peaks
$M_J^{\rm pers}:=M_{\rm pers}^J$, $M_J^{\rm scr}:=M_{\rm tmp}^J$ remain
separate and are not added to $B_J^{\rm full}$.  The memory peaks include the
paid common gate records below; numerical and common records occupy
distinguishable arenas or carry distinguishable epoch tags.

The prefix is \emph{gate-compatible} if it stores an active-branch bit
$\chi_J^h\in\{0,1\}$ and the actual label/length record for each committed
pendant prefix.  Put
\begin{equation}
 H_J:=
 \begin{cases}
  \varnothing,&\chi_J^h=0,\\
  \{h\},&\chi_J^h=1,
 \end{cases}
 \qquad
 U_J:=\{o\}\cup H_J\cup
 \bigcup_{u\in\{o,h\}}\bigcup_{a=1}^2
 \{v_{u,a,1},\ldots,v_{u,a,k_{u,a}}\},
 \label{eq:double-y-composite-prefix-state}
\end{equation}
where $0\leq k_{u,a}\leq N_{u,a}$ and $k_{h,a}=0$ when $\chi_J^h=0$.
Every committed expansion batch was a nonempty subset of the positive tips
of this common set at the exact canonical boundary gate.  The restricted
state was canonically settled and the gate certificate was checked, stored,
and charged after each batch.  Signed trial coordinates, momentum
coordinates, and exposed but uncommitted labels are not in $U_J$.  If Phase I
never commits an expansion, take $U_J=\{o\}$ with the paid seed record.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Paid double-Y Phase-I-to-rank-two-response handoff]
\label{thm:double-y-composite-response-handoff}
On the branch-seeded double-Y just defined, fix any
$\alpha\in(0,1)$ and $\rho\in(0,1/3)$ and any finite Phase-I prefix satisfying
\Cref{def:double-y-gate-compatible-prefix}.  Write
$S^\star:=S^\star(\rho)$ and
$C(S):=\sum_{v\in S}(1+d_v)$.  There is one deterministic paid
common-state conversion which retires all signed numerical and momentum
state, constructs the exact affine pendant records and scalar/rank-two core
state determined by $U_J$, and then invokes the exact reviewed response.  It
does not replay the Phase-I iterates or their batch order.  For an absolute
constant $c$ in a fixed exact-cell convention,
\begin{align}
 W_{\rm post}
 &\leq c\,C(S^\star(\rho)),
 \notag\\
 W_{\rm total}
 &\leq B_J^{\rm full}+c\,C(S^\star(\rho))
 \leq B_J^{\rm full}+\frac{2c}{\rho}.
 \label{eq:double-y-composite-work}
\end{align}
Let $J_{\rm post}$ be the actual number of nonempty committed
support-expansion batches after the handoff.  Irreversibility gives
$0\leq J_{\rm post}\leq\card{S^\star\setminus U_J}$.
The following detailed post-handoff subledger records the work by operation:
\begin{align}
 \mathcal C_{\rm adj}^{\rm post}
 &\leq2\vol(S^\star)+O(1),
 &
 \mathcal C_{\rm conv\text{-}ctl}^{\rm post}
 &=O(C(S^\star)),
 \notag\\
 \mathcal C_{\rm affine\text{-}build}^{\rm post}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 &
 \mathcal C_{\rm core\text{-}build}^{\rm post}
  +\mathcal C_{\rm core\text{-}upd}^{\rm post}
  +\mathcal C_{\rm core\text{-}qry}^{\rm post}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 \notag\\
 \mathcal C_{\rm gate\text{-}ctl}^{\rm post}
  +\mathcal C_{\rm state\text{-}write}^{\rm post}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 &
 \mathcal C_{\rm recovery}^{\rm post}
  +\mathcal C_{\rm val}^{\rm post}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 \label{eq:double-y-composite-resources}\\
 \mathcal M_{\rm persistent}^{\rm post}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 &
 \mathcal M_{\rm scratch}^{\rm post}
 &=O(1),
 \notag\\
 \mathcal C_{\rm term\text{-}mat}^{\rm post}
 &=O(\card{S^\star}),
 &
 \mathcal C_{\rm cert\text{-}reply}^{\rm post}
 &=\Theta(J_{\rm post}+1),
 \notag\\
 \mathcal C_{\rm out}^{\rm post}
 &=\card{S^\star}.
 \notag
\end{align}
Here conversion control charges its traversal and index/membership work
separately from row exposure and from affine/core response construction.  Core
update/query includes the exact canonical Schur settlement after each later
committed batch.  The terminal materialization coordinate charges one stored
exact cell per recovered support coordinate before the separate output pass.

For direct comparison with the shared oracle-hierarchy ledger, in the
exhaustive shared order
\begin{equation*}
 (C_{\rm adj},R_{\rm adj},R_{\rm int},C_{\rm pre},C_{\rm ctl},C_{\rm rec},
 C_{\rm resp},M_{\rm pers},M_{\rm tmp},C_{\rm mat},C_{\rm emit}),
\end{equation*}
the deliberately sequential post-handoff implementation obeys
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm post})
 =\bigl(&O(\vol(S^\star)),O(\card{S^\star}),J_{\rm post}+1,0,
 O(C(S^\star)),0,\\
 &O(C(S^\star)),O(C(S^\star)),O(1),
 O(\card{S^\star}),
 \card{S^\star}+\Theta(J_{\rm post}+1)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:double-y-composite-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The $J_{\rm post}+1$ interaction stages are the gate/certificate replies at
the successive post-conversion common states, including the terminal reply.
Each emits a constant-size certificate reply, while the final output pass
emits exactly $\card{S^\star}$ labeled solution cells.  Thus
$C_{\rm emit}^{\rm post}
=\card{S^\star}+\Theta(J_{\rm post}+1)=O(\card{S^\star})$; certificate
computation remains in $C_{\rm ctl}$.
The sequential row implementation uses at most
$2\card{S^\star}+O(1)$ adjacency-query batches; batching already known labels
can only reduce $R_{\rm adj}$.  Conversion traversal, membership, gate,
validation, and scheduling arithmetic map to $C_{\rm ctl}$.  Affine-record and
core construction/update/query, exact settlement, and terminal recovery map
to $C_{\rm resp}$; response-record writes and control-bit writes are charged
to their respective coordinates.  No row-recurrence direction is generated
after the handoff, so the shared $C_{\rm rec}$ is zero.  The detailed
subledger in \cref{eq:double-y-composite-resources} refines, rather than
replaces, this exhaustive vector.
For $\mathcal E_{\rm total}=\mathcal E_J\circ\mathcal E_{\rm post}$, every
additive work coordinate and both round coordinates compose by summation:
\begin{align*}
 C_\xi^{\rm total}&=C_\xi^J+C_\xi^{\rm post}
 &&(\xi\in\{\mathrm{adj},\mathrm{pre},\mathrm{ctl},\mathrm{rec},
              \mathrm{resp},\mathrm{mat},\mathrm{emit}\}),\\
 R_\xi^{\rm total}&=R_\xi^J+R_\xi^{\rm post}
 &&(\xi\in\{\mathrm{adj},\mathrm{int}\}).
\end{align*}
The sequential phases instead compose peak memory as
\begin{equation*}
 M_{\rm pers}^{\rm total}\leq
 \begin{cases}
  M_J^{\rm pers}+O(C(S^\star)),&\text{retired prefix arena retained},\\
  \max\{M_J^{\rm pers},O(C(S^\star))\},&\text{arena released by its pointer},
 \end{cases}
 \qquad
 M_{\rm tmp}^{\rm total}=\max\{M_J^{\rm scr},O(1)\}.
\end{equation*}
There is no post-handoff ambient-graph preprocessing.

Before $h$ is admitted the converted core is scalar.  After it is admitted
the converted core is positive definite of rank two.  With
\begin{equation}
 \zeta:=\frac{1-\alpha}{1+\alpha},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\rm link}(\alpha):=\frac{\zeta}{3(3+\zeta)},
 \label{eq:double-y-composite-link-threshold}
\end{equation}
every legal order eventually enters the rank-two phase when
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm link}(\alpha)$.  For larger $\rho<1/3$ the theorem and
ledger remain valid, but the terminal response may stay scalar if
$h\notin S^\star$.

The returned vector is the exact RPPR minimizer and hence
\begin{equation}
 R_{\mathrm{fp},\alpha,\rho}(x^\star(\rho))=0.
 \label{eq:double-y-composite-terminal-proximal}
\end{equation}
For the particular composite Catalyst/AESP Phase I of
\cref{sec:composite-hybrid}, separately require its stated
$\alpha\in(0,1/2)$ range.  The structural handoff itself needs no such
restriction.  The theorem retains no accelerated energy, gives no
RPPR-to-PPR conversion, and makes no growing-core, finite-precision, or bit
complexity claim.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Exact gate compatibility and absorption safety give
$U_J\subseteq S^\star(\rho)$.  Connectivity of the double-Y then forces
exactly the shape in \cref{eq:double-y-composite-prefix-state}: before $h$ is
committed there are two actual $o$-arm prefixes, and afterward there are four
actual pendant prefixes.  This is a statement about the irreversible common
gate state, not the possibly signed or nonnested numerical support.

Flip one phase/epoch pointer so that $x_J$, momentum, queues, and uncommitted
caches cannot be read by the response phase.  Their construction, residence,
and any excess exposure are already paid by $B_J^{\rm full}$,
$M_J^{\rm pers}$, and $M_J^{\rm scr}$; retirement therefore requires no
cell-by-cell clearing.  Preserve only the common bit and actual prefix
label/length records from \cref{eq:double-y-composite-prefix-state}.

Now make one outward pass over those records.  For every admitted pendant
vertex, read a compatible cached row or scan that row once, form its constant
affine elimination cell, and append it to the product for its own actual arm
prefix.  Thus, for each nonempty prefix, obtain exact readouts
\begin{equation*}
 x_{v_{u,a,1}}=p_{u,a,k}x_u+q_{u,a,k},
 \qquad u\in\{o,h\},\quad a\in\{1,2\}.
\end{equation*}
Let $\lambda_v:=b_v-\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_v}$,
$\beta_0:=-Q_{oh}>0$, and
$\beta_{u,a,1}:=-Q_{u,v_{u,a,1}}>0$.  The same pass forms
\begin{equation}
 \Delta_u:=Q_{uu}-\sum_{a:k_{u,a}>0}
       \beta_{u,a,1}p_{u,a,k_{u,a}},
 \qquad
 \eta_u:=\lambda_u+\sum_{a:k_{u,a}>0}
       \beta_{u,a,1}q_{u,a,k_{u,a}}.
 \label{eq:double-y-composite-core-state}
\end{equation}
If $\chi_J^h=0$, store the scalar response
\begin{equation*}
 x_o=\eta_o/\Delta_o,
 \qquad g_h=\lambda_h+\beta_0x_o.
\end{equation*}
If $\chi_J^h=1$, store instead
\begin{equation*}
 \mathcal K_J=
 \begin{pmatrix}\Delta_o&-\beta_0\\-\beta_0&\Delta_h\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 \mathcal K_J\binom{x_o}{x_h}=\binom{\eta_o}{\eta_h}.
\end{equation*}
The matrix is the principal Schur complement of a positive-definite principal
submatrix of $Q$, so it is positive definite and has rank two.  Current
pendant-tip violations are constant-size affine readouts from $(x_o,x_h)$ and
the four final prefix records.

These products, aggregates, active-core bit, and tip values are functions only
of the committed set $U_J$.  They do not encode, infer, or replay the order in
which Phase I reached it.  The pass performs one charged membership/index
operation and $O(1)$ exact arithmetic per record, writes
$O(\card{U_J})$ permanent cells, and uses constant scratch.  It therefore
constructs exactly the common state required by the reviewed double-Y
countdown theorem.  Regard this conversion-complete state as the response's
initial state $z_0$: exposure, affine-record, core, and state-write events for
$U_J$ are marked consumed only after the conversion has paid them.  Hence the
imported theorem's mandatory common-updater hypothesis holds from the handoff
onward; no unperformed Phase-I response update is silently credited.

Invoke that response from this reachable state.  Its remaining activation
tokens admit each later positive tip once, append one affine record, update
and query a scalar or fixed $2\times2$ core, and charge gate/control and state
writes.  Its $\kappa=1$ token ledger bounds the suffix by
$O(C(S^\star))$ even for a legal certified bulk order chosen by another
policy.  It uses the actual common support throughout and never catches up by
replaying an order.  At termination it performs one outward recovery pass and
stores the resulting exact support vector once, one stored-row KKT/proximal
validation pass (including the at most four exterior tips), and one separate
exact output pass.  Recovery arithmetic is response work, the stored terminal
cells are charged to $C_{\rm mat}$, validation arithmetic is control work, and
the exact final-output cells are charged to $C_{\rm emit}$.  Each preceding
gate/certificate interaction also emits its separately charged constant-size
reply.

The conversion row traversal, the suffix's first exposures, and a conservative
fresh validation traversal give
$\mathcal C_{\rm adj}^{\rm post}\leq2\vol(S^\star)+O(1)$; the actual number of
nonempty suffix batches is at most $\card{S^\star\setminus U_J}$, and the
conservative sequential row traversals use only linearly many adjacency-query
batches.  All nonadjacency coordinates in
\cref{eq:double-y-composite-resources,eq:double-y-composite-eleven-vector} are
linear in the bounded-degree committed support.  The asserted memory bounds
follow from the separate common and numerical arenas.  Finally
$C(S^\star)\leq2\vol(S^\star)\leq2/\rho$, proving
\cref{eq:double-y-composite-work}.  Exact optimality gives
\cref{eq:double-y-composite-terminal-proximal}.  No estimate-sequence or
objective-gap carryover is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Double-Y product work only from an independent prefix bound]
\label{cor:double-y-composite-product-conditional}
Under \Cref{thm:double-y-composite-response-handoff}, if a separate theorem
proves
\begin{equation}
 B_J^{\rm full}=\softO\!\left(
   \frac{C(S^\star(\rho))}{\sqrt\alpha}
 \right),
 \label{eq:double-y-composite-independent-prefix-bound}
\end{equation}
with every charge in
\Cref{def:double-y-gate-compatible-prefix}, then
\begin{equation}
 W_{\rm total}
 =\softO\!\left(
   \frac{C(S^\star(\rho))}{\sqrt\alpha}
 \right)
 =\softO\!\left(\frac1{\rho\sqrt\alpha}\right).
 \label{eq:double-y-composite-conditional-product-work}
\end{equation}
Without this independent premise, the unconditional statement is only
$B_J^{\rm full}+O(C(S^\star))$.
\end{corollary}

\section{A canonical-checkpoint handoff on the branch caterpillar}
\label{sec:branch-caterpillar-composite-handoff}

We finally import the reviewed exact response theorem in the
adaptive-revisit-control note under its stable label
\texttt{thm:branch-caterpillar-canonical-layer-reporter}.  Fix the unweighted
branch caterpillar $\mathsf{BC}_m$ from that theorem: the degree-three branch
vertices $b_1,\ldots,b_m$ form a path, $b_1$ has the pendant paths
$b_1-a_1-\cdots-a_m$ and $b_1-r_1$, every internal $b_i$ has the leaf $r_i$,
and $b_m$ has the two leaves $r_m,r_{m+1}$.  The single seed is
$s=e_{b_1}$.  Throughout this section
\begin{equation}
 m\geq2,
 \qquad \alpha\in(0,1),
 \qquad
 0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)
 \leq\rho_{\rm cat}(m,\alpha)\leq\frac13,
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-range}
\end{equation}
the gate is the exact canonical all-violations RPPR gate, the external
interface is delta/certificate reporting, and arithmetic uses exact real
cells.  These are the imported theorem's hypotheses.  In particular, its
analysis-side reach threshold need not be uniform in $m$.

For $t\geq0$, put
$B_t:=\{b_1,\ldots,b_t\}$,
$A_t:=\{a_1,\ldots,a_t\}$, and
$R_t:=\{r_1,\ldots,r_t\}$, with all three sets empty at $t=0$.  The actual
canonical checkpoint after $k$ committed batches is
\begin{equation}
 \widehat U_k:=
 \begin{cases}
  B_{k+1}\cup A_k\cup R_k,&0\leq k<m,\\
  V(\mathsf{BC}_m),&k=m.
 \end{cases}
 \qquad
 \card{\widehat U_k}=3k+1,
 \qquad
 \card{S^\star\setminus\widehat U_k}=3(m-k).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-checkpoints}
\end{equation}
Here the last two identities use the imported conclusion
$S^\star=V(\mathsf{BC}_m)$ and
$C(S^\star)=9m+1$.  We record the checkpoints only to define the handoff;
we do not reprove the canonical chronology.

\begin{definition}[Fully charged canonical-checkpoint prefix]
\label{def:branch-caterpillar-canonical-checkpoint-prefix}
Let a finite Phase-I execution $\mathcal E_{J,k}^{\rm pref}$ stop at
$\widehat U_k$ for some $0\leq k\leq m$.  It may have arbitrary signed
numerical iterates, momentum, queues, and other auxiliary state.  It is a
\emph{canonical-checkpoint prefix} if all of the following common records are
present and paid:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item the integer $k$ and one labeled support/layer record for every vertex
       of $\widehat U_k$;
 \item for every $i<k$, the committed support increment is the actual
       canonical layer $\widehat U_{i+1}\setminus\widehat U_i$, with no
       numerical or merely exposed label treated as admitted; and
 \item after each such batch, the restricted RPPR state is exactly settled,
       its canonical all-violations certificate is checked, and the declared
       delta/certificate reply is charged.  The prefix ends after the reply
       for its last completed batch and before querying the boundary of
       $\widehat U_k$; for
       $k=0$ it has paid seed initialization, performs no gate interaction,
       and ends before the first gate query.  Arbitrary fully charged
       numerical Phase-I work may occur between initialization and that
       endpoint; labels exposed by such work remain nonadmitted.
\end{enumerate}
The common layer records occupy an arena distinguishable from the numerical
arena.  No response factors, old boundary keys, or numerical coordinates are
assumed to survive the switch.

In the shared exhaustive order, the prefix reports its actual vector
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{J,k}^{\rm pref}):=\bigl(&
 C_{\rm adj}^J,R_{\rm adj}^J,k,C_{\rm pre}^J,C_{\rm ctl}^J,
 C_{\rm rec}^J,C_{\rm resp}^J,\\
 &M_{\rm pers}^J,M_{\rm tmp}^J,C_{\rm mat}^J,C_{\rm emit}^J\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-prefix-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Thus its declared interface has $R_{\rm int}^J=k$.  The emission coordinate
includes all $3k$ admitted delta labels and the $k$ constant-size certificate
replies, together with any additional Phase-I emissions.  Any extra
speculative external stage must instead be added to both $R_{\rm int}^J$ and
$C_{\rm emit}^J$; it is not covered by the displayed canonical interface.
Under the fixed exact-cell convention, define
\begin{equation}
 B_J^{\rm full}:=
 C_{\rm adj}^J+C_{\rm pre}^J+C_{\rm ctl}^J+C_{\rm rec}^J
 +C_{\rm resp}^J+C_{\rm mat}^J+C_{\rm emit}^J.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-prefix-full-work}
\end{equation}
This sum charges seed/input reads, every first or repeated adjacency return,
old-row and recurrence work, outer/queue/gate/certificate control, exact
settlement or response work, checkpoint materialization, state reads and
writes, and every emitted cell.  The two round coordinates and two peak-memory
coordinates remain separate rather than being hidden in $B_J^{\rm full}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Paid canonical-checkpoint-to-balanced-response handoff]
\label{thm:branch-caterpillar-composite-response-handoff}
Under \cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-range}, let any finite Phase-I
prefix satisfy
\Cref{def:branch-caterpillar-canonical-checkpoint-prefix}.  There is one
deterministic paid support-only conversion that retires every signed numerical
and momentum cell, directly constructs the exact arm absorptions,
tridiagonal-core cells, and balanced transfer state at $\widehat U_k$, and
then invokes \emph{CaterpillarCanonicalLayerDelta} at that checkpoint.  The
conversion neither reconstructs nor queries any earlier canonical face.  In a
fixed exact-cell convention,
\begin{align}
 W_{\rm post}
 &=O\!\left(C(S^\star)\log(2+C(S^\star))\right),
 \notag\\
 W_{\rm total}
 &\leq B_J^{\rm full}
   +O\!\left(C(S^\star)\log(2+C(S^\star))\right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-work}
\end{align}

Put $J_{\rm post}:=m-k$.  One chosen batched-row realization has the complete
post-handoff vector
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm post})=\bigl(&
 O(\vol(S^\star)),O(J_{\rm post}+2),J_{\rm post}+1,0,
 O(C(S^\star)),0,\\
 &O(C(S^\star)+J_{\rm post}\log(2+m)),
 O(C(S^\star)),O(C(S^\star)),\\
 &\Theta(\card{S^\star}),
 \card{S^\star\setminus\widehat U_k}
 +\card{S^\star}+\Theta(J_{\rm post}+1)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The last coordinate consists respectively of each remaining new-positive
delta label once, the exact terminal vector once, and one constant-size reply
per remaining committed batch plus the terminal return.  Certificate
arithmetic is in $C_{\rm ctl}$, not $C_{\rm emit}$.  There is no post-handoff
ambient preprocessing and no row-recurrence direction.  Transfer-tree
construction, update, application, exact settlement, and terminal recovery
are in $C_{\rm resp}$; membership, structural checks, gate logic, tie handling,
and final KKT validation are in $C_{\rm ctl}$.  The terminal candidate is
materialized once before the separate exact output pass.

For the concatenated execution, every additive-work coordinate and both round
coordinates compose by summation:
\begin{align*}
 C_\xi^{\rm total}&=C_\xi^J+C_\xi^{\rm post}
 &&(\xi\in\{\mathrm{adj},\mathrm{pre},\mathrm{ctl},\mathrm{rec},
               \mathrm{resp},\mathrm{mat},\mathrm{emit}\}),\\
 R_{\rm adj}^{\rm total}&=R_{\rm adj}^J+R_{\rm adj}^{\rm post},
 &R_{\rm int}^{\rm total}&=k+(m-k+1)=m+1.
\end{align*}
Peak memory composes as
\begin{align}
 M_{\rm pers}^{\rm total}&\leq
 \begin{cases}
  M_{\rm pers}^J+O(C(S^\star)),&\text{retired prefix arena retained},\\
  \max\{M_{\rm pers}^J,O(C(S^\star))\},
      &\text{numerical arena released by its epoch pointer},
 \end{cases}
 \notag\\
 M_{\rm tmp}^{\rm total}
 &=\max\{M_{\rm tmp}^J,O(C(S^\star))\}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-memory-composition}
\end{align}
In the released case, the paid common support/layer arena remains available
until conversion consumes it; no cell-by-cell clearing is performed.

The returned vector is the exact RPPR minimizer, so
\begin{equation}
 R_{\mathrm{fp},\alpha,\rho}(x^\star(\rho))=0.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-terminal-proximal}
\end{equation}
The theorem is fixed-family, branch-seeded, canonical, delta-interface, and
exact-real.  It makes no claim for $\rho_{\rm can}\leq\rho<\rho_{\rm cat}$,
another seed or policy, repeated complete-positive-list output, a positive
$m$-uniform range of $\rho$, $\kappa=1$, finite precision, bit complexity,
transfer conditioning, RPPR-to-PPR conversion, arbitrary graphs, or
graph-uniform accelerated work.  The particular Catalyst/AESP Phase I in
\cref{sec:composite-hybrid} separately requires $\alpha\in(0,1/2)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The imported theorem states that the actual canonical support after $k$
batches is exactly $\widehat U_k$; when $k<m$, its next batch is the full
three-vertex boundary layer, while $k=m$ is already terminal.  Hence the
common records in
\Cref{def:branch-caterpillar-canonical-checkpoint-prefix} describe an actual
reachable checkpoint, independently of the signed Phase-I trajectory between
certificates.

Flip one phase/epoch pointer so that the Phase-I numerical arena, momentum,
queues, and uncommitted caches can no longer be read.  Their work and peak
residence are already present in the prefix vector.  Retain only $k$ and the
common labeled support/layer records.

Make one direct traversal of $\widehat U_k$.  Query every required support row
again, with every returned incidence charged; the conversion relies on no
retained Phase-I row or response cache.  Check that the stored support has the
branch, arm, and leaf incidences prescribed by
\eqref{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-checkpoints}.  Multiply the active
long-arm transfer cells once, retain their affine product, and obtain its
single absorption pair at $b_1$;
eliminate each active side leaf once into the cell at its own branch; then
form the $\min\{k+1,m\}$ surviving tridiagonal branch cells and build their
balanced product tree bottom-up.  Initialize the reporter's committed/previously
emitted membership set directly as $\widehat U_k\setminus\{b_1\}$.  The
imported canonical reporter retains no old boundary key across a checkpoint,
so no other historical reporting state is needed.  This takes
$O(C(\widehat U_k))$ adjacency, control, response, and state-write work,
$O(C(\widehat U_k))$ retained cells, and $O(C(\widehat U_k))$ scratch in the
deliberately simple bottom-up build.

This is a static construction from the current support, not a replay.  It
does not rebuild $\widehat U_0,\ldots,\widehat U_{k-1}$, apply their historical
point updates, test their old boundaries, or emit their labels again.  Schur
associativity makes the directly built absorption and tridiagonal cells equal
to the state that \emph{CaterpillarCanonicalLayerDelta} retains at
$\widehat U_k$.  Mark every conversion row, response cell, and state write
consumed only after paying it, so no unperformed Phase-I response update is
credited to the suffix.

Invoke the imported reporter from this exact checkpoint state.  Its next
$J_{\rm post}=m-k$ layers are the unique remaining canonical batches.  It
scans and tests each newly live three-row boundary, performs only constant-many
balanced-tree updates and named queries per batch, emits each new-positive
label once, and makes one terminal recovery, materialization, stored-row KKT
validation, and exact output pass.  Its reviewed ledger therefore gives
$O(J_{\rm post}\log(2+m)+C(S^\star))$ response work and linear work in every
other additive coordinate.  A batched scan of the known converted support,
followed by the adaptively revealed layer scans, gives
$R_{\rm adj}=O(J_{\rm post}+2)$; the declared gate interface gives exactly
$J_{\rm post}+1$ post interactions.  This proves every coordinate in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-eleven-vector} and the sum/peak
composition rules.

Finally, $C(S^\star)=9m+1$ and the shared RPPR support bound gives
$C(S^\star)\leq2\vol(S^\star)\leq2/\rho$.  Summing the seven post additive
coordinates proves \cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-work}.  Exact
terminal optimality proves
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-terminal-proximal}.  No numerical
estimate-sequence energy is transported.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Caterpillar product work only from an independent prefix bound]
\label{cor:branch-caterpillar-composite-product-conditional}
Under \Cref{thm:branch-caterpillar-composite-response-handoff}, if an
independent theorem proves the fully charged prefix bound
\begin{equation}
 B_J^{\rm full}=\softO\!\left(
  \frac{C(S^\star(\rho))}{\sqrt\alpha}
 \right),
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-independent-prefix-bound}
\end{equation}
then
\begin{equation}
 W_{\rm total}
 =\softO\!\left(\frac{C(S^\star(\rho))}{\sqrt\alpha}\right)
 =\softO\!\left(\frac1{\rho\sqrt\alpha}\right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-conditional-product-work}
\end{equation}
Without that premise, the proved total is only
$B_J^{\rm full}+O(C(S^\star)\log(2+C(S^\star)))$.  The corollary supplies no
bound on the Phase-I prefix itself.
\end{corollary}

\subsection{A responder-native first-layer prefix}
\label{sec:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-prefix}

The canonical response itself gives a small but exact answer to the
nonzero-checkpoint question.  The point is not to rename a numerical support
as an admission.  Instead, stop after one real gate interaction and retain
the response cells that performed its exact settlement.  This replaces full
row exposure by a genuinely layer-local prefix, although it does not yet make
an accelerated numerical state useful.

Put
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal F_0:=\widehat U_1\setminus\widehat U_0
 =\{b_2,a_1,r_1\},
 \qquad
 \mathcal F_1:=\widehat U_2\setminus\widehat U_1.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-sets}
\end{equation}
The second set is defined because $m\geq2$; when $m=2$ it is the terminal
layer $\{a_2,r_2,r_3\}$.

\begin{definition}[Responder-native first-layer policy]
\label{def:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-policy}
The policy \texttt{FirstLayer-BC}$_1$ starts from the paid seed checkpoint
$\widehat U_0=\{b_1\}$ and performs the following finite Phase I.
\begin{enumerate}
 \item Scan the row of $b_1$, solve the one-coordinate restricted RPPR
       problem exactly, and identify its three live boundary labels.  In the
       next adjacency round scan exactly the rows of
       $\mathcal F_0$.  Compute and strictly test their exact canonical
       demands; make no numerical-envelope scan beyond these rows.
 \item Make one canonical all-violations interaction.  Emit the three delta
       labels in $\mathcal F_0$, commit that batch, construct the constant-size
       arm absorption, leaf absorption, and two-cell tridiagonal response on
       $\widehat U_1$, settle the restricted point exactly, and return the
       charged certificate reply.
 \item Stop immediately at $k=1$.  Retain the exact response cells, the
       committed-membership set, and the common support/layer record.  Invoke
       no Catalyst stage and create no separate signed numerical arena.
\end{enumerate}
The continuation is the native \emph{CaterpillarCanonicalLayerDelta} state,
not the support-only reconstruction in
\Cref{thm:branch-caterpillar-composite-response-handoff}.
\end{definition}

The four arenas at the stopping time are exact and distinct:
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 U_{\mathrm{adm}}^{(1)}&=\widehat U_1,
 &U_{\mathrm{num}}^{(1)}&=\supp(x[\widehat U_1])=\widehat U_1,\\
 E_{\mathrm{row}}^{(1)}&=\widehat U_1,
 &E_{\mathrm{lab}}^{(1)}&=\widehat U_1\cup\mathcal F_1=\widehat U_2.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-arenas}
\end{equation}
Thus there is no excess numerical support and no scanned row outside the
irreversible admission.  The labels in
$E_{\mathrm{lab}}^{(1)}\setminus U_{\mathrm{adm}}^{(1)}=\mathcal F_1$ are merely
lookahead names revealed by the scanned rows: their rows are unscanned, their
coordinates are zero, and they are not admitted.  For $m=2$ these names happen
to be all remaining graph labels, but their rows and numerical coordinates
are still neither exposed nor admitted.

\begin{theorem}[Paid local prefix and native nonzero-checkpoint splice]
\label{thm:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-prefix}
Fix $m\geq2$, $0<\alpha<1/2$, and
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)$.  Then
\texttt{FirstLayer-BC}$_1$ stops after its one declared interaction at the
actual common checkpoint $\widehat U_1$.  In the shared order, one batched-row
realization has the prefix vector
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_1^{\rm pref})
 =\bigl(9,2,1,0,O(1),0,O(1),O(1),O(1),\Theta(1),\Theta(1)\bigr).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-prefix-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Here $C_{\rm adj}=9$ is the degree work of the four rows in
$\widehat U_1$, the two adjacency rounds are the seed-row round and the
parallel first-boundary round, and the emission consists of three delta labels
and one constant-size certificate reply.  The materialization coordinate pays
the common checkpoint record; response-state writes remain in
$C_{\rm resp}$ and retained memory.  Thus the seven additive prefix
coordinates sum to $B_1^{\rm full}=O(1)$.

Put $J_+:=m-1$.  Starting from the retained native state and making no
conversion traversal, the remaining $J_+$ canonical batches and terminal
return have the post vector
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{1\to\star}^{\rm post})=\bigl(&
 \vol(S^\star\setminus\widehat U_1),J_+,J_++1,0,
 O(C(S^\star)),0,\\
 &O(C(S^\star)+J_+\log(2+m)),
 \Theta(C(S^\star)),O(C(S^\star)),\\
 &\Theta(\card{S^\star}),
 \card{S^\star\setminus\widehat U_1}
 +\card{S^\star}+\Theta(J_++1)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-post-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The last coordinate contains every remaining delta label once, every exact
output coordinate once, one reply for each remaining batch, and the terminal
reply.  Additive work and both round coordinates sum.  The retained prefix
response is extended in place, so the concatenated persistent and temporary
peaks are respectively $O(C(S^\star))$ and $O(C(S^\star))$, rather than the
sum of two response arenas.  In particular,
$R_{\rm adj}^{\rm total}=R_{\rm int}^{\rm total}=m+1$, and the adjacency work
is $9+\vol(S^\star\setminus\widehat U_1)=\vol(S^\star)=6m$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The imported canonical-layer theorem says that the boundary of
$\widehat U_0$ is exactly $\mathcal F_0$ and that all three demands are
strictly positive in the stated range.  Hence the first all-violations reply
commits exactly that batch and reaches $\widehat U_1$; no stopping table or
knowledge of $\rho_{\rm can}$ is queried online.  The same theorem gives
strict positivity of the restricted point on its admitted face, proving the
two support identities in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-arenas}.

The degrees of $b_1,b_2,a_1,r_1$ are $3,3,2,1$, respectively.  Their four
row scans therefore cost nine degree cells.  The seed row must precede the
three boundary rows, while those three rows can be returned together in the
second adaptive round.  Their union is exactly $\widehat U_1$.  Because the
graph is a tree, the only new labels in those rows form $\mathcal F_1$;
querying none of their rows proves the exposure identities.  Scalar seed
settlement, three strict comparisons, one batch update, the constant-size
Schur construction, exact restricted settlement, membership writes, and the
declared reply all use constant exact-cell work and space.  No preprocessing,
row recurrence, or ambient response is hidden.  This proves
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-prefix-eleven-vector}.

After commitment, eliminate $a_1$ and $r_1$ into the $b_1$ cell and retain
the two-branch tridiagonal product with $b_2$.  These are exactly the native
cells obtained after the first update in the proof of the imported reporter:
Schur associativity leaves no historical key, and the consumed membership
set is precisely $\mathcal F_0$.  Consequently the suffix neither rebuilds
$\widehat U_0$, rescans a row of $\widehat U_1$, nor emits a first-layer label
again.

At each of the remaining $J_+$ checkpoints, scan the at-most-three newly live
boundary rows in one adjacency round, perform the imported constant number of
balanced-tree updates and named queries, and consume the complete positive
boundary.  These rows partition $S^\star\setminus\widehat U_1$, so their
degree work is the first coordinate of
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-post-eleven-vector}.  Removing the
already paid first batch from the imported canonical ledger gives every other
displayed work, round, memory, materialization, and emission coordinate.
The response cells grow in place, which proves the peak-memory rule.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Exact local product-scale comparator at $k=1$]
\label{cor:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-product}
Under \Cref{thm:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-prefix}, the concatenated
exact-real execution returns $x^\star(\rho)$ with
\begin{equation}
 W_{\rm total}
 =O\!\left(C(S^\star)\log(2+C(S^\star))\right)
 =\softO\!\left(\frac{C(S^\star)}{\sqrt\alpha}\right)
 =\softO\!\left(\frac1{\rho\sqrt\alpha}\right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-total-work}
\end{equation}
The exact first bound is authoritative and is uniform as
$\alpha\uparrow1/2$.  Its soft notation hides only
$\log(2+C(S^\star))$; in particular it hides no
$\log(1/(1-2\alpha))$ term.  This is a responder-native, fixed-family,
branch-seeded, canonical delta-interface RPPR result.  It is not evidence that
an accelerated burn-in is local or useful, and it makes no claim for another
range, seed, policy, graph, precision model, repeated-full-list interface, or
PPR accuracy.
\end{corollary}

\subsection{A full-envelope capped comparison witness}
\label{sec:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-prefix}

The preceding corollary cannot be discharged from the phrase ``any finite
valid composite Catalyst burn-in.''  That phrase specifies correctness of
each stage but neither a stopping time nor an execution budget.

\begin{proposition}[Uncapped finite burn-in does not imply a prefix bound]
\label{prop:branch-caterpillar-uncapped-prefix-obstruction}
Fix $m\geq2$, $0<\alpha<1/2$, and
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)$.  There is no upper bound on
$B_J^{\rm full}$, in terms of only $m,\alpha$, and $\rho$, that holds for
every finite valid composite Catalyst burn-in admitted by
\cref{alg:rppr-hybrid}.  This remains true while the common canonical state
is held at $\widehat U_0=\{b_1\}$ and no gate is queried.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Take any reachable valid prefix before the first canonical gate query.  At
each subsequent outer center, solve the strongly convex composite Catalyst
subproblem exactly.  Its exact minimizer satisfies every permitted inner
correctness condition, so the resulting Catalyst stage is valid; repeat this
construction for any prescribed finite number $N$ of stages.  Keep every
numerical or exposed nonseed label outside the common admission arena, so the
canonical checkpoint remains $\widehat U_0$ throughout.  Independently of the
inner solve's other nonnegative charges, each declared stage performs at least
one outer-stage advance and correctness-control operation.  Hence the appended
suffix has $\Delta C_{\rm ctl}=\Omega(N)$ and therefore increases
$B_J^{\rm full}$ by $\Omega(N)$.  Since $N$ is arbitrary, validity plus
finiteness is not a work bound.  This is an algorithm-specification
obstruction, not a lower bound for a capped policy.
\end{proof}

We next give one capped policy for which the missing premise is provable.  It
uses the reviewed certified-envelope AESP--CD result in the project note
\texttt{aesp\_cd\_l1\_rppr}, stable labels
\texttt{thm:aesp-cd-relative-oracle} and
\texttt{cor:aesp-cd-certified-envelope}.  We restate the execution details
needed by the present ledger.

Put
\begin{align}
 C_\star&:=C(S^\star)=9m+1,
 &\mathcal V_\star&:=\vol(S^\star)=6m,
 \notag\\
 \chi_{\rm A}&:=\frac{\alpha}{1-\alpha},
 &\delta_{\rm A}&:=
   \frac{\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}{2-\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}},
 \notag\\
 T_\star&:=\left\lceil
   \frac{2\log 4}{\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}\right\rceil,
 &L_\star&:=2+\frac34\log_+\!\left(
   \frac{16(1-\alpha)^2\mathcal V_\star}
        {\delta_{\rm A}(1-2\alpha)(1-\alpha)}
                         \right),
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-parameters}\\
 \mathfrak D_\star&:=C_\star+T_\star\mathcal V_\star L_\star,
 &\mathfrak H_\star&:=
  \bigl(1+\log(2+C_\star)\bigr)\mathfrak D_\star.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-budgets}
\end{align}
Here $\log_+(z):=\max\{0,\log z\}$.  The quantities are finite precisely in
the present composite range $0<\alpha<1/2$.

\begin{definition}[Capped full-envelope policy]
\label{def:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-zero-policy}
The policy \texttt{Full-BC-AESP}$_0$ acts on the promised
$\mathsf{BC}_m$ instance as follows.
\begin{enumerate}
 \item Read the sparse seed and make one paid outward traversal from $b_1$.
       Verify the fixed-family incidences, expose and index every vertex and
       row of $U:=V(\mathsf{BC}_m)$, and store its labels and degrees.  Do not
       treat the first returned incidences as a free row cache: every later
       AESP row scan is queried and charged again.  In the common
       canonical arena retain only the paid checkpoint record
       $\widehat U_0=\{b_1\}$.  All other exposed labels belong to a
       distinguishable numerical-envelope arena and are not admissions.
 \item From zero, run exactly $T_\star$ strongly convex composite Catalyst/AESP
       stages on the restricted objective over $U$, with
       $\kappa_{\rm A}=1-2\alpha$ and
       $\beta_{\rm A}=(\sqrt{1-\alpha}-\sqrt\alpha)/
       (\sqrt{1-\alpha}+\sqrt\alpha)$.  At each center $y$, materialize the
       proximal warm start
       \[
        u_U(y)=\prox_{g_{\rho,U}/[2(1-\alpha)]}\!\left(
          y-\frac{\nabla f_{t,U}(y)}{2(1-\alpha)}\right),
       \]
       and repeatedly update an exact maximizer of
       $|\kappa_{t,U,i}(z)|/\sqrt{d_i}$.  Rekey the updated row and its
       neighbors until the locally maintained
       relative certificate
       \[
        C_{t,U}(z)^2\leq
        \delta_{\rm A}(1-2\alpha)(1-\alpha)\norm{z-y}_2^2
       \]
       holds.  Here $C_{t,U}$ is the degree-weighted minimum shifted KKT mass.
       Maintain both sides of this test incrementally; no uncharged global
       certificate scan is permitted.
 \item Invoke no canonical gate, restricted settlement, Schur response, or
       external reply.  After stage $T_\star$, retire the numerical envelope,
       iterates, momentum, heap, and certificates by one epoch pointer, retain
       only the common seed checkpoint, and hand off before the first canonical
       gate query.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Fully charged capped AESP prefix on the caterpillar]
\label{thm:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-prefix}
Fix $m\geq2$, $0<\alpha<1/2$, and
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)$.  The execution in
\Cref{def:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-zero-policy} is a
canonical-checkpoint prefix in the sense of
\Cref{def:branch-caterpillar-canonical-checkpoint-prefix}, with $k=0$.  In the
shared exhaustive order it obeys
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm env}^{\rm pref})
 =\bigl(&O(\mathfrak D_\star),O(\mathfrak D_\star),0,O(C_\star),
 O(\mathfrak H_\star),O(\mathfrak D_\star),0,\\
 &O(C_\star),O(C_\star),O(\mathfrak D_\star),0\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-prefix-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Consequently,
\begin{equation}
 B_{\rm env}^{\rm full}
 =O(\mathfrak H_\star)
 =\softO\!\left(\frac{C(S^\star)}{\sqrt\alpha}\right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-prefix-work}
\end{equation}
The soft order hides only the displayed logarithms in $2+C_\star$,
$1/\alpha$, and $1/(1-2\alpha)$.  It is pointwise over
$0<\alpha<1/2$, not uniform as $\alpha\uparrow1/2$; the exact multivariate
bound $O(\mathfrak H_\star)$ is authoritative.  The numerical endpoint
additionally obeys
\begin{equation}
 F_\rho(x^{(T_\star)})-F_\rho(x^\star)
 \leq\frac12\bigl(F_\rho(0)-F_\rho(x^\star)\bigr),
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-half-gap}
\end{equation}
but the handoff neither uses nor transports this progress.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The imported strict-range canonical theorem gives
$S^\star=V(\mathsf{BC}_m)$, $\mathcal V_\star=6m$, and $C_\star=9m+1$.
Therefore the initial traversal constructs a certified envelope without an
optimal-support oracle.  It is nevertheless a full final-support exposure:
every returned adjacency incidence is charged in $C_{\rm adj}$, graph-family
validation and index construction before the online AESP continuation are
charged in $C_{\rm pre}$, seed reads and traversal scheduling are charged in
$C_{\rm ctl}$, label/degree index writes are charged in $C_{\rm pre}$ and the
memory peaks, and the explicit common seed checkpoint is charged in
$C_{\rm mat}$.

For one relative-accuracy stage, the reviewed local-oracle theorem bounds the
sum of degrees of all warm-start and repeated coordinate-row scans by
$\mathcal V_\star L_\star$; its PageRank contraction parameter is exactly
$2/3$, which gives the factor $3/4$ in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-parameters}.  Repeated reads of an
old row are counted again.  One adjacency-query round per row scan gives
$R_{\rm adj}=O(\mathfrak D_\star)$.  Exact proximal-coordinate arithmetic,
neighbor-gradient arithmetic, and the outer momentum recurrence cost
$O(\mathfrak D_\star)$ and are assigned to $C_{\rm rec}$.
Heap, queue, stopping-test, family, and outer-stage control cost
$O(\mathfrak H_\star)$ after charging a logarithm per touched key.  The
proximal warm start, stage iterate, center, momentum, and checkpoint vectors
are each materialized at most a constant number of times per stage, so
$C_{\rm mat}=O(C_\star+T_\star\card{S^\star})
 =O(\mathfrak D_\star)$.  Every explicit iterate and gradient state-cell read
or write made by a coordinate update is separately charged within this same
$O(\mathfrak D_\star)$ materialization bound; recurrence arithmetic
remains in $C_{\rm rec}$ and heap/key state and logic in $C_{\rm ctl}$.  All
exact KKT mass and displacement norms are updated with those charged local
changes.

There is no Phase-I response primitive or row settlement, so $C_{\rm resp}=0$;
there is no canonical gate or external stage, so $R_{\rm int}=C_{\rm emit}=0$.
The fixed envelope, a constant number of full vectors, their KKT keys, and the
heap require $O(C_\star)$ persistent and temporary cells.  This accounts for
every coordinate in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-prefix-eleven-vector}, including
all excess exposure (there is none beyond $S^\star$), every internal
certificate, and both memory peaks.  Summing the seven additive coordinates
gives the first equality in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-prefix-work}.  Since
$T_\star=O(1/\sqrt\alpha)$ and $C_\star=\Theta(\mathcal V_\star)$, the second
follows.

Every exposed nonseed label remains in the numerical arena, so the common
canonical state is still exactly $\widehat U_0$ and no earlier face exists to
replay.  Thus the policy meets the existing checkpoint interface.  Finally,
the reviewed relative Catalyst estimate is
\[
 F_\rho(x^{(T)})-F_\rho(x^\star)
 \leq2\left(1-\frac{\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}{2}\right)^T
       \bigl(F_\rho(0)-F_\rho(x^\star)\bigr).
\]
The definition of $T_\star$ and $1-u\leq e^{-u}$ prove
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-half-gap}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Product work for the named capped policy]
\label{cor:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-product}
Run \texttt{Full-BC-AESP}$_0$ and then the response in

 W_{\rm total}

      +C_\star\log(2+C_\star)\right)

 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-total-work}

This soft-order conclusion has the same

Its additive and round coordinates sum exactly at the existing interface,
$R_{\rm int}^{\rm total}=m+1$, and releasing the numerical prefix arena gives

fixed-family, full-envelope, zero-checkpoint witness.  It proves no bound for

\end{corollary}

\subsection{The exact signed-state margin at a canonical gate}
\label{sec:branch-caterpillar-signed-gate-margin}

The responder-native policy above avoids a signed conversion.  A genuinely
accelerated local prefix would instead like to use an inexact signed iterate
to decide the next exact canonical batch.  Relative objective accuracy alone
does not make that identification: the missing quantity is the exact
boundary margin.

Write
$\ell_\rho:=b-\alpha\rho D^{1/2}\mathbf 1$ for the shifted RPPR load.
For $0\leq k<m$, let $x_k:=x[\widehat U_k]$ be the exact restricted RPPR
point and let
$\mathcal F_k:=\widehat U_{k+1}\setminus\widehat U_k$.  Every
$v\in\mathcal F_k$ has one parent $p_k(v)\in\widehat U_k$.  Define
\begin{equation}
 \beta_{k,v}:=-Q_{v,p_k(v)}>0,
 \qquad
 g_{k,v}:=(\ell_\rho-Qx_k)_v>0,
 \qquad
 \mu_k:=\min_{v\in\mathcal F_k}\frac{g_{k,v}}{\beta_{k,v}}>0.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-signed-gate-margin}
\end{equation}
These are analysis quantities at the exact checkpoint, not free online
records.

\begin{proposition}[Exact margin barrier for a signed approximate gate]
\label{prop:branch-caterpillar-signed-gate-margin}
Under \cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-range}, fix $0\leq k<m$ and let
$z$ be any signed point supported on $\widehat U_k$.  For every
$v\in\mathcal F_k$,
\begin{equation}
 (\ell_\rho-Qz)_v
 =g_{k,v}+\beta_{k,v}
   \bigl(z_{p_k(v)}-(x_k)_{p_k(v)}\bigr).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-signed-demand-identity}
\end{equation}
Consequently, the symmetric certificate
$\lVert z-x_k\rVert_\infty<\mu_k$ is sufficient to preserve the complete
strict-positive canonical batch.  It is sharp for such a norm-ball
certificate: if $v_\star$ attains the minimum in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-signed-gate-margin}, then
\begin{equation}
 z^-=x_k-\mu_k e_{p_k(v_\star)}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 (\ell_\rho-Qz^- )_{v_\star}=0,
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-signed-gate-equality-witness}
\end{equation}
and any larger perturbation in that direction changes the gate sign.

Because $F_\rho$ restricted to $\widehat U_k$ is
$\alpha$-strongly convex, the objective-gap condition
\begin{equation}
 F_\rho(z)-F_\rho(x_k)<\frac{\alpha\mu_k^2}{2}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-signed-gap-sufficient}
\end{equation}
is sufficient for the same gate conclusion.  When additionally
$0<\alpha<1/2$, if relative-accuracy AESP--CD is used to obtain this
condition from an initial restricted gap
$\Delta_{k,0}$, then $\Delta_{k,0}=0$ is immediate zero-stage success by
strong convexity.  Assume henceforth that $\Delta_{k,0}>0$.  The reviewed
Catalyst estimate guarantees the condition, by this proof route, after any
integer $T$ satisfying
\begin{equation}
 T>
 \frac{2}{\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}
 \log_+\!\left(\frac{4\Delta_{k,0}}
                      {\alpha\mu_k^2}\right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-signed-gate-stage-cap}
\end{equation}
Moreover each imported relative-oracle stage retains the explicit factor
\begin{equation}
 L_k^{\rm rel}:=
 2+\frac34\log_+\!\left(
  \frac{16(1-\alpha)^2\vol(\widehat U_k)}
       {\delta_{\rm A}(1-2\alpha)(1-\alpha)}
                         \right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-signed-gate-oracle-log}
\end{equation}
Thus this available signed-state route has both the margin logarithm in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-signed-gate-stage-cap} and the explicit
$\log(1/(1-2\alpha))$ nonuniformity in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-signed-gate-oracle-log}.  The constant half-gap
guarantee in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-envelope-aesp-half-gap} does not imply
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-signed-gap-sufficient} unless the additional
comparison $\Delta_{k,0}<\alpha\mu_k^2$ is proved.

This is an exact signed-state/gate obstruction for the imported
relative-gap proof interface, not a lower bound for every local algorithm.
It does not refute exact settlement, a one-sided locally checkable interval,
or a new theorem that lower-bounds and charges the margins $\mu_k$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Only the parent coordinate of a tree-boundary vertex is nonzero in the row
$Q_{v,\widehat U_k}$.  Subtracting the exact and approximate demands gives
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-signed-demand-identity}.  If
$\lVert z-x_k\rVert_\infty<\mu_k$, then for every boundary vertex the right
side is strictly larger than
$g_{k,v}-\beta_{k,v}\mu_k\geq0$.  Choosing a minimizer and perturbing exactly
its parent proves
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-signed-gate-equality-witness}.

Strong convexity gives
\[
 F_\rho(z)-F_\rho(x_k)
 \geq\frac\alpha2\lVert z-x_k\rVert_2^2.
\]
Hence \cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-signed-gap-sufficient} implies the strict
infinity-norm condition.  The imported relative Catalyst estimate gives
\[
 \Delta_{k,T}
 \leq2\left(1-\frac{\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}{2}\right)^T
       \Delta_{k,0}
 \leq2e^{-T\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}/2}\Delta_{k,0},
\]
For $\Delta_{k,0}=0$, strong convexity already gives the exact point and no
stage is run; for $\Delta_{k,0}>0$, the displayed estimate proves the
sufficient stage cap.  Substitution of
$\kappa_{\rm A}=1-2\alpha$ and $\mu_t=1-\alpha$ into the imported
relative-oracle work bound gives
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-signed-gate-oracle-log}.
\end{proof}

The smallest instance of the barrier is already the seed face.  Put
$a_0=(1+\alpha)/2$ and $h_0=(1-\alpha)/2$.  Then
\begin{equation}
 (x_0)_{b_1}=\frac{\alpha(1-3\rho)}{a_0\sqrt3},
 \qquad
 \mu_0=(x_0)_{b_1}-\frac{3\alpha\rho\sqrt3}{h_0}>0,
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-seed-gate-margin}
\end{equation}
where the degree-three child $b_2$ attains the minimum; $a_1$ and $r_1$ have
degrees two and one.  Moving the single seed coordinate down by exactly this
amount makes the $b_2$ demand zero.  Because this face is one-dimensional,
\texttt{FirstLayer-BC}$_1$ simply settles it exactly and avoids the margin
logarithm.  At a generic growing checkpoint, retaining a useful signed
accelerated state still requires either paying
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-signed-gate-stage-cap}, proving a stronger
one-sided certificate, or performing the exact response settlement that the
current handoff already charges.  The first nonzero checkpoint is exceptional:
its face and boundary have constant size, so its sharp margin can be computed
and charged on demand.  We make that exception precise next.

\subsection{An on-demand first-layer margin and a numerical gate splice}
\label{sec:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-splice}

The result below does not maintain an eager array of future margins.  It
computes one scalar certificate only when the actual execution is already at
$\widehat U_1$, from the retained constant-size response and one paid scan of
the three currently live boundary rows.  This distinction matters: the scan
exposes rows in $\mathcal F_1$, but it neither admits those vertices nor lets
the numerical method use their coordinates.

Let
\begin{equation}
 \xi_1:=a_0^2-\frac{11h_0^2}{18}>0.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-response-denominator}
\end{equation}
The four restricted equations at $\widehat U_1$ give
\begin{align}
 (x_1)_{b_1}
  &=\frac{\alpha(a_0-3\rho)}{\sqrt3\,\xi_1},
 \notag\\
 (x_1)_{b_2}
  &=\frac{h_0(x_1)_{b_1}/3-\alpha\rho\sqrt3}{a_0},
 &
 (x_1)_{a_1}
  &=\frac{h_0(x_1)_{b_1}/\sqrt6-\alpha\rho\sqrt2}{a_0},
 \notag\\
 (x_1)_{r_1}
  &=\frac{h_0(x_1)_{b_1}/\sqrt3-\alpha\rho}{a_0}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-response-formula}
\end{align}
Define the scan-derived scalar
\begin{equation}
 \underline\mu_1:=
 \begin{cases}
  \min\left\{
   (x_1)_{b_2}-\dfrac{3\alpha\rho\sqrt3}{h_0},
   (x_1)_{a_1}-\dfrac{2\alpha\rho\sqrt2}{h_0}
       \right\},&m>2,\\[7pt]
  \min\left\{
   (x_1)_{b_2}-\dfrac{\alpha\rho\sqrt3}{h_0},
   (x_1)_{a_1}-\dfrac{\alpha\rho\sqrt2}{h_0}
       \right\},&m=2.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-local-margin}
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[Exact on-demand certificate for the first-layer margin]
\label{lem:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-certificate}
Fix $m\geq2$, $0<\alpha<1/2$, and
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)$.  At the actual checkpoint
$\widehat U_1$, scan each row in $\mathcal F_1$ once and retain it.  The local
incidences, degrees, $\alpha$, $\rho$, and
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-response-formula} are an exact-real,
locally checkable certificate that
\begin{equation}
 0<\underline\mu_1=\mu_1.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-equality}
\end{equation}
No value for another checkpoint is computed or retained.  Without the stated
strict-range promise, the same local routine fails closed whenever its
computed minimum is nonpositive; the lemma makes no conclusion in that case.

Put
\begin{equation}
 \nu_1:=\vol(\mathcal F_1)
 =\begin{cases}6,&m>2,\\3,&m=2.\end{cases}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-boundary-volume}
\end{equation}
Incremental to the already paid state of
\texttt{FirstLayer-BC}$_1$, one batched implementation of this on-demand
certificate has the complete vector
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\mu_1})
 =\bigl(\nu_1,1,0,0,O(1),0,O(1),O(1),O(1),O(1),0\bigr).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-certificate-vector}
\end{equation}
The response coordinate pays the constant-size retained-response readout and
algebraic cross-check in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-response-formula}; control pays the
degree, parent, strict-sign, minimum, and certificate checks; persistent and
materialization cells pay the three retained rows and the constant-size
certificate transcript.  There is no external interaction or emission.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
On $\widehat U_1=\{b_1,b_2,a_1,r_1\}$ the diagonal of $Q$ is $a_0$ and its
three off-diagonal entries incident to $b_1$ are
$-h_0/3$, $-h_0/\sqrt6$, and $-h_0/\sqrt3$.  The shifted loads at
$b_1,b_2,a_1,r_1$ are respectively
\[
 \frac{\alpha(1-3\rho)}{\sqrt3},\qquad
 -\alpha\rho\sqrt3,\qquad
 -\alpha\rho\sqrt2,\qquad
 -\alpha\rho.
\]
Eliminating the last three coordinates gives the coefficient
$a_0-11h_0^2/(18a_0)$ at $b_1$ and proves
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-response-formula}.

For any unseeded boundary vertex $v$ with active parent $p$, its load is
$-\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_v}$ and
$\beta_{1,v}=h_0/\sqrt{d_vd_p}$.  Therefore
\begin{equation}
 \frac{g_{1,v}}{\beta_{1,v}}
 =(x_1)_p-\frac{\alpha\rho}{h_0}d_v\sqrt{d_p}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-ratio}
\end{equation}
If $m>2$, the scanned boundary is $\{b_3,a_2,r_2\}$, with degree/parent
pairs $(3,b_2),(2,a_1),(1,b_2)$.  The $r_2$ ratio strictly exceeds the
$b_3$ ratio, leaving exactly the first line of
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-local-margin}.  If $m=2$, the
boundary is $\{a_2,r_2,r_3\}$; all three vertices have degree one and the
two $r$ ratios coincide, giving the second line.  The imported strict-range
chronology makes all three exact demands positive, so their minimum ratio is
strictly positive.  This proves
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-equality}.

The four rows of $\widehat U_1$ were paid by
\texttt{FirstLayer-BC}$_1$.  The three boundary rows have total degree
$\nu_1$ and can be scanned in one additional batched round.  All remaining
arithmetic and storage is constant size.  Assigning each operation as stated
proves
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-certificate-vector}.
\end{proof}

The zero-gap branch in step 2 has $z^{(0)}=x_1$ by strong convexity and runs

\begin{theorem}[Capped numerical gate at the actual first-layer checkpoint]
\label{thm:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-handoff}
Fix $m\geq2$, $0<\alpha<1/2$, and
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)$.  The policy in
\Cref{def:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-policy} certifies the
complete batch $\mathcal F_1$ and returns the exact RPPR minimizer.  Its
pre-handoff phase ends at the actual common checkpoint $\widehat U_1$ before
the second external interaction.  In the shared order it has the complete
prefix vector
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm MC1}^{\rm pref})=\bigl(&
 9+\nu_1+O(\mathfrak A_1),
 3+O(\mathfrak A_1),1,0,O(\mathfrak H_1),
 O(\mathfrak A_1),O(1),\\
 &O(C_2),O(C_1),O(\mathfrak D_1),\Theta(1)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-prefix-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The prefix emission is exactly the first three delta labels and one
constant-size certificate reply; the on-demand margin and all numerical
certificates are internal control/state and emit no cell.  In particular,
\begin{equation}
 B_{\rm MC1}^{\rm full}=O(\mathfrak H_1).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-prefix-work}
\end{equation}

After the handoff, the stored rows of $\mathcal F_1$ are not scanned again.
One batched continuation has the complete post vector
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm MC1}^{\rm post})=\bigl(&
 \vol(S^\star\setminus\widehat U_2),m-2,m,0,
 O(C(S^\star)),0,\\
 &O(C(S^\star)+(m-1)\log(2+m)),
 \Theta(C(S^\star)),O(C(S^\star)),\\
 &\Theta(\card{S^\star}),
 \card{S^\star\setminus\widehat U_1}
 +\card{S^\star}+\Theta(m)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-post-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The $m-2$ adjacency rounds are zero when $m=2$.  The $m$ interactions are
the certified $\mathcal F_1$ batch, the remaining $m-2$ batches, and the
terminal return.  The emission coordinate contains every still-unemitted
positive label once, every exact output coordinate once, one reply for every
remaining batch, and the terminal reply.

All additive and round coordinates sum across the handoff.  Releasing the
constant-size numerical/certificate epoch after the $\mathcal F_1$ response
update gives total persistent and temporary peaks
$\Theta(C(S^\star))$ and $O(C(S^\star))$.  Therefore
\begin{equation}
 W_{\rm total}
 =O\!\left(
   \mathfrak H_1+C(S^\star)\log(2+C(S^\star))
       \right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-total-work}
\end{equation}
The exact margin-dependent expression is authoritative.  It contains
\begin{equation*}
 \log_+\!\left(
 \frac{4\Delta_{1,0}}{\alpha\underline\mu_1^2}
             \right)
 \quad\text{when }\Delta_{1,0}>0,
 \qquad\text{and}\qquad
 L_1^{\rm rel}=2+\frac34\log_+\!\left(
  \frac{144(1-\alpha)}{\delta_{\rm A}(1-2\alpha)}\right)
\end{equation*}
with the full constants fixed in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-oracle-log,eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-stage-cap}.
The second expression retains its explicit
$\log(1/(1-2\alpha))$ upper-end nonuniformity.  Neither factor is hidden by a
claim uniform in the margin or as $\alpha\uparrow1/2$.

This is an exact-real, fixed-family, branch-seeded, fixed-face numerical
gate witness at $k=1$.  Its signed state really supplies the carried
pre-gate coordinate vector in the positive-gap branch, but its one-time
margin certificate was obtained from a separately charged exact
constant-size response.  Hence it is not a speedup over
\texttt{FirstLayer-BC}$_1$, not an automatically exploring accelerated
prefix, and not a graph-uniform locality result.  It proves no claim for
another checkpoint, range, seed, policy, repeated complete-positive-list
interface, finite precision, bit complexity, PPR conversion, or arbitrary
graph.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For $\Delta_{1,0}=0$, strong convexity gives $z^{(0)}=x_1$ and the policy runs
zero stages.  Suppose $\Delta_{1,0}>0$.  By construction,
$T_1(z^{(0)})$ is an integer satisfying the strict stage inequality
\begin{equation*}
 T_1(z^{(0)})>
 \frac{2}{\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}
 \log_+\!\left(
  \frac{4\Delta_{1,0}}
       {\alpha\underline\mu_1^2}
             \right).
\end{equation*}
The imported relative Catalyst estimate and
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-equality} therefore give
\[
 F_{\rho,\widehat U_1}(z^{(T_1)})
 -F_{\rho,\widehat U_1}(x_1)
 <\frac{\alpha\mu_1^2}{2}.
\]
This invokes only the generic strongly convex outer rate on the restricted
objective.  The certified-envelope corollary's separate conclusion that a
restricted solve equals the full optimum is neither assumed nor needed here.
This also covers the case where the logarithm is zero: then $T_1=1$ and the
strict exponential contraction improves the already sufficient
$4\Delta_{1,0}\leq\alpha\mu_1^2$ bound.  Strong convexity and
\Cref{prop:branch-caterpillar-signed-gate-margin} make all three signed
boundary demands strictly positive.  Hence the carried signed state and its
certificate select exactly the actual canonical batch $\mathcal F_1$.

The imported fixed-envelope relative oracle charges at most
$\mathcal V_1L_1^{\rm rel}$ degree work per stage, including every warm start
and repeated row scan.  One adjacency round per charged row scan gives the
$O(\mathfrak A_1)$ additions to the first two coordinates.  Proximal,
gradient, and outer recurrences cost $O(\mathfrak A_1)$ in $C_{\rm rec}$;
heap, queue, strict-gap, signed-demand, interval, and outer-stage logic costs
$O(\mathfrak H_1)$ in $C_{\rm ctl}$.  The exact first-layer settlement and
one-time response readout remain separately visible as $C_{\rm resp}=O(1)$.
Every explicit numerical state read/write and full four-coordinate stage
materialization is covered by $O(\mathfrak D_1)$ in $C_{\rm mat}$.  The
common records, seven scanned rows, numerical face, heap, certificate
transcript, and temporary vectors give the displayed memory coordinates.
There is no preprocessing, no external AESP interaction, and no numerical
emission.  Adding the vectors from
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-prefix-eleven-vector,eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-certificate-vector}
proves
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-prefix-eleven-vector}.

At the second interaction, consume the already scanned $\mathcal F_1$ rows,
emit its three labels once, and pay the exact response build and settlement
on $\widehat U_2$.  Schur associativity gives the native reporter state at
that actual checkpoint; no earlier face is replayed.  The subsequent live
boundary rows partition $S^\star\setminus\widehat U_2$, giving the first two
post coordinates.  Removing the already paid first layer and pre-scanned
second layer from the reviewed canonical reporter ledger gives every other
coordinate in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-post-eleven-vector}.
The stated sum and peak rules now prove
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-numerical-total-work}.  Exact terminal
settlement returns $x^\star(\rho)$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A response-free one-sided certificate and its exposure limit}
\label{sec:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-one-sided-splice}

The sharp scalar $\mu_1$ is useful for prescribing a stage count, but it is
not necessary for certifying a gate sign.  The alternative below separates
three operations that were coupled in the preceding construction:
constructing a lower point from a signed numerical iterate, exposing and
checking the live boundary, and exploring a new admitted face.  Only the
second operation is the gate certificate itself.

For a canonical face $U=\widehat U_k$, put
$w_U:=D_U^{1/2}\one$, and write $[q]_-:=\max\{-q,0\}$ coordinatewise.
Define the restricted lower residual and local lower map
\begin{align}
 \zeta_{\rho,U}^{\rm low}(z)
  &:=\ell_{\rho,U}-Q_{UU}z,
 &
 \Delta_U^{\rm low}(z)
  &:=\frac1\alpha
    \left\lVert D_U^{-1/2}
       [\zeta_{\rho,U}^{\rm low}(z)]_-\right\rVert_\infty,
 \notag\\
 \mathcal L_U(z)
  &:=\left[z-\Delta_U^{\rm low}(z)w_U\right]_+.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-map}
\end{align}
This is the principal-face version of the imported local lower retraction.
It is computed from the rows in $U$ and does not query a restricted optimum.

\begin{lemma}[Response-free lower-point gate certificate]
\label{lem:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-gate-certificate}
Fix $0\leq k<m$ under
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-range}, and let $z$ be any signed point
supported on $\widehat U_k$.  Then
\begin{equation}
 0\leq\mathcal L_{\widehat U_k}(z)\leq x_k
 \quad\hbox{coordinatewise}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-order}
\end{equation}
For $v\in\mathcal F_k$, with active parent $p_k(v)$, put
\begin{equation}
 \underline g_{k,v}(z):=
  (\ell_\rho-Q\mathcal L_{\widehat U_k}(z))_v.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-demand}
\end{equation}
Every strict comparison $\underline g_{k,v}(z)>0$ is a one-sided exact
certificate that $g_{k,v}>0$.  Consequently, if all current boundary rows
have been validated and all displayed lower demands are positive, their
complete set is exactly the canonical all-violations batch.  No coordinate of
$x_k$, restricted optimum-value scalar, $\mu_k$, response factor, or future
margin table is used.

At the actual first-layer checkpoint, assume the four face rows and the lower
point have already been charged.  Scanning and retaining the three rows in
$\mathcal F_1$, validating their degrees and unique parents, evaluating the
three quantities in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-demand}, and storing the strict-sign
transcript has incremental vector
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm LowGate,1})
 =\bigl(\nu_1,1,0,0,O(1),0,0,
         O(1),O(1),O(1),0\bigr).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-certificate-vector}
\end{equation}
Thus, relative to
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-margin-certificate-vector}, the same
boundary exposure and all nonresponse operation classes remain constant,
while $C_{\rm resp}$ is exactly zero rather than a positive constant.  This
is a strict response-coordinate improvement.  It is not a claim that an
arbitrary signed trial point or the work needed to construct its lower
retraction is free.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Write $U=\widehat U_k$, $\Delta=\Delta_U^{\rm low}(z)$, and
$\ell=\mathcal L_U(z)$.  A principal normalized PageRank matrix is Stieltjes,
and
\begin{equation*}
 Q_{UU}w_U\geq\alpha w_U
\end{equation*}
because deleting the nonpositive off-face entries can only increase the
principal row product.  If $\ell_i>0$, then
$\ell_i=z_i-\Delta(w_U)_i$, while
$\ell_j\geq z_j-\Delta(w_U)_j$ for every $j$.  The off-diagonal signs give
\begin{equation*}
 (Q_{UU}\ell)_i
 \leq(Q_{UU}(z-\Delta w_U))_i
 \leq(Q_{UU}z)_i-\alpha\Delta(w_U)_i.
\end{equation*}
Hence
$\zeta_{\rho,U,i}^{\rm low}(\ell)\geq0$ on $\supp(\ell)$.
Restricted Stieltjes obstacle comparison with the strictly positive
restricted solution $x_k$ proves
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-order}.

The row of a tree-boundary vertex has only one entry in $U$, namely
$Q_{v,p_k(v)}=-\beta_{k,v}$.  Therefore
\begin{equation}
 g_{k,v}-\underline g_{k,v}(z)
 =\beta_{k,v}
   \bigl((x_k)_{p_k(v)}-
    \mathcal L_U(z)_{p_k(v)}\bigr)\geq0.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-demand-order}
\end{equation}
Strict positivity of the lower quantity certifies strict positivity of the
exact demand.  Enumerating the complete tree boundary and testing every row
therefore certifies the canonical batch.  At $k=1$ those rows have total
degree $\nu_1$ and are returned in one batch; all other work and storage is
constant.  No restricted-system solve occurs, proving both the vector and
the strict response-coordinate comparison.
\end{proof}

The certificate is online: it can stop on the observed lower demands without
knowing a margin.  The following quantitative statement is only an
analysis-side termination bound.  Since $\lVert Q_{UU}\rVert_2\leq1$ and all
branch-caterpillar degrees are at most three, for
$e=z-x_k$ one has
\begin{align}
 \Delta_U^{\rm low}(z)
 &\leq\frac{\lVert e\rVert_2}{\alpha},
 \notag\\
 0\leq (x_k)_p-\mathcal L_U(z)_p
 &\leq\left(1+\frac{\sqrt3}{\alpha}\right)
        \lVert e\rVert_2
 \qquad(p\in U).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-error}
\end{align}
It follows from
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-signed-gate-margin,eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-demand-order}
that every lower demand is positive whenever
\begin{equation}
 \lVert z-x_k\rVert_2
 <\frac{\alpha\mu_k}{\alpha+\sqrt3}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-neighborhood}
\end{equation}
Strong convexity makes the sufficient objective-gap threshold
\begin{equation}
 F_{\rho,U}(z)-F_{\rho,U}(x_k)
 <\frac{\alpha^3\mu_k^2}
         {2(\alpha+\sqrt3)^2}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-gap}
\end{equation}
Thus the lower test terminates along every convergent fixed-face AESP
sequence, even though the online execution computes neither side of
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-gap}.

For completeness, let $z_k^{(t)}$ be the endpoint after $t$ relative-accuracy
AESP--CD stages on $\widehat U_k$, starting from an explicitly materialized
$z_k^{(0)}$, and put
\begin{equation}
 T_k^{\rm low}:=\min\left\{t\geq0:
  \underline g_{k,v}(z_k^{(t)})>0
  \text{ for every }v\in\mathcal F_k\right\}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-stopping-time}
\end{equation}
In the strict range this stopping time is finite.  If the initial restricted
gap $\Delta_{k,0}$ is zero, strong convexity gives
$z_k^{(0)}=x_k$, so $T_k^{\rm low}=0$ and no numerical stage is run.  If
$\Delta_{k,0}>0$, the imported outer estimate gives the explicit
analysis-side bound
\begin{equation}
 T_k^{\rm low}\leq
 1+\left\lfloor\frac{2}{\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}
 \log_+\!\left(
  \frac{4(\alpha+\sqrt3)^2\Delta_{k,0}}
       {\alpha^3\mu_k^2}
            \right)\right\rfloor.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-stage-bound}
\end{equation}
Positive gap need not imply positive stage count: a sufficiently good initial
lower interval can already pass.  Conversely, for the named zero start on
either $k=0$ or $k=1$, the lower point is zero and every unseeded boundary
load is negative, so the initial test fails and at least one genuine stage is
run.  Equation
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-stage-bound} is not an online cap:
it exposes the same analysis margin dependence that the observed sign test
avoids computing.

We now test whether the response-free certificate also constructs the first
checkpoint.  The answer separates numerical exposure from row exposure.

\begin{definition}[Two-gate response-free prefix]
\label{def:branch-caterpillar-two-face-lower-policy}
The policy \texttt{LowerGate-BC-AESP}$_{0:1}$ uses the promised
$\mathsf{BC}_m$ labels but validates every row, degree, parent, $\alpha$, and
$\rho$ value that it reads.
\begin{enumerate}
 \item Start at $\widehat U_0=\{b_1\}$ with numerical point zero.  Scan the
       seed row, then scan and retain all three rows in $\mathcal F_0$ in one
       batch.  Run fixed-face relative-accuracy AESP--CD, construct
       \cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-map} after every stage, and
       stop at the first strict lower-demand certificate for all of
       $\mathcal F_0$.
 \item Make one external delta/certificate interaction, emit and commit the
       three labels in $\mathcal F_0$, and record the actual admitted face
       $\widehat U_1$.  Perform no exact settlement and build no response.
       Reset the signed numerical face to the explicitly materialized zero
       vector on $\widehat U_1$; this reset deliberately discards the first
       face's numerical value and is charged.
 \item Scan and retain all rows in $\mathcal F_1$.  Repeat the fixed-face
       AESP, lower-retraction, and strict lower-demand test on
       $\widehat U_1$.  Stop after the certificate and before the second
       external interaction.
 \item In the post phase, emit and commit $\mathcal F_1$, build and settle the

\end{enumerate}
This is a sign-certified canonical chronology, but the state after step 2 is
not the older response-native checkpoint: its exact restricted point has not
been materialized.  The direct post proof below therefore does not invoke

\end{definition}

Immediately before the first interaction, the arenas are

 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-lower-first-gate-arenas}

\begin{proposition}[Charged row-validation obstruction at the first layer]
\label{prop:branch-caterpillar-first-layer-row-preexposure-obstruction}
Under the row-validation contract of

in its own access model.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The three exact lower demands use the candidate load
$-\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_v}$ and coupling

the stated contract, each $d_v$ and incidence list is obtained by its charged
row return.  All three values are needed before the complete-batch reply, so
their returned-row union with the seed is exactly $\widehat U_1$.  The degree
sum is $3+2+1=6$.  Calling these facts ``known from the caterpillar
template'' without validating their input rows would change the contract and
does not evade the charge.
\end{proof}

Put

\label{thm:branch-caterpillar-two-face-lower-handoff}

\end{equation}

cross-face acceleration.  The policy pre-exposes the candidate rows required

finite precision, bit complexity, PPR conversion, or graph-uniform work.

\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-error,eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-neighborhood}

positive while the start is zero; hence the gap is positive.  Its initial

and the three rows in $\mathcal F_1$ of total degree $\nu_1$, in three

\end{proof}

\subsection{Transporting a lower anchor through every canonical face}
\label{sec:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-splice}

The zero resets in
\Cref{def:branch-caterpillar-two-face-lower-policy} are not required for
correctness.  What survives an admission is weaker than Catalyst energy but
stronger than a fresh zero start: one coordinatewise lower point.  We state
that invariant separately so that it cannot be confused with a transported
momentum or estimate-sequence certificate.

Put $x_m:=x^\star(\rho)$, and let
$\iota_k:\mathbb R^{\widehat U_k}\to
\mathbb R^{\widehat U_{k+1}}$ denote zero padding on
$\mathcal F_k$.  For vectors on the same face, $\vee$ denotes coordinatewise
maximum.

\begin{lemma}[Canonical-face order and anchored lower transport]
\label{lem:branch-caterpillar-anchored-lower-transport}
Under \cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-composite-range}, for every $0\leq k<m$,
\begin{equation}
 x_{k+1}\big|_{\widehat U_k}\geq x_k,
 \qquad
 0\leq y\leq x_k
 \ \Longrightarrow\
 0\leq\iota_k y\leq x_{k+1}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-padded-lower-transport}
\end{equation}
More generally, if $a$ is any charged anchor with $0\leq a\leq x_k$, define
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal T_{k,a}(z):=
 a\vee\mathcal L_{\widehat U_k}(z).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-anchored-lower-map}
\end{equation}
For every signed $z$ supported on $\widehat U_k$,
\begin{align}
 0\leq a\leq\mathcal T_{k,a}(z)&\leq x_k,
 \notag\\
 (\ell_\rho-Q\mathcal T_{k,a}(z))_v
 &\leq g_{k,v}
 \qquad(v\in\mathcal F_k).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-anchored-lower-invariant}
\end{align}
Thus strict positivity of every displayed anchored demand is a conservative
certificate for the complete canonical batch.

If $y_k$ is such a certified anchored point and the batch
$\mathcal F_k$ is committed, the update
\begin{equation}
 a_{k+1}:=\iota_k y_k
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-transported-anchor-update}
\end{equation}
preserves every old lower coordinate exactly and initializes only the newly
admitted coordinates to zero.  Repeating
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-anchored-lower-map} on the new face can only
increase this anchor coordinatewise.  This statement holds through the last
canonical admission; it uses no restricted response and no table of future
margins.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Every coordinate of $x_k$ and $x_{k+1}$ on their declared canonical supports
is strictly positive.  Hence their restricted KKT equations are equalities.
Writing $U=\widehat U_k$ and $F=\mathcal F_k$, the $U$ block gives
\[
 Q_{UU}\bigl(x_{k+1}|_U-x_k\bigr)
 =-Q_{UF}x_{k+1}|_F\geq0.
\]
The principal Stieltjes inverse $Q_{UU}^{-1}$ is entrywise nonnegative, which
proves the first inequality in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-padded-lower-transport}; positivity on $F$ proves
the zero-padding conclusion.

\Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-gate-certificate} applies at every
canonical face and gives
$0\leq\mathcal L_{\widehat U_k}(z)\leq x_k$.  Taking a coordinatewise maximum
with another vector below $x_k$ proves the first line of
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-anchored-lower-invariant}.  A tree-boundary row
has only its parent entry in the current face, so
\[
 g_{k,v}-(\ell_\rho-Q\mathcal T_{k,a}(z))_v
 =\beta_{k,v}\bigl((x_k)_{p_k(v)}
   -\mathcal T_{k,a}(z)_{p_k(v)}\bigr)\geq0.
\]
The remaining assertions follow by induction from zero padding and
coordinatewise maximum.
\end{proof}

This invariant preserves a numerical lower vector, not the AESP auxiliary
state.  To make the distinction operational, fix a certificate horizon
$q\in\{2,\ldots,m\}$.  The full canonical chronology still has $m$ batches;
$q$ only determines how many consecutive batch signs are certified before
the response handoff.

\begin{definition}[Transported-lower canonical prefix]
\label{def:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-policy}
The policy
\texttt{TransportLower-BC-AESP}$_{0:q-1}$ proceeds as follows.
\begin{enumerate}
 \item Scan and retain the seed row, and set $a_0=0$ on
       $\widehat U_0$.  For
       $k=0,\ldots,q-1$, scan and retain every row in the current boundary
       $\mathcal F_k$, validating its degree, unique active parent, and the
       completeness of the promised tree boundary.
 \item On $\widehat U_k$, initialize a \emph{fresh} relative-accuracy
       AESP--CD run at the explicitly materialized point
       $z_k^{(0)}=a_k$.  Its momentum, proximal, center, and
       estimate-sequence records are new and charged; no such record is
       transported from $\widehat U_{k-1}$.  After every endpoint, including
       $t=0$, make one full stored-row sweep of $\widehat U_k$, compute
       \[
       y_k^{(t)}:=\mathcal T_{k,a_k}(z_k^{(t)}),
       \]
       and test all three anchored boundary demands.  Stop at the first
       all-strict-positive test.
 \item If $k<q-1$, make one delta/certificate interaction, emit and commit
       precisely $\mathcal F_k$, retire the current AESP auxiliary arena, and
       append that batch to the paid common support/layer record.  Then
       materialize the charged warm start
       $a_{k+1}=\iota_k y_k^{(T_k^{\rm tr})}$.  The old coordinates are copied
       unchanged and the three new coordinates are written as zero.  Continue
       on the next face.
 \item Stop on $\widehat U_{q-1}$ after certifying
       $\mathcal F_{q-1}$ and before the $q$th interaction.  In the post phase,
       emit and commit that batch, build and settle the native response
       directly on the retained rows of $\widehat U_q$, retire the lower/AESP
       arena, and continue
       \emph{CaterpillarCanonicalLayerDelta}.  When $q=m$, this direct build is
       on the full support and is followed only by terminal validation and
       return.
\end{enumerate}
Candidate rows are certificate-only until their batch is committed.  At the
pre-handoff endpoint,
\begin{equation}
 U_{\rm adm}=U_{\rm num}=\widehat U_{q-1},
 \qquad E_{\rm row}=\widehat U_q,
 \qquad
 E_{\rm lab}=\widehat U_{\min\{q+1,m\}}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-arenas}
\end{equation}
The last set includes labels revealed while scanning $\mathcal F_{q-1}$, not
rows or numerical coordinates outside $\widehat U_q$.
\end{definition}

For the observed execution, put
\begin{align}
 T_k^{\rm tr}
 &:=
 \min\left\{t\geq0:
  (\ell_\rho-Qy_k^{(t)})_v>0
  \text{ for every }v\in\mathcal F_k\right\},
 \notag\\
 \Delta_{k,0}^{\rm tr}
 &:=
 F_{\rho,\widehat U_k}(a_k)
 -F_{\rho,\widehat U_k}(x_k).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-stopping-time}
\end{align}
These stopping times are online and use only observed strict signs.  They are
also finitely capped in the analysis.  If $\Delta_{k,0}^{\rm tr}=0$, then
$T_k^{\rm tr}=0$.  Otherwise
\begin{equation}
 T_k^{\rm tr}\leq
 1+\left\lfloor\frac{2}{\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}

      {\alpha^3\mu_k^2}
           \right)\right\rfloor.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-stage-bound}
\end{equation}
Indeed, the unanchored lower retraction passes under the sufficient
neighborhood in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-neighborhood}, and the anchored

\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-stage-bound}, and the gaps
$\Delta_{k,0}^{\rm tr}$ that contain $x_k$, are analysis-only: the policy
neither computes nor retains either quantity.

For $0\leq k<m$ and for the full support define
\begin{align}
 \mathcal V_k^{\rm can}
 &:=\vol(\widehat U_k)=6k+3,
 &
 \mathcal C_k^{\rm can}
 &:=C(\widehat U_k)=9k+4,
 \notag\\
 \mathcal V_m^{\rm can}&:=6m,
 &
 \mathcal C_m^{\rm can}&:=9m+1,
 \notag\\
 \nu_k^{\rm can}
 &:=\vol(\mathcal F_k)
 =\begin{cases}6,&k<m-1,\\3,&k=m-1,\end{cases}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-face-counts}\\
 \Lambda_k^{\rm tr}
 &:=2+\frac34\log_+\!\left(
  \frac{16(1-\alpha)^2\mathcal V_k^{\rm can}}
       {\delta_{\rm A}(1-2\alpha)(1-\alpha)}
                         \right),
 \notag\\
 \mathfrak A_k^{\rm tr}
 &:=(T_k^{\rm tr}+1)\mathcal V_k^{\rm can}\Lambda_k^{\rm tr},
 &
 \mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm tr}
 &:=\sum_{k=0}^{q-1}\mathfrak A_k^{\rm tr},
 \notag\\
 \mathfrak D_{<q}^{\rm tr}
 &:=\mathcal C_q^{\rm can}+\mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm tr},
 &
 \mathfrak H_{<q}^{\rm tr}
 &:=\mathcal C_q^{\rm can}
  +\sum_{k=0}^{q-1}
   (1+\log(2+\mathcal C_k^{\rm can}))\mathfrak A_k^{\rm tr}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-budgets}
\end{align}
The added one pays the initial/terminal anchored-retraction sweep even when
the observed stopping time is zero.  In particular, these budgets charge a
full old-face scan at every test; they are not a sublinear diagnostic.

\begin{theorem}[All-layer transported-lower response-free prefix]
\label{thm:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-handoff}
Fix $m\geq2$, $0<\alpha<1/2$,
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)$, and $2\leq q\leq m$.
The policy
\texttt{TransportLower-BC-AESP}$_{0:q-1}$ certifies the first $q$
canonical batches, commits the first $q-1$ without exact settlement, and
preserves its charged lower anchor across every one of those admissions.
Every transported start $a_k$, $1\leq k<q$, is nonzero: the first strict
boundary test forces a positive seed coordinate, and
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-transported-anchor-update} copies that coordinate
unchanged at every later admission.
Before the $q$th interaction its complete vector is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm TL,q}^{\rm pref})=\bigl(&
 \mathcal V_q^{\rm can}+O(\mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm tr}),
 q+1+O(\mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm tr}),q-1,0,
 O(\mathfrak H_{<q}^{\rm tr}),
 O(\mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm tr}),0,\\
 &O(\mathcal C_q^{\rm can}),
 O(\mathcal C_{q-1}^{\rm can}),
 O(\mathfrak D_{<q}^{\rm tr}),\Theta(q)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-prefix-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Thus $C_{\rm resp}=0$ exactly through the response-free prefix.  Its
$q-1$ interactions emit the $3(q-1)$ labels in
$\mathcal F_0,\ldots,\mathcal F_{q-2}$ and one constant-size reply apiece.
All failed sign tests, lower maps, anchors, and AESP states are internal and
emit nothing.

After the handoff, the rows through $\widehat U_q$ are reused rather than
rescanned.  The paid direct response build on $\widehat U_q$ and remaining
canonical continuation have vector
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm TL,q}^{\rm post})=\bigl(&
 \vol(S^\star\setminus\widehat U_q),m-q,m-q+2,0,
 O(C(S^\star)),0,\\
 &O(C(S^\star)+(m-q+1)\log(2+m)),
 \Theta(C(S^\star)),O(C(S^\star)),
 \Theta(\card{S^\star}),\\
 &\card{S^\star\setminus\widehat U_{q-1}}
  +\card{S^\star}+\Theta(m-q+2)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-post-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The post interaction count consists of the already certified $q$th batch,
the $m-q$ later batches, and the terminal return.  Hence the total interaction
count remains exactly $m+1$.  Additive and round coordinates sum; after the
lower/AESP arena is released, both total memory peaks are
$O(C(S^\star))$.  The exact total work is
\begin{equation}
 O\!\left(\mathfrak H_{<q}^{\rm tr}
       +C(S^\star)\log(2+C(S^\star))\right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-total-work}
\end{equation}

This is an exact-real, fixed-$m$, branch-seeded, strict-range,
delta/certificate comparison theorem.  For $q\geq3$ it extends the
lower-point certificate through actual growing faces $k\geq2$ without a
whole-vector zero reset.  It does not transport AESP momentum or
estimate-sequence energy, avoid candidate-row pre-exposure, avoid the
analysis-side margin logarithms, or prove a speedup.  Its full-face repeated
sweeps are explicitly present in $\mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm tr}$, and the suffix
explicitly pays the response build.  It proves no sublinear diagnostic,
margin-uniform or graph-uniform work, other range, seed, graph, or policy,
finite-precision or bit bound, PPR statement, or RPPR-to-PPR conversion.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Start with $a_0=0\leq x_0$.  At face $k$,
\Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-anchored-lower-transport} makes every observed
anchored demand a valid lower bound on its exact demand.  Fixed-face AESP
converges to $x_k$; the local lower map is continuous there and satisfies
$\mathcal L_{\widehat U_k}(x_k)=x_k$.  Since every exact canonical demand is
strict, the observed test eventually passes.  The sufficient neighborhood
and strict integer argument used in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-local-lower-stage-bound} give
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-stage-bound}.  After each of the
first $q-1$ replies,
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-padded-lower-transport} makes the padded endpoint
a valid lower anchor on the next face.  Induction proves the chronology and
the nondecreasing common-coordinate invariant.  Every member of
$\mathcal F_0$ has negative shifted load and positive coupling to $b_1$, so
the first strict lower-demand test forces $(y_0)_{b_1}>0$; the padding update
therefore gives the stated nonzero starts.  No response equation is solved in
this induction.

The seed row and the $q$ successively live boundary batches expose exactly
the rows of $\widehat U_q$, of degree volume
$\mathcal V_q^{\rm can}$, in $q+1$ first-exposure rounds.  Every AESP
oracle call, repeated old-row read, lower residual, positive part, anchored
maximum, and failed or successful sign sweep is covered by
$O(\mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm tr})$ adjacency/recurrence work.  Fresh-stage,
heap, boundary-completeness, strict-sign, certificate, and restart logic is
covered by $O(\mathfrak H_{<q}^{\rm tr})$ control work.  The retained rows,
admitted records, current face, transported anchor, and transcripts give the
displayed persistent peak; only the current numerical face and AESP
auxiliaries are temporary.  All explicit vector copies, zero padding, stage
states, and lower points are covered by
$O(\mathfrak D_{<q}^{\rm tr})$ materialization.  This proves every prefix
coordinate, including exact zero response work and the stated emissions.

At the $q$th interaction, consume the retained
$\mathcal F_{q-1}$ certificate, commit that batch, and append its paid common
layer record.  Build the balanced
native response directly from the current rows of $\widehat U_q$, settle the
face exactly, initialize the reporter's already-emitted membership directly
as $\widehat U_q\setminus\{b_1\}$, and retire the numerical epoch.  Schur
associativity makes this the reporter state at the actual checkpoint without
reconstructing any earlier face.  The remaining first-exposure rows are precisely
$S^\star\setminus\widehat U_q$; the un-emitted labels begin one layer earlier,
at $S^\star\setminus\widehat U_{q-1}$.  Applying the reviewed canonical
ledger from that state gives
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-post-eleven-vector}, including
the direct response build, remaining updates, replies, terminal
materialization, validation, and exact output.  Summation and peak
composition prove
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-total-work}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{An implicit lower heap removes the repeated diagnostic sweep}
\label{sec:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-heap}

The transported point in
\Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-anchored-lower-transport} was materialized in
full after every AESP endpoint.  That implementation is conservative but not
necessary.  The lower map has one global scalar and the caterpillar boundary
has only three active parent coordinates.  We can therefore maintain the
global scalar under local numerical writes and evaluate only the three
coordinates that a gate actually reads.  This section separates that
diagnostic improvement from the dense face-admission shock: a fresh AESP
epoch still materializes its warm start and initializes its numerical state
on the enlarged face.

Fix one canonical face $U=\widehat U_k$, a charged anchor
$0\leq a\leq x_k$, and a signed numerical point $z\in\mathbb R^U$.  For
$i\in U$ define
\begin{align}
 r_i(z)&:=\ell_{\rho,i}-(Q_{UU}z)_i,
 &
 h_i(z)&:=\frac{[-r_i(z)]_+}{\alpha\sqrt{d_i}},
 \notag\\
 \Delta(z)&:=\max_{i\in U}h_i(z),
 &
 \widehat y_i(a,z)&:=
 \max\left\{a_i,[z_i-\Delta(z)\sqrt{d_i}]_+\right\}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-state}
\end{align}
The state \texttt{ImplicitLowerHeap}$(U,a,z)$ stores $a,z,r$, an indexed
exact max-heap for the keys $h_i$, and the three validated
boundary-parent/coupling records.  It does \emph{not} store the vector
$\widehat y(a,z)$.

\begin{lemma}[Exact incremental lower-state diagnostic]
\label{lem:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-heap}
The state in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-state} represents exactly the
anchored lower point:
\begin{equation}
 \widehat y(a,z)=\mathcal T_{k,a}(z),
 \qquad
 0\leq\widehat y(a,z)\leq x_k.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-equality}
\end{equation}
For a validated row $v\in\mathcal F_k$ with unique active parent
$p=p_k(v)$ and $\beta_{k,v}:=-Q_{vp}>0$, its exact implicit query is
\begin{equation}
 \widehat g_{k,v}(a,z)
 :=\ell_{\rho,v}+\beta_{k,v}
 \max\left\{a_p,[z_p-\Delta(z)\sqrt{d_p}]_+\right\}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-query}
\end{equation}
It satisfies $\widehat g_{k,v}(a,z)\leq g_{k,v}$, so three strict positive
queries certify the complete canonical batch.

If one numerical coordinate is written as $z_j\leftarrow z_j+\delta$, then
the exact update is
\begin{equation}
 r_i\leftarrow r_i-Q_{ij}\delta
 \quad\text{for }i\in U\cap(\{j\}\cup N(j)),
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-update}
\end{equation}
followed by rekeying precisely those heap entries.  No other residual or key
changes.  With the row stencil already charged by the numerical host, the
additional diagnostic work is
\begin{equation}
 C_{\rm rec}+C_{\rm mat}=O(d_j+1),
 \qquad
 C_{\rm ctl}=O((d_j+1)\log(2+C(U))),
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-local-cost}
\end{equation}
and all adjacency, adjacency-round, interaction, preprocessing, response,
and emission increments are zero.  Without a supplied stencil, reading it
adds at most $d_j$ adjacency cells and one adjacency round.  Once the three
boundary rows have been exposed and validated, a complete query uses
$O(1)$ control/recurrence operations, $O(1)$ scratch, no materialized old
coordinate, and no new adjacency, interaction, response, or emission work.
The diagnostic state uses $\Theta(C(U))$ persistent cells and $O(1)$ scratch.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The maximum of the keys in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-state} is exactly
\begin{equation*}
 \frac1\alpha
 \left\lVert D_U^{-1/2}[\ell_{\rho,U}-Q_{UU}z]_-\right\rVert_\infty
 =\Delta_U^{\rm low}(z).
\end{equation*}
Thus each coordinate in the implicit query is exactly the corresponding
coordinate of
$a\vee\mathcal L_U(z)=\mathcal T_{k,a}(z)$.  Lowerness and the boundary
comparison now follow from
\Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-anchored-lower-transport}.  Under a coordinate
write, linearity gives
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-update}; the principal PageRank
matrix has no other nonzero entry in column $j$.  An indexed heap supports
each changed exact-real key in logarithmic control work, and its maximum is a
constant-time read.  A caterpillar boundary has three rows and one active
parent per row, proving the query and storage claims.
\end{proof}

There are two ways to initialize the state.  Given retained rows and only a
materialized $z$, one stored-row matrix pass and a linear-time heapify have
the exhaustive vector
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm ILH-init}(U))=
 \bigl(\vol(U),1,0,0,O(C(U)),O(\vol(U)),0,
       \Theta(C(U)),O(1),\Theta(C(U)),0\bigr).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-init-vector}
\end{equation}
If the numerical host already supplies the charged raw product $Q_{UU}z$,
the first two coordinates are zero and only the linear key/heap state is
written.  The relative-accuracy AESP implementation already computes its
shifted gradient at every bulk warm start; subtracting the known shift gives
$Q_{UU}z$, so the two states can be populated during the same charged pass.
Thereafter its coordinate stencil is also the stencil in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-update}.  The following composed
policy nevertheless charges one independent initialization pass on every
new face, so correctness does not rely on an unpriced shared scan.

For comparison, define \texttt{LiteralDenseLowerSweep} to be the specific
implementation that retains neither $r$ nor a maximum key and, at every
test, explicitly rebuilds $Q_{UU}z$, all of
$\mathcal L_U(z)$, and all of $a\vee\mathcal L_U(z)$.

\begin{proposition}[Cost of the named literal dense representation]
\label{prop:branch-caterpillar-literal-dense-lower-sweep-cost}
On $\widehat U_k$, $N_k+1$ invocations of
\texttt{LiteralDenseLowerSweep} have, by its declared interface,
\begin{equation}
 C_{\rm adj}=(N_k+1)\mathcal V_k^{\rm can},
 \qquad
 C_{\rm rec}=\Theta((N_k+1)\mathcal V_k^{\rm can}),
 \qquad
 C_{\rm mat}=\Theta((N_k+1)\card{\widehat U_k}).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-literal-dense-lower-sweep-cost}
\end{equation}
Across one invocation on each face $k=0,\ldots,q-1$, its row work is
$\sum_{k<q}\mathcal V_k^{\rm can}=3q^2$.  This is a representation- and
policy-specific accounting identity, not a lower bound for another lower
certificate or for every exact algorithm.  In particular,
\Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-heap} replaces the
$(N_k+1)$ full diagnostic passes by one initialization pass, local rekeys
piggybacked on the numerical writes, and $N_k+1$ constant-size queries.
\end{proposition}

We now compose the implicit state with the transported policy.  The
chronology, row-validation contract, and observed stopping rule are unchanged;
only the implementation of each lower test changes.

\begin{definition}[Implicit transported-lower canonical prefix]
\label{def:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-policy}
The policy
\texttt{ImplicitLowerHeap-}\allowbreak
\texttt{BC-AESP}$_{0:q-1}$, for $2\leq q\leq m$,
uses the following face epoch for $k=0,\ldots,q-1$.
\begin{enumerate}
 \item It exposes and validates exactly the current live boundary
       $\mathcal F_k$, retaining its three parent/coupling records.  At face
       entry it uses the explicitly materialized transported anchor $a_k$
       (with $a_0=0$), creates fresh
       AESP momentum, proximal, center, and estimate-sequence arrays, and
       performs and charges one stored-row pass to initialize
       \texttt{ImplicitLowerHeap}.  This is the face-admission/restart shock;
       no accelerated auxiliary record crosses the admission.
 \item It queries
       \cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-query} at the initial point
       and after every relative-accuracy AESP endpoint.  Every local
       coordinate write performs
       \cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-update} from the same paid
       row stencil.  Every bulk AESP replacement populates the raw lower
       keys during its already charged gradient pass.  If an implementation
       does not expose that product, the policy performs one additional
       stored-row product pass for that endpoint; its
       $T_k^{\rm ih}\mathcal V_k^{\rm can}$ charge is included in
       $O(\mathfrak A_k^{\rm ih})$ because $\Lambda_k^{\rm tr}\geq2$.
       No query materializes $\widehat y(a_k,z)$ or scans an old face.
 \item At the first all-positive query, if $k<q-1$, it emits and commits
       $\mathcal F_k$.  It then makes one explicit admission sweep: for each
       old coordinate it writes the value in
       \cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-state}, zero-pads the three
       new coordinates, retires every old AESP auxiliary and diagnostic cell,
       and uses this materialized vector as $a_{k+1}$.  This sweep is charged
       even if no AESP stage ran on the old face.
 \item On face $q-1$ it stops after the successful query and before the
       $q$th interaction.  The post phase commits that batch, directly builds
       and settles the native response on $\widehat U_q$, and continues the
       reviewed canonical reporter exactly as in
       \Cref{def:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-policy}.
\end{enumerate}
The row, admission, numerical, and label arenas at the handoff are therefore
exactly those in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-arenas}.  Candidate rows are
still pre-exposed, and no response is built in the prefix.
\end{definition}

For its observed execution, put
\begin{align}
 T_k^{\rm ih}
 &:=\min\left\{t\geq0:
   \widehat g_{k,v}(a_k,z_k^{(t)})>0
   \text{ for every }v\in\mathcal F_k\right\},
 \notag\\
 \Delta_{k,0}^{\rm ih}
 &:=F_{\rho,\widehat U_k}(a_k)
    -F_{\rho,\widehat U_k}(x_k).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-stopping-time}
\end{align}
Because the implicit query equals the materialized anchored query, this
stopping time is finite and obeys the same analysis-side alternatives:
$T_k^{\rm ih}=0$ when the initial gap is zero, while for positive gap
\begin{equation}
 T_k^{\rm ih}\leq
 1+\left\lfloor\frac{2}{\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}
 \log_+\!\left(
 \frac{4(\alpha+\sqrt3)^2\Delta_{k,0}^{\rm ih}}
      {\alpha^3\mu_k^2}
             \right)\right\rfloor.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-stage-bound}
\end{equation}
Neither quantity on the right is computed online.  Put
\begin{align}
 \mathcal N_k^{\rm can}&:=\card{\widehat U_k},
 &
 \mathfrak A_k^{\rm ih}
 &:=T_k^{\rm ih}\mathcal V_k^{\rm can}\Lambda_k^{\rm tr},
 \notag\\
 \mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm ih}&:=\sum_{k=0}^{q-1}\mathfrak A_k^{\rm ih},
 &
 \mathfrak Q_{<q}^{\rm ih}&:=\sum_{k=0}^{q-1}(T_k^{\rm ih}+1),
 \notag\\
 \mathfrak S_{<q}^{\rm row}
 &:=\sum_{k=0}^{q-1}\mathcal V_k^{\rm can}=3q^2,
 &
 \mathfrak S_{<q}^{\rm adm}
 &:=\sum_{k=1}^{q-1}\mathcal N_k^{\rm can}
   =\frac{(q-1)(3q+2)}2,
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-shock}\\
 \mathfrak D_{<q}^{\rm ih}
 &:=\mathcal C_q^{\rm can}
   +\mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm ih}
   +\mathfrak S_{<q}^{\rm row}
   +\mathfrak S_{<q}^{\rm adm}
   +\mathfrak Q_{<q}^{\rm ih},
 \notag\\
 \mathfrak H_{<q}^{\rm ih}
 &:=\mathcal C_q^{\rm can}
   +\mathfrak S_{<q}^{\rm row}
   +\mathfrak S_{<q}^{\rm adm}
   +\mathfrak Q_{<q}^{\rm ih}
   +\sum_{k=0}^{q-1}\mathfrak H_k^{\rm ih},
 \notag\\
 \mathfrak H_k^{\rm ih}
 &:= (1+\log(2+\mathcal C_k^{\rm can}))\mathfrak A_k^{\rm ih}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-budgets}
\end{align}
Here $\mathfrak S^{\rm row}$ charges one raw-residual/heap initialization on
every face, even when it could share a numerical scan, and
$\mathfrak S^{\rm adm}$ charges every old coordinate copied into a new warm
start.  Thus the quadratic growing-face shock is visible rather than hidden
inside the constant-time diagnostic queries.

\begin{theorem}[All-layer implicit-diagnostic response-free prefix]
\label{thm:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-handoff}
Fix $m\geq2$, $0<\alpha<1/2$,
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)$, and $2\leq q\leq m$.
The policy
\texttt{ImplicitLowerHeap-BC-AESP}$_{0:q-1}$ certifies the first $q$
canonical batches, commits the first $q-1$ without exact settlement, and
preserves the same coordinatewise lower anchor as
\texttt{TransportLower-BC-AESP}$_{0:q-1}$.  No diagnostic query scans an old
face or materializes the anchored lower vector.  Before the $q$th interaction
its complete vector is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm ILH,q}^{\rm pref})=\bigl(&
 \mathcal V_q^{\rm can}
   +O(\mathfrak S_{<q}^{\rm row}+\mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm ih}),
 2q+1+O(\mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm ih}),q-1,0,\\
 &O(\mathfrak H_{<q}^{\rm ih}),
 O(\mathfrak D_{<q}^{\rm ih}),0,
 O(\mathcal C_q^{\rm can}),
 O(\mathcal C_{q-1}^{\rm can}),
 O(\mathfrak D_{<q}^{\rm ih}),\Theta(q)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-prefix-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The first $q+1$ adjacency rounds expose the seed and $q$ live boundaries;
the next at most $q$ rounds are the explicitly charged face-initialization
passes, and the final term covers adaptive numerical row touches.  The
prefix has exactly $q-1$ interactions, emits the first $3(q-1)$ labels and
one constant-size reply per interaction, and has $C_{\rm resp}=0$ exactly.

After the handoff, the direct native response and canonical continuation have
the separately charged vector
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm ILH,q}^{\rm post})=\bigl(&
 \vol(S^\star\setminus\widehat U_q),m-q,m-q+2,0,
 O(C(S^\star)),0,\\
 &O(C(S^\star)+(m-q+1)\log(2+m)),
 \Theta(C(S^\star)),O(C(S^\star)),
 \Theta(\card{S^\star}),\\
 &\card{S^\star\setminus\widehat U_{q-1}}
  +\card{S^\star}+\Theta(m-q+2)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-post-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Thus the total interaction count is exactly $m+1$ and the exact total work is
\begin{equation}
 O\!\left(\mathfrak H_{<q}^{\rm ih}
       +C(S^\star)\log(2+C(S^\star))\right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-total-work}
\end{equation}
After releasing the numerical/diagnostic epoch, both total memory peaks are
$O(C(S^\star))$.

The improvement is exact but deliberately narrow.  It removes the extra
full-face construction of the lower vector at every sign test.  It does not
remove the AESP stage work, the one raw-product initialization per face, the
$\Theta(\sum_{k<q}\card{\widehat U_k})$ admission materialization, candidate
row exposure, or the margin and $\log(1/(1-2\alpha))$ terms.  In particular,
for $q=m$ the charged restart/admission shock is still $\Theta(m^2)$, so the
theorem proves no speedup and no subquadratic growing-face prefix.  It
transports no momentum or estimate-sequence energy.  The statement is
exact-real, fixed-$m$, branch-seeded, strict-range, and delta/certificate
scoped; it proves nothing for another seed, graph, range, or policy,
finite precision, bit complexity, PPR, graph-uniform work, or RPPR-to-PPR
conversion.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
At every endpoint,
\Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-heap} evaluates exactly the point
used by
\Cref{thm:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-handoff}.  Its strict signs
therefore certify the same canonical batch, the stopping-time proof and
analysis cap are unchanged, and the explicit successful admission sweep
writes exactly the same padded anchor.  Induction through the first $q-1$
admissions proves lowerness, chronology, and response freedom.

The seed and $q$ boundary batches first-expose precisely
$\widehat U_q$, of volume $\mathcal V_q^{\rm can}$.  One independent
diagnostic initialization pass on each of the $q$ numerical faces contributes
$\mathfrak S_{<q}^{\rm row}$ row work.  Relative-accuracy numerical stages
contribute $O(\mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm ih})$; the raw residual reuses each paid
local stencil or bulk gradient pass, while its exact rekeys contribute the
logarithmic control term in $\mathfrak H_{<q}^{\rm ih}$.  There are
$\mathfrak Q_{<q}^{\rm ih}$ constant-size sign queries.  The $q-1$ explicit
anchor writes and fresh auxiliary allocations contribute
$\mathfrak S_{<q}^{\rm adm}$ materialization cells.  Retained rows through
$\widehat U_q$ dominate persistent storage, while only the current face's
anchor, numerical arrays, residual, and heaps are temporary.  These facts
give every prefix coordinate in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-prefix-eleven-vector}.

The post phase starts from the identical paid common state as in
\Cref{thm:branch-caterpillar-transported-lower-handoff}: the first $q-1$
batches were emitted, the $q$th is certified, and rows through
$\widehat U_q$ are retained.  Its direct response build, remaining canonical
layers, terminal validation, exact output, emissions, and memory peaks are
therefore exactly
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-post-eleven-vector}.  Summing
additive work and both round coordinates, and taking storage peaks, proves
the remaining claims.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Incremental face transitions without old-face rebuilds}
\label{sec:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-transition}

The preceding policy deliberately rebuilt the raw product and copied the
lower anchor at every admission.  Neither operation is required by the lower
certificate.  The signed numerical endpoint itself may be zero-padded onto
the enlarged face: because every new coordinate is zero, every old residual
and heap key is then unchanged.  The lower anchor can be retained by lazy
successful-epoch tags.  This transports no AESP auxiliary or
estimate-sequence energy; a fresh numerical epoch is still initialized on
every face.

We first give an exact representation of the anchor.  Suppose the current
face is $\widehat U_k$.  For each successful earlier face $t<k$, let
$\delta_t$ be the heap maximum $\Delta(z_t)$ at the successful query.  The
state stores an append-only exact range-minimum structure for
$\delta_0,\ldots,\delta_{k-1}$.  For each $i\in\widehat U_k$, it also stores a
resolved lower value $\bar a_i$, a marker
$\sigma_i\in\{-1,0,\ldots,k-1\}$, and the current numerical value $z_i$.
Define
\begin{equation}
 \Phi_i(s,k):=
 \begin{cases}
  [z_i-\sqrt{d_i}\min_{s<t<k}\delta_t]_+,&
      \{t:s<t<k\}\ne\varnothing,\\
  0,&\{t:s<t<k\}=\varnothing,
 \end{cases}
 \qquad
 a_i^{\rm lazy}:=\max\{\bar a_i,\Phi_i(\sigma_i,k)\}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-lazy-anchor-state}
\end{equation}
Before the numerical host changes coordinate $i$, it \emph{flushes} that
coordinate: replace $\bar a_i$ by $a_i^{\rm lazy}$ and set
$\sigma_i=k-1$.  The numerical value has not changed since the preceding
flush, so all pending successful tags use the same $z_i$ and their
coordinatewise maximum is exactly the single range minimum in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-lazy-anchor-state}.  Multiple writes during the
same face need no further range query.  A dynamically balanced append-only
range-minimum tree uses $O(k+1)$ cells on face $k$ and
$O(\log(2+k))$ exact control/state work per tag or nonempty flush.  Its
allocation is charged prefix state, not ambient preprocessing.

\begin{lemma}[Exact lazy lower anchor]
\label{lem:branch-caterpillar-lazy-anchor}
Initialize $\bar a_{b_1}=0$ and $\sigma_{b_1}=-1$.  At a successful query on
$\widehat U_k$, first freeze the current scalar $\delta_k=\Delta(z_k)$ and
append it to the range-minimum structure.  For every newly admitted
$v\in\mathcal F_k$, append the records
\begin{equation}
 z_v=0,
 \qquad \bar a_v=0,
 \qquad \sigma_v=k.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-lazy-anchor-new-records}
\end{equation}
Then, without visiting any old coordinate, the state on
$\widehat U_{k+1}$ represents exactly
\begin{equation}
 a_{k+1}=\iota_k\!\left(
   a_k\vee\mathcal L_{\widehat U_k}(z_k)
                         \right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-lazy-anchor-transition}
\end{equation}
In particular $0\leq a_{k+1}\leq x_{k+1}$.  Every boundary parent queried on
face $k$ has lazy-anchor value zero: it is $b_1$ when $k=0$, and for $k>0$
it belongs to the immediately preceding batch $\mathcal F_{k-1}$ and has no
pending applicable tag.  Thus the named caterpillar gate never traverses an
old anchor chain.  The range-minimum representation nevertheless remains an
exact point-query representation of every interior anchor coordinate.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Assume the representation is exact on face $k$.  If coordinate $i$ has not
been flushed since marker $\sigma_i$, its numerical value is constant over
all pending successful epochs.  Hence
\[
 \max_{\sigma_i<t<k}
 [z_i-\delta_t\sqrt{d_i}]_+
 =[z_i-\sqrt{d_i}\min_{\sigma_i<t<k}\delta_t]_+.
\]
Flushing therefore preserves the represented value before changing $z_i$.
Appending $\delta_k$ adds precisely the coordinate
$[z_i-\delta_k\sqrt{d_i}]_+$ to the old maximum for every old $i$, while
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-lazy-anchor-new-records} gives zero on the new
batch.  This is exactly
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-lazy-anchor-transition}.  Its lowerness follows
from \Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-anchored-lower-transport}.  The parent claim
is the explicit caterpillar chronology: the two distinct parents of
$\mathcal F_k$ are $b_1$ at $k=0$, or newly admitted members of
$\mathcal F_{k-1}$ at $k>0$.
\end{proof}

The raw lower state has an equally local transition.  Let $r,h$ be the
residual and keys in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-state}, freeze the successful
endpoint $z_k$, and put $z^+:=\iota_kz_k$.  Candidate-row validation has
already retained, for each $v\in\mathcal F_k$, its unique active parent
$p_k(v)$ and coupling $\beta_{k,v}=-Q_{v,p_k(v)}>0$.

\begin{lemma}[Three-key exact face expansion]
\label{lem:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-transition}
For every $0\leq k<m$, the exact raw residual on
$\widehat U_{k+1}$ at $z^+$ is
\begin{equation}
 r_i^+=r_i\quad(i\in\widehat U_k),
 \qquad
 r_v^+=\ell_{\rho,v}+\beta_{k,v}z_{p_k(v)}
       \quad(v\in\mathcal F_k).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-residual}
\end{equation}
Thus all old heap entries are retained verbatim and exactly three new keys
$[-r_v^+]_+/(\alpha\sqrt{d_v})$ are inserted.  Combined with
\Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-lazy-anchor}, this operation, called
\texttt{FaceCarryLowerHeap}, represents the exact anchored lower map on the
new face without an old-row read, an old residual rekey, or an old anchor
copy.  Given the already retained candidate rows, one transition has
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{k\to k+1}^{\rm FC})=
 \bigl(0,0,0,0,O(\log(2+\mathcal C_{k+1}^{\rm can})),O(1),0,
 \Theta(\mathcal C_{k+1}^{\rm can}),O(1),
 O(\log(2+\mathcal C_{k+1}^{\rm can})),0\bigr).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-transition-vector}
\end{equation}
The memory coordinate is the peak exact state after the transition, not an
additional allocation of that size.  The materialization term charges the
three numerical/residual/key records, the anchor markers, and heap/range-tree
state writes.  If the three candidate rows have not yet been exposed, their
separate first-exposure increment is
$(\nu_k^{\rm can},1,0,0,O(1),0,0,O(1),O(1),O(1),0)$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The new numerical coordinates are zero.  Consequently the new columns make
no contribution to an old row, and its principal-block product and residual
are unchanged.  A new boundary row has exactly one neighbor in the old face;
all its other in-face coordinates are newly zero.  This gives
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-residual}.  Three exact heap
insertions and one range-minimum append have logarithmic control/state-write
cost, while the residual arithmetic and new scalar records are constant.
No adjacency cell is read because the candidate records were already paid.
The anchor statement is
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-lazy-anchor-transition}.
\end{proof}

This transition removes two representation shocks but not a third one.  To
make that distinction auditable, define \texttt{DenseFreshAESP} to be the
specific host interface that creates at least one explicit momentum,
proximal, center, and estimate-sequence record for every coordinate at the
start of every face epoch.  Retiring an old arena by an epoch pointer is
constant work, but populating the new arrays is not.

\begin{proposition}[Representation-specific fresh-auxiliary shock]
\label{prop:branch-caterpillar-fresh-auxiliary-shock}
Across the $q$ numerical faces $k=0,\ldots,q-1$, the declared
\texttt{DenseFreshAESP} interface writes
\begin{equation}
 \mathfrak X_{<q}^{\rm aux}
 :=\sum_{k=0}^{q-1}\mathcal N_k^{\rm can}
 =\frac{q(3q-1)}2
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-fresh-auxiliary-shock}
\end{equation}
coordinate records, up to the fixed constant number of named arrays.  This
is a definition-level accounting identity for that dense fresh interface,
not a lower bound for a lazy or different accelerated method.  By contrast,
$q$ applications of \texttt{FaceCarryLowerHeap} add only $3q$ raw
residual/key records, $3q$ zero numerical/anchor records, and $q$ successful
scalar tags, with $O(\sum_{k<q}\log(2+\mathcal C_{k+1}^{\rm can}))$ transition
control/state writes.  They perform no fresh old-face raw product and no
eager old-anchor materialization.  Nevertheless
$\mathfrak X_{<m}^{\rm aux}=\Theta(m^2)$, so this comparison alone proves no
subquadratic prefix.
\end{proposition}

We now compose the transition with fresh fixed-face numerical epochs.  The
endpoint $z_k$ rather than $a_{k+1}$ is the numerical warm start: it is
zero-padded and remains a permitted finite signed start.  This choice is what
makes the residual literally transportable.  It does not claim that any
accelerated energy survives.

\begin{definition}[Face-carried lower-heap canonical prefix]
\label{def:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-policy}
For $2\leq q\leq m$, define the policy
\[
 \texttt{FaceCarryLowerHeap-BC-AESP}_{0:q}.
\]
It proceeds as follows.
\begin{enumerate}
 \item Start with $z_0=a_0=0$ on $\widehat U_0$, initialize its one raw
       residual/key directly from the known shifted load, and create fresh
       dense AESP auxiliaries.  For $k=0,\ldots,q-1$, expose and validate
       exactly the live candidate rows $\mathcal F_k$.
 \item On $\widehat U_k$, run a fresh relative-accuracy AESP--CD epoch from
       the carried signed point $z_k$.  No momentum, proximal, center, or
       estimate-sequence record is reused.  Before the first write to a
       coordinate in that epoch, flush its lazy anchor.  Every local write
       applies
       \cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-update}; every sign test
       uses the exact heap maximum and the three boundary-parent queries.
 \item A bulk endpoint replacement first flushes every coordinate it
       overwrites.  If the numerical stage supplies its charged product
       $Q_{\widehat U_k\widehat U_k}z$, rebuild the residual keys during that
       same paid pass.  If it does not supply the product, make and charge one
       additional full stored-row product for that endpoint.  This extra work
       is included in the stage budget below because
       $\Lambda_k^{\rm tr}\geq2$; no product sharing is assumed for free.
 \item At the first all-positive query, emit and commit precisely
       $\mathcal F_k$ and its certificate reply.  Apply
       \texttt{FaceCarryLowerHeap}: append the successful scalar tag, append
       the three zero numerical/anchor entries, compute and insert the three
       new residual keys, and retire the old AESP auxiliary arena.  If
       $k<q-1$, allocate and initialize a fresh dense auxiliary arena on
       $\widehat U_{k+1}$.  If $k=q-1$, stop after the exact lower/heap state
       has reached $\widehat U_q$; no unused AESP arena is created there.
 \item The post phase retires the numerical/lower arena by its epoch pointer,
       directly builds and settles the native response from the retained rows
       of $\widehat U_q$, and resumes
       \emph{CaterpillarCanonicalLayerDelta}.  For $q=m$, the carried state is
       on the full support and the response build is followed only by
       terminal validation and return.
\end{enumerate}
At the response handoff,
\begin{equation}
 U_{\rm adm}=U_{\rm num}=E_{\rm row}=\widehat U_q,
 \qquad
 E_{\rm lab}=\widehat U_{\min\{q+1,m\}}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-arenas}
\end{equation}
The policy has committed all $q$ certified batches, unlike the preceding
pre-$q$th-interaction ledgers.  Candidate rows are still exposed before their
certificates; labels outside $\widehat U_q$ have no exposed row or numerical
coordinate.
\end{definition}

Let $T_k^{\rm fc}$ be the number of positive-gap AESP stages observed on face
$k$, with the initial query counted separately, and put
\begin{align}
 T_k^{\rm fc}
 &:=
 \min\{t\geq0:\widehat g_{k,v}(a_k,z_k^{(t)})>0
       \text{ for every }v\in\mathcal F_k\},
 \notag\\[-1.5ex]
 \Delta_{k,0}^{\rm fc}
 &:=F_{\rho,\widehat U_k}(z_k^{(0)})
       -F_{\rho,\widehat U_k}(x_k).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-stopping-time}
\end{align}
If $\Delta_{k,0}^{\rm fc}=0$, then $T_k^{\rm fc}=0$.  For positive initial
gap, the same lower-neighborhood argument gives
\begin{equation}
 T_k^{\rm fc}\leq
 1+\left\lfloor\frac{2}{\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}
 \log_+\!\left(
 \frac{4(\alpha+\sqrt3)^2\Delta_{k,0}^{\rm fc}}
      {\alpha^3\mu_k^2}
             \right)\right\rfloor.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-stage-bound}
\end{equation}
The gap and $\mu_k$ remain analysis-only.  Define
\begin{align}
 \mathfrak A_k^{\rm fc}
 &:=T_k^{\rm fc}\mathcal V_k^{\rm can}\Lambda_k^{\rm tr},
 &
 \mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm fc}
 &:=\sum_{k=0}^{q-1}\mathfrak A_k^{\rm fc},
 \notag\\
 \mathfrak Q_{<q}^{\rm fc}
 &:=\sum_{k=0}^{q-1}(T_k^{\rm fc}+1),
 &
 \mathfrak K_q^{\rm fc}
 &:=\sum_{k=0}^{q-1}
       \log(2+\mathcal C_{k+1}^{\rm can}),
 \notag\\
 \mathfrak D_{<q}^{\rm fc}
 &:=\mathcal C_q^{\rm can}+\mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm fc}
   +\mathfrak X_{<q}^{\rm aux}+\mathfrak Q_{<q}^{\rm fc}
   +\mathfrak K_q^{\rm fc},
 \notag\\
 \mathfrak H_{<q}^{\rm fc}
 &:=\mathcal C_q^{\rm can}+\mathfrak X_{<q}^{\rm aux}
   +\mathfrak Q_{<q}^{\rm fc}+\mathfrak K_q^{\rm fc}
   +\sum_{k=0}^{q-1}
    (1+\log(2+\mathcal C_k^{\rm can}))\mathfrak A_k^{\rm fc}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-budgets}
\end{align}
The $\mathfrak A$ terms charge all numerical row work, local residual
updates, anchor flushes, and every missing bulk endpoint product.  The
$\mathfrak X$ term separately charges fresh dense auxiliary initialization;
it is present even when every $T_k^{\rm fc}$ is zero.  The $\mathfrak K$ term
charges all three-key heap insertions and range-minimum tag updates.

\begin{theorem}[All-expansion face-carried response-free prefix]
\label{thm:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-handoff}
Fix $m\geq2$, $0<\alpha<1/2$,
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)$, and $2\leq q\leq m$.
Then \texttt{FaceCarry}\allowbreak\texttt{LowerHeap-}\allowbreak
\texttt{BC-AESP}$_{0:q}$ commits the first $q$
canonical batches without an exact response, carries the exact raw residual,
heap, signed numerical vector, and coordinatewise lower anchor across all
$q$ expansions $\widehat U_k\to\widehat U_{k+1}$, and reaches
$\widehat U_q$.  No face transition reads an old row, reinitializes an old
residual/key, or copies an old anchor coordinate.  Its complete prefix vector
is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm FC,q}^{\rm pref})=\bigl(&
 \mathcal V_q^{\rm can}+O(\mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm fc}),
 q+1+O(\mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm fc}),q,0,
 O(\mathfrak H_{<q}^{\rm fc}),\\
 &O(\mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm fc}+\mathfrak Q_{<q}^{\rm fc}+q),0,
 O(\mathcal C_q^{\rm can}),O(\mathcal C_{q-1}^{\rm can}),
 O(\mathfrak D_{<q}^{\rm fc}),3q+\Theta(q)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-prefix-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Here $C_{\rm resp}=0$ exactly.  The emission coordinate contains the $3q$
distinct delta labels and one constant-size certificate reply for each of the
$q$ interactions.  The first $q+1$ adjacency rounds expose the seed and the
$q$ candidate batches; numerical endpoint products not supplied by the host
are charged as additional stage rounds inside the displayed bound.  There is
no admission-time old-face adjacency round.

After the handoff, the retained rows through $\widehat U_q$ are reused.  The
direct native response build and remaining canonical execution have vector
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm FC,q}^{\rm post})=\bigl(&
 \vol(S^\star\setminus\widehat U_q),m-q,m-q+1,0,
 O(C(S^\star)),0,\\
 &O(C(S^\star)+(m-q)\log(2+m)),
 \Theta(C(S^\star)),O(C(S^\star)),
 \Theta(\card{S^\star}),\\
 &\card{S^\star\setminus\widehat U_q}
   +\card{S^\star}+\Theta(m-q+1)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-post-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Consequently the total interaction count is exact, whereas charged numerical
row touches and missing endpoint products remain in the adjacency-round
coordinate:
\begin{equation}
 R_{\rm int}^{\rm total}=m+1,
 \qquad
 R_{\rm adj}^{\rm total}
 =m+1+O(\mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm fc}).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-total-rounds}
\end{equation}
The first-exposure contribution to $R_{\rm adj}^{\rm total}$ is exactly
$m+1$; the displayed additive term is not silently discarded.  Additive
coordinates sum, and after the numerical epoch is released both total memory
peaks are $O(C(S^\star))$.  The exact total work is
\begin{equation}
 O\!\left(\mathfrak H_{<q}^{\rm fc}
       +C(S^\star)\log(2+C(S^\star))\right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-total-work}
\end{equation}

The construction removes the Round-017 raw-product and explicit-anchor-copy
transition shocks.  It does not remove candidate-row pre-exposure, fresh
dense AESP allocation, any numerical stage, missing bulk endpoint product,
analysis-side margin, or the relative-oracle
$\log(1/(1-2\alpha))$ factor.  In particular
$\mathfrak X_{<m}^{\rm aux}=\Theta(m^2)$, so the theorem proves neither a
speedup nor a subquadratic growing-face prefix.  No AESP momentum, proximal,
center, estimate-sequence energy, or objective-gap certificate crosses an
admission.  Coordinatewise order of the represented lower anchor is not a
scalar stagewise-energy or potential-monotonicity statement and gives no
reserve for a later production/reset chain.  The result is exact-real,
fixed-$m$, branch-seeded, strict-range,
RPPR, and delta/certificate specific.  It makes no finite-precision, bit,
PPR, RPPR-to-PPR, other-seed, other-policy, other-graph, graph-uniform, or
margin-uniform claim.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
At face zero the one residual/key is exact and the zero anchor is lower.  On
face $k$, \Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-implicit-lower-heap} makes every query
the exact anchored lower query.  Fixed-face AESP from the finite signed
carried point converges to $x_k$; continuity of the lower map and strictness
of the exact canonical demands imply a finite successful query.  The same
sufficient neighborhood as before proves
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-stage-bound}.  The successful
lower point is below $x_k$, and
\Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-lazy-anchor} represents its zero padding exactly
below $x_{k+1}$.  Simultaneously,
\Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-transition} makes the padded
signed point's residual and heap exact on the enlarged face.  Induction for
$k=0,\ldots,q-1$ proves all $q$ certifications, interactions, and exact
transitions, including the terminal $k=m-1$ expansion when $q=m$.  No
restricted response equation is solved.

The seed and $q$ candidate batches expose exactly $\widehat U_q$, with degree
volume $\mathcal V_q^{\rm can}$.  Numerical stages, local recurrence, anchor
flushes, heap rekeys, and any unshared bulk product are covered by
$\mathfrak A_{<q}^{\rm fc}$ and its logarithmic control multiplier.  Initial
queries and endpoint queries contribute $\mathfrak Q_{<q}^{\rm fc}$.
The exact $q$ three-key/tag transitions contribute
$\mathfrak K_q^{\rm fc}$ but no old-row or old-anchor pass.  Fresh dense
auxiliary arrays contribute $\mathfrak X_{<q}^{\rm aux}$ and are retired at
each interaction.  The lazy anchor uses one resolved value and marker per
coordinate plus an $O(q)$ range-minimum tree, so together with current
$z,r$, the indexed heap, retained rows, and common records its persistent
peak is $O(\mathcal C_q^{\rm can})$; only one face's fresh AESP arena is live,
giving the stated temporary peak.  This proves every prefix coordinate in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-prefix-eleven-vector}.

After the $q$th reply the common support is $\widehat U_q$, all of its rows
are retained, and none of its response cells has been credited.  A direct
paid response build therefore starts at that actual checkpoint, settles it,
and retires the lower/numerical arena.  There remain $m-q$ canonical batches
and one terminal return, exactly the rows and labels in
$S^\star\setminus\widehat U_q$.  The reviewed response ledger gives
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-post-eleven-vector}.  Its exact
first-exposure, interaction, and delta-label partitions combine with the
prefix as
\[
 (q+1)+(m-q)=m+1,
 \qquad q+(m-q+1)=m+1,
 \qquad 3q+3(m-q)=3m.
\]
The first equality counts only first-exposure adjacency rounds.  Adding the
explicit numerical-stage term from the prefix vector gives
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-total-rounds}; only the second
equality is an exact statement about the full interaction-round coordinate.
Summing additive work and taking sequential memory peaks proves the remaining
claims.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Sparse auxiliary appends and the first exact estimate shock}
\label{sec:branch-caterpillar-auxiliary-face-shock}

The dense fresh-array term in
\Cref{prop:branch-caterpillar-fresh-auxiliary-shock} is an interface cost,
not a mathematical necessity.  We now test the smallest favorable
accelerated-state transition: the old face is exactly settled and its
momentum is zero.  Even there, one part of the state carries sparsely and one
part provably does not carry with zero shock.

The definitions below are exactly the composite/AESP definitions imported
from \path{aesp_cd_l1_rppr}.  In that note,
\path{eq:aesp-cd-subproblem} and
\path{eq:aesp-cd-relative-parameters} give the shift, smoothness, and
condition ratio; the paragraph following
\path{eq:aesp-cd-moreau-envelope} gives the envelope parameters.  Thus
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \kappa_{\rm A}&:=1-2\alpha,
 &L_{\rm A}&:=2(1-\alpha),
 &\chi_{\rm A}&:=\frac{\alpha}{1-\alpha},\\
 \mu_{\rm E}&:=\kappa_{\rm A}\chi_{\rm A}
 =\frac{\alpha(1-2\alpha)}{1-\alpha}.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-auxiliary-parameters}
\end{equation}
The imported \path{eq:aesp-cd-smoothed-start}, on a fixed face $U$ at
center $y$, is
\begin{equation}
 u_U(y):=\prox_{g_{\rho,U}/L_{\rm A}}
 \left(y-\frac{\nabla f_{t,U}(y)}{L_{\rm A}}\right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-auxiliary-proximal-start}
\end{equation}
The estimate point and potential are the exact objects in
\emph{eq:aesp-cd-momentum-states} and
\emph{eq:aesp-cd-potential-defect}.  To make their face dependence
explicit, define the restricted envelope
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{Env}_k(y):=
 \min_{w\in\mathbb R^{\widehat U_k}}
 \left\{F_{\rho,\widehat U_k}(w)
       +\frac{\kappa_{\rm A}}2\norm{w-y}_2^2\right\}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-restricted-envelope}
\end{equation}
and the exact analytical estimate certificate
\begin{equation}
 \Psi_k(x,v):=
 \operatorname{Env}_k(x)-F_{\rho,\widehat U_k}(x_k)
 +\frac{\mu_{\rm E}}2\norm{v-x_k}_2^2.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-restricted-estimate-certificate}
\end{equation}
The quantity $\Psi_k$ is an analysis certificate because it contains the
unknown restricted optimum.  It is not a free online scalar.

At a settled zero-momentum checkpoint put the previous and current primal
points equal to $x_k$.  The AESP center is then $y_k=x_k$, the estimate point
is $v_k^{\rm es}=x_k$, the explicit momentum is zero, and
$u_{\widehat U_k}(x_k)=x_k$.  Consequently
\begin{equation}
 \Psi_k(x_k,x_k)=0.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-old-zero-estimate}
\end{equation}
The coordinate records proposed for enlarged-face initialization are therefore
the direct zero-padding of the imported momentum identities,
\begin{equation}
 y_{k+1}^{\rm init}=\iota_kx_k,
 \qquad
 d_{k+1}^{\rm init}=\iota_k0=0,
 \qquad
 v_{k+1}^{{\rm es},{\rm init}}=\iota_kx_k.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-settled-auxiliary-state-append}
\end{equation}
This is an exact algebraic transfer of three arrays.  It is not yet a claim
that the old estimate-sequence proof continues on the enlarged objective.

\begin{lemma}[Exact proximal append at a settled canonical face]
\label{lem:branch-caterpillar-settled-proximal-append}
Fix $m\geq2$, $0<\alpha<1/2$, and
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)$.  For every $0\leq k<m$, zero-pad the
settled center to $y^+:=\iota_kx_k$.  Then the standard composite proximal
start on the enlarged face is
\begin{equation}
 u_{\widehat U_{k+1}}(y^+)
 =\iota_kx_k+
   \frac1{L_{\rm A}}\sum_{v\in\mathcal F_k}g_{k,v}e_v,
 \qquad g_{k,v}>0.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-settled-proximal-append}
\end{equation}
Thus every old proximal coordinate is retained verbatim, but the three new
proximal coordinates are strictly positive.  Appending zeros to the old
proximal array is not the imported warm-start map.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
At the raw center the quadratic shift cancels, exactly as in the imported
\emph{lem:aesp-cd-center-cancel}.  Every coordinate of $x_k$ on
$\widehat U_k$ is strictly positive, so its restricted KKT equality is
\[
 (Qx_k-b)_i=-\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_i}
 \qquad(i\in\widehat U_k).
\]
Soft thresholding in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-auxiliary-proximal-start} therefore returns
$(x_k)_i$ on every old coordinate.  A new $v\in\mathcal F_k$ has center
coordinate zero and only its retained parent contributes to the old-face
product.  Its positive soft-thresholded value is
\[
 \frac{[b_v-(Q\iota_kx_k)_v-\alpha\rho\sqrt{d_v}]_+}
      {L_{\rm A}}
 =\frac{g_{k,v}}{L_{\rm A}}.
\]
The imported strict canonical chronology gives $g_{k,v}>0$ for all three
vertices, proving the formula.
\end{proof}

The proximal writes are not the first obstruction to sparse transport: there
are only three of them.  The first obstruction to carrying the old
accelerated certificate unchanged is the face change itself.  Define the
exact objective shock and analytical estimate shock
\begin{align}
 \Delta_k^{\rm face}
 &:=F_{\rho,\widehat U_{k+1}}(\iota_kx_k)
    -F_{\rho,\widehat U_{k+1}}(x_{k+1}),
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-objective-face-shock}\\
 \Sigma_k^{\rm es}
 &:=\Psi_{k+1}(\iota_kx_k,\iota_kx_k).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-estimate-face-shock}
\end{align}

\begin{proposition}[Positive analytical estimate shock under zero padding]
\label{prop:branch-caterpillar-zero-estimate-carry-fails}
Under the hypotheses of
\Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-settled-proximal-append}, every canonical
admission satisfies
\begin{align}
 \Delta_k^{\rm face}
 &=\frac12\norm{x_{k+1}-\iota_kx_k}_{
                 Q_{\widehat U_{k+1}\widehat U_{k+1}}}^2>0,
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-objective-face-shock-identity}\\
 \Sigma_k^{\rm es}
 &=\operatorname{Env}_{k+1}(\iota_kx_k)
   -F_{\rho,\widehat U_{k+1}}(x_{k+1})
   +\frac{\mu_{\rm E}}2
      \norm{\iota_kx_k-x_{k+1}}_2^2
 \notag\\
 &\geq\frac{\mu_{\rm E}}2
      \sum_{v\in\mathcal F_k}(x_{k+1})_v^2>0.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-estimate-face-shock-positive}
\end{align}
Hence the old exact certificate
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-old-zero-estimate} cannot be reused as a zero
certificate on the new face.  A correct interface must either restart the
estimate-sequence proof at $\widehat U_{k+1}$ or explicitly introduce and
charge an upper bound for the positive analytical shock
$\Sigma_k^{\rm es}$.  The proposition is not a lower bound on the work of
computing that bound and is not an obstruction to another accelerated
potential.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
On the positive orthant the restricted RPPR objective is a quadratic with
linear term.  The enlarged restricted optimum is strictly positive on
$\mathcal F_k$ and satisfies its full KKT equality.  Expanding the quadratic
about $x_{k+1}$ gives the first identity.  It is strict because
$\iota_kx_k$ is zero on $\mathcal F_k$ whereas $x_{k+1}$ is positive there.

The envelope term minus the new optimum is nonnegative because the envelope
minimizes the unshifted objective plus a nonnegative quadratic.  The remaining
squared-distance term is bounded below by its three new-coordinate summands.
Since $\mu_{\rm E}>0$ and those coordinates are strictly positive, the
estimate shock is strict.  Together with
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-old-zero-estimate}, this rules out unchanged
zero-shock carry.
\end{proof}

To separate coordinate transport from this analytical reset, define
\texttt{SettledAuxAppend} as the following representation-level operation.
Incremental to an already paid zero-padding of the numerical vector and to
already retained candidate rows, it retains every old auxiliary cell,
appends three zeros to each explicit center, momentum, and estimate-point
array, appends the three positive values in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-settled-proximal-append} to the proximal array,
and writes one estimate-reset epoch marker.  With one explicit record for
each of those four named arrays, this is exactly twelve new auxiliary
coordinate records and no old auxiliary-coordinate write.  It reads no old
row, applies no old-face product, makes no adjacency query, and invokes no
response.  Its incremental vector is
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{k\to k+1}^{\rm aux})=
 \bigl(0,0,0,0,O(1),O(1),0,
 \Theta(\mathcal C_{k+1}^{\rm can}),O(1),12+\Theta(1),0\bigr).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-settled-auxiliary-transition-vector}
\end{equation}
Here the persistent coordinate is the peak after transition, not a new
linear allocation.  The constant recurrence work computes the three
retained-row demands and divides by $L_{\rm A}$; the materialization
coordinate contains the twelve appends and the scalar reset record.  If the
candidate rows are not retained, their separate first-exposure increment is
the one stated in
\Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-incremental-face-transition}.  If an
implementation instead materializes a numerical value of
$\Sigma_k^{\rm es}$, every response, solve, query, and write used to obtain it
must be added; none is credited in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-settled-auxiliary-transition-vector}.

For completeness, the first nonzero checkpoint gives a concrete paid audit.

\noindent
The policy \emph{FirstAuxShock-BC-AESP}$_{1\to2}$ first runs the existing
response-native \texttt{FirstLayer-BC}$_1$ execution, materializes a paid
numerical copy of $x_1$, and initializes one explicit center, momentum,
estimate-point, and proximal record per coordinate consistently with that
settled state.  It scans the three rows in $\mathcal F_1$ and commits that
exact strict canonical batch.  It then zero-pads the numerical point, applies
\texttt{SettledAuxAppend}, records the reset marker rather than
claiming a numerical value for the analytical shock, and stops before the
first enlarged-face numerical update.  Thus it audits an actual state
transition but performs no accelerated stage.

\begin{theorem}[First-layer auxiliary append and exact shock STOP]
\label{thm:branch-caterpillar-first-auxiliary-shock-handoff}
Fix $m\geq2$, $0<\alpha<1/2$, and
$0<\rho<\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha)$.  The prefix
\texttt{FirstAuxShock-BC-AESP}$_{1\to2}$ reaches
$U_{\rm adm}=U_{\rm num}=E_{\rm row}=\widehat U_2$, carries every old
coordinate of its explicit accelerated arrays without rewrite, appends the
twelve exact auxiliary records above, and resets only the estimate
certificate.  Its complete vector is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm aux,1\to2}^{\rm pref})=
 \bigl(&9+\nu_1,3,2,0,O(1),O(1),O(1),
 O(C_2),O(C_2),\Theta(C_2),6+\Theta(2)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-auxiliary-prefix-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The response coordinate is the already charged constant-size native response
that settled $x_1$; it does not compute $x_2$ or
$\Sigma_1^{\rm es}$.  The six output labels and two constant-size replies
are the first two canonical delta/certificate interactions.

Discarding the audit arrays by one epoch pointer and directly building the
native response on $\widehat U_2$ gives the post vector
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm aux,1\to2}^{\rm post})=
 \bigl(&\vol(S^\star\setminus\widehat U_2),m-2,m-1,0,
 O(C(S^\star)),0,\\
 &O(C(S^\star)+(m-2)\log(2+m)),
 \Theta(C(S^\star)),O(C(S^\star)),\Theta(\card{S^\star}),\\
 &\card{S^\star\setminus\widehat U_2}
  +\card{S^\star}+\Theta(m-1)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-auxiliary-post-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The total interaction and first-exposure round counts are both exactly
$m+1$, and total exact-cell work is
\begin{equation}
 O\!\left(C(S^\star)\log(2+C(S^\star))\right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-auxiliary-total-work}
\end{equation}

This theorem is a response-assisted, settled-state transition audit.  Its GO
is that an explicitly dense old auxiliary arena need not be repopulated:
only a constant number of new vector cells are written.  Its STOP is that the
imported proximal array is not an all-zero append and the old zero analytical
estimate certificate is false on the enlarged face.  Because the execution
uses exact native settlement before the tested admission, resets the
estimate proof, runs no accelerated stage after the append, and hands off to
the native response, it proves no acceleration speedup, no subquadratic
automatically exploring prefix, no amortization of
$\Sigma_k^{\rm es}$, and no lower bound for other representations or
potentials.  All fixed-family, strict-range, exact-real, branch-seed,
delta-interface, non-PPR, and non-finite-precision restrictions remain.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The vector for \texttt{FirstLayer-BC}$_1$ is
$(9,2,1,0,O(1),0,O(1),O(1),O(1),\Theta(1),\Theta(1))$.
The additional scan of $\mathcal F_1$ costs $\nu_1$ adjacency cells and one
round; the exact $x_1$ demands certify one additional interaction with three
delta labels and one reply.  Initializing the four constant-size arrays and
the paid primal copy, then applying \texttt{SettledAuxAppend}, costs constant
recurrence/control work and $\Theta(C_2)$ state/materialization cells, with no
new response.  This proves
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-auxiliary-prefix-eleven-vector} and, by the
two preceding results, the exact state and reset claims.

After the second reply, rows through $\widehat U_2$ are retained and no
response on that face has been credited.  The reviewed direct suffix at
$q=2$ therefore gives
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-auxiliary-post-eleven-vector}.  The round
partitions are
\[
 3+(m-2)=m+1,
 \qquad
 2+(m-1)=m+1.
\]
Summing additive coordinates and taking sequential memory peaks gives
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-auxiliary-total-work}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A charged nonsettled admission and one continuation stage}
\label{sec:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-continuation}

The settled audit above isolates the first unavoidable estimate shock, but it
does not show that an accelerated host can cross a face and execute another
stage.  We now give the smallest such execution.  It is deliberately local to
the first canonical admission.  The price of making every quantity explicit
is a slightly smaller, but still positive, parameter range:
\begin{equation}
 m\geq2,
 \qquad 0<\alpha<\frac12,
 \qquad
 0<\rho<\min\{\rho_{\rm can}(m,\alpha),1/30\}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-range}
\end{equation}

Recall $a_0=(1+\alpha)/2$, $h_0=(1-\alpha)/2$, and
$\kappa_{\rm A}=1-2\alpha$, so that
$a_0+\kappa_{\rm A}=3h_0$.  Starting from zero on
$\widehat U_0=\{b_1\}$, one exact shifted composite stage centered at zero
has the strictly positive endpoint
\begin{equation}
 \widehat x^{\rm ns}
 :=\theta_0 e_{b_1},
 \qquad
 \theta_0:=
 \frac{\alpha(1-3\rho)}{\sqrt3(a_0+\kappa_{\rm A})}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-nonsettled-state}
\end{equation}
The previous point is zero.  Hence the carried momentum, extrapolated center,
and imported estimate point are
\begin{equation}
 d^{\rm ns}=\widehat x^{\rm ns},
 \qquad
 y^{\rm ns}=\widehat x^{\rm ns}+\beta_{\rm A}d^{\rm ns},
 \qquad
 v^{{\rm es},{\rm ns}}
 =\widehat x^{\rm ns}
  +\left(\frac1{\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}}}-1\right)d^{\rm ns},
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-nonsettled-auxiliary-state}
\end{equation}
where
$\beta_{\rm A}=(1-\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}})/(1+\sqrt{\chi_{\rm A}})>0$.
This is not a disguised settled checkpoint: the exact restricted RPPR point
has the same numerator as \cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-nonsettled-state}
and denominator $a_0<a_0+\kappa_{\rm A}$.

\begin{lemma}[One-stage nonsettled first canonical admission]
\label{lem:branch-caterpillar-first-nonsettled-admission}
Under \cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-range}, the three exact demands
at $\widehat x^{\rm ns}$ are
\begin{align}
 (\ell_\rho-Q\widehat x^{\rm ns})_{b_2}
 &=\frac{\sqrt3\alpha(1-30\rho)}{27}>0,
 \notag\\
 (\ell_\rho-Q\widehat x^{\rm ns})_{a_1}
 &=\frac{\sqrt2\alpha(1-21\rho)}{18}>0,
 \notag\\
 (\ell_\rho-Q\widehat x^{\rm ns})_{r_1}
 &=\frac{\alpha(1-12\rho)}9>0.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-nonsettled-margin}
\end{align}
Thus the paid all-violations interaction commits exactly
$\mathcal F_0=\{b_2,a_1,r_1\}$.  Moreover
$0<\widehat x^{\rm ns}<x_0$ coordinatewise, so these are conservative
one-sided demands, not signs inferred from an uncontrolled signed point.
No analysis-side $\mu_0$ or future margin table is used.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The shifted one-coordinate KKT equation is
$(a_0+\kappa_{\rm A})\theta_0
 =\alpha(1-3\rho)/\sqrt3$, proving the endpoint formula and
$0<\widehat x^{\rm ns}<x_0$.  The couplings from $b_1$ to
$b_2,a_1,r_1$ are respectively $h_0/3,h_0/\sqrt6,h_0/\sqrt3$.
Substitution of $a_0+\kappa_{\rm A}=3h_0$ gives
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-nonsettled-margin}.  The first expression
is the restrictive one and is strict exactly when $\rho<1/30$; the other two
are then strict as well.  Since the imported canonical chronology has this
same complete first batch, the charged reply commits precisely $\mathcal F_0$.
\end{proof}

The four explicit arrays also have an exact sparse transition.  Before the
interaction, form $y^{\rm ns}$ and compute the old-face value
$u_{\widehat U_0}(y^{\rm ns})$ from a paid product.  Zero padding does not
change any old row product.  Therefore, after commitment,
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 y^+&=\iota_0y^{\rm ns},
 &d^+&=\iota_0d^{\rm ns},
 &v^+&=\iota_0v^{{\rm es},{\rm ns}},\\
 u_{\widehat U_1}(y^+)|_{\widehat U_0}
 &=u_{\widehat U_0}(y^{\rm ns}),
 &u_{\widehat U_1}(y^+)_v
 &=\frac{(\ell_\rho-Qy^+)_v}{L_{\rm A}}>0
 \quad(v\in\mathcal F_0).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-auxiliary-append}
\end{equation}
The strict inequality follows because $y^{\rm ns}_{b_1}>
\widehat x^{\rm ns}_{b_1}$ and every boundary coupling is positive.  Thus,
after the old proximal cell has been computed and charged, the transition
again appends exactly twelve cells: three in each of the center, momentum,
estimate-point, and proximal arrays.  Unlike the settled audit, the needed
old-face and enlarged-face products are not zero-work identities.

We next register rather than hide the new-face estimate energy.  Let
$\kappa_{\rho,\widehat U_1}(w)$ be the imported minimum-norm RPPR KKT vector
on the first enlarged face and put
\begin{align}
 K_{\rm ns}^2
 &:=\norm{\kappa_{\rho,\widehat U_1}
              (\iota_0\widehat x^{\rm ns})}_2^2,
 &D_{\rm ns}^2
 &:=\norm{\iota_0v^{{\rm es},{\rm ns}}
              -\iota_0\widehat x^{\rm ns}}_2^2,
 \notag\\
 B_{\rm ns}
 &:=\frac{K_{\rm ns}^2}{2\alpha}
   +\mu_{\rm E}D_{\rm ns}^2
   +\frac{\mu_{\rm E}}{\alpha^2}K_{\rm ns}^2.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-shock-budget}
\end{align}
All three scalars are computed from two explicitly charged stored-row
products, the carried state, and exact norm accumulators.  They contain no
unknown optimum and are not free analysis advice.

\begin{lemma}[Computable KKT reset for the nonsettled estimate shock]
\label{lem:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-shock-reset}
Under \cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-range},
\begin{equation}
 0<\Psi_1(\iota_0\widehat x^{\rm ns},
          \iota_0v^{{\rm es},{\rm ns}})
 \leq B_{\rm ns}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-shock-bound}
\end{equation}
Consequently $B_{\rm ns}$ is a valid fresh enlarged-face estimate budget.
It is not an amortization against the old-face estimate value and does not
justify reusing the old certificate unchanged.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Write $\bar x=\iota_0\widehat x^{\rm ns}$,
$\bar v=\iota_0v^{{\rm es},{\rm ns}}$, and let $x_1$ be the enlarged-face
restricted optimum.  The envelope term is nonnegative above the optimum,
while $\bar v$ is zero on $\mathcal F_0$ and every coordinate of $x_1$ there
is strictly positive.  Hence
\[
 \Psi_1(\bar x,\bar v)
 \geq\frac{\mu_{\rm E}}2
      \sum_{v\in\mathcal F_0}(x_1)_v^2>0.
\]
For the upper bound, strong convexity and the minimum-norm subgradient give
\[
 F_{\rho,\widehat U_1}(\bar x)-F_{\rho,\widehat U_1}(x_1)
 \leq\frac{K_{\rm ns}^2}{2\alpha},
 \qquad
 \norm{\bar x-x_1}_2\leq\frac{K_{\rm ns}}\alpha.
\]
Also $\operatorname{Env}_1(\bar x)\leq
F_{\rho,\widehat U_1}(\bar x)$.  The triangle inequality followed by
$(a+b)^2\leq2a^2+2b^2$ yields
\[
 \norm{\bar v-x_1}_2^2
 \leq2D_{\rm ns}^2+\frac{2K_{\rm ns}^2}{\alpha^2}.
\]
Substitution in \cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-restricted-estimate-certificate}
proves the displayed bound.
\end{proof}

Incremental to the paid candidate rows and the ordinary old-face stage-state
writes, call the two-product, twelve-append, one-budget operation above
\texttt{NonsettledShockRegister}.  With
$\mathcal V_1=\vol(\widehat U_1)=9$ and $C_1=C(\widehat U_1)=13$, its complete
vector is
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{0\to1}^{\rm NS})=
 \bigl(2\mathcal V_1,2,0,0,O(1),\Theta(\mathcal V_1),0,
 \Theta(C_1),O(C_1),12+\Theta(1),0\bigr).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-transition-vector}
\end{equation}
One product forms the enlarged-face KKT vector at the carried primal point;
the other forms the next shifted proximal warm start at the extrapolated
center.  This conservative ledger assumes no free product sharing.  The
materialization coordinate contains the twelve appended cells, the exact
$K_{\rm ns}^2,D_{\rm ns}^2,B_{\rm ns}$ accumulators, and an estimate-reset
marker.  No response call or external emission is hidden.

Define \texttt{FirstNonsettled-BC-AESP}$_{0\to1}^{+1}$ as follows.  Expose
exactly the seed row and the live rows $\mathcal F_0$, run the exact
one-coordinate shifted stage in
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-first-nonsettled-state}, certify and commit
$\mathcal F_0$, and execute \texttt{NonsettledShockRegister}.  Then run
exactly one imported relative-accuracy AESP--CD stage on $\widehat U_1$ from
the carried extrapolated center $y^+$.  This is a genuine accelerated
continuation: $d^+\ne0$ and $\beta_{\rm A}>0$.  Its proximal product, every
inner coordinate row, KKT-mass update, stopping test, state write, and the
explicit oracle factor
\begin{equation}
 L_1^{\rm rel}=2+\frac34\log_+\!\left(
 \frac{144(1-\alpha)}{\delta_{\rm A}(1-2\alpha)}\right)
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-oracle-log}
\end{equation}
are charged.  The stage is valid under the imported relative criterion, but
we make no claim that it contracts $B_{\rm ns}$ across the changed face.
After that one stage, retire the accelerated arena, build the native response
directly on the retained rows of $\widehat U_1$, and finish the canonical
suffix.

Put
\begin{align}
 \mathfrak A_{\rm ns}
 &:=\mathcal V_0+2\mathcal V_1+\mathcal V_1L_1^{\rm rel},
 &\mathfrak D_{\rm ns}&:=C_1+\mathfrak A_{\rm ns},
 \notag\\
 \mathfrak H_{\rm ns}
 &:=C_1+(1+\log(2+C_1))\mathfrak A_{\rm ns}.
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-budgets}
\end{align}
Here $\mathcal V_0=3$.  The first term charges the initial exact shifted
row; the second charges both transition products; the last retains the full
relative-oracle logarithm.  No missing endpoint product is assumed free.

\begin{theorem}[Charged nonsettled transition, one-stage GO, and rate STOP]
\label{thm:branch-caterpillar-first-nonsettled-continuation}
Under \cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-range},
\texttt{FirstNonsettled-BC-AESP}$_{0\to1}^{+1}$ makes one actual canonical
admission from a strictly nonoptimal outer point, registers a computable
upper bound for the positive enlarged-face estimate certificate, and runs
one extrapolated relative-accuracy stage afterward.  Immediately before the
native response build its complete prefix vector is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm NS,+1}^{\rm pref})=\bigl(&
 9+O(\mathfrak A_{\rm ns}),
 2+O(\mathfrak A_{\rm ns}),1,0,O(\mathfrak H_{\rm ns}),
 O(\mathfrak A_{\rm ns}),0,\\
 &O(C_1),O(C_1),O(\mathfrak D_{\rm ns}),3+\Theta(1)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-prefix-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
The exposed and admitted row arena is exactly $\widehat U_1$; only the labels
of $\mathcal F_1$ are known.  In particular, no row beyond the live
certificate batch is exposed before the native suffix.

With $J_+=m-1$, the direct native build on $\widehat U_1$, the remaining
$J_+$ batches, and terminal return have vector
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{11}(\mathcal E_{\rm NS,+1}^{\rm post})=\bigl(&
 \vol(S^\star\setminus\widehat U_1),J_+,J_++1,0,
 O(C(S^\star)),0,\\
 &O(C(S^\star)+J_+\log(2+m)),
 \Theta(C(S^\star)),O(C(S^\star)),\Theta(\card{S^\star}),\\
 &\card{S^\star\setminus\widehat U_1}
  +\card{S^\star}+\Theta(J_++1)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-post-eleven-vector}
\end{equation}
Thus $R_{\rm int}^{\rm total}=m+1$, the structural first-exposure component
of $R_{\rm adj}^{\rm total}$ is exactly $m+1$, and
\begin{equation}
 R_{\rm adj}^{\rm total}=m+1+O(\mathfrak A_{\rm ns}),
 \qquad
 W_{\rm total}=O\!\left(
 \mathfrak H_{\rm ns}+C(S^\star)\log(2+C(S^\star))\right).
 \label{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-total-work}
\end{equation}

This is the promised finite GO: a nonsettled canonical transition and a real
post-transition accelerated stage are both fully charged.  It is also an
exact STOP for the stronger interpretation.  The proof restarts the
enlarged-face estimate account at the observable budget $B_{\rm ns}$; it does
not amortize that budget, prove that the old estimate value survives, or
derive a multi-face accelerated rate.  The extra range $\rho<1/30$, live
candidate-row exposure, two transition products, the explicit
$\log(1/(1-2\alpha))$ oracle nonuniformity, and the paid native suffix all
remain visible.  No speedup, graph-uniform theorem, other face/range/seed/
policy result, finite-precision guarantee, bit bound, PPR conversion, or
RPPR-to-PPR claim follows.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The first shifted stage and strict batch certificate are
\Cref{lem:branch-caterpillar-first-nonsettled-admission}.  The preinteraction
row arena consists of the degree-three seed row and the three candidate rows
of total degree six, giving nine first-exposure cells in two rounds and one
three-label/certificate interaction.  The two stored-row products, sparse
array appends, and estimate registration have
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-transition-vector}.  The imported
relative oracle applies to every finite signed center on the fixed face
$\widehat U_1$ and costs at most $\mathcal V_1L_1^{\rm rel}$ degree work.
Because the carried momentum is nonzero, its center is genuinely
extrapolated.  Summing these charges, assigning logarithmic heap/control work,
and taking the one-live-arena memory peaks proves
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-prefix-eleven-vector}.

No row of $\mathcal F_1$ was read by the prefix.  A direct paid response build
from the retained $\widehat U_1$ rows is therefore the usual native state
after the first canonical batch; it reconstructs no historical gate.  The
remaining rows partition $S^\star\setminus\widehat U_1$, and removing the
already emitted first batch from the reviewed canonical ledger gives
\cref{eq:branch-caterpillar-nonsettled-post-eleven-vector}.  The round
partitions are
\[
 2+(m-1)=m+1,
 \qquad 1+(m-1+1)=m+1,
\]
before adding the explicitly displayed numerical adjacency rounds.  Additive
work sums and sequential storage takes the maximum, proving the total bound
and every stated scope restriction.
\end{proof}
