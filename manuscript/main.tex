\begin{abstract}
We study hybrid accelerated algorithms for local graph optimization problems.
As a baseline, we prove that classical approximate personalized PageRank
(APPR) has worst-case degree-weighted work
\(\Theta(1/(\alpha\epsappr))\). The matching lower bound is
witnessed by a center-seeded star and holds for every legal ordering of active
vertices. We then seek to combine accelerated optimization frameworks with
adaptive local solvers to improve theoretical guarantees and practical
performance beyond this obstruction.
\end{abstract}
