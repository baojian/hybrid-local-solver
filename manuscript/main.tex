\begin{abstract}
We study accelerated algorithms for local graph optimization problems.
We first prove that classical approximate personalized PageRank (APPR) has
worst-case degree-weighted work \(\Theta(1/(\alpha\epsappr))\); a
center-seeded star gives the matching lower bound for every legal active
ordering.  For \(\ell_1\)-regularized PageRank, we sharpen the work analysis
of full-batch ISTA and prove matching star and direct-sum lower bounds.  We
then give a residual-thresholded coordinate method and a coordinate-to-batch
hybrid whose worst-case work is exactly \(\Theta(1/(\alpha\rho))\) at fixed
relative KKT accuracy.  Finally, we analyze a coarse-forward-push/SOR hybrid:
its first phase is ordering-independently tight, while a long spider forces
\(\Omega(1/(\alpha\epsppr))\) total work and rules out the desired uniform
square-root improvement for the fixed-relaxation FIFO variant.  All results
use explicit, noninterchangeable accuracy conventions.
\end{abstract}
