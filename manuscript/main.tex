\begin{abstract}
Local PageRank algorithms seek sparse approximations with work bounds
independent of the graph size. Classical local push bounds scale as
$1/\alpha$ in the teleportation parameter $\alpha$. We develop deterministic and
randomized algorithms that achieve a $1/\sqrt\alpha$ dependence in total
local work. For $\ell_1$-regularized personalized PageRank with regularization
parameter $\rho$, both methods attain additive objective accuracy
$\epsobj$ in $\widetilde O(1/(\rho\sqrt\alpha))$ work, in expectation
for the randomized method, with only polylogarithmic dependence on
$1/\epsobj$. This attains the algorithmic running-time target posed by
Fountoulakis and Yang at COLT 2022. An explicit regularization-bias argument
yields personalized PageRank approximations with degree-normalized maximum
error $\epsppr$ in $\widetilde O(1/(\epsppr\sqrt\alpha))$ work,
again in expectation for the randomized method.
The deterministic algorithm combines regularization continuation with
constrained acceleration; its analysis bounds cumulative local work even
when intermediate updates leave the optimal support. The randomized
algorithm expands certified active sets in batches and controls their
number through the PageRank spectrum. The results hold for point sources
on finite connected simple unweighted undirected graphs. The bounds charge
all graph access, computation, certification, and output, without graph-wide
preprocessing.
\end{abstract}
