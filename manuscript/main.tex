\begin{abstract}
We give deterministic and randomized local algorithms for two related
PageRank tasks. For personalized PageRank, we attain degree-normalized
maximum error $\epsppr$ in $\widetilde O(1/(\epsppr\sqrt\alpha))$ work.
For $\ell_1$-regularized personalized PageRank, we attain additive objective
accuracy $\epsobj$ in $\widetilde O(1/(\rho\sqrt\alpha))$ work, with
only polylogarithmic dependence on $1/\epsobj$. Here $\alpha$ is the lazy
PageRank parameter and $\rho$ is the regularization parameter. The second
bound attains the algorithmic running-time target posed by Fountoulakis
and Yang at COLT 2022; an explicit regularization-bias argument yields the
first. All discovery, repeated adjacency scans, arithmetic, state
maintenance, certification, and sparse output are charged, with no
graph-wide preprocessing or supplied solution support.

The deterministic algorithm combines regularization continuation with
accelerated corrections constrained by a degree-scaled box and a mass cap.
Two energies from the same recurrence prove accelerated convergence and
control the response outside a small analytical comparison set. A
signed-flow argument bounds cumulative scanned volume, including repeated
visits, and a deterministic threshold data structure realizes this bound.
We also give a bounded-arithmetic realization with explicit encoding costs.
The randomized algorithm uses support-safe principal solves; a
block-Cholesky and Chebyshev argument limits the threshold-batch depth.
Certified SDD solves give expected work
$\widetilde O(V_*\min\{k_*,\alpha^{-1/2}\})$, where $k_*$ and $V_*$
are the optimum's support cardinality and original-degree volume.
The main results hold for point sources on finite connected simple
unweighted undirected graphs. They concern the stated algorithms and do
not require confinement of standard FISTA iterates.
\end{abstract}
