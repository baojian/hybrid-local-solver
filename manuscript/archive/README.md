# Imported publication archives are held privately

The public release excludes the imported publication source workspaces and reviewer correspondence previously stored here. Their redistribution was not cleared. Original records remain in the private history repository; public source citations and the project's literature notes remain in `docs/literature/`.

This does not exclude the project's standalone research notes in `../notes/`. Readable excerpts of the original deterministic research packages are in `../research-records/`, with original archive checksums and per-file provenance. See `../../docs/public-release/README.md` for the full exclusion inventory and history mapping.
